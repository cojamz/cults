# Batch 2: what the bots did with the 8 new cards

120 games with the hardest bots and 600 with the normal bots, each new card in its old card's place, on the 10-card deck. Compared with 75 hard and 600 normal games of today's game. "Taken" is how many times a bot owned it for each game it showed up in the shop (over 100% means it changed hands or showed up more than once). "Owners won" is how often a player who owned it won; 25% is an even share with 4 players. Owner counts under 15 are rough.

**Spoilsports** (Grey, replaces Scouts): [6 any, worth 1] "Round End: choose a contest you don't lead; nobody wins it this round."
Taken 67% vs Scouts ("On Use, once per round: send any shop members to the bottom of the pile, then refill.") 115%. Owners won 31% with both kinds of bots, vs 23 to 25% for Scouts. The strongest of the batch; watch it.

**Body Snatchers** (Red, replaces Triplets): [4 red + 2 any, worth 2] "Round Start: swap one of your members with a rival's member of the same cost."
Taken 64% vs Triplets ("Worth 3 followers.") 125%. Owners won 25% (normal bots 34%, vs 28% for Triplets). Wanted and fair.

**Hush Money** (Silver, replaces Smooth Talkers): [4 silver + 2 any, worth 1] "Whenever a rival steals from you, you may give them 2 silver instead."
Taken 24% vs Smooth Talkers ("Your silver pays for any color.") 16%. Owners won 38% with the hardest bots (13 owners) but 17% with normal bots. Mixed; a defensive card bots can't judge well.

**Night Market** (Yellow, replaces Ringleader): [4 yellow + 2 any, worth 1] "Round End: before contests are scored, swap up to 3 of your resources for other colors, one for one."
Taken 24% vs Ringleader ("On Recruit: change 1 of each rival's resources one for one. +1 follower per rival changed.") 72%. Owners won 30% (normal 35%). Rarely bought but does well when it is.

**Doorbusters** (Blue, replaces Shopaholics): [5 blue + 3 any, worth 2] "Round Start: recruit once, before anyone places."
Taken 20% vs Shopaholics ("You can recruit twice each turn.") 16%. Owners won 17% with the hardest bots, 31% with normal bots. About the same as the card it replaces.

**Skimmers** (Magenta, replaces Desperado): [4 magenta + 2 any, worth 1] "Whenever a rival buys 10 followers, steal 3 of them."
Taken 13% vs Desperado ("While you have the fewest followers (ties count), your steals take 2 more resources.") 31%. Only 7 hardest-bot owners, and 5 of them won; normal-bot owners won 36%. Bots undervalue it; it may be strong.

**Mirror Image** (Green, replaces Wallflower): [6 green + 3 any, worth 1] "On Use, once per game: choose a rival and a color; gain that color until you hold as much of it as they do."
Taken 7% vs Wallflower ("Whenever you copy an action, double the resources it gives you.") 12%. Owners won 41% with normal bots (27 owners). Bots almost never buy it at 9; the few who do win often. A cheaper price would tell more (Claude's fill-in: 5 green + 2 any).

**Fundraisers** (Silver, replaces Debutantes): [6 silver + 3 any, worth 2] "On Use, once per game: each rival +2 silver, then +1 follower per 2 silver rivals hold in all."
Taken 14% vs Debutantes ("On Use, once per game: double your silver. Each rival +2 silver.") 31%. The 8 hardest-bot owners never won; normal-bot owners 24%. The miss of the batch: it hands every rival silver for a few followers.

## Across the batch
Spoilsports and Body Snatchers are the clear keepers. Fundraisers is the miss. Skimmers, Mirror Image and Night Market are bought rarely but win at or above an even share when they are, so the bots may be undervaluing them; human play would tell.
