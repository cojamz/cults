# Night Owls and Cloak test games (Nov 3)

Normal bots: 600 games each. Hardest bots: 100 to 120 games each (rough). 10-card deck. Night Owls tests force Purple to the table. 25% is an even share with 4 players.

## Night Owls ("From round II: after your last minion, +1 minion, placed after everyone else's.")
Owners won, normal bots / hardest bots:
- Today (price 8): 46% / 34%
- Price 10: 43% / 38%
- Price 12: 39% / not run
- Extra minion not placed on the Goes Last token: 35% / 38%
Raising the price helps a little. Taking the extra minion off the token helps the most with normal bots. With the hardest bots, Night Owls is close to fair already (34%, 29 owners).

## Cloaks (all three changes tested together)
Holder won, normal bots (600 games):
- **Caterer** "Whenever silver is gifted, +1 silver." → +2: 20% → 40%. Overshoots.
- **Impressionist** "+2 of its color" → +1: 35% → 20%. Overshoots.
- **Money Changers** only when you buy followers: 38% → 14%. Overshoots.
Each fix went too far. Claude's read: keep all three cloaks as they are today; Caterer is the weakest and Money Changers the strongest, but none of these steps lands near 25%.
