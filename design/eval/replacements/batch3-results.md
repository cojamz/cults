# Batch 3: what the bots did with the 8 new cards

120 games with the hardest bots and 600 with the normal bots, each new card in its old card's place, on the 10-card deck, against 120 hard and 600 normal games of today's game. "Taken" is how many times a bot owned it for each game it showed up in the shop. "Owners won" is how often a player who owned it won; 25% is an even share with 4 players. Owner counts under 15 are rough.

**Kidnappers** (Magenta, replaces Kingpin): [4 magenta + 2 any, worth 1] "On Recruit and Round Start: steal the cheapest member of the rival with the most members."
Taken 89%, like Kingpin ("Game End: +1 follower per magenta member you have.") at 93%. Owners won 44% with the hardest bots (48 owners), 30% with normal bots. Too strong as priced; the hardest bots ride it. Next: retest at 2 more (Claude's fill-in: 6 magenta + 2 any).

**Impersonators** (Green, replaces Muse): [4 green + 2 any, worth 1] "On Recruit and Round Start: choose a rival's member. Until you choose again, this member has its ability."
Taken 75% vs Muse ("Whenever a rival copies an action, +1 green.") 58%. Owners won 34% (normal bots 35%, Muse 22%). Wanted and a little strong. A hit.

**Creditors** (Silver, replaces Collector): [4 silver + 2 any, worth 2] "Game End: each rival loses 1 follower per 3 silver they hold."
Taken 68% vs Collector ("Whenever a rival recruits paying silver, +1 silver.") 33%. Owners won 24% (normal bots 19%). Wanted twice as often, about even with the hardest bots. A hit; if it plays weak, worth 3.

**Interrupters** (Purple, replaces Courier): [4 purple + 2 any, worth 1] "Whenever a rival takes two turns in a row, you may skip the second."
Taken 15%, like Courier ("Whenever you take two turns in a row, +1 of each other color.") at 21%. Owners won 29% (Courier 10%). Rarely bought; fine when it is.

**Missionaries** (Red, replaces Patriarch): [6 red + 3 any, worth 2] "On Use, once per game: give one of your members to each rival, then recruit a shop member costing up to 2 more for each one given, for free."
Taken 23% vs Patriarch ("Game End: +1 follower per 2 members you have.") 104%. Owners won 25%. Bots avoid giving members away.

**Pacers** (Purple, replaces Shift Crew): [6 purple + 3 any, worth 2] "On Use, once per round: move one of your minions to an open space and do that action."
Taken 19% vs Shift Crew ("Worth 4 followers.") 84%. Owners won 25%. Rarely bought at 9.

**Evictors** (White, replaces Hoarder): [5 white + 3 any, worth 1] "On Use, once per game: cast out a rival's member to the bottom of the pile. You and that rival each gain white equal to its cost."
Taken 10%, like Hoarder ("On Use, once per game: turn all your resources into white.") at 7%. Owners won 17% (normal 19%). A miss: giving the victim the same white cancels the sting.

**Completists** (Blue, replaces Patroness): [6 blue + 3 any, worth 2] "On Use, once per game: recruit every shop member of one color, paying their costs."
Taken 10%, like Patroness ("Whenever shop members go to the bottom of the pile, +1 follower.") at 12%. The 6 hardest-bot owners never won; normal 24%. A miss: nobody has the resources to buy a whole color at once.

## Across the batch
Hits: Impersonators and Creditors. Kidnappers is popular but too strong as priced; retest higher. Misses: Evictors and Completists. Interrupters, Missionaries and Pacers are rarely bought and about even when they are.
