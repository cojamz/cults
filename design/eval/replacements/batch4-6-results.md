# Batches 4, 5 and 6: what the bots did with the 20 new cards

Each batch: 120 games with the hardest bots and 600 with the normal bots, new cards in their old cards' places, 10-card deck, against 120 hard and 600 normal games of today's game. "Taken" is how many times a bot owned it for each game it showed up in the shop. "Owners won": 25% is an even share with 4 players. Hardest-bot numbers first, normal bots in brackets. Owner counts under 15 are rough.

## Hits
**Godparents** (Silver, replaces Hangers-On): [4 silver + 1 any, worth 1] "On Use, once per game: give one of your members to a rival. Gain followers equal to its cost."
Taken 52% (54%) vs Hangers-On ("Whenever a rival gives you something, +1 more of it.") 26% (19%). Owners won 27% (26%), vs 21% (17%). A dead card turned into a fair one. Going live.

**Tithe Takers** (Red, replaces Mentor & Protégés): [4 red + 2 any, worth 2] "On Use, once per game: each rival gives you one of their members."
Taken 94% (51%). Owners won 33% (40%). Too strong as priced; retesting at 2 more. (Mentor & Protégés, "On Recruit: recruit the cheapest member in the shop.", isn't in the 10-card deck, so no old number.)

## Fair, not clearly better than the old card
**Inquisitors** (White, replaces Raiders): [4 white + 1 any, worth 1] "On Use, once per game: each rival casts out one of their members. +2 followers per member cast out." Taken 56% (14%), owners 33% (32%); Raiders ("On Use, once per game: steal 4 followers from a rival. Pay them 2 white.") 50% (41%), 27% (31%).
**Peddlers** (Grey, replaces Alchemists): [5 any, worth 1] "On Recruit and Round Start: put the top member of the pile beside this card. Rivals may recruit members there, paying you instead of the bank." Taken 70% (77%), owners 24% (27%); Alchemists ("All your resources count as any when you recruit a member.") 98% (107%), 33% (29%). One rules bug left in the build (a copied On Recruit can start a stall nobody owns).
**Pallbearers** (White, replaces Old Money): [4 white + 1 any, worth 1] "Whenever a rival casts out a member, gain followers equal to half its cost, rounded up." Taken 36% (17%), owners 37% (29%); Old Money ("Game End: +1 follower per white member you have.") 78% (85%), 29% (29%).
**Sore Losers** (Grey, replaces HR): [5 any, worth 1] "Round End: if you win no contest, steal 3 followers from the rival who wins the most." Taken 71% (79%), owners 21% (25%); HR ("Counts as 4 members. Game End: +2 followers per color you have 3 members of.") 120% (167%), 31% (29%).
**Outbidders** (Blue, replaces Window Dressers): [3 blue + 1 any, worth 1] "Whenever a rival recruits a shop member, you may pay its cost plus 2 to recruit it instead. They get back what they paid and recruit again." Taken 48% (51%), owners 31% (21%); Window Dressers ("On Recruit: send 3 shop members to the bottom of the pile, then refill.") 102% (91%), 24% (26%).
**Copycats** (Green, replaces Roadie): [3 green + 1 any, worth 1] "Round End: in each contest where you hold exactly 1 less than the winner, you tie for it." Taken 56% (47%), owners 16% (33%); Roadie ("On Recruit: +1 green per green member you have. Round Start: +1 green per green member you have.") 152% (174%), 28% (31%).
**Successors** (White, replaces Cashier): [4 white + 1 any, worth 1] "Whenever you cast out one of your members, recruit a shop member costing up to its cost, for free." Taken 25% (18%), owners 29% (24%); Cashier ("On Recruit: turn all your white into followers.") 52% (49%), 15% (27%).

## Rarely bought (bots pass on them)
**Social Climbers** (Silver, the Debutantes slot that Fundraisers flopped in): [6 silver + 3 any, worth 1] "On Use, once per game: gain followers equal to the silver of the rival who holds the most." Taken 22% (10%); the 11 hardest-bot owners won 55%, normal 28%. Better than Fundraisers, still rarely bought at 9.
**Second-Story Men** (Magenta, replaces Sticky Fingers): [4 magenta + 3 any, worth 1] "Round End: before contests are scored, steal 2 resources from one rival." Taken 19% (12%), but owners won 55% (61%). Bots undervalue it; likely strong.
**Jackals** (Magenta, replaces Shoplifter): [4 magenta + 2 any, worth 1] "Whenever a rival steals resources, steal 1 of them from that rival." Taken 9% (11%), owners 40% (41%).
**Defectors** (Red, replaces Proselytizers): [6 red + 3 any, worth 1] "On Use, once per game: swap all your red members, this one too, for all of one rival's red members." Taken 22% (19%), owners 50% (24%).
**Hawkers**, **Late Shoppers**, **Opening Act** (Blue, Blue, Green), **Vultures**, **Tagalongs**, **Full House** (Magenta, Purple, Purple): taken 0 to 18%, owners about even or below. Texts in batch4.md and batch5.md.

## Misses
**Second Fiddles** (Yellow, the Dyer slot that Barterers flopped in): [5 yellow + 3 any, worth 1] "Round End: +2 followers per contest you come second in." Taken 8% (6%), owners won 0 of 3 (13%). The Dyer slot is still open; Dyer itself ("On Recruit: +1 yellow per color you hold. Round Start: +1 yellow per color you hold.") does fine.

## Across the three batches
Batch 6 (White, Silver, Grey) did best. Batch 5 (Purple, Yellow, Magenta) mostly went unbought: its cards cost 7 to 9 and act on rivals' moves, and bots don't price that well. The two magenta round-end and steal cards win a lot when bought, so human play may show them stronger than bots think.
