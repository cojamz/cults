# Batch 1: what the bots did with the 8 new cards

120 games with the hardest bots and 600 with the normal bots, each new card in its old card's place, on the 10-card deck. Compared with 60 hard and 600 normal games of today's game. "Taken" is how many times a bot owned it for each game it showed up in the shop. "Owners won" is how often a player who owned it won; 25% is an even share with 4 players. Hard-bot owner counts are small (6 to 40 owners), so read those win rates as rough.

**Hostesses** (Silver, replaces Gift Wrapper): [4 silver + 2 any, worth 2] "On Recruit and Round Start: give each rival 1 silver. +1 follower per rival who then holds more silver than you."
Hardest bots took it in 71% of the games it showed up; they took Gift Wrapper ("Whenever you give rivals silver, +1 follower.") in 6%. Owners won 26% (normal bots 27%). A dead card turned into one bots want, at a fair strength. Keep.

**Heirs** (White, replaces Exporters): [4 white + 3 any, worth 2] "Game End: +1 follower per 2 white you hold."
Taken 58% vs Exporters ("Whenever you recruit a non-white member, +1 white.") 21%. Owners won 21% (normal 28%). Wanted more, a little weak with the hardest bots. If it needs help, worth 3 instead of 2 (Claude's fill-in).

**Turncoats** (Red, replaces Volunteer): [3 red + 1 any, worth 2] "Whenever a rival takes one of your members into their cult, recruit a shop member costing up to its cost, for free."
Taken 82%; Volunteer ("Worth 1 follower.") is taken almost every time it shows. Owners won 30% (normal 38%, the highest of the batch). Strong with normal bots; watch it.

**Night Watch** (Purple, replaces Clockmakers): [3 purple + 2 any, worth 2] "Whenever a rival takes the Goes Last token, +1 follower per minion on it."
Taken 63%, same as Clockmakers ("Round Start: take your first two turns in a row.") at 61%. Owners won 30% vs 36%. A fair swap.

**Grudge** (Grey, replaces Line Cutters): [4 any, worth 2] "On Recruit: choose a rival. Game End: steal 4 followers from them if they have more than you."
Taken about as often as Line Cutters ("You go first every round."), in nearly every game. Owners won 25% (normal 22%). Even, maybe slightly weak.

**Scavengers** (Blue, replaces Critic): [4 blue + 2 any, worth 1] "Whenever a rival sends shop members to the bottom of the pile, you may recruit one of them, paying its cost."
Taken only 18% vs Critic ("Whenever a rival recruits a blue member, +1 follower.") 67%. The 11 hardest-bot owners won 45%, but normal-bot owners won 21%. Bots rarely want it. It only works when rivals clear the shop, which may not happen often enough.

**Racketeers** (Magenta, replaces Crew): [4 magenta + 2 any, worth 1] "Whenever a rival wins a contest, steal 2 of that color from them."
Taken 27% (normal bots 7%). Owners won 29% (normal 39%). When bought it does well; bots rarely buy it. Crew was already out of the 10-card deck, so there's no old number.

**Barterers** (Yellow, replaces Dyer): [4 yellow + 2 any, worth 1] "On Recruit and Round Start: swap up to 3 of your resources with one rival's, one for one."
Taken 15% vs Dyer ("On Recruit: +1 yellow per color you hold. Round Start: +1 yellow per color you hold.") 125% (Dyer gets taken more than once a game). The 6 hardest-bot owners won none; normal-bot owners 24%. The weakest of the batch: a 3-for-3 swap doesn't add anything, it only moves colors.

## Across the batch
Hostesses, Heirs, Night Watch and Grudge are taken at least as often as the card they replace, and their owners win about an even share. Turncoats is taken a little less than Volunteer, and its owners win the most of the batch. Hostesses and Heirs fix two cards bots ignored. Barterers is the one that looks like a miss. Scavengers and Racketeers are rarely bought but do fine when they are, so the bots may be undervaluing them; human play would tell.
