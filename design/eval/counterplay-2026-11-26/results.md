# Can the strongest bot play better? Three habits tested (Nov 26, 11:20pm)

Each habit was built into the strongest bot (Very Hard) and played against today's strongest bot. 30 deals, each played twice with the seats swapped, so both sides got the same cards and colors: 60 four-player games per row, two seats each side.

| Habit added | Seats won (with it / today's bot) | Followers a player ended with (with it / today's) | Members a player had (with it / today's) | What it means at the table |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Counterplay**: before each move it also plays out the move the leading rival wants most (their best open space, or their best shop member), and takes it if slowing them down comes out ahead | 30 / 35 | 69.0 / 69.1 | 5.7 / 5.5 | No better. Taking the leader's space instead of your own best one did not pay over 60 games. |
| **Let go of contests you can't win**: a contest is worth only its chance of being won with the turns left, so it stops spending on a lead nobody can catch or a gap it can't close | 34 / 33 | 72.0 / 71.4 | 5.8 / 6.1 | No better (it gained about 2 followers a player in the Nov 12 game; on today's game, nothing). |
| **Count what members still do later**: when it looks ahead to the round's end, a member it holds is credited with what its ability will still earn in later rounds (before, only its follower) | 33 / 29 | 69.1 / 69.4 | **7.2 / 5.0** | It recruited 2 more members a player and ended with the same followers. |

The differences in followers are all under 1 a player, and 60 games can tell apart about 2 a player, so none of the three is a real gain or loss.

## What the third row says about "members feel optional"
The bot that saw members' later abilities bought about 2 more members a game (7.2 against 5.0) and finished level. So when a strong player values what members will do, recruiting more neither wins nor loses: today a member pays back about what the same resources would buy as followers. That points at member value in the cards, not only at bots playing badly. Winners with 5 or fewer members: 7 of its 33 wins, against 17 of 29 for today's bot.
