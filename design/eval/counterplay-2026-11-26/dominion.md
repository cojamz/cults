# Dominion test: followers only, against one round I member then followers (Nov 26, 11:45pm)

Both sides are the strongest bot (Very Hard). 30 deals, each played twice with the seats swapped: 60 four-player games, two seats each side. Neither side may take a shop member except as listed; members that come from a space (a free recruit) still join, which is why the followers-only side ends with about 2.

| Side | Rule for buying members | Members a player ended with | Followers a player ended with | Seats won |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| One early member | may buy one shop member, in round I only; then only followers | 2.6 | 77.6 | 36 |
| Followers only | never buys a shop member | 1.9 | 76.5 | 28 |

The one-member side ended about 1 follower ahead a player (give or take 1), and won 36 seats to 28. That leans the way Cory expects (money plus one Smithy beats money) but it is small: over 60 games a difference under about 2 followers can be chance.
