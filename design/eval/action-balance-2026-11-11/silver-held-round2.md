# How much silver players hold when Silver's round II spaces are open (Nov 11)

Cory, Nov 11: "silver's round two spaces sit empty. Huh. I feel like the bots aren't looking at those right."

60 Hard 4-player games, Silver at every table (FORCE=silver, games 1000-1059, code b37c686a), every round II placement.

- The player placing held 0 to 2 silver at 93% of round II placements (average 0.7), 3 to 5 at 7%, never 6 or more.
- Flaunt ("+1 of each other color per 3 silver you hold, up to 3 each. +1 silver.") pays only its +1 under 3 silver. It was taken in 14 of 60 rounds, every time by a player holding 3 or more (3 in 11 of them).
- Buyout ("Recruit a rival's member, paying them its cost in silver. +1 silver.") was taken in 6 of 60 rounds, by players holding 2 to 5.
- The bots' own value for both spaces matches what they pay at the silver held (silver.js estimates), so the bots read them right: the spaces sit empty because nobody holds silver in round II. Players spend silver on followers and members, Polish turns it all into another color, and Silver's contest pays whoever holds the fewest silver at round end.
