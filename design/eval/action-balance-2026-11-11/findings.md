# First action test: today's game, before White, Yellow and Red finish (Nov 11)

This is 400 games with Hard bots and 4 players. The setup is today's game: 10-card decks, each color its own contest, and each space's +1 paid only to its first taker. Each table had 4 random colors. 200 games are the game as it is. The other 200 have Tribute Band out of Green's deck and Ensemble in its place. Both sets played the same deals.

For each space, the report measures what one minion there gives its placer that turn: followers, resources, and members counted at their price. A paid recruit comes out about even, so what is left over is what the space added. Each space is also compared with the players who ended with about as many members. A finding is listed here only when both sets of 200 games agree on it. The full tables are in the repo under design/eval/action-balance-2026-11-11/.

## What stands out

- **Greatest Hits** (Green, round III) "Choose a color other than green. Copy every action with a minion on its spaces. +1 green."
  - One minion there earns about 24, roughly double a typical round III space (about 11).
  - Its takers won 56 to 62% of games. Players who ended with as many members won about 30%.
  - At the table, round III's center goes to whoever reaches it first, and it usually decides the game.
- **Purple Cloak**: players who picked it won 39 to 42% of games, where 25% is an even share. No other cloak passes 33% (White Cloak is 32 to 33%).
- **Crunch Time** (Purple, round II) "+2 purple. Your next two turns come right after this one."
  - Left empty in 57% of the rounds it was open.
  - When it is taken, it is usually one of the last picks in the round.
- **Buyout** (Silver, round II) "Recruit a rival's member, paying them its cost in silver. +1 silver."
  - Left empty in 78% of the rounds it was open.
  - A player rarely holds enough silver to pay a member's full cost.
- **Flaunt** (Silver, round II) "+1 of each other color per 3 silver you hold, up to 3 each. +1 silver."
  - Left empty in 70% of the rounds it was open.
  - So both of Silver's round II spaces sit empty most rounds.
- **Late Shift** (Red, round I) "Recruit a shop member costing up to 2 per minion you've placed this round, for free. +1 red." and **Borrow** (Red, round I) "Recruit the cheapest shop member, for free. Discard it at round end, before scoring. +1 red."
  - Each earns about 3 to 4 more than a typical round I space. That is about 9, against about 5.5.
  - It does not show up in who wins. Late Shift's takers win at an even rate, and Borrow's win about 6 points less than players as wide.

## Your questions from today

- **Does Red run short of red?** No.
  - The player who used Red's spaces most gained about 16 red a game.
  - That is the same as the heaviest users of Green (15), Yellow (16) and Blue (18).
  - This was with Late Shift in Trending's place. No +1 red needed for now.
- **Tribute Band out, Ensemble in: does it change anything?** Not that the games can measure.
  - Small cults won 47% with Tribute Band and 49% without it.
  - Winners scored 83.5 and 83.1 followers.
  - Ensemble's owners won at an even rate.
- **How many members leave cults?** About 4.5 per player a game in these games, counting swaps.
  - Most come from **Promote** and **Demote** swaps (about 2 a game each, across the table).
  - Then Borrow's member going back at round end (1.4).
  - Then **Swindle** (1.2), Test Drive (1.0), Farewell (1.0) and Write-Off (0.9).

## What the bots can't tell us

- Spaces that only turn one thing into another earn about 0 by this count, by design. That includes Farewell, Cash In and Makeover. So "earns less" on White's round II and III spaces says little.
- Going last, extra turns and an extra minion also count as 0 here.
- The 4 AI players' game (diaries every round) is running now. It covers how this feels at the table.
