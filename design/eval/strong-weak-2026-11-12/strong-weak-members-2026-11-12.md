# Strongest and weakest members, Nov 12 morning (new page)

We played the same 150 bot games three times (Hard bots, 4 players, today's rules): once as the game is today, once with the strongest members made dearer or worth less, and once with the weakest members worth 2 more followers. For each member, two numbers for the players who had it at game end: the followers they ended with on average, and the share of them who won (25% is a fair share at 4 players; everyone averaged 69 followers). Only 20 to 50 players had each card, so a share moves 5 to 10 points by chance; a change counts below only when both numbers move the same way.

## Both numbers agree
- Minimalists ("Game End: +2 followers per member you have costing 8 or more.", costs 8). Costing 10: 72 followers instead of 76, won 38% instead of 53%.
- Patron Saints ("Game End: +1 follower per silver member, anyone's.", worth 2). Worth 1: 76 instead of 78, won 24% instead of 41%.
- Duty Collector ("Whenever a rival recruits a member in one of your members' colors, +1 of that color.", worth 1). Worth 3: 69 instead of 63, won 35% instead of 29%.
- Encore ("On Use, once per game: your next On Recruit happens twice.", worth 1). Worth 3: 71 instead of 69, won 35% instead of 20%.

## The numbers disagree (no recommendation)
- Die-Hards ("Game End: if you have the most green members, +6 followers.", costs 7). Costing 9: 73 instead of 71, won 35% instead of 44%.
- Last-Minute Shopper ("Round End: +3 blue, then you may recruit.", costs 5). Costing 7: 74 both times, won 49% instead of 43%. Costing more didn't slow it; its strength is the free recruit after everyone has placed, so it needs a different change.
- Power Shoppers ("Whenever you recruit your second member in a turn, +3 followers.", worth 2). Worth 4: 68 instead of 69, won 30% instead of 25%.
- Extra Hand ("On Recruit: +1 minion this round.", worth 1). Worth 3: 70 instead of 78 (17 players had it today), won 33%.
- Double Agent, Cat Burglars, Foster Parents: too few players had them.

Small cults (5 or fewer members) won 45% of today's games, 51% and 47% in the changed ones (bar: 20%).
