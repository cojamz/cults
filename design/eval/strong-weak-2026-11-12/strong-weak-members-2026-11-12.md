# Strongest and weakest members, Nov 12 morning (new page)

We played the same 150 bot games three times (Hard bots, 4 players, today's rules): once as the game is today, once with the strongest members made dearer or worth less, and once with the weakest members worth 2 more followers. For each member: how many players had it at game end, and the share of those players who won (25% is a fair share at 4 players). Chance moves a share by about 5 to 8 points at these counts.

## Strongest
- Minimalists ("Game End: +2 followers per member you have costing 8 or more.", costs 8): costing 10, players who had it won 38% of games instead of 53%.
- Die-Hards ("Game End: if you have the most green members, +6 followers.", costs 7): costing 9, 35% instead of 44%.
- Patron Saints ("Game End: +1 follower per silver member, anyone's.", worth 2): worth 1, 24% instead of 41%.
- Last-Minute Shopper ("Round End: +3 blue, then you may recruit.", costs 5): costing 7, 49% instead of 43%. Costing more didn't slow it; its strength is the free recruit after everyone has placed.
- Cat Burglars and Foster Parents: too few games to read.

## Weakest, each worth 2 more followers
- Encore ("On Use, once per game: your next On Recruit happens twice.", worth 1 to 3): 35% instead of 20%.
- Duty Collector ("Whenever a rival recruits a member in one of your members' colors, +1 of that color.", worth 1 to 3): 35% instead of 29%.
- Power Shoppers ("Whenever you recruit your second member in a turn, +3 followers.", worth 2 to 4): 30% instead of 25%.
- Extra Hand ("On Recruit: +1 minion this round.", worth 1 to 3): 33%; nobody ended with it in today's games.
- Double Agent (worth 1 to 3): 27% instead of 26%, no real change.

Small cults (5 or fewer members) won 45% of today's games, 51% and 47% in the changed ones (bar: 20%).
