# Leaders' Tax and Heist reworks (Cory, Nov 4: "anything that relies on a rival having more of resources should be reworked")

Numbers are Claude's fill-ins. Each idea was played through a 4-player turn before going in.

## Heist (Magenta round II), now: "Steal half of one rival's pile of one color, rounded down. +1 magenta."
Problem under the new rule: players spend down to buy followers, so half a pile is often 1 or 2.
Siblings: **Shake Down** "Choose a rival. They choose one of their members costing 5 or less, then pay you its cost or you steal it. +1 magenta."; **Poach** (round III) "Recruit a rival's member you choose, for free. +1 magenta."; **Raiders** (White member) "On Use, once per game: steal 4 followers from a rival. Pay them 2 white."

1. [pick] **Heist** "Steal 3 followers from a rival. +1 magenta."
   - Played: round II, 4 players, the leader has 14 followers (round I contest and a buy). You take 3: you +3, they −3, a 6-follower swing between you. Best other round II space for you, **Promote** "...+3 red." plus a swap: about 3 resources and an upgrade. Round III it's still open and still 3.
   - Only recruited or won followers can be stolen (rulebook), and every cult has some by round II, so it never comes up empty.
   - Intuitive: one verb, one number. Fun: the victim feels it, and the leader becomes the target. Interacts: picks a rival by the score track. Powerful: takes score, not fuel. Engine or counter: a counter to the runaway leader (often the wide cult). Its color: Magenta steals; its spaces don't take followers yet (Raiders, a White member, does).
2. **Heist** "Steal 1 follower from each rival. +1 magenta."
   - Played: 4 players, +3 for you and −1 to each rival. Less pointed than 1; at 2 players it's only +1, weak.
   - Intuitive: yes. Fun: a small sting to all. Interacts: everyone. Powerful: less than 1. Engine or counter: mild counter. Its color: theft.
Cut: "steal a member costing 4 or less" (a smaller Poach); "they give a member or 3 followers, their choice" (Shake Down's shape again).

## Leaders' Tax (Yellow round I), now: "+1 yellow. Choose a color: each rival with more of it than you gives you 1 of it."
Problem under the new rule: rivals often hold little, so "more than you" pays 0 or 1.
Siblings: **Harvest** "+3 in round I, +4 in round II, +5 in round III, in colors you choose."; **Dilute** "+2 yellow, then change up to 3 of rivals' resources one for one." (Cory dislikes Dilute; another thread has it). Yellow's goal with By color: most full sets (one of every color).

1. [pick] **Leaders' Tax** "Choose a color: +1 of it per rival with a minion on its spaces. +1 yellow."
   - Played: round I, 4 players, you place your second minion (6th of 12). Two rivals have minions on Red: +2 red, +1 yellow (3). In round III most colors have all three rivals on them: +3, +1 yellow (4). Cory's cap from Wander (Oct 30: "max +2 round 1. and maybe 4 tops in later rounds") holds: round I is usually +1 or +2, never past +3. At 2 players it's at most +1: weak there.
   - Counts minions anyone can see on the board; never depends on what rivals hold.
   - Intuitive: count the rivals on one color. Fun: a timing choice, take it early to be sure of the space or late for a bigger count. Interacts: pays where rivals crowd, so it reads the table. Powerful: about Harvest's size in round I, a little under it later. Engine or counter: feeds full sets for Yellow's goal in whichever color you're short of. Its color: Yellow gets many colors; this takes them from wherever the table is busy.
   - Not Wander ("+1 of each other color whose space has your minion"): that counted your own minions and paid several colors; this counts rivals and pays one.
Cut: "+1 of each color among the shop members" (the "+1 of every other color" shape Cory refused); "turn followers into resources" (a loop with the follower cards); "+1 per rival with more followers" (tiny in round I, when everyone has about 1); Hard Bargain (takes from rivals' piles, same problem).
Only one idea for Leaders' Tax survived the checks.
