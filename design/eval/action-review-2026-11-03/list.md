# Actions worth changing (Nov 3 evening)

Fresh bot games on today's live rules with By color contests: 120 games with Hard bots and 400 with Normal bots, 4 players. Then five changes tried, each on 300 Normal-bot games with its color on every table (same games with and without the change). "Win" below means how often the player who took the space won the game; 25% is even. "Above cults their size" compares them with players who ended with about as many members.

## The big pattern: red on the table makes the game go wide
- With Red in play, the winner had 5 or fewer member cards in 7% of games (Hard) and 9% (Normal). Without Red: 38% and 32%. The bar is 20%.
- Players end with about 2 more member cards when Red is in play (7.7 vs 5.6), but about 5 more members counted (15 vs 10). The extra width comes from red members that count as 2 to 4: **Triplets** (counts as 3), **Mob** (counts as 4) "Game End: +1 follower per red member you have.", **Panel** (counts as 3), **Loyalists** (counts as 3), **Deprogrammers** (counts as 4), **Mentor & Protégés** (counts as 3), **Greeters** (counts as 2). **Patriarch** "Game End: +1 follower per 2 members you have." then pays for all of it.
- Red's spaces are where wide cults spend their minions (cults ending with 20+ members are 18% of players but take 31 to 44% of all uses of **Walk-In**, **Initiation**, **Promote**, **Demote** and **Tryout**), but changing them didn't shrink cults:
  - **Promote** / **Demote** at +1 red instead of +3: average members 16.9 → 16.6, wide cults still won about half.
  - **Walk-In** "Recruit the top member of the pile. Each rival +1 red. +1 red." and **Initiation** "Recruit a member in the shop costing 8 or less, for free. +1 red." with the free member taking the place of your recruit that turn: 16.9 → 16.4. No real change. Hard bots agree (60 games each way, red on every table): average members 16.4 → 15.8, winners with 5 or fewer member cards 10% → 8%.
- Read: the wide plan is fed by red's members that count as several, not by any action. That's a member change.

## Spaces that decide games (round III centers)
- **Poach** (Magenta III) "Recruit a rival's member you choose, for free. +1 magenta.": its taker won 45% (Hard) and 42% (Normal), about 15 points above cults their size. Tried "costing 8 or less": takers 33% → 31%. Barely moves.
- **Largesse** (Silver III) "Give rivals up to 10 silver from the bank, split as you choose. +1 follower per silver you give.": taker won 41% / 38%, 12 to 16 points above. Tried "up to 6": takers 39% → 36%, mostly-silver cults 34% → 27%.
- **Makeover** (Yellow III) "+4 yellow, then rearrange your resources into any mix of colors.": takers win a lot, but it's taken as a leftover by whoever still has a minion at the end; its takers won Yellow's round III contest 0 of 15 times (Hard). Tried +1 yellow: bots just stopped taking it. No change (Cory, Nov 2: "i like makeover as is").

## Spaces that trail at both bot levels
- Blue's shop spaces all trail cults their size by 2 to 11 points: **Savings** "+2 blue in round I, +3 in round II, +4 in round III. Before you recruit, you may send one shop member to the bottom of the pile, then refill.", **Recruit Twice** "Recruit twice this turn. +3 blue.", **Out of Style** "Send every shop member of one color to the bottom of the pile, then refill. +1 blue per member sent. +1 blue.", **Spree** "Recruit 3 times this turn. +5 blue.", **Peek** "Look at the top 4 of the pile and put them back in any order. This turn your recruit may be one of them. +2 blue in round I, +4 in round II, +6 in round III.", **Bury** "Send a shop member to the bottom of the pile, then refill. Gain blue equal to half its cost, rounded down. +1 blue." Cory said Spree isn't weak (Oct 29); bots may undervalue extra recruits.
- Purple's speed spaces trail while its going-last spaces lead. With By color, Purple's goal is placing the round's last minion. **Sleep In** "+4 purple in round I, +5 in round II, +6 in round III. Take the Goes Last token: your next minion is placed after everyone else's." and **Last Call** "+1 purple. +1 follower and +1 purple per minion you have left. Take the Goes Last token: your other minions are placed after everyone else's." are 5 to 11 points above cults their size. **Right Away** "+1 purple in round I, +2 in round II, +3 in round III. Your next turn comes right after this one." and **Crunch Time** "+2 purple. Your next two turns come right after this one." trail (Right Away's takers won 18% with Hard bots), and Hard bots skipped Crunch Time in half the games. Moving your turns earlier works against going last.
- **Estate Sale** "Cast out one of your members to the bottom of the pile, gain white equal to its cost. +1 white.": takers won 16% (Normal), 21% (Hard).

## What wasn't changed and why
Five changes tried tonight; none moved the wide plan, and the two round III nudges moved their takers by 2 to 3 points. More action changes are worth making where a space works against its color's goal (Purple's speed spaces) or trails at both levels (Blue), not as the fix for going wide.
