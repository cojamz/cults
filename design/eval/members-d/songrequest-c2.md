# Card trial: q-d-songrequest-c2
60 games with the change and the same 60 games without it (Hard bots, that see what cards do, 10-card deck, By color contests, 4 players; commit 0e71e228).
Members D: Song Request at 4 green + 3 any (c1 at 3+2: owners 39%)

## The cards
"Taken": games some cult recruited it, per game it showed up. "Owners won": an even share is 25%. Under 30 owners is too few to tell.
- **Song Request**: taken 84%, owners won 35% (46 owners): fair and wanted. Without the change, **Busker**: taken 85%, owners won 32% (71 owners). Owners who won: 7 of 18 were deep in their cloak's color, wide (9+ members).
