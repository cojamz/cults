# Member follower lifts, Nov 12 (new page)

Hard bots, 4 players, today's rules, 200 games per version on the same deals. "Holders won" is the share of games won by players who held the member at game end (25% is an even share). "Followers" is the paired change for the seats that held it, same deal, new version minus today. One run moves a holder's share by about 5 points by chance, and the paired follower change by about 1 to 1.5.

## Line Cutters ("You go first every round.", cost 4)
- Today, worth 3: holders won 36%.
- Worth 2: holders won 23%. Paired followers at its seats: -1.6.
- Worth 1: holders won 22%. Paired followers: -1.6.
Worth 2 and worth 1 can't be told apart at 200 games (the expected gap is 1 follower per holder, inside the noise). Worth 2 is the smaller change that reaches an even share.

## Members that can't pay back their price on paper (design/eval/member-payback)
Each lifted with followers, all in one batch:
- Bookies ("On Recruit: turn in up to 4 silver for 2 followers each.", 4 silver + 4 any), worth 1 to 4: holders won 24% to 21%, paired followers +3.1. Held at game end 45 to 63 times.
- Snobs ("Game End: +1 follower per blue member you have.", 4 blue + 4 any), worth 2 to 4: 32% to 29%, +3.4. Held 60 to 76.
- Window Shopper ("On Recruit and Round Start: discard 2 shop members. +1 of each one's color.", 3 blue + 4 any), worth 1 to 3: 29% to 30%, +3.4. Held 49 to 67.
- Thrift Hunters ("Whenever you recruit from the discard pile, +2 followers.", 3 blue + 4 any), worth 1 to 3: 27% to 32%, +0.3. Held 22 to 59.
- Timekeeper ("Game End: +1 follower per purple member you have.", 2 purple + 4 any), worth 1 to 2: 43% to 29%, +2.0. Today it already wins 43% for bots, so it doesn't need the lift.
Worth N also counts as N members (your rule, Nov 1), so each lift also helps in Most members.
Not tested: Recruiter and Treasurer (not in today's deck), Kingmakers (its problem is the round-end gift, not its payback).
Small cults won 47% of games with the lifts (bar 20%).

## Caveat on the paired numbers (Nov 12, 8am, after Cory asked what "+3.1, -4.0" meant)
"Same deal" seats are counted when the member was held in either version. Most seats held it in only one version (Bookies: 11 of 97 in both, 52 only in the new, 34 only today), so the resource change mostly reflects the games going differently after the first changed choice. A cleaner measure: pin the member in the same seat in both versions (deal it into that cult at the start), then compare.
