# Cloaks, and contest prizes +4/+6/+8 against +4/+6/+10 (Nov 25)

How it was tested: the same 800 four-player games were played twice with Normal bots on the 10-card deck, once with the live round III prize (+8) and once with the old one (+10). Every other card and setting was the same, and so was every deal. Hard bots were too slow to finish tonight.

## Contest prizes

| Prizes | Games where round III's contests changed who won | Winner held 5 or fewer members | Average winning margin | Average members per player | Average final followers |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| +4 / +6 / +8 (live) | 34 in 100 | 42 in 100 | 8.9 followers | 5.66 | 63.7 |
| +4 / +6 / +10 (old) | 37 in 100 | 44 in 100 | 9.8 followers | 5.60 | 63.8 |

What this means at the table: the round III contests decide the winner a little less often (3 fewer games in 100), and games end about 1 follower closer. Players hold almost the same number of members. Small cults win slightly less often, but 42 in 100 is still well above the bar of 20. This is a small change for the better, as you expected.

## Cloaks (the +8 games)

Each player picks a cloak from a color on the wheel at the start. A fair share of wins is 25 in 100. With about 400 owners per cloak, a result within 4 of 25 could be chance.

| Cloak | What it does | Times taken | Owner won (fair: 25 in 100) | Owner's followers against the table average |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Green Cloak | Whenever you copy an action or a member, +2 of its color (grey: any color). | 414 | 32 | +0.9 |
| White Cloak | Whenever you buy followers, +2 white. | 404 | 30 | +1.6 |
| Magenta Cloak | Whenever you steal, +2 magenta. | 403 | 28 | +1.0 |
| Red Cloak | Whenever you recruit a member, +1 red. | 411 | 28 | +0.1 |
| Purple Cloak | Whenever you take the Goes Last token, +2 purple. | 407 | 27 | +0.3 |
| Yellow Cloak | Whenever you recruit a member of a color none of your members have, +2 yellow. | 377 | 24 | +0.2 |
| Silver Cloak | Whenever you gift silver, +2 silver. | 399 | 19 | -2.2 |
| Blue Cloak | Whenever you discard a member, +1 blue. | 385 | 18 | -2.1 |

What this means:
- **Green** and **White** come out a little strong. The AI players said the same about White: two players called its refund "free money" on every pack of followers.
- **Silver** and **Blue** come out weak: their owners won about 1 game in 5 and ended 2 followers behind. Silver's pays only when you gift silver. Blue's pays +1 per member discarded, which is a small amount on a rare action.
- The other four are within chance of fair.
- The kind of fix for Silver and Blue: a bigger payout (Blue +2 instead of +1), or a trigger that happens more often. For White: +1 instead of +2, or only once per round.
