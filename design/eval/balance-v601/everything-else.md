# Everything else the bot games flagged (tonight's cards, v0.99.601)

Tentative: one fast run (600 games with Normal bots, 120 with Hard). "Owners win" is how often the card's owner won; even is about 25-30% at 4 players. Only Normal and Hard agreeing counts as a finding; the rest is to watch. Tonight's deep run (v0.99.606, with Very Hard) confirms or drops each.

## Members both levels agree on
- **Loyalists** [3 red + 3 any, worth 3, counts as 3] "Your other members can't be swapped." Weak at both levels (owners about 7 points below even). It protects against swaps, which are rare.
- **Mannequin** [3 blue + 3 any, worth 1] "Blue members in the shop count as your members when contests are scored." Weak at both (Normal -9, Hard -15). Also never shows in the game log acting (the rules check flags this too).
- **Picket Line** [3 purple + 2 any, worth 3, counts as 3] "Whenever a rival takes a Purple space, +1 purple." Strong at both (Normal +10, Hard +7), but the bot built to break it did not find it over its price. Watch.
- **Cheerleaders** [6 any, worth 3] "Whenever you win a contest, +2 followers." Strong with Normal (+7), small with Hard (+3). Watch.

## Members the levels disagree on (needs Very Hard)
- **Impersonator** [4 green + 3 any] "On Recruit: choose a rival's member. Impersonator has that member's ability." Normal -10, Hard +5: needs a good player.
- **Stunt Double** [3 green + 3 any] "On Recruit: copy the action you just took." Normal -7, Hard +2.
- **Window Shopper** [3 blue + 3 any] "On Recruit and Round Start: send 2 shop members to the bottom of the pile. +1 of each one's color." Normal -7, Hard +4.

## Spaces
- **Largesse** (silver, round III) "Give rivals up to 10 silver from the bank, split as you choose. +1 follower per silver you give." Strongest space at both levels (taker wins +15 Normal, +23 Hard over even). The AI players said the same on Nov 1.
- **Sleep In** (purple) "+4 purple in round I, +5 in II, +6 in III. Take the Goes Last token: your next minion is placed after everyone else's." Strong at both (+7, +9).
- **Makeover** (yellow, round III) "+4 yellow, then rearrange your resources into any mix of colors." Normal +13, Hard -25: Normal bots use it well, Hard doesn't. Unclear.
- **Recruit Twice** (blue, round II) "Recruit an extra time this turn. +3 blue." Weak at both (-6, -8). Power Shoppers' +4 should help it.
- **Gather** (red, round I) "+2 red in round I, +3 in II, +4 in III. This turn, if you recruit a member, +1 follower in round I, +2 in II, +3 in III." Weak at both (-7, -4).
- **Demote** (red, round II) "Swap any member with one in another cult or the shop costing up to 3 less. +3 red." Weak with Hard (-9).

## Colors (players whose main color it was, points above even)

- Green (echo): +6 Normal, +42 Hard (only 12 Hard seats). Rewards skill the most.
- Blue and purple: +3 Normal, +12 Hard. Reward skill.

- White, yellow: about even.

## The game as a whole
- Comebacks: the last player after round I still wins 17-19% (even 25%); the leader after round II wins 50-53%. A winner 10+ behind going into round III happened in 12-16% of games.
- Kingmaking: about 2.4 times a game, a player 15+ behind in round III hit one of the top two.
- Real choices: about 1 in 5 decisions is a close call for the Hard bot, about the same in every color. Green's spaces and grey members are close calls least often (12-13%).
- Steals hit the leader 40-42% of the time (even 33%): stealing does aim at the leader a little.
- Skill: one Hard bot among three Normals won 59% (even 25%).
- Small cults: 36% of winners had 5 or fewer members (bar 20%), same as before tonight.
- Seats: close to even.
