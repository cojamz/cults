# Tonight's cards: first results (partial)

Version tested: v0.99.601 (tonight's cards as first published). Bots: 600 games with Normal bots, 120 with Hard, 40 with one Hard among Normals. AI players: 2 full games (8 players). Still running: the same bot games on the version before tonight (for before/after), and overnight on v0.99.602 the deep run with Very Hard bots plus the skill ladder.

"Owners win" = how often the player who owned the card won (even is about 25-30% at 4 players). The number in brackets is the even share for that card's games.

## Too strong
- **Patron Saints** (4 silver + 3 any, worth 2, counts as 2 members): "Round End: +1 follower per silver member, anyone's." Owners win 44% (vs 30%) with Normal bots and 53% (vs 32%) with Hard; it pays about 14 followers for 7. The bot trying to break it found it clearly worth more than its price. AI players: 4 of 8 rated it strong ("+5 then +8 at round end", "underpriced at 7"); in game 1 too few silver members were out and it was dead. Both bot levels and the abuse check agree.
- **Ballroom Dancers** (4 silver + 3 any, worth 2, counts as 2): "On Use, once per game: +2 followers per minion on Silver spaces." Normal: owners win 43% (vs 32%); Hard: 37% (vs 31%), inside the noise. AI players liked it (fun 3-5): "+12 at the end of round III, a good payoff for waiting". One player noted it counts everyone's minions, so a rival living on silver sets it up for you.

## Too weak
- **Power Shoppers** (4 blue + 3 any, worth 2, counts as 2): "Whenever you recruit your second member in a turn, +2 followers." Hard: owners win 18% (vs 37%). All 8 AI players rated it 1-2: "two members in one turn hardly ever happens".
- **Heist Crew** (6 magenta + 3 any, worth 1): "On Use, once per game: steal half of a rival's biggest pile, rounded down." Bots almost never bought it (11 owners in 600 games). AI players: Lie Low and Bodyguards blank it ("netted 2 yellow"). v0.99.602 made it 6 + 2; overnight run checks.
- **Downsizers** (3 white + 2 any): on v0.99.601 nobody could ever use it (nothing cost 3 or less). v0.99.602 says "costing 4 or less"; overnight run checks.
- **Matchmaker** (4 yellow + 3 any): "Whenever you recruit a member of a color none of your members have, +2 wild, +1 follower." Bought in 10% of games; AI players 1-2 ("never a consideration"). v0.99.602 counts grey as a color; overnight run checks.
- **Snitch** (3 magenta + 2 any): "Whenever a rival steals from you, they lose 1 follower." Bots: about even. AI players 1-2: "a 1-follower penalty never deterred anyone".

## Feels bad though bots say weak
- **Counterfeiters** (3 yellow + 2 any): "On Use, once per round: turn all of a rival's resources into one color you choose." Hard owners win only 13% (vs 26%), but all 4 AI players in game 2 called it the most powerful card at the table: it picks who wins a contest each round. Fun rated 2 by 3 of 4 ("a kingmaker card", "felt bad both times"). The bots don't know how to aim it; people do.

## About right
- **Last-Minute Shopper** (3 blue + 1 any): "Round End: +2 blue, then you may recruit a shop member, paying its cost." Even with bots; AI players 3-5 ("sneaky strong", won blue contests).
- **Kingmakers** (3 silver + 2 any): "Round End: give a rival 3 silver from the bank. +3 followers." Even; AI players 3-4, "choosing who gets the silver is real table politics".
- **Night Traders** (3 yellow + 1 any): "Round End: before contests are scored, swap up to 3 of your resources for other colors, one for one." Normal even, Hard +14 (inside the noise). AI players: "mostly a threat, rarely used".
- **Night Manager** (2 purple + 3 any): "Round End: if you placed the round's last minion, +1 follower per purple member you have." Normal slightly below even, Hard above (noise). AI players 1-2: "only one player can ever use it".

## Whole game
- Skill: one Hard bot among three Normals won 59% (even 25%), same as Nov 4.
- Small cults: winners with 5 or fewer members went from 20% to 36% of games (bar: at least 20%). The +1 on every member price looks like the reason.
