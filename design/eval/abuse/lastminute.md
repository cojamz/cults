# Abuser test: Dusk Buyer

"Round End: +3 blue, then you may recruit from the discard pile." (costs 6; 10-card deck; 4 players; rivals normal; 4 seeds, 5 min)

With the card, the abuser ended +17.5 followers against the best rival compared with the same game without it (give or take 16.3; bought in 4 of 4 games, after paying 6).
It won 3 of 4 with the card and 2 of 4 without. (Games with and without the card go different ways after the first change, so this number swings a lot on few games.)
The card itself gave the abuser 0.8 followers on average (printed, during play and at game end; per game 1, 1, 1, 0) for a price of 6: -5.3 against spending the same on follower buys.
FLAGGED: worth clearly more than its price to a player trying to break it.
