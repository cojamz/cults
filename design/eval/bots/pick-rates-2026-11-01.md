# Normal bot pick rates, every 2.0 color (Nov 1, bug bash)

400 Normal-bot games, 2 to 4 seats, random 2.0 colors, decks 10 and 15 alternating. At every bot buy step each affordable shop member is a chance; rate = picks / chances. Driver: .v4-bugbash/picks.mjs (local). Why-probe: .v4-bugbash/why.mjs <def> ranks the member against the bot's pick at the moments it was on offer.

Finding: the members bots almost never take are not a valuation quirk. Normal values every ongoing ability the same way (2 x its price, fading with turns left), so what separates them is price against what they give. The expensive ones (9 to 11 total) are rarely affordable before round 2, and from round 2 on the bot sees them as spending 9 to 11 for about a follower plus a half-game of ability, below a cheap member, a follower tier, or passing. Old Maid (-3 followers when it joins) is meant to be avoided. Inflating bot values would make bots buy badly, which reads as less realistic, so this is price feedback for the balance pass, not a bot fix.
