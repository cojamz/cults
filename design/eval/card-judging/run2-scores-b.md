## K02 (Green space, round II: move a rival's minion onto this one, its space reopens)
- Q1 2: breaks "one minion per space" and leaves open whether the moved rival keeps what they got or gets this space; needs a ruling.
- Q2 3: the next player notices a good space reopened; the moved rival loses nothing.
- Q3 3: which minion to move is a choice, but the best one is usually obvious (the strongest space).
- Q4 2: you get +2 green and reopen a space for whoever goes next.
- Q5 2: links to nothing in its color or anyone's engine.
- Q6 2: moving minions and reopening spaces is turn and placement bending (Purple's verb), not copying.
- Q7 3: affects turn order and spaces.
- Q8 3: with 2 players the reopened space usually goes back to the rival.
- Q9 n/a
- Q10 2: replaces Encore, a copy space, with a card that does no copying, so Green loses a copy space.
- Played turn (Q1, Q4, Q5): 4 players, round II. P3 is on Promote (swap up, +3 red). P1 takes this space: +2 green and moves P3's minion here. P3 keeps the swap and the red. P4 goes next and takes Promote. P1 gained 2 resources and P4 gained a strong space. The best other space for P1 was Promote itself: +3 red plus a swap up 2 in cost, about 5 in value. That is about 3 less for P1, and the rest went to a rival.
- Verdict: FLAG (Q1, Q4, Q5, Q6, Q10). Gut: no, high.
