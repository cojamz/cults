# How Cory says yes and no

From used answer files and the readers' reasons in runs 3 and 4. Each pattern rests on two or more calls; "soft" marks thin evidence.

## Patterns

**1. The current card stays unless he has already said he dislikes it.**
- Kept High Tide "Each rival +1 silver, then gain 1 follower per silver the rival with the most silver holds.": "these are bad except high tide" (C02).
- Kept Courier "Whenever you take two turns in a row, +1 of each other color.": "I actually kind of like the old courier here" (C15). Grifter: "I actually kind of like Grifter" (D04).
- Readers' mistake: all three replaced High Tide, Build Up and Courier (C02, C06, C15); two of three replaced Grifter (D04). Yet they kept Open Door after he'd said "I don't really like recruit cheapest member" (D01).

**2. Nobody decides anything during another player's turn. A choice the owner makes on their own turn is fine.**
- Scavenger "Whenever a rival sends shop members to the bottom of the pile, you may recruit one of them": "when would you … interrupt somebody else's turn? We generally don't do that" (C17). Wannabe: "I don't want things interjecting" (D02).
- Yes to Sweet Nothings "+3 silver. Give rivals up to 3 of your silver, split as you choose: +2 of any colors each.": "yeah 1" (C01).
- Readers' mistake: they struck Sweet Nothings as a choice on someone else's turn (C01).

**3. A payoff that fires on anyone's move is good, as long as nobody stops to decide.**
- White cloak "Whenever any player buys followers, +1 white.": "i like the artifacts linking to any move, not just your own" (D10).
- Patron Saints "Round End: +1 follower per silver member, anyone's.": yes (K10).
- Readers' mistake: they called the any-player version "passive, fed by rivals" and kept Money Changers (D10).

**4. "Too strong" means an ordinary turn is already huge. A far-fetched best case doesn't count (this half soft: D16, D17).**
- Bury for blue equal to its cost: "it would be like +10 blue in round 2" (K22). Swap 1 silver for a rival's whole pile of one color: "i have one silver, i use that to take all of somebody's gold" (D12). "+1 follower per white member you have" on every follower buy: "so strong… What the fuck is this?" (C12).
- Readers' mistake: all three picked that White member (C12). The other way round, they cut Fill Out "Raise each color to the number of colors you hold." over a +30 case (6 colors at 1 each). Cory said "ok" (D16).

**5. He takes the idea and sets the numbers himself.**
- Melting Pot "+2 of any colors per color among your members": yes at "+1 rainbow per, and then also +2 yellow" (D17).
- Party Favors: "I really like party favors… They should give a little silver as well" (D13). Buy Twice: "Let's make that +1 blue" (K26).
- Readers' mistake: they dropped Melting Pot as too strong (D17).

**6. A member that quietly pays its owner is fine. Not every card needs a sting.**
- Loyalists "Your members can't be swapped.": yes (K35). "Game End: +1 follower per color you hold 3 or more of.": yes (K11). "When recruited: turn all your white into followers.": "a really good one" (K04).
- Readers' mistake: run 1 flagged K35, K11, K04, K13 as "nobody feels it".

**7. One clean sentence. No stacked conditions, no member-cost arithmetic.**
- Pauper (cash in white if you have the fewest followers, before contests score): "very confusing" (D11). Minion on a rival's member: "simpler, more elegant" (K17).
- "Recruit one shop member of each color you hold, paying only its colored part": "you're too focused on member costs" (K03).
- Readers' mistake: all three picked Pauper as "a real choice" (D11).

**8. Each color does its own job and never leans on another color.**
- A Magenta member that pays off Purple's skips, and a Silver member that pays off Green's copying: "you can never rely directly on the mechanics of another color being available" (K12, K43).
- Shoplift: "Red and blue's job" (C13). Pathfinder: "too similar to the blue ones" (C10).
- Readers' mistake: one reader made Pathfinder his first choice (C10).

**9. A near copy of a card in the same color is a no, and so is a tiny change. A sibling that pays in a different thing or at a different size is a yes.**
- Mug the Leader: "too similar to what exists already" (C13). The Magenta fuel space with a steal: "magenta already has this but stealing" (K41). Estate Sale to the shop: "That's like a tiny change" (K09).
- Yes to the white version of Farewell: "improves on mine" (K42). Yes to Drifter, a small Promote: "I like Drifter." (K08)
- Readers' mistake: run 1 flagged Drifter and the white Farewell as twins (K08, K42).

**10. A space finishes the moment it's taken. Nothing for the table to remember.**
- "This round, whenever a rival buys followers, +1 white.": "i don't like actions with ongoing effects" (K39).
- Return the Favor: "the exact fucking kind of thing i dont want players to have to keep track of" (K28).
- Readers' mistake: in run 2 they passed K39.

## When he refuses the whole list
He took nothing on 10 of 20 calls in the second held-out set; readers took something on 9 of those 10. Those lists shared:
- Variations on one shape: three copy spaces (D03, "not thinking broadly enough") and five Yellow members that count your colors (D18, "none").
- Small counts and conditional gains with no link to the color's goal (D09: "they just lack an elegance and a connection to the core theme of the color"; K40: "small ball gains").
- Triggers on rivals' moves or long conditions (D02, D11, D20).
- Often he answered with his own idea (D01, D02, D05, D06).

Rule of thumb: if no option would get a "cool" or "really good" from him, answer "None of these."

## When he keeps the current card
- It is simple, in play, and never complained about: High Tide, Build Up, Courier, Grifter, Rising Tide, Top Up and Trade Seeds (C02, C06, C15, D04, D12, C08).
- The replacement is a near copy or a tiny change (K41, K09).
- He fixes wording or numbers instead of swapping the card: Courier reworded (C15). The Purple Cloak went to +2 purple when a skip happens (D07; soft, since he saw bot numbers).
- Keep is off the table once he has said he dislikes the card (Encore, Doormen, Rush Hour, Open Door, Potluck).

## What readers overrate
- Engines judged by best case (Runner, Bootlegger).
- Stings on the leader (Usurper, Racketeer, Grand Gift).
- Far-fetched top cases as a reason to cut.
- Hard rules applied by keyword.

## What readers underrate
- Plain current cards ("little to miss").
- Quiet members that pay their owner.
- Payoffs that fire on anyone's move.
- Good ideas printed with the wrong number.
- "None of these."
