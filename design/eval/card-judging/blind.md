# Card-judging set (blind)

For each item, judge whether the designer would accept this card or space as printed. Ids are stable; order is shuffled.

Game basics at the time of these pitches (2026-10-15 to 2026-11-07): 2 to 4 players place minions one at a time on a wheel of spaces, over three rounds (I, II, III); the spaces change each round and grow stronger later. A turn is: place a minion, do that space, then one recruit (a member from the face-up shop row, or a follower card at a fixed rate). Every space also gives +1 of its color. Members cost a colored part plus an any part; each is worth some followers and counts as one or more members. Each color in the game has a contest scored at round end (mostly: who holds the most of that color). Most followers at the end wins. Colors: Red, Green, Magenta, Blue, Purple, White, Yellow, Silver, plus grey guest members with no color. Context describes the game as it stood when each card was pitched, which may differ from today.
