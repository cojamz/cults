# Reader 3: holdout 2 (blind)

Only method.md, rulebook.md and holdout2-calls-blind.md were used.

## D08 White, round III
- Top case for A: the owner has 10 members, so +10. B: the owner is last in a 4-player game and takes 2 from each of 3 rivals, so +6 for them and -2 for each rival, a 12-follower swing. C: in a 5-color game the owner leads no contest, so +10.
- Compared with Red's free member costing up to 8 and with Poach, none of them is more than twice as good.
- A: fine. It is clean, but it rewards whoever is biggest, which runs against White's aim of paying whoever is behind.
- B: star. It is one clean catch-up idea. Every leader feels it, and the hit lands on the leaders.
- C: weak. It pays you for losing contests, which is odd, and nobody at the table reacts to it.
- First choice: **B**. Full set: {B}.

## D03 Green, round I beside Echo
- A: star. "Copy your last action" is Green's "your own again" next to Echo's "a rival's". It is clean, and in round I it lets your second minion repeat your first one, for example Harvest-style gains twice.
- B: weak. It fails a hard rule, because the space does not finish when it is taken: it waits for the next action.
- C: weak. The table has to remember which action came first this round. It is also a near twin of Echo.
- First choice: **A**. Full set: {A}.

## D04 Magenta member (Grifter's slot)
- Grifter's best turn: you recruit it with 8 members and steal 4 resources, once. It uses "per 2 members" counting and is close to Accomplice ("steal 3").
- Racketeer's top case: in round III a rival wins 3 contests, so you steal 6. It lands every round end on whoever is winning.
- A, Hijacker: weak. You decide during a rival's turn, which fails the hard rule (Fix 3).
- B, Grifter (current card): fine. It is a working card, but it uses member-count math and is a near twin of Accomplice.
- C, Racketeer: star. It is clean theft that stings contest winners every round. That is a distinct moment, which beats keeping Grifter.
- D, Pickpocket: weak. Buying 10 followers at once is rare, so the card rarely does anything.
- First choice: **C**. Full set: {C}.

## D10 White cloak
- A: weak. It is a quiet payout at round end with no reaction from anyone. It also pulls against Cash In.
- B, Money Changers (current card): fine. "+2 white" is double the other cloaks' +1, and covering both "resources or members" is two triggers in one.
- C: fine. It reacts to every player, but it pays you for rivals' actions.
- D: star. It has one trigger at the same scale as the other cloaks: buying followers, which White does most. It tightens Money Changers without adding a bonus.
- First choice: **D**. Full set: {D}. This is a close call against keeping B.

## D18 Yellow members (four slots)
- A, Pen Pal: weak. It pays when rivals do something, and it rewards you for lacking colors, which runs against Yellow going broad.
- B, Busker: fine. It is clean and rewards spreading your placements across colors.
- C, Tourist: fine. It is clean and pays for holding every color: about +2 each round start, so +4 to 6 over a game.
- D, Pilgrim (reworked): weak. Top case with 6 colors among your members: +6 when recruited, then +6 and +6 at the two round starts, so 18. That is more than twice Big Tent's 12, and it has two triggers.
- E, Collector: fine. It pays at most +6 in total. Hitting "4 or 6 colors" is a clean breadth milestone.
- First choice: **C**. Full set: {B, C, E}. That fills three of the four slots; A and D are not worth taking.

- D: weak. It is a bonus stuck on, and a twin of Gossip.

## D20 Grey mover

## D13 Silver, round II (both spaces)
