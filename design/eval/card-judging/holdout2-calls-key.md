# Card-judging calibration: second set of held-out calls, answer key

Twenty more of Cory's past calls, framed as the picks he made: the options he saw for one slot at one moment, and which he took. None of these cards or slot-calls appear in answer-key.md (K01-K44), holdout-key.md (H01-H18) or holdout-calls-key.md (C01-C17); where a call shares a slot or a message with one of those, its note says so. All are dated before 2026-11-07. Blind version: holdout2-calls-blind.md (same ids, calls in a different order, option letters shuffled; the mapping is on each call's "blind letters" line; "None of these" is always the last letter in both files).

Sources: design/pitch-ledger.md (line numbers as of 2026-11-07), design/decisions.md, .claude/skills/brainstorm/references/verdicts.md, design/eval/quality-loop/batches.html and cory-verdicts-2026-11-04.md (D02, D04, D11), /mnt/project-files/cloaks/magenta-purple-options.md (D07), /mnt/project-files/balance-1.0/grey-movers.md (D20), design/eval/text-review/sheet-2026-10-23.md and sheet-2026-11-01.md (the texts of the day used in contexts and keep options).

How picks are scored:
- **Picked**: the option(s) he said yes to for this slot (went in, or his explicit yes). Strict scoring counts only these. When he took nothing shown, the pick is "None of these".
- **Liked, back pocket**: he said he liked it, said "maybe", or used its shape in another slot, but it did not take this slot. Lenient scoring counts these as picks too.
- A "keep the current card" option is picked only when he kept it.

Totals: 20 calls, 3 to 7 options each counting "None of these" (94 options). Strict picks: 20 (one per call): "None of these" on 10 calls (D01, D02, D03, D05, D06, D09, D11, D18, D19, D20), the current card kept on 2 (D04, D12; D13's keep is lenient only), a new option on the other eight. Lenient adds 7 back-pocket options (D02 1, D04 1, D06 1, D09 1, D11 2, D13 1).

By color: Red 1 (D01), Green 2 (D02, D03), Magenta 1 (D04), Blue 1 (D05), Purple 2 (D06, D07), White 4 (D08-D11), Silver 4 (D12-D15), Yellow 4 (D16-D19), grey 1 (D20). By kind: 11 spaces, 7 members, 2 cloaks.

Uncertain items, flagged in their notes: D11 (no word on Old Money itself, though it stayed live), D02 (Bootlegger's "Maybe" counted as back pocket), D13 (Sweet Talk "okay, consider it", later kept), D06 (option 3 liked but moved to another slot), D07 (leans on a number change), and reconstructed wording in D01, D05, D06, D09, D13, D16.
