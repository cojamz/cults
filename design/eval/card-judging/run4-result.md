# Calibration run 4: version 2 plus four fixes, on 20 new held-out calls (Nov 7 10:25pm Eastern)

Plan fixed before reading: run4-plan.md. Scores: run4-score.txt (script run4-score.py).

**Panel matched Cory's pick on 5 of 20 calls (95% range about 11% to 47%).**
- Chance: about 4.6 of 20. Always answering "None of these": 10 of 20.
- On the 10 calls where Cory refused every option: the panel refused on 1. On the 10 where he took a card: 4.
- Back-pocket likes counted: 7 of 20. Each reader alone: 4, 6, 4. All three readers agreed on 10 of 20.
- Bar (about 4 in 5) not met; the panel is not better than chance on this set.

## What it shows
1. Readers accept far more than Cory does. He turned down the whole list on 10 of these 20 calls; the readers found something to take on 9 of those 10, even with "None of these" offered and the method telling them to use it. That is the largest and most consistent gap across runs 3 and 4.
2. Fix 1 did not hold. Readers still replaced the current card on calls where Cory kept it (D04, D07), and wrote a reason each time.
3. Readers do agree with each other (10 of 20 unanimous), so this is a shared lean, not noise. More readers would not fix it.
4. Some key items are soft (D07 was mostly a number change; D11 and D13 rest on what he didn't say). Even scoring every soft item in the method's favor, it stays near chance.

## Across all four runs
Run 1 (nine questions, 44 calls the questions were then tuned on): 26 of 44. Run 2 (tuned, same 44): 30 of 44. Run 3 (version 2, 17 unseen): 8 of 17, chance about 5.4. Run 4 (version 2 plus fixes, 20 unseen): 5 of 20, chance about 4.6. On calls it hasn't seen, no version predicts Cory's picks reliably.

## Recommendation
Stop tuning it to predict him: two unseen sets say it can't, and more tuning on used sets would only fit them. Use it for what it does well (each color's story, every card with its best turn and top case played out, cards side by side) and let Cory mark star, fine or weak on each color's page. His marks are the ranking, and they become the answer key for later.
