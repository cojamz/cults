## What the misses say about the questions
1. Q2 (someone feels it) flagged 7 of the 9 false flags. Cory says yes to quiet cards that pay their owner: deck members, fuel spaces, converters. Fix: the owner's own payoff moment counts; Q2 is low only when nobody, owner included, would notice it fired.
2. Q6 (its color's own) punished deliberate siblings: a small version of a bigger card (Drifter beside Promote), or a pair that pays in different things (white vs followers). Fix: a sibling with a different size or payoff is fine; low only when it repeats a card in its color at the same scale and payoff.
3. Q4 (worth it) only looked for weak. Cory's no on K22 was "way too strong". Fix: Q4 is two-sided; too strong for its round scores as low as too weak.
4. Nothing asked whether the change beats the card it replaces or whether the color needed it. Four of the false passes are replacements Cory refused because he liked the old card or the color already had that job (K27, K29, K34, K41), and K09 was "a tiny change". Fix: new Q10, "Is it better than what it replaces, and does the color need it?"
5. Q1 (a table can run it) passed a new kind of placement (K17) and a cost-math round III (K03). Fix: a new rule exception, or a card about cost arithmetic, caps Q1 at 2 unless one sentence covers it with nothing to remember. ("simpler, more elegant"; "too focused on member costs")
6. Two misses (K27, K32) have no reason on record. Those may be taste the questions can't see, and stay as known misses.

Integrity note: the fixed questions are re-scored on these same 44 (in-sample, which flatters them) and on 18 new held-out calls the fixes were never shown. Only the held-out number counts for the bar.
