# Card-judging calibration: held-out calls, answer key

The 18 held-out items (H01-H18 in holdout-key.md) reframed as the calls Cory actually made: the options he saw for one slot at one moment, and which he took. 17 calls; H13 and H14 (Inside Job, Mug the Leader) were candidates for the same Stash slot on the same page, so they are one call (C13). Every other H id is its own call. Blind version: holdout-calls-blind.md (same call ids, calls in a different order, option letters shuffled; the mapping is on each call's "blind letters" line).

Sources: design/pitch-ledger.md (line numbers as of 2026-11-07), design/decisions.md, design/eval/quality-loop/batches.html (the page Cory ruled on at 12:35am Nov 4, for C05, C10, C13, C15, C17) and cory-verdicts-2026-11-04.md, design/notes/potluck-options-2026-11-06.md (C01), design/notes/silver-members-pitch-2026-11-05.md (C03), /mnt/project-files/quality/pitches/*.md (prices).

How picks are scored:
- **Picked**: the option(s) he said yes to for this slot (went in, or his explicit yes). Strict scoring counts only these.
- **Liked, back pocket**: he said he liked it or kept it for later, but it did not take this slot. Lenient scoring counts these as picks too.
- A "keep the current card" option is picked only when he kept it. "Leave the slot to other ideas" is the alternative when he was shown a single new card.

Totals: 17 calls, 2 to 7 options each (68 options). Strict picks: 19 (none on C04; one on thirteen calls; two on C03, C07 and C08, where C08's two are the current cards kept by default). Lenient adds 7 back-pocket options (C05 2, C10 1, C13 1, C15 1, C17 2).
