# Card-judging held-out set (blind)

For each item, judge whether the designer would accept this card or space as printed. Ids are stable; order is shuffled.

Game basics at the time of these pitches (2026-10-15 to 2026-11-07): 2 to 4 players place minions one at a time on a wheel of spaces, over three rounds (I, II, III); the spaces change each round and grow stronger later. A turn is: place a minion, do that space, then one recruit (a member from the face-up shop row, or a follower card at a fixed rate). Every space also gives +1 of its color. Members cost a colored part plus an any part; each is worth some followers and counts as one or more members. Each color in the game has a contest scored at round end (mostly: who holds the most of that color). Most followers at the end wins. Colors: Red, Green, Magenta, Blue, Purple, White, Yellow, Silver, plus grey guest members with no color. Context describes the game as it stood when each card was pitched, which may differ from today.

## H11
- color: Yellow
- kind: space, round III
- printed text: "+4 yellow, then rearrange your resources into any mix of colors."
- context: Yellow round III slot. Yellow's theme (designer): rewards going broad (a combination of colors, being flexible) and translating currencies. Guest Launderers "Worth 3 followers. On Recruit: rearrange your resources into any mix of colors." Each color's contest: most of its color held at round end. Other round III spaces: Red takes a shop member free; Magenta's Poach takes any rival's member; White's Cash Out turns all white into followers; Silver's High Tide.

## H04
- color: Green
- kind: space, round II
- printed text: "+1 green. One of your used One Time Use members can be used again."
- context: Replaces Encore "Copy an action with a rival minion. +1 green." (designer: Green has one copy too many; the replacement should not be a copy). Green's theme: copying (Echo "Copy the previous action."; Reprise "Copy an action with your minion."; Cover "Copy an action with no minion on it."). One Time Use members: a once-per-game power printed on a member (e.g. Tribute Band "One Time Use: choose a rival. Do every action they have a minion on this round.").

## H16
- color: Purple
- kind: member [4 purple + 2 any, worth 1, counts as 1]
- printed text: "Whenever you take two turns in a row, recruit once more on the second."
- context: Replaces Courier [4 purple + 2 any, worth 1] "Whenever you take two turns in a row, +1 of each other color." Purple's spaces that give two turns in a row: Right Away "Take another turn right after this one." (round I); Crunch Time "Your next two turns come right after this one." (round II). Normally one recruit a turn.

## H01
- color: Silver
- kind: space, round II
- printed text: "+3 silver. Give rivals up to 3 of your silver, split as you choose: +2 of any colors each."
- context: Replaces Potluck "+2 silver. Each rival gives you 1 resource of their choice, then gains 2 silver." on Silver round II, beside Sweet Talk "+3 silver. ## H17

## H12
- color: White
- kind: member
- printed text: "Whenever you recruit followers, +1 follower per white member you have."
- context: Filling White's member deck of 12.
