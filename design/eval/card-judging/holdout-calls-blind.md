# Card-judging held-out calls (blind)

Each item is one slot in the game and the options the designer was shown for it at one moment. For each, say which options the designer would pick: zero, one or several. Ids are stable; order is shuffled, and option letters are in no particular order. A "keep the current card" option means leaving the slot as it is; "leave the slot to other ideas" means taking none of what is shown.

Game basics at the time of these pitches (2026-10-15 to 2026-11-07): 2 to 4 players place minions one at a time on a wheel of spaces, over three rounds (I, II, III); the spaces change each round and grow stronger later. A turn is: place a minion, do that space, then one recruit (a member from the face-up shop row, or a follower card at a fixed rate). Every space also gives +1 of its color. Members cost a colored part plus an any part; each is worth some followers and counts as one or more members. Each color in the game has a contest scored at round end (mostly: who holds the most of that color). Most followers at the end wins. Colors: Red, Green, Magenta, Blue, Purple, White, Yellow, Silver, plus grey guest members with no color. Context describes the game as it stood when each slot was open, which may differ from today.
