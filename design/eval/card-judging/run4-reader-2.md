# Reader 2: holdout set 2

Method: hard rules first, then moment (top case played with numbers), idea, ties, side by side; the current card is the default; "None of these" allowed.

## D01 Red R1 (Even Swap's and Open Door's spaces)
Top case of current: Open Door in round I gives a free member costing up to 4 plus 1 red, about 2 to 3 resources of value. Even Swap trades a 1-cost member for a 4-cost one.
- A weak: gives every rival a free member. A gift stuck onto Red's own gain, so it is no better.
- B weak: it is a translator (Yellow's job) and not Red's.
- C weak: the next player decides on your turn, which breaks the hard rule.
- D star (keep): one clean free recruit, and it is Red's idea.
- E weak: round I players have no members, so the first turn is dead, and it is a near twin of Promote.
- F fine (keep): a clean swap, and it works on the shop and rivals when you have no members.
First: D. Set: D, F.

## D02 Green member (Chronicler's slot)
- A weak: you decide on a rival's turn, which breaks the hard rule.
- B weak: it fires only when a rival copies you, so it is rare and passive.
- C star: once per round, copy Accomplice to steal 3, or Con Men to steal 5. Over rounds I to III that is 15 resources, a real engine and pure Green copying.
- D fine: 5 minions on Green in round III gives +5. Small, and tied to Green.
- E weak: no copying, and the wrong color's theme.
- F weak: generic follower count; nobody would miss it.
First: C. Set: C.

## D03 Green R1 beside Echo
- A weak: on the first turn there is nothing to copy, and it is a near twin of Reprise.
- B weak: the gain comes after the space is taken, which breaks the hard rule.
- C star: clean, a table can track it, and it is distinct from Echo. The first player's space is often the best one.
First: C. Set: C.

## D04 Magenta member (Grifter's slot)
Grifter top case: recruit with 8 members to steal 4, once. That is cost math and a modest moment.
- A weak: you decide on a rival's turn, which breaks the hard rule.
- B fine (keep): it works but is flat, and its "per 2 members" is cost math.
- C star: round end against 3 rivals who win about 3 contests is 6 stolen. That stings the leader and is clearly bigger than Grifter.
- D weak: someone buying 10 followers is rare, so it almost never fires.
First: C. Set: C.

## D05 Blue member (Doormen's slot)
- A fine: two recruits at once is a strong moment, but it twins the Recruit Twice space.
- B weak: it is a near twin of Red's Promote.
- C fine (keep): small, and it ties to Red swaps.
- D star: a once-per-game shop wipe denies the member a rival was saving for. Clean Blue burying.
First: D. Set: D, A.

## D06 Purple R2 (Rush Hour's space)
Rush Hour top case: late round II with 16 minions placed gives +8 purple. Late placement matters a little.
- A fine (keep): cost-math counting, and it ties only loosely to turn order.
- B weak: a bigger twin of Sleep In.
- C weak: it pays out after the space is taken and needs remembering.
- D star: skipping the next player is a direct turn-order sting, one clean idea. Hold Up later grew from this shape.
First: D. Set: D.

## D07 Purple cloak
