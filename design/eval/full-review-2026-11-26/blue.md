## Blue
Contest: Most members — counts your members when the round ends. Blue seats were strong in the bot games (won 17 more in every 100 with the strongest bots, 12 more with normal bots) and one player won all three rounds of this contest in 14 of every 100 games; in the AI-player games a cheap-member player took it every round, and a rival with 5 members took it back in round III.

**Works and is interesting**
- (No Blue card cleared the bar: no test showed one of them clearly positive in both bot levels or felt at the table.)

**Maybe**
- **Try Out** (round I space) "Discard a shop member. Use its effect now, unless it is once per game. +1 blue." — Normal bots saw nothing (1 fewer win in 100, within chance), strong bots did a bit worse (5 fewer); players liked it on Monks (+4 in round I), so it is felt only with the right shop.
- **Buy Secondhand** (round I space) "Recruit a member from the discard pile now, paying 2 less. +3 blue." — Even in both bot levels (1 fewer, 1 more); players called it dead on an empty or junk discard pile in one game and rich in another.
- **Spread the Word** (round II space) "+1 follower per 2 members you have. +1 blue." — Normal bots 3 fewer wins in 100, strong bots even; no judge or player mentioned it.
- **Rag-and-Bone Man** (1 blue + 2 any) "Whenever you discard a member, +1 blue." — too few games in both bot levels; no test showed anything either way.
- **Gravedigger** (6 blue + 4 any) "Whenever shop members go to the discard pile, +1 follower." — too few games in both bot levels; no test showed anything either way.
- **Blue Bloods** (4 blue + 4 any) "Game End: +1 follower per blue member you have." — Normal bots 5 fewer wins in 100, strong bots even (wide range); no other test spoke to it.
- **Old-Clothes Dealers** (3 blue + 4 any) "On Use, once per round: swap one of your members for a discard-pile member that costs the same." — Normal bots even (2 fewer, within chance), bought in only 1 game in 4; too few strong-bot games.
- **Scavengers** (3 blue + 4 any) "Whenever you recruit from the discard pile, +2 followers." — Normal bots even (1 more, within chance), bought in 1 game in 3; too few strong-bot games.
- **Homecomers** (4 blue + 4 any) "On Recruit: +3 blue, then recruit from the discard pile." — Normal bots 2 fewer, strong bots 3 more, both within chance; no other test spoke to it.
- **Culler** (3 blue + 4 any) "On Recruit and Round Start: discard 2 shop members. +1 of each one's color." — Normal bots even, strong bots 6 fewer (wide range); bought often by both, so the bots like it but it does not win games.
- **Market Sweeper** (3 blue + 3 any) "On Use, once per game: discard the whole shop and refill." — too few bot games, but the combo hunt had a player using it hard gain 3.8 followers over the best rival; small, not broken.
- **Firstborn** (5 blue + 5 any, counts as 3) "Round Start: recruit once, before anyone places." — Normal bots 4 fewer wins in 100, strong bots 2 more; judges say it plays as a 3-member body and its recruit can fire only once (round III start), though the body drew a steal worth about 15 followers.
- **Tagalongs** (3 blue + 3 any) "On Recruit: recruit again this turn." — too few games in both bot levels; no test showed anything either way.
- **Dusk Buyer** (3 blue + 3 any) "Round End: +3 blue, then you may recruit from the discard pile." — Normal bots won 4 more in 100 (about the edge of chance), strong bots even; two players found nothing worth buying in the discard pile, but two others gave it away to break a rival's Fewest members lead.

**Cut, replace or rework**
- **Clear Out** (round I space) "Discard 3 shop members and refill. +1 of each discarded member's color. +1 blue." — Slightly under both bot levels (1 fewer in 100 each); players hit long option prompts and one read "3 to go" as only two discards. Fix: rework the effect (a simpler discard that does not need a color choice).
- **Bring Back** (round II space) "Recruit a member from the discard pile now. +3 blue." — Under both bot levels (4 fewer wins in 100 with normal bots, 3 fewer with strong); judges saw discard recruits add little. Fix: rework the effect.
- **Salvage** (round III space) "Recruit two members from the discard pile now. +7 blue." — Flagged WEAK with normal bots (7 fewer in 100), even with strong bots; players called it dead on an empty or junk discard pile. Fix: rework the effect.
- **Bargainer** (2 blue + 3 any) "Game End: +1 follower per member you have costing 5 or less." — Bought in 98% of games it showed but strong bots won 7 fewer in 100 and normal bots 0; judges saw only +3 at game end, never changing a result. Fix: rework the effect.
- **Gleaner** (3 blue + 4 any) "You may recruit from the discard pile as you would from the shop." — Flagged WEAK with normal bots (11 fewer in 100); judges called it clear but quiet, never a moment, no rival noticed. Fix: cut.
- **Market-Day Shopper** (4 blue + 4 any) "Whenever you recruit your second member in a turn, +3 followers." — Normal bots 7 fewer wins in 100 and bought in only 15% of games it showed; the trigger almost never fires. Fix: rework the effect.
- **Penny-Pincher** (2 blue + 3 any) "Whenever you buy followers, +1 blue." — Under the bar in the paper scoring; too few bot games and no table test spoke to it. Fix: rework the effect.
