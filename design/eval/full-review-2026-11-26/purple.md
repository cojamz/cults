## Purple
Contest: Went last — counts whoever placed the round's last minion. One player won it in all three rounds in 22 in every 100 games (the most of any color) and that player won 72 in every 100 of those games. At the table, players called the +10 round III prize a coin flip on a last-second token grab ("ping-pong decided a +10 in a way I couldn't affect"), but also called the token fights and extra-turn chains exciting.
**Works and is interesting**
- **Keep Going** (round I space) "+2 purple for this turn and each turn of yours right before it. Take the Goes Last token: your other minions are placed after everyone else's." — The owner won 7 more games in every 100 than players with as many members with normal bots and 9 more with the strongest bots; normal bots flag it strong.
- **Hurriers** (3 purple + 4 any) "Whenever you take a consecutive turn, +2 followers." — Normal-bot owners won 10 more games in every 100 than players with as many members; the strongest bots rarely had it, and the extra-turn chains it rewards were called exciting at the table.
- **Juggler** (3 purple + 4 any) "On Use, once per game: +3 followers per turn in a row you've taken just before this one." — Won 7 more games in every 100 with normal bots; in the AI-player game it paid 9 after a chain and all four players called it clearly worth it.
**Maybe**
- **Lie Abed** (round I space) "+5 purple. Take the Goes Last token: your other minions are placed after everyone else's." — Won 2 more games in every 100 with normal bots (chance) and 7 more with the strongest bots (wide range); one AI player won two rounds of Went last with it but lost the game.
- **Bide Your Time** (round II space) "+1 purple. +1 follower and +1 purple per minion you have left to place. Take the Goes Last token: your other minions go after everyone else's." — Small plus in both bot levels (4 and 5 more wins in every 100), both within chance; no test showed anything more.
- **Flurry** (round II space) "+2 purple. Your next two turns come right after this one." — 2 more wins in every 100 with normal bots, 9 more with the strongest bots but a wide range; AI players noted it does nothing on your last minion and the board doesn't warn you.
- **Errand Boy** (5 purple + 4 any) "On Use, once per game: +1 minion, placed right after this turn." — Bots rarely bought it; no test showed anything either way.
- **Early Riser** (5 purple + 4 any) "Round Start: if you have the fewest followers (ties count), +1 minion, placed before anyone else." — Bots rarely bought it; no test showed anything either way.
- **Courier** (4 purple + 4 any) "Whenever you take a consecutive turn, +1 of each other color." — Normal bots won 5 more games in every 100 but the range is wider than the gain; the strongest bots rarely had it.
- **Latecomers** (2 purple + 4 any) "On Recruit: take the Goes Last token: your other minions are placed after everyone else's." — Tests disagree: normal bots won 2 fewer games in every 100, the strongest bots won 16 more (wide range, bought in 38% of games it showed).
- **Sleepyhead** (1 purple + 2 any) "Whenever you take the Goes Last token, +2 purple." — Bots rarely bought it; no test showed anything either way.
- **Usher** (3 purple + 4 any) "Round Start: choose your place in this round's turn order." — Owners won no more games than players with as many members (normal bots); the strongest bots rarely had it.
- **Laggard** (3 purple + 4 any) "On Use, once per round: take the Goes Last token." — Won 1 more game in every 100 with normal bots (chance); the strongest bots rarely had it.
- **Shirker** (3 purple + 3 any) "When you place your last minion of the round, +1 purple per minion rivals still have to place." — Fired every game for 3 to 6 purple and made one Keep Going worth 10, but never changed who was ahead; rivals often swapped it away. Normal bots won 2 more games in every 100 (chance).
- **Turnpike Keeper** (3 purple + 3 any) "Whenever a rival takes a Purple space, +1 purple." — Small plus in both bot levels (2 and 3 more wins in every 100), both within chance; no test showed anything more.
**Cut, replace or rework**
- **Burn the Candle** (round III space) "+4 purple. +1 extra minion to place this round. Take the Goes Last token: your other minions are placed after everyone else's." — Owners won 10 more games in every 100 with normal bots and 20 more with the strongest bots, both flagged strong; it feeds the Went last prize that decides games and chains into Juggler. Fix: rework the effect (drop the Goes Last token from this space or the +4 purple).
- **Make Haste** (round I space) "+3 purple. Your next turn comes right after this one." — Owners won 1 fewer game in every 100 with normal bots and 6 fewer with the strongest bots; it does nothing on a last minion. Fix: rework the effect.
- **Timekeeper** (2 purple + 4 any) "Game End: +1 follower per purple member you have." — Bots bought it in 80 to 89 of every 100 games it showed and its owners still won 2 fewer (normal) and 5 fewer (strongest) games in every 100; a trap. Fix: +1 follower printed on the card.
- **Curfew Ringers** (2 purple + 4 any) "Round End: if you placed the round's last minion, +1 follower per purple member you have." — Owners won 1 fewer game in every 100 with normal bots and 6 fewer with the strongest bots. Fix: rework the effect.
- **Night Porter** (3 purple + 4 any) "Each minion you place once all rivals are out of minions: +2 followers." — Fired in only 1 of 3 games for about 4 followers total, against 10 for buying followers, and the preview showed +2 on every option so it never changed a pick. Fix: rework the effect.
- **Gate-Shutter** (3 purple + 4 any) "When you place the last minion of the round: +4 purple." — Normal-bot owners won 8 fewer games in every 100 than players with as many members, a clear miss; the strongest bots rarely had it. Fix: cut.
