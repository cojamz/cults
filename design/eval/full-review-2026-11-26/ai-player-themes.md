# Player-agent games, Nov 26: theme report

16 players (4 per game, 4 games). Every player said they would play again. "Raised by" counts players out of 16 and games out of 4. Card text is from each game's cards.md.

## 1. The four games

**Game 1 (red, purple, silver, yellow).** Cal 96, Dee 92, Ben 84, Ava 75. Cal won holding 9 members.
- Cal: chained free recruits (Borrow into Governess, Call to Arms, Journeyman, Benefactor) to reach 9 members, won both round III +10 member contests, and finished with a 5-follower buy.
- Dee: played purple only (Lie Abed, Make Haste, Keep Going, Flurry), held 0 members and 0 silver, bought follower packs, and won Went last and Fewest silver in rounds I and II. Lost by 4 when Ava took Went last in round III and Dee had no member contest to fall back on.
- Ben: chased Most members of one color with Jester and Lord of the Manor. Lost Lord of the Manor to a swap and was overtaken by Cal's pile.
- Ava: drifted on two members (Bannermen, Beggar) and converted scraps into small packs.

**Game 2 (market/white, thieves/magenta, trading/blue, echo/green).** Dee 81, Ava 65, Ben 64, Cal 58. Dee won holding 1 member.
- Dee: copied spaces with the Green Cloak to win Most minions on green, held a big resource pile for Most resources, and gave Dusk Buyer to Ava to split her Fewest members lead. About 35 followers came from contests.
- Ava: stayed at 0 or 1 members and bought packs with the White Cloak refund (57 followers bought), but got only 13 from contests.
- Ben: Magenta Cloak, Pilfer and Cutthroats chasing Most resources. Dee's pile passed his in round III.
- Cal: Blue Most members with Lord of the Manor and Mutineers. Ben's 5 members took Blue from him in round III.

**Game 3 (red, thieves/magenta, yellow, echo/green).** Ava 80, Dee 64, Ben 56, Cal 52. Ava won holding 1 member (Bannermen).
- Ava: Magenta Cloak with Stash, Pilfer and Plunder held Most resources every round (+4, +6, +10). Added Bannermen's +3 a round and 28 bought followers.
- Dee: Jester, Gardeners and Hold a Fair for the green and member-colors contests. Lost Jester and Lord of the Manor to steals and swaps in round III and ended with 0 resources.
- Ben: 10 members and swaps, but lost Lord of the Manor in round II and never challenged Ava's pile.
- Cal: wasted round I on Borrow members that left at round end, then built 7 red members for the round III +10, which came too late.

**Game 4 (purple, silver, market/white, trading/blue).** Dee 113, Ava 85, Cal 85, Ben 82. Dee won holding 3 members (Paupers, Juggler, Mendicant).
- Dee: won Went last all three rounds (+4, +6, +10), shared Fewest silver every round, bought 50 followers, and added Juggler's +9 after a chain of consecutive turns.
- Cal: cheap member engine (Lord of the Manor, Monks, Matchmaker) won Most members each round but bought only 4 followers.
- Ava and Ben: 0 members, shared Fewest members and bought packs (Ava with the White Cloak refund). Neither contested Went last or Most members.

## 2. Themes (strongest first)

### Complaints and problems

**1. Lord of the Manor is far too cheap. Raised by 15 of 16 players, all 4 games.**
- Card: Lord of the Manor, 4 any, worth 3 followers, counts as 3 members, "You go first every round."
- At the table: one recruit gives 3 followers, three toward every member-count contest, and first turn. A 1-follower pack buy gives 1. Whoever takes it first leads the member contests, and rivals then spend a placement (Mutineers, Promote, Sleight of Hand) just to take it.
- Quotes: "was the best deal in the game" (g01 Cal). "absurdly cheap for what it does" (g02 Ben). "the pass I regret most" (g03 Ava).
- Fix: raise its price, or make it count as fewer members. It is also the card most swaps target (theme 3).

**2. Follower packs make members feel optional. Raised by 13 players, all 4 games.**
- At the table: a 0-member or 1-member cult buying 5- and 10-follower packs won or nearly won in 3 games (g01 Dee 92 against 96, g03 Ava 80, g04 Ava 85).
- Quotes: "I wasn't sure what a member can do that a pack can't" (g01 Dee). "a 1-card cult wins by hoarding, so the member game feels optional" (g03 Ben).
- Counter-view: in g04 Ben and Ava said the no-member line felt like a trap next to Monks, Paupers and Juggler (Monks paid Cal 12, Paupers paid Dee 9).
- Fix: either make packs cost more per follower (or limit them per round), or give members a stronger reason to exist. Several ideas aim at the second, for example a grey member counting as 2 and members that pay for contests you share.

**3. Members and followers get swapped or stolen with no defence. 7 players complained, 10 liked it; 4 games.**
- Cards: Mutineers ("take a member from a rival with more members than you", once per game), Promote, Sleight of Hand, Poach, Plunder ("Steal 3 followers and 3 resources"), Hire Away, Pawnbrokers.
- At the table: a key member (Lord of the Manor, Jester) is gone in one placement and the plan with it. g03 Lord of the Manor changed hands three times in round II.
- Complaints from: g01 Ben; g02 Ava, Dee; g03 Ben, Cal, Dee; g04 Ava.
- Likes: Promote and Demote flipping a contest (g01 Ava, Ben, Cal), Mutineers on the leader (g02 Cal), Sleight of Hand on Monks (g02 Ben), Poach on the leader (g03 Ben, Dee).
- Quote: "my choices were undone by someone else's single placement" (g03 Dee).
- Players asked for a defence 6 times in 3 games. Ideas: Bodyguards (g01 Ben), Bodyguard (g02 Ava), Safe House (g03 Ben), Safe Hands and Vault Keeper (g03 Cal), Safe Harbor (g03 Dee), Counterspell (g02 Dee). Warden ("Your other members can't be swapped") already exists; g01 Ben said it never appeared for him. The fix is a way to protect one member, not weaker steals.

**4. Round III's +10 contests decide the game. Raised by 9 players, games 1, 2 and 4.**
- At the table: contests pay 4, 6, then 10. The leader of two or three round III contests wins, and the players behind (g02 Ava, Cal; g04 Ben, 25 behind) have placements with nothing to affect.
- Quotes: "made most other decisions feel tiny" (g02 Cal). "decided before the round starts" (g04 Cal).
- Fix: lower the round III prize, or give players behind a way to contest late.

**5. Went last and the Goes Last token. All 8 purple-game players, g1 and g4.**
- Cards: Lie Abed, Keep Going, Burn the Candle, Bide Your Time, Make Haste, Flurry, Purple Cloak, Latecomers.
- At the table: whoever places the round's last minion wins +4, +6, +10. Dee won all three rounds in g04 and rounds I and II in g01. One placement can take the token back, so rivals can only watch.
- Quotes: "a +10 prize that swings on a last-second token grab feels like a coin flip" (g04 Dee). "ping-pong decided a +10 in a way I couldn't affect" (g01 Cal).
- Related: Flurry and Make Haste do nothing on a last minion and the board doesn't warn you (g01 Dee, Ava).
- Likes: token fights and extra-turn chains were exciting (g01 Ava, Dee; g04 Dee, Ben).
- Ideas: a space that locks the token for a round (g04 Dee, Hold the Line), a purple space that makes the token lose credit (g04 Ava), a member letting you take consecutive turns when a rival takes Purple (g04 Cal).
- Fix: a counter to the token, or make last placement count for less.

**6. Fewest members and Fewest silver pay for owning nothing. 8 players, g1, g2 and g4 (some on both).**
- Fewest members (white): 0 or 1 member collects +4, +6, +10 or a shared half (g02 Ben, Cal, Dee; g04 Ben, Cal, Dee). Quote: "a free +6 and +10 for passivity" (g02 Dee).
- Fewest silver: in round III four players tied at 0 and each got +5 (g01, all 4). In g04 three tied at 0. Ties pay half to every tied player, so a 4-way tie hands out 20 followers. Quote: "a +10 contest that everyone zeroes out is pointless" (g01 Ava).
- Silver spaces and the silver contest pull against each other: Raise a Glass and Throw Coins give rivals silver, while Hire Away and Flaunt (want silver) sat unused (g04 Dee).
- Fix: make the contest cost something (for example a member of the color must be held), or make gifted silver count.

**7. Most resources (Magenta) forces a hoard and dead recruits. 5 players, g2 and g3.**
- Cards: Stash, Pilfer, Plunder, Magenta Cloak ("Whenever you steal, +2 magenta").
- At the table: the leader skips buying or buys one lone follower to protect the lead, then converts leftovers on the last minion. Ava won g03 with +4, +6, +10 from this contest every round.
- Quotes: "every real purchase risked the lead, so I passed" (g03 Ava). "Holding 13 to 16 resources and passing recruit after recruit felt dead" (g02 Ben).
- Fix: let the leader spend without losing the lead, or give a catch-up. Ideas: Vault (g03 Ava), Tithe Collector and Tithe Spoiler (g02 and g03).

**8. Previews, prompts and wording hard to read. 13 players, all 4 games.**
- Long option lists: Replay All, Clear Out, Throw Coins, Paint Over (eight "turn nothing" prompts), about 270 swap options (g03 Cal, Ben, Ava; g02 Cal).
- Silver gifts: "I can't tell if it helps them or me" (Raise a Glass; g01 Cal, g04 Ava, Ben).
- Preview numbers: Redeem showing -1 white beside +2 followers (g02 Ben); Demand Tribute showing rivals losing yellow (g03 Cal); Yellow contest numbers (g03 Ben).
- Borrow "returns to the shop at round end, before scoring" misread as a real recruit (g01 Ben, g03 Cal, who spent all of round I on it).
- Fix: shorter prompts, grouped choices, a line on what a copied action will actually fire.

**9. Dead cards and spaces that depend on others' discards.**
- Dusk Buyer ("Round End: +3 blue, then you may recruit from the discard pile") had nothing worth buying (g02 Ava, Dee). Salvage and Buy Secondhand were dead on an empty or junk discard pile (g02 Cal), yet rich in g04 (Dee, Ben liked them).
- Fix: depend less on what rivals happened to discard.

### Likes

Chain turns and combos. 11 players, all 4 games.** Borrow into Governess (g01 Cal), Jester plus Hold a Fair (g01 Ben, g03 Dee), Follow Suit and Imitate with the Green Cloak (g02 Dee, Ben), Replay All on your own Plunder (g02 Cal), Count Heads into Stash (g03 Ava), Burn the Candle into Juggler (g04 Dee), Try Out on Monks or Pawnbrokers (g02 Ava, g04 Cal).

**12. Cloaks that pay at once.** White Cloak refund on every pack (g02 Ava, g04 Ava: "free money"), Magenta Cloak with steals (g02 Ben, g03 Ava), Green Cloak with copies (g02 Dee). Both g02 Ava and g04 Ava wondered if the White Cloak is too strong.

## 4. Possible rule or text bugs (not confirmed)

- **Mutineers after a swap (g03, round II, Dee):** Dee couldn't tell if the "once per game" mark moves with the card. The transcript shows Cal's Mutineers "already used" after the trade, so the game keeps the mark, but nothing on screen says so.
- **Demand Tribute preview (g03, round III, Cal):** the preview showed rivals losing yellow though Cal said he had no yellow members. Cal held Jester (counts as every color), so it is probably correct. The preview doesn't say why.
- **Redeem preview (g02, round III, Ben):** it showed -1 white next to +2 followers. The card turns all white into followers, then the space gives +1 white, so net -1 white is probably right, but it reads like a mistake.
- **Call to Arms preview (g03, round II, Ben):** it printed "+-5..1 followers". That looks like a display fault.
- **Clear Out prompts (g02, round III, Cal):** the prompts read "3 to go", "2 to go", "last one", with a "+1 of which color?" prompt in between. Cal read it as only two discards. Wording is probably the cause.
- **Magenta numbers mid-steal (g02, round II, Cal):** Cal said Ben's magenta (6) differed between the table and the choice list. Not checked against the log.
- **Ties (g01 round III, g04):** a tie pays half the prize to every tied player (4-way tie at 0 silver paid +5 each). Check this against the rulebook; players treated it as a surprise.
- **Borrow (g03, round I, Cal):** the preview shows "+1 followers" and "+1 members" for a member that leaves before scoring. The text matches the rule, but the preview does not say the follower goes away.
