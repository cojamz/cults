## Yellow
Contest: Most member colors — counts the colors among your members when the round ends (grey is a color) — Played twice in the AI-player games: with Jester it tied 5 colors and the holder won 88 to 87, and Drifters never decided a game. Bots disagree on Yellow as a main color: players with mostly yellow members won 22 more games in every 100 against Normal bots, and lost 15 per 100 against Hard bots.

**Works and is interesting**
- **Hold a Fair** (round II space) "For each color among your members: +1 follower and +1 of that color (grey: 1 of any color)." — Normal bots won 7 more games in every 100 than players with as many members (flagged strong), Hard bots 5 more; AI players used it for +4 or +5 and built around it with Jester.
- **Hostages** (3 yellow + 4 any) "On Recruit: swap one of your members for a rival’s member that costs the same." — Won 9 more games in every 100 with Normal bots (flagged strong) and 5 more with Hard bots; bought in about 58% of games it showed.
- **Harlequin** (3 yellow + 4 any) "Round End: +1 follower per color among your members." — Won 19 more games in every 100 with Normal bots (the biggest yellow number, flagged strong); no Hard result, and the abuse hunt did not flag it.
- **Jack-of-All-Trades** (4 yellow + 5 any) "Game End: +2 followers per color you have 1 to 3 members of." — Positive in both levels (4 more wins per 100 Normal, 7 more Hard) and bought in about 70% of games it showed.

**Maybe**
- **Harvest** (round I space) "+3 in colors you choose." — Won 2 more per 100 with Normal bots, lost 1 with Hard; both within chance.
- **Count Heads** (round I space) "+1 yellow per minion on the color with the most minions. +1 yellow." — Both bot levels slightly negative (within chance), but AI players saw it give +6 and chained it into Stash; tests disagree.
- **Welcome Strangers** (round I space) "+2 yellow. If you recruit a member this turn in a color none of your members have: +3 yellow." — +1 per 100 in both levels (chance); a judge noted it paid the +3 before Drifters' color was chosen.
- **Demand Tribute** (round III space) "+5 yellow. Each rival discards 2 resources of each color among your members." — Won 3 more per 100 with Normal bots, 0 with Hard; one AI player was confused that the preview showed rivals losing yellow (the Jester was why).
- **Tithe Collector** (3 yellow + 4 any) "Whenever a rival recruits a member in one of your members’ colors, +1 of that color." — Won 6 more per 100 with Normal bots, bought in 39% of games; no Hard result.
- **Barterer** (3 yellow + 3 any) "Whenever a space gives you resources, you may change 1 of them to any color." — No test showed anything either way.
- **Haggler** (4 yellow + 4 any) "On Recruit: swap up to 3 of your resources one for one. Round Start: swap up to 3 of your resources one for one." — No test showed anything either way.
- **Herald** (4 yellow + 4 any) "Whenever you recruit a yellow member, +2 wild, +3 followers." — Even with Normal bots (0 more wins per 100), bought in 29% of games; no Hard result.
- **Dawn Traders** (3 yellow + 2 any) "Round Start: +2 yellow. Swap up to 4 of your resources for other colors, one for one." — 2 more wins per 100 with Normal bots, 0 with Hard; Hard bots bought it in 79% of games, but nothing decided a game.
- **Drifters** (3 yellow + 2 any) "On Recruit: choose a color; these count as a member of that color." — Positive in both levels (2 more wins per 100 each, chance-sized); easy to read but changed no outcome in three judged games.
- **Witch** (5 yellow + 5 any) "On Use, once per round: choose a color; 1 of each rival's biggest color becomes it." — No test showed anything either way.
- **Alchemists** (6 yellow + 6 any, worth 3, counts as 3) "On Use, once per game: rearrange your resources into any mix of colors." — Too few games in both levels; the abuse hunt did not flag it.
- **Peddlers** (5 yellow + 4 any) "On Recruit: +1 of each color. Round Start: +1 of each color." — 2 more wins per 100 with Normal bots (chance); no Hard result.
- **Storekeeper** (4 yellow + 4 any) "Game End: +1 follower per color you hold 3 or more of." — No test showed anything either way.
- **Benefactor** (4 yellow + 4 any) "On Use, once per game: recruit a member costing up to 7 for free." — Won 8 more per 100 with Normal bots but bought in only 24% of games; an AI player chained it into a 9-member win of both member contests; no Hard result.
- **Hostess** (1 yellow + 2 any) "Whenever you recruit a member of a color none of your members have, +2 yellow." — No test showed anything either way.

**Cut, replace or rework**
- **Paint Over** (round II space) "+4 yellow. Turn up to 8 of rivals' resources into yellow, split as you choose." — Lost 4 more games per 100 than expected with Normal bots (a real gap), and judges saw eight "turn nothing" prompts and a very long option list. Fix: rework the effect (a simple fixed turn of rival resources, with no per-color prompts).
- **Pilgrims** (3 yellow + 4 any) "Round End: +1 follower per color among the spaces your minions are on." — Never beat the best other option that turn (+2 to +3 against +4 to +7), nobody reacted, and bots lost 1 per 100; one bot overpaid for it. Fix: +1 follower printed (worth 2).
- **Spice Merchant** (4 yellow + 5 any) "On Recruit: +1 follower per color you hold. Round Start: +1 follower per color you hold." — Lost 5 more games per 100 than expected with Normal bots; judges called it clear but modest (5 to 7 followers for 7 to 9 resources plus a member) and rivals never noticed it. Fix: +1 follower printed (worth 2).
- **Gilder** (5 yellow + 4 any) "On Recruit: +1 yellow per color you hold. Round Start: +1 yellow per color you hold." — Under the paper bar (scored low on printed text), and bots only broke even (2 more wins per 100, chance). Fix: rework the effect (pay followers, not yellow).
