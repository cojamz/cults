## Red
Contest: Most members of one color — counts your members of your biggest color when the round ends (grey is a color) — the AI players chased it with Jester and Lord of the Manor and one lost the lead to a swap, so it was felt; Red as a main color did well with bots (won 14 more games in every 100 with the strongest bots, 11 more with the normal ones) and one player swept it all three rounds in 10 of every 100 games.

**Works and is interesting**
- **Wet Nurse** (2 red + 3 any) "Whenever you recruit the cheapest member in the shop, +3 red." — won 5 more games in every 100 than players with as many members, with both normal and strongest bots; the judges found one real engine (an 80 to 36 win) but the table did not notice it.
- **Swindler** (3 red + 4 any) "Round Start: swap one of your members with a rival's member costing up to 3 more." — won 6 more in every 100 with normal bots and 13 more with the strongest bots, and bots bought it in over 30% of games it showed.
- **Guild Master** (4 red + 3 any) "Whenever you recruit a member of a color you already have, +1 follower." — won 8 more in every 100 with normal bots and 7 more with the strongest bots, the steadiest Red member.
- **Chapman** (3 red + 4 any) "Whenever one of your members is swapped, +2 followers." — won 3 more with normal bots and 7 more with the strongest; judges saw +6 to +14 followers a game (best with Promote), though rivals hand it points without anyone noticing.

**Maybe**
- **Borrow** (round I) "Recruit the cheapest shop member, for free. It returns to the shop at round end, before scoring. +1 red." — won 4 more in every 100 with normal bots and 1 more with the strongest bots, but two AI players misread it as a real recruit and one wasted all of round I on it.
- **Call to Arms** (round I) "Recruit a shop member costing up to 2 per minion you've placed this round, this one included, for free. +1 red." — three AI players called it strong and one called it dead when nothing cheap was in the shop; bots showed 0 (normal) and 4 fewer wins in every 100 (strongest), within chance.
- **Promote** (round II) "+3 red. Swap any member for one costing up to 3 more, from another cult or the shop." — three AI players liked it flipping a contest, but bots found it slightly worse than nothing (2 and 3 fewer wins in every 100, within chance).
- **Demote** (round II) "+3 red. Swap any member for one costing up to 3 less, from another cult or the shop." — same: liked for flipping contests by AI players, no gain for bots (0 and 3 fewer, within chance).
- **Induct** (round III) "Recruit a shop member costing 10 or less, for free. +1 red." — normal bots 1 more win in every 100, the strongest bots 8 fewer (a wide margin); no player or judge spoke to it, so tests disagree.
- **Journeyman** (2 red + 3 any) "Round Start: you may put this in the shop and recruit a shop member costing up to 3 more, for free." — bots saw no gain (0 and 3 fewer wins in every 100, within chance); one AI player won with it in a chain and two others passed on it as dead.
- **Governess** (5 red + 3 any) "On Recruit: recruit the cheapest member in the shop." — normal bots 3 fewer wins in every 100, strongest bots no change; strong in one AI player's chain, passed on by two others.
- **Bannermen** (3 red + 3 any) "Round End: if your members have 2 or fewer colors, +3 followers." — normal bots 2 fewer wins, strongest bots 13 more; one AI player won a game on it, so tests disagree.
- **Dyer** (3 red + 4 any) "Your grey members count as members of your biggest color." — normal bots 4 fewer wins, strongest bots 6 more; no judge or player spoke to it, so tests disagree.
- **Clan Chief** (5 red + 3 any) "Round End: +1 follower per member in your biggest group of one color (grey is its own color)." — normal bots 4 more wins in every 100 (within chance); too few games with the strongest bots.
- **Abbess** (3 red + 3 any) "Whenever a member joins your cult from a rival's, +2 followers." — normal bots 5 more wins, strongest bots 5 fewer, both within chance; no player or judge spoke to it.
- **Fairy Godmother** (5 red + 4 any) "On Use, once per round: promote or demote another of your members." — normal bots 1 fewer win, within chance, and bought in under 30% of games; too few games with the strongest bots.
- **Tavern Keeper** (3 red + 4 any) "On Recruit: +1 red per 2 members you have. Round Start: +1 red per 2 members you have." — too few games in both bot levels; no test showed anything either way.
- **Pied Piper** (4 red + 4 any) "On Use, once per game: swap a rival's member with a shop member that costs as much or more." — too few games in both bot levels; no test showed anything either way.

**Cut, replace or rework**
- **Gather the Kin** (round I) "+3 red. If you recruit a member this turn of a color you already have: +4 followers. +1 red." — 3 fewer wins in every 100 than players with as many members with both normal and strongest bots; in round I nobody has a member of a color yet, so the follower bonus almost never fires. Fix: rework the effect so it works with an empty cult.
- **Innkeeper** (3 red + 3 any) "Whenever one of your members goes back to the shop, +3 red." — 3 fewer wins in every 100 with both normal and strongest bots; it fires once or twice a game, almost only from Borrow, and nobody felt it. Fix: cut.
- **Warden** (2 red + 3 any) "Your other members can't be swapped." — 2 and 4 fewer wins in every 100 (normal, strongest); three AI players called it dead or never saw it, even though players asked for a defence 6 times. Fix: rework the effect into a way to protect one member.
- **Ferryman** (5 red + 3 any) "Whenever you recruit a member, +1 red." — under the paper-judge bar and too few bot games to show anything (bots rarely bought it). Fix: rework the effect to +2 red per recruit.
