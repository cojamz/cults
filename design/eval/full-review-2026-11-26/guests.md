## Guests
Contest: none (Guests have no spaces and no contest, only members that cost "any" color) — no test spoke to a Guests contest.

**Works and is interesting**
- **Mutineers** (6 any) "On Use, once per game: take a member from a rival with more members than you." — The strongest guest in the bot games: owners won 6 more games in every 100 with Normal bots (flagged strong) and 11 more with Hard bots; 8 AI players called it swingy and strong. Fix: costs 1 more (7 any).
- **Trumpeters** (7 any) "Whenever you win a contest, +2 followers." — Bots won 2 more games in every 100 (Normal) and 4 more (Hard) than players with as many members, both small but positive, and 4 AI players said they should have bought it.
- **Comforter** (8 any) "Round End: +2 followers per contest you don't win." — Normal bots won 5 more games in every 100 and Hard bots 1 more, positive in both levels, and the strong-play abuse test did not flag it.

**Maybe**
- **Protesters** (6 any) "On Use, once per round: put a spare minion token on an open space. Nobody can place there this round." — Normal bots 2 fewer wins in every 100, Hard bots 3 more, both within chance; no player or judge spoke to it.
- **Gift Swappers** (8 any) "On Use, once per game: every player, you too, gives one member they choose to the player on their left." — Normal bots 3 fewer wins in every 100, Hard bots 1 more, within chance; in the judges' games it moved Mendicant and Purifiers around but never decided a game.
- **Conjurer** (7 any) "All your resources count as any when you recruit a member." — Bots won 2 fewer games in every 100 (Normal) and 1 more (Hard), both within chance; one AI player called it weak.
- **Extra Hand** (10 any) "On Recruit: +1 minion this round." — Normal bots won 2 fewer games in every 100 but Hard bots 9 more; two AI players called it dead, so the tests disagree.
- **Jester** (5 any) "Counts as a member of every color." — Five AI players called it exciting, but bots found nothing (2 fewer wins in every 100 Normal, 1 more Hard) and the text says every color while the member-colors contest counts it as one.
- **Meddler** (8 any) "On Use, once per round: swap any 2 members that cost the same, in any cult or the shop." — Normal bots won 4 fewer games in every 100 but Hard bots 7 more; judges saw it feed Chapman's swap points once.
- **Spoilsport** (8 any) "Round End: choose a contest; nobody wins it this round." — Normal bots won 1 fewer game in every 100 and Hard bots 5 more, both within chance.
- **Robbers** (9 any) "On Recruit: steal 5 resources." — Bots bought it in about 80 games in every 100 it showed, yet it moved wins by 1 or 0 in every 100; nothing flagged it.

**Cut, replace or rework**
- **Purifiers** (6 any) "On Use, once per game: discard one of your members. Each rival with more members than you discards one too." — Bots bought it in about half the games it showed and still won 3 fewer in every 100 (Normal) and no more (Hard); two AI players never found a turn to use it. Fix: rework the effect.
- **Lord of the Manor** (4 any) "You go first every round." — 15 of 16 AI players called it far too cheap (worth 3 followers, counts as 3 members, first turn), and it changed hands three times in one game; bots see nothing (1 more win in every 100 Normal, 1 fewer Hard) only because they buy it nearly every time. Fix: a cost step, costs 1 more (5 any).
- **Plague Bearer** (5 any) "Worth 0 members. Whenever this member joins your cult, −5 followers." — Scored under the paper bar and bots find nothing (1 and 3 more wins in every 100, within chance), only bought about a quarter of the time. Fix: cut.
- **Weeders** (7 any) "On Use, once per round: discard any shop members and refill." — Weak in both bot levels (1 fewer win in every 100 Normal, 6 fewer Hard), and no one in the table tests praised it. Fix: rework the effect.

(Also dealt now and then, too rarely for any test to read: Squanderer, Moneychangers, Trumpeter, Taskmaster, Crooked Judge. No test showed anything either way.)
