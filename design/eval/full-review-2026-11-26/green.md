## Green
Contest: Most minions on green — counts your minions on Green spaces when the round ends. One AI-player game had a player copy spaces with the Green Cloak to win it as part of about 35 followers from contests, and another used Jester, Gardeners and Hold a Fair for it, so it was played for; seats that were mostly green members won 10 more games in every 100 than expected with the strongest bots and 15 more with the normal bots.

**Works and is interesting**
- **Replay All** (round III space) "Choose a color other than green. Copy every action with your minion on its spaces. +1 green." — With the normal bots its owner won 17 more games in every 100 than expected (flagged strong); with the strongest bots 7 more, and AI players listed copying Plunder with it among their exciting plays. One player found its preview confusing.
- **Admirers** (3 green + 5 any, worth 1) "On Recruit: copy an action of the rival with the most followers." — Positive with both bot levels (4 more wins in every 100 each) and bought in about 3 of every 4 games it showed.

**Maybe**
- **Follow Suit** (round I space) "Copy the previous action. +1 green." — Normal bots 2 more wins in every 100, strongest bots no difference; chance-sized.
- **Do It Again** (round I space) "Copy an action with your minion. +1 green." — Normal bots 3 more wins in every 100 but the strongest bots 3 fewer; the tests disagree.
- **Draw a Crowd** (round II space) "+1 follower per minion on Green spaces, this one included. +3 green." — Normal bots 4 more wins in every 100, strongest bots 4 fewer; the tests disagree.
- **Imitate** (round II space) "Copy an action with a rival’s minion on it. +1 green." — Normal bots 2 fewer, strongest bots 5 more; AI players named copying Plunder with it as exciting, but the evidence is thin either way.
- **Mimics** (4 green + 4 any, worth 1) "On Recruit: use the On Recruit of any member in play." — Small plus with both bot levels (2 more wins in every 100) and bought in over half of the games it showed; nothing felt at the table.
- **Gardeners** (2 green + 3 any, worth 1) "Round End: +1 follower per minion you have on Green spaces." — Bots slightly negative (1 to 2 fewer wins in every 100), but three AI players said they should have bought it for a cheap +3 a round.
- **Druids** (3 green + 4 any, worth 1) "Game End: if you have the most green members, +6 followers." — Normal bots no difference, strongest bots 5 more; two AI players called it dead because Jester and swaps change the green count.
- **Understudy** (3 green + 3 any, worth 1) "On Recruit: copy the action taken the previous turn." — Too few games with either bot level; no test showed anything either way.
- **Chorus** (3 green + 4 any, worth 1) "Whenever you copy your own action, +1 follower." — Too few games with either bot level; no test showed anything either way.
- **Forger** (2 green + 4 any, worth 1) "Whenever you copy an action or a member, +2 of its color (grey: any color)." — Too few games with either bot level; no test showed anything either way.
- **Tall-Tale Teller** (7 green + 5 any, worth 1) "Whenever you copy an action, double the resources it gives you." — Too few games with either bot level; no test showed anything either way.
- **Copyist** (3 green + 4 any, worth 1) "Whenever you copy an action, +2 followers." — Normal bots 5 more wins in every 100 (bought in only 36% of games it showed); too few games with the strongest bots.
- **Shadow** (3 green + 4 any, worth 1) "On Recruit: copy the action you just took." — Small plus with both bot levels (1 and 2 more wins in every 100) and bought in most games it showed; nothing felt.
- **Impostors** (3 green + 5 any, worth 1) "On Recruit: this member becomes a copy of a rival’s member." — Too few games with either bot level; no test showed anything either way.
- **Trapper** (2 green + 3 any, worth 1) "Whenever anyone copies one of your actions, they −1 follower and you +1 follower." — Too few games with either bot level; no test showed anything either way.

**Cut, replace or rework**
- **Flock Together** (round I space) "+1 green per minion on Green spaces, this one included. +1 green." — Fewer wins than expected with both bot levels (2 and 4 fewer in every 100). Fix: rework the effect.
- **Lackey** (3 green + 3 any, worth 1) "Whenever you place a minion on the same color as the minion placed just before yours, +2 green." — Play judges saw it in a cult in only 1 of 18 games, firing once for an unseen +2; bots were at chance. Fix: rework the effect.
- **Doppelganger** (4 green + 4 any, worth 1) "On Recruit: this member becomes a copy of another of your members." — Normal bots 5 fewer wins in every 100 than players with as many members, bought in under half the games it showed; strongest bots had too few games. Fix: worth 2 followers printed instead of 1.
- **Secret-Keepers** (2 green + 3 any, worth 1) "Your actions can’t be copied." — No difference with the normal bots and 8 fewer wins in every 100 with the strongest bots, bought in under 40% of games. Fix: rework the effect.
- **Minstrel** (3 green + 4 any, worth 1) "On Use, once per game: your next On Recruit happens twice." — Normal bots 6 fewer wins in every 100, bought in about a third of games; strongest bots had too few games; it can fire only once a game and needs another On Recruit member to matter. Fix: rework the effect.
