## Magenta
Contest: "Most resources — counts all the resources you hold when the round ends, every color together" — In the AI-player games it forced a hoard: the leader skipped buying or bought one lone follower to protect the lead ("holding 13 to 16 resources and passing recruit after recruit felt dead"), though one player won with it (+4, +6, +10 across the rounds). One player sweeps it in 10 in every 100 games, and Magenta's main-color seats won no more than average with the strongest bots.

**Works and is interesting**
- **Stash** (round I space) "+3 magenta." — Won 8 more games in every 100 with the strongest bots (the margin is wide) and was even with ordinary bots; it fed the Most resources win in an AI-player game.
- **Beg** (round I space) "+2 magenta per rival with more resources than you. +1 magenta." — Even with both bot levels, but a player said "being at zero didn't feel hopeless", and it works as a catch-up for the contest.
- **Poach** (round III space) "Recruit a rival's member you choose, for free. +1 magenta." — Won 6 more games in every 100 with ordinary bots (flat with the strongest, wide margin), and players liked taking the leader's member.
- **Tax Men** (2 magenta + 5 any, worth 1) "Whenever a rival recruits a member costing 7 or more, they give you 2 resources of their choice." — Won 4 more games in every 100 with ordinary bots and 10 more with the strongest, bought in about 4 of every 10 shops it showed in.
- **Extortionist** (4 magenta + 3 any, worth 1) "On Recruit and at Round Start: steal 2 resources." — Positive in both bot levels (2 and 7 more wins in every 100) and bots bought it in 8 of every 10 shops it showed in.

**Maybe**
- **Plunder** (round II space) "Steal 3 followers and 3 resources. +1 magenta." — Even with ordinary bots, 3 more wins with the strongest (chance range); AI players called it swingy and strong when copied by Replay All, Imitate or Follow Suit.
- **Sleight of Hand** (round II space) "Steal a rival's member costing up to 7; give them a shop member of your choice in its place. +1 magenta." — Even in both bot levels; one player liked it on Monks, others complained that one placement undoes their plan.
- **Hoarder** (3 magenta + 4 any, worth 1) "Round End: if you hold 10 or more resources, +3 followers." — Positive with ordinary bots (4 more in 100, chance range); judges saw nobody buy it on purpose and said spending down is the better play.
- **Night Thieves** (4 magenta + 5 any, worth 1) "Round End, before scoring: steal 3 resources." — Positive with ordinary bots but bought in only 15 of every 100 shops; the combo hunt found it the strongest card in the color (+5 followers over the best rival), so its price of 9 is already set.
- **Starveling** (3 magenta + 4 any, worth 1) "While you have the fewest followers (ties count), your steals take 2 more resources." — Even with ordinary bots; no test showed anything about the strongest bots or how it felt.
- **Robin Hood** (3 magenta + 3 any, worth 1) "Whenever you steal from the player with the most followers, +1 follower." — Bots rarely bought it and no test showed anything either way.
- **Highwayman** (6 magenta + 3 any, worth 1) "On Use, once per game: steal half of a rival's biggest pile, rounded down." — Bots rarely bought it and no test showed anything either way.
- **Twins** (2 magenta + 3 any, worth 2, counts as 2) "Worth 2 followers." — Bots rarely bought it and no test showed anything either way.
- **Changeling** (4 magenta + 6 any, worth 1) "On Recruit: swap this member for a rival's member costing up to 8." — Even with ordinary bots, 9 more wins in 100 with the strongest (wide margin); tests disagree.
- **Briber** (4 magenta + 4 any, worth 1) "On Use, once per game: spend magenta equal to a rival's member's cost to steal it." — Bots rarely bought it and no test showed anything either way.
- **Informer** (3 magenta + 3 any, worth 1) "Whenever a rival steals from you, they lose 1 follower." — Even with ordinary bots, 7 more wins in 100 with the strongest (very wide margin); tests disagree.
- **Cutpurse** (4 magenta + 6 any, worth 1) "Whenever you recruit, steal 1 resource." — Positive with ordinary bots but bought in only 12 of every 100 shops; price of 10 already set by Cory.
- **Fence** (3 magenta + 4 any, worth 1) "Whenever you steal, +2 magenta." — Bots rarely bought it; in the AI-player games (where it appeared as Magenta Cloak) it paid at once with steals and helped hold Most resources, but no test measured it.

**Cut, replace or rework**
- **Pilfer** (round I space) "Steal 1 resource per minion you've placed this round. +1 magenta." — Lost games in both bot levels (3 and 4 fewer wins in 100); it only felt big when paired with Cutthroats to take followers. Fix: rework the effect.
- **Cutthroats** (4 magenta + 4 any, worth 1) "Whenever you steal resources, you may steal that many followers instead." — Judges called it the most felt new member (an 8-follower swing won a game) but the "instead" is never a choice since followers are always better, and bots lost 3 in every 100 with it. Fix: rework the effect so it always takes followers, with no choice.
- **Thief-Takers** (6 magenta + 4 any, worth 2, counts as 2) "Whenever a rival steals from you, steal 1 back from them." — Lost 7 more games in every 100 than players with as many members, with ordinary bots. Fix: worth 3 followers printed (not a cheaper price).
- **Press Gang** (6 magenta + 4 any, worth 2, counts as 2) "Round End: a rival you choose gives you a member of their choice." — Under the paper-judge bar and even with ordinary bots; the rival picks, so it gives away its worst member. Fix: rework the effect.
