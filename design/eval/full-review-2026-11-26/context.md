# What was tested on the latest build (v0.99.807/809), Nov 25-26

Use these sources only. Card text and prices: cards-data.md (live, read from the engine).

1. BOTS line under each card (balance run, 400 Normal-bot and 120 Hard-bot 4-player games, bots that push back on an empty cult).
   The number is how many more games in every 100 its owner won than players with as many members ("+6±4" = 6 more wins per 100, give or take 4).
   Under about the ± is chance. Hard bots play much better (a Hard bot beat three Normals 56% of the time), so where Normal and Hard disagree, trust Hard but note its ± is wide.
   "bought in N% of games it showed": how often a bot took it when it was in the shop.
   STRONG/WEAK/TRAP tags are the tool's own flags.
2. Play judges (two Sonnet judges each read 3 strong-bot games where the card sat in a cult) for the 18 newest members: design/eval/full-review-2026-11-26/play-judges.md (copy at /mnt/project-files/review-nov26/play-judges.md).
