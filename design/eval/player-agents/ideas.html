Every card idea the players wrote

All 99 ideas from eight games, word for word, nothing added. Sorted into spaces and members by what the idea describes; within each, in the order the games were played. The first game asked for no ideas, so it has none.

Why so few: the players were told ideas are "usually empty", and offered a slot on every move. They almost never used it during play; nearly all of these came in the notes after a round or after the game, about 3 per player. Where a game was reviewed afterwards, the reviewer's note says whether the idea answers something that really happened in that game.
