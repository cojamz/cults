What to change, from nine player games

The players’ own recommendations combined with what we saw from the whole-game view. One line each; tap a line for the evidence and card text.

High seen in 4+ games, players and our read agreeMedium 2 to 3 games, or one side onlyLow one game, or mostly 2 and 3 players

Change

  1. 1HighRound III’s last moves take members and contests, and the target can’t answer Idea with examples · from players and our read DemotePromoteMutineersKidnappers

    Players: 8 players in 5 games named it; the most common complaint from experienced players.

    What we saw: One leader lost about 30 followers to five late plays and answered one. Players still loved the swaps themselves (see Keep), so the fix is a reply or a safer last placement, not fewer swaps.

    Demote
    +3 red. Swap any member for one costing up to 3 less, from another cult or the shop.
    Promote
    +3 red. Swap any member for one costing up to 3 more, from another cult or the shop.
    Mutineers
    6 any. On Use, once per game: take a member from a rival with more members than you.
    Kidnappers
    6 magenta + 4 any, worth 2, counts as 2 members. Round End: a rival you choose gives you one of their members.
  2. 2HighSpoilsport decides games after everyone’s last move One card · from players and our read Spoilsport

    Players: 4 players said they could do nothing about it; its holder called it the tensest choice in the game.

    What we saw: Bought in 4 games and decided something every time: 16 followers off two rivals in one game; cancelled a 3-3 tie in another, and that player lost by 1.

    Spoilsport
    8 any, worth 1. Round End: choose a contest; nobody wins it this round.
  3. 3HighDabblers wins member contests, and nobody can tell how it counts One card · from players and our read DabblersRed contest

    Players: Confused players in 5 games ("wild colors or just bulk?"); one called it a sweep a one-member cult can’t answer.

    What we saw: Whoever held it at a round end won or tied Red, often Blue or Yellow too. The game counts it as 2 in Red though Red prints "each card counts 1".

    Dabblers
    5 any, worth 2, counts as 2 members. Counts as 2 wild members.
    Red contest
    Most members of one color: your biggest group of members of one color when the round ends, grey members included (each card counts 1).
  4. 4HighA recruit spent on a 1-follower buy feels like sweeping crumbs Idea with examples · from players and our read Buying followers

    Players: Nearly every player, in all 9 games. Player ideas (unchecked): a space that turns leftovers into followers without a recruit.

    What we saw: In one game 39 of 52 recruits were follower buys. True that the recruit is the real cost; this is how the turn feels, mostly late.

    Buying followers
    1, 5 or 10 resources of any colors for that many followers, one per recruit.
  5. 5HighMembers bought in round III don’t pay back, so the shop is skipped Idea with examples · from our read Buying followers

    Players: Hinted: "Building a cult felt like it should matter more than it did at the end."

    What we saw: In one game 1 of 8 round III members was bought and 19 of 20 round III recruits were follower buys. A member bought then is worth about its 1 printed follower.

    Buying followers
    1, 5 or 10 resources of any colors for that many followers, one per recruit.
  6. 6MediumMember contests end up as one player’s private prize Idea with examples · from players and our read Fewest membersKidnappersGuest of HonorWrite-Off

    Players: Players in 4 games: "nobody, me included, ever considered contesting it".

    What we saw: Fewest members went to one player at 0 or 1 members, unchallenged, for +16 to +20 in 3 games. A 14-member cult took +30 from two member contests in one round. When members did move (Kidnappers counting as 2, Guest of Honor and Write-Off handing members around), players called it the most memorable part. That is the shape to grow.

    Fewest members
    Contest: the fewest members when the round ends (0 counts).
    Kidnappers
    6 magenta + 4 any, worth 2, counts as 2 members. Round End: a rival you choose gives you one of their members.
    Guest of Honor
    2 white + 3 any. On Use, once per round: give a rival one of your members. +4 followers.
    Write-Off
    Give a member to a rival for its cost in white. +1 white.
  7. 7MediumWhite Cloak pays about double any other cloak One card · from players and our read White Cloak

    Players: One player called it "a money printer".

    What we saw: Paid 20 to 24 white in each of 4 games; the next best cloak paid 10 to 14. Its owner won 2 of the 4.

    White Cloak
    Whenever you buy followers, +2 white.
  8. 8MediumCopies of copies pay every link, and one player takes 4 or 5 turns in a row Idea with examples · from players and our read Crunch TimeCopyistGreen Cloak

    Players: 4 players in 2 games: "five straight turns while I sat and watched".

    What we saw: Copyist paid 16 to 18 for 5 resources. Mostly seen with 2 or 3 players; once with 4 (39 to 83 followers in one round III).

    Crunch Time
    +2 purple. Your next two turns come right after this one.
    Copyist
    2 green + 3 any, worth 1. Whenever you copy an action, +2 followers.
    Green Cloak
    Whenever you copy an action or a member, +2 of its color.
  9. 9MediumKidnappers reads as "you pick the member", but the rival picks One card · from players and our read Kidnappers

    Players: 2 players: the rival always hands over a used-up card, never the one beating you.

    What we saw: In one game it cost 10 and returned about 3 followers. In another it won Most members for whoever held it, since it counts as 2 members.

    Kidnappers
    6 magenta + 4 any, worth 2, counts as 2 members. Round End: a rival you choose gives you one of their members.
  10. 10MediumAnchorites pays 15 followers for 6 resources One card · from our read Anchorites

    Players: Players liked it: "far better than anything I bought".

    What we saw: Most players stay at 5 or fewer members anyway, so the condition costs nothing. Best bought card in two 4-player games.

    Anchorites
    3 white + 3 any, worth 1. Round End: if you have 5 or fewer members, +5 followers.
  11. 11MediumLine Cutters takes the first seat from the player in last place One card · from players and our read Line Cutters

    Players: "I was dead last on 9 and still placed second."

    What we saw: Broke the catch-up seat in two 4-player games; does nothing for a player who is already behind.

    Line Cutters
    4 any, worth 1. You go first every round.
  12. 12MediumMost resources in round III goes to the one player who doesn’t spend Idea with examples · from players and our read Magenta contest

    Players: Players in 3 games: one player held 14, nobody else held any.

    What we saw: In rounds I and II holding against spending was a real choice players praised. In round III everyone spends to 0 and one player takes +10 holding 3 to 14.

    Magenta contest
    Most resources: all the resources you hold when the round ends, every color together.
  13. 13MediumWent last is decided by who still has minions, not by fighting for the token Idea with examples · from players Last CallRight Away

    Players: Players in 4 games.

    What we saw: Partly true: the last open token space, not the first, wins it. Some of this is players taking token spaces too early.

    Last Call
    +1 purple. +1 follower and +1 purple per minion you have left. Take the Goes Last token: your other minions go after everyone else’s.
    Right Away
    +2 purple in round I, +3 in round II, +4 in round III. Your next turn comes right after this one.
  14. 14LowFewest silver ends in all-way ties at 0 Idea with examples · from players and our read Silver contestSilver CloakToastPolishKingmakers

    Players: 6 players; 4 said the Silver Cloak works against the contest.

    What we saw: Partly how players played: gift spaces taken early, Kingmakers split 1 to each rival. Not worth over-weighting.

    Silver contest
    Fewest silver: the silver you hold when the round ends (0 counts; tied leaders each get half, rounded up).
    Silver Cloak
    Whenever you gift silver, +2 silver.
    Toast
    +4 silver. Each rival +2 silver. +1 silver.
    Polish
    +2 silver. Turn all your silver into one other color. +1 silver.
    Kingmakers
    Round End: give rivals up to 3 of your silver, split as you choose. +1 follower per silver given.
  15. 15LowMarkdown finds an empty discard pile late in the game One card · from players Markdown

    Players: 2 players, in the 2-player game.

    What we saw: Only seen with 2 players, after the pile reshuffled.

    Markdown
    Recruit a member from the discard pile now, paying 2 less. +3 blue.
  16. 16LowPurifiers rarely has a target One card · from players Purifiers

    Players: 2 players in 2 games.

    What we saw: Rivals with more members than you are uncommon when you hold it.

    Purifiers
    On Use, once per game: discard one of your members. Each rival with more members than you discards one too.
  17. 17LowCash In barely beats buying followers One card · from players Cash In

    Players: 2 players: a saved-up plan that "fizzled"; it can hand back 1 white that needs a whole recruit.

    What we saw: Seen in 2 games.

    Cash In
    Turn all your white into followers. +1 white.
  18. 18LowHeist and its copies all land on the leader One card · from our read Heist

    Players: 1 player complained of the pile-on; others praised it as the clearest way to hit the leader.

    What we saw: Taken or copied 7 times in one game, 16 followers off the leader in round III. One game only.

    Heist
    Steal 3 followers and 3 resources. +1 magenta.

Keep

  1. Swapping members in and out of rival cultsPromoteDemoteSwindle

    Called the most exciting part of the wheel in 4 games.

    Promote
    +3 red. Swap any member for one costing up to 3 more, from another cult or the shop.
    Demote
    +3 red. Swap any member for one costing up to 3 less, from another cult or the shop.
    Swindle
    Steal a rival’s member costing up to 7; give them a shop member of your choice in its place. +1 magenta.
  2. Members passed around to break contest countsGuest of HonorWrite-Off

    All four players praised it in two games.

    Guest of Honor
    2 white + 3 any. On Use, once per round: give a rival one of your members. +4 followers.
    Write-Off
    Give a member to a rival for its cost in white. +1 white.
  3. Free members that grow with the roundLate Shift

    Praised in 4 games: placement order became a puzzle.

    Late Shift
    Recruit a shop member costing up to 2 per minion you’ve placed this round, this one included, for free. +1 red.
  4. A member for the player losing contestsConsolation Prize

    Paid 8 to 10 for 7 in 4 games and kept trailing players in it.

    Consolation Prize
    7 any. Round End: +2 followers per contest you don’t win.
  5. Using a shop member without buying itTest Drive

    The best single placement for three players in one game.

    Test Drive
    Discard a shop member. Use its effect now. +1 blue.
  6. The face-up discard pile as a second shopOut of StyleBlue Cloak

    4 of 13 members in one game came from it.

    Out of Style
    Discard 3 shop members and refill. +1 of each discarded member’s color. +1 blue.
    Blue Cloak
    Whenever you discard a member, +1 of its color.
  7. Exact-change planning for follower buysCatch Up

    "Made some follower buys feel planned instead of dead."

    Catch Up
    +2 magenta per rival with more resources than you. +1 magenta.

    Players in 3 games planned two placements as one move.

Rules the cards don’t settle

  1. Does Dabblers count as 2 in Red?DabblersRed contest

    Red prints "each card counts 1"; the game counts 2.

    Dabblers
    5 any, worth 2, counts as 2 members. Counts as 2 wild members.
    Red contest
    Most members of one color: your biggest group of members of one color when the round ends, grey members included (each card counts 1).
  2. Is grey a color?

    No for Yellow’s contest; yes for several cards. Miscounted 4 times in one game.

  3. Is taking a member "recruiting" it?SwindleMutineers

    The game says yes, so recruit abilities fire on takes; in one game that was the winning margin.

    Swindle
    Steal a rival’s member costing up to 7; give them a shop member of your choice in its place. +1 magenta.
    Mutineers
    6 any. On Use, once per game: take a member from a rival with more members than you.
  4. In what order do round-end abilities happen?KingmakersSpoilsport

    The rules don’t say; in one game the order decided round III.

    Kingmakers
    Round End: give rivals up to 3 of your silver, split as you choose. +1 follower per silver given.
    Spoilsport
    8 any, worth 1. Round End: choose a contest; nobody wins it this round.
  5. Can Downsizers discard itself?Downsizers

    The game says no; the text reads yes.

    Downsizers
    On Use, once per round: discard one of your members. +4 white.
