# Findings across 9 games (360 findings, 113 subjects)

## Recruiting members (whole game) · 7 game(s) (4 with 4 players), 13 held up, weighted strength 30
- [pilot2, 4p] v1 observation: Pricey members arrive free far more than they are bought. Evidence: Only 10 members were bought by paying; 7 more came free from Late Shift, Initiation, Seat Filler and Borrow, besides members moved by Swindle, Promote, Demote, Poach and Mutineers.
- [r3g1, 4p] v1 complaint (Dee, Cal, Ava): Late in the game several rival cards aim at whoever leads on members, and the leader can answer almost none of them. Evidence: Mutineers, Repossessors, Demote with Ghost, Con Men and Protesters took about 30 followers off Dee in rounds II and III; she led by 18 after round II and lost by 8, answering only one (buying Copyist back).
- [r3g2, 4p] v1 complaint (Ben, Cal): So many cards take members that building a cult makes you a target. Evidence: 7 members were taken (Swindle 2, Kidnappers 2, Mutineers, Double Agent, Poach), but most were junk, Anchorites came back the same round, and Cal, hit 4 times, won.
- [r3g2, 4p] v1 observation: Giving a member to a rival usually helps them, because bodies hurt only the Fewest members leader and Write-Off and Farewell turn a body into its cost. Evidence: 6 of the 7 members given or swapped into a rival's cult helped the receiver (e.g. Talent Scouts gave Cal a Yellow tie, Double Agent gave Ben a Farewell for 10).
- [r3g2, 4p] v1 observation (Ava): Round III's member shop is ignored because a late member is worth only its printed follower. Evidence: 1 of 8 round III members was bought; 19 of the 20 round III recruits were follower buys.
- [r3g2, 4p] v1 observation: A close game was won by a 2-member cult; the 0-member cult was last and the 8-member cult second. Evidence: Final 91, 87, 86, 83; 38% of followers bought, 28% from contests.
- [r3g3, 4p] v1 complaint (Ben, Dee, Ava): Member takes (Swindle, Kidnappers, Mutineers, Body Snatchers) feed one pile that scores both Most members and Most members of one color, so one player can sweep both member contests plus Most resources in round III. Evidence: Cal went from 5 to 14 members in round III and took three +10 prizes, 35 of his 52 contest followers.
- [r3g3, 4p] v1 observation: Taking a member by a swap or steal counts as recruiting it, so recruit triggers fire on member takes, and no card text tells the player so. Evidence: 3 of Shoplifter's 11 triggers came from Body Snatchers, Swindle and Mutineers; Ava's Red Cloak paid on Promote, Demote and Poach, and Poach also set off Snitch.
- [r3g4, 3p, player-count] v1 complaint (Ava, Cal): Picking a cloak first or second feels like a blind bet or automatic. Evidence: Three cloaks for three players; the last player took the one left.
- [r3g4, 3p, player-count] v2 complaint (Ben): A focused one-color plan wins at most one contest a round while a spread-out cult wins several. Evidence: Ben placed 0 of 12 minions on green and had 1 member color all game; Ava's mostly one-color line still finished 5 behind the winner.
- [r3g4, 3p, player-count] v1 observation (Ava, Ben): Members were under-used to contest Most member colors: one cheap member in a second and third color would have contested 20 followers. Evidence: A yellow member for Ava in round III would have tied the +10 (5 each), about a 7-point swing in a game decided by 5.
- [r3g5, 3p, player-count] v1 observation: A cult of 2 members won the game. Evidence: Ben won 87 to 86 holding 2 members, on 51 bought followers, 20 from Fewest members and 10 from Anchorites.
- [r3g5, 3p] v1 observation: Members from free-recruit spaces paid as well as the best bought members. Evidence: Late Shift and Borrow gave 5 members worth 31 resources by price; Dabblers (about 12) and Patrons (7) matched Consolation Prize (10) and Purists (9).
- [r3g6, 2p, player-count] v1 complaint (Ava, Ben): With one rival, players buy members only to keep them from that rival, which feels hollow. Evidence: Mutineers (6) and Anarchist (8) were both bought to deny; Anarchist later became the winning swap.
