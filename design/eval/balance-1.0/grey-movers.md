# Grey members that move members (counterplay)

Goal (yours): counterplay through moving members, in the grey deck so every game has it. Protection now stops steals, never swaps, and swaps read "give one of yours, recruit one of theirs". Names and prices are Claude's fill-ins.

Cards compared against:
- **Anarchist**: "On Use, once per round: swap any 2 members, in any cult or the shop, that cost within 2 of each other." (7)
- **Mentor & Protégés**: "On Recruit: recruit the cheapest member in the shop." (8, worth 3)
- **Con Men**: "Counts as 2 members. On Recruit: steal 5 resources." (7)
- **Old Maid**: "Whenever this member joins your cult, −5 followers."
- **Encore**: "Copy the On Recruit of a rival's member. +1 green." **Greatest Hits**: "Copy the On Recruit of each of your members, in any order. +1 green."

## 1. Usurper (about 8, worth 2) [pick]
"On Use, once per game: give one of your members to the rival with the most followers and recruit any one of theirs."
- Round III, 4 players: Dee leads with 74 and is stacking On Recruits for Greatest Hits, Mentor & Protégés among them. Ava uses Usurper: gives Dee her Old Maid, recruits Dee's Mentor & Protégés. Dee loses 5 (Old Maid) and Mentor's 3, gains Old Maid's 1: down 7, and her Greatest Hits loses its best On Recruit. Ava recruited Mentor, so its On Recruit fires: she gets the cheapest shop member too. About an 11-follower swing on the leader, no resources, no recruit used. Buying 10 followers instead costs 10 and the recruit; Anarchist can't make this trade (5 and 8 are more than 2 apart).
- Who feels it: the leader, and everyone watches who leads while Usurper is unused. Answers: Loyalists, Anarchist or Promote to trade back.
- Not Anarchist (no cost limit, you're always one side, only the leader), not Double Agent (you pick what you give), not Poach (you give one back).
- Risk: with Old Maid it's strong; price it high.

## 2. Exile (about 6, worth 1)
"On Use, once per game: send a rival's member to the bottom of the pile, then recruit the top member of the pile."
- Round III: Ben holds Con Men; Cal is about to Encore it. Dee exiles Con Men and recruits the pile's top card (say a 4-cost worth 1). Ben loses 2, Cal's Encore has nothing to copy, Dee keeps her 9 green for the +10 contest. Anarchist would only move Con Men into the shop, where Cal buys it and fires the steal himself.
- Who feels it: the target; copiers lose their target.
- Not Poach: nobody keeps the member, and it reaches a player behind Lie Low or Bodyguards (it isn't a steal).
- Risk: in round II it takes a member away early, which you've wanted to stay a mild sting.

## 3. Royalties (about 5, worth 1)
"Whenever a rival copies one of your actions or one of your members' On Recruits, give them this member and recruit their most expensive member costing 7 or less."
- Round III: Ava takes Largesse (+10); Cal Echoes it (+10). Royalties goes to Cal; Ava recruits Cal's Con Men. Cal still nets about +9, Ava +1 and a Con Men. Royalties now sits with Cal and taxes whoever copies him next.
- Who feels it: every copier, who sees the tax before copying. A soft, moving version of the new Agents.
- Risk: it fires on another player's turn; doesn't touch Greatest Hits copying your own members (Usurper does).

## Cut while playing them out
Leader-only once-per-round Promote (a smaller Anarchist); a member that gives a rival "can't be copied" (that's where Agents is going); a crown that moves to the trailer (nobody wants it); shop-member swaps within 2 (Anarchist or Promote); give a member for its cost in resources (a gift, not a counter); pass members left (seating decides); move the leader's member to the trailer (kingmaking, owner gains nothing); take one back when yours is taken (a rescue); trade for a rival's latest recruit (needs memory); grab a rival's big buy when made (a cheaper Poach on someone else's turn).
