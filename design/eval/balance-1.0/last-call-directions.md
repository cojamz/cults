# Last Call: two directions, played out

**Last Call** today: "+1 purple. +1 follower and +1 purple per minion you have left. Skip your turns until everyone else is out of minions."

## Why it wins games now
The payout isn't the main thing. Going last is. Round III, 4 players, 5 minions each. Ava takes Last Call with her first minion: +1 purple, +4 followers, +4 purple. Then the other three players place all 15 of their minions, and Ava places her last 4 after them. By then every pile is final, so Ava knows exactly which contests she can still take and by how much. In one game she won all four round III contests (10 followers each) by one resource each. Nobody can answer, because nothing in the game places after her.

So cutting the payout weakens it a little; giving other players a way to go after her weakens what actually wins.

## Direction 1: pay less per minion left
- **1a. Drop the purple per minion:** "+1 purple. +1 follower per minion you have left. Skip your turns until everyone else is out of minions." Same turn: Ava gets +1 purple and +4 followers. She's 4 purple worse in the purple contest, which she often wins too.
- **1b. Drop the follower per minion:** "+1 purple. +1 purple per minion you have left. Skip…" Same turn: Ava gets +5 purple, 4 fewer followers.
- Either way she still goes last with full information, so expect it to stay the best space, just by less.

## Direction 2: other purple ways to go last (keeps the payout)
- **2a. Night Owls** today: "Once per round: after you place your last minion, +1 minion, placed right away." Change "placed right away" to "placed after everyone else's minions." Same round: Dee owns Night Owls. Ava takes Last Call and waits; Dee places her last minion, then her extra minion goes after Ava's whole block. Ava no longer sees the final board. Dee gets a reason to buy a 6-purple member; Ava feels the sting. Goes well with **Closer** ("When you place the last minion of the round: +4 purple.").
- **2b. Sleep In** today: "+4 purple (+5 in round II, +6 in round III). Skip your next turn." Change the skip to "Your next turn moves to the end of the round." Same round: Cal takes Sleep In, gets +6 purple, and his next minion waits until everyone else, Ava's Last Call block included, has placed. One minion of his goes after her. This is a space anyone can take, so it's the cheapest answer, but it also hands every player a strong round III placement (my worry: it may become the new Last Call).
- **Needed for either:** when two players are both waiting to go last, the one who started waiting later goes last. That order is visible on the board, so a table can track it.

Claude's lean: 2a first (a member, so it costs something and only one player has it), and 1a if Last Call still reads too strong after.
