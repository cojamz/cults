# Every card in this game

## Spaces on the wheel

### Purple (purple). Contest: Most purple: the purple you hold when the round ends
- Sleep In (outer ring, opens round I): I: +4 purple | II: +5 purple | III: +6 purple Skip your next turn.
- Hold Up (outer ring, opens round I): I: +2 purple | II: +3 purple | III: +4 purple The next turn is skipped.
- Right Away (outer ring, opens round I): I: +1 purple | II: +2 purple | III: +3 purple Your next turn comes right after this one.
- Crowd In (center, opens round III): +3 purple. +1 minion this round.
- Last Call (middle ring, opens round II): +1 purple. +1 follower and +1 purple per minion you have left. Skip your turns until everyone else is out of minions.
- Crunch Time (middle ring, opens round II): +2 purple. Your next two turns come right after this one.

### Magenta (magenta). Contest: Most magenta: the magenta you hold when the round ends
- Help Yourself (outer ring, opens round I): I: Steal 3 resources. | II: Steal 4 resources. | III: Steal 5 resources. Split among rivals as you choose.
- Stash (outer ring, opens round I): I: +2 magenta. This turn, if you recruit a member, +1 follower. | II: +3 magenta. This turn, if you recruit a member, +2 followers. | III: +4 magenta. This turn, if you recruit a member, +3 followers.
- Lie Low (outer ring, opens round I): Steal 1. Rivals can’t steal or recruit from you this round. +1 magenta.
- Crime Wave (middle ring, opens round II): Steal half of one rival’s pile of one color, rounded down. +1 magenta.
- Shake Down (middle ring, opens round II): Choose a rival. They choose one of their members costing 5 or less, then pay you its cost or you steal it. +1 magenta.
- Poach (center, opens round III): Recruit a rival’s member you choose. +1 magenta.

### White (white). Contest: Most white: the white you hold when the round ends
- Allowance (outer ring, opens round I): I: +2 white. This turn, if you recruit a member, +1 follower. | II: +3 white. This turn, if you recruit a member, +2 followers. | III: +4 white. This turn, if you recruit a member, +3 followers.
- Splurge (outer ring, opens round I): This turn, if you recruit a member, +1 follower for every 2 resources you pay for it. +1 white.
- Exchange (outer ring, opens round I): I: Turn up to 1 resource into 2 followers. | II: Turn up to 2 resources into 2 followers each. | III: Turn up to 3 resources into 2 followers each. +1 white.
- Farewell (middle ring, opens round II): Cast out one of your members to the bottom of the pile, gain followers equal to its cost. +1 white.
- Estate Sale (middle ring, opens round II): Cast out one of your members to the bottom of the pile, gain white equal to its cost. +1 white.
- Cash In (center, opens round III): Turn all your white into followers. +1 white.

## Artifacts (one per color, picked before round I; the artifact comes with 1 resource of its color)

- Purple: Pocket Watch: Whenever a player’s turn is skipped, +1 purple.
- Magenta: Fence: Whenever you steal, +1 magenta.
- White: Money Changers: Whenever any player buys followers, +1 white.

## Members (cost: a colored part paid only in the member's color, plus an "any" part; "worth" is its followers; the shop deals them from one deck, cheaper ones first)

### Purple
- Early Birds — cost 2 purple + 1 any, worth 2 followers, counts as 2 members: Your turns can’t be skipped.
- Bouncers — cost 1 purple + 3 any, worth 2 followers, counts as 2 members: On Recruit: the next two rivals’ turns are skipped.
- Timekeepers — cost 1 purple + 3 any, worth 1 follower: Game end: +1 follower per purple member you have.
- Shift Crew — cost 2 purple + 2 any, worth 4 followers, counts as 4 members: Worth 4 followers.
- Clockmakers — cost 2 purple + 3 any, worth 2 followers, counts as 2 members: Round Start: take your first two turns in a row.
- Closer — cost 2 purple + 3 any, worth 1 follower: When you place the last minion of the round: +4 purple.
- Hot Streak — cost 2 purple + 3 any, worth 1 follower: On Use, once per game: +3 followers per turn in a row you’ve taken just before this one.
- Courier — cost 3 purple + 3 any, worth 1 follower: Whenever you take two turns in a row, +1 of each other color.
- Gossip — cost 3 purple + 3 any, worth 1 follower: Whenever a player’s turn is skipped, +1 follower.
- Night Owls — cost 6 purple + 2 any, worth 2 followers, counts as 2 members: Once per round: after you place your last minion, +1 minion, placed right away.

### Magenta
- Cutpurse — cost 1 magenta + 2 any, worth 1 follower: On Recruit: steal 1 resource from the rival with the most resources. Round Start: steal 1 resource from the rival with the most resources.
- Accomplice — cost 1 magenta + 2 any, worth 1 follower: On Recruit: steal 3 resources.
- Sticky Fingers — cost 2 magenta + 2 any, worth 1 follower: Your steals take 1 more resource.
- Kingpin — cost 1 magenta + 3 any, worth 1 follower: Game End: +1 follower per magenta member you have.
- Desperado — cost 2 magenta + 3 any, worth 1 follower: While you have the fewest followers (ties count), double what you steal from spaces.
- Extortionist — cost 4 magenta + 3 any, worth 1 follower: On Recruit: steal 2 resources from the rival with the most resources. Round Start: steal 2 resources from the rival with the most resources.
- Shoplifter — cost 3 magenta + 4 any, worth 1 follower: Whenever you recruit, steal 1 resource.
- Grifter — cost 2 magenta + 6 any, worth 1 follower: On Recruit: steal 1 resource per 2 members you have.
- Bodyguards — cost 5 magenta + 3 any, worth 2 followers, counts as 2 members: Rivals can’t steal from you.
- Double Agent — cost 3 magenta + 5 any, worth 1 follower: On Recruit: swap this member for a rival’s member you choose.

### White
- Cashier — cost 2 white + 1 any, worth 1 follower: On Recruit: turn all your white into followers.
- Recluse — cost 2 white + 2 any, worth 1 follower: Game End: +2 followers per rival with more members than you.
- Shopkeeper — cost 2 white + 2 any, worth 1 follower: On Recruit: +2 white. Round Start: +2 white.
- Martyrs — cost 2 white + 2 any, worth 3 followers, counts as 3 members: Whenever this member leaves your cult, gain followers equal to its cost.
- Brokers — cost 2 white + 3 any, worth 3 followers, counts as 3 members: Whenever a White action gives you followers, +1 follower.
- Raiders — cost 2 white + 3 any, worth 2 followers, counts as 2 members: On Use, once per game: steal 4 followers from the leader. Pay them 2 white.
- Old Money — cost 1 white + 5 any, worth 1 follower: Game End: +1 follower per white member you have.
- Exporters — cost 2 white + 5 any, worth 2 followers, counts as 2 members: Whenever you recruit a non-white member, +1 white.
- Hoarder — cost 4 white + 4 any, worth 1 follower: On Use, once per game: turn all your resources into white.
- Heiresses — cost 4 white + 5 any, worth 2 followers, counts as 2 members: On Use, once per game: +1 white per member you have.

### Guests (no color)
- Line Cutters — cost 2 any, worth 3 followers, counts as 3 members: You go first every round.
- Dabblers — cost 3 any, worth 2 followers, counts as 2 members: Counts as 2 wild members.
- Scouts — cost 3 any, worth 2 followers, counts as 2 members: On Use, once per round: send any shop members to the bottom of the pile, then refill.
- Party Crasher — cost 4 any, worth 1 follower: On Use, once per game: your next minion can be placed on a space a rival already took.
- Alchemists — cost 5 any, worth 3 followers, counts as 3 members: All your resources count as any when you recruit a member.
- Old Maid — cost 5 any, worth 1 follower: Whenever this member joins your cult, −5 followers.
- Anarchist — cost 7 any, worth 1 follower: On Use, once per round: swap any 2 members, in any cult or the shop, that cost within 2 of each other.
- Con Men — cost 7 any, worth 2 followers, counts as 2 members: Counts as 2 members. On Recruit: steal 5 resources.
- Extra Hand — cost 9 any, worth 1 follower: On Use, once per game: +1 minion this round.
- HR — cost 10 any, worth 4 followers, counts as 4 members: Counts as 4 members. Game End: +2 followers per color you have 3 members of.

## Buying followers

- 1 of anything → +1 followers (pay 1, any colors, as often as you like; one recruit each)
- 5 of anything → +5 followers (pay 5, any colors, as often as you like; one recruit each)
- 10 of anything → +10 followers (pay 10, any colors, as often as you like; one recruit each)
