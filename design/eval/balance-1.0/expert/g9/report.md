META: The swaps, the copies and the contest fights are the best part of the game; there's always something on the board to react to. What isn't working: copying piles up. Greatest Hits plus Reprise plus Mentor/Doppelganger, and Echo/Party Crasher/Tribute Band on Party Gifts and Largesse, mean the biggest turns are copies of the biggest spaces, and a big On Recruit cult runs away in round III. First change: stop copies (Echo, Reprise, Cover, Tribute Band) from repeating follower-granting spaces, or make Greatest Hits copy one On Recruit per Mentor-type card, not every one. Also settle whether Lie Low blocks Red's swaps; the rulebook and the game disagree.
