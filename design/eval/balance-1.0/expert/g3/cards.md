# Every card in this game

## Spaces on the wheel

### Red (red). Contest: Most red: the red you hold when the round ends
- Tryout (outer ring, opens round I): Use the On Recruit effect of a shop member; it stays in the shop. +2 red.
- Gather (outer ring, opens round I): I: +2 red. This turn, if you recruit a member, +1 follower. | II: +3 red. This turn, if you recruit a member, +2 followers. | III: +4 red. This turn, if you recruit a member, +3 followers.
- Walk-In (outer ring, opens round I): Recruit the top member of the deck. Each rival +1 red. +1 red.
- Promote (middle ring, opens round II): Swap a member with a shop or rival member costing up to 2 more. +3 red.
- Demote (middle ring, opens round II): Swap a member with a shop or rival member costing up to 2 less. +3 red.
- Recruit a Member (center, opens round III): Recruit a member in the shop costing 8 or less. +1 red.

### Magenta (magenta). Contest: Most magenta: the magenta you hold when the round ends
- Help Yourself (outer ring, opens round I): I: Steal 3 resources. | II: Steal 4 resources. | III: Steal 5 resources. Split among rivals as you choose.
- Stash (outer ring, opens round I): I: +2 magenta. This turn, if you recruit a member, +1 follower. | II: +3 magenta. This turn, if you recruit a member, +2 followers. | III: +4 magenta. This turn, if you recruit a member, +3 followers.
- Lie Low (outer ring, opens round I): Steal 1. Rivals can’t steal or recruit from you this round. +1 magenta.
- Crime Wave (middle ring, opens round II): Steal half of one rival’s pile of one color, rounded down. +1 magenta.
- Shake Down (middle ring, opens round II): Choose a rival. They choose one of their members costing 5 or less, then pay you its cost or you steal it. +1 magenta.
- Poach (center, opens round III): Recruit a rival’s member you choose. +1 magenta.

### Yellow (yellow). Contest: Most yellow: the yellow you hold when the round ends
- Harvest (outer ring, opens round I): I: +3 | II: +4 | III: +5
- Trade Seeds (outer ring, opens round I): +2 yellow, then swap up to 3 of your resources for other colors.
- Dilute (outer ring, opens round I): +2 yellow, then change up to 3 of rivals' resources one for one.
- Big Dilute (middle ring, opens round II): +4 yellow, then change up to 6 of rivals' resources one for one.
- Melting Pot (middle ring, opens round II): +1 per color among your members, in colors you choose. +2 yellow.
- Makeover (center, opens round III): +4 yellow, then rearrange your resources into any mix of colors.

### Green (green). Contest: Most green: the green you hold when the round ends
- Build Up (outer ring, opens round I): I: +3 green | II: +4 green | III: +5 green
- Echo (outer ring, opens round I): Copy the previous action. +1 green.
- Reprise (outer ring, opens round I): Copy an action with your minion. +1 green.
- Encore (middle ring, opens round II): Copy the On Recruit of a rival’s member. +1 green.
- Cover (middle ring, opens round II): Copy an action with no minion on it. +1 green.
- Greatest Hits (center, opens round III): Copy the On Recruit of each of your members, in any order. +1 green.

## Artifacts (one per color, picked before round I; the artifact comes with 1 resource of its color)

- Red: Recruiter: Whenever a member leaves a rival’s cult, +2 red.
- Magenta: Fence: Whenever you steal, +1 magenta.
- Yellow: Guest Book: Whenever you recruit a member of a color none of your members have, +2 yellow.
- Green: Impressionist: Whenever you copy an action, +1 of its color.

## Members (cost: a colored part paid only in the member's color, plus an "any" part; "worth" is its followers; the shop deals them from one deck, cheaper ones first)

### Red
- Volunteer — cost 1 any, worth 1 follower: Worth 1 follower.
- Triplets — cost 1 red + 2 any, worth 3 followers, counts as 3 members: Worth 3 followers.
- Recruit Bonus — cost 1 red + 2 any, worth 2 followers, counts as 2 members: Your On Recruits also give +1 of that member’s color.
- Deprogrammers — cost 1 red + 2 any, worth 4 followers, counts as 4 members: Whenever you take a rival’s member out of their cult, they lose 1 follower.
- Loyalists — cost 2 red + 3 any, worth 3 followers, counts as 3 members: Your other members can’t be swapped.
- Patriarch — cost 3 red + 3 any, worth 1 follower: Game End: +1 follower per 2 members you have.
- Panel — cost 4 red + 3 any, worth 3 followers, counts as 3 members: On Recruit: promote or demote another of your members.
- Proselytizers — cost 3 red + 5 any, worth 2 followers, counts as 2 members: Whenever you recruit, +1 follower.
- Mentor & Protégés — cost 5 red + 4 any, worth 3 followers, counts as 3 members: On Recruit: recruit the cheapest member in the shop.
- Mob — cost 7 red + 5 any, worth 5 followers, counts as 5 members: Game End: +1 follower per red member you have.

### Magenta
- Cutpurse — cost 1 magenta + 2 any, worth 1 follower: On Recruit: steal 1 resource from the rival with the most resources. Round Start: steal 1 resource from the rival with the most resources.
- Accomplice — cost 1 magenta + 2 any, worth 1 follower: On Recruit: steal 3 resources.
- Sticky Fingers — cost 2 magenta + 2 any, worth 1 follower: Your steals take 1 more resource.
- Kingpin — cost 1 magenta + 3 any, worth 1 follower: Game End: +1 follower per magenta member you have.
- Desperado — cost 2 magenta + 3 any, worth 1 follower: While you have the fewest followers (ties count), double what you steal from spaces.
- Extortionist — cost 4 magenta + 3 any, worth 1 follower: On Recruit: steal 2 resources from the rival with the most resources. Round Start: steal 2 resources from the rival with the most resources.
- Shoplifter — cost 3 magenta + 4 any, worth 1 follower: Whenever you recruit, steal 1 resource.
- Grifter — cost 2 magenta + 6 any, worth 1 follower: On Recruit: steal 1 resource per 2 members you have.
- Bodyguards — cost 5 magenta + 3 any, worth 2 followers, counts as 2 members: Rivals can’t steal from you.
- Double Agent — cost 3 magenta + 5 any, worth 1 follower: On Recruit: swap this member for a rival’s member you choose.

### Yellow
- Gardener — cost 2 yellow + 1 any, worth 1 follower: On Recruit: +1 of each color you have none of. Round Start: +1 of each color you have none of.
- Drifters — cost 2 yellow + 1 any, worth 2 followers, counts as 2 members: Worth 2 followers. On Recruit: choose a color; these count as a member of that color.
- Go-Between — cost 2 yellow + 2 any, worth 1 follower: Whenever a space gives you resources, you may change 1 of them to any color.
- Customs — cost 2 yellow + 4 any, worth 1 follower: Whenever a rival steals or changes any of your resources, +1 yellow.
- Ringleader — cost 3 yellow + 3 any, worth 1 follower: On Recruit: change 1 of each rival's resources one for one. +1 follower per rival changed.
- Tastemaker — cost 3 yellow + 3 any, worth 1 follower: Whenever you change rivals' resources, +1 follower.
- Harmonist — cost 4 yellow + 3 any, worth 1 follower: Game End: +1 follower per full set you hold.
- Dyer — cost 4 yellow + 3 any, worth 1 follower: On Recruit: +1 yellow per color you hold. Round Start: +1 yellow per color you hold.
- Big Tent — cost 3 yellow + 4 any, worth 1 follower: Game End: +2 followers per color you have exactly one member of.
- Caravan — cost 4 yellow + 5 any, worth 3 followers, counts as 3 members: Worth 3 followers. On Recruit: +1 of each color you hold. Round Start: +1 of each color you hold.

### Green
- Roadie — cost 1 green + 2 any, worth 1 follower: On Recruit: +1 green per green member you have. Round Start: +1 green per green member you have.
- Agents — cost 1 green + 2 any, worth 2 followers, counts as 2 members: Rivals can’t copy your actions.
- Busker — cost 1 green + 3 any, worth 1 follower: On Recruit: +2 green. Round Start: +2 green.
- Muse — cost 1 green + 3 any, worth 1 follower: Whenever a rival copies an action, +1 green.
- Stunt Double — cost 2 green + 3 any, worth 1 follower: On Recruit: copy the action you just took.
- Doppelganger — cost 2 green + 3 any, worth 1 follower: On Recruit: this member becomes a copy of another of your members.
- Chronicler — cost 1 green + 4 any, worth 1 follower: Game End: +1 follower per green member you have.
- Fan Club — cost 2 green + 4 any, worth 3 followers, counts as 3 members: On Recruit: +1 green per Green space with anyone’s minion.
- Wallflower — cost 6 green + 4 any, worth 1 follower: Whenever you copy an action, double the resources it gives you.
- Tribute Band — cost 8 green + 4 any, worth 3 followers, counts as 3 members: On Use, once per game: choose a rival. Do every action they have a minion on this round.

### Guests (no color)
- Line Cutters — cost 2 any, worth 3 followers, counts as 3 members: You go first every round.
- Dabblers — cost 3 any, worth 2 followers, counts as 2 members: Counts as 2 wild members.
- Scouts — cost 3 any, worth 2 followers, counts as 2 members: On Use, once per round: send any shop members to the bottom of the pile, then refill.
- Party Crasher — cost 4 any, worth 1 follower: On Use, once per game: your next minion can be placed on a space a rival already took.
- Alchemists — cost 5 any, worth 3 followers, counts as 3 members: All your resources count as any when you recruit a member.
- Old Maid — cost 5 any, worth 1 follower: Whenever this member joins your cult, −5 followers.
- Anarchist — cost 7 any, worth 1 follower: On Use, once per round: swap any 2 members, in any cult or the shop, that cost within 2 of each other.
- Con Men — cost 7 any, worth 2 followers, counts as 2 members: Counts as 2 members. On Recruit: steal 5 resources.
- Extra Hand — cost 9 any, worth 1 follower: On Use, once per game: +1 minion this round.
- HR — cost 10 any, worth 4 followers, counts as 4 members: Counts as 4 members. Game End: +2 followers per color you have 3 members of.

## Buying followers

- 1 of anything → +1 followers (pay 1, any colors, as often as you like; one recruit each)
- 5 of anything → +5 followers (pay 5, any colors, as often as you like; one recruit each)
- 10 of anything → +10 followers (pay 10, any colors, as often as you like; one recruit each)
