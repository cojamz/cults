# Going last, from first principles

## What a table can track without memory
One physical thing with one holder, like a first-player marker. A queue, a row, or "who started waiting first" all need the table to remember an order. So: **one Late token. Only its holder goes last.** Competing for last means taking the token.

## The one rule
"Whoever holds the Late token places the minions on it after everyone else is out of minions. If someone takes the token from you, your minions on it go back to taking turns as usual."
No out-of-turn placing, no order to remember: the token is in front of exactly one player, and the minions sitting on it are the count.

## The three cards (Claude's draft words; numbers unchanged)
- **Last Call** (today: "+1 purple. +1 follower and +1 purple per minion you have left. Skip your turns until everyone else is out of minions."): "+1 purple. +1 follower and +1 purple per minion you have left. Take the Late token and put your other minions on it."
- **Sleep In** (today: "+4 purple (+5 in round II, +6 in round III). Skip your next turn."): "+4 purple (+5 in round II, +6 in round III). Take the Late token and put your next minion on it."
- **Night Owls** (today: "Once per round: after you place your last minion, +1 minion, placed right away."): "Once per round: after you place your last minion, take the Late token with 1 more minion on it."

## One round III, 4 players, 5 minions each
- Ava's 1st minion: Last Call. +1 purple, +4 followers, +4 purple. She takes the token and puts her 4 other minions on it.
- Cal, a few turns later: Sleep In. +6 purple. He takes the token from Ava and puts his next minion on it. Ava's 4 minions go back to normal turns: she places them in seat order with everyone else, so she no longer sees the final board. She keeps the followers and purple she already got.
- Dee places her last minion; she owns Night Owls, so she takes the token from Cal with 1 extra minion on it. Cal's minion goes back to his normal turn.
- Everyone else runs out. Dee places her Night Owls minion last, with every pile final.

Who feels it: Ava is stung (paid for last place, lost it); Cal and Dee get a real reason to take Sleep In or buy Night Owls; everyone can see one token and who has it.

## Fallback
Keep going last as Last Call's alone, and Night Owls and Sleep In keep today's text. Simplest table, but Last Call stays unanswered.
