# Counterplay in the 12 expert AI games: players' skill, or missing counters?

Sources: the 12 expert AI-player games (four with 4 players, three with 3, one with 2; deck of 10 per color), every player's reasoning and postgame, the rulebook, and the live cards. Card text below is the live text. Three things changed after some of these games were played: Promote and Demote briefly swapped only your own members (the two games on that build, the Red/Magenta/Green/Silver game and the Red/Yellow/Blue game, still played rival swaps) and then went back to any cult; a second copy of Last Call no longer pays skips; Lie Low now says plainly that swaps go through. None of these changes the findings.

## The short answer

I went through 30 decisive offensive plays. For each one I checked the board just before it: what the victims could have done, whether anyone saw it coming, and whether they did it.

- **Counter existed and was used: 5**
- **Counter existed but was missed (players' error): 12**
- **Counter existed but cost too much: 3**
- **No counter on the board: 10**

So it is both, split by kind of play. Most of the misses are around **Last Call**, member theft, and Party Crasher, and in those cases the players could have done better. The ten with no counter fall into four places, listed at the end. In those places a careful player still had nothing to do.

One more pattern: in every game with Magenta on the table, the winner was a player who could not be stolen from in round III. Four of the six were won by whoever held Lie Low in round III, a fifth by the player who bought Bodyguards, and in the sixth the Lie Low holder came second to a Last Call run. Protection decided more of those games than any single attack did.

## Every decisive play, by kind

### Last Call closing runs (5 plays: 2 used, 3 missed)

**Last Call** reads: "+1 purple. +1 follower and +1 purple per minion you have left. Skip your turns until everyone else is out of minions."

1. **One AI game (Red, Purple, Silver, Yellow), round III.** Ava took Last Call on her first placement and swept all four +10 contests on her last four minions (6-5 red, 5-3 purple, 4-3 silver, 3-2 yellow). Before her, Cal had two placements (he took Largesse and Demote) and Dee had one (Toast). Neither mentioned Last Call. Cal said afterward that "the only real counter was taking Last Call myself on my round III double." Once she had it, Ben tried to hold 8 red and she beat it with Dilute, Promote and Trade Seeds. **Missed.**
2. **One AI game (Purple, Silver, Blue, White), round III.** Ben, last at 36 followers, took Last Call after Dee (two placements) and Ava had passed it up. He ran Party Crasher onto Largesse for +10, then Extra Hand into Farewell for 9, and won 102 to 101. Cal had **Extra Hand** ("On Use, once per game: +1 minion this round.") in reach and bought 5 followers instead. By his own count, either Extra Hand or Farewell on his Shopkeeper wins the game. **Missed.** (Party Crasher itself reached the shop during Ben's run, so nobody else could have bought it.)
3. **One AI game (Silver, Purple, White, Magenta), round III.** Ben took Last Call after Cal (Lie Low) and Ava (Largesse). He had held **Party Crasher** ("On Use, once per game: your next minion can be placed on a space a rival already took.") since round II, where everyone could see it. Then he landed on Dee's Toast, fired **Debt Collectors** ("On Use, once per game: each rival gives you 2 silver, if they have it."), added **Night Owls** ("Once per round: after you place your last minion, +1 minion, placed right away."), and took silver 6 to 5. During his ten-turn gap Ben's purple pile and his Party Crasher were both exposed. **Poach** ("Recruit a rival's member you choose. +1 magenta.") was open, and Cal used it on Ava's Extra Hand instead. **Crime Wave** ("Steal half of one rival's pile of one color, rounded down. +1 magenta.") was open, and Dee used it on Ava's silver. Dee: "I had Party Crasher and Debt Collectors in plain view and still didn't price them into his reach." **Missed.**
4. **One AI game (Purple, Magenta, White), round III.** Ava took Last Call first. While she had no turns, Ben Poached her Line Cutters, used **Shake Down** ("Choose a rival. They choose one of their members costing 5 or less, then pay you its cost or you steal it. +1 magenta.") to take her Closer, and hate-drafted Hot Streak so she couldn't use it. Ava went from leading at 26 to third. **Used.**
5. **One two-player AI game (Green, Purple), round III.** Ben opened on Last Call so he would place after Ava's Tribute Band. Ava used **Echo** ("Copy the previous action. +1 green.") on his Last Call, which made her go last too. **Used** (by Ava, against Ben's counter).

What this means: in the three missed cases, the counter was to take Last Call before the eventual taker. That moves the closing run to another player but does not answer it. Once someone has placed on Last Call, the only answers I found were Magenta hitting the taker during the gap (case 4), a Green copy of it (case 5), and Extra Hand (case 2). On a table with no Magenta or Green (cases 1 and 2), nothing on the board touches the closer.

### Largesse, and copying or joining it (3 plays: 3 no counter)

**Largesse** reads: "Rivals gain up to 10 silver, split as you choose. +1 follower for each."

6. **One AI game (Purple, Silver, Blue, White), round III, the last placement of the game.** Ben used Party Crasher to join Dee's Largesse. That gave him +10 followers without using a recruit, and the 10 silver went to Ava, which tied Dee's silver and cost Dee 5. No card stops a Party Crasher join. Party Crasher came into the shop during Ben's own closing run, while everyone else was out of minions. Dee could only have defended silver with a lead of more than 10. **No counter.**
7. **One AI game (Magenta, Green, Silver, Purple), round III.** Largesse was used four times for 40 followers: Ava took it (she placed first because she had Line Cutters), Cal Echoed it, Ava used **Reprise** ("Copy an action with your minion. +1 green.") on her own Largesse, and Ben joined it with the Party Crasher he had held since round I. That last join handed 10 silver to Cal and took silver from Ava. **Agents** ("Rivals can't copy your actions.") would have stopped Cal's Echo, but it was never dealt in this game. Agents doesn't stop the owner's own Reprise or a Party Crasher join anyway. Ben was under **Lie Low** ("Steal 1. Rivals can't steal or recruit from you this round. +1 magenta."), so nobody could Poach or Shake Down his Party Crasher, and there was no Red to swap it away. Ben also used **Stunt Double** ("On Recruit: copy the action you just took.") on **Crowd In** ("+3 purple. +1 minion this round.") to get two extra minions. Ava had wanted Crowd In and didn't take it in time. **No counter** for the Largesse copies and the join.
8. **One AI game (Red, Magenta, Green, Silver), round III.** Ben took Largesse knowing "Cal can Echo either one", and Cal Echoed it for +10. The only Agents in the game was in Ava's cult (bought in round II), so Ben could not protect his action. Later in that round Dee used **Tribute Band** ("On Use, once per game: choose a rival. Do every action they have a minion on this round.") on Ben's spaces, Largesse and Party Gifts included, for about +20. She chose Ben because Ava's Agents blocked copying Ava. **No counter on the board** for Ben. The counter existed in the game, but only one player could own it, and that came down to the deal.

### Party Crasher on another space (1 play: missed)

9. **One AI game (Red, Yellow, Blue), round III, the last placement.** Ben used Party Crasher to join Cal's **Makeover** ("+4 yellow, then rearrange your resources into any mix of colors."). He rearranged 19 resources into 7 red, 8 yellow and 4 blue, which beat Cal's red by one and Ava's yellow by one, for +30. Party Crasher had been in the shop at the start of round III, where any player could have bought it, and Ben's own refill brought it back. After that it sat in Ben's cult in plain view. Ava had counted Ben's reach without it, and so had Cal. Holding one or two more yellow and red would have cost each of them one or two followers against the +10 they lost. **Missed.**

### Copied steals that land before the victim can move (3 plays: 3 no counter)

**Con Men** reads: "Counts as 2 members. On Recruit: steal 5 resources." **Encore** reads: "Copy the On Recruit of a rival's member. +1 green." **Greatest Hits** reads: "Copy the On Recruit of each of your members, in any order. +1 green."

10. **One AI game (Blue, Magenta, White, Green), round III.** Ava placed first (Line Cutters) and took Lie Low. Then Ben used Encore on her Con Men and took 5 of Dee's green, and Cal Echoed Ben's Encore and took 5 of Dee's white. Later Ava's Greatest Hits fired Con Men and Accomplice for an 8-steal that left every rival at zero, and nobody could steal back from her. Dee had no placement before the first two steals. Lie Low was gone at the first placement. **Bodyguards** ("Rivals can't steal from you.") was not in the round III shop. Agents does not stop Encore, because Encore copies a member's On Recruit, not an action. Ben and Cal used the one counter left, which was cashing out (Ben bought 10, Cal took Spree), and that handed Ava the contests. **No counter.**
11. **One AI game (Yellow, Green, Blue), round III.** Ava bought Con Men and stole 5 of Ben's green. She then bought Agents so the copies would stop, and Cal used Encore on her Con Men anyway for another 5. Ben had 21 resources from round II and no turn before either steal. With no Magenta on the table, there was no Lie Low and no Bodyguards. Ben did answer with Echo on Cal's Encore and stole 5 back, but he still lost the green contest and the game by 3. **No counter** to the first two steals.
12. **One AI game (Magenta, Green, Silver, Purple), round II.** On her first placement of the round, Ava used Crime Wave on Ben's 14 green and then Con Men for 5 more. Ben's pile came from round I and from the round start of his Busker and Roadie. Round-start income arrives before anyone places, so he never had a turn to spend or shield it. **No counter.**

### Big steals: Desperado, Crime Wave, Help Yourself (5 plays: 1 no counter, 3 missed, 1 too costly)

**Desperado** reads: "While you have the fewest followers (ties count), double what you steal from spaces." **Help Yourself** reads: "Steal 3 resources in round I, 4 in round II, 5 in round III, split among rivals as you choose."

13. **One AI game (Red, Magenta, Yellow, Green), round III, first placement.** Ava had the fewest followers, so she placed first and her Desperado was on. Her Help Yourself stole 10, including all 8 of Cal's red, which he had held to win round II's red contest and kept for round III. Cal had no turn in between, Lie Low needs a placement, and Bodyguards never showed up in this game. **No counter.** Later in that round, Cal Poached Ben's Triplets partly so that Ben, not Ava, had the fewest followers, which switched off her Desperado. That was a counter, used after the damage.
14. **Same game, round II.** Ava's Crime Wave, doubled by Desperado, took all of Ben's green. Desperado had been in Ava's cult since early in round II. Ben: "didn't count Desperado doubling." **Missed.**
15. **One AI game (Red, Magenta, Green, Silver), round III.** Ava's Desperado Help Yourself took Dee's whole red pile for 10. Dee had given Desperado to Ava herself in round II, with a Demote that swapped Desperado for Ava's Triplets. She also had a placement just before and could have spent the red. **Missed.**
16. **Same game, round III.** Ben's Crime Wave halved Cal's 13 silver. Cal saw it coming ("Crime Wave on me is the big risk") but held the silver for the silver contest and his Debt Collectors plan. Ava had Lie Low and Dee had Bodyguards, so Cal was one of only two players anyone could steal from. **Too costly**: the only defense was to spend the silver, which meant giving up the silver contest.
17. **One AI game (Magenta, Green, Silver, Purple), round III.** Dee lost 22 silver before her second turn, to Cover into Help Yourself, Help Yourself, Debt Collectors and Encore on Con Men. After Ava bought the only Bodyguards and Ben took Lie Low, the thieves could only hit Cal and Dee. Dee: "I should have converted the Toast silver even harder in round II or bought protection instead of Debutantes." **Missed** (she could have spent it), although both protection cards were already gone.

### Member theft: Shake Down and Poach (6 plays: 4 missed, 2 too costly)

18. **One AI game (Blue, Magenta, White, Green), round II.** Ava used Shake Down on Ben, then Reprise on her own Shake Down, and took Martyrs and then Line Cutters. Ben had 1 white, because earlier steals had stripped him, so he couldn't pay either cost. Cal had already taken Lie Low, and Bodyguards cost 8, which Ben didn't have. **Too costly**: to pay a Shake Down you have to hold 4 or 5 resources, and on that table anything held got stolen.
19. **Same game, round III.** Dee Poached Cal's **Snobs** ("Game End: +1 follower per blue member you have.") and then used Reprise on her own Poach to take his Patroness. One turn earlier Cal had used Echo for a 5-steal, when he could have placed on Poach or Reprise himself. He didn't consider either. **Missed.**
20. **One AI game (Purple, Magenta, White), round III.** Ben Poached Ava's Line Cutters and Shake Down took her Closer while she was waiting out her Last Call. Ava had cashed out to 2 resources on purpose, against resource theft ("ten rival turns of Crime Wave and Help Yourself"). Being broke made Shake Down free, and Lie Low was already Cal's. **Too costly** for Ava (this is case 4 from her side).
21. **One AI game (Silver, Purple, White, Magenta), round II.** Dee used Shake Down on Ava's spent Accomplice. It fired its steal again for Dee and took 3 silver and Ava's +6. Ava chose not to pay and later called that a mistake. **Missed.**
22. **Same game, round III.** Cal Poached Ava's unused Extra Hand and used it himself. Ava: "sitting on Extra Hand into round III." **Missed** (she could have used it first).
23. **One AI game (Red, Magenta, Green, Silver), round III.** Ava Poached Cal's unused Debt Collectors and fired it, which turned a 20-20 silver tie into her +10. Cal had been saving it for after a Crime Wave. **Missed.**

### Red swaps (5 plays: 3 no counter, 2 used)

**Promote** reads: "Swap a member with a shop or rival member costing up to 2 more. +3 red." **Demote** reads: "Swap a member with a shop or rival member costing up to 2 less. +3 red." **Recruiter** (Red's artifact) reads: "Whenever a member leaves a rival's cult, +2 red." **Loyalists** reads: "Your other members can't be swapped."

24. **One AI game (Red, Purple, Silver, Yellow), rounds II and III.** On a snake double turn in round II, Cal Promoted Ava's **Deprogrammers** ("Whenever you take a rival's member out of their cult, they lose 1 follower.") and Demoted her Line Cutters, with Recruiter paying +2 red each time. In round III he Demoted her HR on his first two placements. Loyalists never appeared in this game. In round II Ava placed just before Cal and could have blocked one of the two swap spaces, but Cal's double turn would still have taken the other. In round III Cal placed first. **No counter** (only a half block, which was missed). Three players also called the rival swaps a bug in their postgames. They are your logged call (any cult), so this is a feel report, not a fault.
25. **One AI game (White, Red, Yellow), round II.** Cal, on a double turn, used Demote and then Promote on Ava. He took Line Cutters, and Recruiter turned her 8-4 red lead into his 14-8. Loyalists never appeared in this game. Ava's Harvest came just before and could have blocked one of the two spaces. **No counter** (same half block).
26. **One AI game (Red, Magenta, Green, Silver), round II.** Dee's Demote took Ava's Triplets while Ava was under Lie Low. Lie Low doesn't stop swaps, and Loyalists reached the shop only late in round III. **No counter.**
27. **One AI game (Red, Magenta, Yellow, Green), round III.** Ava used Demote to push **Old Maid** ("Whenever this member joins your cult, −5 followers.") into Dee's cult in exchange for Dee's Loyalists, a swing of 7 or 8. Dee owned the counter, but it doesn't protect itself. **Used**, and beaten through a gap in its own text.
28. **One AI game (Red, Yellow, Blue), round III.** Ben used **Anarchist** ("On Use, once per round: swap any 2 members, in any cult or the shop, that cost within 2 of each other.") to put Old Maid on Cal, then Promoted Cal's Patroness. Cal answered by Demoting Old Maid back onto Ben's unprotected Loyalists. **Used**: a real back-and-forth.

### Copy engines (2 plays: 1 missed, 1 used)

29. **One AI game (Red, Magenta, Green, Silver), round III.** Dee used Greatest Hits to fire **Mentor & Protégés** ("On Recruit: recruit the cheapest member in the shop.") twice, through a **Doppelganger** ("On Recruit: this member becomes a copy of another of your members.") copying it, and used Reprise on her own Greatest Hits to do it all again. Then she bought Bodyguards with 25 green and used Tribute Band to finish. She won by 41. Between her Greatest Hits and her Reprise, only Ava placed (a double turn), and she could have blocked Reprise; she took Lie Low and Help Yourself instead. Poach was open all round and nobody used it on Mentor & Protégés or the Doppelganger before Bodyguards shut it off. Bodyguards itself came into the shop mid-round from a refill. **Missed**, with the shop's luck finishing it.
30. **The two-player AI game (Green, Purple), round III.** Ben saw Tribute Band coming ("Placing last is about the only defense") and opened on Last Call. Ava's Echo of his Last Call beat that, and her Tribute Band replayed six of his purple spaces. The only Agents was Ava's. To get Echo first, Ben would have had to give up his Last Call opening. **Used, and beaten.**

### Tally by kind

- Last Call closing runs: 5 (2 used, 3 missed). The misses were all "take it first", so a race for the seat, not an answer to it. On tables without Magenta or Green there is no answer once it's placed.
- Largesse copied or joined: 3 (3 no counter).
- Party Crasher on other spaces: 1 (1 missed).
- Copied steals and steals at a round's opening: 3 (3 no counter).
- Big steals (Desperado, Crime Wave, Help Yourself): 5 (1 no counter, 3 missed, 1 too costly).
- Member theft (Shake Down, Poach): 6 (4 missed, 2 too costly).
- Red swaps: 5 (3 no counter, 2 used).
- Copy engines (Greatest Hits, Tribute Band): 2 (1 missed, 1 used).

Mostly the players' skill: Last Call (as a race), member theft, Party Crasher accounting, and Desperado or Crime Wave on piles the victim could have spent. Mostly missing counters: Largesse copies and joins, steals at a round's opening, and Red swaps when Loyalists isn't dealt.

## Where the game most lacks a counter

### 1. Piles hit before their owner gets a turn

**The turn:** in one AI game (Red, Magenta, Yellow, Green), Cal held 8 red to win round II's red contest, which is what the contest asks for. Round III opened with Ava, who placed first because she was last on followers. Her Desperado Help Yourself took all 8 before Cal could move. Two other cases fit the same pattern: Ben's 21 resources in one AI game (Yellow, Green, Blue), which Con Men and Encore hit for 10 before his first turn, and Ben's 14 green from round-start income in another (Magenta, Green, Silver, Purple), which Crime Wave and Con Men hit on Ava's first placement.

Contests reward holding at round end, round-start members add to your pile before anyone places, and the next round's first player is the one most likely to have Desperado switched on. The victim gets no turn. Lie Low needs a placement and only one player can have it each round.

**Counters that exist but are hard to reach:**
- Bodyguards ("Rivals can't steal from you."): Magenta tables only, one copy, costs 8 (5 magenta + 3 any). It reached the shop in 5 of the 12 games and was bought in 3.
- **Line Cutters** ("You go first every round."): the only way to act before the first thief. One copy, and it changes hands by Shake Down, Poach and swaps.
- **Snitch** ("Whenever a rival steals from you, they lose 1 follower."): never dealt in these 12 games.
- **Customs** ("Whenever a rival steals or changes any of your resources, +1 yellow."): Yellow tables only, and it softens a steal rather than stopping it. Two players got it free from Walk-In (in the Red, Yellow, Blue game it made Ben drop a Big Dilute on Ava). In two other games it sat in the shop and nobody bought it.
- Agents looks like it should stop Encore copying Con Men, and the player in the Yellow, Green, Blue game bought it for that reason. It doesn't, because Encore copies a member, not an action.

### 2. Largesse when it is copied or joined

**The turn:** in one AI game (Magenta, Green, Silver, Purple), round III, Largesse was used four times: Ava, Cal's Echo, Ava's own Reprise, then Ben's Party Crasher at the end. That was 40 followers, and Ben's join gave 10 silver to Cal and took the silver contest from Ava. In the Purple, Silver, Blue, White game the same join on the last placement tied Dee's silver and decided the winner by one follower.

Party Crasher has no counter card. Each join also hands out 10 silver, and only a silver lead of more than 10 survives that.

**Counters that exist but are hard to reach:**
- Agents ("Rivals can't copy your actions."): one copy, dealt in 4 of the 12 games, and not in the game where Largesse paid 40. It stops Echo, Cover and Tribute Band, but not the taker's own Reprise and not a Party Crasher join.
- Blocking the copy spaces (Echo, Reprise, Cover) by placing there first, which costs a placement.
- Burying Party Crasher while it's in the shop: **Savings** ("+2 blue in round I, +3 in round II, +4 in round III. Before you recruit, you may send one shop member to the bottom of the pile, then refill."), **Bury** ("Send a shop member to the bottom of the pile, then refill. Gain blue equal to half its cost, rounded down. +1 blue."), **Out of Style** ("Send every shop member of one color to the bottom of the pile, then refill. +1 blue per member sent. +1 blue."), **Scouts** ("On Use, once per round: send any shop members to the bottom of the pile, then refill."). These need Blue on the table or Scouts in the shop, and in the Red, Yellow, Blue game a buried Party Crasher came back.
- Taking Party Crasher from whoever holds it: Poach, Shake Down, Promote, Demote, Anarchist. Lie Low on the holder blocks Poach and Shake Down, as it did for Ben.

### 3. Red swaps when Loyalists isn't dealt

**The turn:** in one AI game (White, Red, Yellow), round II, Cal used Demote and then Promote on a snake double turn. He took Ava's Line Cutters, gave her cheaper members back, and Recruiter paid him +2 red on each swap, turning her 8-4 red lead into his 14-8. The Red, Purple, Silver, Yellow game had the same pattern, three times across rounds II and III. Loyalists didn't appear in either game.

Lie Low and Bodyguards don't stop swaps. A single placement can block only one of the two swap spaces, and the swapper often has a double turn at the end of the snake.

**Counters that exist but are hard to reach:**
- Loyalists ("Your other members can't be swapped."): one copy. It reached the shop in 3 of the 5 games with Red, and late in round III in one of those. It doesn't protect itself, and Old Maid was pushed through that gap twice.
- Swapping back with your own Promote, Demote, **Panel** ("On Recruit: promote or demote another of your members.") or Anarchist. This works when you have one and a red space is left, as in the Red, Yellow, Blue game.
- Taking Promote or Demote before the swapper does, which blocks only half.

### 4. Last Call once it has been placed, on tables without Magenta or Green

**The turn:** in one AI game (Red, Purple, Silver, Yellow), round III, Ava's Last Call gave her four placements after everyone else was out, and she took all four +10 contests by margins of one or two. With no Magenta or Green on the table, nothing could reach her during the gap. Her turns were already being skipped, so **Hold Up** ("+2 purple (+3 in round II, +4 in round III). The next turn is skipped.") did nothing to her. Every resource a rival held to guard a contest was a follower not bought, and she had four tries to beat it.

The counter players missed was taking Last Call first. That only changes who gets the closing run.

**Counters that exist but are hard to reach:**
- Hitting the taker during the gap with Poach, Shake Down, Crime Wave or Help Yourself. These are Magenta only, and they worked in the Purple, Magenta, White game.
- **Dilute** ("+2 yellow, then change up to 3 of rivals' resources one for one."), **Big Dilute** ("+4 yellow, then change up to 6 of rivals' resources one for one.") and Makeover change the taker's pile. These are Yellow only, and the taker can still answer afterward.
- Echo on Last Call to go last as well (Green only, and only right after it is placed).
- Extra Hand, Crowd In or Night Owls for a placement late in the round. In the Purple, Magenta, White game, Crowd In's extra minion landed before the Last Call player, not after, so only Extra Hand and Party Crasher reliably come after the closer.

## Two smaller notes

- **Raiders** ("On Use, once per game: steal 4 followers from the leader. Pay them 2 white.") and Debt Collectors go through both Lie Low and Bodyguards. Two players thought they were shielded and lost followers or silver anyway (Ben in the Purple, Magenta, White game; Cal in the Silver, Purple, White, Magenta game). Those cards read as counters to these plays, but they are not.
- Many of the misses were a player holding an unused once-per-game member (Extra Hand, Debt Collectors, Accomplice) into a round where Poach or Shake Down was open. That is players' skill, but it happened in three different games.
