# Cults, deck 10: how the whole game feels (balance-1.0 meta read)

Instruments: Normal (800 games), Hard (142) and Very Hard (42, a lead only) bots; the choice log (Hard, Very Hard); the feel probe (Normal, 2-4 players); 16 expert AI players over 4 games; the Nov 1 AI bug playtest. All 4 players unless marked. Red's numbers carry the Promote/Demote bug (rival-to-rival swaps), so Red is left out of every verdict below.

## What's working

1. **The contest core.** All 16 expert META notes open with it ("every round ended with real fights over who holds what"). The choice log agrees: the strong bots' close calls cluster in hold-or-spend choices, not spaces (Very Hard: buying 1 follower and passing are the only REAL CHOICE tags, 12-13% close, 18% in round I).
2. **Nobody is out of it early.** Round I leader wins 27% (probe, even 25%); last after round II still wins 14%. Margins shrink as play gets better: 10.3 Normal, 8.6 Hard, 6.3 Very Hard. Expert g4 came back from 45 behind.
3. **Wide, live turns.** 11.9 open spaces per placement, 4% forced, 3% dead (probe).
4. **Colors read as their fantasy.** Nearly every space got 8/8 "fits its color" from the experts. Their top cards are the "value from the table" designs Cory likes: **Last Call** ("+1 purple. +1 follower and +1 purple per minion you have left. Skip your turns until everyone else is out of minions.") 5.0 strategic / 4.3 fun, **Lie Low** ("Steal 1. Rivals can't steal or recruit from you this round. +1 magenta.") 4.8/3.5, **Echo** ("Copy the previous action. +1 green.") 4.4/3.5.

## What isn't working as well

1. **Copies multiply steals.** **Echo**, **Reprise** ("Copy an action with your minion. +1 green."), **Cover** ("Copy an action with no minion on it. +1 green."), **Encore** ("Copy the On Recruit of a rival's member. +1 green.") and **Greatest Hits** ("Copy the On Recruit of each of your members, in any order. +1 green.") replay **Con Men** ("Counts as 2 members. On Recruit: steal 5 resources."), **Poach** ("Recruit a rival's member you choose. +1 magenta.") and **Shake Down** ("Choose a rival. They choose one of their members costing 5 or less, then pay you its cost or you steal it. +1 magenta."). Evidence: all 8 experts in g2 and g3 named this first; the copy spaces gain 8.0-8.5 per placement in round III against 5.4 average (probe); Nov 1 game 2 was won by 52 copying steals onto one rival, who ended with 0 members. Who feels it: the trailing player, stripped with "nothing to do and nothing to lose" (g2 Ben, 35 followers).
2. **Round III is decided by who places last.** Last Call is strong at every level (lift Normal +6, Hard +16, Very Hard +14), and in g1 its taker placed four times after everyone was out and won all four +10 contests by one each: 40 followers. **Party Crasher** ("On Use, once per game: your next minion can be placed on a space a rival already took.") is Hard +13 and let g4 jump 36 to 102 in one round. 8 of 16 experts named the closing run. Who feels it: everyone else, who can only watch.
3. **The round III shop goes quiet.** Round III buys: followers 51%, members 41% (probe); experts in 5 of 16 notes say buying 10 beats most members. But the followers-only seat wins under even (Hard 13%, Normal 20%), so members still carry the game; it's round III that loses them. Small cults stay short of the bar: winners with 5 or fewer members 10% Normal, 13% Hard, 20% Very Hard.
4. **Round I has a low floor.** 2.8 gain per placement, 9% dead, 45% of round I recruits pass (probe). **Right Away** ("+1 purple (+2 in round II, +3 in round III). Your next turn comes right after this one.") is dead in 100% of round I placements (probe), experts 1.4/1.4; **Splurge** ("This turn, if you recruit a member, +1 follower for every 2 resources you pay for it. +1 white.") is dead 100% in every round, experts 1.4/1.6; **Crunch Time** ("+2 purple. Your next two turns come right after this one.") 1.0/1.0 from experts. Meanwhile **Help Yourself** ("Steal 3 resources in round I, 4 in round II, 5 in round III, split among rivals as you choose.") is taken 95% of the times it's open in round I (Hard), 96% (Very Hard). Who feels it: whoever picks last in round I.
5. **Giving pays the giver more than the gift.** **Party Gifts** ("Rivals gain up to 3 silver, split as you choose. +2 followers for each.") gains 7.0 in round I against 2.8 average; **Largesse** ("Rivals gain up to 10 silver, split as you choose. +1 follower for each.") 11.0 in round III (probe). Experts: fun, but "sends silver to whoever isn't racing you, which kingmakes" (g1 Ben). At 2 players Silver drops to lift -32 (Normal), since the gift goes to your only rival. Who feels it: the rival not chosen.

## Where to focus design revisions

1. **Copies versus steals.** Directions: a copy gets the printed resource gain but not a steal or member-take; or a copied steal must hit a different rival than the original. A player: Green still rides the best space; the trailing seat stops being farmed.
2. **The round III closing run.** Directions: bound Last Call's end block in round III so others still have placements after it (keep the trade Cory loved); keep Party Crasher off center spaces. A player: the last +10s stay a fight; going last is a bet, not a sweep.
3. **Round I floor, then round III members.** Directions: lift the dead round I spaces with a hook rather than more resources; give round III members a reason to beat a 10-follower buy (more members counted, or a payoff for recruiting now). A player: no wasted first turns, and the shop peaks when piles do. (My guess: the second half also moves the small-cult number.)

## Where the instruments disagree

Green: experts and the Nov 1 game call copies the strongest thing on the wheel, yet Green as a main color is Normal +4, Hard +6, Very Hard -15. Bots don't aim copy chains at one victim, so on feel I'd trust the experts. **Fence** ("Whenever you steal, +1 magenta.") is Very Hard -19 but won Nov 1 game 2 by 52: same reason. **Cash In** ("Turn all your white into followers. +1 white.") is dead 84% for Normal (it never banks white) but Very Hard +16: Normal misjudges timing cards, as it does Last Call (MISJUDGED in the Hard log). Where Hard, Very Hard and experts agree (Last Call, copy-on-steal), call it a finding. Where only Normal speaks, treat it as a lead. On whether something feels bad, trust experts and human play over any win rate. Rerun Red after the Promote/Demote fix before judging it.
