# Cults, deck 10: how the game feels (balance-1.0 meta read, 12 expert games)

Sources: 12 expert AI games (g1-g12, 42 players), their ratings (strategic / fun, 1 to 5) and end-of-game notes, the bug log, interim bot runs (Normal 800, Hard 142, Very Hard 42 games, 4 players). Red's bot numbers ran on a swap change since reverted, so none is used. Misclick-bent ratings (a tool bug, fixed Nov 1) are discounted.

## What's working

1. **The contests.** 33 of 42 notes call holding a color to round end the heart of the game. 6 of 12 games were won by 12 or less; g4 by 1.
2. **Red's swaps.** **Promote** ("Swap a member with a shop or rival member costing up to 2 more. +3 red.") rates 4.1 strategic, **Demote** ("Swap a member with a shop or rival member costing up to 2 less. +3 red.") 4.2. 8 players in 4 of 5 Red games (g1, g9, g10, g12) named them among the best decisions. They stay as you set them.
3. **Spaces that read the table.** **Echo** ("Copy the previous action. +1 green.") 4.4 / 3.8; **Crime Wave** ("Steal half of one rival's pile of one color, rounded down. +1 magenta.") "the best counter to hoarding" (g9 Ben). Every space was called a fit for its color by at least three quarters of its raters.
4. **The last-seat puzzle, when it can be counted.** All four g3 players named it the best part. **Last Call** ("+1 purple. +1 follower and +1 purple per minion you have left. Skip your turns until everyone else is out of minions.") is the top-rated space, 4.6 / 3.9.

## What isn't

1. **Copies multiply whatever is biggest and flat.** All 6 games with Green (g2, g3, g5, g8, g9, g11) named copying a top problem (21 notes). The target shifts: **Con Men** ("Counts as 2 members. On Recruit: steal 5 resources.") through **Encore** ("Copy the On Recruit of a rival's member. +1 green.") in g2, g3, g5, g9; silver's gifts in g9, g11; an On Recruit engine through **Greatest Hits** ("Copy the On Recruit of each of your members, in any order. +1 green.") in g9, won by 41. The verb isn't the complaint (Echo is Green's best-rated space); what it repeats is. Bots don't aim chains at one target, so Green's bot numbers (Very Hard −15) don't count against this.
2. **Largesse out-earns any contest.** **Largesse** ("Rivals gain up to 10 silver, split as you choose. +1 follower for each.") paid 10 four times in g11's round III through Echo, **Reprise** ("Copy an action with your minion. +1 green.") and **Party Crasher** ("On Use, once per game: your next minion can be placed on a space a rival already took."); in g9 three times plus **Tribute Band** ("On Use, once per game: choose a rival. Do every action they have a minion on this round."). All 5 Silver games flagged the gift spaces, **Party Gifts** ("Rivals gain up to 3 silver, split as you choose. +2 followers for each.") too; all 8 g9 and g11 players made it their first change. Whoever gets the 10 silver wins silver (g1 Ben, g11 Ben). They're fun to pick (3.8, 3.9); the size is the issue. Bots (Very Hard +9, Hard 0) don't copy it.
3. **Round III's last placements break the count.** In 5 of 12 games (g1, g4, g7, g10, g11) the winner's deciding swing came in their closing placements, from a card that acts after everyone or on a taken space: Last Call's block, Party Crasher onto a taken **Makeover** ("+4 yellow, then rearrange your resources into any mix of colors.") for all three contests (g10, +30), **Stunt Double** ("On Recruit: copy the action you just took.") on **Crowd In** ("+3 purple. +1 minion this round.") then Party Crasher into Largesse (g11). 12 players' first change was here. Bots agree: Last Call strong at all levels (Normal +6, Hard +16, Very Hard +14), Party Crasher Hard +13.
4. **Protection funnels theft onto whoever is left.** **Lie Low** ("Steal 1. Rivals can't steal or recruit from you this round. +1 magenta.") rates 4.5 strategic, 3.3 fun. With it and **Bodyguards** ("Rivals can't steal from you.") down, g11 Dee lost 22 silver before her second round III turn, then two members. Named in 6 games. All four g9 players also expected Lie Low to block swaps (your Nov 1 call lets them through).
5. **White feeds itself.** **Money Changers** ("Whenever any player buys followers, +1 white.") is where every instrument agrees: Normal +17, Hard +18, Very Hard +20; g12 Ben won white all three rounds. **Farewell** ("Cast out one of your members to the bottom of the pile, gain followers equal to its cost. +1 white.") and **Estate Sale** ("Cast out one of your members to the bottom of the pile, gain white equal to its cost. +1 white.") pay full cost after a once-per-game ability is spent: g12 Ben fired **Heiresses** ("On Use, once per game: +1 white per member you have.") for 7 white, then cast it out for 9. All 3 g12 players and 2 in g4 made this their first change.
6. **A few spaces never matter.** **Crunch Time** ("+2 purple. Your next two turns come right after this one.") 1.7 / 1.7, untaken in g11; **Right Away** ("+1 purple (+2 in round II, +3 in round III). Your next turn comes right after this one.") 1.9 / 2.0; **Splurge** ("This turn, if you recruit a member, +1 follower for every 2 resources you pay for it. +1 white.") 1.6 / 1.9. Greatest Hits swings instead: it won g9 and was dead to all four g11 players.

## Where to focus design time

1. **What a copy or a join can repeat.** Keep the verbs; change the targets. Directions: the biggest flat payouts (Largesse, Party Gifts, Con Men's steal) pay less when repeated; or Largesse shrinks, now that testing shows where it falls; or **Agents** ("Rivals can't copy your actions.") also stops Encore on its owner's members, as owners expected (g5, g9). A player: Green still rides the table, but nobody is farmed and no one space is the round.
2. **A round III ending the table can count.** Keep Last Call's trade; bound what happens after or on top of everyone. Directions: Party Crasher can't join a center space; Last Call's round III block ends before the final placements; extra-minion stacks can't double. A player: you lose to a play you could have seen.
3. **White's loop.** Directions: Money Changers fires on rivals' buys only, or bigger buys; a cast-out pays less for a spent once-per-game member. A player: white is a contest again.

Next in line (my guess): a cap on steals per victim per round, so protection moves theft around instead of piling it on one cult.

## Notes

- All four newest games (g9-g12, newer builds) were won by 20 or more; of the first 8, only g8 (2 players). Too few to call; watch it.
- Winners with 5 or fewer members: Normal 10%, Hard 13%, Very Hard 20% (bar 20%). Interim.
- **Recruiter** ("Whenever a member leaves a rival's cult, +2 red.") pays on its owner's own Promote and Demote (g12 Ava: her red lead of 8 to 4 became 14 to 8). Your call; a feel note only.
