# How three games felt: g1, g3, g5 (Nov 12)

Four experienced players, three finished games:

- **Game 1**: Green, Purple, Magenta, White. Dee won 88, Ben 68, Ava 65, Cal 61.
- **Game 3**: Green, Red, White, Yellow. Ben won 96, Cal 89, Dee 83, Ava 66.
- **Game 5**: Green, Magenta, Silver, Yellow. Ben won 105, Cal 79, Dee 75, Ava 73.

Green was in all three games. Magenta, White and Yellow were each in two. Purple, Red and Silver were each in one, so anything said about those three rests on a single game.

---

## 1. The counts

### How hard each placement felt
After every placement, each player marked it as a hard call, obvious, or stuck.

| Round | Hard | Obvious | Stuck | Share hard |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | 16 | 20 | 0 | 44% |
| II | 31 | 15 | 2 | 65% |
| III | 44 | 16 | 2 | 71% |

Per game and round (hard / obvious / stuck, all four players together):
- Game 1: I 4/8/0 · II 9/7/0 · III 15/7/0
- Game 3: I 7/5/0 · II 11/4/1 · III 15/4/1
- Game 5: I 5/7/0 · II 11/4/1 · III 14/5/1

Round I is the flattest round in every game. Two players stand out the other way:
- Cal in game 1 marked all 3 of his round I placements obvious.
- Dee in game 1 marked all 4 of her round II placements obvious. Every one was "copy the Heist".

Every turn also had a recruit. Most recruits were follower buys: 33 follower buys against 7 members in game 1, 26 against 20 in game 3, and 21 against 16 in game 5. In game 1 Ava never recruited a member herself; the only two she ever held were given to her in round III.

### How often each space was taken
In round I every space fills, so taking a round I space tells us nothing. These spaces sat empty in later rounds:
- **Game 1**: in round II, Right Away, Stash, Splurge and Nest Egg. In round III, Crunch Time and Cash In.
- **Game 3**: in round II, Borrow, Nest Egg, Write-Off and New Blood. In round III, Gather, Splurge, Cash In and New Blood.
- **Game 5**: in round II, Toast, Polish, Flaunt and Buyout. That is every Silver space except Party Gifts. In round III, Toast, Buyout, Harvest and New Blood.

Green filled completely in every round of every game.

### Copies
These spaces were copied by Echo, Reprise, Cover or Greatest Hits:
- **Game 1, round II**: Heist went off 4 times (Ben's, plus Cal's Echo, Dee's Cover, and Dee's Reprise of her Cover).
- **Game 1, round III**: Heist went off 4 times again (Ava's, plus Cal's Echo, Ben's Cover, and Ava's Greatest Hits). Overtime went off twice.
- **Game 3, round II**: Melting Pot went off 3 times (Ben's, plus Cal's Cover and Cal's Reprise).
- **Game 3, round III**: Initiation went off 3 times. Promote and Farewell each went off twice.
- **Game 5, round II**: Swindle went off 3 times.
- **Game 5, round III**: Largesse went off 4 times. That is 40 followers from one space in one round: 30 to Dee and 10 to Ben.

### Did a rival change their plan?
Each player wrote a diary after every round.
- **Game 1**: all 12 diaries named a rival's move that changed the writer's plan.
- **Game 3**: 10 of 12 did. In round I, Ava and Dee named what rivals did but said they couldn't respond to it.
- **Game 5**: all 12 did.

Everyone was reacting to everyone else in every game.

### Fun by round
Each player gave every round a fun score from 1 to 5. These are the averages.

| | Round I | Round II | Round III |
|---|---|---|---|
| Game 1 | 3.25 | 3.25 | 3.0 |
| Game 3 | 3.25 | 3.5 | 4.25 |
| Game 5 | 3.75 | 4.0 | 3.75 |

Game 1 is the only game where round III was the least fun round. In game 3 it was clearly the best.

## 2. Scores against the play

The players' end-of-game scores are kinder than their own play and diaries.

- **Dee, game 1**, gave "satisfying choices" a 4. She marked 9 of her 14 placements obvious, including all of round II. Trust the play: for her, about a third of her turns were real choices.
- **Cal, game 1**, gave satisfying choices a 4 and wrote "my diaries tagged far more hard than obvious". His marks were 6 hard and 6 obvious. That is half, not "far more".
- **Ben, game 1**, gave fun a 4. His three round diaries said 3, 4 and 3, and he wrote: "the ending felt bad."
- **Comeback, game 1**: Ava gave it 2 and Cal gave it 4. Both are true. Heists pulled the leader back every round, but in round III the player holding the Goes Last token got a closing run nobody could answer. The 4 is about Heist. The 2 is about how the game ended.
- **Choices, game 5**: Dee gave satisfying choices a 4, but she marked her two copies of Largesse obvious ("once Largesse was down, copying it was clearly best"). Cal said many recruits were "place, then convert, which felt like bookkeeping".
- **Fun, all games**: the overall fun scores average 3.5 to 4 in every game. The round diaries hold 2s and 3s at the moments players lost the most: Ava in game 1 round II, Cal in game 1 round III, Ava in game 3 round II.

Regrets show what players wanted back:
- In game 1, Dee held 11 magenta to win "Most resources" and lost all of it to three Heists at the start of round III. Cal did the same with 10 resources and lost the contest anyway.
- Most "best choice" answers named either a copy or a move that took a member from a rival.

## 3. Moments

**Felt great**
- Game 1, Ava, round III: "Greatest Hits on my own steals felt great." She copied both her Heist and her Help Yourself in one placement.
- Game 3, Ben, round II: "Promote and Demote were hard and the most fun turns of the game so far". He took Loyalists and Panel off Ava in two turns.
- Game 3, Cal, round III: "Spoilsport changed hands twice and members flew around; a round I'd tell someone about."
- Game 5, Dee, after the game: "Line Cutters for a Ghost was the best feeling of the game." She made the swap with Swindle.
- Game 1, Ava, round II: Crunch Time "gave me three straight placements and 14 resources; a real timing puzzle."

**Feel-bads**
- Game 1, Ava, round II: "the copy-of-a-copy Heist chain was a feel-bad". She lost 6 followers to Heists before she had placed much of anything.
- Game 1, Cal, round III: "the round ended with me watching one player take four uninterrupted turns that stole my pile."
- Game 3, Ava, after the game: "Being the engine-builder at a table with open Promote/Demote felt like being a shop for everyone else."
- Game 5, Ava, after the game: "a cheap grey member that cancels a 10-follower contest at round end with no way to answer turned my best round III into nothing". Cal's Spoilsport cancelled her Green contest in both round II and round III.
- Game 3, Dee, after the game: "White's fewest-members contest can be taken from you by rivals giving you members… The defender has no answer". Ben gave her Magpie with Write-Off and Line Cutters with Guest of Honor.

**Races to one space**
- Game 1: Heist in rounds II and III. Cal, round II: "the round became whoever copies Heist last wins".
- Game 5: Largesse in round III. Ben: "Largesse plus Greatest Hits plus Reprise: 30 followers and 24 silver to me and Cal. That space with copies is out of hand."
- Game 3: Melting Pot in round II. Ben: "watching my own Melting Pot get copied twice stung."
- Game 1: the Goes Last token in round I. It changed hands three times in the first three turns.

**Spaces nobody wanted**
- Game 1, Ben, round III: "Cash In sat empty all round because nobody kept white long enough to use it."
- Game 1, Ben, after the game, on Right Away: "did nothing on a last minion three times this game." (It was taken twice.)
- Game 5, Cal, on Buyout: "nobody took it all game; paying rivals silver to buy a member never looked worth a placement."

**Plans changed by a rival**
- Game 1, Ben, round II: "Cal swindled my Martyrs… which forced me to Farewell Cheerleaders to stop him cashing Martyrs".
- Game 5, Ben, round II: "Dee's Swindle took my Line Cutters and gave me Ghost (-5), so I bought Anarchist instead of Matchmaker".

**Swings**
- Game 1, Dee: her last four placements of round III came one after another, after everyone else had run out of minions. She took Poach, Swindle, Con Men, Hot Streak +9, Splurge and Cat Burglars, then won "Went last" and "Most resources". She went from 48 to 88.
- Game 3, Ben: his very last action, Guest of Honor giving Line Cutters to Dee, was about a 17-follower swing between the two of them.
- Game 5, Dee: Largesse, then Greatest Hits on Silver, then Reprise. That was +30 followers in one round.

## 4. Spaces, one line each

"Taken" counts placements, not copies. The most a space can be taken is 3 for an outer space (rounds I to III), 2 for a middle space and 1 for the center.

### Green (all three games)
- **Build Up** (+1 green per minion on Green spaces, this one included. +1 green): taken 3 of 3 in every game, usually as the last open Green space so the player could tie the Green contest. Few called it a real choice; players called it "mostly a filler" or "a green minion". *Working*, as a filler.
- **Echo** (Copy the previous action. +1 green): taken 3 of 3 in every game. Whether it was worth taking depended on what had just been placed, and players tracked that closely. Game 3 Ben: "depends entirely on what was placed just before". It was the most-praised space overall. When a Heist or Largesse had just been placed, it became an automatic copy of that space. *Working*, but see the copy finding below.
- **Reprise** (Copy an action with your minion. +1 green): taken 3 of 3 in every game. Copying your own Cover meant running a rival's Heist twice: game 1 Dee, "Copying a copy of a Heist. I don't love that this is legal". Copying your own Overtime gave Dee a second extra minion in game 1, and copying Largesse gave +10 in game 5. Very strong. *Working* and loved, but it is the link that lets one big space go off three or four times.
- **Crowd** (+1 follower per minion on Green spaces. +3 green): taken 2 of 2 every game, late in the round, for about +4 followers. "Solid, rarely a real question." *Working*, as a filler.
- **Cover** (Copy an action with a rival's minion on it. +1 green): taken 2 of 2 every game. Most of the time it meant "copy the biggest space a rival just took": Heist in game 1, Melting Pot and Initiation in game 3, Largesse in game 5. Game 1 Cal: "it just means 'Heist again' most of the time, which is the problem with Heist more than with Cover." *Working*, but it feeds the copy problem.
- **Greatest Hits** (Choose a color other than green. Copy every action with your minion on its spaces. +1 green): taken 1 of 1 every game, and named as a best turn every time. It needs setting up: Ava copied her own Heist and Help Yourself in game 1, and Dee copied her Promote in game 3. In game 5 Dee used it only to copy her own Largesse, and she called that turn obvious. *Working*. It becomes automatic when the one space it copies pays +10.

### Purple (game 1 only)
- **Sleep In** (I: +4 / II: +5 / III: +6 purple. Take the Goes Last token: your other minions are placed after everyone else's): taken 3 of 3. It opened the token fight every round; game 1 Ben: "the most interactive thing in the game". In round I it was a decoy, because two copy and token spaces could take the token back straight away. *Working*.
- **On a Roll** (+2 purple per consecutive turn, this one included. Take the Goes Last token): taken 3 of 3, all by Ava, who held the Purple Cloak. "Mostly a cheap way to steal the Goes Last token." *Working*, mainly as the answer to Sleep In.
- **Right Away** (I: +2 / II: +3 / III: +4 purple. Your next turn comes right after this one): taken 2 of 3, both times on a player's last minion, where it did nothing. It sat empty in round II. Average fun score 1.0. Game 1 Cal: "nobody wanted it until it was the only thing left." It is also *unclear*: Ava and Ben both seemed surprised that it does nothing on your last minion. *Never wanted.*
- **Last Call** (+1 purple. +1 follower and +1 purple per minion you have left. Take the Goes Last token): taken 2 of 2. It was a real timing question, because it is best early, but it pushes all your other minions to the end of the round. In round III it parked Dee's four minions at the end of the round. *Working*, but it is one half of the closing-run problem.
- **Crunch Time** (+2 purple. Your next two turns come right after this one): taken 1 of 2. Ava's chain in round II was one of the best turns of the game, and the space sat empty in round III. *Working* (one game, one use).
- **Overtime** (center, +4 purple. +1 minion this round. Take the Goes Last token: that minion goes after everyone else's): taken 1 of 1, then copied by Reprise for a second extra minion. All four players named it as what decided the game. *Feel-bad* in the hands of a leader, because the run of turns at the end could not be answered.

### Magenta (games 1 and 5)
- **Help Yourself** (Steal 1 resource per minion you've placed this round. +1 magenta): taken 3 of 3 in both games. It grows with the minions you've placed, so whether to take it early or late was a real choice. With the Magenta Cloak it took whole piles: game 5 Ben, "a feel-bad on the receiving end". *Working.*
- **Stash** (+3 magenta): taken 2 times in game 1 and 3 times in game 5, always as the last resort. Average fun score 1.5 to 1.8. *Never wanted.*
- **Catch Up** (+2 magenta per rival with more resources than you. +1 magenta): taken 3 of 3 in both games, by whoever had just been robbed. "A fine last placement." *Working*, as a catch-up.
- **Heist** (Steal 4 followers and 4 resources. +1 magenta): taken 2 of 2 in both games. With copies it went off 8 times in game 1. Every player in game 1 called it "the space of the game". Game 1 Cal: "exciting the first time, oppressive by the third." In game 5 it went off only twice (Ben's Agents blocked one copy), and players there rated it fun, 4 to 5. *Always taken* in game 1, where every copy space was free to use it. In game 5 it was *working*.
- **Swindle** (Steal a rival's member costing up to 7; give them a shop member of your choice in its place. +1 magenta): taken 2 of 2 in both games, plus 2 copies in game 5. It was behind the Line Cutters-for-Ghost swap and the Martyrs grab. Strategy score 3.8 to 4.2; game 5 players called it "the most fun table moments". *Working.*
- **Poach** (center, Recruit a rival's member you choose, for free. +1 magenta): taken 1 of 1 in both games. Both times it was used to change a contest, not to gain the member: game 1 Dee took Copyist to split Ben's "Fewest members" prize, and game 5 Cal took Spoilsport back. *Working.*

### White (games 1 and 3)
- **Modest Means** (+2 white. +1 white per rival with more members than you): taken 3 of 3 in both games, usually as filler or to keep another player from getting +5. *Working*, as a filler.
- **Splurge** (I: +2 white; if you recruit a member this turn, +1 follower for every 2 resources you pay | II: the same at 1 follower per 1 resource | III: back to 1 per 2): taken 2 of 3 in both games. It paid well once in each game: Cal got +8 on Talent Scouts in round II of game 3, and Dee got +4 in game 1. It sat empty in game 1's round II and game 3's round III. "Only matters if you buy a member that turn." *Working*, but only in round II, the round with the better rate.
- **Nest Egg** (+3 white): taken 2 of 3 in both games, always as filler. Average fun 1.5 to 1.8. *Never wanted.*
- **Farewell** (Discard a member for its cost in followers. +1 white): taken 2 of 2 in both games. With Martyrs it paid 10 to 14 followers. Ben and Cal raced each other for it in game 1. Game 3 Dee: "came off exactly as planned." *Working.*
- **Write-Off** (Give a member to a rival for its cost in white. +1 white): taken twice in game 1 and once in game 3. Every time it was used as an attack on the "Fewest members" contest: Ben gave Last Callers and then Regulars away in game 1, and gave Dee Magpie in game 3. Ben, game 1: "my favorite". The receivers felt they had no defense. *Working.* For the receiver it is a *feel-bad*, which finding 3 covers.
- **Cash In** (center, Turn all your white into followers. +1 white): never taken in either game. Nobody held white into round III, because Heist, Help Yourself and Offering kept taking it, and because a follower buy did the same job without using a placement. *Never wanted.* This is a round III center space that nobody used.

### Red (game 3 only)
- **Borrow** (Recruit the cheapest shop member, for free. Discard it at round end, before scoring. +1 red): taken 2 of 3, both times by Dee, who borrowed a member and gave it away with Guest of Honor or Patrons for +4 or +7. Otherwise "a non-pick". Dee misread whether the discard follows the member once it is given away; it doesn't. *Unclear.*
- **Late Shift** (Recruit a shop member costing up to 2 per minion you've placed this round, for free. +1 red): taken 3 of 3, every time as a player's last minion. Ava: "my favourite space every round"; Cal: "a timing puzzle, worth it only on your 3rd-5th minion". *Working.*
- **Gather** (+3 red. If you recruit a member this turn of a color you already have: +4 followers. +1 red): taken 2 of 3. Ava opened with it every round, and it sat empty in round III. "Nearly nothing in round I." *Working* (one game).
- **Promote** (+3 red. Swap any member for one costing up to 3 more, from another cult or the shop): taken 2 of 2, plus a copy by Greatest Hits. Two players called it the best space in the game. On the receiving end, Ava gave it fun 2. *Working.* For a player whose members it took, it is a *feel-bad*.
- **Demote** (+3 red. Swap any member for one costing up to 3 less, from another cult or the shop): taken 2 of 2. Cal used it to take Spoilsport off Dee, which decided who could cancel a contest. *Working*, with the same sting as Promote.
- **Initiation** (center, Recruit a shop member costing 10 or less, for free. +1 red): taken 1 of 1, then copied twice by Cal. Ben got Spoilsport from it, and that card shaped the whole of round III. Ben, looking back: "Melting Pot or Survey would have handed Cal less." *Working.*

### Yellow (games 3 and 5)
- **Harvest** (+3 in colors you choose): taken 3 times in game 3 and 2 in game 5, always as filler. Ava misread it as having a first-taker +1. *Never wanted.*
- **Survey** (+1 yellow per minion on the busiest color's spaces. +1 yellow): taken 3 of 3 in both games. It paid 4 to 6, and players sometimes took it to keep it from a rival. *Working*, mostly as a filler.
- **New Blood** (+2 yellow. If you recruit a member this turn in a color none of your members have: +3 yellow): taken once in game 3 and twice in game 5. It sat empty in round III of both games. Only Ben, with the Yellow Cloak, made it pay. *Never wanted.*
- **Makeover** (+4 yellow. Then rearrange your resources into any colors): taken 2 of 2 in both games. It was huge once in game 5: Ben turned 25 resources, mostly silver other players had dumped on him, into yellow and a 10-follower buy. *Working.*
- **Melting Pot** (For each color among your members: +1 follower and +1 of that color; grey gives 1 of any color): taken 2 of 2 in both games. In game 3 Ben took it for +5 and 5 resources, and then Cal copied it twice. *Working*, but the copies made it a race in game 3.
- **Offering** (center, +5 yellow. Each rival discards 2 of each color among your members): taken 1 of 1 in both games. In game 5 it decided "Most resources" (Ben: "my best turn"). In game 3 Ava took it on her last minion and it "barely mattered". *Working.* How much it does depends on the taker having many member colors.

### Silver (game 5 only)
- **Toast** (+4 silver. Each rival +2 silver. +1 silver): taken once, in round I by Cal (Silver Cloak), and empty after that. Others: "I never wanted it." *Never wanted.* Only the Silver Cloak holder can use it.
- **Polish** (+2 silver. Turn all your silver into one other color. +1 silver): taken 2 of 3, as a way to empty your silver before scoring. "Flexible, nothing exciting." *Working*, as a filler.
- **Party Gifts** (Give rivals up to 3 silver from the bank. +2 followers per silver given): taken 3 of 3, all by Cal, and copied twice by Ava's Echo. With the Silver Cloak it paid 6 every round. Cal himself: "an always-take". *Always taken.*
- **Flaunt** (+1 of each other color per 3 silver you hold, up to 3 each. +1 silver): taken once, in round III, after Largesse had dumped 12 silver on Cal. It paid +9. "Only good once someone has dumped silver on you." *Working* (once), and it depends on another player's move.
- **Buyout** (Recruit a rival's member, paying them its cost in silver. +1 silver): never taken. Players gave it 1s across the board. *Never wanted.*
- **Largesse** (center, Give rivals 12 silver from the bank, split as you choose. +10 followers): taken once and copied three times, for 40 followers in one round. All four players named it as the first thing to change. *Always taken.* Copies make it a *feel-bad*, and the players receiving the silver then turned it into followers with Makeover and Flaunt.

## 5. Members players called out

- **Martyrs** (Whenever this member is discarded, +4 white and +4 followers; worth 3, cost 6). With Farewell it paid 10 to 14 followers in both White games, and Cal swindled it to set up exactly that. Everyone said it was worth its price. *Working*, maybe on the strong side. Game 3 Ben: "maybe too strong".
- **Copyist** (Whenever you copy an action, +2 followers; cost 5). Cal's whole game 3 ran on it, together with Doppelganger as a second copy. In game 1 it paid only once, because the copy spaces were gone before his turns ("it has paid 0 so far"), and then Dee took it with Poach. *Working.*
- **Hot Streak** (once per game, +3 followers per turn in a row you've taken just before this one; cost 7). It paid +9 on Dee's four-turn finish in game 1. Ben: "a feel-bad when one player gets the closing run". *Feel-bad*, as part of that run.
- **Last Callers** (once per round, take the Goes Last token; cost 7). Ben bought it in game 1 and didn't need it, then gave it away with Write-Off. Ava said it was "why Went Last never felt winnable for me after round I". *Working.*
- **Cat Burglars** (Round End, before scoring: steal 3 resources; cost 8) and **Con Men** (On Recruit: steal 5 resources; cost 9). Both decided "Most resources" at the last moment, in game 1 and game 5. Nobody could answer them. *Feel-bad* for the player robbed right before scoring.
- **Spoilsport** (Round End: choose a contest; nobody wins it this round; cost 8). In game 3 it was "the most-fought-over card", with three owners in one round, and everyone loved it. In game 5 Cal used it twice to cancel Ava's Green contest, and Ava called it the first thing to change. *Working* when other cards can move it, as Red and Magenta could in game 3. A *feel-bad* when it hits the same player twice.
- **Guest of Honor** (once per round, give a rival one of your members, +4 followers; cost 5). In game 3 it was +4 every round for Ben and the gift that won him White. Dee gave it away on Borrow by mistake. *Working.* Possibly *underpriced*, since it pays +4 every round for 5.
- **Line Cutters** (You go first every round; worth 3, cost 4). Worth 3 for 4, plus first pick. It was stolen three times in game 5 and used as a gift to break White in game 3. *Underpriced* by every reading: three followers and first pick for 4.
- **Dabblers** (Counts as 2 wild members; cost 5) and **Alchemists** (grey; cost 7). Neither table knew how wild and grey members count for "Most member colors", Red's biggest group, or Melting Pot. In game 5 Ava paid 7 for Alchemists thinking it was a new color, and it wasn't. *Unclear.*
- **Die-Hards** (Game End: if you have the most green members, +6 followers; cost 7). In game 3 Cal expected +6 and got nothing: "I still don't know why." The likely reason is Ben's Dabblers counting as 2 wild members next to his green Copyist, but the table never saw why. *Unclear.*
- **Loyalists** (Your other members can't be swapped; worth 3, cost 5). Because it doesn't protect itself, it was "the target of every swap"; Ava: "felt wrong for its name". *Unclear.*
- **Ghost** (worth 0; whenever it joins your cult, -5 followers). It was passed back and forth in game 5 with Swindle and Anarchist, and remembered as the best table moment ("the Ghost hot potato"). *Working.*
- **Agents** (Your actions can't be copied; worth 2, cost 5). It stopped Ava's Cover on Heist in game 5, which caught her by surprise. Nobody bought it in game 1, though all four said it would have stopped the Heist copies. *Unclear* to whoever was blocked by it. Otherwise *working*.
- **Wallflower** (7 green + 5 any). "7 green is a price nobody could reach." *Overpriced.*
- **Snitch** (Whenever a rival steals from you, they lose 1 follower; cost 6). Ava bought it in game 5 and nobody ever stole from her: "Dee never stole from me anyway". In game 1 it went unbought even though the table was full of steals. *Never wanted.*
- **Bodyguards** and **Heist Crew** (6 magenta each, plus 4 and 3 any). Players at the steal-heavy table wanted them but "the 6 magenta price never lined up". *Overpriced*, at least in the magenta part.
- **Kidnappers** (Round End: a rival you choose gives you one of their members; cost 10). Dee's regret in game 5: it cost all her resources and her magenta lead. *Overpriced* (one game).
- **Kingmakers** (Round End: give rivals up to 3 of your silver, +1 follower per silver given). It won Ava the round II "Fewest silver" alone, after everyone had stopped placing. The other three called it sniping. *Feel-bad* for the players it beat.
- **Courier** (Whenever you take a consecutive turn, +1 of each other color; cost 8). Cal: "'consecutive turn' was unclear to me". *Unclear* (one game).

## 6. The game as a whole

- **Best round**: round III of game 3 (fun 4.25, 15 hard calls against 4 obvious). The round was a fight over Spoilsport and Loyalists using Promote, Demote, Write-Off and Guest of Honor. Two players gave it a 5. That round was a payoff of earlier choices: Cal's round I Copyist built into his round II engine and his round III Doppelganger and Minimalists, and Dee's round I Martyrs paid off in round II.
- **Worst rounds**: round I in every game. It had the fewest hard calls (44%), and players said "round I felt like collecting 5s" (game 1 Dee) and "collecting 4-5 at a time" (game 3 Cal). In round I the outer spaces giving a flat +3 (Stash, Nest Egg, Harvest) and the follower buy make most turns obvious. The other low point was round III of game 1, where fun fell to 3.0 and Cal scored it 2.
- **Did round III pay off earlier choices?**
  - Game 3: yes.
  - Game 5: partly. Ben's members built into his round III win (Cheerleaders, Patron Saints, four member colors). Dee's +30 came from copying one space, not from anything built.
  - Game 1: no. Ava, Ben and Cal all said round III was decided by the closing run; Ben: it was "settled by turn order (four Dee turns in a row) more than by earlier setup" (Ben). Ava never recruited a member, and Dee recruited none until round III.
- **Interaction** is strong everywhere. Every diary named a rival's move, and plans changed every round. The players' main complaint was the opposite of "nothing mattered": the biggest swings came from rivals' moves or from round-end effects they could not answer.

## 7. The five findings that matter most

1. **Copy spaces turn the strongest space each round into a race, and the same space goes off 3 or 4 times.** This happened in all three games: Heist 4 times in each of rounds II and III of game 1, Melting Pot 3 times in game 3, and Largesse 4 times (40 followers) in game 5. The player who placed the strong space first, or who was robbed by it, felt it most: "exciting the first time, oppressive by the third." In a real turn, the next three players each copy the same space, and the turns become automatic. Dee marked all four of her round II placements in game 1 obvious. The copy spaces themselves are the best-liked spaces in the game. The fix players asked for is in how copies stack: a copy of a copy, more than one copy of the same space in a round, or the very largest payouts (Heist, Largesse) being copied at full value.

2. **Contests decided after nobody can answer.** In game 1, Dee held the Goes Last token in round III with two extra minions (Overtime, then Reprise of Overtime). She placed her last four minions in a row after everyone else had run out and gained about 40 followers. Every other player called it the moment that ended the game and called it a feel-bad. In game 5, Spoilsport (twice), Kingmakers and Cat Burglars changed contests at round end, and in game 3 Ben gave Dee a member with his very last action. Players like the round-end fights when they can still respond. They dislike it when one player's run of turns, or a round-end card, settles a 10-follower contest after everyone else is out of minions. Some things to look at: how many minions can wait on the token, and whether extra minions can be copied. The Purple part rests on one game.

3. **Moving members between cults is the best part of the game, but the player who loses the member has no reply.** Promote, Demote, Swindle, Poach, Write-Off, Farewell and Guest of Honor got the highest strategy scores, and players named them as the moments they would tell someone about. The same moves left the player who lost the member with fun scores of 2. Ava in game 3: "like being a shop for everyone else". Dee in game 3: a gift of a member "is a free 10-follower attack" on the "Fewest members" contest. Loyalists, the card meant to protect members, doesn't protect itself. Players asked for a cost to the player taking or giving the member, or a way for the defender to answer. They did not ask for fewer of these moves.

4. **About a dozen spaces are filler nobody chooses, including two round III centers.** Cash In was never taken in either White game. Buyout was never taken. In game 5's round II, every Silver space except Party Gifts sat empty. Right Away did nothing both times it was taken, because each time it was the player's last minion. Stash, Nest Egg, Harvest, New Blood and Toast were taken only when nothing else was left, and they score 1 to 2 on fun. In round I every space fills, so these turn into "take +3 and buy followers", and round I has the fewest real choices of any round. Spaces that need a pile you can't protect (Cash In, Buyout, Flaunt) or a member you can't afford that turn (New Blood, Splurge outside round II) went empty. These spaces may need a reason to take them that doesn't depend on holding something through rivals' steals.

5. **Rules about how members count kept surprising players and cost them followers.** There were problems in all three games: wild and grey members in color counts (Dabblers in games 3 and 5, Alchemists in game 5, Die-Hards paying nothing in game 3), Borrow's discard not following a member that was given away, Agents silently blocking a Cover, the Magenta Cloak paying on Poach but not Swindle, and Right Away doing nothing on your last minion. Cal paid 7 for Die-Hards and lost the 7-point gap to the winner partly because of it. Most of these are about the card words, or about the table being able to see what each contest is counting. Ben, game 3: "make what each contest counts visible".
