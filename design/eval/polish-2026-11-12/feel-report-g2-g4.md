# How two games felt: game A and game B (Nov 12)

Two 4-player games, both on the 10-card deck, with the setting where only the first player ever to take a space gets its +1 of that color.

- **Game A**: Red, Blue, Silver, Yellow. Ben 79, Dee 71, Ava 64, Cal 62.
- **Game B**: Purple, Blue, Magenta, Silver. Ben 89, Ava 74, Dee 70, Cal 64.

Blue and Silver were in both games. Red and Yellow were only in game A, and Purple and Magenta only in game B, so anything about those four colors rests on one game.

---

## 1. Counts first

**How players marked their own placements** (a close call between two or more options / one clear best option / no good option):

- Game A, round I: 8 close, 3 clear, 1 no good option
- Game A, round II: 13 close, 3 clear, 1 no good option
- Game A, round III: 15 close, 5 clear, 0 no good option
- Game B, round I: 8 close, 4 clear, 0 no good option
- Game B, round II: 10 close, 6 clear, 0 no good option
- Game B, round III: 16 close, 4 clear, 1 no good option

By player, all three rounds:

- Game A: Ava 9 close / 2 clear / 1 none; Ben 10 / 2 / 0; Cal 9 / 3 / 0; Dee 8 / 4 / 1.
- Game B: Ava 8 / 4 / 1; Ben 7 / 5 / 0; Cal 10 / 2 / 0; Dee 9 / 3 / 0.

About three placements in four were close calls. Round III had the most close calls in both games. The "no good option" turns were all a player's last minion with only leftovers open (Borrow, New Blood, Markdown).

**What the recruit after each placement went on:**

- Game A: round I, 9 members and 2 follower buys; round II, 6 and 6; round III, 2 and 15.
- Game B: round I, 9 members and 2 follower buys; round II, 4 and 11; round III, 6 and 12.

In game B, players moved to buying followers a round earlier. Four of their reasons say outright they were spending before Ben could steal it.

**Rivals changing plans:** all 24 round diaries named at least one rival's move that changed the writer's plan. Every player scored "dynamic" 4 or 5.

**How often each space was taken** (round I / II / III):

- Red (A): Borrow 1/0/1, Late Shift 1/1/1 (all Dee), Gather 1/1/1, Promote –/1/1, Demote –/1/1, Initiation –/–/1
- Yellow (A): Harvest 1/1/0, Survey 1/1/1, New Blood 1/1/0, Makeover –/1/1, Melting Pot –/1/1, Offering –/–/1
- Purple (B): Sleep In 1/1/1 (all Ava), On a Roll 1/1/1, Right Away 1/1/1, Last Call –/1/1, Crunch Time –/1/1, Overtime –/–/1
- Magenta (B): Help Yourself 1/1/1, Stash 1/1/1, Catch Up 1/1/1, Heist –/1/1, Swindle –/1/1, Poach –/–/1
- Blue (both games together): Test Drive 2/2/2, Out of Style 2/2/1, Markdown 2/2/2, Recruit Twice –/2/2, Word of Mouth –/2/2, Spree –/–/2
- Silver (both games together): Party Gifts 2/2/2, Polish 2/1/2, Toast 2/1/1, Flaunt –/0/0, Buyout –/0/1, Largesse –/–/2

## 2. Scores against the play

Most scores match the play. Where they don't:

- **Game A, Dee, "impactful 4."** Her round III diary says "my own moves felt like they built things that rivals then took apart," and she won no contest in round III. Her play says closer to 3.
- **Game A, Ben's round I.** He scored fun 3 in his round I diary and 4 after the game. Round I was the low point for him ("felt like collecting small amounts and setting up"). The postgame score reflects his round III win.
- **Game B, Ava.** Her round II diary scored fun 2 ("being robbed... three turns in a row was a feel-bad"); her postgame score was 3. The diary is the truer reading of round II.
- **Game B, Ava, "satisfying choices 3."** Her placements were mostly close calls (8 of 13), so the low score isn't about placements. It's about recruits: "a lot of turns ended in buy 5 followers before it gets stolen." The 11 follower buys in round II back her up.
- **Party Gifts in game A** was scored 2 for decision by three players, and their own marks agree. Dee took it as her first placement in round I ("obvious"), and Ben took it as the first space he could reach in round II ("obvious"). Game B players marked it as a close call, but it still went early in all three rounds. Trust the play: it is always taken.
- **Crunch Time in game B** got 5s from three players. Dee scored it 2 for decision: "first player always takes it." The play agrees with her. It was the first placement of rounds II and III, both times by the player holding Line Cutters.

## 3. The moments

**Turns that felt great**

- Game B, Ben, round II: "the three-turn chain was a combo I'd tell people about, and Cal's Repossessors answer was a real reply." (fun 5)
- Game A, Ben, round III: "a round I'd tell someone about tomorrow; every turn someone took a member from someone." (fun 5)
- Game A, Ava, round II: "the back-and-forth over the members contest with Cal was great." She cleared the shop's blue members with Scouts so Cal's Mannequin counted nothing.
- Game B, Ava, round II: "Markdown into Line Cutters for 2 blue after Cal's Repossessors dumped it in the discard pile." It was the bright spot of a bad round.
- Game B, Dee, round III, on Last Call with all 5 minions in hand: "+4 followers and +5 purple on my first minion felt great."
- Game A, Dee: "Late Shift's timing gamble is the best design on the wheel."

**Feel-bads**

- Game B, Ava, round I: "being stolen to zero twice was a feel-bad." Cal, round I: "watching Ben's cloak pay on every steal was a feel-bad."
- Game B, Dee, round III: "my contest plans were undone by round-end gifts I could never answer." Ben's Kingmakers put 1 silver on her after the last minion in all three rounds.
- Game A, Dee, round III: "the round-end silver gift after I'd placed my last minion was a feel-bad I could do nothing about."
- Game A, Ava, round III: "losing Cheerleaders to a Ghost felt brutal." Game B, Ava: "Ghost, which felt like a punishment card."
- Game A, Cal, round III: "losing two contests to things done after my last minion stung" (Spoilsport, Loan Officers, Kingmakers).

**Races to one space**

- **Party Gifts**: taken every round of both games, and the first placement of round I in both. Game A, Dee: "6 followers in round I is more than a contest prize."
- **Largesse**: taken in the first lap of round III in both games, both times by Ava, who held Line Cutters and so placed first.
- **Crunch Time** (game B): the first placement of rounds II and III. Dee: "The race was for Ben's Crunch Time opener."
- **Late Shift** (game A): Dee took it every round. Ben and Cal both lost plans to her ("Dee took Late Shift right before my third minion").

**Spaces nobody wanted**

- **Flaunt**: never taken in either game. Game A, Ben: "Flaunt and Buyout went untouched all round: nobody holds silver because Fewest silver punishes it."
- **Buyout**: taken once in two games.
- **Right Away**, game B, round I: Cal took it with nothing else left and it did nothing ("Right Away does nothing").

**Swings**

- Game B, round II: Ben opened with Crunch Time, then took Heist, Con Men and Swindle with his next two placements. He went from 16 to 31 followers before anyone else placed. Every other player named it the biggest swing of the round.
- Game A, round III: Ben's last turn was Repossessors on Dee's Dabblers, then Loan Officers pushing silver onto Cal and Dee, then Spoilsport on Blue. He went from tied last after round II to first. Dee: "my own moves felt like they built things that rivals then took apart."
- Game A, round II: Cal's Promote moved Ava's Loyalists to Ben and cost two players a follower through Deprogrammers. Ben's Demote undid it two turns later. Cal: "my swap felt big for a minute and then got undone in two turns."

## 4. Spaces, one line each

### Red (game A only)
- **Borrow** (taken 2 times): weak, because the member leaves before contests are scored. Ava's round III Borrow of Gift Swappers, to pass her Ghost to the leader, was "the most fun move I made." *working, but only for the rare one-turn effect.*
- **Late Shift** (3, all Dee): a real timing gamble, waiting for a later minion to reach a pricier free member. Rivals planned around it and lost it to her. *working.*
- **Gather** (3): the +4 followers never paid in three takes. The space was +3 red, and once a player came up one short because the first taker's +1 was gone. *never wanted for its bonus.*
- **Promote** (2): the most praised space in game A. Picking a target was a real puzzle, and it hit two players at once. *working.*
- **Demote** (2): just as strong, and it carries the game's worst feel-bad: handing a rival the Ghost from the shop. *working, with a feel-bad.*
- **Initiation** (1): a free member costing up to 10, taken early in round III. Solid, not exciting. *working.*

### Yellow (game A only)
- **Harvest** (2): plain filler that fills an exact shortfall. *working.*
- **Survey** (3): paid 3 to 5 when players crowded one color. Ben: "nicer than it looks." *working.*
- **New Blood** (2): good only with the Yellow Cloak (+7 for Ben once). Otherwise +2. *never wanted without the cloak.*
- **Makeover** (2): a quiet setup for a big buy (Cal turned 12 resources into 10 followers). *working.*
- **Melting Pot** (2): +4 followers and 4 resources for a cult with members of many colors, taken by whoever had 4 colors. *working.*
- **Offering** (1): Cal took it for the +5 yellow. Rivals held none of the colors it hits, so nobody discarded anything. *unclear in value: the rival part did nothing.*

### Purple (game B only)
- **Sleep In** (3, all Ava): steady purple, but the Goes Last token was taken off her within a lap every time. *working.*
- **On a Roll** (3): mostly used to take the token back. Dee locked Went last with it in round II. *working.*
- **Right Away** (3): the leftover space. In round I it did nothing on the last minion; in round III it made Cal place his last minion at once, right after he took the token. *never wanted, and once unclear.*
- **Last Call** (2): +4 followers and the token, best on the first minion of a round. *working.*
- **Crunch Time** (2): three placements in a row before anyone can answer. Taken as the very first placement of rounds II and III by whoever held Line Cutters, and it decided game B. *always taken: whoever goes first takes it.*
- **Overtime** (1): an extra minion plus the token. It won Ava Went last in round III. *working, too few games to say more.*

### Magenta (game B only)
- **Help Yourself** (3): with the Magenta Cloak, Ben stole 4 and gained 2 more. Dee took it in round III only to keep it from him. *feel-bad when paired with the cloak.*
- **Stash** (3): a flat +3 taken when nothing else was left. *never wanted except as a leftover.*
- **Catch Up** (3): +6 or +7 for whoever had spent down. It rewards spending first, and players liked it. *working.*
- **Heist** (2): stealing 4 followers and 4 resources was part of both big openers. Ben: "the biggest swing on the wheel; whoever goes first takes it." *always taken when the first player can reach it.*
- **Swindle** (2): took Picket Line, then Line Cutters with a Ghost handed back. *working, with a feel-bad.*
- **Poach** (1): Ben took Cal's Cheerleaders the turn after Cal bought it. A real swing. *working, too few games to say more.*

### Blue (both games)
- **Test Drive** (6): it lives or dies on the shop. It was a big play with Accomplice, Repossessors, Extra Hand, Con Men and Debutantes, and did nothing with Kingmakers, whose effect comes at round end. *working, but unclear on Round End members.*
- **Out of Style** (5): good only with the Blue Cloak (Cal took it all three rounds in game B). Twice it emptied the pile in round I, which left Markdown with nothing to buy. *never wanted without the cloak.*
- **Markdown** (6): empty in round I in both games. Later it gave the best bargain of game B (Line Cutters for 2). *working from round II.*
- **Recruit Twice** (4): in 3 of 4 takes the player recruited nothing from the discard pile and kept only the +5 blue. Dee: "the name says twice but it's one discard recruit at full price." *unclear: the name promises more than it gives.*
- **Word of Mouth** (4): a steady +3 to +7 for big cults, never fought over. *working.*
- **Spree** (2): good for a blue engine (Ava's Repeat Customers chain, Cal's Snobs plus Heist Crew turn). *working.*

### Silver (both games)
- **Party Gifts** (6): +6 followers in round I, taken every round in both games, and the first placement of round I both times. *always taken.*
- **Polish** (5): the dependable way down to 0 silver. Valued by everyone. *working.*
- **Toast** (4): it mostly hands rivals silver they turn straight into followers. Two takers regretted it. *feel-bad for the taker.*
- **Flaunt** (0): never taken. Every player gave it 1s: "nobody holds silver because Fewest silver punishes it." *never wanted.*
- **Buyout** (1): Cal's one use (taking Line Cutters with Ava's own gifted silver) was a real swing, but nobody held silver in game B. *never wanted, apart from one game.*
- **Largesse** (2): +10 flat, the first pick of round III in both games. In game B the 12 silver Ava handed out paid for Cal's next big turn. *always taken by whoever goes first.*

## 5. Members players called out

- **Line Cutters** (4 cost, worth 3, counts as 3, go first every round): *underpriced*. Seven of the eight postgame notes called it cheap. It changed hands five times. Its holder placed first in all four of rounds II and III, and that is what fed Crunch Time and Largesse.
- **Heist Crew** (9 cost, worth 4, counts as 4, steals half a rival's biggest pile once): *underpriced*. All four game B players said so. One game.
- **Kingmakers** (round end: give rivals up to 3 silver, +1 follower each): *feel-bad*. Its gift lands after the last minion, so the player it hits can't answer. It won Ben the silver contest alone in rounds I and III of game B (+4, then +10 and +2 from Cheerleaders). In game A it cost Dee her silver share and Paupers' +3. Dee: "the best member in the game for Ben... I had no answer."
- **Ghost** (worth 0, −5 followers when it joins your cult): *feel-bad*. It was handed over three times, through Demote, Swindle and Gift Swappers, each time −5 plus the lost member. Giving it was "the most fun of the night" (game A, Cal). Getting it was "a punishment" (game B, Ava).
- **Spoilsport** (round end: cancel one contest): decided game A from after the last minion. Cal: "strong but a feel-bad for whoever was about to win it." *working, with a feel-bad.* One game.
- **Toll Collectors** (+1 follower whenever anyone buys followers with silver): game A, "probably the best card in the game" (Cal) and "too much for a passive card" (Dee), paying Ben about 7 to 8. In game B nobody bought it. *working, but it depends on the table.*
- **Last-Minute Shopper** (round end: +3 blue, then recruit): quietly strong in both games. Its recruit after the last minion took a contest in game B round I, and let Ben shed a gifted silver in game A. *working.*
- **Accomplice** (on recruit: steal 3) and **Con Men** (steal 5): in game B with the Magenta Cloak, each steal paid +2 more. The round I chain of Test Drive on Accomplice, Help Yourself, then buying Accomplice gave Ben about 13 resources by his second placement. Ben himself said "feels like a lot of free stuff in round one." *underpriced next to the Magenta Cloak.* One game.
- **Deprogrammers** (worth 4, counts as 4, for 5): liked by all. The −1 tax on every swap was a real sting. *working.*
- **Debutantes**: through Test Drive, Cal's cloak paid before the doubling, so his own trick backfired. *unclear.*
- **Power Shoppers**: Ava expected it to count itself as the first member that turn. *unclear.*
- **Loyalists**: it also stops your own swaps, which surprised Cal twice. *unclear.*
- **Dabblers**: three players weren't sure which contests its "2 wild members" counts in. *unclear.*
- **Extra Hand** (10): never bought, only used through Test Drive. *overpriced.*
- **Never bought in either game**: the silver members that want you to hold silver (Hustlers, Bookies, Smooth Talkers, Patron Saints, Splurgers) and the purple members that need runs of turns or skipped turns (Timekeeper, Gossip, Closer, Hot Streak, Courier). Players gave the same reason as for Flaunt: nobody holds silver, and runs and skips are rare. *never wanted.*

## 6. The game as a whole

- **Best round**: round III in both games had the most close calls (15 and 16), the biggest swings and the +10 contests. **Worst**: game A round I, where three of four diaries said their moves felt like "collecting small amounts," and game B round II for everyone but Ben (Ava fun 2, Dee and Cal 3).
- **Was round III a payoff?** Partly. In game A, Ben built it from round I: Seat Filler upgraded for free twice, into Repossessors and Spoilsport ("arc 5"). Dee and Cal had their engines taken apart instead (Body Snatchers, Dabblers, Mannequin), so for them it felt like "a new fight each round." In game B, round III was big for everyone, but Ben's lead from the first three placements of round II held to the end. Dee: "the game was mostly settled by Ben's round II opener."
- **Contest splits**: in game A, 7 of 12 contests were tied, including a four-way tie on Fewest silver in round II. Dee: "split +6 contests made round end flat." In game B only 2 of 12 were tied, and players found the contests sharper.
- **The one-time +1 per space**: in game A it caught out two players in round II. Ava came up one short at Gather and passed ("miscounted Gather's bonus, it only goes to the first taker ever"), and Ben's Toast paid 1 less than he planned. Ava and Ben listed it among the rules that surprised them. Nobody in game B mentioned it. It didn't change any plan beyond those two turns.

## The five findings that matter most

1. **Going first plus Crunch Time decides the game.** Whoever holds Line Cutters (4 cost, worth 3, go first every round) opens rounds II and III. In game B that player took Crunch Time and then two of Heist, Con Men, Swindle and Largesse before anyone else placed: Ben went from 16 to 31, and Ava's round III opener was worth about 20. Every player named it the swing, and the leader after it won by 15. Line Cutters was called underpriced in seven of eight notes. Possible changes: a higher price for Line Cutters or a weaker going-first effect, or keep Crunch Time from being the first placement of a round. (Crunch Time rests on one game; Line Cutters on both.)
2. **Fewest silver makes half of Silver dead and lets round-end gifts decide it.** Flaunt was never taken, Buyout once, and the silver members were never bought, because holding silver loses the contest. With everyone at 0, a single silver handed over after the last minion (Kingmakers, and Spoilsport or Loan Officers next to it) decided the contest with no possible answer. Dee lost to it in all three rounds of game B and in round III of game A. Possible changes: gifts that land before the last placement, or a silver contest the holder can still defend; and give Flaunt and Buyout a reason to hold silver.
3. **Taking members from each other is the best part of the game, and the Ghost is its worst moment.** All 24 diaries named a rival's move that changed their plan, and six of eight closing notes called the swaps and steals the highlight (Promote, Demote, Swindle, Poach, Repossessors, Buyout). The Ghost, handed over through Demote, Swindle or Gift Swappers, cost the receiver 5 followers plus the member taken, about 8 to 9 in one turn. Both times that player was Ava, and both times she named it her worst moment. Possible change: limit what Demote and Swindle can hand back, or soften what the Ghost costs when it arrives.
4. **Stacked steals make the other players stop building (game B only).** The Magenta Cloak's +2 per steal on top of Accomplice, Help Yourself, Con Men and Extortionist gave Ben about 13 resources by his second round I placement. The other three answered by turning everything into followers at once: 11 follower buys against 4 members in round II, versus 6 and 6 in game A. Ava: "the best plan for a non-magenta player was to turn everything into followers immediately, which flattens choices." Possible change: cap how often the cloak pays in a turn, or make steals that come from members pay less.
5. **Timing that the card doesn't say costs players whole turns.** Each of these surprised a player mid-game and cost them a placement:
   - Test Drive on a Round End member does nothing.
   - Recruit Twice gave only blue in 3 of 4 takes.
   - The Blue Cloak paid before Debutantes doubled.
   - The pile reshuffles the moment it empties, which left Markdown with nothing.
   - Right Away overrides the token taken that same turn.
   - Loyalists also locks your own members.
   - Grey members and Dabblers' wild didn't count the way players expected for member colors.
   - The +1 goes only to a space's first-ever taker.

   Possible change: print when each of these happens on the card or space itself.
