# Polish pass, Nov 12: what stood out (v0.99.738)

Two sources: 300 games of Hard bots on today's rules, and 6 games played by AI players who wrote a diary each round. Only things that stood out are here. A thing flagged by both comes first.

Small cults: winners with 5 or fewer members took 47% of the bot games (bar: at least 20%). Winners ended near 81 followers.

## Flagged by both bots and AI players

**1. Going first, going last and runs of turns decide games.**
- **Line Cutters** (cost 4, worth 3): "You go first every round."
- **Crunch Time** (Purple, round II): "+2 purple. Your next two turns come right after this one."
- **Overtime** (Purple, center): "+4 purple. +1 minion this round. Take the Goes Last token: that minion goes after everyone else's."
- **Reprise** (Green, round I): "Copy an action with your minion. +1 green."

At the table: in one AI game the Line Cutters holder opened rounds II and III with Crunch Time and then took two of Heist, Con Men, Swindle and Largesse before anyone else placed. That player went from 16 to 31 followers and won by 15. In another, a player took Overtime and then Reprise of Overtime, held the Goes Last token with two extra minions, and placed their last four minions in a row after everyone else had run out, for about 40 followers. All four players named that turn as what ended the game. Seven of eight players called Line Cutters cheap.
Bots: a player holding the Purple Cloak won 42% of games (25% is even). Players who took Overtime won 17 points more often than players with as many members; Crunch Time 12 points more.
What kind of change: a price or follower change on Line Cutters, or limits on how many minions can wait behind the Goes Last token (for example, Reprise not copying Overtime).

**2. Silver's round II spaces sit empty, and a round-end gift decides Fewest silver.**
- Silver's contest: Fewest silver, counts the silver you hold when the round ends.
- **Flaunt** (round II): "+1 of each other color per 3 silver you hold, up to 3 each. +1 silver."
- **Buyout** (round II): "Recruit a rival's member, paying them its cost in silver. +1 silver."
- **Kingmakers** (cost 2): "Round End: give rivals up to 3 of your silver, split as you choose. +1 follower per silver given."

At the table: Flaunt was never taken in 4 AI games with Silver, Buyout once. The silver members that want you to hold silver (Hustlers, Bookies, Patron Saints, Splurgers) were never bought. With everyone at 0 silver, one silver handed over by Kingmakers after the last minion decided the contest, and the player it hit could not answer (Dee, three rounds in a row in one game).
Bots: Buyout left empty in 82% of rounds it was open, Flaunt in 73%.
You said players should give up the contest to take the resources. The AI players did not, in any of the games.

**3. Cash In sits empty.**
- **Cash In** (White, center): "Turn all your white into followers. +1 white."

At the table: never taken in either White AI game. Bots took it in half the rounds it was open, last of any center, and its takers won 14 points less often than players with as many members. You said you use it to good effect, so this may be how bots and AI players read it, not the card.

**Member movement by color set (your Purple, Green, Silver game).**
Bots, counting members that leave a cult (taken, swapped, given or discarded) across all 4 players in a game:
- All tables: 16 a game.
- Red on the table: 23. Without Red: 10.
- Purple, Green and Silver together: 7 (5 when the fourth color is not Red or Magenta).
Members bought were about the same everywhere (4.4 held per player at game end with Purple, Green and Silver, 4.7 on all tables). So those tables recruit as much; members just don't change cults. Almost all movement comes from Red's **Promote** and **Demote** and Magenta's **Swindle** and **Poach**. Purple and Green have no space that moves a member, and Silver's only one, **Buyout** ("Recruit a rival's member, paying them its cost in silver. +1 silver."), sits empty. Green only moves members when it copies a Red or Magenta space.

## Flagged by the AI players only

**4. Copy spaces make the biggest space go off 3 or 4 times in one round.**
- **Cover** (Green, round II): "Copy an action with a rival's minion on it. +1 green."
- **Heist** (Magenta, round II): "Steal 4 followers and 4 resources. +1 magenta."
- **Largesse** (Silver, center): "Give rivals 12 silver from the bank, split as you choose. +10 followers."

At the table: Heist went off 4 times in each of rounds II and III of one game (the space, then Echo, Cover, and Reprise of Cover). Largesse went off 4 times in one round III: 40 followers, 30 of them to one player. "Exciting the first time, oppressive by the third." The copy spaces are still the best-liked spaces in the game. Bots: Cover earns about 4 more than a typical round II space.

**5. The Ghost hurts most when Demote or Swindle hands it over.**
- **Ghost** (worth 0): "Whenever it joins your cult, -5 followers."
- **Demote** (Red, round II): "+3 red. Swap any member for one costing up to 3 less, from another cult or the shop."
- **Swindle** (Magenta, round II): "Steal a rival's member costing up to 7; give them a shop member of your choice in its place. +1 magenta."

At the table: the receiver lost 5 followers plus the member taken, about 8 to 9 in one turn. Giving it was "the most fun of the night"; getting it was the worst moment in two games. Taking members from each other was the most-praised part of every game.

**6. Rules about how members count surprised players.**
Dabblers ("Counts as 2 wild members") and grey members like Alchemists in "Most member colors" and Melting Pot; Die-Hards paying nothing because of a rival's Dabblers; Loyalists ("Your other members can't be swapped") not protecting itself and also locking your own swaps. Recruit Twice ("Recruit a member from the discard pile now. +5 blue.") reads as two recruits; it is one. These belong in the rename and words pass.

## Bots only (bot numbers, not design calls)

Members whose holders won much more or less often than players with as many members:
- Up: Last-Minute Shopper +16, Big Tent +15, Cheerleaders +12.
- Down: Splurgers -17, Brokers -16, Encore -15, Bookies -14, Agents -13.
Greatest Hits (your own minions version) takers still won 17 points more often than players with as many members. The AI players called it a best turn every game and working.

## Test Drive (you asked)
**Test Drive** (Blue, round I): "Discard a shop member. Use its effect now. +1 blue."
- Bots: taken in 91% of rounds it was open. One placement there gave 4.3 in round I, 5.1 in round II and 5.6 in round III, a little under a typical round I space (5.0, 5.7, 6.0). Its best turns gave 30 and 33. Its takers won as often as players with as many members.
- AI players: it was a big play on Accomplice, Con Men, Extra Hand and Debutantes ("lives or dies on the shop"), and did nothing on Round End members like Kingmakers. In game 6 a player called it "a free 8-cost member in round I" on Seat Filler, then got nothing (the bug below).
- Two things pull the bot numbers down: bots that picked Seat Filler got nothing until tonight's fix, and a Hard bot judges the discarded member's effect roughly. Both point the same way as your read: the real card is stronger than these numbers.

## Fixed
**Test Drive** ("Discard a shop member. Use its effect now. +1 blue.") on **Seat Filler** ("Round Start: you may put this in the shop and recruit a shop member costing up to 3 more, for free.") asked which member to take, then gave nothing. It now recruits that member for free, as you said Nov 10 ("Seat Filler is fine").

## Not a bug
Recruit Twice giving only blue: the players could not afford the member in the discard pile those turns.
