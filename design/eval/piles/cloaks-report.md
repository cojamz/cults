# Cloaks under each shop

What was played: 120 games with Hard bots, 4 players, the 10-card deck, on 40 tables of 4 colors with no Blue (All piles has no Blue, so all three shops played the same tables). Each table was played three times: once with the Current shop, once with Grey piles, once with All piles. The grey piles in these games were the earlier grey set (Party Crasher, Old Maid, Anarchist, Line Cutters, Con Men), before the switch to 5 grey piles. No card was changed.

"Paid" is how many resources a cloak gave its wearer over one game. "Range" is the least and the most it paid in any one game. Each cloak was worn in 20 to 25 games per shop, so a win share can move 15 or 20 points by luck alone; with 4 players, an even cloak wins about 25%.

## What each cloak paid, and how often its wearer won

**Black Cloak** (Fence): "Whenever any player steals, +1 magenta."
- Current: paid 19 a game, range 14 to 32, wearer won 30%.
- Grey piles: paid 23, range 12 to 41, won 26%.
- All piles: paid 26, range 10 to 55, won 26%.

**Green Cloak** (Impressionist): "Whenever any player copies an action, +2 of its color."
- Current: paid 21, range 16 to 36, won 25%.
- Grey piles: paid 22, range 16 to 32, won 45%.
- All piles: paid 24, range 16 to 56, won 45%.

**Silver Cloak** (Caterer): "Whenever silver is gifted, +1 silver."
- Current: paid 10, range 7 to 14, won 5%.
- Grey piles: paid 10, range 8 to 12, won 5%.
- All piles: paid 11, range 7 to 24, won 33%.

**Purple Cloak** (Pocket Watch): "Whenever a player's turn is skipped, +1 purple."
- Current: paid 8, range 3 to 13, won 22%.
- Grey piles: paid 8, range 3 to 10, won 9%.
- All piles: paid 8, range 3 to 17, won 17%.

**Red Cloak** (Recruiter): "Whenever you recruit a member, +1 red."
- Current: paid 14, range 10 to 20, won 24%.
- Grey piles: paid 16, range 11 to 26, won 44%.
- All piles: paid 14, range 9 to 21, won 40%.

**Yellow Cloak** (Guest Book): "Whenever you recruit a member of a color none of your members have, +2 yellow."
- Current: paid 13, range 8 to 18, won 30%.
- Grey piles: paid 13, range 8 to 18, won 35%.
- All piles: paid 12, range 4 to 18, won 13%.

**White Cloak** (Money Changers): "Whenever any player buys followers, +1 white."
- Current: paid 15, range 7 to 21, won 44%.
- Grey piles: paid 11, range 7 to 15, won 20%.
- All piles: paid 11, range 6 to 18, won 4%.

## What this says about Cory's worry

He was right about the cloaks that pay on what any player does. Under All piles, the Black, Green and Silver Cloaks pay about the same on average, but how much they pay depends on which piles were dealt far more than it does with the Current shop. The Black Cloak paid anywhere from 10 to 55 in a game, against 14 to 32 now. The Green Cloak paid 16 to 56, against 16 to 36 now.

The reason is that a pile of 10 copies of one stealing or copying member puts that effect in many cults at once. In the game where the Black Cloak paid 55, the piles included **Cutpurse** ("On Recruit: steal 1 resource from the rival with the most resources. Round Start: steal 1 resource from the rival with the most resources.") and **Shoplifter** ("Whenever you recruit, steal 1 resource."). Several players owned them, so steals came every round. In the game where it paid 10, no stealing member was dealt.

The same goes for the Green Cloak. Its 56-paid game had **Stunt Double** ("On Recruit: copy the action you just took.") and **Muse** ("Whenever a rival copies an action, +1 green.") in the piles. In its 16-paid game, the copying members were minor.

The cloaks that pay on your own recruits (Red, Yellow) swing about the same under every shop. That's because what they count is something every player does every game.

The White Cloak's wearer went from winning most often under the Current shop to almost never under All piles, while its payout fell only a little. That looks like a shift in how the bots win under piles (more members, fewer bought followers) more than the cloak itself. On 25 games it could also be luck.

## Winning with few members

Games won by someone with 5 or fewer members (the bar is at least 20%):
- Current: 6 of 40 (15%).
- Grey piles: 2 of 40 (5%).
- All piles: 2 of 40 (5%).

Both pile shops push winners toward bigger cults.
