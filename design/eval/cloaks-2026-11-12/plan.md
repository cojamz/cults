# Cloak rebalance: plan (Nov 12)

Cory, Nov 12: "It also means then we need to rebalance cloaks."

Why wait: today's Hard bots place only about 1 in 4 minions on their cloak's color, so they mostly ignore what the cloak does. Cloak numbers from them say little about the cloak itself. The skill thread is building a bot that weighs the cloak's power; measure with that bot.

What we already have (today's Hard bots): Nov 12, holders of the Purple Cloak won 42% of 4-player games (25% is even). Nov 3: the starting cloak moved win chance by about 25 points; Silver lowest, White and Green highest.

Steps once the new bot exists:
1. Control run: the action harness, DECK=10, 4 players, 300 games, the new bot in every seat, today's cloaks. Per cloak: win share, final followers, resources gained, how often the holder places on the cloak's color, how often the cloak pays and how much.
2. Check the bot uses each cloak: placements on the cloak's color well above 1 in 4. If not, report back to the skill thread before reading any cloak number.
3. For each cloak far from even (win share outside about 18% to 32%, or followers more than 5 from the table average), one change at a time against the control, on the same deals: a follower or resource number on that cloak (Cory designs the numbers; these are test settings only).
4. Results page for Cory: one table, a row per cloak, each cloak's full text and the setting spelled out with a worked example, leading with how the cloak feels at the table. Changes go to him for the call.
