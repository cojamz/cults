# Largesse numbers: test plan (Nov 24)

**Cory's question** (Nov 11): "Largesse is good, numbers need some adjustments." / "largesse we figure out via balance testing. I think with some of the green changes it should be less absurd."

**The card today** (Silver, round III): **Largesse** "Give rivals 12 silver from the bank, split as you choose. +10 followers."

**What we expect**: fewer followers makes it weaker; we don't know by how much, or which number puts its takers back among the other spaces. That is what the run measures. Earlier runs (Nov 1 to 12) had its takers winning about 41%, and AI players saw it go off 4 times in one round III through the copy spaces (40 followers).

**Control**: the game as it is today, Largesse at 12 silver and +10 followers.

**The one change**: Largesse's followers only, silver stays 12. Three settings, each against the same control games:
- 12 silver, +8 followers
- 12 silver, +6 followers
- 12 silver, +4 followers

**Table**: Hard bots that read cards, 4 players, 10-card deck, By color contests, Silver dealt at every table (so Largesse is on every wheel), 120 games per setting, same deals game for game. Each game has a time limit in the runner (a stuck game is dropped).

**What we read** (per setting, on the same games): how often Largesse is taken per game, how often its takers win, how many followers they end with, and how that sits against the other spaces' range in the same games (the report's bar: takers' win rate across the middle 80% of spaces). Also Silver's win rate as a whole, since Largesse is its round III space.

**Pick rule**: the highest follower number where Largesse's takers land inside the other spaces' range and it is still taken in most games. If +10 already lands inside, the answer is "keep it".

**Cost**: plain-code games, about 20 seconds each, 480 games on 4 cores, about 45 minutes. Runs through the shared trial runner so idle runner threads can help. Checked every 30 minutes.
