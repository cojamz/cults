Largesse numbers

Largesse (Silver, round III): "Give rivals 12 silver from the bank, split as you choose. +10 followers."

At the table today: in 1 game of every 5, other players copy Largesse in round III (Cover, Echo, Fan Club and the like), so it happens 4 to 6 times in one round. That is 40 to 60 followers and 48 to 72 silver handed out in a single round. Even so, the player who takes it first usually loses: they hand rivals 12 silver, and rivals turn it into members and followers.

At 6 followers, players stop copying it. It happens once, at most three times.

An even share is 25%: one winner among 4 players. With about 35 games per row, a gap under 10 points can be chance, so the three 6-follower rows win about the same. How much silver it gives did not clearly change anything.

A second, larger run (65 games, the same deals twice) checked 10 against 8 followers: the first player to take it won 25% at 10 and 22% at 8, ending with 81 and 77 followers.

How it was played: Hard computer players, 4 players, 10-card deck, Silver dealt at every table, each row playing the same deals. Nov 24.
