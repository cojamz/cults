# Magenta, review 2 (Nov 8)

Magenta's contest is **Most resources** (every color together; +4 / +6 / +10, ties split rounded up). That makes every steal count twice in the contest: you go up by what you take and the rival goes down by the same amount. Taking 3 from the leader closes a 6-resource gap. It also pulls against Silver's **Fewest silver**: taking silver from a Silver player helps them, so a thief takes their other colors.

**The color in one line.** Magenta steals resources and members (rulebook). Cory: "Whenever you say steal, that's the inherent… meaning" (from any rivals). "Aggression is intentional."

**A great Magenta game.** In round I you take Help Yourself and a second steal, so you finish the round holding the most resources and win +4. Rivals learn to spend down before your turn and to keep off the Magenta spaces you're working. In round II you hit whoever is hoarding (often a rival going for the Most resources lead, or a White player saving up for Cash In), and you steal followers from the leader with Heist. In round III Poach takes the best member on the table, while steal members (Second-Story Man, Accomplice, Extortionist) keep you on top of the resource count when the +10 contest is scored. Rivals answer with Bodyguards and Snitch, by spending before round end, and by taking the Magenta spaces first.

**A fresh start, if Magenta had nothing.** A plain steal that grows each round (Help Yourself). A steal that reads the table: where rivals' minions are, or who leads what. A big steal aimed at whoever is hoarding. A member steal as the round III finish (Poach). Members that steal at fixed moments, members that turn steals into followers, and one or two counters (Snitch, Bodyguards). Help Yourself, Heist, Poach, Accomplice, Second-Story Man, Heist Crew, Kidnapper and Snitch already fit. Stash, Lie Low, Shake Down and Desperado don't.

## Assessment

**Spaces**
- **Help Yourself** (round I) "Steal 3 resources in round I, 4 in round II, 5 in round III." **Keep** (Cory: star; now from any rivals, as he asked). It's the color's plain steal that grows each round, and under Most resources each one is a 6-to-10-resource swing in the contest.
- **Stash** (round I) "+2 magenta in round I, +3 in round II, +4 in round III. This turn, if you recruit a member, +1 follower in round I, +2 in round II, +3 in round III." **Replace** (Cory: "not on identity at ALL"; Nov 4: "too many, like, if you buy a member, get some followers"). It's Gather and Allowance with a Magenta label: it steals nothing.
- **Lie Low** (round I) "Steal 1 resource. Rivals can't steal from you this round. +1 magenta." **Replace** (Cory: "too frustrating for opponents, no counter. kind of a corner to hide in"). It also breaks a hard rule: it's the only space whose effect lasts after it's taken (Cory, Nov 4: "I don't like this, like, giving you a passive for the round"), and the table has to remember who's protected until round end.
- **Heist** (round II) "Steal 3 followers, split among rivals as you choose. +1 magenta." **Keep** (Cory: fine, "not super fun"). It's the only space that steals followers, and taking 3 from the leader is a 6-follower swing. It's quiet, but it does its job, and nothing below beats it.
- **Shake Down** (round II) "Choose a rival. They choose one of their members costing 7 or less, then pay you its cost or you steal it. +1 magenta." **Replace** (Cory: "really feels bad annoying"). It breaks a hard rule: the rival has to decide during your turn, twice (which member, then pay or lose it).
- **Poach** (round III) "Recruit a rival's member you choose, for free. +1 magenta." **Keep** (Cory: star).

**Members** (prices as a player sees them)
- **Accomplice** (5) "On Recruit: steal 3 resources." **Keep** (fine). It's Magenta's cheap round I member. Under the new contest its 3 is a 6-resource swing in the count.
- **Second-Story Man** (8) "Round End: before contests are scored, steal 2 resources from one rival." **Keep.** Cory marked it weak because "contests don't really rely on resources". Now Magenta's contest does: just before scoring, 2 from the leader is a 4-resource swing, often enough to flip the lead. Cory, Nov 8: "I can hear an argument."
- **Desperado** (7) "While you have the fewest followers (ties count), your steals take 2 more resources." **Replace** (Cory: "Confusing"). It also does nothing once you're ahead, which is a standing no ("I don't like stuff that becomes useless when you're winning").
- **Extortionist** (9) "On Recruit and at Round Start: steal 2 resources." **Fix, next in line** (Cory: "boring and complicated, bad mix"). The two timings are the complicated part. Fix: "Round Start: steal 3 resources." That's one timing, bigger, and still fires in round III. Not one of this review's picks.
- **Bodyguards** (10) "Rivals can't steal from you." **Keep** (Cory: "too breaking if it's hard to move members"). Since tonight, Body Snatchers can swap it away from any rival, so it's no longer locked in. Watch it.
- **Heist Crew** (9) "On Use, once per game: steal half of a rival's biggest pile, rounded down." **Keep** (Cory: "i love it"). Flag: the name is close to the Heist space.
- **Double Agent** (10) "On Recruit: give this member to a rival and recruit one of theirs." **Keep, watch** (Cory: "too good"). The usual fix for "too good" is price: try 11 (Claude's fill-in; Cory sets the number).
- **Kidnapper** (10) "On Recruit and at Round Start: steal a rival's cheapest member." **Keep** (Cory: "Kinda like. a little fun").
- **Snitch** (6) "Whenever a rival steals from you, they lose 1 follower." **Keep** (Cory: star). It's the counter.
- **Shoplifter** (9) "Whenever you recruit, steal 1 resource." **Replace, next in line** (Cory: "i don't like this triggering so often. annoying to remember"). It fires on every recruit, including follower buys. Not one of this review's picks. The member survivor below could fill this slot instead of Desperado's.

**The cloak**
- **Fence** (Magenta Cloak) "Whenever you steal, +1 magenta." **Keep** (Cory: star as a cloak, "I like the robes a little weaker and in a vacuum"). His "boring… members should lean more towards point generation" is about members, which is why the member pick below pays in followers.

(Crew "Worth 2 followers." and Fixer "On Use, once per game: spend magenta equal to a rival's member's cost to steal it." aren't in the 10-card deck. Both stay out.)

## Picks

1. **Stash** (round I). Cory marked it weak and said "I don't know what Magenta should have. We have to brainstorm." It's the clearest miss in the color: a Magenta space that steals nothing.
2. **Lie Low** (round I). Cory marked it weak, and it breaks the hard rule that a space finishes the moment it's taken. With Stash it opens two round I slots side by side, so the two are brainstormed from one pool, and the two that fill them must touch different pieces.
3. **Shake Down** (round II). Cory marked it weak ("really feels bad annoying"), and it breaks the hard rule that no one decides during another player's turn.
4. **Desperado** (member, 7). Cory: "Confusing." It also does nothing once you're winning. It's the weakest member: you'd miss it least.

## Replacements

### Picks 1 and 2: the two round I slots beside Help Yourself

What a round I turn looks like: 4 players, 3 minions each, snake order 1-2-3-4-4-3-2-1-1-2-3-4. Each player starts with 1 resource (the cloak's) and gains about 2 to 4 per space, so rivals hold about 2 to 6 resources when they're stolen from, and almost no one has a member. Comparison spaces at that moment: **Toast** "+3 silver in round I… Each rival +1 silver in round I…", **Gather** "+2 red in round I… This turn, if you recruit a member, +1 follower in round I…", **Sleep In** "+4 purple in round I… Take the Goes Last token…", and **Help Yourself** "Steal 3 resources in round I, 4 in round II, 5 in round III."

**Survivor A (for Stash's slot): Turf**, Cory's own idea (Nov 4: "for each minion on a magenta space, steal one…"; bot-tested, his "Yeah, maybe")
- **Printed:** "Steal 1 resource from each rival per minion they have on Magenta spaces. +1 magenta."
- **Played, round I:** 4 players, Magenta, Red, Silver and Purple in the game. You place your second minion as the 8th placement of the round. P2 is on Help Yourself, and P4 is on the other Magenta round I space. You steal 1 from P2 and 1 from P4, plus +1 magenta: +3 for you, and two rivals lose 1 each. The best other open space is **Toast**, +3 silver, which also gives each rival +1. In the Most resources count that's a 5-resource swing on P2 and P4, against Toast's +3 for you and +1 for each rival. Taken as the 1st placement of the round, Turf gives only +1 magenta.
- **The choice:** it's yours: when to take it. If you wait, more rivals stand on Magenta, but a rival Magenta player may take Turf first and steal from you. Both sides are real.
- **Top case:** round III, 5 minions each, 6 Magenta spaces. Late in the round rivals hold 4 Magenta spots (P2 two, P3 one, P4 one), so you steal 4, plus +1 magenta. That's below Help Yourself's 5 in the same round, so it isn't broken. A Green player who copies Turf steals from rivals on Magenta in the same way.
- **Who feels it:** everyone who went to Magenta, the other thief most of all. It gives a reason to block it and a reason to keep off Magenta late in the round.
- **Against the current card:** Stash's best turn (round III, +4 magenta, +3 followers with a recruit) is bigger in raw numbers. Turf's moment is new: the thieves get robbed. Cory has already said he dislikes Stash, so keeping it isn't the default.
- **Six principles.** Intuitive: one sentence; the minions are on the wheel to count. Fun: a sting on whoever came to steal. Interacts: with its own color's spaces and every rival who uses them. Powerful: the amount depends on the table, not a fixed small number. Engine or counter: counters a rival thief. Its color: new for Magenta; no Magenta card reads the wheel yet.
- **Not refused:** Oct 31, "choose a color, steal 1 from each rival with a minion on its spaces" was refused in a list. On Nov 4 Cory picked Inside Job, "Steal 2 resources from each rival with a minion on a Magenta space." ("interesting idea. I kind of like it"), and gave Turf as his own version. Inside Job is the flat-number variant of the same card: shown here, not counted as a separate pick.
- **Hard rules:** no one decides on another player's turn (you pick which resources, on your turn). Nothing to remember. Works in any game that has Magenta.

**Survivor B (for Lie Low's slot): Racket** (Cory, Nov 4: "It's cool… keep it in our back pocket")
- **Printed:** "Steal 1 resource from the leader of each contest you aren't leading. +1 magenta." (A tied lead counts as one contest; you choose which tied leader to take from. Claude's fill-in.)
- **Played, round I:** same table, your second minion. Most resources: P3 leads with 5, so you steal 1. Most members of one color: P2 holds the only member, a red one, so you steal 1. Fewest silver: you hold 0 silver, so you're tied for that lead and get nothing. Went last: you'll be the last placer when it resolves, so nothing. That's 2 stolen plus +1 magenta = +3, two different rivals each lose 1, and the Most resources leader is pulled back. The best other open space is **Toast**, +3.
- **The choice:** you choose among tied leaders. That's a real pick (the one closest to you in the resource count).
- **Top case:** round III, you lead none of 4 contests, so 4 steals plus +1 = 5. That's level with Help Yourself's 5, so it isn't broken. If you lead every contest it gives only +1 magenta. That's the "useless when winning" risk, but leading all four at once is rare. Flagged.
- **Who feels it:** every contest leader. It brakes a player running away with any color's contest, so it ties Magenta to every contest without naming another color.
- **Against the current card:** Lie Low's best turn is steal 1, +1 magenta, and no rival can steal from you for the rest of the round. Racket gives more and leaves nothing to remember. Cory has already said he dislikes Lie Low.
- **Six principles.** Intuitive: one sentence, but you read each contest's leader off the rim. It's the least intuitive survivor (4 to 5 rims to check). Fun: small stings, spread out. Interacts: through every color's contest, the shared piece. Powerful: grows with how far behind you are. Engine or counter: counters a runaway contest leader. Its color: new for Magenta, a thief working the contests ("design more from the competitions").
- **Not refused:** Cory picked its earlier form on Oct 31 ("Steal 1 of each contest's color from its leader", ✅ "but i want more ideas") and kept it in his back pocket on Nov 4. Same shape as nothing he refused.
- **Hard rules:** pass. Readers often overrate stings on the leader, so weigh that.

**Why Turf and Racket together:** they touch different pieces (minions on the wheel, contests), so the round I set is a plain steal, a steal off the wheel, and a steal off the contests. Risk: Cory refused a list of "steal 1 from each rival who…" ideas on Oct 31 as "don't feel thoughtful or interesting". Both of these got a later yes-maybe from him, but side by side they could read as that list again.

**Sharp question if he says no to both:** should Lie Low's slot be a steal at all, or should Magenta's third round I space do something other than steal (as Gather does beside Red's swaps)? No non-steal idea survived the judging below.

**Cut for the round I slots, and why**
- "Steal 1 resource from each rival." 3 at 4 players, which is Help Yourself's round I split three ways. A near copy.
- "Steal 1 follower from each rival." 3 followers at any round. Heist does the same in round II, so this is a near copy that also comes earlier.
- "Steal 2 (3, 4) resources from the rival who placed the previous minion." Always a worse Help Yourself.
- Pay for your recruit with a rival's resources, as a round I space. It's open all game, and in round III a rival holding 12 pays for a 10-cost member, which is round III center size. Too strong at round I. It moves to pick 3.
- "Swap all your resources with a rival's." Round III you hold 2 and the hoarder 20, so +18. That's far over 2 times any round III space. It's also a swap (Red's verb) and the same shape as the refused magenta swap (Oct 31, B).
- "Steal what the previous action gave its taker." Echo's job with a steal on it. Cory doesn't want overlaps with Green (Tryout).
- "Steal a rival's cloak" (for the round, or swap cloaks). The round version lasts past the moment. The swap is Red's verb. Both were tested before at 16 against 18.
- "+2 (3, 4) magenta, steal 1." Cory: "magenta already has this but stealing" (Oct 31).
- Mug the Leader "Steal 2 resources from the rival with the most followers…". Cory, Nov 4: "too similar to what exists already."
- "Steal 2 resources from each rival with more members than you." In round I almost no one has members, so it does nothing (the same failure as the refused round I swap).

### Pick 3: Shake Down's slot (round II)

What round II looks like: 4 minions each. Rivals hold about 4 to 10 resources between recruits, and the Most resources leader holds more. Players have 1 to 4 members. Bar at this moment: **Heist** "Steal 3 followers, split among rivals as you choose. +1 magenta.", **Promote** "Swap any member with one in another cult or the shop costing up to 3 more. +3 red.", **Recruit Twice** "Recruit an extra time this turn. +3 blue.", and Shake Down itself.

**Survivor C: On Their Tab** (new, Claude's)
- **Printed:** "This turn, pay for your recruit with resources you steal from one rival. +1 magenta." (Pay any part they can't cover yourself. It's a steal, so Bodyguards blocks it and Snitch and the cloak fire. Claude's fill-ins.)
- **Played, round II:** your second minion. P3 holds 7 (3 red, 2 purple, 2 silver), and a 7-cost red member (3 red + 4 any) sits in the shop. You recruit it, paying with all 7 of P3's resources. You gain a 7-cost member, P3 drops from 7 to 0, and your own pile is untouched for the Most resources count. The best other space, **Heist**, takes 3 followers. Shake Down at its best (the rival pays 7 or loses a 7-cost member) is the same size, so this replaces it like for like.
- **Both sides:** you choose which rival and what to recruit (a member, or a 1, 5 or 10 follower card). Buying followers with someone else's 10 is a real alternative to a member. No rival decides anything.
- **Top case:** round III, the richest rival holds 14. You recruit the shop's 10-cost member with their resources. Compare **Poach** (a rival's member for free) and **Initiation** "Recruit a member in the shop costing 10 or less, for free." The gain is the same size, plus a 10-resource sting. That's at most about 2 times the best round III space, at the line, not over it. Shop prices (top about 11) and the 10-follower card cap it on their own. Flag: in round III Magenta would have two spaces of center size.
- **Who feels it:** whoever is holding the most. That's usually the Most resources leader, or a White player saving white for Cash In. They have to spend before your turn, or lose it to your recruit. It takes Silver's other colors, not silver, because silver taken from a Silver player helps them.
- **Against the current card:** the same size as Shake Down, but no rival decides anything during your turn. The moment is new: "I bought Double Agent with her money." It also keeps Cory's old plan of holding resources and buying followers alive: you recruit without spending your own pile, so you can still win Most resources.
- **Six principles.** Intuitive: one sentence, and the recruit works the usual way. Fun: a big, visible sting. Interacts: with whoever hoards, in any color. Powerful: the size depends on the rival's pile, not a fixed number. Engine or counter: an engine with Magenta's contest (you keep your own pile), and a counter to hoarding. Its color: new for Magenta, stealing the recruit itself.
- **Not refused:** nothing in the ledger has this shape. Possible worry: Cory dropped cost reductions. This isn't one, since the full cost is paid, but say so.

**Survivor D: Mugging** (Cory, Nov 4: "I actually do like this one… so elemental and interesting… should be a default that we consider")
- **Printed:** "Steal half of one rival's resources, rounded down. +1 magenta."
- **Played, round II:** your second minion. P3 holds 9, so you take 4, plus +1 magenta: +5, and P3 drops to 5. That's a 9-resource swing in Most resources. **Heist** takes 3 followers. **Help Yourself** steals 4 in round II, but it's a round I space, usually taken by now.
- **Both sides:** you choose the rival. A big pile, or the rival nearest you in the count.
- **Top case:** round III, the hoarder holds 20, so you take 10. That's a Poach-size gain and a 20-resource swing in the +10 contest. Under 2 times the best round III space. Not broken.
- **Who feels it:** the hoarder, every time. Holding a big pile becomes risky, which is the answer to Magenta's own contest.
- **Against the current card:** in round II, about the same size as Shake Down when a rival holds 8 or more. Simpler than Shake Down: no rival decides anything.
- **Six principles.** Intuitive: "half" needs no explaining. Fun: a sting on the hoarder. Interacts: with any rival's pile. Powerful: the amount is half the rival's pile, not a fixed number. Engine or counter: counters hoarding. Its color: the purest Magenta card in the list.
- **Main risk, not refused but close:** Heist Crew "On Use, once per game: steal half of a rival's biggest pile, rounded down." is a near copy in the same color, and Cory picked it a day after praising Mugging. They differ in size (a whole pile against one color's pile) and in kind (a space against a once-per-game member). Cory has said yes to siblings that differ in size and no to near copies. Rank it below On Their Tab for that reason.

**Cut for Shake Down's slot, and why**
- Clean Out "Steal all of one color from a rival, up to 5." Uncapped, it's the shape Cory called too strong ("i have one silver, i use that to take all of somebody's gold"). At 5, it barely beats Help Yourself's 4 in round II. Cory: "I don't like clean out."
- Squeeze "Steal 2 resources from each rival with more members than you." A good contest tie (it taxes the member-heavy cults Cory worried about at 11:43pm), but it's the same "steal from each rival who…" shape as Turf and Racket. Sent together, it would make the list read as one idea. His Nov 4 answer was "Yeah, maybe."
- Shake Down with the thief choosing ("steal a member costing 7 or less, or resources equal to its cost"). A smaller Poach in round II, and the member side nearly always wins.
- "Steal a rival's cheapest member." A copy of Kidnapper and a smaller Poach.
- "Recruit a rival's member, paying its cost to them." A smaller Poach that also hands the rival as much as you get (that shape flopped in testing).
- "Steal 1 resource per member from the rival with the most members." Matches Help Yourself's 4 only when a rival holds 4 or more members. No bigger moment.
- Pickpocket "Use a rival's member's On Use as your own; it counts as used for them." Leans toward Green's copying. Tested at 13.
- "Steal a rival's cloak for the round." Lasts past the moment.
- "Steal 3 followers from the leader." A copy of Heist.

### Pick 4: Desperado's slot (member, 7)

**Survivor E: Cat Burglar** (new, Claude's; same price, 7)
- **Printed:** "Whenever you steal, +1 follower." (Once per steal, however many rivals it takes from, as the cloak pays. Claude's fill-in.)
- **Played, round II:** bought in round II. That round you take Heist (3 followers) and Turf (2 resources), and Second-Story Man fires at round end: 3 steals, +3 followers. Desperado in the same round does nothing if you aren't last in followers.
- **Top case:** round III, built around stealing: Help Yourself, Turf, Mugging or On Their Tab, Accomplice gone, Extortionist and Second-Story Man firing. About 5 steals, so +5. Over a game from round II, about 8 to 10 followers for a 7-cost. A 7 card buys 7 followers. Close to Proselytizers "Whenever you recruit, +1 follower." (Cory: "I like it"). Strong, not broken.
- **Both sides:** no choice in it.
- **Who feels it:** the owner, as a payoff for every steal. Each steal already stings someone, and this puts points on top. Cory has said a quiet member that pays its owner is fine.
- **Against the current card:** Desperado's best turn: last in followers, Help Yourself in round III takes 7 instead of 5. That's bigger, but it vanishes once you lead, and Cory called it confusing. Cat Burglar pays every time, whatever the score. It's a clearly better card to own, though a smaller single moment.
- **Six principles.** Intuitive: one sentence. Fun: a steady payoff for what Magenta already does. Interacts: through every steal, its own color's and other colors' (Gold, grey Con Men). Powerful: modest per steal, it adds up. Engine or counter: the engine piece for a steal build. Its color: Magenta steals turned into points, which Cory asked for ("members should lean more towards point generation").
- **Main risk:** it shares its trigger with the cloak, Fence "Whenever you steal, +1 magenta.", and Cory wants cloaks different from the color's members. They pay in different things (followers, magenta), and Cory has said yes to siblings like that, but he may call them too close. Robin Hood (+1 follower on steals from the leader) was "eh, it's ok", disliked only because it went dead when you were winning, and this has no such condition.
- **Not refused:** no.

**Cut for Desperado's slot, and why**
- "Round End: +1 follower per 3 resources you hold." A formula on your own pile, with no stealing in it. It also echoes White's Misers ("Game End: +1 follower per 2 white you hold.").
- "On Use, once per round: steal 1 follower." At most 3 a game. Too small to remember.
- "Round End: before contests are scored, steal 1 resource from each rival." Too close to Second-Story Man.
- "Whenever a rival steals from you, steal 2 back." Too close to Snitch, and it fires on a rival's turn.
- "Whenever you steal a member, +3 followers." Red's Foster Parents "Whenever a member joins your cult from a rival's, +2 followers." (Cory: "Core red").
- "Your steals take 2 more resources." Every steal 2 bigger, about 20 extra resources a game: too strong. At +1 it's the retired Sticky Fingers.
- "Round End: +1 follower per rival you stole from this round." The table has to remember who you stole from.
- "Round End: steal 1 follower from each rival holding fewer resources than you." Goes dead when you're behind, and stacks conditions.
- "Whenever you steal from the rival with the most followers, +1 follower." Robin Hood; dead when you're winning.
