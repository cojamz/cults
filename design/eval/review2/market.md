# Blue review 2 (Nov 8)

Blue's story, in Cory's words where he gave them: Blue "works the shop and recruits more", and its contest is Most members ("blue should be most members since it gets more recruits"). A great Blue game: round I, Savings or Peek for blue and a shaped row, Out of Style to clear the color a rival is building; cheap bodies that count as several members (Window Dressers counts 3 for 5). Round II, Recruit Twice with Power Shoppers paying +4, Last-Minute Shopper adding a member right before contests. Round III, Spree for three recruits in one turn and Snobs at game end. Rivals push back by taking or swapping Blue's members away, or by denying the shop.

Fresh start, if Blue had nothing: one fuel space that shapes the row, one that clears a color, one extra-recruit space per round from II; members that pay followers for extra recruits, count as several members, recruit outside the turn, and use the discard pile the shop keeps filling. Measured against that, Blue's spaces are complete; its members have three that pay resources or depend on rivals and one gap: nothing uses the discard pile Blue fills, and nothing keeps Cory's "hold resources and buy followers" plan alive inside Blue.

## Assessment

Spaces
- **Savings** (round I): "+2 blue in round I, +3 in round II, +4 in round III. Before you recruit, you may send one shop member to the discard pile, then refill." **Keep.** Cory's own pick (C, Oct 31); plain fuel with a small dig, feeds Patroness and the Blue Cloak.
- **Out of Style** (round I): "Send every shop member of one color to the discard pile, then refill. +1 blue per member sent. +1 blue." **Keep, star.** Cory kept it tonight; now a sting on Red's Most members of one color and Yellow's Most member colors (clear the color they need).
- **Peek** (round I): "Look at the top 4 of the pile and put them back in any order. This turn your recruit may be one of them. +3 blue in round I, +4 in round II, +5 in round III." **Keep.** The biggest Blue fuel, plus a recruit rivals can't see coming; also sets what refills for them.
- **Recruit Twice** (round II): "Recruit an extra time this turn. +3 blue." **Keep, star.** Cory: "I like Buy Twice"; the direct contest space.
- **Bury** (round II): "Send a shop member to the discard pile, then refill. Gain blue equal to half its cost, rounded down. +1 blue." **Keep.** Best turn: round II, bury the 10-cost card a rival saved for, +6 blue and a sting. Doesn't feed the new contest, but it's the color's one denial and Cory has never complained; the full-cost version was "way too strong".
- **Spree** (round III): "Recruit two extra times this turn. +6 blue." **Keep, star.** Cory: "I still don't think this is weak."

Members
- **Patroness** (10): "Whenever shop members go to the discard pile, +1 follower." **Keep.** Pays on anyone's move with nobody deciding (the kind Cory likes); 8 to 12 followers in a Blue-heavy game, fair at 10.
- **Snobs** (8, counts 2): "Game End: +1 follower per blue member you have." **Keep.** A quiet scorer that pays its owner; with five blue members about +6, under the round III spaces.
- **Power Shoppers** (8, counts 2): "Whenever you recruit your second member in a turn, +4 followers." **Keep, star.** Cory: "i actually do like power shoppers"; the payoff for Recruit Twice and Spree.
- **Mannequin** (7): "Blue members in the shop count as your members when contests are scored." **Keep.** Cory: "Pretty cool"; rivals answer it with Out of Style on blue, and it counts against you in White's Fewest members.
- **Window Shopper** (7): "On Recruit and at Round Start: send 2 shop members to the discard pile. +1 of each one’s color." **Replace.** Pays resources, which Cory now calls boring ("i don't like just +1ing resource gains"); the third card in the color that sends shop members to the discard pile on your own timing (Window Dressers, Trendsetters). Note: the 2-member version is Cory's own Nov 1 call.
- **Window Dressers** (5, counts 3): "On Recruit: send 3 shop members to the discard pile, then refill." **Keep.** The cheap body that counts 3 toward Most members; the dig is a bonus.
- **Regular** (5): "Whenever you buy followers, +1 blue." **Replace.** A +1 resource trigger on the same move as the White Cloak ("Whenever you buy followers, +2 white."), and no link to Most members. It is Blue's only card about buying followers, so its replacement should keep that plan alive.
- **Trendsetters** (6, counts 3): "Whenever you recruit a member, send 1 shop member to the discard pile, then refill." **Keep, watch.** A body that counts 3; the trigger fires every recruit but on your own turn with nothing to track. If Window Shopper goes, it and Window Dressers stop being three of a kind.
- **Critic** (5): "Whenever a rival recruits a blue member, +1 follower." **Replace.** Small, depends on rivals buying blue (a Blue player wants that blue for itself), and does nothing for Most members. Already named for replacement on the Nov 3 principles page Cory said yes to.
- **Last-Minute Shopper** (5): "Round End: +3 blue, then you may recruit." **Keep, star.** Cory: "Last minute shopper is good"; a member before contests are scored.

## Picks

1. **Regular**: the plainest "+1 resource" trigger left in Blue, and a twin of the White Cloak's trigger. Cory: members should "lean more towards point generation"; and he doesn't want to lose "the strategy to hold resources and buy followers". The slot is where Blue can keep that plan.
2. **Window Shopper**: resource gain he called boring tonight, and a near copy of two Blue members beside it. Cory wrote this one, so it goes to him as a swap, not a cut.
3. **Critic**: the weakest moment in the deck (+1 follower, on a rival's choice), nothing for the contest.

Not picked: Bury and Trendsetters are the next weakest, but each has a moment players would miss (the bury sting, a cheap body that counts 3).

## Replacements

### Pick 1, Regular's place: **Impulse Buyers** [3 blue + 2 any, 5 as seen; worth 1] (price is Claude's fill-in)
Prints: "Once per turn, you may buy followers as an extra recruit."

- **The moment.** Round III, 4 players. You take Savings (+4 blue) and hold 18. The shop has Snobs (8) and you want the 10-follower card. Without the card you pick one, and the other waits a turn (a turn's recruit spent). With it: recruit Snobs and buy the 10. Round II, same shape: Recruit Twice for two members, then buy 5 followers with what's left. Best other choice for that minion: Spree, two extra recruits once; this gives one extra recruit, every turn you can pay for it.
- **Top case with numbers.** Round II to III, built around it, 9 turns: resources, not recruits, become the limit. A rich Blue player turns 3 or 4 turns a game into a member plus a follower card, about 2 or 3 more members than without it (2 to 3 more members toward Most members, worth about 5 followers, plus a round III prize swing of up to 10). The best round III space, Spree, gives 2 extra recruits and 6 blue in one turn. Under twice that: not broken. It makes no resources: a player who can't pay for both gets nothing.
- **Against the current card.** Regular's best turn: +1 blue. Impulse Buyers' best turn: a member and 10 followers in one turn. A bigger, different moment.
- **The idea.** Clean: one rule bent, in words already on the cards ("each follower purchase is one recruit"). Sounds like Blue (recruit more).
- **The ties.** It lets a Blue player keep both plans: members for Most members, resources held then spent on followers. Red's Proselytizers ("Whenever you recruit, +1 follower.") pays on each follower buy too, without needing Red.
- **Choices.** Only the owner, on their own turn: buy followers or not. Both sides live (holding resources for Magenta's Most resources or the next turn's member is still an option).
- **Who feels it.** The owner (a quiet engine, like Loyalists). Rivals see one cult growing in members and followers at once.
- **Hard rules.** Trackable (once per turn, on your turn), works without any other color, no token, no rival decides.
- **Not refused.** Shopaholics ("You can recruit twice each turn.") was swapped for Last-Minute Shopper, not refused; this one limits the second recruit to followers. Bargain Hunter (same rule, only while you have the fewest followers) was tested overnight, never pitched; the condition was the muddy part and is gone. Not the refused "+1 follower whenever you recruit followers".
- Intuitive: one sentence, game words. Fun: the combo turn (a member and a big follower card) lands for the owner. Interacts: with the shop and follower cards everyone uses, and Magenta's Most resources (spend down or hold). Powerful: bends the one-recruit limit every turn. Engine or counter: engine with Recruit Twice, Spree and Power Shoppers. Its color: a new way into "recruits more": recruiting followers, the plan Cory wants to keep.

### Pick 2, Window Shopper's place: **Thrift Shoppers** [4 blue + 3 any, 7 as seen; worth 1] (price is Claude's fill-in)
Prints: "On Use, once per round: recruit a member from the discard pile, paying its cost."

- **The moment.** Round II, 4 players. The discard pile holds round I's cleared row plus whatever Out of Style, Savings and Bury sent this round: say Con Men ("On Recruit: steal 5 resources.", 9), which a rival buried on their last turn so you couldn't recruit it. You take Recruit Twice, recruit from the shop twice, then use Thrift Shoppers on Con Men: three members, Power Shoppers +4, Con Men steals 5. Best other choice: Recruit Twice alone, two members. A rival who buried the card to deny you just fed you.
- **Top case with numbers.** Bought in round II (it costs 7), used in rounds II and III: 2 extra members a game, each paid in full. Spree gives 2 extra recruits and 6 blue in one turn, so this is about one Spree spread over two rounds, without the blue. Not over twice the best space.
- **Against the current card.** Window Shopper's best turn: +2 resources of chosen colors (+2 more with the Blue Cloak). Thrift Shoppers' best turn: a member out of the discard pile on top of your recruit. Bigger, and a new piece.
- **The idea.** Clean: the face-up discard pile Cory made ("then you know what's out") becomes one player's second shop. Sounds like Blue (works the shop, recruits more).
- **The ties.** Every color that discards feeds it: Blue's own Savings, Out of Style, Bury, Window Dressers; White's members that do their thing and get discarded (a White player's spent On Recruit card comes back for you, and does its On Recruit again); the row cleared between rounds. Works with none of them, since the row clears every round. Denial stops being free: burying a card now risks handing it to the Thrift Shoppers player.
- **Choices.** Only the owner, on their own turn: which member, or none. Rivals decide nothing on your turn.
- **Who feels it.** The rival who discarded the card (a sting), and anyone eyeing the discard pile. The owner gets a combo with Window Dressers (send 3, recruit the best of them back).
- **Hard rules.** A table already keeps the discard pile face up beside the deck; once per round like Panel; no token; no other color needed.
- **Not refused.** Scavenger ("Whenever a rival sends shop members to the bottom of the pile, you may recruit one of them") was refused for acting on a rival's turn; this acts only on yours. Ragpicker (top card of the pile, as if in the shop) and Dumpster Dive (a space) were tested, never pitched. "Pull a pile card into the shop" was refused as access without power; this is an extra recruit, not access.
- Intuitive: one sentence, and the discard pile is on the table. Fun: rivals watch what they throw away. Interacts: with every discard in the game, Blue's and White's most. Powerful: an extra recruit each round from the best of what's gone by. Engine or counter: engine with Window Dressers and Power Shoppers; counters denial. Its color: a new way into the shop: the discard pile Blue keeps filling.
- Number for Cory to set: once per round at 7 [default], or "On Recruit:" once, at 5.

### Pick 3, Critic's place: **Big Spenders** [3 blue + 2 any, 5 as seen; worth 1] (price is Claude's fill-in)
Prints: "On Use, once per game: recruit an extra time this turn."

- **The moment.** Round III, last turns, 4 players. You and a Red player are tied on members for Most members (+10). You take Spree (+6 blue, two extra recruits) and use Big Spenders: four recruits in one turn, Power Shoppers +4, and you pass the Red player by two. Best other choice: Spree alone, three recruits.
- **Top case with numbers.** One extra recruit, once a game: about one more member (2 to 3 followers of worth plus the contest swing). Far under Spree; the moment is the timing, not the size.
- **Against the current card.** Critic's best turn: +1 follower when a rival recruits blue. Big Spenders' best turn: flipping the round III contest on your own Spree. Bigger, and on your own move.
- **The idea.** Clean, one sentence. It is Cory's grey Big Spender, which he marked weak as "too blatantly blue": moved into Blue, where he says it belongs. Plus-Ones ("On Recruit: recruit again this turn.", counts 2) is the live near version; Big Spenders lets you pick the turn, which is the moment.
- **The ties.** Most members for Blue; against Red's Most of one color and Yellow's Most member colors, an extra member at the last turn changes those leads too.
- **Choices.** Only the owner, on their own turn: now or later. Both live (round II Recruit Twice or round III Spree).
- **Who feels it.** The rival who led the members contest, at the last turn.
- **Hard rules.** Once per game, used up like Extra Hand; no token; no other color needed.
- **Not refused.** The grey card was refused as grey, for being Blue's; this puts it in Blue. If Cory's "too blatantly blue" meant he doesn't want the effect at all, this falls.
- Intuitive: one sentence. Fun: a last-turn contest swing everyone sees. Interacts: with every contest that counts members. Powerful: a fourth recruit on a Spree turn. Engine or counter: engine with Spree and Power Shoppers. Its color: recruits more; not new to Blue, so it is the weakest survivor on that principle.

Sharp question for Cory on this slot: by "too blatantly blue", did you mean Big Spender belongs in Blue, or that Blue doesn't need another extra recruit? If the second, nothing passes for Critic's place and Critic stays.

### Cut, and why

Regular's place
- "Whenever you buy 10 followers at once, recruit an extra time this turn.": needs 15 or more resources in one turn, fires once or twice a game; a smaller Impulse Buyers.
- "Whenever you buy followers, +1 follower.": Cory's refused shape ("Is this a joke?").
- "Whenever you buy followers, you may send a shop member to the discard pile, then refill.": a mechanic with no payoff.
- Ragpicker, "You may recruit the top member of the discard pile as if it were in the shop.": who sets the top card after a batch discard is unclear at a table, and it's access, not an extra recruit; Thrift Shoppers does it better.
- "Whenever you recruit a member costing 4 or less, +1 follower.": cost arithmetic, small ball.
- "On Recruit: recruit a shop member costing 4 or less for free.": a free member is Red's job (Initiation, Mentor).
- Bargain Hunter with "while you have the fewest followers": useless once you're winning, and a stacked condition.
- Players-who-hold-most counting as extra members: Cory refused members counting extra in contests ("the incentives need to be more inherent").

Window Shopper's place
- Window Shopper paying "+1 follower each" instead: about the same best turn as today's, and a near copy of Patroness; not clearly better.
- "Round Start: recruit a shop member costing 4 or less, paying its cost.": a near copy of Last-Minute Shopper; First Dibs, the same shape, flopped.
- "Whenever you send shop members to the discard pile, you may recruit one of them, paying its cost.": with Trendsetters it chains a recruit off every recruit; needs a once-per-turn patch, muddier than Thrift Shoppers.
- "Whenever a member leaves your cult, recruit a shop member costing up to its cost, for free.": only works in games with Red, Magenta or White taking members, and a free member is Red's.
- "Round Start: send the whole shop to the discard pile, +1 follower per member sent.": +7 or +8 followers a round, too strong; Restockers already does the sweep.
- "On Use, once per round: send one of your members to the discard pile and recruit a shop member costing up to 2 more.": Red's Promote and Seat Filler.
- "On Recruit: recruit every shop member costing 3 or less, paying their costs.": "recruit a whole color" flopped; cost arithmetic.

Critic's place
- Critic at "+2 followers": still depends on rivals buying blue; small.
- "Round End: +1 follower per rival with fewer members than you.": Red's Mob shape, which Cory marked "don't like this".
- Personal Shopper, "On Use, once per round: pay a shop member's cost and give it to a rival. Gain followers equal to its cost.": the Grand Gift and Godparent shape (refused for Silver; "more a White card").
- "Whenever any player recruits their second member in a turn, +1 follower.": small, and a near copy of Power Shoppers.
- "Whenever you send 3 or more shop members to the discard pile at once, +3 followers.": a near copy of Patroness.
- "On Use, once per round: turn a shop member sideways; only you may recruit it this round.": refused as "claim" (Oct 20), and needs memory.
- "Counts as 1 member per blue member in the shop.": a copy of Mannequin.
- "Whenever a rival recruits a member, you may send a shop member to the discard pile.": a choice on a rival's turn.
