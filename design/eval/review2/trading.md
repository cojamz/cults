# White: review 2 (Nov 8)

White's story, in Cory's words: "fewer followers… you use stuff and then you get rid of it", "White gains followers". Its contest is now **Fewest members** (0 counts; a member worth 2 or 3 counts as that many). A great White game: round I, fuel up on white and buy followers instead of members; round II, recruit a member or two, use them, then discard them for followers or white (Farewell, Estate Sale, Downsizers) or hand them to rivals (Godparent, Guest of Honor); round III, Cash In with an empty or near-empty cult and take the +10 for fewest members. Rivals answer by buying White's members first, swapping their own unwanted members onto White (Red), or holding off on members themselves.

What changed tonight that matters for every card below: anything that pays for having more members now pulls against White's own contest, and a member that counts as 2 or 3 costs White that many in its contest every round it stays.

## Assessment

### Spaces
- **Allowance** (round I): "+3 white in round I, +4 in round II, +5 in round III. This turn, if you recruit a member, +1 follower in round I, +2 in round II, +3 in round III." **Fix.** The bonus pays for recruiting a member, which now works against White's Fewest members contest, and it's the same shape as Red's Gather and Magenta's Stash (Cory on Stash: "not on identity at ALL"). The white part is right (Cory raised it Nov 6).
- **Splurge** (round I): "This turn, if you recruit a member, +1 follower for every 2 resources you pay for it (every 1 in round II). +2 white." **Keep.** It also pays for recruiting a member, but it pays more for a pricey one, and a pricey member is exactly what Farewell and Estate Sale later turn into followers or white at its cost. Recruit big, use it, discard it: that's White's new loop. Cory picked its shape.
- **Exchange** (round I): "+2 white. Then turn up to 1 resource (2 in round II, 3 in round III) into 2 followers each." **Keep, star.** White's clean converter at a better rate than the follower cards; needs no members, so it suits the contest. Cory: "I like 1 actually."
- **Farewell** (round II): "Cast out one of your members to the discard pile, gain followers equal to its cost. +1 white." **Keep, reword** (below). Now right on theme: one member fewer for the contest and its cost in followers. Cory: "a little weird, but maybe they're good"; the new contest is what makes them good.
- **Estate Sale** (round II): "Cast out one of your members to the discard pile, gain white equal to its cost. +1 white." **Keep, reword.** Farewell's sibling that pays in white for Cash In (Cory: "improves on mine"; he refused moving it to the shop).
- **Cash In** (round III): "Turn all your white into followers. +1 white." **Keep, star.** The anchor: past the 10-follower card and without spending the recruit.

### Members
- **Recluse** (price 6): "Round End: +2 followers per rival with more members than you." **Replace** (makes room for Guest of Honor). Cory: "too overlapping with the competition now"; it pays a second time for what the contest already pays.
- **Downsizers** (price 6): "On Use, once per round: cast out one of your members to the discard pile. +4 white." **Keep, reword** (both readings of Cory's note below). Cory: "I like downsizers."
- **Barker** (price 7): "Each time you recruit a member, you may also recruit followers." **Keep.** Keeps the buy-followers plan alive on member turns. Bot trial: taken 38%, owners won 46%.
- **Heiresses** (price 11): "On Use, once per game: +1 white per member you have." **Replace** (makes room for Godparent). Pays for having more members (Cory: "supposed to get changed"); top case is about 6 white once, for the priciest card in the deck.
- **Brokers** (price 7, worth 3, counts as 3): "Whenever a White action gives you followers, +1 follower." **Keep, flag.** The ability is fine (fires 3 to 5 times a game). But counting as 3 members costs White 2 extra members in its own contest every round it stays. Question for Cory: worth 1 at the same price, or keep the tension on purpose.
- **Misers** (price 9, worth 2): "Game End: +1 follower per 2 white you hold." **Keep, fine.** Quiet, and it keeps the hold-resources plan Cory doesn't want to lose. Weaker than Cash In per white, so it's the fallback for the player who missed Cash In. First to go if Cory would rather keep a reworked Heiresses.
- **Cashier** (price 5): "On Recruit: turn all your white into followers." **Keep, star.** Cory: "a really good one".
- **Money Changers** (price 6, worth 2; also the White Cloak): "Whenever you buy followers, +2 white." **Keep, star.** Cory: "strong baseline mechanic of buying 1 follower."
- **Martyrs** (price 6, worth 3): "Whenever this member leaves your cult, gain followers equal to its cost." **Keep, star.** Already uses "leaves your cult", so it fires on a discard, a gift (Godparent, Guest of Honor), a Red swap or a Magenta theft. Counting as 3 suits it: the bigger body is what you get rid of.
- **Old Money** (price 8): "Game End: +1 follower per white member you have." **Fix** (Cory: "should stay and be reworked to be a little better"). Pays for having more members; top case about +5.
- **Magpie** (price 10): "On Use, once per game: turn all your resources into white." **Keep, flag.** Sets up Cash In. Two flags: Cory refused this exact text Oct 23 as "too strong" before it went in at price 10; and a magpie is a bird, not a person (naming rule): **Hoarders** would fix the name.

### Coming in (Cory: "should be white")
- **Godparent** (price 7): "On Use, once per game: give one of your members to a rival. Gain followers equal to its cost." Fits: one member fewer for you, one more for that rival, so it moves White's contest by two. Close to Farewell (both pay a member's cost in followers); the difference is where the member goes, and the rival who gets it feels it.
- **Guest of Honor** (price 5): "On Use, once per round: give a rival one of your members. +4 followers." Fits, and it's strong: top case, three rounds of +4 (12 followers) plus the contest swing each round, from a 5-cost card. Flag for Cory: may need to cost more in White, where the gift also wins the contest. Claude's reading: it can give itself away.

## Picks

1. **Recluse and Heiresses make room for Godparent and Guest of Honor.** Recluse on Cory's word ("too overlapping with the competition"); Heiresses because it pays for having more members, which Cory already wanted changed, and it's the priciest, flattest member in the deck. No brainstorm: the replacements are Cory's. Alternative if he'd rather rework Heiresses: cut Misers.
2. **Old Money, reworked** (Cory: stay, "a little better", stop paying for more members). Brainstorm below.
3. **Allowance**, from the judging: its bonus pays for recruiting a member, against the new contest, in the same shape as Gather and Stash. Brainstorm below.
4. **Wording: "discard" and "leaves your cult"** (Cory's call). Proposed texts:
   - **Farewell**: "Discard one of your members. Gain followers equal to its cost. +1 white."
   - **Estate Sale**: "Discard one of your members. Gain white equal to its cost. +1 white."
   - **Downsizers**, reading A (his "+2 followers" replaces the white): "On Use, once per round: discard one of your members. +2 followers." Reading B (only the wording changes): "On Use, once per round: discard one of your members. +4 white." A pays fewer points (top case 6 followers a game against 12 white) but pays in followers, which is what Cory wants members to pay. Ask him which.
   - **Martyrs**: unchanged; it already says "Whenever this member leaves your cult".
   - **Undertaker** (in White's 12, not the 10): "Whenever one of your members leaves your cult, +2 followers."
   - One-line flag: Blue Cloak reads "Whenever you discard a member, +1 of its color", so after this change it also pays a Blue Cloak holder who takes Farewell, Estate Sale or Downsizers. That's a tie through a shared word, not friction.

## Replacements

### Old Money (member, price 8, worth 1)

Current best turn, to beat: game end, Ana holds Old Money, Cashier and Money Changers: +3 followers. Top case +5, and every white member it counts also counts against her in White's contest.

Candidates (11): (a) +3 per member fewer than the rival with the most; (b) whenever another of your members leaves your cult, +2 followers; (c) +1 follower per white you hold at game end; (d) +2 per member you discarded this game; (e) followers equal to your priciest other member's cost at game end; (f) whenever you buy followers, +1 follower; (g) +1 follower per member in the discard pile at game end; (h) whenever a member joins your cult from a rival's, discard it and gain followers equal to its cost; (i) counts as no member, +2 followers at each round end; (j) on use once per game discard any of your members for their total cost in followers; (k) +2 followers per white member your rivals have at game end.

#### Survivor 1 (Claude's pick): Old Money
**Printed:** [price 8, worth 1] "Game End: +2 followers per white member your rivals have."
Texts it touches: **Guest of Honor** ("On Use, once per round: give a rival one of your members. +4 followers."), **Godparent** ("On Use, once per game: give one of your members to a rival. Gain followers equal to its cost."), Silver's **Patron Saints** ("Game End: +1 follower per silver member, anyone's.").
**Played (4 players, game end):** Ana (White) holds Old Money and Guest of Honor. With Guest of Honor she gave Cashier to Ben at the end of round I and Downsizers to Cleo in round II; Ben also recruited Brokers and Dee recruited Martyrs. Rivals hold 4 white members: Ana +8. The current Old Money in the same game: Ana holds 2 white members, +2. Against simply buying followers with the 8 resources (+8 at once), the new card is worth 9 (its own 1 plus 8), and it counts as one member, not five, in White's contest.
**Top case:** Ana gives away 4 members over the game and rivals buy 3 more white ones: 7, so +14. The best round III space, Cash In, typically pays 10 to 15. Under twice: fine.
**Choices:** Ana chooses which member to give and to whom, on her own turn. Rivals choose whether to buy white members, knowing each one pays Ana 2 at game end; the shop's best card is still often worth it, so neither side always wins.
**Who feels it:** the rival handed a member they didn't want (it also costs them in White's contest); every rival weighing a White member in the shop.
**Six principles.** Intuitive: one sentence, a count of cards everyone can see. Fun: gifts now sting twice, and the shop gets a small tax. Interacts: every rival who recruits white members, and Godparent and Guest of Honor (it works without them, through plain recruits). Powerful: grows with the table, about +8 in a normal game. Engine or counter: engine with the giving members. Its color: new; White is paid for what has left its cult, not what stays.
**Not refused:** "Game end: +1 follower per white member" (your own) was refused Oct 22; this counts rivals' members. Patron Saints is Silver's "anyone's" count; the shape is near, so flag it to Cory. Claude's fill-in: each card counts 1.

#### Survivor 2: Old Money
**Printed:** [price 8, worth 1] "Whenever another of your members leaves your cult, +2 followers."
Texts it touches: **Farewell**, **Estate Sale**, **Downsizers** (as reworded above), **Martyrs** ("Whenever this member leaves your cult, gain followers equal to its cost."), Red's **Promote** ("Swap any member with one in another cult or the shop costing up to 3 more. +3 red."), Magenta's **Poach** ("Recruit a rival's member you choose, for free. +1 magenta.").
**Played (round II, 4 players):** Ana holds Old Money and Magpie (cost 10, used). She takes Farewell: discards Magpie for +10 followers, +2 from Old Money, so 12. The best other round II space for her: Estate Sale, 10 white. Later Ben takes Promote and swaps his cheap member for Ana's Downsizers: Ana +2.
**Top case:** round I to III, Downsizers every round (3), Guest of Honor every round (3), Farewell and Estate Sale (2): 8 leaves, +16 over the game, on top of each card's own pay. Under twice the best round III space; in a usual game 4 to 5 leaves, +8 to 10.
**Choices:** which member to let go, and when. Rivals who swap or steal from Ana now pay her 2.
**Who feels it:** Red and Magenta players taking Ana's members; the rival handed a gift.
**Six principles.** Intuitive: one sentence, in Martyrs' words. Fun: every member Ana loses still pays. Interacts: with Red's swaps, Magenta's thefts and White's gifts, through members only. Powerful: steady, about +8 a game. Engine or counter: engine with the discard members, quiet counter to swaps and thefts. Its color: the "leaves your cult" tie Cory asked for.
**Not refused:** Cory said yes to "whenever you get rid of one of your members, +2 followers" (Oct 22); this is Undertaker's job under the new wording, moved onto Old Money. Flag: if Undertaker is in the deck too, they're twins; only one should be.

#### Cut
- (a) +3 per member fewer than the rival with the most: the contest again (Recluse's problem).
- (c) +1 follower per white held: Misers' twin at a better rate.
- (d) +2 per member you discarded: nobody can count it at a table.
- (e) followers equal to your priciest other member's cost: refused shape Oct 22 ("complicated for no reason").
- (f) whenever you buy followers, +1 follower: refused Oct 23 ("Is this a joke?").
- (g) per member in the discard pile: the old row joins it every round, so it pays 15 or more for nothing you did.
- (h) discard whatever a rival hands you, gain its cost: only answers rivals' moves; dead in most games.
- (i) counts as no member, +2 each round end: Ghost's first half plus a small steady gain.
- (j) discard any number for their total cost: round III top case about 30, more than twice Cash In (and Dowagers lost its trial).

### Allowance (round I space)

Current best turn, to beat: round I, Ana takes Allowance (+3 white) and recruits a 6-cost member: +1 follower. Round III: +5 white and +3 followers with a member. What players would miss: the biggest plain white gain in round I (Cory raised it Nov 6).

Candidates (11): (a) the bonus on buying followers instead of recruiting a member; (b) Low Profile, doubled if you have the fewest members; (c) +1 white per rival with more members; (d) discard a member for white in round I; (e) buy followers twice this turn; (f) white equal to the cheapest shop member's cost; (g) plain +4/+5/+6 white; (h) +3/4/5 white and you may not recruit a member; (i) turn 1 white into 2 followers; (j) +1 white per member in rivals' cults; (k) rivals with more members each give you 1 follower.

#### Survivor 1 (Claude's pick): Allowance
**Printed:** "+3 white in round I, +4 in round II, +5 in round III. This turn, if you buy followers, +1 follower in round I, +2 in round II, +3 in round III."
Texts it touches: **Money Changers** ("Whenever you buy followers, +2 white."), **Exchange** ("+2 white. Then turn up to 1 resource (2 in round II, 3 in round III) into 2 followers each."), Red's **Gather** and Magenta's **Stash** (the current shape).
**Played (round I, 4 players):** Ana holds 2 white. Allowance: +3, so 5; she buys the 5-follower card: 5 + 1 = 6 followers, and still holds no members, so she leads White's contest (+4). The current card: +3 white, recruit a 5-cost member worth 1, +1 follower: 2 followers and a member that costs her the contest. Exchange instead: +2 white and 2 followers for 1 resource. Round III: Ana holds 7, takes Allowance (+5, so 12), buys 10 followers: +13.
**Top case:** round III with Money Changers: +5 white, buy 10, +3, +2 white back: 13 followers and 2 white. Under Cash In's usual 10 to 15 plus. Fine.
**Choices:** buy followers now for the bonus, or save the white for Cash In or a member. Cash In pays 1 for 1 without the 10 cap, Allowance's bonus pays now; both sides get picked.
**Who feels it:** Magenta players (Ana holds white to steal); the contest leaders, since Ana can stay empty without falling behind.
**Six principles.** Intuitive: same sentence as now, one word changed. Fun: mostly the owner's moment, a big buy. Interacts: through the shared follower cards and the contest. Powerful: as now. Engine or counter: engine with Money Changers and Barker. Its color: buying followers, White's own recruit, instead of Red's and Magenta's.
**Not refused:** pitched Oct 31 as "+2 followers if you buy followers this turn" with no verdict. Honest weakness: still a bonus stuck on a fuel space; "Keep it" is a fair answer.

#### Cut

- (c) +1 white per rival with more members: Recluse's shape on a space.
- (d) discard a member in round I: nobody holds one yet (tonight's named failure).
- (e) buy twice: Blue's job (extra recruits).
- (f) white equal to the cheapest shop member's cost: refused Oct 22 ("uhg").
- (g) plain +4/+5/+6: small ball, no choice.
- (h) a restriction for no gain.
- (i) Exchange's twin.
- (j) "+1 white per member" shape, refused Oct 22.
- (k) taking followers is Magenta's, and it echoes the contest.
