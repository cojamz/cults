# Yellow review 2 (Nov 8)

**Yellow's story.** Cory, Oct 30: Yellow "rewards going broad, i.e. having a combination of colors and being flexible. it also allows you to translate currencies (kind of like launderer)." Its contest is now **Most member colors** (Dabblers counts as one color; grey members don't count, since grey isn't a color in play). The parked goal is "at least one member of every color". Cory's worry from tonight: the contests have become mostly about members, and he doesn't want to lose the plan of holding resources and buying followers. Yellow's resource changing still has to matter.

**A great Yellow game.** Round I: Harvest or New Blood for fuel in the colors you need, and a cheap member of a color you don't have yet (the Guest Book cloak pays +2 yellow for it). Round II: Housewarming brings in a free member of a new color, Melting Pot pays for every color among your members, and Tourist or Caravan keep a spread of colors coming. Round III: Makeover turns your whole pile into exactly the colors the last members need, and the contest pays +10 to the cult with the most member colors. Big Tent pays at game end. Rivals push back by grabbing the color you need first, by stealing your one red, or with Counterfeiters turning your pile into a single color.

**A fresh start**, if Yellow had nothing: one fuel space in colors you choose; one space that brings in a member of a new color; one that pays per member color; the round III rearrange; members that bend one rule about colors, pay off holding a spread of colors, change rivals' colors, and count your breadth at game end. Today's Yellow matches that shape except in one place: with full sets gone from the contest, **nothing pays off holding a spread of resources any more**. Tourist and Dyer count the colors you hold, but they don't care whether you spend them. That gap is the one Cory named tonight.

Prices below are what a player pays (the deck file's numbers).

## Assessment (every card)

Spaces
- **Harvest** (round I) "+3 in round I, +4 in round II, +5 in round III, in colors you choose." **Keep.** Cory's yes ("fuel spaces get the color's flavor"). Choosing your colors is Yellow's translation at its simplest, and it now pays for the colored part of a new-color member.
- **Survey** (round I) "+1 yellow per minion on the busiest color's spaces. +1 yellow." **Keep (fine), the weakest space.** It pays only yellow, so it neither goes broad nor translates. Its saving grace is a real timing call: in round I every color's 3 spaces fill, so taken early it pays 2 or 3, taken late 4, and in round III up to 7, but wait too long and someone else takes it. Cory said "not all of the yellow spaces feel like winners". This is the likeliest one he means, but I found nothing that beats it (see the note under Picks).
- **New Blood** (round I) "+2 yellow. This turn, if you recruit a member of a color none of your members have, +2 yellow in round I, +3 in round II, +4 in round III." **Keep.** Under the new contest it pays for exactly the move the contest rewards. It's the third card on the same trigger, with Guest Book and Housewarming. That's acceptable for the color's goal, but there's no room for a fourth.
- **Housewarming** (round II) "+2 yellow. Recruit a shop member of a color none of your members have, costing up to 5, for free." **Keep (star).** A free member of a new color is +1 in the contest in one move. One flag on tonight's cap: 5 is the cheapest price in the game, so in a 7-card round II shop there's usually one member that fits, and sometimes none. If players report it whiffing, 6 is the next step. No change now.
- **Melting Pot** (round II) "+2 yellow, and +1 of each color among your members." **Keep** (Cory: "cool"). It pays for the contest's own count.
- **Makeover** (round III) "+4 yellow, then rearrange your resources into any mix of colors." **Keep** (star, Cory's own anchor). Contests no longer count held colors (apart from Silver's fewest silver and Magenta's total), so its job is now paying the colored parts of round III recruits. That still matters, and Harlequins below gives it a second job.

Members
- **Duty Collector** (8) "Whenever a rival recruits a member of a color you have a member of, +1 of that color." **Keep (fine).** Going broad now makes it fire more, and it fires on anyone's move without anyone stopping to decide, which Cory likes. But it's a "+1 resource" trigger, which he calls boring. If it's ever touched, change the payout to +1 follower. Not a pick.
- **Tourist** (9) "On Recruit: +1 follower per color you hold. Round Start: +1 follower per color you hold." **Keep (star).** Followers, simple, and the one card that already rewards holding a spread. Top case: bought in round I holding 4 colors, it pays 4 + 4 + 4 + 1 worth = 13 for 9. That's big but within reach of a round III space.
- **Seamstress** (8) "On Use, once per game: double your full sets." **Cut** (Cory: "those can probably got to get cut"). Pick 2.
- **Matchmaker** (8) "Whenever you recruit a yellow member, +3 wild, +1 follower." **Keep.** Cory's own fix from Nov 6.
- **Dyer** (9) "On Recruit: +1 yellow per color you hold. Round Start: +1 yellow per color you hold." **Replace if something beats it** (Cory, Nov 5: "eh lets leave it for now", and he dislikes it). It's Tourist's twin at the same price, paying yellow instead of followers, and Cory wants members to lean toward followers. Pick 3.
- **Night Traders** (5) "Round End: before contests are scored, swap up to 4 of your resources for other colors, one for one." **Keep (fine).** "Before contests" now matters only at tables with Silver (swap your silver away for Fewest silver). Otherwise it sets up next round's colored parts. Cory raised it to 4 on Nov 6, so leave it.
- **Counterfeiters** (6) "On Use, once per round: turn all of a rival's resources into one color you choose." **Keep (star).** Cory's idea. Under the new contest it bites harder: turn the pile of a rival who is saving purple for a purple member into yellow, and that member waits.
- **Big Tent** (9) "Game End: +2 followers per color you have 1 to 3 members of." **Keep.** It's the contest's count paid once more at the end (top case +8 at a 4-color table). It's fine, but Yellow is now full of cards that count colors, so no new card should count them.
- **Caravan** (11) "On Recruit: +1 of each color. Round Start: +1 of each color." **Keep.** Plain, big income across every color (12 resources over a game, worth 3).
- **Jack-of-All-Trades** (6) "When counting your full sets (1 of each color), your yellow can stand in for any missing color." **Cut** (Cory). Pick 1.
- **Guest Book** (the Yellow Cloak) "Whenever you recruit a member of a color none of your members have, +2 yellow." **Keep** (Cory: "works").

## Picks

1. **Jack-of-All-Trades' place** (cheap member, 6, buyable in round I). It's cut, and its job was to bend a rule about colors. Fill it so it answers Cory's worry: give the player who holds a spread of resources a reason to keep holding it.
2. **Seamstress' place** (mid member, 8, a once-per-game moment). It's cut. Fill it with a big moment from Yellow's other verb: changing resources, rivals' included.
3. **Dyer** (9). Cory's dislike on record, a twin of Tourist, and a resource payout where he wants followers.

Survey was considered as a fourth. Quick run, all cut: "+1 of each color in play" ("+2 of every other color" and Mix were refused), "+1 of each color a minion's space shows" (Wander's shape: "Wander is bad"), "gain a shop member's colored part" (Window Shopping: "isnt really aligned"), "pay a colored part with any colors this turn" (Haggle: "too focused on member costs"), turning rivals' resources into yellow (Dilute: "I still don't like Dilute"), swapping resources with rivals (Barterers handed rivals as much as you got), and a small rearrange (a smaller copy of Makeover). Survey stays.

## Replacements

### Pick 1, Jack-of-All-Trades' place: **Harlequins**

**Prints:** [3 yellow + 3 any, price 6, worth 1] "Whenever you buy followers paying with every color, +2 followers."
(Numbers are my fill-ins. "Every color" means one of each color in play, as on any card.)

**What it touches.** The follower cards (1, 5 or 10 followers for as many resources, any colors; a 1-follower buy can never use every color, so it only fires on 5 and 10). Compared with: **Regular** (Blue) "Whenever you buy followers, +1 blue." **Money Changers** (White) "Whenever you buy followers, +2 white." **White Cloak** "Whenever you buy followers, +2 white." **Tourist** "On Recruit: +1 follower per color you hold. Round Start: +1 follower per color you hold."

**One played turn.** Round II, 4 players, colors Red, Blue, Yellow, Silver. Ava bought Harlequins in round I. Her third turn: she holds 3 yellow, 1 red, 1 blue, 1 silver. She buys the 5-follower card paying yellow, red, blue, silver and 1 more yellow: 7 followers. Her best other recruit is the same card without Harlequins (5 followers), or **Seat Filler** (2 red + 3 any, "Round Start: you may give this to the shop and recruit a shop member costing up to 3 more, for free."), which she can't pay because she has only 1 red. The gain is +2. If a rival had stolen her one blue, the gain would be 0.

**Top case.** Round III, Ava built around it: **Caravan** (+1 of each color each round start), **Harvest** (+5 in colors you choose), **Makeover** (turn the pile into a spread). She buys followers on 4 of her 5 turns paying with every color: +8, plus +2 to +4 in round II, so about +10 to +12 over the game for a 6-cost card. The best round III spaces give about 10 (**Initiation** "Recruit a member in the shop costing 10 or less, for free. +1 red."; **Largesse** "Give rivals 12 silver from the bank, split as you choose. +10 followers."). It stays under twice that, so it isn't too strong. At 2 players (2 colors) nearly every 5 or 10 buy qualifies, so expect it to run hotter there. Watch for that in human games.

**Both sides of every choice.** The owner, on every turn: spend your one red on a red member's colored part (a new member color for the contest) or keep it for the rainbow buy (+2). Both Yellow plans pull on the same resources. Which one wins depends on the shop and the contest, so neither side always wins. No rival decides anything.

**Who feels it.** Thieves get a target: Magenta's **Help Yourself** ("Steal 3 resources in round I, 4 in round II, 5 in round III.") takes your lone blue, and the bonus is gone until you rebuild. A rival Yellow's **Counterfeiters** ("On Use, once per round: turn all of a rival's resources into one color you choose.") shuts it off for a turn. At Silver tables, paying silver into the rainbow also helps you toward Fewest silver.

**Not already refused.** White, Oct 23: "+1 follower whenever you recruit followers" → "Is this a joke? Like, this would be so strong". That card doubled a 1-follower buy on every turn. This one never fires on 1-follower buys, needs a full spread, and adds 20 to 40% to a 5 or 10 buy. The closest earlier idea, Spice Traders ("Whenever you recruit paying with every color, +2 followers."), passed the paper checks on Nov 4 but never went to Cory. This version narrows it to buying followers, the plan Cory wants kept. It isn't a full-set card: nothing is held to round end or counted, and you pay it away.

**Six principles.**
- Intuitive: one sentence, and the table sees the rainbow as you pay.
- Fun: a small puzzle on every buy, and thieves now have a reason to pick your lone color.
- Interacts: with steals, Counterfeiters and Fewest silver, without naming them; works at any table.
- Powerful: about +10 over a game from a cheap card; flat +2, so it's the quiet kind (Cory has said yes to quiet members that pay their owner).
- Engine or counter: engine; it's the payoff for Harvest, Caravan, Melting Pot, Night Traders and Makeover.
- Its color: a new way in. Translating currencies pays off at the follower cards, not through members. That's the hold-resources-and-buy-followers plan, in Yellow.

### Pick 2, Seamstress' place: **Influencers**

**Prints:** [4 yellow + 4 any, price 8, worth 1] "On Use, once per game: choose two colors; every player's resources of the first become the second."
(Price is my fill-in.)

**What it touches.** Every player's resources, so every colored part in the shop. Compared with: **Counterfeiters** "On Use, once per round: turn all of a rival's resources into one color you choose." and **Makeover** "+4 yellow, then rearrange your resources into any mix of colors."

**One played turn.** Round III, 4 players, colors Red, Blue, Yellow, Purple. Ava has yellow, red and blue members (3 member colors). Ben has the same 3 and holds 4 purple, planning to recruit **Lookouts** (3 purple + 4 any, "Whenever you take a consecutive turn, +2 followers.") next turn for a 4th color. That would take the +10 contest from Ava; a tie would give them 5 each. On her turn Ava uses Influencers: purple becomes yellow. Ben's 4 purple, Cara's 6 purple and Ava's 1 purple all turn yellow. To get purple back, Ben has to spend a minion on a Purple space (**Hold Up** "+2 purple in round I, +3 in round II, +4 in round III. Whoever's turn is next skips it, even yours." pays 4) before he can recruit Lookouts, and if those spaces are full he can't. The use comes on top of Ava's placement and recruit. She protects up to 10 followers (5 against a tie), and Ben spends a turn rebuilding. Her other option was to save it, and Seamstress, the card it replaces, did nothing at this table without full sets.

**Top case.** It never gives you more resources than you hold; it changes colors. Its biggest turn is a sting like the one above, or a self-translation that also scrambles the table. At a Silver table, turning everyone's silver into another color leaves everyone at 0 silver, so Fewest silver is a 4-way tie (+10 split, 3 each), which costs the player who planned to win it alone. That's strong, but it's once a game and doesn't break anything.

**Both sides of every choice.** The owner chooses the two colors and when to use it. Using it early (round II) disrupts one plan. Waiting for round III hits the biggest one but risks the moment passing. That's a real timing choice. Rivals choose nothing during your turn.

**Who feels it.** The whole table, every time: whoever was saving the first color feels a sting, and whoever needed the second color gets a gift. Rivals can play around it before it's used by not hoarding one color.

**Not already refused.** Cory, Nov 1, on the old deck: "way too much change rivals resource… nothing really benefits from that". He then asked for Counterfeiters himself on Nov 5. Under the member-colors contest something does benefit now: changing a rival's colors delays the new-color member that would beat you. The paper checks named Influencer in Tourist's slot on Nov 4 (scored highest, ranked low in bot play), but it never reached Cory. **Flag:** it's a second card in the 10 that changes rivals' resources. It differs from Counterfeiters in size and target (one color for everyone, once a game, against one rival's whole pile every round). If Cory reads it as Counterfeiters again, the answer is no.

**Six principles.**
- Intuitive: one sentence ("everyone's purple is yellow now").
- Fun: the whole table reacts, and it's a story told after the game.
- Interacts: every rival's resources and every colored price; works at any table.
- Powerful: rule-bending and relative, scaling with what the table is saving.
- Engine or counter: a counter to a rival hoarding one color; also a once-a-game translation for you.
- Its color: changing resources, Yellow's own verb, at the scale of the whole table. That's a new way in beside Counterfeiters (one rival) and Makeover (yourself).

### Pick 3, Dyer: none passes

Ten candidates, all cut (below). Dyer stays until Cory says what he dislikes about it.
**Question for Cory:** Is Dyer's problem that it pays yellow instead of followers, or that it reads like Tourist?

### Cut, and why

Pick 1 (Jack's place):
- Spice Traders "Whenever you recruit paying with every color, +2 followers.": a variation on Harlequins. Including members blurs the follower plan Cory asked to keep.
- Drifters "On Recruit: choose a color; these count as a member of that color.": a near copy of Dabblers in the very contest Cory worried Dabblers would win outright ("I think just counts as one"). A table would also need something to mark the chosen color.
- Interpreters "Your yellow can pay any member's colored part.": a twin of Silver's Smooth Talkers ("Your silver pays for any color.") and grey Alchemists, and it's about member costs ("you're too focused on member costs").
- Duty Free "Members of a color none of your members have cost you 3 less.": cost cuts are ruled out, and bots bought it 0 times in 9.
- Go-Between "Whenever a space gives you resources, you may change 1 of them to any color.": fires on nearly every turn ("no triggers that fire constantly").
- "Your grey members count as a member color.": muddy (which cards it touches) and tiny.
- Spice Route "Game End: +1 follower per color you hold 3 or more of.": +4 at most at a 4-color table, and another color counter.
- "Round End: if you hold 2 or more of every color, +4 followers.": the full-set card Cory just cut, under another name.
- Peddlers "On Recruit: +1 of each color.": small gain.

Pick 2 (Seamstress' place):
- Square Up "On Use, once per game: +1 of each color you hold, for each color you hold.": counts colors, a formula on your own pile, and the capped version read "confusing".
- Rainmaker "On Use, once per game: raise each of your colors to match your yellow.": Makeover first turns it into +60.
- Book Cookers "On Use, once per game: rearrange your resources into any mix of colors.": a twin of Makeover.
- Quilter "On Use, once per game: turn up to 3 full sets into 6 followers each.": the full-set family that was just cut.
- "On Use, once per game: swap all your resources with a rival's.": the refused shape ("i have one silver, i use that to take all of somebody's gold").
- Sign Painters "On Use, once per round: a member in any cult becomes a member of a color you choose.": the table would need a marker for the new color (no new tokens).
- Tastemaker, reworded "Whenever you change resources, yours or a rival's, +1 follower.": only fires if you also draw Counterfeiters, Night Traders or Makeover, and small triggers read weak.
- "On Use, once per game: swap one of your members for a shop member of a new color, costing up to as much.": Red's swap.
- Leveler "On Use, once per round: choose a color; 1 of each rival's biggest color becomes it.": a third card changing rivals' colors, with smaller gains.

Pick 3 (Dyer):
- "On Recruit and Round Start: +1 follower per color you hold.": that's Tourist.
- Ambassadors "On Recruit and Round Start: +1 of each color you have a member of.": Melting Pot every round, and a +1 resource payout.
- Gardeners "+1 of each color you have none of", On Recruit and Round Start: pays less the better you do ("nothing that goes useless when you're winning").
- "Round Start: +1 of each color you hold 3 or more of.": small gain.
- Polyglot "Round Start: +1 follower per color you hold 3 or more of.": refused Oct 31 ("none").
- Master Dyer "On Use, once per round: turn all of one of your colors into another color.": another swap of your own pile ("swap any resources one for one" was "terrible").
- "Game End: +1 follower per 2 resources you hold.": a twin of Gold's Tycoon and White's Misers.
- "Round End: each rival with more member colors than you gives you 2 resources.": pays when you're losing your own color's contest.
- "On Recruit: recruit a member of a new color from the discard pile, paying its cost.": an extra recruit (Blue's job), and a smaller Housewarming.
- Harlequins: better in Jack's place, which has to be filled.
