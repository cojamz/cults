# Green review 2 (Nov 8)

Green's story in one line: Green copies what the table is doing, and its contest (Most minions on Green spaces) pays the player who keeps going back to Green's spaces. Cory, on the contest: "it leans towards copying… a chaotic strategy, you should be awarded for doubling down on it."

A great Green game: in round I you take two of the three Green spaces (Echo after a rival's big action, Reprise on your own best one) and win the +4. You recruit Copyist so every copy pays followers. In round II you hold three of the five Green spaces, copying the best things rivals took, and recruit a member that copies (Song Request, Doppelganger). In round III Greatest Hits re-fires your members' On Recruits, and you fight for the +10. Rivals answer by taking Green spaces just to block you, by moving Agents around, or by taking your members.

A fresh start would have: a fuel space, a copy of the previous action, a copy of your own, a copy of a member, one big round III copy; members: a cheap payoff for copying, a defense against copying, a member that copies a member, and one member that pays off the contest itself. Today's Green has everything except that last one: no Green member cares how many minions you have on Green spaces.

## Assessment

Spaces
- **Build Up** "+3 green in round I, +4 in round II, +5 in round III." **Keep.** Cory kept it twice. It is also the one Green space that adds to the contest without copying, so a player who doesn't want to copy can still fight for the +4/+6/+10.
- **Echo** "Copy the previous action. +1 green." **Keep, star.** Cory: "absolutely perfect". Every Echo is a minion on Green, so it now pays twice.
- **Reprise** "Copy an action with your minion. +1 green." **Keep.** Taken first in round I it copies nothing, and that is on the taker (Cory: no rescue clauses). Later it doubles your best turn and holds a Green space.
- **Sample** "Copy the On Recruit of a rival's member. +1 green." **Keep.** Round II, rivals hold 1 to 3 members; copying Con Men ("On Recruit: steal 5 resources.") is the moment Cory loves ("i love conmen", copies included).
- **Cover** "Copy an action with no minion on it. +1 green." **Keep, the weakest space.** Its usual turn is "take an open space and leave it open", which nobody at the table feels. Its best turn is fine: round III, copy Initiation ("Recruit a member in the shop costing 10 or less, for free. +1 red.") while it's still open, and it's still open for the next player. Under the new contest it is also a Green minion, which is what it needed. Not a pick.
- **Greatest Hits** "Copy the On Recruit of each of your members, in any order. +1 green." **Keep.** Top case, round III: Con Men (steal 5) + Accomplice (steal 3) + Doppelganger as Con Men (steal 5): 13 resources, against Largesse's +10 followers. Under 2x.

Members
- **Song Request** (9) "On Use, once per round: copy an action a rival has a minion on." **Keep.** Strong and simple: one rival action a round. It doesn't add a Green minion, which is fine; not every card must feed the contest.
- **Impersonator** (8) "On Recruit: choose a rival's member. Do its On Recruit, and Impersonator has its ability." **Keep.** It already copies a rival member's whole ability (On Use, Round End, Game End), which is why every "copy a rival's Round End / Game End / On Use" idea below is cut as a narrower twin.
- **Impressionist** (cloak) "Whenever you copy an action or a member, +2 of its color (grey: any color)." **Keep.** Cory: "I like it, but yeah hard to do and keep a strategy." The new contest is the strategy his note asked for: the player taking Green spaces copies three or four times a round. It pays in other colors (Copyist and Wallflower pay followers and doubles), so it differs from the members. Not a pick; if he still wants a tweak, ask what "hard to keep a strategy" means first.
- **Wallflower** (12) "Whenever you copy an action, double the resources it gives you." **Keep.** Top case round III: an Echo of Toast (9 silver) becomes 18. Big but costs 12 and only doubles resources, not swaps or members.
- **Fan Club** (8) "On Recruit: copy an action the player with the most followers has a minion on." **Replace.** Cory, Nov 3: "Fan club really makes no sense." It is a one-time Song Request with a target rule, beside Stunt Double, the third member whose whole job is "copy one action". It adds nothing to the contest.
- **Copyist** (5) "Whenever you copy an action, +2 followers." **Keep.** Cheap, pays followers (Cory's lean), and now pays the exact plan the contest rewards. Same trigger as the cloak, different payout.
- **Stunt Double** (7) "On Recruit: copy the action you just took." **Keep** (Cory: "I really like stunt double").
- **Tribute Band** (16) "On Use, once per game: choose a rival. Do every action they have a minion on this round." **Replace.** On Cory's cut list since Nov 6. Its top case (round III, a rival with 4 or 5 minions down) is 4 or 5 actions at once.
- **Doppelganger** (8) "On Recruit: this member becomes a copy of another of your members." **Keep, star** (Cory loved it).
- **Agents** (5) "Rivals can't copy your actions." **Keep.** Cory: "i like agents moved around as a sabotage blocker." The table's counter to Green, inside Green's own deck.
- **Encore** (7) "On Use, once per game: your next On Recruit happens twice." **Replace.** Cory has said he dislikes it (rework list); he kept it only because "bots can't use this right". It doubles your own card, which is Doppelganger's and Greatest Hits' job, and doesn't read the table.

## Picks
1. **Encore**: Cory dislikes it; keeping it is off the table. Slot: a cheap once-per-game member, about 7.
2. **Tribute Band**: Cory's own cut list. Slot: Green's pricey once-per-game member.
3. **Fan Club**: Cory "makes no sense"; third "copy one action" member. Slot: a mid-price member, about 8. This is also where the missing contest member goes.

## Replacements

### Pick 1, Encore's slot: **Lookalikes** [Claude's price: 7]
**Prints:** "On Use, once per game: choose a rival and a color. Gain as many of that color as they hold."
1. **Text compared with:** Encore (above); Magenta's Heist Crew "On Use, once per game: steal half of a rival's biggest pile, rounded down." (same target, other verb); Magenta's contest Most resources (every color together).
2. **One played turn**, round III, 4 players. Dee has held 9 yellow to lead Most resources. Ben uses Lookalikes: +9 yellow, then places on Echo copying Build Up (+5 green, +1) and buys the 10-follower card with 10 of his now 15+. Best other choice at the same price, Encore: Encore then Con Men steals 5 twice = 10 resources, but only if Con Men is in the shop that turn. Lookalikes: 9 here, 5 to 7 in a usual round II. Top case: a rival holding 12 of one color, +12, about 12 followers through the follower cards: beside Largesse's +10 followers, under 2x. Round I: rivals hold 3 to 5 of a color; you'd wait, and the wait is the decision.
3. **Both sides:** the owner chooses rival, color and when. Mostly "the biggest pile", but not always: taking silver hurts you in Silver's Fewest silver, and taking the color you're short of can beat the biggest pile. The real choice is when to fire: now for 6, or wait for the hoarder to reach 10 and risk them spending it.
4. **Who feels it:** the hoarder. They lose nothing, but Ben now sits next to them in Most resources and can take the prize. Holding resources gets a reader at the table: spend or be copied.
5. **Not refused:** no copy-a-pile idea in the ledger. Nearest no's: Yellow's "raise one color to match your biggest" (your own pile; this reads a rival's) and Return the Favor (counting past gifts; this counts a pile on the table right now).
- Principles: intuitive (one sentence, the pile is on the table); fun (the table watches who's hoarding); interacts (every color's resources, Magenta's contest, the hold-or-spend plan Cory wants to keep) without naming a color; feels powerful (a relative amount, Cory's "steal half of one rival's pile" kind); counters a hoarding engine; new way into copying: piles, not actions or members.
- Against Encore: Encore's moment needs a second card and repeats your own; Lookalikes' moment is reading a rival.
- Risk, one line: close to Heist Crew in target; the verb and the victim's loss differ.

### Pick 2, Tribute Band's slot: **Leakers** [Claude's price: 11, a round II member]
**Prints:** "On Use, once per game: copy an action that opens in a later round."
1. **Text compared with:** Tribute Band (above); Cover "Copy an action with no minion on it. +1 green." (open actions only); the round III actions it can reach: Initiation "Recruit a member in the shop costing 10 or less, for free. +1 red.", Poach "Recruit a rival's member you choose, for free. +1 magenta.", Largesse "Give rivals 12 silver from the bank, split as you choose. +10 followers.", Spree "Recruit two extra times this turn. +6 blue.", Cash In "Turn all your white into followers. +1 white."
2. **One played turn**, round II, 4 players. Ana has built around Panel ("On Use, once per round: promote or demote another of your members.") and expects Poach not to exist until round III. Ben uses Leakers to copy Poach and takes Panel, free. Ana loses her engine a round early, with a round left for Ben to use it. Best other round II spaces: Promote or Demote (swap a member, +3 red), Shake Down (a member costing 7 or less or its cost). Poach beats them. Top case: copy Largesse in round II for +10 followers: one round III action, a round early; under 2x of the best round III space. Earlier followers also mean Ben goes later next round (fewest followers go first). Tribute Band's top case was 4 or 5 actions at once.
3. **Both sides:** Ben chooses which action and when. A real choice: Poach when a rival's member is worth it, Initiation when the shop is good, Largesse when he needs followers.
4. **Who feels it:** whoever thought a round III action was a round away. A sting and a reason to Poach or swap Leakers away from Ben before he uses it.
5. **Not refused:** Yellow's "do any other color's round III action" was cut as too strong with Green copying it; that was a space anyone could take every game. This is one use, on one card, at a high price. Not the same shape as "copy the next / your last / the first action" (Oct 18): it reaches a piece no Green card touches, the round lock.
- Principles: intuitive (the dark spaces are on the wheel); fun (a round III action a round early); interacts with every color through its center action, without naming one; feels powerful (breaks a limit everyone feels); an engine piece with Copyist (+2 followers on the copy) and the cloak; new way into copying: the future, not the past.
- Against Tribute Band: smaller, but a moment Tribute Band can't make, and no 5-action top case.

### Pick 3, Fan Club's slot: **Superfans** [Claude's price: 8]
**Prints:** "Each of your minions on a Green space counts as 2 in Green's contest."
1. **Text compared with:** Fan Club (above); Green's contest Most minions on Green spaces (+4 / +6 / +10, ties split, rounded up); grey Dabblers "Counts as 2 wild members." (the counts-as-2 wording players already know).
2. **One played turn**, round II, 4 players. Five Green spaces are open. Ben has Superfans and minions on Echo and Cover: he counts 4. Cleo took Build Up, Reprise and Sample: 3. Ben wins +6 instead of losing it. Best other card at 8: Copyist at 5 pays +2 per copy, so +4 for Ben's two copies this round. Top case: round III, Ben holds 3 of the 6 Green spaces and counts 6 against the other 3; he wins the +10. Over a game, at most +20 if every round was contested and he'd have lost each one; in practice the gain is the rounds he'd tie or lose.
3. **Both sides:** no choice on the card. The choice it makes for rivals: block a Green space early, or concede the contest.
4. **Who feels it:** everyone chasing Green's contest needs two spaces for each of Ben's one, so Green spaces get taken early to block, which is the chaos Cory starred. It's also a member Red and Magenta want to move or take.
5. **Not refused:** the earlier contest variant "minions on green plus green members" counted members (no verdict); this still needs you on the spaces, which is Cory's reason for liking the contest. Not Copycats ("you tie its leader", saved for the contests color): it doesn't reach other colors' contests.
- Principles: intuitive (Dabblers' "counts as 2"); fun (the doubled count is plain on the rim); interacts with every player who takes a Green space; feels powerful (the +10 swing); builds the copy engine with Copyist and Echo; a new way in: the first Green member about the contest.
- Against Fan Club: Fan Club's best turn is one copied action, the same as Stunt Double's or one use of Song Request; Superfans' is a contest swing no other card makes.
- Risk, one line: in a game where nobody else wants Green spaces it does nothing extra (Ben would win anyway); Spoilsport and blocking are the counters.

### Cut, and why
Encore's slot
- "On Use, once per game: copy the On Use of a rival's member": a narrower Impersonator, which already gains a rival member's whole ability.
- Backup Singers "Round End: copy the Round End ability of a rival's member.": the same (Impersonator twin), and dead in round I when rivals hold 0 or 1 members.
- "On Use, once per round: copy the On Use of a rival's member": top case copies Extra Hand every round, +1 minion a round.
- "Whenever a rival places a minion on a Green space, +1 green.": Purple's Picket Line shape, and the +1 resource trigger Cory finds boring.
- "Whenever you take a Green space, +1 follower.": Silver's Toastmaster with another color's name on it.
- "Whenever a rival buys followers, +1 follower.": fires almost every turn; about +10 a game for 7.
- "Whenever a rival copies one of your actions, +2 followers.": Bait, Royalties and Flatterers' shape, all refused or retired.
- "On Use, once per game: gain what a rival got from their last action.": Echo on a member.
- "On Use, once per game: copy every action you have a minion on this round.": Greatest Hits and Stunt Double on yourself.

Tribute Band's slot
- "On Use, once per game: +2 followers per minion on Green spaces.": Silver's Ballroom Dancers.
- Bootleggers "Game End: copy the Game End ability of a rival's member.": Impersonator twin.
- "On Use, once per game: this round you win Green's contest.": a Copycats shape, saved for the contests color.
- "On Use, once per game: copy the On Recruit of each member of one rival.": Greatest Hits pointed at a rival; near copy.
- "Whenever you copy an action, +1 follower per Green space you have a minion on.": a formula stacked on Copyist; top case about 20 a game.
- "On Use, once per game: copy every action with the most-followers player's minion.": Tribute Band again.
- "On Use, once per game: copy every action with no minion on it.": early round III that is 10+ actions.
- "On Recruit: copy each rival's latest action.": three round III actions at once, well over 2x.
- "On Use, once per game: +1 minion this round, placed on a Green space.": grey Extra Hand.

Fan Club's slot
- "Counts as a minion on a Green space.": a muddier, smaller Superfans.
- "Your green members count as minions on Green spaces.": wins Green's contest without going to Green, against Cory's reason for liking it.
- "Whenever you copy a rival's action, you and they each +1 follower.": Flatterers' shape (refused).
- "Whenever you copy an action, do the On Recruit of one of your members.": Con Men four times a round.
- "Round End: +1 follower per minion you have on Green spaces.": Toastmaster at round end.
- "On Recruit: +1 follower per minion on Green spaces.": Ballroom Dancers at half size.
- "Whenever you copy an action, +1 follower per rival with a minion on a Green space.": Copyist plus a count.
- "On Use, once per round: copy the On Recruit of a member in the discard pile.": Tryout's shape (Cory: Tryout "overlaps with green"); and Cashier from the discard every round is too strong.
- "On Recruit: do the On Recruit of a member of the player with the most followers.": Sample and Impersonator.
- "Round End: if you lead Green's contest, +1 follower per minion on Green spaces.": pays only the winner of a contest that already pays.
