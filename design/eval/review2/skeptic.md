# Review 2: skeptic pass (Nov 8)

One line per survivor in the nine reviews: KEEP or KILL, with the reason. Tests used: does it do something at the real table in its round; same shape as a refused idea; readable at a glance; another color's job; needs memory or watching rivals all the time; a cloak that pays followers or copies its color's members; best case over about twice the best alternative; would Cory like it, given his notes. The current card staying is always allowed.

37 survivors read. 19 kept, 18 killed.

## Red
- KILL **Walk-In 1A** "Recruit a shop member costing 6 or less, for free. Each rival +1 red. +1 red.": a smaller Initiation in round I, the shape refused as "a smaller copy of a stronger space"; in round I a free 6-cost member beats every other round I space by miles.
- KILL **Walk-In 1B** (same, from the shop or the discard pile): the same smaller Initiation; and recruiting from the discard pile goes to Blue (Thrift Shoppers), so it would clash.
- KILL **Exchange Students** "On Use, once per game: give a rival all your other members of one color, and take as many of theirs as you gave, your pick.": not readable at a glance (which of theirs? any color?), and a bigger Body Snatchers in the same color.
- KEEP **Long-Lost Cousins** "On Recruit: recruit a member of the color you have the most of, from the shop or the discard pile, for free.", with "or the discard pile" dropped (the discard pile goes to Blue). It answers Cory's own complaint about Mentor ("too hard to build around") with a card you build around. Risk: no price cap; at its best a 13-cost card brings a 14-cost card free, about twice the best alternative.
- KEEP **Concerned Parents** "Whenever a rival takes one of your members, +3 followers.": the counter Cory likes (Snitch), and Red's side of his "when a member leaves your cult" tie. Risk: rivals just take from someone else, so it fires rarely.

## Blue
- KILL **Impulse Buyers** "Once per turn, you may buy followers as an extra recruit.": White's Barker "Each time you recruit a member, you may also recruit followers." in other words. Another color's job.
- KEEP **Thrift Shoppers** "On Use, once per round: recruit a member from the discard pile, paying its cost.": an extra recruit (Blue's job) from the face-up discard pile Cory made. The one card across all nine reviews that reaches into the discard pile, after the Red ones are cut back.
- KILL **Big Spenders** "On Use, once per game: recruit an extra time this turn.": Cory cut its grey twin as "too blatantly blue", and Blue's Plus-Ones already does it. A near copy; his question on it stays open.

## White
- KEEP **Old Money** "Game End: +2 followers per white member your rivals have.": pays White for members that left, not ones it holds, so it stops fighting White's Fewest members. Risk: close in shape to Silver's Patron Saints.
- KILL **Old Money** "Whenever another of your members leaves your cult, +2 followers.": a twin of Undertaker in White's own 12, and it overlaps Red's Concerned Parents and Martyrs. Three "leaves your cult" cards is one too many.
- KEEP **Allowance** with the bonus on buying followers instead of recruiting a member: a one-word fix that stops the space paying against White's own contest. Goes on the fix list, not as a new card.
- (Cory's own moves, not judged: Godparent and Guest of Honor into White. Wording fixes for Farewell, Estate Sale, Downsizers kept as fixes.)

## Silver
- KEEP **Silver Cloak A** "Whenever you gift silver, +2 of any colors.": pays resources like every other cloak, and no longer costs its holder the Fewest silver contest. Risk: "a little different from silver" may mean a new trigger.
- KEEP **Silver Cloak B** "Whenever any player gifts silver, +1 of any color.": the any-player shape Cory praised on the White Cloak; quiet, as cloaks should be.
- KEEP **High Tide**, back in round II for Sweet Nothings: "Each rival +1 silver. Then gain 1 follower per silver the rival with the most silver holds.": Cory's own starred card, never refused, and Fewest silver makes "pay yours down" the contest.
- KILL **Socialites** "Whenever a rival recruits paying silver, +1 follower.": the owner has to watch every rival's payment all game (Cory on Shoplifter: "annoying to remember").
- KEEP **Silver Barons** "Round End: +1 follower per 3 silver all players hold together.": Cory short-listed it ("i also like barons"); one count at round end; it pulls against the contest, which makes it a plan.

## Magenta
- KEEP **Turf** "Steal 1 resource from each rival per minion they have on Magenta spaces. +1 magenta.": Cory's own idea; works in round I (Help Yourself fills first).
- KILL **Racket** "Steal 1 resource from the leader of each contest you aren't leading. +1 magenta.": you read four or five rims every time, and in round I most contests are ties at 0 with you in them, so it does little.
- KILL **On Their Tab** "This turn, pay for your recruit with resources you steal from one rival. +1 magenta.": needs a rule for paying colored parts from a rival's pile and for what they can't cover (cost math), and buying the 10-follower card with a rival's 10 is +10 for you and −10 for them in round II, over twice Heist.
- KEEP **Mugging** "Steal half of one rival's resources, rounded down. +1 magenta.": Cory: "so elemental and interesting… should be a default that we consider". Risk: Heist Crew is the same verb in the same color, once per game.
- KILL **Cat Burglar** "Whenever you steal, +1 follower.": the same trigger as Magenta's own cloak (Fence "Whenever you steal, +1 magenta."); Cory wants cloaks and members to differ.

## Yellow
- KILL **Harlequins** "Whenever you buy followers paying with every color, +2 followers.": a full set under another name, a week after Cory cut Jack-of-All-Trades and Seamstress for being full-set cards; and a small conditional +2 (his "small ball").
- KEEP **Influencers** "On Use, once per game: choose two colors; every player's resources of the first become the second.": Yellow's own verb at the table's scale; one plain sentence, whole-table reaction. Risk: a second card that changes rivals' resources beside Counterfeiters; and turning everyone's silver into another color ties the Fewest silver contest four ways.

## Green
- KILL **Lookalikes** "On Use, once per game: choose a rival and a color. Gain as many of that color as they hold.": the shape of Matching Gift, refused Oct 31; nobody loses anything, and it's a lump of resources on a member, where Cory wants followers.
- KEEP **Leakers** "On Use, once per game: copy an action that opens in a later round.": a new way into copying (the future), bends a limit everyone feels, one sentence. Risk: dead if bought in round III.
- KILL **Superfans (Green)** "Each of your minions on a Green space counts as 2 in Green's contest.": a member that counts extra in its own contest, the shape Cory refused ("the incentives need to be more inherent") and the overlap he cut Recluse for. Also shares its name with grey's Superfans.

## Purple
- KEEP **Latecomers** "On Recruit: take the Goes Last token: your next minion is placed after everyone else's.": exactly Cory's Nov 8 ask ("a purple that gives the go last token"); shows up in the shop first, so rivals can answer it.
- KILL **Picket Line, paying +1 follower**: it becomes Silver's High Society ("Whenever a rival takes a Silver space, +1 follower.") with Purple's name on it. Green's review cut the same move for that reason.
- KILL **Night Porters** "Whenever you recruit another purple member, take the Goes Last token…": its own review puts its best case near 22 followers, over twice the best alternative.
- KEEP **Pocket Watch** "Whenever any player takes the Goes Last token, +2 purple.": answers Cory's note on the cloak ("going last is secretly kind of good"), stops doubling Sleepyheads' trigger, stays weak.

## Grey
- KEEP **Anarchist A** "On Use, once per round: swap any 2 members that cost the same, but not your own.": Cory's "needs redesign", made the table's disruptor instead of a second Red.
- KILL **Anarchist B** (once per game, as printed): still pulls members into your own cult, which is Red's Body Snatchers.
- KEEP **Ghost** "Worth −5 followers and 0 members. On Recruit: give this member to a rival.": no longer needs another card to work, as Cory asked.
- KEEP **Scouts** "On Use, once per round: send any shop members to the discard pile, then refill.": Cory's Nov 8 ask ("a neutral that refreshes shop"), and his own liked card. Risk: with the Blue Cloak (+1 per discarded member) it can pay 5 to 7 a round.
- KILL **Masqueraders** "On Recruit: swap cloaks with a rival.": cloaks are weak on purpose, so the best swap is a few resources a round; the cloak-taking shape already lost ("small trigger, rarely a big moment").
- KILL **Superfans (grey)** "Game End: +2 followers per grey member, anyone's.": Patron Saints in grey, and a name clash with Green's Superfans.
- KEEP **Bulk Buyers** "Whenever you buy 10 followers at once, +5 followers.": keeps Cory's "hold resources and buy followers" plan alive in every game. Risk: buying followers is White's ground (White Cloak, Money Changers, Barker).

## Clashes across colors
- Two "Superfans" (Green, grey): both killed.
- Recruiting from the discard pile (Red's Walk-In 1B and Long-Lost Cousins, Blue's Thrift Shoppers, grey's cut list already calls it Blue's): only Thrift Shoppers keeps it.
- Buying followers as an extra recruit (Blue's Impulse Buyers) is White's Barker: killed.
- "+1 follower when a rival takes one of this color's spaces" (Purple's Picket Line fix) is Silver's High Society: killed.
- "Leaves your cult": Red's Concerned Parents and White's Martyrs and Undertaker stay; White's second Old Money killed.
- Buying-followers payoffs: White's Allowance fix and grey's Bulk Buyers both pay on buying followers; flagged on Bulk Buyers.
- Resources changed for every rival: Yellow's Influencers can wipe out Silver's Fewest silver race (flagged).
