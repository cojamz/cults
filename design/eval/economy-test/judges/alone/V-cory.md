# Economy V, judged as Cory

A. Tracking: 3/5
Game 3 is easy to follow because almost nobody holds members (Ben ends round II with only Fan Club, Ava with none). Game 1 gets hard in round II and III: members move between cults all the time. Cal's Demote swaps Cory's Snobs for Window Dressers, Ava takes Dabblers off Cal with Shake Down, then in round III Cory's Kidnapper takes Dabblers, Ava's Poach takes Kidnapper and Dabblers back, and Cory's Anarchist sends Dabblers to the shop, all inside two turns. On top of that, a color's contest changes each round (Game 1 Silver goes from most resources to most of one color to fewest silver), so it's one more thing to recheck every round.

B. Recruits: 2/5
Members rarely feel planned. Most come free off the top of the pile from Walk-In (Game 1, Cory round II turn 5 gets Snobs, round III turn 9 gets Kidnapper; Game 2, Ava turns 1, 4 and 10), which is a blind draw. In Game 3 recruiting barely happens: Ben buys one member (Fan Club, round II turn 7) and cashes it in with Estate Sale in round III, and Ava wins with 0 members. A few bought members did feel like events, like Cory's Scouts and then Alchemists in Game 1, round II.

C. Choices: 3/5
Round I and early round II turns are real weighs: Game 1 Cory turn 2 (Lie Low or Help Yourself or Party Gifts) and Game 2 Ava turn 6 (Sweet Nothings into Spoilsport). From mid round III on, the buy is obvious: pay 10, get 13. Game 3 Ben turns 10, 11 and 12 all end that way, and Game 2 Ava turns 9, 11 and 12 do too. About a third of the seat's buys are a 1-follower tidy-up of leftovers, and Game 1 Cory turn 4 and turn 8 pass with almost nothing on hand.

D. Resources: 3/5
Round I is tight (Game 1, Cory holds 1 to 5 resources all round and passes on turn 4). By round III they pile up and get dumped into followers: Toast gives 9 silver, and Ben's Makeover in Game 3 turns 27 resources into green. Steals land hard on one player in Game 1. Ben loses 5 blue to Cory's Con Men (round II turn 7), 4 to Ava's Help Yourself, then 5 more to Ava's Heist Crew in round III, and he finishes last on 50.

E. Paths: 2/5
Buying followers beats the other plans. Game 3 ends 111 / 108 / 108 / 102, won by Ava with 0 members, while Cory recruits Debutantes, Godparent, Copyist and Con Men and comes last. In Game 2 Cory builds the biggest cult (14 members) and comes last on 86. Holding colors for contests rarely decides anything, because many contests end in a tie that pays everyone (Game 3 round III: fewest silver pays all four +5).

F. The race: 4/5
Games 2 and 3 are close. In Game 2, Ava leads 87 to 60 at her round III turn 12, and Ben passes her to win 102 to 100 with three 13-follower buys and Patron Saints. In Game 3 the top three end within 3. Game 1 is the weak one: Ava leads from round II on and wins 98 to 87.

G. Size: 4/5
Every color's tricks happen. Copies chain (Game 3 round III: Cal's Echo copies Ben's Cover, which copies Makeover). There are skips (Game 2: Hold Up makes Ava skip in round I and again in round II), steals, swaps, Largesse's 12 silver, Spoilsport cancelling a contest, and free recruits from Housewarming and Initiation. The game still feels big. The member side feels small only in Game 3.

Best moment: Game 2, round III, Ben passes Ava's 87 on the last turns with Right Away and Gather into 13-follower buys, then Patron Saints, and wins 102 to 100.
Worst moment: Game 3, round III, Ava wins the game holding 0 members.

Overall: 2/5. The game is about recruiting members, and here the last round is everyone paying 10 to get 13 followers. A cult with no members can win, which is the "biggest number every turn" flaw again.
