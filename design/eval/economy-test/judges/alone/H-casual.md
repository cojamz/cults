# H, casual judge (a friend at game night)

A. Tracking: 2/5
Game 1 was hard to follow: by Round III Ava had 10 cards and members kept changing hands every turn (Cal's Body Snatchers swapping at round start, Cory's Demote taking Cal's Snobs on turn 9, Ben's Anarchist taking Snobs from Cory on turn 11, Ben's Poach taking Con Men), plus Ava's Critic paying her +1 on almost every recruit. I could not have told you who owned what without the screen. Game 3 was easier because cults stayed small (Cal had 0 to 3 members all game, Ava 4 to 5).

B. Recruits: 3/5
Some buys felt planned and worth it: Cory's Cheerleaders at the end of Round II in game 1 (turn 8) and Ben's Brokers in game 3 (turn 7) were things they saved for. But by Round III most turns were "buy 10 followers" instead of a member (game 2, Ava turns 8 and 11; game 3, Ben turn 11 and almost every rival turn), so members stopped being the exciting part.

C. Choices: 2/5
Too many turns where nothing could be done. Game 1, Cory could recruit nothing on turns 2, 3 and 4 of Round I (turn 2 got 6 followers from Party Gifts, turns 3 and 4 gave 1 resource each) and had 0 members until Round II, and turn 6 says "Cory couldn't afford anything". Game 2, Ava passed on turns 3, 4 and 9; game 3, Ben's Echo on turn 2 and Greatest Hits on turn 12 did nothing at all. I count about 9 of the 38 seat turns as dead or obvious; the Round III turns in games 1 and 2 had real choices (Largesse vs Toast vs Demote).

D. Resources: 3/5
Round I is tight, maybe too tight (game 1, everyone at 0 to 2 resources). Round III floods: in game 3 Ava held 14 yellow and turned everything into 25 yellow with Makeover, then bought 10 followers three turns in a row. Steals mattered but piled on Ben in game 1 (Cory's Extortionist on turn 8, Ava's Con Men, Extortionist and Heist Crew all hit him), which would feel bad in that seat.

E. Paths: 4/5
Different plans really did compete. Game 3, Cal won "fewest members" with 0 to 3 members and finished 87, against Ben's 8 members at 84 and Ava's follower buying at 90. Game 1, Cory finished 59 with 5 members against Ava's and Ben's 16.

F. The race: 4/5
All three were close at the end: 66/65 in game 1, 90/88/87/84 in game 3. The lead changed late in each (game 2, Ben led 66 to 49 on Ava's turn 12 and Cory still won 91). But in game 2 Cory's win came from end-of-game bonuses (+10 Went last, +8 Big Tent, +9 Patron Saints) that you would not see coming as a casual player, and game 1 was decided by Ben's Snobs +9 at the very end.

G. Size: 4/5
Every color's tricks showed up: free recruits (Walk-In, Mentor, Housewarming), steals (Con Men, Help Yourself), copies (Cal's Reprise copying Largesse in game 3), extra turns and skips (Ben's Right Away, Cal's Hold Up in game 2). The skips were the bad side: game 2 Round III, Cory and Ava both had turns skipped back to back.

Best moment: Game 2, Round III, Cory's Makeover turned all his resources into 11 purple and he bought 10 followers in one go; a big, easy-to-see payoff.
Worst moment: Game 1, Round I, Cory's turns 3 and 4: one resource each, nothing to buy, pass and pass while Ava and Cal recruited.

Overall: 3/5
The endings are close and exciting, but too many turns in Round I are spent unable to do anything, and in game 1 the constant member swapping made it hard to know who had what.
