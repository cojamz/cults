# Economy test G, judged as Cory

A. Tracking: 2/5
Game 1 is too much to follow. By Round III Ben has 14 members and Ava 11, and Con Men changes hands four times (Cal recruits it in Round II, Ben's Demote takes it, Cal's Panel swap takes it back, Cory's Kidnapper takes it in Round III, Cory's turn 9). Cory's Promote in Round III (turn 10) offers about 100 swaps. Game 3 is easy to follow, because Ava and Cal finish with 1 member each.

B. Recruits: 2/5
Some recruits were planned: Cory peeks Line Cutters to the top and buys it in Round I (game 1, turn 3), and Ava saves up for Mob, then Dabblers (game 2, Round II). Most members arrive free and incidental, though: Walk-In, Initiation, Housewarming, Mentor's tag-along and Kidnapper copies. In game 3 the 1-member cults finish second and third, so a member was often just a routine 10-resource buy, or something to cash in with Farewell or Estate Sale.

C. Choices: 3/5
Most of the seat's turns had two or more real options. Some examples: Shake Down on Ava against Heist (game 1, Round II), and Largesse against Toast against Makeover (game 2, Round III). The obvious turns were Cory's game 1 Round I turn 4 (only Toast or Stash left), Ben's game 3 Round II turn 5 (Echo copied nothing and he passed), and several "couldn't afford anything" passes. In Round III almost every turn ends in "buy 10 followers", which is the same answer every time.

D. Resources: 2/5
In game 1 resources are scarce, but the thieves pile on one player. Between Cory's Round II turns 5 and 6, Ben's Con Men, Ben's Extortionist and Cal's Con Men take 8 of his resources. He has already lost Dabblers back through Cal's Promote, and he sits at "holds nothing". In games 2 and 3 silver floods the table instead: Largesse hands out 12, Toast gives +3 to everyone, and Ava doubles her silver to 32 with Debutantes (game 2, Round III, turn 10). Holding a color for its contest rarely mattered.

E. Paths: 4/5
Different plans really compete. In game 3, Cory wins at 102 with 9 members, while Cal reaches 99 and Ava 93 with 1 member each by buying followers and cashing members out. In game 1, Ben's wide cult of 14 members takes the member contests and finishes 1 behind Cory. The contests themselves mostly reward "fewest silver" or member counts rather than what the color does, and many split three or four ways (game 2, Round III: fewest silver pays all four players +5).

F. The race: 4/5
Games 1 and 3 stay close and the lead changes late. In game 1, Cal leads 57 to 54 before Round III scores, and Cory wins 78 to 77. In game 3, Cal leads 99 to 79 going into the last scoring, and Cory wins 102 with contests and Cheerleaders. Game 2 runs away: Ben goes from 44 to 113 in Round III with Ballroom Dancers +8 and three 10-follower buys, and wins by 14.

G. Size: 4/5
The colors' tricks happen all the time. Shake Down takes a member when the rival can't pay, Kidnapper steals a member, Spree gives two extra recruits, and Makeover turns everything into one color. Ben's Fan Club chains Reprise into Echo into Sweet Nothings (game 3, Round II). If anything the numbers are oversized: three-figure scores, and +10 followers from Largesse every game. The game feels like "biggest number every turn" more than a payoff you build toward.

Best moment: Game 1, Round II, Cory's turn 5: Shake Down on Ava, who can't pay 7, so Dabblers joins Cory. It's clean, public, and a punchy member swing.

Worst moment: Game 1, Round II, between Cory's turns 5 and 6: three steals in a row and a swap strip Cory to nothing. Then in Round III he has to answer Ava's Shake Down on her turn (move 213).

Overall: 3/5. The deciding reason: the paths and the close finishes are good, but steals pile on one player, members keep changing hands, and Round III ends in a 10-follower buy every turn. That is too much to track, and the numbers are too big for the payoff to land.
