A. Tracking: 2/5
Members change hands too often to follow. In game 1, Con Men went Cal to Ben to Cal to Cory between round II and Cory's turn 9 (Kidnapper), and Cory's Promote on his turn 10 offered about 90 swaps. By round III, Ben (game 1) and Cal (game 2) each held 6 to 7 cards with live effects (Window Shopper, Mannequin, Toastmaster, Kingmakers, Debt Collectors), plus a cloak each.

B. Recruits: 3/5
Paid members cost 7 to 13 and some felt planned: game 1, Cory bought Patron Saints for 10 silver as round II's last buy and Patriarch for 12 on round III turn 10, and both paid off at game end. Many members came free or by swap instead (Walk-In, Tryout, Mentor, Demote, Guest of Honor), and in round III a member often lost out to "pay 10, get 10 followers" (game 3, Ben's turns 9 and 11).

C. Choices: 3/5
Placements were real choices most turns, with 10 to 19 spaces open in rounds II and III: Shake Down or Heist or Bury for Cory in game 1 round II. The recruit step was often obvious. I count about 9 of the 39 seat turns as automatic, either because nothing but 1 follower was affordable (game 1, Cory's turns 2, 5 and 9; game 2, Ava's turn 6) or because buying 10 followers was clearly best (game 3, Ben's turns 9 and 11).

D. Resources: 3/5
Round I is tight. In game 1, Ava "couldn't afford anything" and Cory passed on turn 4, and contests on holdings ("spending this gives up your contest lead in magenta") make you weigh every spend. By round III resources flood the table. Debutantes doubled Ava's silver to 32 and Makeover turned it into 22 yellow (game 2, Ava's turns 10 and 11). Toast gives +9 to +12, Largesse hands out 12 silver, and almost every round III turn ends in a 10-follower buy.

E. Paths: 4/5
Different plans really competed. In game 3, Cal (1 member, 99 followers) and Ava (1 member, 93) ran close to Cory (9 members, 102) and Ben (10 members, 87). In game 1, Ben built on follower buys and contests with 3 cards, and Cory built on a wide cult with end-game bonuses.

F. The race: 4/5
Two games of three were close at the end with lead changes: game 1 ended 78 to 77 after Cal, then Ava, then Cory led, and game 3 ended 102 to 99. Game 2 ran away: Ben pulled ahead from round III turn 10 and won by 14. Game 3 was settled by kingmaking: Ben, in last place, used Spoilsport and Kingmakers in a way that cost the leader, Cal, the win.

G. Size: 5/5
Every color's tricks happened, often several times a round. Game 3, Ben's turn 6: Fan Club copied Reprise, which copied Echo, which copied Sweet Nothings. Game 2 had skips and Goes Last fights (Hold Up, Sleep In, Last Call), and game 1 had free recruits (Seat Filler, Mentor, Kidnapper), steals (Con Men, Heist) and extra recruits (Recruit Twice, Spree).

Best moment: Game 1, round III. Cory's planned Patron Saints (+7) and Patriarch (+4) paid off at game end, winning 78 to 77 over Ben.
Worst moment: Game 3, end of round III. Ben, in last place, spoiled the Fewest members contest that Cal (1 member) led, which handed the win to Cory. The write-up line is also unclear: the choice reads "White (Ava & Cal lead)" while the result names Fewest members.

Overall: 3/5. What decides it is that the tension is gone by round III. Resources pile up, and most turns become "take the biggest space, buy 10 followers", so the close finishes come from end-game bonuses and a last-place player's Spoilsport, not from tight trade-offs.
