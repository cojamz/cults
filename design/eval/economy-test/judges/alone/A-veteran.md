# Judge: veteran, sheets A

A. Tracking: 3/5
Game 3 is easy to follow: nobody holds more than 4 members until late (Ben ends with 2). Game 1 is hard: Dabblers moves from Ava to Cal (Shake Down, R2 opening), to Ben (Promote), to the shop and back, to Cory (R2 turn 8), to Cal (Promote, R3 opening), to Ava (Poach), to Ben (Kidnapper) and to Cory again (Initiation, R3 turn 11). In Game 2 R3, Ben's Counterfeiters turns all 11 of Ava's resources into red right before her last turn, which is a lot to keep in your head.

B. Recruits: 2/5
Members often feel incidental. In Game 1 Cory has 1 member (Kingmakers) from R1 turn 4 until R2 turn 8 and still wins. In Game 3 Cory has 0 members on R3 turn 10 and 65 followers, after casting out Alchemists for 8 followers with Farewell. Ben gets Guest of Honor free (Game 3 R2 turn 5) and finally gives away Fan Club with it for +4. The planned recruits that do land, like Cory's free Kidnapper from Initiation in Game 1 R3 turn 11, are the exception.

C. Choices: 3/5
R1 and early R2 have real trade-offs. In Game 1 R1 turn 4, Cory weighs Toast into Kingmakers against buying 5 followers, and in Game 2 R2 turn 4 Ava gifts silver into Spoilsport. Some turns are hollow, though. Cory passes 3 times in Game 1 R1 because he can afford nothing, and Ava takes Right Away with no minions left (Game 2 R1 turn 3). By late R3 almost every seat turn is the obvious "buy 10 followers" (Game 3 Ben turns 11 and 13, Game 2 Ava turns 11 and 12).

D. Resources: 2/5
R1 is tight: Cory sits at 1 follower holding 1 to 2 resources for 3 turns in Game 1, and steals of 1 to 4 resources hurt. R3 floods. Toast gives +9, Largesse hands rivals 12 silver, and Ava's Makeover turns everything into 12 purple (Game 2) or 18 yellow (Game 3). In Game 2 R3, 7 turns in a row end in a 5 or 10 follower buy. Scarcity is gone exactly when the big contest prizes are on the line.

E. Paths: 3/5
Different plans do appear. Ben wins Game 3 with 2 members on few-members and green contests, Ava wins Game 2 with 10 members plus Cheerleaders and Spoilsport, and Cory wins Game 1 on contests and big follower buys with 6 members. But buying followers in R3 is the common road in every game. The member-heavy seats (Ava with 7 in Game 3, Ben with 10 in Game 1) finish last or near last.

F. The race: 4/5
The race stays close and there are lead changes. Game 3 ends 102/97/95/94. In Game 1, Cory is last with 9 followers at R2 turn 5 and wins 76 to 62. In Game 2, Ava trails Cory 58 to 80 before her last turn and wins 95 to 86 on Went last +10, three Cheerleaders triggers and Spoilsport. Catch-up works, but so much rides on the final R3 turns and the +10 contests that earlier rounds count for less.

G. Size: 4/5
Each color's tricks show up. Ben's Spree recruits two members and buys 10 followers in one turn (Game 1 R3). Cory steals a member twice with Heist and Kidnapper (Game 1). Copies chain: Cal's Echo copies Ben's Cover, which copied Largesse (Game 3 R3). Extra turns and skips happen too: Overtime takes the Goes Last token with 4 of Ben's minions on it (Game 2 R3). The game feels big.

Best moment: Game 1 R3, Cory's last turn: he gives away 3 silver with Sweet Nothings and spends the rest buying 10 followers, so he drops to 0 silver and takes Fewest silver for +10. That is a planned contest play that wins the game.
Worst moment: Game 3 R3: Ben copies Largesse (turn 9), then Cal copies it with Echo and Cory takes it himself, so +10 followers three times and about 36 free silver handed out in one stretch. The economy stops being a trade-off at all.

Overall: 3/5. R3 turns into a flood of resources and follower buys, so the tension built in R1 and R2 is gone at the end, where the game is decided.
