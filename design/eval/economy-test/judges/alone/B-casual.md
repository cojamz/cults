# Economy test B, casual judge (three games: Cory's seat seed 3, Ava's seat seed 11, Ben's seat seed 23)

A. Tracking: 2/5
Game 1 was the hardest to follow: Con Men changed cults six times (Cal recruits it in round II, then Ava's Demote, Cal's Promote, an Ava swap after Lie Low whose cause the write-up doesn't give, Cory's Promote on his round III turn 9, Cal's Demote, Cory's Double Agent on turn 13), and by round III Cory and Ava each held 7 to 9 member cards. Games 2 and 3 were easier because members moved less, and in game 3 Cory and Ava held none, but on Ben's round III turn 9 one turn ran through Sweet Talk, Extra Hand, Song Request copying Echo, then Sweet Talk again, and that is a lot to watch.

B. Recruits: 2/5
Some recruits felt like big moments, like Cory's Con Men on game 1 round II turn 5 stealing 5. But Ben swapped it away on the very next turn, and in game 3 Ava recruited Con Men and Fan Club in round II only to sell each one to Estate Sale the same turn. Members felt like loose change to spend, not something you planned for and kept.

C. Choices: 3/5
Most of the seat's turns had 5 to 20 places to go and 2 or more sensible buys, so there was usually something to think about. Still, a lot of turns ended in "buy 1 follower" or a pass (game 1 Cory turns 2 and 4, game 2 Ava turns 2, 4 and 6, game 3 Ben turns 3, 6, 7, 13), and about four of those were obvious because nothing in the shop was affordable.

D. Resources: 2/5
Resources were tight in rounds I and II of game 1, with steals every few turns (Cal's Con Men took 5 from Cory in round II). By round III they piled up everywhere: Cal held 20 silver in game 1, Makeover turned Cal's whole stack into 29 green in game 3, and nearly every round III turn ended with a "buy 10 for 10" or more.

E. Paths: 3/5
Plans really did differ: in game 2 Ben went for purple and going last (Night Managers +8 at the end), Cal for many member colors, and in game 3 Ava won 110 to 99 with zero members by buying followers and taking the fewest-members contest. That counts as competing plans, but as a newcomer I'd be confused that the game about recruiting members was won by never keeping one.

F. The race: 4/5
Game 1 was close and the lead changed: Ben led 56 to 39 going into Cory's last turn (turn 14), and Cory won 63 to 61 on the round III contests and his Spoilsport. Game 2 ended 93 to 90 after Cory passed Ben in round III. Game 3 was not close: Ava led from round II (37 to Ben's 15) and was never caught.

G. Size: 4/5
Every color's tricks showed up: free recruits (Walk-In, Mentor, Seat Filler), steals and follower heists, copies (Echo, Cover, Song Request), skipped turns (Hold Up skipped Ava in game 2, round II), and extra minions (Extra Hand, Overtime). The game felt big, and that bigness is a big part of why it was hard to follow.

Best moment: Game 1, round III, Cory's turn 14: a single Spoilsport cancels the magenta contest Cal was leading, and Cory wins 63 to 61 on the last scoring.

Worst moment: Game 1, round II: Con Men goes Cal to Ava to Cal to Ava within a few turns, and every move steals 5 again, so the player who keeps getting hit (Cal, down to 9 followers) can't see what to do about it.

Overall: 2/5. The swings are fun, but I couldn't keep track of who owned what in game 1, and in game 3 the winner never kept a member, so I wouldn't understand why I won or lost.
