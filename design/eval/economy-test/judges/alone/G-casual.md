# G, casual judge

A. Tracking: 2/5
In game 1, members keep changing hands. In round II, Con Men went from Cal (bought off Peek) to Ben (Demote swap) and back to Cal (Panel swap), stealing 5 each time it arrived. Dabblers went Ava → Cory (Shake Down) → shop → Ben → Cal → Ben → Cory → Cal by round III. By Cory's turn 12 Ben held 7 member cards, which is too much to keep in your head. Game 3 was easier because Ava and Cal held 0 to 2 members most of the game.

B. Recruits: 3/5
Some recruits feel planned. In game 1, round I, Cory's turn 3, he puts Line Cutters on top with Peek and buys it. In game 2, round III, Ava's turn 10, she uses Debutantes to double her silver to 32, then buys High Society. Many members arrive without anyone choosing them, though: Walk-In's free top card, Mentor's free extra, Guest of Honor and Godparent handing cards to rivals. In game 3, round II, Ava buys Big Tent and casts it out for 11 white on her very next turn, so the member was only a way to convert resources.

C. Choices: 3/5
Cory's turns in game 1 usually had a real trade-off: Shake Down on Ava or Ben (turn 5), which Heist target (turns 6 and 12), and Kidnapper's pick (turn 9). Some turns were empty or forced. Game 1, turn 4: Cory had only Toast or Stash open. Game 2, turn 4: Ava took Last Call and then passed. Game 2, turn 12: Ava's Right Away did nothing because she had no minions left. In round III of every game, most turns end in "buy 10 followers", which takes no thought.

D. Resources: 3/5
Early on, resources are worth fighting for. In game 1, turn 2, Cal steals 2 of Cory's 3 blue right after Cory takes them, and Shake Down makes Ava give up Dabblers when she can't pay 7. By round III, resources pile up. Ava (game 2, turn 11) turned 22 resources into yellow, and Ben (game 3, turn 8) was holding 17 silver. Steals of 5 at that point barely change anything.

E. Paths: 4/5
Game 3 shows real competing plans. Ava finished on 93 with 0 or 1 members, mostly by buying followers. Cal had 99 with 1 member. Cory won with 102 and 9 members, and Ben (10 members) finished last on 87. In game 2, Ben won by holding the Goes Last token and having Spoilsport cancel two contests.

F. The race: 4/5
Game 1 ended 78 to 77, and the lead changed: Cal was ahead on 57 going into round end, then Cory's Patron Saints (+7) and Patriarch (+4) won it. Game 3: Cal led 99 to 79 before scoring, and Cory won 102 to 99 on contests and Cheerleaders. Game 2 got away late. Ben went from 53 to 113 in round III while Ava, 2 behind Cory on turn 11, finished last on 68. A casual player might not see an end-game bonus win like game 1's coming.

G. Size: 4/5
Every color did its tricks. Largesse paid +10, Con Men stole 5, Hold Up and Last Call skipped turns, Makeover turned everything into one color, Spree and Recruit Twice gave extra recruits, and Debutantes doubled silver. Game 3, round II, Ben's turn 6 was a copy chain: Fan Club, then Reprise, then Echo, then Sweet Nothings again. It felt big, but it was hard to follow.

Best moment: Game 1, round I, Cory's turn 3: he uses Peek to put Line Cutters on top, buys it, and picks first for the rest of the game. It was a clear plan that paid off.
Worst moment: Game 1, round II: Con Men changes owner three times in one round and steals 5 every time it lands, and on turn 6 Cory sits with nothing left.

Overall: 3/5
The finishes were close and the turns were big, but the deciding problem is that members keep moving between cults through swaps, gifts and theft, so at the table you lose track of who has what and why the score moved.
