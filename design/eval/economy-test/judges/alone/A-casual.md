# Economy test A, judged by the casual player

A. Tracking: 2/5
No cult ever holds many cards (most have 2 to 6), but the cards keep changing hands, so I couldn't keep track of whose card was whose. In game 1, Dabblers went from Ava to Cal, then Ben, then Cal, then Cory in round II, then in round III Cal swapped it away, Ava stole it, Ben's Kidnapper took it, and Cory's Kidnapper took it back. In game 2, round III, Ben's Counterfeiters turned all 11 of Ava's resources red in one go, which I'd only notice if I was watching closely.

B. Recruits: 2/5
Members mostly come free or show up as side effects (Walk-In, Housewarming, Initiation, swaps), and by round III every turn is a follower buy instead. In game 3, Ben won holding 2 members, and Cory had 0 members on Ben's turn 10. Members got cast out for cash (Farewell, Estate Sale) or given away for +4 (Guest of Honor) as often as they were kept.

C. Choices: 3/5
Where to put a minion usually felt like a real pick. The buy step often didn't. In game 1, round I, Cory's turns 2 and 3 offered only "buy 1 follower or pass". In game 2, round I, Ava's turn 3 Right Away did nothing because she had no minions left. In round III of all three games, nearly every seat turn ended with "buy 10 followers", and by then that was obvious (game 2, Ava turns 11 and 12; game 3, Ben turns 11 and 13).

D. Resources: 2/5
Round I is tight: in game 1, Cory had nothing worth buying until turn 4. Round III floods. Largesse alone hands out 12 silver, and it was played three times in game 3, round III (Ben through Cover, Cory, and Ben again through Fan Club). In game 3, round III, Ava sat on 19 yellow before Ben's turn 10. Steals in game 1 (Help Yourself, Con Men, Heist) did sting, but late in the game nobody was short.

E. Paths: 3/5
Different builds did win: game 1 by Cory with 6 members, game 2 by Ava with 10, game 3 by Ben with 2. Contests like fewest silver and went last decided a lot. But under every plan, the follower buy was the engine, so the plans felt more alike than they look.

F. The race: 4/5
All three were close at the end and the lead changed. Game 3 finished 102, 97, 95, 94. In game 2, Ava trailed Cory 58 to 80 on her last turn and won 95 to 86. In game 1, Cory was last at the start of round III (25 against Cal's 31) and won 76. The catch: the swing comes at round-end scoring, so from my seat it might feel like the win showed up out of nowhere.

G. Size: 4/5
Every color's trick happened. There were free recruits (Walk-In, Initiation), steals (Heist, Kidnapper, Shake Down), copies (Echo copying Party Gifts in game 3, round I), skipped turns (Hold Up in game 2, round I), extra minions (Overtime), and swaps. The game felt big, sometimes too busy.

Best moment: Game 3, round III, Ben's turn 12: he recruits Fan Club, uses it to copy Cory's Largesse for +10, then hands Fan Club to Cory with Guest of Honor for +4. A clear, clever combo anyone at the table can see.

Worst moment: Game 1, round I, Cory's turns 2 and 3: he ends both turns with nothing to recruit while rivals steal from him, and he is still on 1 follower going into his last turn of the round.

Overall: 3/5. The close finishes would bring me back, but losing track of whose members are whose, and round III turning into "take a big space, buy 10 followers" every turn, would wear me out by the second play.
