# V, judged by the casual friend

A. Tracking: 3/5
Game 3 is easy to follow: Ava and Cory have 0 members through round II, and Ava finishes with none. Game 1 is hard. Dabblers changes hands five times (Cal to Ava by Shake Down in round II, then Cory's Kidnapper in round III turn 9, Ava's Kidnapper right after, Anarchist, Ben, then Cory's Kidnapper on turn 12), and I couldn't keep straight who held it.

B. Recruits: 2/5
Most members come free off Walk-In, Tryout or Initiation, not from saving up. In game 3 members barely matter: Ben sells his only one, Fan Club, on round III turn 10 for 10 white, and Ava wins with 0 members. The one buy that feels planned is game 2, round II, Ava's turn 6: Spoilsport, which she then uses to cancel Ben's Went Last contest.

C. Choices: 2/5
Where to place is a real choice. The buy is almost always automatic: in round III you buy "pay 10, get 13" any time you have 10, and otherwise spend your last 1 on a single follower. In game 3, Ben's turns 10 to 13 and Ava's game 2 turns 9, 11 and 12 all end with that same buy. Most early turns are "buy 1 follower" or pass because nothing else is affordable.

D. Resources: 2/5
Resources are tight in round I but pile up late. Game 3 round III: Ben turns 27 resources into green with Makeover, and Toast gives +9 silver. Steals do bite, though. In game 1 round II, Cory's Con Men takes 5 of Ben's 9 blue. In game 3 round III, Ben loses 9 green to Con Men and its copy in one go-around.

E. Paths: 2/5
Game 1 has some contrast: Cal reaches 78 with 2 members while Cory reaches 87 with 11. But game 3 is everyone buying followers. The contests there mostly split: Fewest silver goes four ways in rounds I and III, and Most minions on green splits three ways in round III.

F. The race: 4/5
Games 2 and 3 come down to the wire. Game 2: Ava leads 87 to 60 after round III turn 12, and Ben wins 102 to 100 with three big follower buys and Patron Saints' +9 at game end. Game 3 ends 111, 108, 108, 102. Game 1 is less close: Ava leads from round II and wins 98 to 87.

G. Size: 3/5
The tricks do show up. Game 1 has Kidnapper, Heist Crew, Anarchist and Spree. Game 2 has Hold Up skips, the Goes Last token fight and two Spoilsports. Game 3 has Echo copying Party Gifts in round I and Makeover. But by round III of every game, the tricks are mostly there to feed one more 13-follower buy.

Best moment: Game 2, round III. Ava sits at 87, and Ben takes Right Away, goes twice and buys 13 twice, then Patron Saints' +9 at game end lets him win 102 to 100.
Worst moment: Game 3, round III. Ben's turns 10 to 13 are four straight turns of "buy 13 for 10" after selling his only member, and Ava wins without ever holding one.

Overall: 3/5. The close finishes would bring friends back, but round III turns into everyone buying 13 followers for 10 each turn. Scores reach 100 and up, and you can't tell why the winner won.
