# H, judged as a veteran worker-placement player

A. Tracking: 3/5
Game 3 is easy to read: every cult holds 2 to 4 members all game, so you only watch resource piles. Game 1 is the opposite: by round III Ava holds 10 member cards (Game 1, Round III, Cory's turn 11), and members change hands almost every turn (Promote, Demote, Body Snatchers, Anarchist, Guest of Honor, Poach), so you can't plan around a rival's cards when they may not be theirs next turn.

B. Recruits: 3/5
Some members feel planned: Cory saves up for Cheerleaders on his last turn of round II (Game 1, Round II, turn 8), and Ben builds his Fan Club turn around copying Largesse (Game 3, Round III, turn 12). But in Game 3 members barely matter (Ben wins holding 2), and in Game 1 Cory gets his first member by taking it off Cal with Shake Down rather than buying it, so recruiting often feels beside the point.

C. Choices: 3/5
Rounds I and II hold real trade-offs: in Game 2, Round II, Ava weighs Survey and Alchemists against a cheap follower buy. Still, early on a lot of turns are dead. In Game 1, Cory passes his buy on turns 2 to 6 (turn 6 says "couldn't afford anything"), and turns 3 and 4 are just +1 magenta and +1 red. Late in Game 3 the opposite happens: Ben's turns 10, 11 and 13 are "take the biggest gain, then buy 5 or 10 followers", which is obvious because he can afford anything.

D. Resources: 2/5
Round I is tight and the steals hurt there (Game 1, Round I: Accomplice takes 2 from Cory when he holds next to nothing). By round III the money floods in and nothing is scarce. Toast gives +9, Largesse hands out 12 silver at a time, Ava sits on 19 yellow after Makeover (Game 3, Round III), and in Game 2, Round III, Ava, Ben, Cal and Cory all make 10-follower buys. When everyone is cashing piles, nobody fights over a single resource.

E. Paths: 4/5
The winners won different ways. Game 1: Ben, with 16 members and Snobs' +9 at game end. Game 2: Cory, with Big Tent +8, Patron Saints +9 and the round III "went last" contest. Game 3: Ben, with 2 members and nothing but follower buys. Inside Game 3, though, all four players end up on the same plan, buying followers with silver gifts.

F. The race: 5/5
All three finishes are close, and the lead changed late. Game 1 ends 66 to 65: Ava leads 55 to 52 after the last turn, and Ben's Snobs passes her. Game 2: Ben leads 66 to 54 going into scoring, and Cory wins 91 to 86 on game-end bonuses. Game 3 ends 102, 97, 95, 94, and Cory and Cal were tied for the lead at 77 two turns before the end.

G. Size: 4/5
Every color got to do its trick: Hold Up and Sleep In skipping turns, Right Away doubling Ben's turn into two 10-follower buys (Game 2, Round III), Shake Down taking a member, and Cal's Echo copying Ben's Cover copying Largesse (Game 3, Round III). The flip side is that copying makes blocking pointless. In Game 3, Round III, Largesse pays +10 followers four times: Cory takes the space once and copies get it three more times.

Best moment: Game 1, Round III, the last turns: Ava gets her Last-Minute Shopper buy and leads, then Ben's Snobs +9 at game end wins it 66 to 65.
Worst moment: Game 3, Round III: Largesse (give rivals 12 silver, +10 followers) is done four times in one round through copies, and the space that was supposed to be blocked gets done anyway.

Overall: 3/5. What decides it: the opening is tight and the finishes are close, but by round III money piles up so much that placements stop being trade-offs. Every turn is "take the biggest gain, buy 10 followers", and copies get around blocking.
