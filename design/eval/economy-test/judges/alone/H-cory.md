A. Tracking: 2/5
By round III of game 1, Ava has ten members with live text (Window Shopper, Extortionist, Critic, Heist Crew, Seat Filler, Regular, Last-Minute Shopper, Scouts, Mannequin, Line Cutters) and Cal has seven, and Cory's Demote on round III, turn 9 offers about 160 possible swaps. Game 3 is lighter (Cal wins a contest holding 3 members), but game 1 is the "track 10 member cards across each player" problem.

B. Recruits: 3/5
Cory's own buys in game 1 feel planned: Cheerleaders on round II, turn 8 after stealing toward it, Kingmakers on round III, turn 11. But most members arrive free or by force (Walk-In, Shake Down, Housewarming, swaps), and by round III recruits give way to buying 10 followers every turn (game 2, Ava's turns 8 and 11; game 3, Ben's turn 11).

C. Choices: 2/5
In game 1, Cory passes on the recruit step on 7 of 13 turns, and on round I, turns 2 to 4, he can afford nothing at all. In round III the choice is usually obvious: take the biggest space and buy 10 followers. Party Gifts is the same pick four times in game 1 (turns 2, 6, 11), with the same three-way split of silver each time.

D. Resources: 2/5
Round I is starved: Cory holds nothing on three of his four turns. Round III floods: Ava's Makeover turns everything into 18 and then 25 yellow (game 3), Toast gives +9, and Largesse hands out 12 silver one at a time. Steals pile on one player: in game 1, Ben loses resources to Con Men, Extortionist twice, Help Yourself and Heist Crew in rounds II and III.

E. Paths: 4/5
Different plans really compete. In game 3, Ava wins with 4 members and Cal is a point back with 3 members, holding the "fewest members" contest. In game 1, Ben and Ava win with 16 members each. In game 2, Ben lives on going last and Cory wins on end-of-game members (Big Tent +8, Patron Saints +9).

F. The race: 4/5
Game 3 ends 90, 88, 87, 84, and game 1 ends 66 to 65 at the top. In game 2, Cory goes from 54 (third) to 91 at game end with Went last +10, Big Tent and Patron Saints, which is a real lead change. But the end-of-game bonuses come out of nowhere for the table, even though they were face up.

G. Size: 4/5
Every color's trick shows up. Ava's Echo copies Cory's Cover, which copies Makeover (game 3, round III). Ben's Scouts empty the shop twice (game 1). Hold Up and Sleep In skip turns, Cory's Shake Down takes Deprogrammers outright, and Spree gives two extra recruits. Some of it is too big for an ordinary turn, like an 18-yellow Makeover becoming 10 followers.

Best moment: Game 1, round II, Cory's turn 5: Shake Down on Cal, who can't pay 5, so Cory takes Deprogrammers and jumps from 9 to 13 followers. It is one clean idea, a sting everyone saw coming, and it fits the color.
Worst moment: Game 1, rounds II to III: Ben is robbed again and again (Ava's Con Men, Cal's Con Men, Cory's and Ava's Extortionist, Heist Crew taking 4 magenta). Meanwhile Cory sits through round I with nothing to buy.

Overall: 3/5
The race stays close and the plans differ, but round III turns into "biggest space, buy 10 followers" behind a wall of member text. That is the "every turn is biggest number" flaw, with too much to track.
