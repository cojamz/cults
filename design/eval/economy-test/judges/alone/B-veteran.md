# B, judged by the veteran worker-placement player

A. Tracking: 2/5
Game 1 is hard to follow by round II: Con Men changes hands four times in one round (Cal recruits it, Ava swaps for it on Demote, Cal swaps back on Promote, Ava again on Lie Low), and Cory ends holding 9 member cards plus a cloak. Game 2 piles up the same way (Cal has 10 member cards in round III, plus Counterfeiters recoloring whole piles and the Goes Last token moving every few turns); only game 3 is easy, because nobody holds more than 4 members.

B. Recruits: 3/5
Some recruits are planned events: game 1, round II, Cory's turn 5 uses Tryout on Mentor, then buys Mentor, and comes out with Loyalists, Mentor and Window Shopper in one turn. But in game 3 members barely matter (Cory wins 95 followers while holding 0 members for most of round III, and Ben wins with 2), and many late recruits in games 1 and 2 are "spend what's left" buys like Cory's Extra Hand in game 1, round III, turn 10.

C. Choices: 3/5
Rounds I and II offer real trade-offs. Game 1, round I, Cory's turn 3: Lie Low for safety versus Toast or Party Gifts. Game 2, round III, Ava's turn 9: Godparent gives Dyer to Cory, the leader, for +9. But a few turns were forced (game 1, round I, turn 4: only Gather or Stash were left open). Many round III turns are obvious: take the biggest space, then buy 5 or 10 followers with whatever you hold. That is game 3, Ben's turns 10, 11 and 13, and game 2, Ava's turns 10 and 12.

D. Resources: 2/5
Round I is tight (game 1, round I: Ben "couldn't afford anything", and Cory holds 1 red on turn 2), and the steals in game 1 sting. By round III resources flood the table. Toast gives +9, and Largesse hands out 12 silver at a time and is copied three times in game 3, round III. Ava sits on 19 yellow (game 3, Ben's turn 10), and Ben holds 18 resources (game 2, Ava's turn 12). Nobody fights over a resource that late.

E. Paths: 3/5
Plans differ on paper. Game 1 is a many-members win (Cory, 19 members) against Ben's follower buys. Game 2 is the purple Goes Last plan (Ben's Night Managers +8, Went last +10) against a broad member spread. But every game ends the same way, with 10-for-10 follower buys in the last round. In game 3 all four players converged on silver spaces and follower buys, so members never competed as a path.

F. The race: 4/5
All three are close at the end: 63/61/57/48, 93/90/76/72 and 102/97/95/94. The lead changed late in two of them. In game 1, Ben leads 56 to 39 before Cory's last turn, and Cory wins on Spoilsport plus the +10 member contest. In game 3, Cory leads 77 going into the last turns and finishes third. The catch-up comes mostly from the size of round III payouts (+10 contests, 10-follower buys, Largesse +10), so rounds I and II count for less than they should.

G. Size: 4/5
Each color's tricks show up often. Game 1: free recruits off Walk-In and Mentor, Peek setting the deck, Body Snatchers and Kidnapper swaps, Heist stealing followers three times. Game 2: Hold Up skips, Right Away, Counterfeiters. Game 3: Echo and Reprise copies, though with so few members there the game feels smaller.

Best moment: Game 1, round III, Cory's turn 14: from behind, he recruits Spoilsport, cancels a contest he can't win, and takes the +10 most-members contest, which flips a 17-point gap into a 2-point win.
Worst moment: Game 3, round III, Ben's turn 12: Fan Club copies Largesse for +10 followers and 12 free silver to rivals, Ben gives Fan Club away for +4, then buys 10 followers. The payouts are so big that earlier turns stop mattering.

Unclear in the write-up: in game 1, round III, Cory's turn 14, Cory picks "Magenta (Cal leads)" for Spoilsport, but the result says the most-resources contest was spoiled. It reads as though the contests changed colors by round III, but the write-up never says so.

Overall: 3/5
Rounds I and II are tight and full of real blocking and stealing. Round III floods the table with resources and 10-for-10 follower buys, so the last round is mostly a counting race, and in game 3 members hardly mattered at all.
