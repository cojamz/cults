# Code A, judged as Cory

A. Tracking: 2/5
Members change hands too often to follow. In game 1, Dabblers went Ava to Cal (Shake Down, round II), to Ben (Promote), back to the shop (Ava's Body Snatchers swap), to Cory (round II, Cory's turn 8), to Cal (Promote, round III), to Ava (Poach), to Ben (Kidnapper), and to Cory again (Cory's turn 11). Game 2 adds Spoilsport cancelling a contest each round and Mentor, Godparent and Anarchist moving cards around. Game 3 is easy to track because by round III three cults hold one or three members.

B. Recruits: 3/5
Early recruits feel planned. In game 1, Cory has no member until his 4th turn (Kingmakers), and in game 3, Ben saves up for Agents and Cashier. Later, members are often something you sell. In game 3, Cory gets 9 followers by sending Con Men away with Farewell (round II) and 12 by sending Smooth Talkers (round III). Cal and Ben finish with one member each, so recruiting stops mattering by the end.

C. Choices: 2/5
Round placements are real choices, but too many turns are obvious. In game 1, Cory passes on turns 2 and 3 with nothing he can buy. In game 3, Ben's turn 5 is Reprise with nothing to copy and his turn 6 is "couldn't afford anything." In game 2, Ava's turn 12 is Right Away with no minion left, so it does nothing. In round III of games 2 and 3, almost every buy is "pay 10, get 10 (or 13)," the biggest number every turn.

D. Resources: 2/5
Scarce in round I and flooded in round III. In game 1, Cory holds 1 or 2 resources through his first three turns. By round III of game 2, Ava doubles to 32 silver with Debutantes and turns 22 into yellow with Makeover. In game 3, every player buys 13 followers on nearly every turn. Steals pile onto one player: in game 2, round III, Ava loses 5 silver to Ben's Con Men and then 5 yellow to Cory's Con Men in the same round.

E. Paths: 3/5
Different plans can win. In game 3, Cal with 1 member ties Ava with 10 members at 111. In game 2, Ben wins on the Went-last contest (+6, then +10) with purple. In game 1, Cory wins with 6 members off follower buys and the Fewest silver contest (+10 in round III). Still, follower buying is the finish for everyone, and the member path gets sold off for followers.

F. The race: 3/5
Game 3 is very close (111, 111, 105, 103), and in game 1 Cory goes from last (31 on turn 11) to winning 76 to 62. Game 2 runs away: Ben leads from round III onward and wins 113 to Ava's 68. Game 3 is close mostly because all four players made the same buy every turn.

G. Size: 4/5
Each color's tricks show up. There are free recruits (Walk-In, Initiation into Kidnapper in game 1), copies of copies (game 3, round III: Cory's Echo copies Cover, which copies Makeover), extra minions (Overtime in game 2), skips (Hold Up) and big swings (Largesse +10). Spoilsport cancelling a contest in two rounds of game 2 takes some of the contests away.

Best moment: Game 1, round III, Cory's turn 11. He uses Initiation to take Kidnapper free, steals Ben's Dabblers and buys 10 followers, going from last to first.
Worst moment: Game 2, round III. Ava is hit by two Con Men steals in one round, then on her turn 12 she places on Right Away and it does nothing, and she ends last at 68.

Overall: 2/5. Round III turns into piles of resources and the same biggest follower buy every turn, with members passed between cults too often to track.
