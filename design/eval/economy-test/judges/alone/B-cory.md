# Economy B, judged as Cory

A. Tracking: 2/5
Game 1 ends with Cory on 19 members and Ava on 16. Con Men (steal 5 when recruited) changes cults at least six times: Cal recruits it in round II, Ava's Demote takes it, Cal's Promote takes it back, Ava takes it with Lie Low, then Cory's Promote takes it in round III, Cal's Demote takes it again, and Cory's Double Agent takes it back on his last turn. Nobody at a real table follows that. Game 3 is the other extreme: Ava and Cory finish with 0 members, so there is almost nothing to track.

B. Recruits: 2/5
Some recruits feel like events. In game 1, round II, Cory's first turn uses Tryout on Mentor to get Loyalists free, then buys Mentor, which brings Window Shopper free. More often a member is fuel. In game 3, round II, Ava recruits Con Men, sends it to the discard pile with Estate Sale, recruits Fan Club, then discards that too, and she wins with no members.

C. Choices: 3/5
Cory's seat in game 1 mostly had a real choice between spaces and buys. A few turns were forced: on round I turn 4 only Gather and Stash were open. Ben's seat in game 3 had many turns that were just a space plus "buy 1 follower" (turns 3, 6, 12, 13), where nothing else was worth weighing.

D. Resources: 2/5
Resources pile up. In game 3, Ava holds 15 white in round II and 17 yellow in round III, then buys 10 followers at a time. Largesse (+10 followers) and Party Gifts (+6) hand out big numbers too. Where steals do happen they land on one player: in game 1, round II, Ben loses resources to both Con Men steals and then 3 followers to Ava's Heist, all in one round.

E. Paths: 3/5
Different plans did compete. Game 1 went to the cult with the most members, game 2 to members plus Largesse, and in game 3 Ava's no-member follower buying beat Ben's 9 members, 110 to 99. But game 3 shows that buying followers can carry a game by itself: there were 26 follower buys, and the members barely mattered.

F. The race: 4/5
All three finishes were close: 63-61-57 in game 1, 93-90 in game 2, and 110-99 in game 3. Game 1 had a late lead change: Ben was on 56 to Cory's 39 before Cory's last turn. Cory's Spoilsport cancelled the Magenta contest, and his +10 for most members won it. Game 3 was the weakest of the three, because Ava led from round II (60 to Ben's 41) and stayed ahead.

G. Size: 4/5
Each color's tricks showed up. Game 1 had the Mentor free-recruit chains, Kidnapper and Double Agent taking members, and Extra Hand's extra minion. Game 2 had Sleep In taking the Goes Last token and Hot Streak. Game 3 had Echo copying Estate Sale twice in one turn. The game feels big, but the scores of 90 to 110 followers make every turn a race to the biggest number.

Best moment: Game 1, round II, Cory's turn 5: Tryout on Mentor gives a free Loyalists, then buying Mentor brings a free Window Shopper. It was planned, it paid off, and the table could see it happen.

Worst moment: Game 1, round II: Con Men passes Cal to Ava to Cal, stealing 5 each time, and Ava's Heist then takes 3 followers from Ben. The steals pile onto one player, and nobody could follow whose Con Men it is.

Overall: 2/5. The deciding reason is mental load. Members change cults so often, and so many steals pile onto one player, that a table can't follow it. On top of that, resources pile up into 10-follower buys, so the big turns stop feeling like a payoff.
