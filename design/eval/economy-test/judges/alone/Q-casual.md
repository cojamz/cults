# Q, casual judge

A. Tracking: 2/5
Members change hands constantly, so I'd lose track of who owns what. In game 1 Con Men goes Cory, then Ben (round II, Ben's Promote), then Ava (round III, Ava's Demote), then back to Cory (round III, Cory's turn 9 Poach), and Cory ends with 12 member cards. In game 2 the Goes Last token hops between four players in round III with minions "going back to hand", and in game 3 chains like Echo copying Cover copying Makeover (round III, Cory) are hard to follow from across the table.

B. Recruits: 3/5
Some recruits clearly feel planned. Game 1, round II, Cory's turn 8: Recruit Twice into Power Shoppers, then Scouts clearing the shop. Game 1, round III, turn 9: Poach takes Con Men and its steal goes off again. But by round III most turns skip members and just buy followers, and in game 3 Ben holds 1 or 2 members all game and only recruits twice (Agents, Cashier).

C. Choices: 3/5
Round I is thin. Game 1, Cory's turn 4 had 2 places to go and then passed. Game 3, Ben's turn 4 had 2 places, and his turn 6 "couldn't afford anything". Many buy steps were just "buy 1 follower or pass". From round II on there are 10 to 25 options per placement, and turns like game 2, Ava's turn 8 (Demote, Anarchist, Guest of Honor) were real choices.

D. Resources: 2/5
Early on, steals sting (game 1, round I: Cal's Accomplice takes 2 blue from Cory and leaves him with nothing). By round III resources pile up and get dumped into "pay 10, get 13" buys almost every turn. Cal sits on 20 silver in game 1, and Cory's Makeover turns everything into 22 yellow in game 3, round III.

E. Paths: 4/5
Different plans really compete. Game 1: Ben ties for most followers (93) with 3 members against Cory's 12. Game 3: Cal ties Ava at 111 with a single member against her 10. Game 2: Cal wins with 6 members over Ava's 16.

F. The race: 5/5
All three finishes are within 2 to 8 points: 93/93/92/91, 86/82/81/79 and 111/111/105/103. Two of them came down to the member-count tiebreak. The lead changes hands often: in game 1 Cal leads round I, Ava leads at 82 late in round III, and Cory wins.

G. Size: 4/5
Every color's trick happens: free recruits (Walk-In, Seat Filler, Mentor), steals (Con Men, Heist), copies (Echo, Cover, Fan Club), and skipped and extra turns (game 2: Hold Up, Last Call, Crunch Time). The game feels big. It loses some of that in round III, when most turns turn into buying followers.

Best moment: Game 1, round III, Cory's turn 9: Poach takes Ava's Con Men, its steal grabs 5 blue right back from her, and the second member that turn pays Power Shoppers' +4.
Worst moment: Game 2, end of round III: Cal's Sore Loser steals 5 followers from Ava, which flips her from winning (87) to second (82). It comes from a card nobody had looked at all round. Ben being skipped three times in round III (two from Last Call, one from Hold Up) is a close second.

Overall: 3/5
The finishes are nail-bitingly close, but I couldn't keep track of whose members were whose, and the last round turns into "buy 13 followers" over and over, so I'd come back but not every week.
