In game 1, round III, Cory's turn 10, he recruits three members in one turn (Heist Crew free, Spoilsport, Loyalists) and it feels routine.

C. Choices: 2/5
About half the seat's turns were obvious. Game 2, Ava's turns 1 to 3, 6 and 11: buy 1 follower or pass, nothing else in reach. Game 3, Ben's turns 10 to 13: the pile is so big that buying 10 followers for +13 is plainly the move. The real weighing happened at placement, not at the buy.

D. Resources: 2/5
Starved, then flooded. Game 2, round II, Ava's turn 6: she holds 1 yellow. Game 3, round III: Cory's Makeover turns everything into 22 yellow twice in a row, Toast gives +9 silver, and Largesse hands out 12 silver. Steals were spread around, never piled on one player (game 1), which is good.

E. Paths: 4/5
Different plans really competed. Game 1: Cory with 24 members ties Ben with 3 members at 93. Game 3: Cal with 1 member ties Ava with 10 at 111. The 10-for-13 follower buy may be the strongest path late, and that is worth watching.

F. The race: 4/5
All three finishes were tight (93/93/92/91, 86/82/81/79, 111/111/105/103) with lead changes. Game 2: Cal was last on 18 in round II and won. Game 1: Cory was last on 28 at the start of round III and won on the +10 Most members contest. But the comebacks came from the round III contests and bulk buys, so rounds I and II mattered less than they should.

G. Size: 4/5
Each color's tricks showed up: free recruits (game 1, Seat Filler into Snobs, then Mentor bringing Scouts), copies (game 3, Ben's turn 9, Cover copying Largesse for +10), extra turns (game 2, Ava's turn 11, Overtime), steals (game 1, Con Men stealing 5). In game 3 the members barely mattered; the spaces did everything.

Best moment: Game 1, round II, Cory's turn 5: Demote trades his Snobs for Ava's Mentor, and Mentor brings Scouts along free. It was planned, it was punchy, and Ava felt it.
Worst moment: Game 3, round III: Cory's Echo copies Ben's Cover, which copies Makeover, to make 22 yellow and buy 13 followers. That is a copy chain nobody could follow, and it is the biggest number winning again.

Overall: 3/5
The ending is close and the paths compete, but the middle is starved (turns with only "buy 1 follower") and round III turns into who dumps the biggest pile into a 10-for-13 buy: "every turn is biggest turn/number".
