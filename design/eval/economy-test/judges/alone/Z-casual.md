# Z, judged by the casual friend

A. Tracking: 2/5
Members keep changing hands, so I couldn't keep track of who owned what. In game 2, round II, Ava's turn 6, Anarchist swaps Cory's Deprogrammers for her Loyalists. In round III, Ava's turn 8, Demote and Anarchist pass Night Managers and Latecomers back and forth between Ben and Cal. In game 1, round III, Cory's turn 10, one turn fires Initiation, Trendsetters, Scouts, Heist Crew, Loyalists and Power Shoppers. Game 3 was easy to follow because hardly anyone had more than 3 members.

B. Recruits: 3/5
In game 3 a member felt like an event: Ben's Cashier in round I, turn 4, cashed in his white. In game 1 round III, Cory got free recruits and sent shop members to the discard pile every turn (turns 9 to 12, 24 members by the end), and that became routine. By round III most players stopped recruiting members at all and just bought followers.

C. Choices: 2/5
Round III turns are mostly the same thing: take the space that gives the most resources, then buy 13 followers. Examples: game 3, Ben's turns 11 to 13, and in game 2, round III, Cal, Ben and Cory each buying 13. Early round I turns often had nothing to buy. In game 1, round I, Cory's turn 4 had two open spaces and he passed. In game 3, round I, Ben sat on 1 follower while two rivals had 7. I count about a third of the seat's turns as obvious.

D. Resources: 2/5
Resources are tight in round I. By round III they pile up: Cal holds 20 silver in game 1, round III, Cory's turn 11; Ava holds 18 silver in game 3, Ben's turn 11; Cory holds 12 yellow on Ben's turn 10 in game 3. Steals like Con Men's 5 take a bite, but the next big space refills you.

E. Paths: 4/5
Very different plans finished level. In game 3, Cal won 111 followers with 1 member and tied Ava, who had 10 (her extra members won the tie). In game 1, Ben reached 93 with 3 members, the same as Cory with 24. Buying followers is the main road, though, and every plan ends up on it in round III.

F. The race: 4/5
All three finishes were tight: 93/93/92/91, 86/82/81/79 and 111/111/105/103. The lead changed hands. In game 1, Cal led rounds I and II, Ava led late in round III, and Cory won on the tiebreak. In game 2, Ava was 29 behind in round III and finished 4 off first. With scores this close and the big end-of-game bonuses, I'm not sure a casual player would know why they won.

G. Size: 4/5
Each color's tricks showed up. Ava's Overtime gave her an extra turn (game 2, round III, turn 11). Ben's Reprise copied Cover, which copied Sweet Nothings (game 3, round III, turn 10). There were steals, member swaps, and the Largesse 12-silver handout (game 3, round III, turn 9).

Best moment: Game 1, end of round III: four cults land within 2 followers, and Cory's 24 members beat Ben's 3 on the tiebreak.
Worst moment: Game 3, round III, turns 11 to 13: everyone, Ben included, takes a space and buys 13 followers, turn after turn.

Overall: 3/5. The finish is tense, but round III turns into everyone buying 13 followers a turn, so the close race comes down to adding up numbers instead of plays you can point to.
