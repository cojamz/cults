# P24, judged by: casual (friend at game night), seat Ben

**A. Tracking: first, slightly.** In the first game Cory and Ava hold 0 members from round II on, because Estate Sale and Farewell turn their members into white or followers. That leaves only Ben's and Cal's few cards to watch. In the second game, by round III Cory has 6 member cards with live effects (Recluse, Copyist, Cheerleaders, Extra Hand, Debt Collectors, Counterfeiters) and Ben has 7. Debt Collectors pulling silver from everyone on Cory's Build Up, and Counterfeiters turning all of Ava's resources yellow, are hard to see coming.

**B. Recruits: second, clearly.** In the second game, recruits are events Ben sets up. Round II, turn 6: Fan Club copies Reprise, which copies Echo, which runs Sweet Nothings again. Turn 7: Stunt Double fires Toast a second time. Turn 8: Con Men steals 5. In the first game, Agents costs only 5 and Dabblers 5, so they feel like routine buys. Round II, turn 5 is a 10-cost Extra Hand that Ben doesn't use until round III.

**C. Choices: second, slightly.** The first game has more dead turns for Ben. Round II, turn 6: he takes Sample with nothing to copy and buys 1 follower. Turn 7: Allowance, then pass. Rivals hit the same wall: Cory's Reprise "had nothing to copy." The second game has one flop (round II, turn 5: Echo into Housewarming, but no member is cheap enough to take). Its round III turns are real trade-offs: Estate Sale on Con Men, then Makeover into a 10-follower buy, then Dyer vs. Spoilsport.

**D. Resources: second, slightly.** Higher prices in the second game (Agents 7, Con Men 11, Extra Hand 12) make Ben save up. Steals and Debt Collectors make holding resources risky. In the first game, piles build up and get dumped: in round III, Cal Makeovers into 29 green and Ava into 27 yellow, each followed by a 10-follower buy.

**E. Paths: second, slightly.** In both games, several plans compete. In the first, though, the zero-member plan (Ava and Cory sell off every member and buy followers) collects the "fewest members" contest for free and wins. In the second, Cory wins with 9 members and Cal comes 3 behind with 1 member, so both plans hold up.

**F. The race: second, clearly.** The first game ends 110 / 99 / 83 / 81. Ava leads from the middle of round II and is never caught. The second ends 102 / 99 / 93 / 87. Ava leads into round III, Cal pulls ahead at 89 to 99, and Cory passes him on the last count with Cheerleaders and his contest shares. Ben's Spoilsport on the last turn changes who scores.

**G. Size: second, clearly.** The second game shows almost every color's trick: free recruits, copy chains, Con Men's steal copied twice off Ben's card by Sample and Echo, Counterfeiters, Debt Collectors, Guest of Honor handing a member away, and Spoilsport. The first game is mostly gift-for-followers and big follower buys.

Best moment (second game): round II, Ben's turn 6. One Fan Club recruit sets off a three-step copy chain and earns 12 resources.
Worst moment (first game): round II. Ava's Estate Sale, Echo and Fan Club loop sells three members for 23 white, and from there she just buys followers to the win with no members.

**Overall: second, clearly.** The second game asks more memory at the table. But it's close to the last count, and you can see why Cory won. I'd want to play it again next week. The first game is decided by the middle of round II.

What the economy changed at the table: pricier members and spaces that give a bit more made recruits feel like planned events and kept the scores tight. The cheaper version let players sell their members off and buy followers in bulk, so one player ran away with it.
