# P10, judged as Cory

**A. Tracking: second, clearly.** In the first game Con Men changed hands seven times (Cal recruits it in round II, Ava swaps it, Cal swaps it back, Ava again, Cory promotes it in round III turn 9, Cal demotes it, Cory takes it with Double Agent), and every move set off another steal of 5. On top of that Cory ran Line Cutters, Loyalists, Mentor, Window Shopper, Body Snatchers, Scouts, Kidnapper and Deprogrammers at once. In the second game Cory ended with 3 cards (Cheerleaders, Kingmakers, Anarchist) and Con Men moved four times. Ava's 10 cards late in round III were still a lot.

**B. Recruits: second, slightly.** In the first game Cory recruited on almost every turn, and in round II turn 5 Tryout into Mentor brought in three members (Loyalists, Mentor, Window Shopper) in one go, so buying became routine. In the second game Cheerleaders (round II turn 8) and Kingmakers (round III turn 11) felt planned for. But Cory got no member at all in round I, and his first came on his 9th turn.

**C. Choices: first, clearly.** In the second game Cory's round I turns 3 and 4 were Stash for +1 and Gather for +1, then pass, and his round II turns 5 and 6 were also pass or buy 1 follower. That is four turns with nothing to weigh. In the first game nearly every buy offered two to five members he could afford (round II turn 7: Mannequin or Body Snatchers; round III turn 11: eight choices).

**D. Resources: second, slightly.** In the second game spaces paid less, so the 1 stolen red or magenta mattered and the Fewest silver contest was tied at zero for most of the game. In the first game resources piled up: Cory held 25 in round III before Cal's Con Men took 5 red. The thefts did fall heavily on Cory, which felt bad.

**E. Paths: same.** Both games showed the many-members plan against gifts and follower buys. In the first, Ben's 8 members with Largesse came within 2 of Cory's 19. In the second, Cory's 5 members with Party Gifts, Largesse and two follower buys reached 59 against 66.

**F. The race: second, slightly.** The second game started round III with all four players at 28 or 29, the lead passed from Ben to Ava and back, and Ben won by 1 thanks to Snobs at game end. The first game was also close (63 to 61), but Ava led until Cory pulled ahead on contests in round III.

**G. Size: first, clearly.** The first game had the Mentor chain, Extra Hand's extra minion, Double Agent, Kidnapper on Body Snatchers, and Spoilsport cancelling a contest. The second still had Spree, Heist Crew, Snobs +9 and Spoilsport, but Cory's own seat had very few of these moments.

**Best moment:** second game, round III turn 10: Largesse paid +10 followers and pulled Cory back into a four-way race.
**Worst moment:** first game, round III: Con Men's seventh change of hands, with steals of 5 landing on Cory again and again.

**Overall: second, slightly.** It has less to track and fewer piled-up steals, which matter most to me. But it costs dead turns in round I and fewer big turns, so prices sit right at the edge of too expensive.

What the economy changed at the table: spaces paid less and members cost relatively more. Cults stayed smaller and members stopped bouncing between players, but the early turns went quiet and the game had fewer big moments.
