# P21: casual judge (seat: Ben)

**A. Tracking: second, clearly.** In the first game Ben ends with 9 members and Cal with 7. By Round III Ben has Agents, Dabblers, Night Traders, Extra Hand, Song Request, Big Tent and Old Money in front of him, and I'd lose track of who does what. In the second game nobody holds more than 2 or 3 members for most of the game (Ben sits on 2 from Round II's Estate Sale to the end), so I could follow every cult.

**B. Recruits: first, slightly.** In the first game Ben's recruits feel planned: Round II turn 5 he copies Housewarming to get Night Traders free, then saves for Extra Hand and uses it in Round III to chain Song Request into Echo into Sweet Talk. In the second game members mostly get bought to be sold or given away (Agents cashed out with Estate Sale, Fan Club given to Cory with Guest of Honor), so they feel less like events of their own.

**C. Choices: same.** In both games Ben has real options most turns. Both also have dead turns: in the first, turns 6 and 7 (Sample for +1 green, then a pass); in the second, turns 7 and 9 (Splurge and a pass, Estate Sale and a 5-follower buy). In both, the late turns turn into "take Largesse or buy 10 followers".

**D. Resources: first, slightly.** In the first game Ava's Con Men steals from Ben and Cal in Round II, and Ben spends his silver as soon as he gets it. In the second game Ben sits on 12 silver through turns 10 and 11 of Round III and pays it straight into followers. Resources pile up there more than they get fought over.

**E. Paths: first, slightly.** In the first game Ava wins with 0 members by selling off her members and buying followers, while Ben ends with 9 members and wins the member-colors contest. Those are two clearly different plans. In the second game everyone ends up doing the same thing: hold about 2 members, take Largesse, buy followers.

**F. The race: second, clearly.** In the first game Ava leads from Round II on (60 to Ben's 40 going into Round III) and wins 110 to 99. In the second game Ben is last at 14 against Cory's 35 in Round II and comes back to win 102, with all four players between 94 and 102. That finish would get everyone talking.

**G. Size: first, slightly.** The first game has more tricks: a steal, an extra minion, a Song Request copy chain, and Ava's Estate Sale copied with Echo and Fan Club. The second game still has copies (Fan Club copying Largesse, Sample copying Tourist), but more of its turns are plain follower buys.

**Overall: second, slightly.** I could follow the second game, and its four-way finish with a comeback would make me want to play again. The first game had more going on but was harder to track and was decided early. Both games lean hard on Largesse: it gives +10 followers and gets taken or copied four or five times in Round III of each.

What the economy changed at the table: in the second game members were pricier or less worth keeping, so players held only a couple and bought followers instead. That made it easier to follow and closer at the end, but there were fewer card tricks and resources piled up.
