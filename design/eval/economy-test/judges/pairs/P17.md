# P17 (veteran judge): Ben's seat, same deal, two economies

**A. Tracking: same.** In the first game the table ends with about 10 member cards (Ava 5 including Counterfeiters, which rewrote Cory's whole pile in round II), and in the second about 9 (Ben 6, Cal 3, Cory and Ava none). Both are easy to follow until round III, when Makeover turns 20+ resources into one color in a single move.

**B. Recruits: second, clearly.** In the second game Ben's members were planned events: round II turn 5 Cover copied Housewarming for a free Night Traders, then he bought Extra Hand. In round III turn 9 he used Extra Hand and Song Request to copy Echo and do Sweet Talk twice, then built toward Big Tent and Old Money. In the first game members were mostly something to cash out: Cory's Con Men went to Farewell for 9, Ben cast out Agents for 5 white (round II turn 7), Cal sold Big Tent through Estate Sale, and Ben ended with 1 member.

**C. Choices: second, slightly.** First game: Ben passed in round I turn 3, could afford nothing in round II turn 6, and his round III turns were mostly the obvious "pay 10, get 13". Second game: he still had dead turns (round II turns 6 and 7), but in round III he weighed Sore Loser, Spoilsport, Big Tent and followers on most turns.

**D. Resources: same.** Both games feel tight in rounds I and II: Con Men steals 5 in each, Counterfeiters hits in the first, and Ben is at 0 resources in several snapshots. Both then flood in round III through Toast for 9, Largesse for 12 and Makeover for 22 to 29.

**E. Paths: first, slightly.** In the first game Ava's 10-member cult and Cal's 1-member follower buying finished tied at 111, so the two plans really competed. In the second, Ava won with 0 members over Ben's 9-member cult. That is still a contest of plans, but the members plan clearly fell short by 11.

**F. The race: first, clearly.** The first game had the lead change hands several times: Cory led in round II (28 to 31), Cal at the start of round III (46), Cory at 63, then Cal at 88. It ended 111/111/105/103, with Ava winning the tie on members and the last member-colors contest deciding it. In the second, Ava led by about 20 from the start of round III (60 against the 40s) and was never caught, finishing 110 to 99.

**G. Size: same.** Both games had copies chaining (Echo into Cover into Largesse), steals, gifts and big round III swings. The second added a free recruit and an extra minion. The first had Counterfeiters and Guest of Honor handing members around.

**Best moment:** first game, round III. Four cults are within 8 followers going into the last contests, and Ava wins only on the member-count tiebreak.
**Worst moment:** first game, round II turn 6. Ben "couldn't afford anything" while Cal bought 13 followers.

**Overall: first, slightly.** The second makes recruiting feel more like a plan, but the first gives the close, swinging finish and two plans tied at the top. That is what brings a worker-placement group back.

What the economy changed at the table: in the first economy, a big follower buy pays a bonus (10 resources for 13 followers), so everyone dumps resources into followers in round III and the scores stay close. In the second, buys are 1 for 1, so members look relatively better (Ben recruited much more), but the player who banked followers early stayed ahead.
