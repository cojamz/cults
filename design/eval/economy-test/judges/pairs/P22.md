# P22, judged as Cory (Ben's seat, seed 23, same deal both games)

**A. Tracking: first, slightly.** In the first game Cory and Ava finish with no members and Cal holds 3 cards, so only Ben's six (Agents, Dabblers, Night Traders, Extra Hand, Song Request, Big Tent) need reading. In the second, by round III Cory holds six members with ongoing effects (Recluse, Copyist, Cheerleaders, Extra Hand, Debt Collectors, Counterfeiters) and Ben holds seven. Copy chains also pile up there, like Ben's turn 6: Fan Club copies Reprise, which copies Echo, which copies Sweet Nothings. In both games the members bought and sold back the same turn are hard to follow (Ava's Con Men and Fan Club in round II of the first game, Cal's Sore Loser in the second).

**B. Recruits: second, clearly.** In the first game, members are fuel for the selling spaces: Ava buys Con Men and sells it to Estate Sale on the next minion, then does the same with Fan Club, and she wins with 0 members. In the second, recruits are events. Ben's Stunt Double copies Toast for a second +6 silver (round II, turn 7). His Con Men steals 5, and then Cory and Cal both copy it in round III. Cory builds a cult that wins.

**C. Choices: second, slightly.** First game, obvious turns: Ben's turn 6 (Sample with nothing to copy, then buy 1 follower), turn 7 (Allowance, then pass with nothing affordable), turn 13 (Survey, then buy 1). That is 3 to 4 dead turns. In the second, most buy steps had 3 or more affordable members to weigh (turn 8: Con Men, Debutantes or Extra Hand). One turn fizzled: turn 5, where Echo copied Housewarming but found no member to recruit.

**D. Resources: second, slightly.** First game: Ben holds nothing on turns 6 and 7 (scarce), but late game Makeover turns 29 into green and 27 into yellow, and that becomes followers. Second game: resources move between players. Con Men takes Ava's white, Cory pulls 2 silver from each rival with Debt Collectors, and Counterfeiters turns all of Ava's resources yellow. The steals spread across several rivals rather than landing on one player.

**E. Paths: second, slightly.** In the first, the two players with no members finish first and third (Ava 110, Cory 83), and Ben's 9-member cult comes second. In the second, Cory wins with 9 members, Cal is close behind with 1, and Ben with 10 comes last. Both plans are live there.

**F. The race: second, clearly.** In the first, Ava leads from mid round II (37 to 15) and wins 110 to 99 with no lead change. In the second, Cal leads through round III (89 at Ben's turn 12, 99 at the end), but Cory passes him at round end with contests and Cheerleaders, finishing 102 / 99 / 93 / 87.

**G. Size: second, slightly.** Both feel big. In the first, Largesse (give rivals 12 silver from the bank, +10 followers) is taken three times in round III (Cal through Cover, Ava through Echo then Cover, Ben directly), and Makeover makes two 27+ swings. The second shows more colors' tricks: Stunt Double, Con Men copied twice, Debt Collectors, Counterfeiters, Guest of Honor giving members away, and Spoilsport cancelling a contest. Unclear line: Spoilsport's menu says "White (Ava & Cal lead)", but the result says Fewest members was spoiled, which the board lists under Green.

**Best moment:** second game, Ben's turn 7 in round II: he recruits Stunt Double and copies Toast, for a planned +12 silver on one turn.
**Worst moment:** first game, round III opening: Largesse gets copied twice before Ben moves. That is +20 followers to Cal and Ava from one space, and one 12-silver gift is handed out a silver at a time.

**Overall: second, slightly.** Members mattered and the race was close to the last turn, which the four friends would want again. The cost is more cards per cult to track.

What the economy changed at the table: higher member prices and richer spaces made players keep their members and use them, instead of selling them back for followers, and that kept the scores close. It also left more cards in each cult to remember.
