# P19, judged as Cory (same deal, Ben's seat, two economies)

**A. Tracking: second, clearly.** In the first game Ben ends with 9 members and Cal with 7, so by round III you are tracking Night Traders' round-end swaps, Extra Hand, Song Request copying a copy (turn 9: Song Request > Echo > Sweet Talk again), Old Money and Big Tent. In the second game cults sit at 2 to 7 members, and Ben carries 2 for most of the game.

**B. Recruits: second, slightly.** In the first game Ben recruits on almost every turn in round I and II (Agents, Dabblers, a free Night Traders, then Extra Hand on the same turn at 10). In the second, members are a step pricier (Agents 6, Sore Loser 8) and land as moments: the free Guest of Honor from Housewarming in round II, and on turn 12 Fan Club copying Largesse, then given away to Cory for +4.

**C. Choices: second, slightly.** In the second game Ben passes on turns 3, 5 and 8 because nothing fits, and on turn 7 he weighs selling Agents at Estate Sale for 5 followers. In the first game more turns were "buy whatever is affordable", and turn 7 was a pass with resources spread thin. Both games have the same rote 12-click Largesse handouts.

**D. Resources: second, slightly.** In the second game holdings stay at 0 to 4 through round II and Ben has to cast out a member for white. In the first game Ava piles 15 white by round II turn 6, and round III starts with Cal on 10 silver and Ben on 8.

**E. Paths: second, clearly.** In the first game Ava and Cory keep 0 members all game, take the "fewest members" prize every round, buy followers and win 110 to 99. The 9-member plan loses, so the members plan feels like the trap. In the second game the winner holds 2 members, the last-place player holds 7, and all four finish within 8 followers.

**F. The race: second, clearly.** First: Ava leads from round II on (37 to 15 over Ben at turn 8) and is never caught, final 110 / 99 / 83 / 81. Second: Cory leads round II (41), Cal ties him at 77 in round III, and Ben comes from 44 at turn 10 to win 102 / 97 / 95 / 94.

**G. Size: same.** Both games have copies chaining into Largesse (+10 followers each time: 3 times in the first game, 4 in the second), free recruits from Housewarming, gifts and an extra minion. The first game has Song Request and Night Traders. The second has Fan Club, a member given away by Guest of Honor and Godparent, and a turn 13 copy of Cal's Tourist.

**Overall: second, clearly.** The second game is the one these four would play again: a close finish with lead changes, fewer cards to track, and buying members did not lose to buying no members. The Largesse copying (+10 followers, 12 silver dealt out one click at a time) shows up in both games and looks like the same chore in each.

What the economy changed at the table: members cost about one more and resources ran thinner, so players recruited fewer, sharper members, nobody won by simply refusing members, and the scores stayed within a few followers to the end.
