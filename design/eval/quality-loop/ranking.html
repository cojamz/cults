Cults Card Ranking

Every live member and wheel space in the 10-card game, worst first, scored by three judges who never saw your past calls. Twelve questions, 0 to 2 each (clear, trackable, feels good, fun, interacts, fits its color, momentum, agency, catch-up, worth it, elegant; "defendable" is noted but left out of the total because it didn't predict real games). Out of 22; the bar is 13 with no 0 on clear, feels good or fun. Four fake cards were hidden in the list: the three bad ones came 1st, 2nd and 3rd worst, the good one 92nd of 99. The judges agree with each other at 0.81 to 0.90.

How a replacement gets picked

The old card and 3 to 5 new ones go to three fresh judges together, under shuffled labels. A live card is slipped in twice as a check: if its two copies score differently, the whole result is thrown out (all three checks below held). A new card has to clear the bar and beat the old card by 3.
