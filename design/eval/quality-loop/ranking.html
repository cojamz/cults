Cults Card Ranking

Every member and wheel space, worst first, scored by three judges who never saw your past calls. Each card is scored 0 to 2 on ten questions (clear, trackable, feels good, fun, interacts, fits its color, momentum, agency, catch-up, worth it), out of 20. The bar is 13 with no 0 on clear, feels good or fun. Gold is left out because it's off every table. A wrong singular or plural name is just a rename now, not a fail.

Three worked examplesMembers (83 of 135 under)Spaces (17 of 48 under)

How a replacement gets picked

The old card and 3 to 5 new ones go to three fresh judges together, under shuffled labels. A live card is slipped in twice as a check: if its two copies score differently, the whole result is thrown out (all three checks below held). A new card has to clear the bar and beat the old card by 3.
