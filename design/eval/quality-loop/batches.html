Cults First Batches

The first two batches the loop would test, five changes each across seven colors, members and wheel spaces. Each old card and its candidates were scored together by three fresh judges under shuffled labels. A pick must clear the bar, beat the old card by 3, and not lower its color's whole-set score. Scores are medians from the run each slot was judged on (out of 20 before the elegance question, 22 after), so compare within a slot. Nothing here is live: each pick still has to be proven to work as written, then go through 60 bot games a side and a judge who plays it, before it ships.
