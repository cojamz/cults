# Economy test: how each setting plays (Nov 12)

**How it was run.** Hard bots, 4 players, 10-card decks, each space's +1 only for the first player ever to take it. The same 60 deals were played under every setting, so a difference comes from the setting, not the deal. Numbers are per player, per game. Resource and member numbers held steady across the 60 games. Who-wins shares from 60 games can move about 10 points by chance, so read those loosely.

## Today
Members cost their printed price + 1 of their color + 2 any.
- Each player earns about 62 resources a game (5.1 a placement).
- Members bought: 1.8 in round I, 2.4 in round II, 3.0 in round III. About 5 at the end.
- Ends with 70 followers. 23 of them come from the follower cards, 17 of those in round III.
- On 37% of shop looks, no member is affordable.
- The winner had 5 or fewer members in a third of games. The winner is about 12 followers ahead.

## Richer
Members cost 1 more of their color and 1 more any; every space gives 1 more of its color.
- You earn about 10 more resources a game, but members cost 2 more, so you buy about 1 fewer member, fewer in every round.
- The extra money goes into the follower cards: about 10 more followers bought, 6 of them in round III. Everyone ends about 6 followers higher.
- More turns with no member affordable: 43% of shop looks instead of 37%.
- Small cults win much more: 62% of winners had 5 or fewer members (33% today). Games end closer: the winner is about 9 ahead instead of 12.
- **Trade-off**: bigger turns and closer finishes, but members matter less and round III is mostly buying followers.

## In between
Richer with 1 less any: members cost 1 more of their color; every space gives 1 more of its color.
- The same extra income as Richer: about 11 more resources a game.
- Members bought stay the same as today in rounds I and II, slightly fewer in round III (2.8).
- The extra money goes to the follower cards (about 8 more followers bought, 5 in round III) and to unspent piles: 1 to 2 more resources left over at the end of each round.
- Fewer turns with no member affordable: 33% of shop looks.
- Everyone ends about 6 followers higher. Small-cult wins up a little (43%), within chance.
- **Trade-off**: today's member pace with bigger turns, but more of the score comes from follower buys, and more resources sit unspent.

## One less color
Members cost their printed price + 2 any, with no extra of their own color.
- Income is unchanged.
- Members cost about 1 less, so you buy about 1 more member: half a member more in each round (2.3, 2.9, 3.5).
- Turns with no member affordable drop the most: 24% of shop looks instead of 37%.
- Slightly fewer followers bought from the follower cards (about 2 fewer). Everyone ends about 2 followers higher.
- Small-cult wins unchanged (34%).
- **Trade-off**: members come out more often and the shop is open more turns, but a member's own color matters less when you pay, so which resources you hold matters less.

## Three new levers, each added to today
**Unclaimed +1 grows** (a space's +1 nobody has taken is +2 in round II, +3 in round III)
- Almost every round I space is taken in round I, so few +1s are left to grow. About 3 more resources and 3 more followers a player. Small.

**Round III spaces +1** (every space gives 1 more of its color in round III)
- About 6 more resources each, all in round III. 0.4 more members bought in round III, 2 more followers bought. Rounds I and II are untouched.
- The cleanest way to change round III alone.

**First player on a space gets +2 instead of +1**
- About 3 more resources each, almost all in rounds I and II. 0.2 more members bought in each of rounds I and II; round III unchanged.

## What moves what
- Cheaper members mean more members bought; richer spaces mean more follower buying. Richer does both at once (pricier members, richer spaces), which is why members drop and follower buys climb.
- Every setting ends with leftovers around 5 resources and with most follower buys in round III.
