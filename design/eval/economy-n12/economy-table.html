What these numbers can't tell you

  • These are bot counts, not feel. They say how often you can buy something and where the score comes from, not whether a turn was fun or a choice felt hard.
  • The Nov 7 economy test read feel by having judges compare whole games of two settings on the same deal; the same can be done here.
