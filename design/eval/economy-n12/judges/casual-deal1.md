# Casual judge: deal 1 (Cory's seat, four economies)

I judged as a friend who plays a few times a month: can I follow the table, how often do I sit with nothing useful to do, do my turns feel exciting, would I understand why I won or lost, would I play again next week.

## Game K (final: Cal 81, Ben 58, Cory 54, Ava 54)

- **A. Tracking: 1.** By round III every cult held 9 to 10 cards, and members changed hands almost every turn. Round III alone had Body Snatchers, Poach, Promote, Demote, Swindle, Gift Swappers, Purifiers and a Double Agent each moving cards. Cory's Snitch went to Cal, then to Ava, then got discarded. I could not keep track of who owned what.
- **B. Recruits: 2.** Members cost 4 to 9, so they were a routine buy. In round I Ava had 7 members by her second turn, and in round III Cory added Seat Filler, Last-Minute Shopper and Splurgers on three turns in a row as filler.
- **C. Choices: 3.** Cory nearly always had real options. Round II turn 7 was a good one: Out of Style, then Repeat Customers, then a free Con Men from the discard pile. Several late turns were obvious one-follower buys, though (round III turns 9, 10 and 12).
- **D. Resources: 3.** Resources never piled up, but only because they kept getting stolen. Cory lost 3 magenta to Heist Crew, 2 to Ben's Heist and more to Cat Burglars and Extortionist. It felt more like being robbed than like resources being scarce.
- **E. Paths: 4.** Cal won with lots of members plus two 10-follower buys. Ben used Word of Mouth and party gifts. Cory used Largesse for 10 followers. Several plans were in play.
- **F. The race: 3.** With one turn each left it was Ava 49, Ben 45, Cory 38, Cal 37. Then Cal bought 10 followers twice and won Most members and Most resources at round end, finishing 23 ahead. The lead did change hands (Ava, then Ben, then Cal), but the ending was a runaway, and a casual player would not see where Cal's 31-follower final stretch came from.
- **G. Size: 5.** Every color's trick happened, many times over: free recruits, steals, copies of shop effects, swaps.
- **Best moment:** Round II turn 7, when Cory's Repeat Customers pulled Con Men from the discard pile and took 5 magenta from Ava.
- **Worst moment:** Round III, Cal's last turns: Purifiers made Cory and Ava discard, Swap Shoppers brought back Double Agent, Double Agent took Ava's Deprogrammers, and Cal bought 10 followers, all in one turn.
- **Overall: 2.** It is exciting but too much: four friends would spend round III asking "wait, whose card is that now?"

## Game P (final: Ava 59, Ben 45, Cory 42, Cal 42)

- **A. Tracking: 4.** Most cults held 3 to 7 cards, so I could follow them. The one confusing stretch was Ava's round III Promote and Initiation turns, which moved Loyalists and Scouts back and forth.
- **B. Recruits: 3.** When Cory recruited, it felt earned: Deprogrammers in round II, and Cheerleaders in round III after Con Men stole 5. But he paid for only those two members all game, so recruiting was more absent than special.
- **C. Choices: 2.** Cory passed with nothing worth buying on round I turns 1 and 3, round II turns 6 and 7, and round III turns 9 and 10. That is six of twelve turns where the buy step was "pass".
- **D. Resources: 3.** Resources were scarce and worth fighting for, but the fight went one way. In round III, Ben's Con Men and Extortionist took 6 from Cory, and Ben's Hustlers swapped away Cory's 6 silver for nothing.
- **E. Paths: 2.** Ava won by having the most members and spoiling a contest with Spoilsport. Everyone else bought followers one or five at a time, so there was not much variety in plans.
- **F. The race: 2.** Ava led from the end of round II (36 against 26 or less) and was never caught. She won by 14.
- **G. Size: 2.** The game felt small: the winner had 59, and Cory finished with 7 members. The big tricks happened only once or twice each.
- **Best moment:** Round III turn 8, when Cory's Poach took Ben's Con Men and stole 5 straight back from Ben.
- **Worst moment:** Round II turn 6 through round III turn 10, where Cory took resource spaces and passed, or took Heist and passed, while Ava pulled away.
- **Overall: 2.** Too many turns of sitting with a handful of resources and nothing to buy; a casual table would get bored.

## Game R (final: Ava 76, Ben 73, Cory 61, Cal 60)

- **A. Tracking: 3.** Cults of 4 to 9 cards were manageable through round II. Round III got busy: Body Snatchers took Cory's Snobs, Con Men passed between three players, and Gift Swappers and Kidnappers moved cards at the very end. Ben's Mannequin counting shop cards as his members is hard to see at a table.
- **B. Recruits: 3.** Cory paid for only one member (Paupers, round I). The rest came free from Borrow, Late Shift, Poach and Mentor, so those turns felt planned. His bulk buy was the real event: he saved 10 resources for 10 followers on round III turn 8.
- **C. Choices: 4.** Most of Cory's turns weighed something real: Heist against Catch Up in round II, and Poach plus the 10-follower buy against recruiting Desperado or Shoplifter in round III. Round II turns 4 and 6 were plain passes.
- **D. Resources: 4.** Resources built up enough to plan around (Cory held 14 at the end of round II and won Most resources twice), and steals still bit: Ava's Con Men took 4 from Cory, and Cal's Heist Crew took 3.
- **E. Paths: 4.** Ben went for many members (15), Cory for few members and follower buys (61 points with 6 members), Cal for Patron Saints and Toll Collectors, and Ava for a mix. All four finished within 16.
- **F. The race: 5.** With two turns left it was Ben 48, Ava 47, Cory 46, Cal 41. Cory had led at 41 after his 10-follower buy. Ava won by 3, and a table could see why: Late Shift for Panel, Panel swapping for Cheerleaders, and a 10-follower buy.
- **G. Size: 4.** Free recruits, steals, swaps, Largesse, Gift Swappers and Kidnappers all happened, without the round III flood that K had.
- **Best moment:** Round III turn 8: Poach took Cal's Mentor, Mentor brought Brethren along free, and Cory then bought 10 followers to take the lead.
- **Worst moment:** Round III end: Cory had to give Mentor to Cal's Kidnappers after already giving Paupers away to Gift Swappers, and lost two members in one beat.
- **Overall: 4.** Close all the way, several different plans, and a finish everyone could follow. Friends would want a rematch.

## Game T (final: Ava 62, Cal 58, Cory 40, Ben 29)

- **A. Tracking: 3.** Cults stayed at 4 to 6 cards. But Con Men changed owners four times in round II (Ben, Cal, Ava, then Cory in round III, then Cal, then Ben), which is hard to follow.
- **B. Recruits: 4.** Cory bought Line Cutters on round I turn 2 (exactly the "somebody can get one in round I" feel), Snitch through Recruit Twice in round II, Cheerleaders after the Con Men steal in round III, and Anarchist with saved silver. Each felt planned.
- **C. Choices: 3.** There were real weighings: Swindle choices in round II turn 4, and Demote into Con Men in round III. But round II turn 7 was "couldn't afford anything", and several buys were forced one-follower buys or passes.
- **D. Resources: 4.** Resources were scarce: Cory held 0 or 1 resource at four of his turns. Steals mattered both ways: Cory's Help Yourself in round III, Cal's Cat Burglars at the end.
- **E. Paths: 4.** Cal came second with only 4 members by buying followers in bulk and using Largesse. Ava won on members and contests, and Cory led for a while with many members.
- **F. The race: 4.** With one turn left it was Cal 44, Cory 38, Ava 36. Ava's last Double Agent took Cory's Deprogrammers, which swung both member contests (20 followers) to her. The race was close and the lead changed (Cory, then Cal, then Ava). But Ben spent the whole game far behind, the target of steals and swaps, and finished at 29.
- **G. Size: 4.** Free recruits, steals, Largesse, Scouts wiping the shop twice and Buyout all happened. It felt full without being a flood.
- **Best moment:** Round III turn 8, when Cory's Demote traded Snitch for Ava's Con Men and stole 5, then bought Cheerleaders.
- **Worst moment:** The last turn of round III, when one Double Agent swap moved 20 contest followers from Cory to Ava. It was legal and visible, but it felt like losing to one card.
- **Overall: 3.** A good, tense game for three players. Ben's evening was miserable, and the ending hinged on one swap.

## Ranking (best to worst for four friends at the table)

1. **R**: the closest finish (four players within 7 with two turns left, a 3-point win) and you can see why you won or lost.
2. **T**: tight at the top with planned buys, but one player was out of it all game and the winner was decided by a last-turn swap.
3. **K**: lots happening, but by round III nobody could track whose cards were whose, and the winner pulled away at the end.
4. **P**: half of Cory's turns ended with nothing to buy, and the leader was never challenged.

## What the economy did at the table

- **K:** What the economy did at the table: a member was buyable almost every turn, resources never piled up because they were spent or stolen at once, buys felt routine rather than planned, and the race was close until one player ran away in the last round.
- **P:** What the economy did at the table: members were out of reach on most turns (Cory paid for two all game), resources trickled in and went on small follower buys, the few buys felt planned but rare, and the leader was never caught.
- **R:** What the economy did at the table: paid members were a stretch but free recruits filled in, resources built up enough to save for a big 10-follower buy, buys felt planned, and the race stayed close to the last turn.
- **T:** What the economy did at the table: a member was buyable now and then (one in round I, a few later), resources stayed scarce and steals hurt, buys felt planned, and the race was close among three players while the fourth fell far behind.
