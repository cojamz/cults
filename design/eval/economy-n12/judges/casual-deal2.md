# Casual judge, deal 2 (Ava's seat, same deal, four economies)

Judged as a friend who plays a few times a month: can I follow the table, do I sit around with nothing to do, are my turns exciting, would I know why I won or lost, would I come back next week.

## Game K (Cal wins 102, Cory 71, Ben 64, Ava 54)

- **A. Tracking: 5.** Easy all game. Ava held one member at a time (Anarchist for one turn, then Patron Saints, then Bookies), and the biggest cult had four or five cards.
- **B. Recruits: 2.** Ava recruited a member exactly once in 13 turns (Round II, turn 7, Anarchist for 9, swapped straight away for Patron Saints). Every other turn she bought followers or passed, so a member never felt like something she could plan for.
- **C. Choices: 2.** Most of Ava's buy steps offered only "buy 1 follower, buy 5, or pass" (Round I turns 1-3, Round II turns 4-6, Round III turns 10-12). Placement had some choice; the buy step almost never did.
- **D. Resources: 3.** Nothing piled up. She spent every round down to zero, but only because she turned it all straight into followers. The steals (Con Men took 5 from Cal in Round III) and the yellow Offering mattered.
- **E. Paths: 3.** Plans were different: Cal won with 5 members on contests, Cory had 15 member points, Ava had almost no members. But Ava's plan of only buying followers came last by a long way.
- **F. Race: 2.** It was close going into Round III (Ben 44, Cal 39, Cory 54 at Ava's turn 10). Then Cal took three of the four Round III contests for +30 and won by 31, which feels like a blowout at the table.
- **G. Size: 3.** The big tricks happened (Largesse for +10, Con Men, Overtime, Hot Streak), but Ava herself mostly did small things: +1 follower on five of her turns.
- Best moment: Round III, Ava's turn 13. Makeover turned 14 resources red and she bought 10 followers in one go.
- Worst moment: Round II, turn 6. Late Shift gave nothing ("no shop member costs 6 or less") and she bought 1 follower.
- **Overall: 2.** A friend in Ava's seat sat through a game of "buy a few followers" and lost by 48. They would not ask to play this again.

What the economy did at the table: members were almost never in reach for Ava (one buy all game, and that took a full 9 from a Toast). Resources never piled up but went straight into followers. Buys felt like whatever was left over, not a plan. The race was close until one player swept the last round's contests.

## Game P (Ava wins 89, Ben 86, Cal 82, Cory 68)

- **A. Tracking: 2.** Hard by Round II. Ava had 13 member points by Round III, and her cult kept changing: Cal swapped away her Dabblers (Round II), she took Cal's Con Men with Demote, Exchange Students swapped Kingmakers for Picket Line, Body Snatchers swapped Paupers for Dabblers, Ben swapped Dabblers back out again, and Gift Swappers moved four cards round the table at the end. I would lose track of who owns what.
- **B. Recruits: 2.** Ava recruited a member on each of her first six turns (Paupers, Kingmakers, Dabblers, Seat Filler, Body Snatchers, Cheerleaders). It was a routine buy every turn, not an event.
- **C. Choices: 3.** The buy menus had real options (Round I turn 2: Kingmakers, Purifiers or Line Cutters), but with everything cheap, picking among four affordable members felt more like shopping than a hard call.
- **D. Resources: 3.** Resources never piled up because they were spent on members every turn. Con Men stealing 5 (Round II, turn 4) was a real moment. Silver gifts mattered for the contests.
- **E. Paths: 3.** Nearly everyone went for lots of members (Ava 14, Cory 13, Ben 10, Cal 9). Small cults never had a chance to show up.
- **F. Race: 5.** Very close: 89, 86, 82 at the end. Ava's Round III Largesse (+10) and her last 10-follower buy decided it, and you could see it coming.
- **G. Size: 5.** Everything happened: free recruits, swaps, steals, a 12-silver Largesse, Gift Swappers. It felt big.
- Best moment: Round II, turn 4. Ava used Demote to take Cal's Con Men and stole 5 straight away.
- Worst moment: Round III, the start. Ava's Seat Filler, Exchange Students and Body Snatchers fired one after another, swapping three cards before anyone placed. That is a lot to follow.
- **Overall: 3.** Exciting and close, but four friends would spend half the game asking "wait, whose card is that now?"

What the economy did at the table: a member was buyable on almost every turn, often two or three at once. Resources never piled up because they went straight into members. Buys felt automatic rather than planned. The race was close to the end, but the table ended up with 9 to 14 cards each and a lot of swapping.

## Game R (Cory wins 83, Ava 80, Cal 80, Ben 62)

- **A. Tracking: 4.** Ava had 3 members all game and the biggest cults had about 10. Most cards were simple ones (Paupers, Hot Streak, Consolation Prize). Ben's 10 by Round III was the most to keep an eye on.
- **B. Recruits: 4.** Each of Ava's three members took saving up. Round II turn 5: she held 7 purple from Last Call, took Gather for 3 red, then bought Hot Streak for 9. Round II turn 7: Toast, then Consolation Prize for 9. They felt like events she had planned.
- **C. Choices: 3.** Some turns had nothing to buy (Round II turn 4 pass with 3 purple, turn 6 pass with 2 yellow). Round II turn 5 was a real choice (Scouts, Hot Streak, Gift Swappers, or 10 followers). Round III was mostly "buy 5 followers".
- **D. Resources: 4.** Scarce and worth fighting for. Ben's Offering made Cal throw away 6 resources. Ava held purple across turns to reach a buy, and the 10-follower buy was only in reach twice.
- **E. Paths: 4.** Paths really competed: Ava with 3 members nearly won, Cory with 10 won, Cal won the Round II contest for most of one color with 4 members. Hot Streak paid +3 at the right moment.
- **F. Race: 5.** The closest finish. Cory and Cal were tied at 70 before Ava's last turn; Ava took the last minion of the round and won Went last for +10, ending 3 behind. The lead changed hands through Round III.
- **G. Size: 3.** Tricks happened (Largesse +10, Buyout taking Cal's Dabblers, Scouts clearing the shop, Overtime), but fewer free recruits, and Round I contests were all ties because nobody had more than one member.
- Best moment: Round III, Ava's last turn. Makeover, 5 followers, and going last for +10 put her one step from the win.
- Worst moment: Round II, turn 4. Ava gets Last Call's 3 followers and then has nothing she can buy.
- **Overall: 4.** Easy to follow, buys feel earned, and three players finished within 3 followers. I would want to play this one again.

What the economy did at the table: a member was buyable only every few turns and took saving up. Resources were tight and never piled up. Buys felt planned. The race was very close, with a lead change in the last round.

## Game T (Cal wins 80, Ben 79, Ava 72, Cory 69)

- **A. Tracking: 2.** By Round III, Ben had 14 members, Cal 13 and Ava 11. Cards kept moving: Promote took Ben's Dabblers, Ben took them straight back, Anarchist moved Timekeeper, Exchange Students moved it again, and Cory's Purifiers made three players each discard one. It was hard to follow.
- **B. Recruits: 3.** Ava recruited often (Picket Line, Timekeeper free, Purifiers, Alchemists, Courier, Closer, Exchange Students), but some buys did feel planned. Round II turn 5: Sleep In for 5 purple paid for Alchemists. Round II turn 6: Toast paid for Courier.
- **C. Choices: 3.** There were good choices (Round III turn 8: eight affordable members after Largesse), but several turns were plain passes with nothing worth buying (Round II turn 7, Round III turns 9 and 11).
- **D. Resources: 3.** Fairly tight. Con Men took 4 of Ava's resources in Round II and the Offering discards stung. Late in the game, Alchemists let Ava buy anything with any color, so color stopped mattering for her.
- **E. Paths: 3.** Mostly big cults (Ben 14, Cal 13). Cory with 6 members came last, so small cults did not show up as a real plan.
- **F. Race: 5.** Cal won by one follower. He sat at 41 at Ava's turn 10 and came back with +20 from contests at the end. The lead changed several times.
- **G. Size: 5.** Lots happened: free recruits, steals, Largesse, Purifiers, Bookies cashing in silver, Ava's 10-follower buy after Makeover.
- Best moment: Round III, turn 10. Demote took Cory's Bookies and cashed 2 silver for 4 followers.
- Worst moment: Round III, during Cory's turn. Cory's Purifiers made Ava throw away a member she had just got (Exchange Students), along with members from two other players.
- **Overall: 3.** A thrilling finish, but too many cards and too much swapping for a casual table to follow.

What the economy did at the table: a member was in reach about every other turn. Resources stayed fairly scarce and did not pile up. Some buys felt planned and some were routine. The race was very close, but cults grew to 10 to 14 cards.

## Ranking (best to worst, for four friends at the table)

1. **R**: three players finished within 3 followers, and everyone could still follow every cult.
2. **T**: a one-follower finish with lead changes, held back by 11 to 14 cards per cult.
3. **P**: close, but members came every turn and cards swapped constantly, so it was the hardest to follow.
4. **K**: Ava hardly ever got a member, and the game ended in a 31-follower blowout from one round's contests.
