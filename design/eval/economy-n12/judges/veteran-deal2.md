# Veteran judge, deal 2 (Ava's seat, same deal, four economies)

## Game K (final: Cal 102, Cory 71, Ben 64, Ava 54)
- A. Tracking: 4. Ava held one member all game and nobody held more than five cards, so following the table was easy; the hard part was only the Round II/III swaps (Anarchist, Demote, Promote) moving cards around.
- B. Recruits: 2. Ava bought one member in 13 turns (Anarchist, Round II turn 7, 9 any), then swapped it straight away; every other turn the shop said "short 4 to 8", so recruiting never felt like a plan, just out of reach.
- C. Choices: 2. About 10 of 13 turns ended in "buy 1 or 5 followers with whatever I have" or a pass; placement mattered (Party Gifts, Survey, Makeover) but the buy step was obvious almost every time.
- D. Resources: 3. Resources were scarce for members but never piled up, because they drained into 1-for-1 followers every turn; Con Men's 5-steal on Cal (Round III) and Offering discards hurt, which is good tension.
- E. Paths: 2. Ava's pure follower-buying finished last by 48; Cal won with four members and three Round III contests, so one path (hold the contest states) beat the other clearly.
- F. The race: 2. Close at 54-64 going into the last placements of Round III, then Cal took Went last, Fewest silver and Most member colors for +30 and won by 31; the lead changed, but the finish was a sweep.
- G. Size: 3. Steals, free recruits (Late Shift, Borrow), copies and extra turns all happened, but mostly for the rivals; Ava's own game was small.
- Best moment: Round III turn 9, Ava's Promote swaps Patron Saints for Cal's Bookies and cashes 3 silver for 6 followers.
- Worst moment: Round II turn 6, Late Shift finds no shop member costing 6 or less and pays nothing.
- Overall: 2. Members cost too much to plan around, so most buy steps were automatic follower purchases, and the end was decided by one player's contest sweep.

## Game P (final: Ava 89, Ben 86, Cal 82, Cory 68)
- A. Tracking: 2. By Round III Ava had 13 to 14 member points and the table saw Body Snatchers, Exchange Students, Gift Swappers, Anarchist, Seat Filler, Demote and Promote all moving cards between cults; after Round II turn 4 (Demote pulling Con Men to Ava) I would have lost track of who owned what.
- B. Recruits: 3. Ava recruited on 7 of her first 9 turns (Paupers, Kingmakers, Dabblers, Seat Filler, Body Snatchers, Cheerleaders at 4 to 6 each); a member was a routine buy, not an event.
- C. Choices: 4. Most buy steps had three or more affordable members to weigh against 5 followers (Round II turn 6: Ghost, Cheerleaders, Consolation Prize), and placements had real trade-offs.
- D. Resources: 2. Resources rarely mattered: Ava spent every turn down to zero on a cheap member, and steals (Con Men) cost little because the next member was easy to afford again.
- E. Paths: 4. Ava won with many members plus Largesse, Ben was close with members and late 10-follower buys, Cal with contests; three plans finished within 7.
- F. The race: 4. Ava jumped to 52 vs 36 at the start of Round III and led from there, but Ben closed to 3 through 10-follower buys and Patron Saints; close, but no lead change late.
- G. Size: 5. Every color's tricks fired: free recruits, steals, swaps, extra turns, gifts, a 12-silver Largesse.
- Best moment: Round III turn 8, Ava's Largesse gives out 12 silver for +10, and rivals then have to spend it or lose the Fewest silver contest.
- Worst moment: Round III start, Exchange Students and Body Snatchers both swap members before anyone has placed, two more changes to remember.
- Overall: 3. Big and close, but members are so cheap that buying one stops being a decision, and the card-swapping makes the table hard to follow.

## Game R (final: Cory 83, Ava 80, Cal 80, Ben 62)
- A. Tracking: 5. Ava held 3 members, rivals 3 to 6 cards each, and only a few swaps happened; anyone could follow every cult.
- B. Recruits: 4. Ava's three members each took a saved-up turn: Paupers (Round I turn 2, after Party Gifts), Hot Streak (Round II turn 5, after passing with purple on turn 4), Consolation Prize (Round II turn 7, after Toast); each felt planned.
- C. Choices: 3. Round I and II had real "save or spend" calls (Round II turn 4, passing with 7 purple), but in Round III four of five turns were buy 5 followers.
- D. Resources: 4. Resources were tight: the shop said "short 5 to 10" most turns, Offering discards and Ben's Buyout of Cal's Dabblers stung, and nothing piled up.
- E. Paths: 3. Cory won with 10 members and Cheerleaders, Cal with contests and 6 members, Ava with 3 members and follower buys, all within 3; but member counts stayed low, so the paths were narrower than in P or T.
- F. The race: 5. Cory and Cal 70, Ava 57 going into the last turn; Ava's Makeover placed the round's last minion for Went last +10, and Cory's two Cheerleaders triggers decided it by 3. Three players finished within 3.
- G. Size: 2. Members were thin all game; Late Shift found nothing (Round I, Cal), Hot Streak paid only 3, and the game felt like a follower race more than a cult.
- Best moment: last turn of Round III, Ava's Makeover takes the last placement and Went last for +10, which pulls her level with Cal and 3 behind Cory.
- Worst moment: Round III turns 9 to 11, three turns in a row of take purple, buy 5 followers.
- Overall: 4. The tightest and closest of the four, where every buy is a trade-off, but members are scarce enough that the game feels a size smaller than it should.

## Game T (final: Cal 80, Ben 79, Ava 72, Cory 69)
- A. Tracking: 3. Members climbed to 11 to 14 points per cult by Round III, with Body Snatchers, Exchange Students, Anarchist, Mentor and Cory's Purifiers (Round III, everyone discards one) moving cards; manageable, but more than a casual table wants.
- B. Recruits: 4. Ava recruited about every other turn, usually right after a big gain (Alchemists after Sleep In, Round II turn 5; Closer after Largesse, Round III turn 8); members cost 6 to 8 and you had to set them up.
- C. Choices: 4. Most turns had a real weigh: an affordable member against 5 followers, or a resource space against a swap (Round III turn 10, Demote to take Cory's Bookies and cash 2 silver).
- D. Resources: 4. Cal's Con Men took 4 from Ava in Round II, and Offering and Purifiers hit everyone; Ava rarely held more than 6 resources, so each one counted.
- E. Paths: 4. Cal won on contests (Went last and Fewest silver in Round III), Ben on 14 members and Lookouts, Ava on purple members and Timekeeper; three plans finished within 8.
- F. The race: 5. Cal sat last at 41 with two minions left in Round III and won 80 to 79 on two contests; Ben led most of Round III; a lead change in the final placements.
- G. Size: 4. Free recruits, steals, swaps, extra turns, a mass discard and Largesse all happened; Round III felt like the burst.
- Best moment: last placements of Round III, Cal goes from 4th to 1st by holding no silver and placing the last minion.
- Worst moment: Round III, Cory's Purifiers makes Ava discard Exchange Students, a card she had just used, with no say in it.
- Overall: 4. Members feel like a purchase you plan for, resources are worth fighting over, and the race stayed open until the last minion.

## Ranking, best to worst for four friends at a table
1. T: the race stayed open to the last minion while members still felt like planned buys.
2. R: the closest, tensest finish, but too few members to feel like building a cult.
3. P: big and close, but members were so cheap and swapped so often that buys stopped being choices and the table was hard to track.
4. K: members were out of reach for the seat, buy steps were automatic, and a three-contest sweep decided it by 31.
