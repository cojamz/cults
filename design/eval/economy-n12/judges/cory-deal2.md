# Deal 2, judged as Cory (Ava's seat, four economies)

## Game K
- A. Tracking: Easy. Ava held one member all game, and nobody held more than five cards. At the end Cal's five cards (Early Traders, Paupers, Protesters, Hot Streak, Patron Saints) were the most anyone had to read. Score 4.
- B. Recruits: Ava bought one member in the whole game (Anarchist, round II turn 7, for 9) and used it the same turn to trade it for Patron Saints. In round I she was short 3 or 4 on every member, and Cory's first member came from Polish plus a gift. Members were rare, but they felt out of reach more than planned. Score 2.
- C. Choices: most of Ava's turns ended the same way: take the biggest color space, buy 5 followers (round I turns 1 and 2, round II turn 4, round III turn 9). On about 8 of her 13 turns she could afford no member, so the only real question was where to place. Score 2.
- D. Resources: scarce. Ava held 0 to 6 resources at almost every turn and spent them all. Con Men's steal of 5 from Cal and Offering's discards mattered. They were scarce enough that nothing could be saved up. Score 4.
- E. Paths: Cal won by holding a mix of member colors and no silver, and taking the last minion. Ava tried buying followers with almost no members and finished last. There were two paths, but buying followers alone did not compete. Score 3.
- F. The race: close at move 172 (Ben 59, Cal 56, Cory 54). Then Cal won all four round III contests for +40 and finished 102 to 71. That one round-end scoring decided everything. Score 1.
- G. Size: some big moments: Largesse +10, Makeover turning 14 resources red, Con Men stealing 5. But most cults stayed small, and few member abilities went off. Score 3.
- Best moment: Ava's round III turn 9: Promote took Cal's Bookies, she cashed 3 silver for +6, then bought 5 followers.
- Worst moment: the round III scoring: Cal +40 from four contests turned a 3-point race into a 31-point blowout.
- Overall: 2. The decider: a round-end sweep of four contests outweighed every buy, and Ava could not afford a member until round II.

## Game P
- A. Tracking: by round III Ava had 8 cards and Ben 8, and members changed hands almost every round. Demote took Con Men (round II turn 4), then Exchange Students, Body Snatchers, Ben's Demote, Anarchist and Gift Swappers each moved a card again. Too many swaps to follow. Score 2.
- B. Recruits: routine. Ava recruited on her first two turns (Paupers for 5, Kingmakers for 5), and in round II on nearly every turn (Seat Filler, Body Snatchers, Cheerleaders). A member was the normal buy every turn, not an event. Score 2.
- C. Choices: real ones. Most turns had three to five members Ava could afford, plus 5 followers. Round II turn 6 (Cheerleaders or Consolation Prize or followers) and round III turn 9 (Gossip or Last Callers) were real weighs. Score 4.
- D. Resources: spent every turn and never piled up. But cheap members meant steals barely hurt: Con Men's 5 was a nice bonus, not a blow. Score 3.
- E. Paths: everyone went wide on members. Follower buys came only late in round III, and Cal's Went last contest was the one other line. Score 3.
- F. The race: close at the end (89, 86, 82, 68). Ava led from the end of round II, though, and her Largesse +10 kept her there; the lead never changed hands in round III. Score 3.
- G. Size: everything happened: steals, swaps, mass gifts, free recruits, a Gift Swappers rotation, Picket Line income. It felt big. Score 5.
- Best moment: round III start: Seat Filler turned into Exchange Students, which took Cal's Picket Line, then Body Snatchers took Ben's Dabblers. A planned two-step.
- Worst moment: round III, Cal's Gift Swappers: four members change hands at once, and everyone has to reread four cults.
- Overall: 3. The decider: it felt big and the choices were real, but the pile of swaps and the 8-card cults are the mental load I don't want.

## Game R
- A. Tracking: the easiest. Ava had 3 members all game, Cal 3 cards, and Cory and Ben 4 to 6. You could hold the whole table in your head. Score 5.
- B. Recruits: rare and planned. Round I turn 2: Harvest picked red and purple so Ava could pay 8 for Paupers, and Paupers paid +3 in all three rounds. Hot Streak (9) was saved up for. Consolation Prize (9) never paid, because Ava always tied a contest. Score 4.
- C. Choices: mixed. In round III Ava never had a member in reach (short 5 to 9 every turn), so each turn was place, then buy followers. In round II, Last Call or Gather, and Hot Streak or 10 followers, were real weighs. Score 3.
- D. Resources: scarce and fought over. Ava ended most turns at 0 to 1. Offering's discards and Ben's Buyout paying Cal 7 silver both mattered. Score 4.
- E. Paths: Ava's 3-member cult finished tied for 2nd at 80, Cory won with 10 members, and Cal won by contests. Three plans finished within 3 followers of each other. Score 4.
- F. The race: the closest: 83, 80, 80. The lead changed (Cal 24 in round II, Cory 42 at round III start, Cal and Cory tied at 70 late), and Ava's last-minion +10 kept her in it to the final turn. Score 5.
- G. Size: smaller. Few member abilities went off; Hot Streak gave +3 once. The big moments were spaces (Largesse +10, Makeover). Members mostly sat there. Score 3.
- Best moment: round III, Ava's last turn: Makeover turned everything purple, she bought 5 followers, and her last minion won Went last for +10. She finished tied for 2nd.
- Worst moment: round III turn 9 onward: every shop member cost 9 to 12, so Ava placed and bought followers four turns in a row.
- Overall: 3. The decider: the tightest race and the lightest tracking, but in round III members felt out of reach and every turn was "biggest number, buy followers".

## Game T
- A. Tracking: medium. Ava reached 7 cards and Ben and Cal about 7 each in round III, with swaps (Body Snatchers, Anarchist, Exchange Students, Demote) and Purifiers' discard for everyone. Readable, but near the limit. Score 3.
- B. Recruits: planned and often punchy. Round I turn 2: Survey for 4 yellow, then Picket Line for 6, which paid purple all game. Round III turn 8: Largesse paid for Closer. Three members in round I was a lot, though. Score 3.
- C. Choices: good. Ava often had both members and followers in reach. On round III turn 11 she passed with resources in hand to set up Makeover, then bought 10 followers: a planned two-turn buy. Score 4.
- D. Resources: tight. Cal's Con Men stole 4 from Ava in round II, and Ben's Offering made Cory discard 4. Alchemists softened color needs, but Ava still finished most turns with 0 to 3. Score 3.
- E. Paths: Ben won member colors with many members, Cal won by contests, and Ava ran a purple engine plus followers. All three ended within 8 followers. Score 4.
- F. The race: a real comeback. Cal was last at move 163 (41, against Ben's 52), Ava led late (62), and Cal took Went last and Fewest silver for +20 to win 80 to 79. Score 5.
- G. Size: every color's tricks went off: free recruits (Late Shift, Borrow, Mentor), steals, Largesse, double turns, Purifiers. It felt big. Score 5.
- Best moment: round III turns 11 and 12: Ava passes to save resources, then Makeover turns all 12 purple and she buys 10 followers.
- Worst moment: round III, Cory's Purifiers makes three rivals each discard a member on his turn. Each has to choose on someone else's turn.
- Overall: 4. The decider: members could be bought from round I, turns were real choices, and the last-place player came back to win, though tracking was near the limit.

## Ranking, best to worst
1. T: the only game with real choices on most turns, a big feel, and a last-to-first finish.
2. R: the closest race and the lightest tracking, but round III was all follower buys with no member in reach.
3. P: it felt big and the choices were real, but swaps and 8-card cults put too much on everyone's mind.
4. K: a 40-follower contest sweep decided it, and the seat could not afford a member until round II.

## What the economy did at the table
- K: What the economy did at the table: a member was almost never in reach for Ava (one in the whole game). Resources were spent on 5-follower buys every turn and never piled up. Buys felt forced, not planned. The race was close until round III scoring broke it open.
- P: What the economy did at the table: a member was in reach nearly every turn, so recruiting was routine. Resources never piled up because members soaked them all. Buys were planned but small. Cults grew to 8 cards with constant swaps. The finish was close, but the leader never changed.
- R: What the economy did at the table: a member was in reach only a few times (3 for Ava, none in round III). Resources stayed scarce and went into follower buys. The few member buys felt planned. The race was the closest of the four.
- T: What the economy did at the table: a member was in reach on about half of Ava's turns, from round I on. Resources stayed tight but could be saved for a planned buy. Members and follower buys both competed. The race was close with a late comeback.
