# Cory judge, deal 1 (seed 3, Cory's seat, Blue/Silver/Red/Magenta)

Note on the write-ups: in several games a member moved by a swap (Promote, Demote, Body Snatchers, Poach) fired its "On Recruit" line, for example Con Men stealing 5 each time it changed hands (T, Round III: Cory, then Cal, then Ben). The rulebook says a member a rival moves to you isn't your recruit, so this reads unclear; I judged what the logs show.

## Game K

- A. Tracking: 2. By Round III every cult held 8 to 10 cards (Cory: Window Shopper, Body Snatchers, Repeat Customers, Con Men, Alchemists, Seat Filler, Dabblers, Accomplice, Last-Minute Shopper, Splurgers), and Gift Swappers, Purifiers and Double Agent moved cards between cults several times in one round. Nobody at a real table follows that.
- B. Recruits: 2. Members were bought almost every turn by someone; Cory recruited in Round I on his first turn (Accomplice for 4) and Ava recruited twice in one turn more than once. Buys read as routine, not as events.
- C. Choices: 4. Cory had two or more real options on nearly every turn (Round II turn 7: Out of Style, then Repeat Customers into Con Men from the discard pile); only Round I turn 3 was a plain pass.
- D. Resources: 3. Nothing piled up, but because everything was stolen: Cory lost 3 blue and 2 silver to Con Men at the start of Round II, 2 magenta to Ben's Heist, 3 magenta to Ava's Heist Crew. The steals piled onto whoever held anything.
- E. Paths: 3. Cal won on resources plus two 10-follower buys, Ben on silver members (Patron Saints), Cory on one-color members. Several plans existed, but members churned so much that no plan held.
- F. The race: 3. The lead changed (Ben 27 in Round II, Ava 51 mid Round III), but Cal went from 22 to 81 in Round III alone (two 10-follower buys, +20 from contests, +6 Cheerleaders) and won by 23.
- G. Size: 5. Every color's trick fired: free recruits, steals, swaps, Largesse for +10, Poach, Kidnappers.
- Best moment: Round III turn 9, Cory's Largesse: 12 silver handed out for +10, a big public turn.
- Worst moment: Round III, Ava's Gift Swappers rotated four members at once, then Cal's Purifiers made two cults discard; nobody could say who held what.
- Overall: 2. Fun to watch, but too many cards and effects to track, the exact thing the game is trying to fix.

## Game P

- A. Tracking: 4. Most cults held 4 to 6 cards all game; Cory ended with 4 (Window Shopper, Double Agent, Con Men, Cheerleaders). Easy to follow.
- B. Recruits: 4. When a member came it was an event: Cory got Deprogrammers through Test Drive into Seat Filler (Round I turn 2) and paid 9 for Cheerleaders after Poach stole 5 resources (Round III turn 8). But no one could pay for a member in Round I without a big resource turn first.
- C. Choices: 2. Cory passed his buy on 7 of 12 turns because nothing was in reach (Round I turns 1 and 3, Round II turns 6 and 7, Round III turns 9 and 10 with 5 blue and nothing to buy). Many turns were obvious.
- D. Resources: 4. Scarce and fought over: Cal's Help Yourself took 3 of Cory's magenta, Ben's Hustlers swapped away Cory's 6 silver right after Largesse gave them.
- E. Paths: 3. Ava won on member counts plus two Spoilsports that cancelled the Magenta contest twice; follower buys were the only other road. Spoilsport cancelling a contest twice works against "contests reward your color".
- F. The race: 2. Ava led from Round II (36 against 20 to 26 at the start of Round III) and won 59 to 45; no lead change late. Scores of 42 to 59 feel small.
- G. Size: 3. Steals, swaps and Poach still happened, but the big moments were rare and members few.
- Best moment: Round III turn 8, Poach took Ben's Con Men, which stole 5 back from Ben, and that paid for Cheerleaders.
- Worst moment: Round III turn 10, Cory took Recruit Twice for +5 blue and could buy nothing at all.
- Overall: 2. Clean to read, but too many turns where you can do nothing and one player runs away.

## Game R

- A. Tracking: 3. Cory stayed at 4 cards (Paupers, Loyalists, Mentor, Brethren); Ben reached 9 cards with Mannequin counting shop members. Manageable, harder by Round III.
- B. Recruits: 4. Cory's buys felt planned: Out of Style set up Paupers for 6 in Round I turn 2; Round III turn 8 Poach took Mentor, which brought Brethren free. Somebody did buy in Round I.
- C. Choices: 3. Most turns weighed saving against spending (Round II turns 4 to 7 Cory saved to 14 resources and won the resources contest; Round III turn 8 he spent 10 for 10 followers). About four turns were plain passes.
- D. Resources: 3. Holding resources was a plan (resources contest), and steals took real bites (Cal's Heist Crew took 3 magenta, Ben's Help Yourself took 2), but nobody was buried.
- E. Paths: 4. Cory scored 61 with 6 members on follower buys and the resources contest, Ben 73 on member count, Ava 76 on members plus 10-follower buys. Small cults could compete.
- F. The race: 4. Cory led at 41 after Round III turn 8; Ava passed him with a 10-follower buy; final 76, 73, 61, 60.
- G. Size: 4. Largesse, Poach, Mentor chains, Gift Swappers and Kidnappers all fired; it still felt big without burying the table.
- Best moment: Round III turn 8, Poach on Mentor, free Brethren, then 10 followers in one buy.
- Worst moment: Round III end, Kidnappers took Mentor from Cory at the last moment; a sting he could not answer.
- Overall: 4. Buys felt chosen, the race stayed close, and the table could still read every cult.

## Game T

- A. Tracking: 3. Cults held 4 to 6 cards; Cory ended with 5 (Line Cutters, Cheerleaders, Hustlers, Anarchist, Deprogrammers). Con Men passing through four cults in one round was hard to follow.
- B. Recruits: 4. Line Cutters for 5 in Round I turn 2 and Cheerleaders for 8 right after a Con Men steal (Round III turn 8) both felt earned.
- C. Choices: 3. Round II turns 4 to 7 were mostly 1-follower buys with nothing else in reach (turn 7: "couldn't afford anything"); Round III had real choices (Anarchist swap, Initiation into Hustlers to drop silver for the contest).
- D. Resources: 3. Scarce, but steals piled on one player: Cal was robbed by Cory, Ben and Ava in the same round, and Ben fell to 16 followers.
- E. Paths: 4. Ava won 62 on member contests; Cal 58 with only 4 members on resources and follower buys. Two plans competed to the end.
- F. The race: 4. Cal led at 44 late; Ava passed him on the last contests; Cory was within 6 until the end. Ben at 29 was out of it, and winning at 62 feels a little small.
- G. Size: 4. Steals, swaps, Repossessors, Anarchist and Largesse all fired; Spoilsport again cancelled the Silver contest twice.
- Best moment: Round III turn 9, Initiation into Hustlers handed Cal all of Cory's silver to set up the fewest-silver contest.
- Worst moment: Round III, Con Men changing hands three times, each time stealing 5 again.
- Overall: 3. Close and readable, but too many dead turns in Round II and steals piled onto one player.

## Ranking (best to worst for four friends)

1. R: buys felt planned and the race stayed close to the last buy.
2. T: just as close, but Round II had too many turns with nothing to buy.
3. K: every turn did something, but no one could track 10 cards per cult.
4. P: too many turns where you could buy nothing, and one player led from Round II on.

## What the economy did at the table

- K: What the economy did at the table: a member was buyable on almost every turn from Round I on, resources never piled up because they were spent or stolen at once, buys felt routine rather than planned, and the race swung to a late blowout.
- P: What the economy did at the table: a member was buyable only after a big resource turn, so most turns ended with a pass; resources were scarce and fought over; the few buys felt planned; one player led from Round II and won by 14.
- R: What the economy did at the table: a member was buyable every two or three turns, resources built up a little before each buy, buys felt planned, and the race stayed close (76 to 73).
- T: What the economy did at the table: a member was buyable now and then, Round II was dry, resources stayed scarce, buys felt planned, and the race was close (62 to 58).
