Economy settings, judged by feel

Three judges read whole games blind: one judging as you, one seasoned worker-placement player, one casual friend. Each read the same deal played four times, once per setting, without being told which was which, and ranked them for how four friends would feel at the table. Two deals, so six rankings per setting. Spaces give what they print; each space's +1 goes to its first player only.

Scores are 1 to 5, averaged over the six readings. Cost line: Purists ("Round End: if your members have 2 or fewer colors, +3 followers."), printed 2 red + 1 any.
