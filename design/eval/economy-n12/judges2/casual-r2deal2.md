# Casual judge: deal 2, Ava's seat, three price settings

I judge as a friend who plays a few times a month: can I follow the table, do I sit stuck, are my turns exciting, do I know why I won or lost, would I come back next week.

## Game F

- **A. Tracking: 2.** Too many cards moved between cults. Round II, Ava's turn 4: the Promote list ran to about 60 swap choices. Ava's Purifiers went to Ben and came straight back, Cory's Anarchist took her Timekeeper in round III, and she then swapped Paupers for it back with Exchange Students. By round III Ben held 7 cards and Cal held 7 cards plus Body Snatchers swapping at round start. I would have lost track of whose card was whose.
- **B. Recruits: 3.** Ava recruited on most turns: Picket Line, Timekeeper free and Purifiers in round I, Alchemists and Courier in round II, Closer in round III. Some buys were planned (Sleep In for 5 purple, then Alchemists), but a lot of them were just "it's affordable, buy it".
- **C. Choices: 4.** Most turns had two or three real options, like round III turn 8 (Largesse for 10 followers against Last Call), or turn 12 (Makeover to 12 purple, then 10 followers or a member). Only round II turn 7 felt empty: Harvest, then nothing worth buying.
- **D. Resources: 2.** Resources rarely felt tight. Free members kept coming from Late Shift, Borrow, Mentor and Initiation, and Alchemists let any color pay for anything. Ava spent everything every turn, but she never felt starved.
- **E. Paths: 3.** Ben won lots of members (14 at one point), Cal got there through round-end contests, Cory bought followers. Most players still did a bit of everything.
- **F. The race: 5.** This was the best ending of the three. Going into the last turns Cal had 41 followers against Ben's 58, then took Went last and Fewest silver for +10 each and won 80 to 79. All four finished within 11 of each other.
- **G. Size: 5.** Every color's tricks fired: free recruits, the Goes Last token changing hands again and again, Con Men stealing 5, Purifiers making everyone discard, Largesse for 10. It felt big.
- Best moment: round III, Cal's last turns (Toast, then Polish) took Fewest silver, and that won him the game by 1 follower.
- Worst moment: round II turn 4, reading the Promote list of about 60 swaps to make one choice.
- **Overall: 4.** Four friends would want a rematch after a 1-point finish, even though the card swapping got confusing.

## Game J

- **A. Tracking: 5.** Easy to follow. Ava held 0 or 1 members all game, Ben 4 cards, Cal about 5. You could read the whole table at a glance.
- **B. Recruits: 2.** Ava's members almost never came within reach. She had no member until round II turn 7 (Anarchist for 9), which she swapped straight away for Patron Saints, which was then swapped for Bookies. Round II turn 6: Late Shift gave nothing because "no shop member costs 6 or less".
- **C. Choices: 2.** Many turns had an obvious answer: take a space, buy 1 follower. That happened on round II turn 6, round III turns 8, 10, 11 and 12. Round I turn 3 and round II turn 5 were straight passes. The shop showed 8 or 9 any most of the time and Ava was short.
- **D. Resources: 3.** Resources were scarce, but in a flat way. There was nothing to save toward, so everything went into followers right away. Offering and Con Men did sting, which was nice.
- **E. Paths: 3.** Cal won with 5 members through contests, Cory piled up 15 member points, and Ava bought followers one at a time. Different plans did exist, but Ava's was a fallback, not a choice.
- **F. The race: 1.** Round III's contests decided it: Cal took three of the four for +10 each and finished on 102, against 71, 64 and 54. In mid round III the scores had been close (Cory 54, Ben 44, Cal 39, Ava 41), but the end was a blowout, and last place had 1 member.
- **G. Size: 2.** Ava's part of the game felt small: no member effects of her own, Late Shift with nothing to take. Others still had swaps and steals, but from her seat the game was spaces and follower buys.
- Best moment: round III turn 13, Makeover turned 14 resources red and Ava bought 10 followers.
- Worst moment: round II turn 6, Late Shift's free recruit had nothing it could take, and the turn ended on a 1-follower buy.
- **Overall: 2.** I would not want to sit in Ava's seat again: one member all game, and the winner was 48 followers ahead.

## Game W

- **A. Tracking: 4.** Each cult held 1 to 6 cards, so the table was readable. A few swaps still needed watching (Anarchist swapping Latecomers for Paupers, Cory's Demote handing Ava the Ghost), but far fewer than in F.
- **B. Recruits: 4.** Buys felt planned. Round II turns 5 and 6: Makeover turned everything red, then On a Roll for purple, then Cheerleaders for 8. Round I turn 2: Sleep In for purple, then Latecomers. Round III's free Mentor plus Night Managers was a nice burst.
- **C. Choices: 4.** Most turns had a real weigh-up: round III turn 9 (Melting Pot, then Alchemists for 8 or 5 followers), turn 10 (Promote Night Managers into Big Tent). Only round III turn 12 felt forced (Polish, then 1 follower).
- **D. Resources: 4.** Ava often had 0 to 3 resources and had to build up to a buy over two turns. Largesse handing her 7 silver set up a real decision. Offering made people discard. It felt worth fighting for.
- **E. Paths: 4.** Ben won with only 3 members through contests and follower buys. Ava ended with 10 member points and Big Tent. Cal took contests. These really were different plans, and both kinds worked.
- **F. The race: 3.** Ben pulled ahead in round II (he swept three contests for +18) and was never caught: 79 against 64, 63 and 62. Second to fourth were within 2 of each other, so the scramble below the leader was tight.
- **G. Size: 4.** Most tricks happened: free recruits (Late Shift, Borrow, Initiation with Mentor), Purifiers, Scouts clearing the whole shop, Hustlers, and the Goes Last token passed around. Slightly less wild than F.
- Best moment: round II turn 6, after setting up red the turn before, Ava bought Cheerleaders for 8. It felt earned.
- Worst moment: round II, Cory's Demote put the Ghost on Ava: −5 followers and −1 from Deprogrammers, leaving her on 7 while others had over 20.
- **Overall: 3.** It felt good to play turn by turn, but everyone could see Ben had won from round II on.

## Ranking (best to worst for four friends at the table)

1. **F**: the 1-point finish with a comeback from third. A close ending is what makes friends want to play again, and it outweighs the card-swapping confusion.
2. **W**: the clearest and most planned turns, but the winner was obvious a round early.
3. **J**: one seat spent the whole game unable to recruit, and the end was a 31-point blowout.

## What the prices did at the table

- F, what the prices did at the table: members were buyable almost every turn, and with free recruits on top, resources never piled up because they all got spent. Buys were often grabs rather than plans. The race stayed close to the last turn.
- J, what the prices did at the table: members were out of reach for Ava nearly all game, so resources went into followers one at a time. Nothing felt planned, and the contests at the end of round III decided a lopsided race.
- W, what the prices did at the table: members took a turn or two of saving up, so buys felt planned. Resources did not pile up and did not run dry. One player pulled away in round II while the rest stayed close together.
