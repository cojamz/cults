# Veteran judge: deal 2, Ava's seat, three price settings

## Game F (Cal wins 80, Ben 79, Ava 72, Cory 69)

- A. Tracking: 3. Ava has 7 cards by the end and Cal has 7, which is readable, but members keep moving: Promote, Demote, Anarchist, Body Snatchers, Exchange Students and Purifiers swap or discard cards almost every round (Round III alone moves Paupers, Timekeeper, Bookies and Purifiers between cults). Following who holds what gets hard in round III.
- B. Recruits: 3. Ava recruits often (Picket Line in round I turn 2, then Timekeeper free plus Purifiers in turn 3, Alchemists, Courier, Closer), so buying feels steady rather than rare. Some buys were set up, such as Sleep In for 5 purple before Alchemists in round II turn 5, but by round III turn 12 she could afford six of the eight shop members and took 10 followers instead.
- C. Choices: 4. Most turns had a real trade-off, such as round II turn 4, Promote to take Ben's Dabblers versus Last Call. Only round II turn 7, Harvest with nothing to buy, and round III turn 11, Right Away and then pass, were obvious.
- D. Resources: 3. Ava ends nearly every turn holding 1 to 3 resources, so nothing piles up. Con Men stealing 4 from her in round II and Ben's Offering both hurt. Still, the seat's money ran out on most turns rather than feeling tight on every placement.
- E. Paths: 4. Cal won on round III contests (Went last and Fewest silver, +20), Ben on many members plus end-game cards, Ava on a 10-follower buy plus Timekeeper. All three plans finished within 11 followers of each other.
- F. The race: 5. Ben leads most of the game (58 in mid round III), Ava jumps to 62 on her last turn, and Cal comes from 41, last place, to win by 1 at round end. The final spread of 11 followers is about as close as this kind of game gets.
- G. Size: 4. Free recruits (Late Shift, Borrow, Mentor), steals (Con Men, Offering), extra turns (Overtime, Right Away) and Largesse giving 12 silver for 10 followers all happened. Round III felt like the big round it should be.
- Best moment: round III, Cal's last two placements (Toast, then Polish) win Went last and Fewest silver and take him from third to first.
- Worst moment: round III, Cory's Purifiers makes every rival discard a member right before scoring, a hit that lands on everyone at once.
- Overall: 4. The economy was loose enough that everyone built something and tight enough that the last placements decided the game.

## Game J (Cal wins 102, Cory 71, Ben 64, Ava 54)

- A. Tracking: 4. No cult goes past 5 cards. Ava holds a single member all game, so the table is easy to read.
- B. Recruits: 2. Ava never pays for a member except Anarchist in round II turn 7, which she swaps away at once. Her only lasting member is Bookies, taken from Cal by Promote. A member was something she could almost never reach, not something she planned for.
- C. Choices: 2. Most of Ava's turns are "take resources, buy 5 or 1 follower" (round I turns 1 and 2, round III turns 10, 11 and 12). Round II turn 6, Late Shift, gave nothing because no shop member cost 6 or less, a wasted minion.
- D. Resources: 4. Resources were scarce and fought over: Cory's Con Men took 5 from Cal and Offering made rivals discard. The scarcity mostly pushed resources into follower buys instead of members.
- E. Paths: 3. Cal won with 5 members by sweeping three round III contests. Cory went wide with 15 members and came second, 31 behind. Ava's pure follower-buying finished last. The member path never had the money to compete.
- F. The race: 2. Going into round III it was 34/31/29/28, very close, and Cory led at 54 to 59 mid round. Then the round III contests paid Cal +30 at once and the game ended in a 31-follower blowout.
- G. Size: 2. A few colors' tricks still happened (Con Men, Largesse, Overtime), but Ava's game was small and repetitive, with many turns of "+1 follower". The game felt reduced.
- Best moment: round III turn 13, Ava's Makeover turns 14 resources into red for a 10-follower buy, the one big turn she had.
- Worst moment: round II turn 6, Ava's Late Shift does nothing because every shop member is too expensive.
- Overall: 2. One seat could not afford members for the whole game, and the round III contests swamped everything else.

## Game W (Ben wins 79, Cal 64, Cory 63, Ava 62)

- A. Tracking: 4. Ben wins holding 2 or 3 cards and Cal 2 to 4. Ava's 6 cards are the most anyone held. Ben's Scouts clearing six shop cards in round II is a lot to follow but happens once.
- B. Recruits: 3. Ava's buys were planned. In round II turn 5 she used Makeover to turn everything red, then bought Cheerleaders with it the next turn. In round III turn 8, Initiation gave Mentor, which brought Night Managers along free. There were enough turns between buys that each one counted.
- C. Choices: 3. Several turns weighed real options (Makeover color, Melting Pot then Alchemists versus 5 followers). Round II turn 5, Makeover and then pass, and round III turn 12, Polish for one follower, were obvious.
- D. Resources: 4. Members at 7 to 10 meant saving up across turns. Offering and Hustlers took real amounts, and Ava rarely held more than a few resources.
- E. Paths: 4. Ben won with 3 members on round II contests (+21) and follower buys. Ava built 10 members and the most member cards and came last, 17 behind. Both plans were live, but holding few members paid better here.
- F. The race: 2. Ben jumped from 19 to 44 at the end of round II and led by 12 to 25 for all of round III. Second to fourth finished within 2 of each other, so the close fight was only for second.
- G. Size: 3. Mentor chains, Largesse, Overtime and Purifiers all happened. Round III still felt big for Cal and Cory, less so for Ava.
- Best moment: round III turn 8, Ava's Initiation brings in Mentor, which brings Night Managers free, a two-member turn she set up.
- Worst moment: round II, Cory's Demote swaps the Ghost into Ava's cult for −6 followers when she already had the fewest.
- Overall: 3. Buys felt earned, but one player sweeping a round II contest set the winner with a full round to go.

## Ranking, best to worst for four friends at the table

1. F: the closest finish and a last-turn lead change, with every seat able to build its plan.
2. W: buying a member felt like a planned event, but the winner was clear from the end of round II.
3. J: one player could almost never recruit, and round III contests decided the game by 31.

## What the prices did at the table

- F: A member was within reach most turns, so buys came often and sometimes felt routine. Resources were spent each turn rather than piling up, and the race stayed close to the last placement.
- J: Members were out of reach for Ava nearly all game. Resources went into small follower buys, buys rarely felt planned because there was little to plan for, and the race turned into a blowout once the round III contests paid out.
- W: A member took a turn or two of saving, so buys felt planned. Resources never piled up, but contests mattered more than members and the leader pulled away after round II.
