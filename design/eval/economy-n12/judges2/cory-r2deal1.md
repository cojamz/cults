# Cory judge: round 2, deal 1 (F, J, W)

Judged as Cory: one clean idea at a time, little to remember, real choices each turn, punchy members, contests that reward your color, no steals piling on one player.

## Game F

- A. Tracking: 3. Only 6 member swaps all game, and most cults held 4 to 8 members through round II. It gets heavy in round III when Ava and Ben reach 11 members each, with Toll Collectors and Kingmakers firing on other people's turns.
- B. Recruits: 4. Cory paid for only two members (Cheerleaders on turn 4, Window Shopper on turn 7). Each came after saving up and felt like an event. His round I member was a free one from Late Shift.
- C. Choices: 3. In round I and early round II Cory could afford no member (turns 1, 2, 3, 5, 6), so the buy step was "1 follower or pass". From turn 8 on he had 4 or 5 members he could afford and still picked followers, which is a real trade-off.
- D. Resources: 4. Resources stayed thin. Cory held nothing at the start of turns 9 and 10, and steals hurt: Ben's Con Men took 5 from him, and Cal took 4, then 3.
- E. Paths: 4. Cal ended 1 behind with 4 members by buying followers and winning most resources (+10). Ava and Ben went for many members (11 each). Cory sat between them with 8.
- F. The race: 5. It ended 65, 64, 63. Cal led from round II on (34 to Cory's 16 at the end of round II), and Cory passed him with a 10-follower buy on his very last turn.
- G. Size: 4. Free recruits (Late Shift, Borrow, Test Drive into Seat Filler), Heist, Swindle, Toast and Kingmakers gifts all happened.
- Overall: 4. The tightest finish of the three, and Cory's two member buys felt earned.
- Best moment: turn 12, Cory's 10-follower buy wins by one.
- Worst moment: round I, turns 1 to 3: Cory can buy nothing while Ava already has 5 members' worth.

## Game J

- A. Tracking: 2. 14 member swaps, plus Gift Swappers passing a member around all four seats, Kidnappers taking Cory's Mentor, and Ben counting 17 members at the end. Too much to follow across the table.
- B. Recruits: 2. 26 members recruited, 9 of them free. Ava had 7 members' worth in round I. Members flowed in as routine buys, not planned events.
- C. Choices: 3. Cory had 2 to 5 affordable members on most buy steps in rounds II and III and often passed to keep resources for the most-resources contest. That is a real choice, but he passed on 6 of 12 buys.
- D. Resources: 2. Resources piled up for Cory: 14 at the end of round II and 9 at the end of round III, each time winning most resources. Steals still landed on him: Con Men twice, two Heist followers, Heist Crew took 3 magenta.
- E. Paths: 4. Cory held resources and stayed small (6 members, +6 and +10 from most resources). Ava and Ben went for many members. Cal mixed both. All four ended within 16.
- F. The race: 4. Ava 76, Ben 73. Ben led 48 to 47 on turn 11, then Ava's 10-follower buy and free Panel put her back in front. Cory and Cal were 15 behind at the end.
- G. Size: 4. Everything happened: free recruits, Poach, Heist, Kidnappers, Gift Swappers. Arguably too much.
- Overall: 3. The game felt big, but members came too cheap and fast to track, and Cory's best plan was to sit on resources.
- Best moment: turn 8, Cory's Poach takes Cal's Mentor, which brings Brethren in free, then he buys 10 followers.
- Worst moment: round III, Gift Swappers moves four members at once and Kidnappers takes Cory's Mentor. Nobody at the table could follow who ended up with what.

## Game W

- A. Tracking: 3. The fewest steals of the three (42 steal lines). But Cory's count still went 12 members, then 8, in round III as Buyout took his Deprogrammers and Anarchist swaps changed hands.
- B. Recruits: 3. Line Cutters for 5 on turn 2 felt like a round I event, which is what Cory likes. After that, 7 of his 12 buy steps had no affordable member at all.
- C. Choices: 2. On turns 3 to 6 and 9 Cory could afford no member, so he bought 1 follower each time, five times out of habit. In round II he held nothing at the start of turns 5 and 7.
- D. Resources: 3. Resources were scarce and fought over, but scarce to the point of being stuck: Cory spent round II buying single followers. A bit too expensive.
- E. Paths: 3. Cal went the follower-buying way with 4 members (58) and Ava went for members (62). Ben could not get either going and finished on 29.
- F. The race: 2. Cory led 30 to 29 at the start of round III and finished 40, 22 behind. Ava's Heist took 4 of his followers over the game, Buyout took his Deprogrammers, and Snitch cost him one more. Ben was never in it.
- G. Size: 3. Spoilsport cancelled the silver contest in both round II and round III, and there were fewer free recruits and big turns than in the other two games.
- Overall: 2. Too many turns where you can buy nothing, and the steals all landed on the leader, Cory, until he dropped out of the race.
- Best moment: turn 2, Cory buys Line Cutters in round I and leads round II going first.
- Worst moment: round III, turns 9 to 11: Ben's Buyout takes Cory's Deprogrammers, Ava's Heist takes his followers again, and his lead is gone.

## Ranking, best to worst

1. F: the closest finish (one follower between first and second, one more to third), with member buys that came from saving up.
2. J: big and lively, but too many cheap members and swaps to track, and resources piled up for anyone not buying.
3. W: too many turns with no member in reach, and steals piled on the leader until he fell out of the race.

## What the prices did at the table

- F, what the prices did at the table: Members were out of reach for most of round I and early round II, then reachable from round II's middle, so each buy was saved for. Resources stayed thin, and the race stayed close to the last turn.
- J, what the prices did at the table: A member was buyable almost every turn from round I on, so members came in as routine buys and piled up. Resources piled up for the one player who stopped buying. The race was close between the top two only.
- W, what the prices did at the table: A member was buyable on only about one turn in three, so most turns ended with a single follower bought. Buys were planned, but there was a lot of waiting, and the race spread out.
