# Can each member pay for its own price? (Nov 12)

Cory, Nov 11: "if it can't even pay for its own flat cost what's the point", and "that's a basic principle you should be applying to members where applicable".

How this was counted (Claude, on paper, no games run):
- Price is the shop price (printed cost plus the 3 any every member adds).
- A follower and a resource count the same, since 1 of anything buys 1 follower.
- Payback is the member's own printed followers plus the most its effect gives in a good game for its owner, 4 players.
- Members priced 6 or less join in round I and get three Round Ends and two Round Starts. Members priced 7 or more join in round II and get two Round Ends and one Round Start.
- Not counted: what a member adds to contests (a body in the Red contest, a color in Yellow's), and harm to rivals. That makes this a floor.
- Skipped as not applicable: members whose value is a rule or harm to rivals (Line Cutters, Loyalists, Agents, Snitch, Spoilsport, Protesters, Repossessors, Purifiers, Anarchist, Gift Swappers, Ghost, Mannequin, Panel, Smooth Talkers, Alchemists, Splurgers, Scouts, Swap Shoppers, Exchange Students, Body Snatchers), and members that only trade one resource for another (Early Traders, Loan Officers, Magpie).

## Clear failures: best case is under the price

1. **Banker** "On Recruit: +3 gold. Round Start: +3 gold." Price 11. Best: 3 + 3 + 1 printed = 7. Fix: price 7 (pays 7).
2. **Brethren** "Whenever you recruit a member of a color you already have, +1 follower." Price 10. Best: about 3 matching recruits over two rounds + 2 printed = 5. Fix: price 7, or +2 per recruit as Cory floated ("maybe make it like a +2").
3. **Merchant** "Whenever anyone recruits a gold member, +1 gold." Price 9. Best: about 4 gold recruits at the table over two rounds + 1 = 5. Fix: price 7.
4. **Extortionist** "On Recruit and at Round Start: steal 2 resources." Price 9. Best: 2 + 2 + 1 = 5 for you, and the rivals lose 4. Fix: price 7.
5. **Mentor** "On Recruit: recruit the cheapest member in the shop." Price 11. Best: the cheapest shop member is usually 5 or 6, + 1 = 7. Fix: price 8.
6. **Clan** "Round End: +1 follower per member in your biggest group of one color (grey counts)." Price 10. Best: group of 3 at round II's end, 5 at round III's, + 1 = 9. Fix: price 8. (Cory, Nov 11: "Round End might be a bit too strong".)
7. **Bookies** "On Recruit: turn in up to 4 silver for 2 followers each." Price 8. Best: 8 followers for 4 silver is a gain of 4, + 1 = 5. Fix: price 7 still falls short; the 2 per silver is Cory's number.
8. **Snobs** "Game End: +1 follower per blue member you have." Price 8. Best: 4 blue members + 2 printed = 6. Fix: price 7, or +2 per blue member (Cory's number).
9. **Recruiter** "Whenever you recruit a member, +1 red." Price 8. Best: about 4 recruits over two rounds + 1 = 5. Fix: price 7 still falls short. Price 6 would put it in round I, where nobody recruits.
10. **Treasurer** "On Recruit: +1 gold per member you have, this one included." Price 7. Best: 4 to 5 members in round II + 1 = 6. Short by 1; 7 is already round II's lowest price.
11. **Window Shopper** "On Recruit and Round Start: discard 2 shop members. +1 of each one's color." Price 7. Best: 2 + 2 + 1 = 5.
12. **Thrift Hunters** "Whenever you recruit from the discard pile, +2 followers." Price 7. Best: 2 discard-pile recruits + 1 = 5.
13. **Kingmakers** "Round End: give rivals up to 3 of your silver, split as you choose. +1 follower per silver given." Price 6. Each silver given becomes 1 follower, the same as buying a follower with it, so the card itself pays only its 1 printed follower, and rivals get 9 silver.
14. **Timekeeper** "Game End: +1 follower per purple member you have." Price 6. Best: 4 purple members + 1 = 5. (Cory, Nov 11: "Maybe even we make it two".)

## Close calls: best case about equals the price
**Talent Scouts** (8, a free member up to 6, pays 7), **Duty Collector** (8, pays about 6 to 7), **Die-Hards** (7, pays 7 only when you have the most green members, 1 otherwise), **Stunt Double** (7, one copied action, about 5 to 7), **Tycoon** (9, half your leftover gold), **Speculator** (11, double your gold once, needs 10 gold held to pay), **Debutantes** (11, double your silver once, and each rival gets 2), **Bodyguards** (10, pays only when rivals steal from you), **Consolation Prize** (7, pays 11 only if you win no contest twice), **Gossip** (7, needs turns skipped), **Last Callers** (7, the token and 1 printed follower), **Latecomers** (6, one Goes Last token + 2 printed).

## Pay for themselves
Every other member with a counted effect pays back its price or more in a good game, for example Purists (6, pays up to 10), Anchorites (6, up to 16), Paupers (6, up to 10), Closer (7, up to 9), Cosmopolitans (7, about 8), Tourist (9, about 10), Caravan (11, about 13), Consolation Prize when it hits.
