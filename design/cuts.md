# Members Cory cut (the lasting list)

Every member Cory has said to cut, with his words. `src/test/engine/v4/cuts-log.test.js` reads every id below and fails if
any of them is dealt in a new game (default rules, the 10-card decks, the 15-card decks and the Test cards). So a cut
written here can't slip back in.

How to use it:
- When Cory cuts a member, add its line here the same turn (id, name, date, his words), add the id to `CUT` in
  src/engine/v4/content/cull.js, and log it in design/decisions.md.
- A sort page, a triage list or a pitch saying "cut" is not a cut. Only his words are. If he says he dislikes a card
  without cutting it, put it under "Disliked, not cut" and ask him once.
- Older cuts (before Nov 11) are in cull.js with their dates and reasons; the ones below are the Nov 11 member sort on.
