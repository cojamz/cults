# Silver actions, Nov 11 pass (thread "Redo Silver, White and Yellow actions")

Contest: Fewest silver (0 counts), at round end. Verb: gives. Cory's words for Silver: "communal silver", "scales with … resources total. not just yours but the whole board" (Nov 5); Nov 10 principles from the other colors: spaces scale with the game, not printed round numbers; tie to the contest; a follower space around +4 to +5.

## Live
- I Toast: "+3 silver in round I, +6 in round II, +9 in round III. Each rival +1 silver in round I, +2 in round II, +3 in round III."
- I Polish: "Turn 2 of your silver into 4 of any colors."
- I Party Gifts: "Give rivals up to 3 silver from the bank. +2 followers per silver given."
- II Sweet Nothings: "+3 silver. Give rivals up to 3 of your silver. +2 of any colors per silver given."
- II Sweet Talk: "+3 silver. Then turn any of your silver into other colors."
- III Largesse: "Give rivals 12 silver from the bank, split as you choose. +10 followers."

## Whole game, played (Claude)
Round I, 4 players, every space fills. Toast early: +3 silver, rivals +1 each. Party Gifts any time: +6 followers, rivals +3 silver (strongest round I space in the color). Polish: needs 2 silver; on a first turn most players hold 0 silver, so it waits for Toast; +2 resources net in every round.
Round II: Sweet Nothings nets +6 any and parks 3 silver on rivals. Sweet Talk is +3 silver plus a color fix: under the round II bar (Promote, Heist "Steal 4 followers and 4 resources").
Round III: Largesse +10 followers and 12 silver on rivals.
Every gift space puts silver in rivals' piles, which is exactly what wins Fewest silver, and no space pays you for that silver while it sits there. Silver held by rivals is the color's shared board state; nothing reads it.

## Weak spots
1. Sweet Talk: +3 and a color change in round II; a third way to turn silver into colors beside Polish and Sweet Nothings.
2. Toast: grows by printed round numbers.
3. Polish: flat +2 every round. Kept for now (Silver's one plain color changer); no replacement survived the paper test (below).

## Pitches (names and numbers Claude's fill-ins)

### A. Gratitude, Sweet Talk's slot (II): "+1 follower per silver your rivals hold. +1 silver."
Round II turn, 4 players, late in the round: rivals hold silver from Toast (+2 each in round II, live), Party Gifts (3), Sweet Nothings (3), Kingmakers (up to 3): about 4 to 8 between them → +4 to +8 followers. Early in round II, after rivals spent: 0 to 2. Best other round II spaces: Heist 4 followers + 4 resources; Crowd +1 follower per minion on Green spaces +3 green. Gain: on par late, weak early.
Round III: after Largesse (12 silver on rivals) up to +12 or more; Greatest Hits on Silver copies it for the copier's rivals. Big, and it's the burst round.
Choices: none.
Who feels it: every rival holding silver; it is a reason to spend silver before the Silver player gets here, and the Silver player wants that silver to stay (it also wins them Fewest silver). Sting and race.
How often: silver lands on rivals 3 to 6 times a round with Silver on the wheel.
Steps: count rivals' silver, gain that many followers, +1 silver.
Ties: any space or member that gives rivals resources of a color can give silver; in a game with no other silver source, Silver's own spaces cause it.
Intuitive: yes, one count everyone can see. Fun: yes, a race to spend or keep. Interacts: yes, reads rivals' piles. Powerful: up to +8 in II, more in III. Engine: Toast, Party Gifts, Sweet Nothings, Kingmakers feed it. Its color: giving pays back through the shared silver, and it pulls the same way as Fewest silver.
Meets the bar: one verb on a piece other than your own pile (rivals' silver), a visible count, grows with the table (High Tide, "Each rival +1 silver, then gain 1 follower per silver the rival with the most holds", was a yes on Oct 22; this reads all rivals).

### B. Toast, same slot (I), growing with the table: "Each rival +1 silver. Then +1 silver per silver your rivals hold."
Round I turn 1, rivals hold 0 silver: each +1, you +3 (live: +3, rivals +1 each). Late round I after Party Gifts: rivals 4 to 6 after the +1, you +4 to +6. Round III: 3 if rivals spent everything, 10+ after Largesse. Live round III: +9.
Choices: none. Who feels it: rivals get 1 silver each (a gift, and silver held at round end loses them Fewest silver). Steps: each rival +1, count rivals' silver, gain that much.
Intuitive: yes. Fun: medium. Interacts: yes. Powerful: 3 up to 10. Engine: feeds A. Its color: the gift first, then the whole table's silver. Meets the bar: a fix to a kept space, his Nov 10 principle (scale with the game, not the round number).

### Cut before sending
- Round Robin (the Test version's Polish): "+1 silver. Then every player, you too, gives all their silver to the player on their left." The swing depends on seating; who gets hurt is fixed by the table, not chosen.
- "Give all your silver to one rival. +1 follower per silver given.": the same shape as Kingmakers and Sweet Nothings.
- "+1 follower per 2 minions your rivals have left. Each rival +1 silver.": a forward space, but arithmetic with no tie to silver.
- Any "steal silver" back: Magenta's verb.
