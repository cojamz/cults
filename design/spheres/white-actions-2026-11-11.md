# White actions, Nov 11 pass (thread "Redo Silver, White and Yellow actions")

Contest: Fewest members (0 counts), at round end. Verb: cash white into followers; get rid of members for value. Cory's words: "White is all about like fewer [members]… you use stuff and then you get rid of it" (Nov 7); "followers scaled to the recruited member's cost are White's: encourages white to go big members rather than lots" (Nov 10); "white needs a bigger white gain in the begining" (Nov 6).

## Live
- I Allowance: "+3 white in round I, +4 in round II, +5 in round III. If you recruit a member this turn: +1 follower in round I, +2 in round II, +3 in round III."
- I Splurge: "+2 white. If you recruit a member this turn: +1 follower for every 2 resources you pay (every 1 in round II)."
- I Exchange: "+2 white. Then turn up to 1 resource (2 in round II, 3 in round III) into 2 followers each."
- II Farewell: "Discard one of your members. Gain its cost in followers. +1 white."
- II Estate Sale: "Discard one of your members. Gain its cost in white. +1 white."
- III Cash In: "Turn all your white into followers. +1 white."

## Whole game, played (Claude)
Round I, 4 players, 1 resource each, every space fills. Allowance: +3 white; its follower part needs a recruit, and on most round I turns nobody can afford one (Nov 10 rule: no round I effect on recruiting that turn). Splurge: +2 white, followers only with a recruit. Exchange: +2 white and 1 resource into 2 followers: +1 net follower-value; the only round I White space that pays without a recruit.
Round II: Farewell and Estate Sale discard a member for its cost (followers or white): the contest move (fewer members) and the fuel for Cash In.
Round III: Cash In turns the white pile into followers with no buy used.
What the contest pulls against: Red (most of one color), Blue (most members) and Yellow (most member colors) all reward more members; White rewards fewest. White is the small-cult color, the 1.0 bar (winners with 5 or fewer members at least 20%).
Gap: nothing in White reads how big the other cults are, and two of three round I spaces pay for recruiting, which works against Fewest members and rarely happens in round I.

## Weak spots
1. Allowance: printed round numbers twice, and its follower part needs a recruit (rare in round I, and against Fewest members).
2. Exchange: printed round numbers; a smaller Cash In.
3. Splurge: keep (Cory's Nov 10 line endorses cost-scaled followers for White).
