## Classic table (Cory, Oct 31: low to moderate complexity, still shows how colors connect)
Claude's ranking, simplest first, with how much each touches the other colors (his to overturn):
- Yellow: simple (gain colors you choose, change your mix); connects most, it feeds every other color's costs.
- Red: simple (recruit, swap members); connects through the shop and rivals' members.
- Blue: simple to moderate (shop control, recruit twice or 3 times); connects through the shop.
- Silver (not built): simple (gain silver, give rivals resources, turn silver into colors); connects at every seat, since rivals gain from it.
- Purple: moderate (skip turns, extra turns, turns in a row); connects least, it only bends turn order, and skips are the hardest thing for a new table to follow.
- Magenta: moderate (steals); out by Cory.
- White: moderate (cast out members, turn resources into followers); out by Cory.
- Green: most complex (copying rivals' On Recruit); Cory unsure.
Decided (Cory, Oct 31 11:56pm): Blue, Red, Yellow, Silver once Silver ships with members; Purple holds the fourth seat until then.
