# Pitch ledger

Every pitch Claude (or an agent) makes to Cory, and his answer, gets one line here, newest first, appended the same turn he answers.
Format: `- YYYY-MM-DD · pitch · ✅ yes / ❌ no / ↔ changed / … no verdict yet · his reason, quoted when we have it`.
The patterns feed "Cory's taste" in design/strategy.md; reread both before pitching (/brainstorm).
Tags (✅ yes / ❌ no; ↔ and … uncounted): `too-strong` more power than its slot or price (1✅ 2❌); `too-weak` not enough punch for its round (0✅ 2❌); `too-complex` hard to track, show or decipher (0✅ 4❌); `redundant` repeats a choice players already have (0✅ 1❌); `win-more` rewards whoever is already ahead (0✅ 0❌); `rescue-clause` a fallback for a space or card you can't use (0✅ 1❌); `out-of-turn` extra or out-of-turn minion turns (1✅ 4❌); `spends-to-use-space` a board action that charges to use it (1✅ 2❌); `shallow-pitch` variations on what exists, not broad enough (0✅ 3❌); `core-redesign` changes the core loop or a base rule (0✅ 1❌); `agent-forged` designed wholesale by Claude or agents (0✅ 5❌); `unapproved` treated as agreed or left open without his yes (0✅ 1❌); `his-call` he replaced the pitch with his own number or pick (0✅ 3❌); `rule-bender` members that bend a base rule (3✅ 1❌); `direct-harm` steals, swaps or takes from rivals (3✅ 3❌); `scoring-shape` how followers are scored or contested (2✅ 2❌); `pricing` member or follower costs and how they're paid (2✅ 2❌); `presentation` look, names, labels and pickers on screen (3✅ 4❌); `shop-mixing` the shop as a lever (face-up members, rearranging) (1✅ 0❌); `false-alarm` a problem that wasn't real (bot blind spot, Claude's error) (0✅ 2❌).
