# Brainstorm: one name theme per color (members)

Date: 2026-11-24
Asked by Cory (12:08pm Eastern): "You're running a cult. You get a "Landlord" in it. The Landlord.... charges every other member 1 red? somehow? Idk. it doesn't follow. The colors should also be a little thematically linked. Like one could be more social archetypes, another could be corporate, another could be outdoors, etc. but it needs to also feel aligned with what the color does!" Also: "The names help give the cult a funny interesting flavor"; "the idea is youve recruited this person into your cult"; playful, 5-10% dark.
Skill read: bash .claude/hooks/brainstorm-read.sh at 11:55am Eastern
Coordinator's check adopted: every name passes "You recruited a ___, and of course they ___ (the card's effect)" in writing before Cory sees it.

Method: each color's theme comes from its spaces and contest (live-board.mjs, 12:10pm), so the theme and the color's play say the same thing. Most colors already lean one way in their live names; Red and Blue are the ones furthest from a theme.

| Color | What it does (live spaces, contest) | Theme | Why it fits | Example people |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Red | Free recruits (Borrow, Late Shift, Initiation), Promote and Demote swap members up or down, contest most members of one color | The office | hiring, shifts, promotions and demotions are office life; a big department of one kind | Temp (his yes), Intern, Yes-Man, Office Gossip |

Wingman and Warden (his yes) don't sit inside the office theme; asked whether they stay as they are.
Verdict: send (themes only; names follow his answer)
