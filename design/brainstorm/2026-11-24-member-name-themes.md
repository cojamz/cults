# Brainstorm: one name theme per color (members)

Date: 2026-11-24
Asked by Cory (12:08pm Eastern): "You're running a cult. You get a "Landlord" in it. The Landlord.... charges every other member 1 red? somehow? Idk. it doesn't follow. The colors should also be a little thematically linked. Like one could be more social archetypes, another could be corporate, another could be outdoors, etc. but it needs to also feel aligned with what the color does!" Also: "The names help give the cult a funny interesting flavor"; "the idea is youve recruited this person into your cult"; playful, 5-10% dark.
Skill read: bash .claude/hooks/brainstorm-read.sh at 11:55am Eastern
Coordinator's check adopted: every name passes "You recruited a ___, and of course they ___ (the card's effect)" in writing before Cory sees it.

Method: each color's theme comes from its spaces and contest (live-board.mjs, 12:10pm), so the theme and the color's play say the same thing. Most colors already lean one way in their live names; Red and Blue are the ones furthest from a theme.

| Color | What it does (live spaces, contest) | Theme | Why it fits | Example people |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Red | Free recruits (Borrow, Late Shift, Initiation), Promote and Demote swap members up or down, contest most members of one color | The office | hiring, shifts, promotions and demotions are office life; a big department of one kind | Temp (his yes), Intern, Yes-Man, Office Gossip |
| Green | Copies actions (Echo, Reprise, Cover, Greatest Hits), Crowd, contest most minions on green | Show business | the spaces are already music words; performers who copy acts | Understudy, Stunt Double, Impersonator, Superfan |
| Magenta | Steals resources and members (Help Yourself, Heist, Swindle, Poach), contest most resources | The underworld | thieves and their crew | Pickpocket, Getaway Driver, Snitch, Fence |
| Purple | Turn order: Sleep In, Right Away, Crunch Time, Last Call, Overtime, Goes Last token, contest went last | After hours | people who show up late, stay late and get the last word | Night Owl, Bartender, Insomniac, Party Animal |
| Blue | The shop and discard pile: Test Drive, Out of Style, Markdown, Recruit Twice, Spree, contest most members | Shoppers | the spaces are shopping words; bargain hunters fill a cult fast | Bargain Hunter, Shopaholic, Window Shopper, Returns Clerk |
| White | Fewest members, giving or discarding members, Nest Egg, Cash In | Simple living | people who own little and save the rest | Monk (live), Hermit, Minimalist, Miser |
| Silver | Gifts to rivals (Toast, Party Gifts, Largesse), Flaunt, Buyout, contest fewest silver | High society | rich people who give lavishly and buy what they want | Socialite, Philanthropist, Heiress, Debutante |
| Yellow | Every color: Harvest, Survey, New Blood, Dye Job, Melting Pot, contest most member colors | Travelers and the outdoors | people who've been everywhere and bring back a bit of every color | Tourist, Backpacker, Forager, Exchange Student |
| Grey | No color, in every game | Drifters | people who belong to no group and turn up anywhere | Hitchhiker, Drifter, Ghost (live), Odd-Job Man |

Considered and dropped: Red as family (fits Foster Parents and Clan, but not Promote, Demote, Late Shift); Blue as the dating scene (the discard pile as exes is funny, but every Blue space is a shopping word); Purple as clocks and timekeepers (too literal, no people).
Wingman and Warden (his yes) don't sit inside the office theme; asked whether they stay as they are.
Verdict: send (themes only; names follow his answer)
