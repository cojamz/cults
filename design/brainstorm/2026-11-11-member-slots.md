# Brainstorm: new members for every open slot after the Nov 11 member sort

Date: 2026-11-11
Asked by Cory: "then we need o generate new ideas here, given the updated actions and the color competition. hold new ideas until the end and i look at all colors." Then (3:58pm): "there should be some on recruit, there should be at least 2 to 3, if not more, that are like a way to get followers or points in the end. Stuff that's activatable… not just repeating the actions… complementary, feels on theme… other colors may want to buy the member of the color." And (4:01pm): "it like provides kind of a complementary thing that like helps grease the wheels, even if you're another color… like an income stream."
Skill read: bash .claude/hooks/brainstorm-read.sh at 3:58pm Eastern.
Also his Nov 11 direction: members that give followers on a condition ("members are victory conditions"), "jam people up" (Agents), memorable game to game, nothing like the cloak, careful with chains of copies.

Open slots after his read: Red 1 (Mob; Mentor and Deprogrammers not yet confirmed), Purple 1 (a Goes Last token grabber, his ask), Silver 6, Yellow 3, Blue 3 (discard pile wanted), White 1 (his own ideas fill it), Green 2, Magenta reworks (Bodyguards; Kidnapper and Second-Story Man are his own designs), Grey: Consolation Prize (his).
Real table state used: 4 players; round I most hold 0 or 1 members; round III 6 to 12 members; a winner ends near 70 to 110 followers; a contest pays 4/6/10. Green counts measured: most-Green player 1.5 / 2.2 / 2.4 minions by round (design/notes/green-red-members-pitch-2026-11-11.md).

## Idea: Organizers

1. Printed text: "Whenever you swap a member, +2 followers." (Claude's numbers). Cards it touches: Promote "+3 red. Swap any member for one costing up to 3 more, from another cult or the shop."; Demote "+3 red. Swap any member for one costing up to 3 less, from another cult or the shop."; Panel "On Use, once per round: promote or demote another of your members."; Body Snatchers "Round Start: swap one of your members with any rival's member."; Seat Filler "Round Start: you may put this in the shop and recruit a shop member costing up to 3 more, for free."
2. Played turn: round II, 4 players, you hold Organizers and 3 others. You take Promote: +3 red and a 5-cost member becomes an 8-cost, +2 followers from Organizers. The best other round II space, Heist "Steal 4 followers and 4 resources. +1 magenta.", gives about 8 in value; Promote with Organizers gives +3 red, +3 cost of member, +2 followers. Gain about 2 followers per swap over Promote alone.
3. Both sides: no choices on the card.
4. Who feels it: the rival whose member you swap away (Promote and Demote take from cults) loses it while you get paid; rivals have a reason to take Promote and Demote first.
5. Not refused: pitch-ledger has no swap-pays-followers member; it is not Foster Parents ("Whenever a member joins your cult from a rival's, +2 followers.", which needs a rival's member) and not Recruiter (red per recruit). Different piece: the swap itself.
6. Judged: card-judging-v2 on its own: clear trigger, pays every Red verb, and any color that takes Promote or holds Panel buys it (Cory's complementary lens). Round I: no swaps exist yet, so it is a round II member by price; it reads no member count.
7. Public and certain: yes, every swap is public.
8. Color interactions: the shared moment is a member changing places, which Red, Magenta (Swindle) and Green (Impersonator) all cause; the card names none of them.
9. Biggest case: round III with Panel, Promote and Demote all yours and Body Snatchers at round start: 4 swaps = 8 followers over the round, at most 4 in one turn (Promote plus Panel). Over a game about 12 to 16, against a winner's 70 to 110. Under the 10-in-a-turn cap.
Verdict: send

## Idea: Jilted Lovers

1. Printed text: "Whenever a rival takes one of your members, +3 followers." (Claude's numbers). Cards it touches: Poach "Recruit a rival's member you choose, for free. +1 magenta."; Swindle "Steal a rival's member costing up to 7; give them a shop member of your choice in its place. +1 magenta."; Buyout "Recruit a rival's member, paying them its cost in silver. +1 silver."; Promote and Demote (above); Loyalists "Your other members can't be swapped."
2. Played turn: round III, a rival takes Poach on your 10-cost member. Without this you lose 10 cost of member; with it you get 3 followers back. It is a defense, not a turn of your own; the gain is 3 followers per loss and a reason rivals pick someone else.
3. Both sides: the rival chooses whom to take from; with Jilted Lovers on the table, taking from you costs them giving you 3, so they pick another cult unless your member is clearly best. A real choice.
4. Who feels it: every member-taker (Red, Magenta, Silver's Buyout) weighs you as a target. Gift: none.
5. Not refused: Cory asked for Bodyguards-style defense "that doesn't block it outright" and dislikes Loyalists-style blocks only when they narrow play; this softens without blocking. Snitch is the same shape for resource steals (his 9), not members.
6. Judged: simple, memorable ("don't take from her, she has the Jilted Lovers"), any color buys it against a member-taking table.
7. Public and certain: yes.
8. Color interactions: the shared moment is a member leaving your cult for a rival's (Red, Magenta, Silver cause it); names none.
9. Biggest case: round III, Poach, Swindle and Body Snatchers all aimed at you: 9 followers, 3 per event. Under the cap.
Verdict: send

## Idea: Squatters

1. Printed text: "On Use, once per round: take the Goes Last token." Cards it touches: the Goes Last token ("your other minions are placed after everyone else's"); Pocket Watch (Purple Cloak) "Whenever you take the Goes Last token, +3 purple."; Purple's contest, whoever placed the round's last minion; Sleep In, On a Roll, Last Call, Overtime (each takes the token).
2. Played turn: round II, a rival took the token on Last Call. On your turn you use Squatters: the token is yours, your remaining minions wait, and you place the round's last minion: +6 from the Purple contest. The best Purple space for the token, Last Call, may be taken already; this needs no space.
3. Both sides: you choose when; using it early lets rivals take it back on a later Purple space, using it late risks no turn left. Real timing choice.
4. Who feels it: the token holder loses the Purple contest; Purple spaces taken later take it back, so it is a fight, not a lock.
5. Not refused: Go-Getter of Nov 7 had this text (no verdict logged); Cory asked today for "one other member that does something with the goes last token… a way to grab it or take it". Different from Sleepyheads (cut: pays for taking it) and Latecomers (On Recruit).
6. Judged: one verb on the one token; any color that wants to place last (the Purple contest is in every game with Purple) buys it.
7. Public and certain: yes.
8. Color interactions: none named; turn order is a shared piece.
9. Biggest case: round III, Purple contest +10 if nobody takes the token back, plus 3 purple with the Purple Cloak. The +10 is the contest itself, not the card.
Verdict: send

## Idea: Insomniacs

1. Printed text: "Round Start: take the Goes Last token." Cards it touches: as Squatters.
2. Played turn: round II start, you hold the token before anyone places; every rival can take it back by placing on Sleep In, On a Roll, Last Call or Overtime. Gain: the token without a space, but everyone knows you hold it.
3. Both sides: no choice; it always fires.
4. Who feels it: rivals who want the Purple contest must spend a placement on a Purple space to take it back.
5. Not refused: no Round Start token card on the ledger.
6. Judged: simpler than Squatters but forces every round to start the same way; weaker as a decision.
7. Public and certain: yes.
8. Color interactions: none.
9. Biggest case: +10 contest in round III if never taken back.
Verdict: cut (Squatters does the same with a timing choice; Insomniacs makes every round open the same way)

## Idea: Paupers

1. Printed text: "Round End: if you hold no silver, +3 followers." (Claude's numbers). Cards it touches: Silver's contest, fewest silver (0 counts); Toast "+4 silver. Each rival +2 silver. +1 silver."; Party Gifts "Give rivals up to 3 silver from the bank. +2 followers per silver given."; Largesse "Give rivals 12 silver from the bank, split as you choose. +10 followers."; Smooth Talkers "Your silver pays for any color."
2. Played turn: round II, you hold 2 silver from a rival's Toast. You recruit spending both silver; at round end, +3 followers. Without the card spending silver is neutral.
3. Both sides: no choices on the card.
4. Who feels it: the Silver player can give you silver (Toast, Party Gifts, Largesse) to stop it; a reason to aim gifts.
5. Not refused: not a held-silver contest (he disliked "held color" contests for most silver); this is a condition on a member, the opposite direction, matching the fewest-silver contest. Anchorites' shape ("if you have 3 or fewer members, +5") on a resource.
6. Judged: one visible condition, a follower scorer (his "members are victory conditions"), and any color with no silver plan buys it.
7. Public and certain: yes.
8. Color interactions: none named; silver is a resource every player holds.
9. Biggest case: 3 per round end, 9 a game. Under the cap.
Verdict: send
