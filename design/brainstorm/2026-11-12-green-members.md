## Idea: Mimics

1. Printed text: "On Recruit: use the On Recruit of any member in play." (Claude's words). Cards it touches: Hustlers "On Recruit: swap all your silver with a rival's.", Repeat Customers "On Recruit: +3 blue, then recruit from the discard pile.", Bookies "On Recruit: turn in up to 4 silver for 2 followers each.".
2. Played turn: round II, a rival holds Repeat Customers: recruit Mimics, +3 blue and a discard-pile recruit; the plain buy gives the member alone.
3. Both sides: you pick which On Recruit; several live picks at any table, no dominated one.
4. Who feels it: the rival whose best On Recruit you use; any rival hit by a copied Hustlers swap.
5. Not refused: Cory, Nov 11: "careful about like adding chains of copies"; this copies once, at recruit, and only On Recruits (no On Recruit copies another copy unless Mimics meets Mimics, one deck copy).
6. Judged: Green's verb on members (Cory: "Copies a member… Why are you so against this?"), cross-color through the table's On Recruits. On Recruit, simple.
7. Public and certain: yes, every member is face up.
8. Color interactions: names none; it reads every color's On Recruit members.
9. Biggest case: round III, copy Repeat Customers into a Thrift Hunters and Power Shoppers turn: +3 blue, a recruit, +2 and +3 followers, about 5 followers and a member in one turn.
Verdict: send
