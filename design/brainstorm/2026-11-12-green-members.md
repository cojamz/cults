## Idea: Mimics

1. Printed text: "On Recruit: use the On Recruit of any member in play." (Claude's words). Cards it touches: Hustlers "On Recruit: swap all your silver with a rival's.", Repeat Customers "On Recruit: +3 blue, then recruit from the discard pile.", Bookies "On Recruit: turn in up to 4 silver for 2 followers each.".
2. Played turn: round II, a rival holds Repeat Customers: recruit Mimics, +3 blue and a discard-pile recruit; the plain buy gives the member alone.
3. Both sides: you pick which On Recruit; several live picks at any table, no dominated one.
4. Who feels it: the rival whose best On Recruit you use; any rival hit by a copied Hustlers swap.
5. Not refused: Cory, Nov 11: "careful about like adding chains of copies"; this copies once, at recruit, and only On Recruits (no On Recruit copies another copy unless Mimics meets Mimics, one deck copy).
6. Judged: Green's verb on members (Cory: "Copies a member… Why are you so against this?"), cross-color through the table's On Recruits. On Recruit, simple.
7. Public and certain: yes, every member is face up.
8. Color interactions: names none; it reads every color's On Recruit members.
9. Biggest case: round III, copy Repeat Customers into a Thrift Hunters and Power Shoppers turn: +3 blue, a recruit, +2 and +3 followers, about 5 followers and a member in one turn.
Verdict: send

## Idea: Tag-Alongs

1. Printed text: "On Recruit: move one of your minions to a Green space." (Claude's words). Cards it touches: Regulars "Round End: +1 follower per minion you have on Green spaces.", the most-minions-on-Green contest, Crowd "+1 follower per minion on Green spaces. +3 green.".
2. Played turn: round II, tied 2 to 2 on Green spaces with a rival; recruit Tag-Alongs, move your minion off Toast onto Echo: you lead 3 to 2 for the contest (10 vs 6) and Regulars pays +1 more.
3. Both sides: you choose which minion and which Green space; moving one off a space you already used costs nothing, so it is a free move, with the choice in where.
4. Who feels it: the rival racing you for the Green contest; a minion that lands on a space blocks it for rivals.
5. Not refused: no ledger verdict on this shape or name.
6. Judged: complementary (the Green contest and Regulars), cross-color (any player near the Green lead). On Recruit, simple.
7. Public and certain: yes, minions are on the board.
8. Color interactions: none named; it reads Green spaces only.
9. Biggest case: round III, one minion moved: a contest swing of 4 to 10 followers plus 1 from Regulars, once.
Verdict: send

## Idea: Muses

1. Printed text: "Whenever a rival copies your action, +2 green." (Claude's numbers). Cards it touches: Echo "Copy the previous action. +1 green.", Cover "Copy an action with a rival's minion on it. +1 green.", Greatest Hits (above), Agents "Your actions can't be copied.".
2. Played turn: round II, you take Flaunt and the Green player Covers it: +2 green for you, on top of losing nothing.
3. Both sides: no card choice; the copier chooses whether to copy you and pay you.
4. Who feels it: the Green player, whose copies now feed you.
5. Not refused: Gold Diggers ("Whenever a rival gives you silver, +2 followers.") refused tonight; same shape on copying. Flagged.
6. Judged: cross-color counter, the opposite of Agents.
7. Public and certain: yes, copies are announced.
8. Color interactions: names none; any copy counts.
9. Biggest case: round III, Greatest Hits copying 3 of your actions: +6 green in one turn, 6 resources.
Verdict: cut (Gold Diggers' shape, refused tonight)

## Idea: Originals

1. Printed text: "Your members can't be copied." (Claude's words; Agents' shape, "Your actions can't be copied.").
2. Played turn: round II, a rival recruits Mimics to use your Repeat Customers' On Recruit: they can't.
3. Both sides: no card choice; the copier picks another target instead.
4. Who feels it: the Green player with Mimics or Doppelganger "On Recruit: this member becomes a copy of another of your members." (their own only, unaffected) and any copier of your members.
5. Not refused: Agents' shape, which Cory asked for more of ("jam people up").
6. Judged: cross-color jam in Green's own deck; but Agents already protects actions, so two near-twins.
7. Public and certain: yes, the rule is printed.
8. Color interactions: none named; any player can hold it.
9. Biggest case: blocks one Mimics copy, worth 3 to 6 resources and a recruit; 0 followers of its own.
Verdict: send
