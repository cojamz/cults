## Idea: Exiles

1. Printed text: "On Recruit: discard one of your members. +1 follower per its cost." (Claude's words). Cards it touches: Leavetakers "Whenever one of your members leaves your cult, +2 white.", Martyrs "Whenever this member is discarded, +4 white and +4 followers.", the fewest-members contest.
2. Played turn: round II, you hold a 6-cost member whose job is done: recruit Exiles, discard it, +6 followers and +2 white from Leavetakers; your member count stays the same for the fewest-members contest. A plain buy of 6 followers costs 6 resources.
3. Both sides: you choose which member; a big one pays more but loses its ability, a small one keeps your engine.
4. Who feels it: rivals racing the fewest-members contest (you stay level while recruiting); Blue players, who can recruit the discard.
5. Not refused: Downsizers "On Use, once per round: discard one of your members. +4 white." is the repeatable white version; this is once, on recruit, for followers.
6. Judged: complementary (recruit without growing), the discard theme Cory asked for. On Recruit, simple.
7. Public and certain: yes, members and costs are face up.
8. Color interactions: Blue's discard-pile recruits gain the member you discard; names neither color.
9. Biggest case: round III, discard an 8-cost member: +8 followers and +2 white once, under the 10-in-one-turn line.
Verdict: send
