# Brainstorm: Red, a space that gets better later in the round (replaces Trending, round I)

Date: 2026-11-11
Asked by Cory: "Red later in round, and Yellow White strings we should solve for. ... the other threee (red yellow white) we do need to solve for those gaps"
Skill read: bash .claude/hooks/brainstorm-read.sh at 12:15pm Eastern

Frame. Red's contest: most members of one color (grey counts). Red's verbs: recruit and move members. Live Red: Borrow "Recruit the cheapest shop member, for free. Discard it at round end, before scoring. +1 red." · Trending "+1 of the shop’s most common color per shop member of that color. +1 red." · Gather "+3 red. If you recruit a member this turn of a color you already have: +4 followers. +1 red." · Promote "+3 red. Swap any member for one costing up to 3 more, from another cult or the shop." · Demote "+3 red. Swap any member for one costing up to 3 less, from another cult or the shop." · Initiation "Recruit a shop member costing 10 or less, for free. +1 red."
Slot: Trending is the weakest (2 to 3 resources of whatever color the shop shows most, no member moves, the only Red space that isn't Red's verb). Round I rule: nothing may depend on members you hold, so the idea must recruit from the shop or pay resources. Prices today: round I members cost 5 to 6 in all (cheapest live member 2 of its color + 3 any), round II about 7, round III up to 14 (Mob 8 red + 6 any).
Players: 2 to 6, so any count of minions on the whole wheel changes with player count; a count of your own minions doesn't (Help Yourself's shape, which Cory took: "Steal 1 resource per minion you've placed this round. +1 magenta.").

## Idea: Late Shift

1. Printed text: "Recruit a shop member costing up to 2 per minion you've placed this round, for free. +1 red." (2 per minion is Claude's fill-in; counts the minion just placed, as Help Yourself does.) Cards it touches: Borrow "Recruit the cheapest shop member, for free. Discard it at round end, before scoring. +1 red."; Initiation "Recruit a shop member costing 10 or less, for free. +1 red."; Gather "+3 red. If you recruit a member this turn of a color you already have: +4 followers. +1 red."
2. Played turn: 4 players, round I, your 3rd minion (last), you hold 4 resources and no members. Here: any round I member free (cost up to 6, e.g. Seat Filler 2 red + 3 any), and your own recruit still buys a follower card, or a second member if you can afford one. Best other: Gather gives +3 red (its +4 followers needs a member of a color you already have, rare on a 3rd round I minion), Borrow gives the cheapest member but it's discarded at round end. Gain: about a 5 to 6 resource member kept, versus 3 red: +2 to +3 over Gather, and a kept member over Borrow. On your 1st minion it gives nothing (cost up to 2), on your 2nd nothing in round I (up to 4; cheapest member 5). Round II: 4th minion up to 8. Round III: 4th minion up to 8, 5th up to 10.
3. Both sides: the only choice is when to take it. Early it is worth nothing but the once-a-game +1 red; late it is a free member. Waiting risks a rival taking it first: a rival on their 3rd round I minion takes it for the same free member. Both sides are real: take it now on your 2nd minion in round II (up to 4) to be sure, or wait for your 4th (up to 8) and risk losing it.
4. Who feels it: everyone at the table watches one space get better as the round goes, and whoever places their last minion first gets it. A rival can block it early, paying a minion for almost nothing, which is a real cost: a reason to block that hurts the blocker. The free member also leaves the shop, so a rival loses that card.
5. Not refused: pitch-ledger Red lines checked: Oct 27 round I "pick a shop member costing up to 3/4/5... else you recruit it free" (no verdict), Oct 30 "+1 red per shop member costing 4 or less" (refused: flat in round I), Gather refinements (accepted). verdicts.md: no "recruit capped by a count" refusal. Different shape from Promote/Demote (no swap) and from Borrow (kept, and its cap grows). Cory took the same count on Help Yourself (Nov 10).
6. Judged: The moment: "I held my last minion for Late Shift and got Clan free right before round end." One clean idea in Red's verb (a free recruit), with one count anyone can see (your minions on the wheel). Ties: it makes the timing of your placements matter, which plays with Purple's Goes Last token (more of your minions placed later) and Right Away. Round I real state: 0 or 1 members per player; it depends on nothing you hold.
7. Public and certain: the count is your minions on the wheel, and the shop row is face up. No pile draws, no chance, nothing hidden.
8. Color interactions: none named; it reads only your own minions and the shop, so it works in every game. Purple's turn-bending (Goes Last, Right Away, Crunch Time) changes when you place, so those players can time it, through shared pieces only.
9. Biggest case: round III, your 5th minion: a shop member costing up to 10 for free, e.g. Clan (6 red + 4 any, "Round End: +1 follower per member in your biggest group of one color (grey counts).") taken right before round end, paying about 6 to 10 followers from Clan plus 1 worth and a contest count against a winner's 70 to 110. That equals Initiation's cap (10), only on your last minion of the game. Green's Echo right after copies it for the Green player's own count (their 5th: up to 10; their 3rd: up to 6). A free 10-cost member is a member, not 10 followers; Initiation already pays this any time. In round I the most it pays is one 6-cost member. Flag for Cory: in round III it matches Initiation on a 5th minion.
Verdict: send

## Idea: Roll Call

1. Printed text: "Recruit a shop member costing up to 1 per 2 minions already on the wheel, for free. +1 red." Cards it touches: Borrow, Initiation (text above).
2. Played turn: 4 players, round I, 11 minions already on the wheel: up to 5, most round I members free, versus Gather's +3 red. Gain about +2 and a member.
3. Both sides: when to take it, as Late Shift; early it's worth nothing.
4. Who feels it: everyone, as the whole wheel fills.
5. Not refused: no matching ledger line.
6. Judged: clean, but its value depends on player count: at 2 players round I has 6 minions, so it never reaches a round I member (up to 2); at 6 players it reaches 8 in round I.
7. Public and certain: yes, minions on the wheel are public.
8. Color interactions: none.
9. Biggest case: 6 players, round III, 29 minions already placed: up to 14, any member in the game free (Mob 8 red + 6 any, worth 4, counts as 4). Over Initiation's 10.
Verdict: cut (breaks at 2 players, a round III monster at 6)

## Idea: Red Survey

1. Printed text: "+1 red per 2 minions on the wheel. +1 red."
2. Played turn: round I, 11 minions: +6 red versus Gather's +3. Gain +3 resources.
3. Both sides: when to take it.
4. Who feels it: nobody but the taker.
5. Not refused: same shape as Yellow's Survey ("+1 yellow per minion on the busiest color's spaces. +1 yellow.").
6. Judged: arithmetic, not Red's verb; small ball.
7. Public and certain: yes.
8. Color interactions: none.
9. Biggest case: 6 players, round III: +15 red in one turn.
Verdict: cut (Survey's shape, nobody feels it, too big at 6 players)

## Idea: Rising Promote

1. Printed text: "Swap any member for one costing up to 1 more per minion you've placed this round, from another cult or the shop. +1 red."
2. Played turn: round I needs a member you hold, so it can't sit in round I (Nov 10 rule). Round II, 4th minion: up to 4 more, versus Promote's 3.
3. Both sides: which member to give up.
4. Who feels it: the rival whose member is taken.
5. Not refused: a small tweak to Promote ("Swap any member for one costing up to 3 more...").
6. Judged: a near twin of Promote beside it.
7. Public and certain: yes.
8. Color interactions: none.
9. Biggest case: round III 5th minion: give a 5-cost, take a rival's 10-cost.
Verdict: cut (Promote with a different number; needs held members in round I)

## Idea: Defectors

1. Printed text: "Swap one of your members with a member of a rival who has placed all their minions this round. +1 red."
2. Played turn: round II, 16 minions; players run out around placements 13 to 16, so it works only in the last 3 placements, and round I players hold 0 or 1 members.
3. Both sides: which done rival; they can't answer.
4. Who feels it: the player who finished first.
5. Not refused: Body Snatchers already does an uncapped swap each Round Start.
6. Judged: narrow window, uncapped, depends on held members.
7. Public and certain: yes.
8. Color interactions: Purple's Goes Last protects you from it.
9. Biggest case: take a rival's Mob (14) for a 5-cost.
Verdict: cut (too narrow, too big, round I rule)

## Idea: Table Head Count

1. Printed text: "Recruit a shop member costing up to the number of members in every cult, for free. +1 red."
2. Played turn: round I late, about 3 members on the table: nothing (up to 3).
3. Both sides: when to take it.
4. Who feels it: everyone recruiting feeds it.
5. Not refused: no ledger line.
6. Judged: grows over the game more than the round.
7. Public and certain: yes.
8. Color interactions: Blue's extra recruits feed it.
9. Biggest case: round III, 25+ members on the table: any member free.
Verdict: cut (grows by game, not round; round III monster)
