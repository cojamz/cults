# Brainstorm: Purple, Gossip's open member slot

Date: 2026-11-12
Asked by Cory: "yes, dont we have a shortlist or something for purple?"
Skill read: bash .claude/hooks/brainstorm-read.sh at 6:35pm Eastern (same thread)

Shortlist check: no Purple short list exists. His held asks were "one other member that does something with the goes last token… a way to grab it or take it" (Nov 11, filled by Last Callers) and "more orthogonal victory conditions on the cards that are semi-related to the color".

Frame. Contest: Went last (whoever placed the round's last minion). Spaces: Sleep In "+4 purple in round I, +5 in round II, +6 in round III. Take the Goes Last token: your other minions are placed after everyone else’s."; On a Roll "+2 purple per consecutive turn, this one included. Take the Goes Last token…"; Right Away "+2/+3/+4 purple. Your next turn comes right after this one."; Last Call "+1 purple. +1 follower and +1 purple per minion you have left. Take the Goes Last token…"; Crunch Time "+2 purple. Your next two turns come right after this one."; Overtime "+4 purple. +1 minion this round. Take the Goes Last token…". Members now: Courier, Lookouts, Hot Streak (consecutive turns pay), Latecomers, Last Callers (take the token), Night Managers, Closer (last minion pays), Picket Line (rival takes a Purple space), Timekeeper (Game End per purple member). Missing: a card that holds the token against rivals (the token changes hands often: "somebody who wants it will usually just snatch it back", Nov 10), and a card on the turn order itself. Cory likes members that "jam people up" (Agents "Your actions can't be copied.").

## Idea: Doorkeepers

1. Printed text: "Rivals can't take the Goes Last token from you." Cards it touches: Sleep In, On a Roll, Last Call, Overtime (each "Take the Goes Last token"); Last Callers "On Use, once per round: take the Goes Last token."; Latecomers "On Recruit: take the Goes Last token…"; Agents "Your actions can't be copied." (same shape, Green).
2. Played turn: round II, 4 players. You take Last Call with 2 minions left: +3 purple, +2 followers, the token. A rival then takes Sleep In: they get +5 purple but the token stays with you, so your 2 minions still place last and you win Went last (6). Without it the rival takes the token and likely the 6. Gain: the contest held, about 6 followers in round II, 10 in round III.
3. Both sides: no choices on the card. The rival's side: they still take Sleep In for its purple, just not the token.
4. Who feels it: every rival who wanted the token that round; it is a block that shuts the snatch-back, which Cory said is what happens to the token.
5. Not refused: no ledger line protects the token; Loyalists and Agents are the same "can't" shape in other colors and both stay. Not Ushers (giving the token away, refused Nov 10).
6. Judged: one plain rule, a table reads it at once; jams rivals the way he asked for. Round I: it reads no members; whoever buys it can hold the token from round I's first Purple space.
7. Public and certain: yes, the token and the card are face up.
8. Color interactions: the shared piece is the Goes Last token, which only Purple spaces and members hand out; in a game where the holder's rivals have no Purple card, the card does little but still holds what you take. Names no color.
9. Biggest case: round III, you take Overtime (+4 purple, +1 minion, the token) early; nobody can take it back, so you place 3 or 4 minions last and win Went last (10). One contest a round, never 10 in one turn; a winner ends near 70 to 110. Risk: strong, so it should be pricey (Claude's note).
Verdict: send

## Idea: Place Keepers

1. Printed text: "Round Start: choose your place in this round's turn order." Cards it touches: Line Cutters "You go first every round." (grey); the snake order (I to IV, then IV to I); Went last.
2. Played turn: round II start, 4 players, 4 minions each. The snake runs A B C D, D C B A, A B C D, D C B A: seat A places the round's last minion. You pick seat A and are on track to win Went last (6) unless someone takes the token. Gain: the contest's 6 in round II, or first pick of spaces if you take first in a round you need one space.
3. Both sides: you choose; first seat and last-placing seat are the same seat in an even round, different in an odd one, so the choice is real in rounds I and III (3 and 5 minions: the last minion goes to seat D).
4. Who feels it: everyone's order shifts by one; the rival who would have gone first or last loses it.
5. Not refused: scored on paper Nov 4 (lane C, not sent to Cory); it isn't Line Cutters (always first, no choice). Conflict with Line Cutters needs a plain rule: Line Cutters first, then you choose among the rest (Claude's fill-in).
6. Judged: one trigger, one effect, turn order is Purple's whole subject.
7. Public and certain: yes, the order is announced at round start.
8. Color interactions: turn order is a piece every color uses; names none.
9. Biggest case: round III, 5 minions, picking the place that puts down the last minion: Went last pays 10 unless the token moves, or first pick of Overtime (+4 purple, +1 minion). At most 10 followers a round, from the contest only, against a winner's 70 to 110.
Verdict: send

## Idea: Holdouts (again)

1. Printed text: "Round End: +2 followers per minion you placed after every rival was out of minions."
2. Played turn: round III with the token and 3 minions left: 6 followers.
3. Both sides: none.
4. Who feels it: nobody.
5. Not refused: refused Nov 1 as Holdouts ("I also don't like holdouts").
6. Judged: same shape as a refused card.
7. Public and certain: yes, all on the table.
8. Color interactions: none.
9. Biggest case: Overtime plus the token, 4 minions: 8.
Verdict: cut (refused Nov 1)
