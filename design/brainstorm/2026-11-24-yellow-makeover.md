## Idea: Dye Job

1. Printed text: "+6 yellow. Turn up to 8 of rivals' resources into yellow, split as you choose." (Cory's own numbers: 8 from Oct 30, +6 from Nov 11; whether the +6 includes the space's own +1 is Claude's reading: yes). Cards it touches: Makeover "+4 yellow. Then rearrange your resources into any colors." (leaves); Counterfeiters (Yellow member) "On Use, once per round: turn all of a rival's resources into one color you choose."; Melting Pot "For each color among your members: +1 follower and +1 of that color (grey: 1 of any color)."; Silver's contest, fewest silver.
2. Played turn: 4 players, round II, third placement. The Red player holds 5 red toward a red member (4 red + 3 any); the Magenta player 10 mixed. You turn 5 of the Red player's red and 3 of the Magenta player's blue into yellow; you +6 yellow. The Red player can't pay that red part this turn (yellow pays only the any part, or a follower card). Best other round II space for you, Melting Pot with 3 member colors: +3 followers and 3 resources, about 6. Makeover today: +4 yellow and a rearrange worth about 0 to 1. Gain over Makeover about +2 resources plus the sting; level with Melting Pot for you, plus the sting.
3. Both sides: only you choose (which rivals, which colors, up to 8). No rival choice. You'd never turn a rival's silver (it helps them toward fewest silver), so the real pick is the colors rivals need for a colored part.
4. Who feels it: a sting to the rival saving one color for a member's colored part: their planned recruit slips a turn or goes to a follower card. Totals stay the same, so Magenta's most resources doesn't move. A reason to block it when you're saving one color. At worst a rival loses 8 of one color they needed for two recruits.
5. Not refused: his own card (Oct 30 "I like dye job", Nov 11 "okay", "+6 yellow and turn"). Different from Dilute ("ok but complex": 1 resource of a color a rival held more of) and from Oct 30 12:00pm's rearrange-rivals version he moved to; Dye Job turns into yellow, his Nov 11 framing. The Nov 1 note "way too much change rivals resource… nothing really benefits from that" was about the member deck (Puppeteers, Explorer); one space is not that.
6. Judged: on its own, round II real state (rivals hold 6 to 12 resources, 2 to 4 members): one sentence, done when taken. Intuitive: yes, "turn their stuff yellow". Fun: medium-high, a hate-pick against whoever is saving a color. Interacts: hits any rival, the only round II Yellow space that does. Powerful: 6 for you and up to 8 resources denied. Engine or counter: counters single-color saving (Red's colored parts, Silver's no). Its color: translate currencies, played at rivals. Flag: it doesn't move Yellow's own contest count; and it is Counterfeiters' effect as a space.
7. Public and certain: every pile is face up; you choose what turns; nothing drawn, hidden or random.
8. Color interactions: the shared moment is a rival holding one color for a colored part; names no color; works in any game.
9. Biggest case: round III, Green's Echo ("Copy the previous action.") right after it: the copier also gets +6 yellow and turns 8 of anyone's (yours included). Each taker: 6 yellow, at most 6 followers through a recruit, and 8 rival resources changed, not taken. Against a winner's 70 to 110 and a contest's 10: under the 10-in-one-turn line.
Verdict: send
