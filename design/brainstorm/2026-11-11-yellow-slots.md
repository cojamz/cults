## Idea: Fresh Fields

1. Printed text: "+1 of each color with no minions on its spaces. +1 yellow in round I, +2 in round II, +3 in round III." (name and numbers Claude's fill-ins). Cards it touches: Survey: "+1 yellow per minion on the busiest color's spaces. +1 yellow."; Harvest: "+3 in round I, +4 in round II, +5 in round III, in colors you choose."; Wander (refused Oct 30): "+1 of each other color whose space has your minion".
2. Played turn: round I, 4 players, colors Yellow, White, Red, Blue. First placement of the round: your minion goes on Yellow, White, Red and Blue are empty: +1 white, +1 red, +1 blue, +1 yellow = 4, against Harvest's +3. Seat 4's first turn, after three minions on two colors: +1 or +2 and +1 yellow = 2 or 3. Last third of round I, every color has minions: +1 yellow. Gain over Harvest: +1 early, −2 late, so it wants to be taken first.
3. Both sides: no choices on the card. The choice is when: take it on your first turn of a round or lose most of it.
4. Who feels it: the table, through timing: the first player of each round (fewest followers goes first from round II) gets the most; a rival who wants it must place early, and placing on an empty color takes 1 away from whoever still wants it. A mild race, no sting.
5. Not refused: Wander was refused as weak ("max +2 round 1. and maybe 4 tops in later rounds"); this counts every player's minions and pays more early (4 in round I, 6 in round III). Not Survey's count (the busiest color) but its opposite, so the two pull in different directions. Not a pile formula on your own holdings (verdicts 3): it reads the board.
6. Judged: one visible count (colors with no minions), done when taken, a reason to take a Yellow space early in every round (Cory asked the same of Magenta's open slot: "a space you want to take earlier"). Round I real state: works with 0 members. Intuitive: yes. Fun: medium, a race for the first placement. Interacts: reads where everyone has placed. Powerful: 4 to 6 at its best, small late. Engine or counter: gives one of each color, which feeds recruiting members of different colors (Yellow's contest) and Melting Pot later. Its color: going broad, many colors at once.
7. Public and certain: minions on the wheel are public; no pile draws, randomness or hidden cards.
8. Color interactions: the shared moment is the round's first placements; Purple's going last and Green's Build Up ("+1 green per minion on Green spaces") pull the other way. Names no color; works in any game.
9. Biggest case: round III, first placement of the round, 3 other colors still empty: +3 resources and +3 yellow = 6. Green copying it later in the round copies a smaller count (fewer empty colors). A 5-color table (grey members don't add wheel colors) still caps at 4 + 3 = 7. Against a winner's 70 to 110 and Harvest's +5 in round III; under the 10 cutoff.
Verdict: send
