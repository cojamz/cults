## Idea: Body Snatchers, up to 3 more

1. Printed text: "Round Start: swap one of your members with a rival's member costing up to 3 more." Same price, same worth. Wording follows Promote's "costing up to 3 more" so the two Red swaps read alike. Cards it touches: Promote (text above), Mob, Big Family, Heist Crew, Loyalists (above).
2. Played turn: 4 players, round III start. You hold Seat Filler (5, worth 1) and Cheerleaders (7, worth 3); a rival holds Big Family (8, worth 4) and Mob (14). Today: Seat Filler for Mob, about +4 worth and up to +6 at round end to you, the same off them. Here: Seat Filler reaches up to 8, so Big Family: +3 to you, -3 to them. The swap still beats the other round-start option (none: Body Snatchers is the only round-start swap you hold) by 3 followers plus the 6-follower gap between you and that rival.
3. Both sides: the only choice is yours: which pair, or don't swap. Swapping is never worse when a pair gains you worth, so "don't swap" is for when every reachable member is worse for you; a real choice only in odd boards, as today.
4. Who feels it: a sting to the rival whose member you take (they get your cheaper one back); a reason to hold Loyalists (blocks it). The rival's worst case drops from losing their single best member to losing one up to 3 more than your cheapest.
5. Not refused: pitch-ledger has no Body Snatchers line; the Nov 7 marks said the same-cost version was weak ("would be way better if it swapped for any") — this sits between same cost and any. Usurper (leader-only swap, Nov 1) was refused and this is not leader-targeted.
6. Judged: card-judging-v2 on its own: intuitive (one trigger, one swap, Promote's range), fun (pick your target at the round's start, visible to all), interacts (takes a rival's member), feels powerful (still a 6-follower swing for 7 resources), engine or counter: neither, a one-time swing; its color: Red swaps. Round III table state: players hold 6 to 12 members, so a pair within 3 nearly always exists.
7. Public and certain: everything is in public cults; no piles, no randomness.
8. Color interactions: none: a Red-only card on Red's existing swap verb; it takes members of any color.
9. Biggest case: round III start, you give your 11-cost member and take a 14-cost Mob (worth 4, +2 per rival with fewer red at round end): about 4 + 6 = 10 to you, but you also lose an 11-cost member's worth (2 to 3), so net about 7 to you and 7 off them; the common case is Big Family, 3 to you and 3 off them. A Green copy of a round-start member is not live. Against a winner's 70 to 110 and a contest's 4/6/10, a 6 to 7 swing once a game is in line; today's card reaches 10 to you and 10 off them from a 1-worth starter.
Verdict: send

## Idea: Body Snatchers, pricier

1. Printed text: unchanged, "Round Start: swap one of your members with any rival's member." Price 4 red + 5 any (9) instead of 3 red + 4 any (7), worth 2, kept in round II's deck (pinned with round: 2 so the higher price doesn't move it to round III, where it would never fire). Cards it touches: Mob, Big Family, Heist Crew, Loyalists (above).
2. Played turn: round II, 4 players, you hold 9 resources. Buying followers gives 9 followers; Body Snatchers gives 2 now and, at round III start, your worst member for their best: about +4 to +10 to you and the same off them. Gain over buying followers: about -3 to +3 to you, plus the harm to one rival. Today at 7 that gain is about -1 to +5.
3. Both sides: the swap choice is yours as today; buying it at 9 versus 9 followers is a real choice now, where at 7 it was nearly always right when a rival held a big member.
4. Who feels it: the same sting to the rival whose best member goes; Loyalists still blocks it.
5. Not refused: Cory asked for it mid priced (Nov 8) and once said "maybe Body Snatchers cheaper" (Nov 5): a price rise goes against both, so it is offered as the plain alternative, not the pick.
6. Judged: card unchanged, so it keeps everything Cory liked ("a little more interesting"); only the cost moves. At round II's table state a 9 is reachable by about the second half of round II.
7. Public and certain: no change.
8. Color interactions: none: price change on a Red card.
9. Biggest case: round III start, your Volunteer (worth 1) for a rival's Mob: up to 10 to you and 10 off them, for 9 resources, once a game; against a winner's 70 to 110 that is still the strongest single member turn in Red. The price cuts the margin by 2 followers, not the swing.
Verdict: send

## Idea: Body Snatchers, shop only

1. Printed text: "Round Start: swap one of your members with a shop member."
2. Played turn: round III start, the shop holds 7 members of 5 and up; you give a 1-worth member and take the best shop member, up to +3 worth or a fresh engine.
3. Both sides: your choice only.
4. Who feels it: nobody; no rival loses anything, it only denies a shop card.
5. Not refused: overlaps Seat Filler "Round Start: you may put this in the shop and recruit a shop member costing up to 3 more, for free." and Promote's shop swap.
6. Judged: loses the rival interaction Cory liked about it.
7. Public and certain: yes.
8. Color interactions: none.
9. Biggest case: take Mob from the shop for a Volunteer: up to 9 to you, nothing off a rival.
Verdict: cut (nobody at the table feels it; overlaps Seat Filler)
