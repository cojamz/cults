Asked by Cory: "yeah, do members names and art fixes." Nov 11: "you should remember them game to game… oh no, he has the heist crew."
Skill read: bash .claude/hooks/brainstorm-read.sh at 11:55am Eastern

Direction: the game is Cults now, so each member is a kind of person you'd find in a cult, and the name says what the card does. Red is the recruiter (design-core: "I get members without spending my recruit, and I trade up"). Rename only names tied to an old theme (party host, office panel, corporate headhunter, sci-fi) or that don't say what the card does; keep the rest. Card text, price and worth unchanged on every card.

Kept as they are (name already says the card, already a cult-ish person): Body Snatchers, Seat Filler, Mentor, Brethren, Purists, Clan, Foster Parents, Loyalists.

## Idea: Den Mother

1. Printed text: unchanged, "On Recruit: +1 red per 2 members you have. Round Start: +1 red per 2 members you have." Replaces the name Host.
2. Played turn: a name change; the turn plays exactly as Host does today (round III, 4 players, holder with 8 members gets +4 red at round start). The gain against other spaces is unchanged; what changes is the table reading "the house mother, whose house pays more the bigger it gets".
3. Both sides: no choices on the card.
4. Who feels it: the holder; the name tells rivals why a big Red cult has extra red every round.
5. Not refused: pitch-ledger has no Den Mother; Host was never pitched for a rename; "Clan Elders" was a different card refused for its rule.
6. Judged: intuitive (a den mother runs a big household; more members, more red), a person, not tryhard; one plain word pair.
7. Public and certain: rule unchanged, nothing hidden.
8. Color interactions: none: a name change on a single-color card.
9. Biggest case: unchanged rule; round III with 12 members it pays 6 red at round start, the same as Host today, against a winner's 70 to 110 followers.
Verdict: send

## Idea: Elders

1. Printed text: unchanged, "On Use, once per round: promote or demote another of your members." Replaces the name Panel.
2. Played turn: name change only; round II, the holder promotes a 4-cost member to a 7-cost one, the same as Panel today.
3. Both sides: the holder picks promote or demote; unchanged.
4. Who feels it: the holder; the name says who moves members up and down the ranks in a cult.
5. Not refused: "Watchful Elders" and "Clan Elders" were other rules refused for their rule, not the word; no Elders on any live card.
6. Judged: intuitive (cult elders decide who rises and who falls), a group of people, short.
7. Public and certain: the rule is unchanged, so nothing drawn, random or hidden is added.
8. Color interactions: none: a name change.
9. Biggest case: unchanged; round III one swap up to 3 more in cost each round, as today, against a contest's 4/6/10.
Verdict: send

3. Both sides: the holder picks the rival, the member and the shop member; unchanged.
4. Who feels it: the rival losing the member; the name says someone got inside their cult and moved a member out.
5. Not refused: no Infiltrator in the ledger or live cards; Magenta's Double Agent is a different word.
