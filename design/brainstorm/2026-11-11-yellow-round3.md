## Idea: Rainbow Feast

1. Printed text: "+2 followers per color among your members. +1 yellow." (name and +2 Claude's fill-ins). Cards it touches: Melting Pot "+2 yellow. +1 of each color among your members." (same count, paid in resources); Largesse "Give rivals 12 silver from the bank, split as you choose. +10 followers."; Dabblers (counts as one more color).
2. Played turn: round III, 4 players, you hold members in 4 colors: +8 followers, +1 yellow. Best other space for you, Initiation (a free member up to 10, worth about 1 to 3 followers plus its ability, and maybe a new color for the contest): Rainbow Feast is about +5 followers more, Initiation gives a body. A cult with 2 colors gets +4.
3. Both sides: no choices on the card.
4. Who feels it: the wide cults race for the one round III center space; a rival who holds 5 colors takes it first or blocks it. No sting.
5. Not refused: Bumper Crop "+3 followers per color among your members" was pitched Oct 30 as a direction with no verdict on it (the no that day was for Wander, Window Shopping and Graft). Not "+2 of every other color" (resources, refused) and not a full-set count.
6. Judged: one visible count, done when taken, pays in followers which is what matters late (Cory: resources matter less for contests). Intuitive: yes. Fun: medium, a payoff for the plan the contest asked for. Interacts: the race for the center only. Powerful: 8 to 10 followers. Engine or counter: the end of New Blood, Housewarming-style recruits and Dabblers. Its color: going broad pays off.
7. Public and certain: members in each cult are public; nothing hidden or random.
8. Color interactions: none: a single-color payoff on Yellow's own count; works in any game.
9. Biggest case: round III, 5 colors (4 plus grey) with Dabblers adding none past 5: +10 followers. Green's Greatest Hits copying it counts the Green player's own colors: up to +10 for them too, in the same round. Against Largesse's +10 and a winner's 70 to 110; a round III center at 10 is the allowed top.
Verdict: send

9. Biggest case: a 12-cost member free, against Initiation's 10.
Verdict: cut (often does nothing in round III; Initiation's twin)

## Idea: Even Spread

1. Printed text: "+3 followers per member of the color you have the fewest members of. +1 yellow." (only colors among your members count) (name and +3 Claude's fill-ins; grey counts as a color, as everywhere). Cards it touches: Melting Pot "For each color among your members: +1 follower and +1 of that color (grey: 1 of any color)."; Red's contest, most members of one color; Dabblers (counts as one color more).
2. Played turn: round III, 4 players. You hold 11 members: 3 red, 3 blue, 2 white, 2 yellow, 1 grey: smallest group 1 (grey): +3 followers. Recruit a second grey first and it is 2: +6. A cult of 15 spread 3/3/3/3/3: +9. Best other round III space for a wide cult, Initiation "Recruit a shop member costing 10 or less, for free. +1 red." (a 10-cost member, worth about 2 to 3 followers plus its ability). Gain: 0 to +6 over Initiation, more the evener your cult.
3. Both sides: no choices on the card; the choice is earlier, recruiting your thinnest color instead of your best member.
4. Who feels it: a rival who wants it must also be even; the Red player stacking one color gets little from it, so they block it only to deny you. Mild: a race for the center space.
5. Not refused: not a count of colors (Melting Pot's job now), not "+2 of every color" (resources, refused), not a full set (a set of resources). New shape: reads the depth of your thinnest color.
6. Judged: one count, done when taken, follower payoff (Cory: resources matter less late). Intuitive: yes, "your smallest color group". Fun: medium-high, gives Yellow players a reason to balance recruits in rounds II and III. Interacts: pulls against Red's one-color stack and rewards recruiting colors other cults leave. Powerful: 3 to 9. Engine or counter: the end of Melting Pot and New Blood; counter to one-color stacks. Its color: going broad, evenly.
7. Public and certain: members and their colors are public; nothing hidden or random.
8. Color interactions: the shared moment is the round II and III recruit choice between a second member of your stack (Red, Blue) or a thin color; names no color.
9. Biggest case: round III, a 20-member cult spread 4/4/4/4/4 (beyond the 8 to 14 real wide cults): +12. Real biggest: 15 members at 3 each, +9. Green's Greatest Hits copying it counts the Green player's own smallest group, usually 1 or 2: +3 to +6. Against a winner's 70 to 110 and a contest's 10; round III center.

## Idea: Shun

1. Printed text: "Choose a color. Each rival with exactly one member of that color discards it. +4 yellow." (name and +4 Claude's fill-ins; grey counts as a color). Cards it touches: Martyrs (White) "Whenever this member is discarded, +4 white and +4 followers."; White's contest, fewest members; Melting Pot "For each color among your members: +1 follower and +1 of that color (grey: 1 of any color)."; Poach "Recruit a rival's member you choose, for free. +1 magenta." (the other round III sting).
2. Played turn: round III, 4 players. Rivals hold 9 to 14 members; two of them hold a single grey and one a single white. You choose grey: two rivals discard their grey member (worth about 1 to 2 followers each) and drop from 5 member colors to 4, while you keep 5; you +4 yellow. Against Initiation "Recruit a shop member costing 10 or less, for free. +1 red." (a free member for you), Shun gives you nothing to keep but moves you ahead of two rivals in the Yellow contest (round III prize 10, ties split rounded up: a 3-way tie at 5 colors pays 4 each; leading alone pays 10), so about +6 followers to you plus 1 to 2 lost by each hit rival.
3. Both sides: you choose the color: the best pick is the color the most rivals hold one member of; no rival choice. If no rival has a singleton, it pays only +4 yellow, which the table can see before you place.
4. Who feels it: a real sting to every rival with a singleton; a reason to recruit a second member of your thin colors (the same pull as the contest) and a reason to take the center first. A White player hit by it may like it (one fewer member for Fewest members; Martyrs pays +4 white and +4 followers).
5. Not refused: not a steal (nothing moves to you), not a swap (Red's), not Release. No ledger line refuses discarding rivals' members from a space. Same aggression level as Poach, which Cory approved for round III ("Aggression is intentional").
6. Judged: one choice, one visible count (rivals' members by color), done when taken. Round III real state: wide cults often hold a single grey or a single member of one color. Intuitive: yes, "everyone with only one of that color loses it". Fun: high tension, a round III swing in Yellow's contest. Interacts: hits every rival; counters a cult that went wide with one of each. Powerful: up to 3 members gone and the contest lead. Engine or counter: counters Yellow's own race and Melting Pot counts. Its color: the color race of Yellow's contest, played at rivals.
7. Public and certain: members and colors are public; nothing hidden or random.
8. Color interactions: the shared moment is a member being discarded (White's Martyrs, Farewell, Estate Sale also cause it); names no color; works in any game.
9. Biggest case: round III, all three rivals hold a single grey (round II grey members are common in a 4-color game): 3 members discarded, each rival drops one contest color; you lead Yellow's contest alone for 10 followers instead of a 4-way split at 3. Swing about +7 for you, -1 to -3 each for rivals. Green's Greatest Hits copying it later that round makes the copier choose again: a second color can be hit, up to 3 more members. Against a winner's 70 to 110 and Poach (a rival's best member, worth up to 4 or 5 followers plus its ability); round III center.
Verdict: send
