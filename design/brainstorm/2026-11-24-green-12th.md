# Brainstorm: Green, the 12th member

Date: 2026-11-24
Asked by Cory: "lets get everything to 12. we'll worry about 15 later" and, on Packed House, "always going to max out. its a bit oto good."
Skill read: bash .claude/hooks/brainstorm-read.sh at 10:25pm Eastern

All three are cards already written and not dealt (their old prices kept as written). Live Green spaces: Flock Together "+1 green per minion on Green spaces, this one included. +1 green."; Follow Suit "Copy the previous action. +1 green."; Do It Again "Copy an action with your minion. +1 green."; Draw a Crowd "+1 follower per minion on Green spaces, this one included. +3 green."; Imitate "Copy an action with a rival's minion on it. +1 green."; Replay All "Choose a color other than green. Copy every action with your minion on its spaces. +1 green."

## Idea: Superfans

1. Printed text: "You can place on Green spaces rivals have taken." Cards it touches: Flock Together and Draw a Crowd (they count minions on Green spaces), Imitate.
2. Played turn: Round II, 4 players: a rival took Draw a Crowd with 4 minions on Green; you join them on it: +5 followers +3 green, where the best open space, Imitate, copies a rival's +3 of some color; gain about +4 followers.
3. Both sides: No choices on the card; each turn the taken Green spaces stay open to you only.
4. Who feels it: Every Green taker: blocking a Green space no longer blocks you; a reason to take the member from you.
5. Not refused: Pitch-ledger Green lines checked: Costumers (cloak sharing) was the "hard to track" no; this reads only the wheel. Packed House was "too good" because it paid on every minion, anyone's, every round with no choice; Superfans pays nothing by itself.
6. Judged: Intuitive: fans pile in where the show is. Fun: a seat nobody can block. Interacts: Green takers. Powerful: a sure good space each turn. Engine: Flock Together and Draw a Crowd count your extra minion. Its color: Green's crowding. Bends one rule (taken spaces are closed).
7. Public and certain: the wheel is public; nothing drawn or hidden.
8. Color interactions: none: a single-color card on Green's own spaces.
9. Biggest case: Round III, 6 minions on Green, you join Draw a Crowd: +7 followers +3 green in one turn, about 3 more than an open space; against a winner's 70 to 110 and a contest's 10.
Verdict: send

## Idea: Song Request

1. Printed text: "On Use, once per round: copy an action a rival has a minion on." Cards it touches: Imitate "Copy an action with a rival's minion on it. +1 green."
2. Played turn: Round II, 4 players: a rival is on Plunder ("Steal 3 followers and 3 resources."); you use Song Request at no minion cost: +3 followers and 3 resources, on top of your turn's space; gain the whole copy each round.
3. Both sides: No choices beyond which rival action; you pick the best one, always.
4. Who feels it: The rival whose big space you copy; a reason to take the member.
5. Not refused: It is Imitate on a member once a round. Pitch-ledger Green: "repeat of a space" is a shape Cory has turned down before (the Setlist note: judges marked a near-copy a repeat); flagged.
6. Judged: Intuitive yes; Fun yes; but it restates Green's own space, which breaks the member bar ("must not just restate their color's mechanic", Nov 25).
7. Public and certain: yes.
8. Color interactions: none.
9. Biggest case: Round III, copy a rival's round III space each round: 6 to 10 resources or followers a round for free, about 10 more than any member; too big.
Verdict: cut (restates Imitate, and too big in round III)

## Idea: Opening Act

1. Printed text: "Round Start: copy any action." Cards it touches: every space on the wheel.
2. Played turn: Round III start, 4 players: copy Replay All or Plunder before anyone places: a full round III space each round, free; gain a whole extra turn.
3. Both sides: No choices beyond which action; always the biggest one.
4. Who feels it: Nobody at the table: it fires before anyone places.
5. Not refused: Warm-Up Act (copy a round I action) was tried; Opening Act is the unbounded version.
6. Judged: Nobody feels it (step 4), so cut.
7. Public and certain: yes.
8. Color interactions: none.
9. Biggest case: a free round III action every round: 10+ in one turn outside a contest; cut by the rule.
Verdict: cut (nobody feels it; pays 10+ in round III)

## Idea: Understudy

1. Printed text: "On Recruit: copy the action taken the previous turn." Cards it touches: Follow Suit "Copy the previous action. +1 green."
2. Played turn: Round II, you recruit it right after a rival took Draw a Crowd at 4: +5 followers +3 green once; best other Green member at that price, Hourglass "On Recruit: copy the action you just took.", copies your own.
3. Both sides: No choices on the card; you time the recruit after a rival's big turn.
4. Who feels it: The rival whose big space you copy once; small.
5. Not refused: Hourglass is live and does nearly the same (copy the action just taken); Follow Suit is the space. Same shape twice.
6. Judged: a repeat of Hourglass and Follow Suit; cut.
7. Public and certain: yes.
8. Color interactions: none.
9. Biggest case: once, after a round III space: about 8; fine, but a repeat.
Verdict: cut (repeats Hourglass and Follow Suit)

## Idea: Village Belle

1. Printed text: "Whenever a rival copies one of your actions, +2 followers." Green, 3 green + 3 any (Claude's fill-in). Cards it touches: Follow Suit "Copy the previous action. +1 green."; Imitate "Copy an action with a rival's minion on it. +1 green."; Agents (live) "Your actions can't be copied."
2. Played turn: Round I, 4 players: you take Harvest; the next player takes Follow Suit and copies it: you get +2 followers without placing. Best other Green round I member effect at this price, Regulars "Round End: +1 follower per minion you have on Green spaces.", pays about +1 to +2 a round; gain about +1 to +2 a round, more when Green players are at the table.
3. Both sides: No choice on the card. The rival choosing to copy you now weighs their copy against handing you 2 followers; both sides stay live (a big action is still worth copying, a small one no longer is).
4. Who feels it: Every Green player: copying the Belle's actions costs them 2 followers to the Belle, so they look elsewhere or pay it; a sting and a reason to block.
5. Not refused: Admirer (the same text) was cut by Claude on paper on Nov 24 ("needs rivals copying you"), never shown to Cory; Muse "Whenever a rival copies an action, +1 green." is written and not dealt (any copy, small). Not Gawker, Mummer or Green Man (none read copies of your actions), not a contest booster (no Green contest count).
6. Judged: Intuitive: everyone copies the belle, and she is admired for it. Fun: a turn of yours pays twice. Interacts: with every copy space, Green's or a Green rival's. Powerful: +2 followers a time. Engine: Imitate and Follow Suit feed it. Its color: Green's copying, seen from the copied side, which no live card does. Round I: works from turn 1 with no members needed.
7. Public and certain: every copy is announced at the table; nothing hidden, drawn or random.
8. Color interactions: none: a single-color card reading Green's own copy spaces (and any copy member).
9. Biggest case: Round III, 4 players, two Green players copying (Imitate, Follow Suit, Replay All on your actions, plus a copy member): about 4 copies of your actions, +8 followers that round, under 10; against a winner's 70 to 110 and a contest's 10.
Verdict: send
