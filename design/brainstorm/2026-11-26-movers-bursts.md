## Idea: Turncoat

1. Printed text: "On Use, once per round: swap this member for a rival's member." (Red; Claude's fill-in, replaces Pied Piper). Cards it touches: Warden "Your other members can't be swapped." (protects against it); Abbess "Whenever a member joins your cult from a rival's, +2 followers."; Chapman "Whenever one of your members is swapped, +2 followers."; Swindler "Round Start: swap one of your members with a rival's member costing up to 3 more."
2. Played turn: round II, 4 players. You hold Turncoat and 3 others; the leader holds Firstborn (worth 3) and Night Thieves "Round End, before scoring: steal 3 resources." You use Turncoat: Night Thieves joins you, Turncoat goes to the leader, still turned sideways (used this round). You gain a 9-cost engine and drop a worth-1 card; the best other choice, Promote "+3 red. Swap any member for one costing up to 3 more, from another cult or the shop.", needs a minion and caps at 3 more. Gain: a member costing about 4 more than Turncoat, with no minion spent. Round III: the leader uses Turncoat on their turn to take a member back, or takes from someone else.
3. Both sides: you choose the rival and the member: always the member you most want, so no false choice. The rival who receives Turncoat chooses next round whom to hit; hitting you back and hitting the new leader are both live.
4. Who feels it: the rival loses their best member each time and is handed a card that lets them hit back next round. A sting with a built-in answer; Warden is the wall.
5. Not refused: pitch-ledger has no hot-potato swapper; Changeling swaps once and stops; Sellswords passes itself for resources, not a member. Not verdict 45 (no taken spaces), not 39 (no minion given).
6. Judged: one line, one verb (swap), and the card itself moves, so the table watches where it sits. At round II's real state (3 to 5 members each) every rival has a target. Clears the bar on its own: a rule-bender that keeps moving.
7. Public and certain: everything face up; the card turned sideways shows it was used this round.
8. Color interactions: none named. Members leaving and joining cults is the shared moment Red, Magenta and White cause; Abbess, Chapman and Mourners pay off on it with no color named.
9. Biggest case: round III, it takes a worth-3 member (Firstborn or Lord of the Manor): +2 followers and 3 more members of a color for you, −2 for the rival; at most one use per round across the whole table because it stays sideways when it moves, so 2 to 3 uses a game. With Abbess +2 and Chapman (rival) +2. Well under 10 in a turn.
Intuitive: one sentence, the card itself changes hands.
Fun: the leader dreads it, and whoever holds it decides who gets hit next.
Interacts: every rival's best member is exposed; Warden and Abbess gain a job.
Powerful: takes anyone's best member, every round.
Engine or counter: counter to runaway cults; feeds Abbess and Chapman.
Its color: Red swaps members.
Verdict: send

## Idea: Burglar

1. Printed text: "On Use, once per round: steal 1 of each color from a rival." (Magenta; Claude's fill-in). Cards it touches: Cutthroats "Whenever you steal resources, you may steal that many followers instead."; Fence "Whenever you steal, +2 magenta."; Extortionist "On Recruit and at Round Start: steal 2 resources."; Night Thieves "Round End, before scoring: steal 3 resources."
2. Played turn: round II, 4 players (4 colors). A rival holds 2 red, 3 yellow, 1 blue, 4 magenta and wants a red member. Burglar takes 1 red, 1 yellow, 1 blue, 1 magenta: 4 resources, and their red member now costs a resource they no longer have. Extortionist gives 2 at round start; the best round II space, Plunder "Steal 3 followers and 3 resources. +1 magenta.", costs a minion. Gain: 4 resources with no minion, once a round.
3. Both sides: you choose the rival; the colors are set by the card. Take from the broadest pile for the most, or from the rival about to buy a member of one color to deny them.
4. Who feels it: the rival loses up to 4, one of each color, capped (verdict 33's ceiling holds). With Cutthroats they lose 4 followers instead.
5. Not refused: not Protection Racket (per-member, no ceiling). Not "+N by formula on your pile": it reads a rival's spread. Steal caps follow verdict 33.
6. Judged: one verb on a rival's pile, every round, and it pairs with Cutthroats as a small build (Cory asked for these). On its own it is a solid Magenta steal; with Cutthroats it is the build.
7. Public and certain: every pile is public, the card names its colors, and nothing comes off the pile.
8. Color interactions: none named. It reads whatever colors are on the table.
9. Biggest case: round III, 4 colors, 2 uses a game (joins round II): 8 resources, or 8 followers taken with Cutthroats (a 16-follower swing over two rounds), 4 in any one turn. Under the 10-in-a-turn line.
Intuitive: steal one of each color.
Fun: the Cutthroats pairing; and denying a rival the color they need.
Interacts: hits a rival every round, feeds Fence and Cutthroats.
Powerful: 4 resources a round with no minion.
Engine or counter: engine piece for steal builds; counters wide Yellow piles.
Its color: Magenta steals.
Verdict: send

## Idea: Go-Between

1. Printed text: "You may recruit a rival's member as if it were in the shop, paying its cost to that rival." (Blue; Claude's fill-in, replaces Bargainer). Cards it touches: Hire Away "Recruit a rival's member, paying them its cost in silver. +1 silver." (Silver space, the same move once, in silver); Abbess; Chapman; Blue's contest "Most members".
2. Played turn: round III, 4 players. A rival built around Tall-Tale Teller "Whenever you copy an action, double the resources it gives you." You recruit it with your normal recruit, paying them 12. You spend a recruit exactly as on a shop member; they lose their engine and get 12 resources to rebuild with. Gain against the shop: the same price, but you take the card that matters to a rival and the shop no longer limits you.
3. Both sides: the rival has no choice. You choose: a shop member, or a rival's at the same price; the rival's is better only when it hurts them or builds you, so it's a real choice each turn.
4. Who feels it: anyone holding an engine piece. They are paid in full, so it stings only the plan, not the pile. Counterplay against a runaway member engine.
5. Not refused: Hire Away was a yes as a Silver space; Cory's "one spot taking rival members is enough" was about adding a second Silver space. This is a member, paid in any colors. Not a discount (price unchanged).
6. Judged: one rule bent (rivals' cults count as shop), simple, Blue's shop verb. Round III state: 5 to 8 members per cult, so always targets.
7. Public and certain: every member in every cult is face up, and the price is printed on it.
8. Color interactions: members joining and leaving cults (Red, Magenta, White's moment). Abbess pays you +2, Chapman pays the rival +2.
9. Biggest case: round III with Bring Back and Salvage-style extra recruits, 2 or 3 rival members bought in a round, each at full price; resources move to the victims one for one, so no follower burst for anyone; the swing is abilities and member counts (Blue +1, rival −1 per buy).
Intuitive: rivals' members are for sale at their price.
Fun: nobody's engine is safe; the victim gets paid.
Interacts: every rival's cult becomes your shop.
Powerful: breaks the shop row limit.
Engine or counter: counters member engines; feeds Abbess and Blue's member count.
Its color: Blue shops.
Verdict: send

## Idea: Huckster

1. Printed text: "On Use, once per round: give one of your members to a rival, and take resources equal to its cost from them, colors of your choice." (White; Claude's fill-in, replaces Matchmaker). Cards it touches: Give Away "Give a member to a rival for its cost in white. +1 white." (the space, paid from the bank); Matchmaker "On Use, once per round: give a rival one of your members. +4 followers."; Redeem "Turn all your white into followers, 1 for 1. +1 white."; White's contest "Fewest members".
2. Played turn: round II, 4 players. You hold Robbers "On Recruit: steal 5 resources." (spent, costs 9). The Magenta-contest leader holds 12. Hawker: Robbers goes to them, you take 9 of their resources. You: −1 member (White's contest), +9 resources. Give Away pays the same 9 but in white from the bank and costs a minion. Gain over Give Away: no minion, colors you pick, and the rival pays.
3. Both sides: the rival has no choice. You choose the member: a spent, costly one; and the rival: the richest, which also knocks them in the Most resources contest.
4. Who feels it: the rich rival loses up to the member's cost and gets a member (+1 to their member counts, hurting their White contest, helping their Blue one). A real sting with something handed back.
5. Not refused: not Matchmaker's fixed +4; not verdict 40 (no followers subtracted); a rival acts on nobody's turn but yours, and makes no choice.
6. Judged: White's own shape (trade a member away for value), now aimed at a rival's pile. Round II state: you hold a spent member to sell.
7. Public and certain: piles and members are public; no card comes off the pile.
8. Color interactions: members leaving your cult (Chapman, Mourners, Martyrs pay); members joining a rival's (Abbess pays them).
9. Biggest case: round III, a 12-cost member sold to a rival holding 15: +12 resources once (followers only through a recruit or Redeem); 2 to 3 uses a game, each needs a costly member to sell. Highwayman "steal half of a rival's biggest pile" is the size it sits beside. Flag: 12 in one use is at the top of the band; a cap of "costing up to 8" is the fallback.
Intuitive: sell a member to a rival at its price.
Fun: dumping a spent card on the rich player.
Interacts: hits the hoarder; changes two players' member counts.
Powerful: resources equal to a member's cost, every round.
Engine or counter: counter to hoards; engine with Redeem, Chapman and Mourners.
Its color: White trades members away.
Verdict: send
