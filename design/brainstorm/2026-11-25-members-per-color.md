## Idea: Innkeeper

1. Printed text: "Whenever one of your members goes back to the shop, +3 red." Red, 3 red + 3 any (Claude's fill-in).
2. Played turn: Round II, 4 players: you Borrow a 3-cost member (it goes back at round end: +3 red) and Promote another (yours goes to the shop: +3 red). Best other round II space Promote alone gives +3 red; here +6 red over the round.
3. Both sides: No choices on the card; the pull is toward Borrow, Promote and Demote, which send your members to the shop.
4. Who feels it: Rivals see your swaps refill the shop with members they can buy; you play the swap spaces more.
5. Not refused: Not a ledger no: not a space upgrade (#19), no tokens or pile draws; Ferryman pays per recruit, this pays per member leaving.
6. Judged: Intuitive: guests come and go at an inn. Fun: makes Borrow pay twice. Interacts: refills the shared shop. Powerful: +3 a time. Engine: Borrow and Promote each round. Its color: Red's moving members around.
7. Public and certain: everything it reads is on the table (members, shop, wheel, resources, followers); no pile draws, nothing hidden or random.
8. Color interactions: none: a single-color card that reads only general pieces.
9. Biggest case: Round III: Borrow, Promote and Demote all taken by you, plus a Green copy of Borrow: 4 members back to the shop, +12 red, about 4 to 6 followers' worth, against a winner's 70 to 110 and a contest's 10.
Verdict: send
