## Idea: Flattery

2. Played turn: round II, 4 players, standings 11 / 16 / 17 / 27 (game 1 of the measured games). The player on 11: 3 rivals ahead, +6 followers and +1 silver, rivals +3 silver each. The player on 17: 1 rival ahead, +2 followers. The leader on 27: +1 silver only. Best other round II spaces: Heist "Steal 4 followers and 4 resources. +1 magenta." (8 in all), Buyout. Gain for the last player: +6 followers against Heist's 8 resources-and-followers, so on par; for the leader it is a weak space, which is the point.
3. Both sides: no choices; who gets silver follows from the follower counts.
4. Who feels it: the players ahead get 3 silver each (a gift worth 3 followers each if spent, and a loss in fewest silver if they still hold it at round end). It helps whoever is behind: catch-up.
5. Not refused: rewards the player behind, not the leader; not keyed to who leads a contest (verdict 30); reads followers, which the table already counts for turn order.
6. Judged: one count everyone can see (who has more followers than you), a gift that lands on the players ahead. Round II's real table: standings spread 7 to 29, so 1 to 3 rivals qualify for most players.
7. Public and certain: followers are public.
8. Color interactions: none: a single-color catch-up card; it reads followers, which every color makes.
9. Biggest case: last place in round III, 3 rivals ahead: +6 followers and 9 silver to rivals; Green copying it pays the copier by its own standing, at most +6 again. Against a winner's 70 to 110 and a contest's 10: small and bounded.
Verdict: cut (Cory, Nov 11: "we already have a follower action")
