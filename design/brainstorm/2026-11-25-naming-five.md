## Round Start: recruit once, before anyone places. (Blue, worth 3) — was Doorbusters, then Forestallers
Candidates: Firstborn, Early Birds, Queue-Jumpers, First Comers, Eldest Sons, Forestallers (control).
Cut: Early Birds (modern idiom; twin of live Early Riser, which also acts "before anyone"); Queue-Jumpers (modern); First Comers (a phrase, no person); Eldest Sons (read as "count as 2"); Forestallers (reader: word unknown).
Pick: **Firstborn**. Read: "goes first, first pick or first placement". "You recruited the Firstborn, and of course they get first pick." Worth 3: three eldest children in the picture.

## On Use, once per game: discard the whole shop and refill. (Blue) — was Restockers
Candidates: Market Sweeper, Auctioneer, Dustman, Spring Cleaner, Bailiff.
Cut: Auctioneer (read as bidding), Dustman and Bailiff (word unsure), Spring Cleaner (modern).
Pick: **Market Sweeper**. Read: "clears the shop row and refills it" (exact). "You recruited a Market Sweeper, and of course they sweep the whole market clear." Shares "Market" with Market-Day Shopper; different second word and picture, kept.

## Whenever you buy followers, +1 blue. (Blue) — was Regular
Candidates: Penny-Pincher, Change-Giver, Barker, Tout, Moneylender.
Cut: Barker, Tout (word unsure), Moneylender (read as a loan), Change-Giver (coined, no smile).
Pick: **Penny-Pincher**. Read: "saves or gains cheap resources". "You recruited a Penny-Pincher, and of course they get a coin back every time you pay." (A retired white card once had this name; not dealt.)

## On Use, once per round: take the Goes Last token. (Purple) — was Last Callers
Candidates: Laggard, Straggler, Rearguard, Hindmost, Tail-Ender, Dawdlers.
Cut: Hindmost (unknown), Rearguard (unsure, read as protection), Tail-Ender (modern), Straggler (benched cards Straggler and Stragglers did turn-order things), Dawdlers (Cory refused the Dawdler card Nov 24).
Pick: **Laggard**. Read: "goes late in turn order". "You recruited a Laggard, and of course they go last."

## On Use, once per game: rearrange your resources into any mix of colors. (Yellow, worth 3) — was Book Cookers
Candidates: Alchemists, Transmuters, Dyers (live Dyer), Moneychangers (live).
Cut: Transmuters (word unsure), the live names.
Pick: **Alchemists**. Read: "turn one resource color into another" (exact). "You recruited the Alchemists, and of course they turn what you have into something else." Worth 3: three alchemists round a flask.
