## Idea: Purple Cloak +2

1. Printed text: "Whenever you take the Goes Last token, +2 purple." (Claude's number; live is +3.) Cards it touches: Sleep In and Last Call take the token; Sleepyheads: "Whenever you take the Goes Last token, +3 followers."; Crunch Time: "+2 purple. Your next two turns come right after this one."
2. Played turn: 4 players, round II, Purple Cloak player takes Last Call with the token: live gives the space plus 3 purple, this gives the space plus 2. Against the best other round II space (about 7 in the bot games), the token turn drops by 1, and a game where the player takes the token 5 times drops by 5 purple, so 5 followers bought.
3. Both sides: no choices on the card.
4. Who feels it: the cloak's holder only; rivals still feel the token itself (their minions go before). Rivals who take the token from them now cost them 2, not 3.
5. Not refused: pitch-ledger Nov 7 and Nov 8 lines on Pocket Watch are about how to take the token, not its size; Cory asked for this nerf himself.
6. Judged: same card, smaller number; still a clear "go last and get paid" cloak, the shape Cory kept.
7. Public and certain: yes, the token and its taker are public.
8. Color interactions: none: a Purple cloak paying off Purple's token.
9. Biggest case: the Nov 11 AI game winner took the token every round, about 2 takes a round: 6 takes a game is 18 purple live, 12 at +2, so 6 fewer followers bought, against that winner's 121 and second place's 80. Bot cloak owners won 40 to 44% live; the test reads where +2 lands.
Verdict: send

## Idea: Magenta Cloak +2

1. Printed text: "Whenever you steal, +2 magenta." (Claude's number; live is +1.) Cards it touches: Heist counts as one steal (fix Nov 11); Help Yourself, Poach and the other Magenta steals.
2. Played turn: 4 players, round II, Magenta Cloak player takes Help Yourself and steals 4: live +1 magenta, this +2. A player who steals 2 times a round gains 6 magenta more over the game, about 6 followers bought.
3. Both sides: no choices on the card.
4. Who feels it: the rival stolen from loses the same as before; only the thief gains 1 more, so stealing becomes a little more worth doing.
5. Not refused: no ledger line on the Magenta Cloak's number; Cory asked for the buff.
6. Judged: same card, bigger number; still small next to the Purple Cloak's +3.
7. Public and certain: yes.
8. Color interactions: none: Magenta's cloak paying off Magenta's steals.
9. Biggest case: round III with Green copying Heist for the cloak's owner is still one steal, +2. A heavy thief steals about 8 times a game: 16 magenta at +2 against 8 live, against a winner's 70 to 110. Cloak owners won 13% live, the lowest.
