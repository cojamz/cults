## Idea: Hand-Me-Downs

1. Printed text: "Each rival with more members than you gives you one of their members, their choice. Give each of them 2 silver from the bank. +1 silver." (2 silver is Claude's fill-in). Cards it touches: Buyout: "Recruit a rival's member, paying them its cost in silver. +1 silver."; Gift Swappers (grey member): "On Use, once per game: every player, you too, takes one member they choose from the player on their right."; Swindle: "Steal a rival's member costing up to 7; give them a shop member of your choice in its place. +1 magenta."
2. Played turn: round II, 4 players. You hold 3 members; rivals hold 6, 5 and 2. The two bigger cults each give you a member, and they pick the one they want least, say a 5-cost member worth 1 follower whose On Recruit already fired. You gain 2 members (2 followers, +2 toward member-count contests such as Red's Most members of one color, and any Round End or Whenever ability still on them); each giver gets 2 silver, which counts against them in Fewest silver (6 in round II) unless they spend it. Best other round II space: Toast "+3 silver in round I, +6 in round II, +9 in round III. Each rival +1 silver in round I, +2 in round II, +3 in round III." gives you +6 silver. Gain: two members and a step toward the leaders, against 6 silver.
3. Both sides: the givers choose which member; each always gives the one they value least, so the card is "take the least wanted member of each bigger cult", which still moves a real card (worth at least 1 follower, counts as a member). You choose nothing but when to take it: it pays most when you are behind on members.
4. Who feels it: every rival with more members than you (a sting: one member each, softened by 2 silver that also hurts their Fewest silver). A small cult catches up; a big cult has a reason to take the space first. The White player, if bigger, loses a member, which helps them in Fewest members: that player may not mind, which is fine.
5. Not refused: pitch ledger and verdicts.md checked. Gift Swappers (Nov 9, a member, "The giver picks!") is the closest and was liked; Foreclose (same "rival with more members than you" target) had no verdict and puts the member in the shop, not with you. Nothing lent: the members stay with you.
6. Judged: one verb, members come to the smaller cult, Silver pays the givers (Silver's giving). Round II real table: players hold 2 to 7 members, so the space is live for anyone below the leaders and empty for the biggest cult (it gives that player only +1 silver: flag). Feels powerful when two or three cults are bigger than yours; a catch-up card, which Cory asked for on Nov 9.
7. Public and certain: member counts and every member are face up; the givers' choice is made in the open; no draws.
8. Color interactions: the shared moment is a member changing cults, which Red, Magenta and Blue also cause; the card names none of them.
9. Biggest case: round III, you hold 2 members and all 3 rivals hold more: you gain 3 members, each the giver's least wanted (worth 1 to 2 followers each, so 3 to 6 followers), plus up to one contest swing on member count (10 in round III), and the table gets 6 silver. Green copying it with Cover "Copy an action with a rival's minion on it. +1 green." takes from cults bigger than the copier's, so a big Green cult gets little. At most about 16 in one rare case, usually 3 to 8, against a winner's 70 to 110.
Verdict: send

## Idea: Collection Plate (cut)
1. Printed text: "Each rival gives you one of their members costing 5 or less, your choice. Give each of them 5 silver from the bank."
2. Played turn: round II, three members of your choice; Poach "Recruit a rival's member you choose, for free. +1 magenta." does one in round III.
3. Both sides: you always take the best cheap member.
4. Who feels it: everyone.
5. Not refused: not pitched.
6. Judged: three chosen members in round II beats round III's Poach; too big.
7. Public and certain: yes.
8. Color interactions: Magenta's taking.
9. Biggest case: 3 chosen members plus contest swings, over 10 outside round III.
Verdict: cut (bigger than Poach)

## Idea: Gift a Member (cut)
1. Printed text: "Give one of your members to a rival. Gain its cost in silver."
2. Played turn: round II, give an 8-cost member, +8 silver, then Polish turns it.
3. Both sides: you give your least useful member.
4. Who feels it: the receiver, a gift.
5. Not refused: same shape as the Patrons member "On Use, once per game: give one of your members to a rival. Gain its cost in followers." and Estate Sale "Discard one of your members. Gain its cost in white. +1 white."
6. Judged: a copy of two live cards.
7. Public and certain: yes.
8. Color interactions: White's giving away.
9. Biggest case: an 11-cost member for 11 silver.
Verdict: cut (Patrons and Estate Sale)

## Idea: Introductions, Dinner Bell, Haggle, Reserve a Table (cut)
1. Printed text: Introductions "A rival recruits a shop member you choose, paying you its cost in resources of their choice"; Dinner Bell "Every player may recruit now, paying silver as any color"; Haggle "This turn each silver you pay toward a member counts as 2"; Reserve a Table "Put 3 of your silver on an open space; whoever takes it gains them".
2. Played turn: each at round II against Toast's +6 silver.
3. Both sides: Introductions forces a purchase the rival never wants.
4. Who feels it: Haggle, nobody; Reserve a Table, barely.
5. Not refused: Dinner Bell is Blue's extra recruit.
6. Judged: Introductions feels bad to the victim (a forced buy); Haggle and Reserve a Table are too small for round II.
7. Public and certain: all public.
8. Color interactions: Dinner Bell overlaps Blue.
9. Biggest case: Introductions takes up to 11 resources in round III (over 10); the others stay small.
Verdict: cut (feels bad, Blue's verb, or too small)
