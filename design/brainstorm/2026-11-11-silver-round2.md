## Idea: Buyout

1. Printed text: "Recruit a rival's member, paying them its cost in silver. +1 silver." (name Claude's fill-in). Cards it touches: Swindle: "Steal a rival's member costing up to 7; give them a shop member of your choice in its place. +1 magenta."; Promote: "+3 red. Swap any member for one costing up to 3 more, from another cult or the shop."; Toast: "+3 silver in round I, +6 in round II, +9 in round III. Each rival +1 silver in round I, +2 in round II, +3 in round III."
2. Played turn: round II, 4 players, the Silver player took Toast (+6) and holds 7 silver. A rival holds a 7-cost member (round II members cost about 6 to 8 as seen). Here: that member joins you without using your recruit, the rival gets 7 silver, and you drop to 1 silver (0 by turn end if you recruit with it). Best other round II space: Swindle takes a member up to 7 and hands back a shop member of your choice; Promote upgrades by up to 3. Gain: a recruit's worth (the turn's normal recruit still happens), paid at full price, plus both halves of Fewest silver move your way (you down, a rival up).
3. Both sides: you choose the rival and the member; the rival has no choice. Rival's side: they lose the member and get its full cost in silver, which they can spend next turn but which counts against them in Fewest silver if they still hold it at round end, and which raises their pile for Most resources.
4. Who feels it: the rival losing the member (a sting softened by full payment); a White player may welcome it (Fewest members); a Blue or Red leader loses ground in Most members / Most of one color. Reason to block: anyone holding a key member.
5. Not refused: "buy a rival's member by paying them its cost" was refused on Nov 7 only as a Red x Blue tie ("these aren't really blue and red interactions"), not as a Silver space; the gold version (Oct 21) was withdrawn by Claude, never answered. Different from Swindle (theft with a shop replacement) and Poach (free).
6. Judged: one verb on a rival's member, a visible price, a payment that lands on the rival's pile (Silver's giving). Round II's real table: most cults hold 2 to 7 members, so targets exist. Risk to flag: the space asks you to pay to use it (Cory's "no board action should require spending"); Markdown ("Recruit a member from the discard pile now, paying 2 less. +3 blue.") is the live precedent for a recruit space that you pay for.
7. Public and certain: every member and its cost is face up.
8. Color interactions: the shared moment is a member changing cults, which Red, Magenta and Blue also cause; this card makes it happen by itself and names none of them.
9. Biggest case: round III, the Silver player holds 15 silver after Toast (+9), buys a rival's 12-cost member: no free followers at all, only a recruit's worth (up to the member's printed followers and ability) and the rival holding 12 silver at round end if bought on the last turn (Fewest silver +10 to you). Green copying it needs the copier's own silver. Never more than the member is worth plus the contest, against a winner's 70 to 110.
Verdict: send

## Idea: Gift Exchange (cut)
1. Printed text: "Give each rival 2 silver from the bank. Recruit a member; your silver pays for any color." (in code, off the wheel)
2. Played turn: round II, an extra recruit plus 6 silver to rivals.
3. Both sides: no choices.
4. Who feels it: rivals get 2 silver each.
5. Not refused: not pitched to Cory before.
6. Judged: an extra recruit is Blue's verb (Recruit Twice, Spree, Markdown, Cory: "scaling versions of the same thing").
7. Public and certain: yes.
8. Color interactions: overlaps Blue's verb instead of meeting it.
9. Biggest case: round III, an extra recruit of up to 10 followers plus 6 silver to rivals.
Verdict: cut (Blue's extra recruit)
