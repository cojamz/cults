## Idea: Bell-Ringer (first in turn order)

1. Printed text: "Round End: if you were first in this round's turn order, +3 followers." (Purple; fill-in). Cards it touches: Usher, Lord of the Manor, the rule that fewest followers goes first from round II.
2. Played turn: round II, you trail by 12 followers, so you go first; at round end +3. In round III you still trail: +3. Usher "choose your place" or Lord of the Manor make it every round. Gain: 3 to 9 followers a game for the trailing player.
3. Both sides: no chooser; Usher's holder chooses first place over a later one only when it pays, a real choice.
4. Who feels it: a late catch-up the leader sees coming; Lord of the Manor becomes worth stealing.
5. Not refused: not a Goes Last token card (verdict 47's Dawdler); a hedge on Purple's last-minion contest, the shape verdict 48 asks for.
6. Judged: small; a plain scorer. Weak next to batch 1.
7. Public and certain: turn order is public every round.
8. Color interactions: none.
9. Biggest case: 3 rounds × 3 = 9 followers with Lord of the Manor.
Verdict: cut (small-ball scorer; Cory's own Timekeeper read-back goes instead)

## Idea: Tinker (Blue Bloods' slot)

1. Printed text: "When you recruit a member, you may discard one of your members; its cost pays toward the new one." (Blue; fill-in). Cards it touches: Bring Back "Recruit a member from the discard pile now. +3 blue."; Salvage; Gleaner "You may recruit from the discard pile as you would from the shop."; Rag-and-Bone Man "Whenever you discard a member, +1 blue."; Mourners, Chapman (leaving your cult).
2. Played turn: round III, you hold Robbers (spent, cost 9) and want a 12-cost member. Discard Robbers, pay 3 more. Promote "swap any member for one costing up to 3 more" needs a minion. Gain: a trade-up every recruit, and the spent member lands in the discard pile where your Bring Back and Salvage (and rivals') can reach it.
3. Both sides: you choose whether to trade in; trading in is right for spent members, keeping is right for ones still paying, a real choice.
4. Who feels it: rivals with discard-pile cards see better members there; mild.
5. Not refused: not a running discount (the price is printed and paid in full, part of it by a member); not Old-Clothes Dealers (swap for a discard-pile member of the same cost).
6. Judged: a Blue engine card: feeds the discard pile that Blue's spaces read. Clears the bar as an engine piece.
7. Public and certain: discarded members go face up to the discard pile.
8. Color interactions: members leaving your cult (Chapman, Mourners pay).
9. Biggest case: round III, 3 trade-ins each worth about 6 to 9 resources: about 20 resources of value over the round, through normal recruits; no follower burst.
Intuitive: trade an old member in toward a new one.
Fun: your spent members keep paying.
Interacts: fills the shared discard pile.
Powerful: upgrades every recruit.
Engine or counter: engine with Bring Back, Salvage, Gleaner.
Its color: Blue shops and works the discard pile.
Verdict: send

## Idea: Steward (Ferryman's slot)

1. Printed text: "Whenever one of your members leaves your cult on your turn, recruit a shop member costing less, for free." (Red; fill-in). Cards it touches: Promote, Demote (the swapped member goes to the shop or a rival), Turncoat, Journeyman, Give Away, Huckster, Bid Farewell "Discard a member for its cost in followers. +1 white.", Chapman.
2. Played turn: round II, Promote swaps your 6-cost for a 9-cost from the shop; the 6-cost leaves, so Steward recruits a shop member costing up to 5, free. Promote alone gives the 9-cost. Gain: a free member costing up to 1 less than what left, each time.
3. Both sides: no chooser.
4. Who feels it: rivals lose the shop member you take; Red's swap players gain a payoff.
5. Not refused: Temps (Nov 7) needed another color to cause its moment (verdict 27); here Red's own Promote and Demote cause it in any game.
6. Judged: an engine piece that rewards Red's swaps and White's shedding; "on your turn" keeps the choice off rivals' turns.
7. Public and certain: yes.
8. Color interactions: members leaving a cult is the moment Red, Magenta and White all cause (Martyrs model).
9. Biggest case: round III, 3 swaps with Promote, Demote and Fairy Godmother: 3 free members costing 4 to 9; about +3 to +6 followers in worth and three abilities. Under the line per turn.
Intuitive: someone leaves, a new one comes in.
Fun: every swap brings a second member.
Interacts: pays for Red's and White's moves.
Powerful: a free member per departure.
Engine or counter: engine with Promote, Demote, Turncoat.
Its color: Red recruits free and swaps.
Verdict: send
