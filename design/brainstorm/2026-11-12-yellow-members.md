## Idea: Exchange Students

1. Printed text: "On Recruit: swap one of your members for a rival's member that costs the same." (Claude's words). Cards it touches: the most-member-colors contest, Big Tent (above), Buyout "Recruit a rival's member, paying them its cost in silver. +1 silver.".
2. Played turn: round II, you hold two red members and no blue; a rival holds a 4-cost blue member. Recruit Exchange Students and swap your 4-cost red for their blue: +1 member color for the contest (the 4/6/10 race) and Big Tent. Without it, a blue member costs a full recruit.
3. Both sides: you choose which of yours and whose; the rival gets a member too, so it is a trade, chosen to hurt (their contest color) or to fit you.
4. Who feels it: the rival whose member moves; a Red player losing a one-color group, a Blue player keeping a count.
5. Not refused: Hustlers' shape (swap, approved tonight) on members; Cory asked for member movement (8:00pm). Red Matchmakers (swap with a shop member) pitched Nov 11 for Red; this swaps with a rival.
6. Judged: complementary (a new color without a new recruit) and cross-color (anyone wanting a rival's member). On Recruit, memorable, simple.
7. Public and certain: yes, both members are face up.
8. Color interactions: Red's one-color groups and Yellow's spread meet at the swap; names neither.
9. Biggest case: round III, swap a 2-cost for a rival's best 2-cost; a member's worth (2 to 10 resources) and a contest swing up to 10 followers.
