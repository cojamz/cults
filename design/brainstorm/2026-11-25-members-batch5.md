## Idea: Footman

1. Printed text: "Whenever you recruit a member costing 8 or more, +4 red." Red, 3 red + 3 any (Claude's fill-in).
2. Played turn: Round II, 4 players: you Promote a 5-cost member into an 8-cost one: Promote's +3 red plus +4 red here, 7 in all. Best other round II space, Demote, gives +3 red; gain +4.
3. Both sides: No choices on the card; every recruit becomes a choice between the dear member that pays 4 red and a cheaper one now.
4. Who feels it: Rivals see you aim at the 8s in the shop and can take or Demote them first; a reason to block.
5. Not refused: Pitch-ledger Red lines and verdicts 13, 15 and 23 checked: not a discount (the price stays), not small-ball (+4 at once). Connoisseurs (White) pays followers for 8s at game end; this pays red at the moment of recruiting.
6. Judged: Intuitive: a grand arrival needs a footman. Fun: makes Induct and Promote land big. Interacts: the 8s in the shop become contested. Powerful: +4 at a time. Engine: red feeds the next recruit. Its color: Red's recruiting. Round I: rarely fires, which is fine for a member.
7. Public and certain: everything it reads is on the table (members, minions, resources, followers); no pile draws, nothing hidden or random.
8. Color interactions: none: a single-color card that reads only general pieces.
9. Biggest case: Two 8-cost recruits in round III (Induct and Promote) plus a Green copy of Induct: 3 times +4 = 12 red, worth about 4 to 6 followers, against a winner's 70 to 110 and a contest's 10.
Verdict: send
