## Idea: Purifiers
Source: Cory, 2026-11-11 11:36pm Eastern: "Maybe: when this leaves your cult" (his design; Claude only words it and plays it out)

1. Printed text: "Whenever this member leaves your cult, each rival discards a member. On Use, once per game: discard this member." Grey, 3 any, worth 1 (unchanged). Live today: "On Use, once per game: discard one of your members. Each rival with more members than you discards one too." Cards that move it out of a cult: Promote ("+3 red. Swap any member for one costing up to 3 more, from another cult or the shop."), Demote ("+3 red. Swap any member for one costing up to 3 less, from another cult or the shop."), Swindle ("Steal a rival's member costing up to 7; give them a shop member of your choice in its place. +1 magenta."), Poach ("Recruit a rival's member you choose, for free. +1 magenta."), Farewell, Write-Off, Guest of Honor ("once per round, give a rival one of your members, +4 followers").
2. One played turn, round III, 4 players, holdings from AI game g3 (Ben 7 members, Cal 6, Dee 5, Ava 3): Ava holds Purifiers and uses it on her turn. She loses Purifiers (1 follower, 1 member). Ben, Cal and Dee each discard their weakest member, about 1 to 2 followers each and 1 member each. Ava gains nothing herself; the gain is relative: she drops 1, each rival drops about 1.5, and in "Fewest members" (White) or "Most members" (Blue) the gap moves by 2 against each rival. The best other use of that moment is no use at all (On Use costs no minion), so the whole effect is extra.
3. Choices: the owner chooses when to use it; using it early (round I) hits rivals holding 0 or 1 members, so most of them lose nothing or their only member; using it in round III hits every rival. Each rival chooses which member to discard: always their least useful one, and a rival holding Martyrs ("Whenever this member is discarded, +4 white and +4 followers.") discards Martyrs for +4 and +4, so it can help a rival. A player deciding whether to Poach, Swindle or Demote Purifiers away knows they will discard a member too, since they are a rival of the owner it leaves: moving it costs the mover one member.
4. Who feels it: every rival loses a member at once, a sting everyone feels; the mover pays too. It gives Red and Magenta a reason to leave it alone, and gives the owner a threat to hold.
5. Not already refused: the ledger has the live Purifiers (discard one, rivals with more discard too) and Pallbearers/Mourners pay on discards; nothing refused with this shape. Cory's own idea.
6. Judged like his marks: intuitive (one trigger, one plain effect, plus a once-per-game way to set it off); fun (a table-wide sting with a visible threat); interacts with Red and Magenta, who move members, and with White, who discards them; feels powerful (three rivals lose a member); counters a big cult; Grey, so no color theme to fit. It also adds member movement on tables without Red or Magenta (his Purple/Green/Silver note).
7. Public and certain: it is in the owner's cult, face up; every rival chooses their own discard in the open. No pile draws.
8. Color interactions: the shared moment is "a member leaves a cult", which Red (swaps), Magenta (steals) and White (discards, gifts) all cause; the card names none of them. With none of those colors it still works through On Use.
9. Biggest real case: Red, Magenta and Green at one table, round III. Purifiers leaves a cult on Demote, then again on Poach, then again on a Green Cover of Poach: three times, each time 3 players discard (the mover among them). That is up to 9 members gone in one round, about 12 to 15 followers across the table and up to 3 from any one player, plus contest swings (Most members pays 10 in round III). No single player gains 10+ in one turn from it; the swing is spread across the table, and each mover pays a member to cause it. Bot test running (200 paired deals, PURIFIERS=leave) to measure how often it moves and what it costs holders and rivals.
