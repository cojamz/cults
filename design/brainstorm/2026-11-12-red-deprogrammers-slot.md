## Idea: Free Riders

1. Printed text: "Whenever you recruit a member for free, +2 followers." (Claude's numbers). Cards it touches: Borrow, Late Shift, Initiation (above); Mentor "On Recruit: recruit the cheapest member in the shop."; Seat Filler "Round Start: you may put this in the shop and recruit a shop member costing up to 3 more, for free."
2. Played turn: round II, 4 players, you hold Free Riders and 2 others. You take Late Shift as your 3rd minion: a 6-cost member free, +2 followers. The best other round II space, Promote, gives +3 red and a member 3 cost better. Gain over Late Shift alone: +2 followers each free recruit; over a game a Red player makes about 4 to 6 free recruits, so 8 to 12 followers.
3. Both sides: no choices on the card.
4. Who feels it: rivals get a reason to take Borrow and Late Shift before you (blocking); Green copying Red's spaces or Blue's free recruits feed it too. No sting.
5. Not refused: no member in the ledger pays free recruits; it is not Proselytizers (cut, paid every recruit) nor Brethren (color match). Card review 2's refused defaults (Long-Lost Cousins, Concerned Parents) are different shapes.
6. Judged: one trigger, one effect, reads in one go; rewards Red's own verb; joins round I by price would pay off Borrow and Late Shift at once, which needs no members held (Nov 10 round I rule is met: it reads recruits, not holdings).
7. Public and certain: yes, every free recruit is public.
8. Color interactions: the shared moment is a free member, which Red, Blue (discard-pile recruits) and grey members cause; names none. In a game with only Red, Borrow, Late Shift and Initiation feed it.
9. Biggest case: round III, Initiation (free 10-cost) plus Mentor's free recruit in the same turn: 2 triggers = 4 followers in one turn; across round III with Late Shift and Borrow too, 4 triggers = 8. A game total of 10 to 14 against a winner's 70 to 110; under the 10-in-a-turn cap.
Verdict: send

## Idea: Organizers

1. Printed text: "Whenever you swap a member, +2 followers." (Claude's numbers). Cards it touches: Promote, Demote (above); Panel "On Use, once per round: promote or demote another of your members."; Body Snatchers "Round Start: swap one of your members with any rival's member."
2. Played turn: round II, 4 players, you hold Organizers and 3 others. You take Promote: +3 red, a 5-cost becomes an 8-cost, +2 followers. Best other round II space, Demote, gives the same without the 2. Gain: +2 followers per swap.
3. Both sides: no choices on the card.
4. Who feels it: the rival whose member you swap away loses it while you are paid; rivals take Promote and Demote first to stop you.
5. Not refused: it was in the Nov 11 all-colors list that Cory sent back unread as rushed ("i suspect you didnt think through these deeply"), not refused on its own; it is not Foster Parents (pays only a rival's member arriving).
6. Judged: one trigger, one effect; pays Red's other verb. Round I: no swap spaces in round I, so it joins round II by price.
7. Public and certain: yes, every swap happens face up on the table.
8. Color interactions: the shared moment is a member changing cults, caused by Red, Magenta (Swindle) and grey Anarchist; Panel and Body Snatchers give Red its own way.
9. Biggest case: round III with Panel, Promote and Demote and Body Snatchers at round start: 4 swaps = 8 followers, at most 4 in one turn (Promote plus Panel). About 12 to 16 a game; under the cap.
Verdict: send

## Idea: Replacements

1. Printed text: "Whenever one of your members is discarded, recruit a shop member of its color costing up to its cost, for free."
2. Played turn: round I end, Borrow's 3-cost is discarded; you take a 3-cost of its color free.
3. Both sides: no choices beyond which member.
4. Who feels it: nobody; a shop member leaves.
5. Not refused: but it is Seat Filler's shape (give one up, get one free).
6. Judged: weak (Borrow takes the cheapest), and round III discards come after the game ends.
7. Public and certain: yes, all face up, nothing from the pile.
8. Color interactions: discards from Blue and White.
9. Biggest case: 2 free cheap members a game, about 4 followers of worth.
Verdict: cut (repeats Seat Filler, small)

## Idea: Twins

1. Printed text: "Counts as 2 members of its color."
2. Played turn: any round, adds 2 to your red group for Red's contest.
3. Both sides: none.
4. Who feels it: the contest race only.
5. Not refused: Triplets was retired for Body Snatchers; vanilla counts read weak (skill, Members).
6. Judged: no ability; Cory wants members that do something or score.
7. Public and certain: yes, all face up, nothing from the pile.
8. Color interactions: Blue's most-members contest.
9. Biggest case: one extra member in two contests, worth up to a 10 swing at round III.
Verdict: cut (vanilla, Triplets retired)
