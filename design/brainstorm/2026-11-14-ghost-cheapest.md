# Brainstorm: Ghost as the free cheapest member (Borrow, Mentor)

Date: 2026-11-14
Asked by Cory: "also, Ghost is often the cheapest for free"
Skill read: bash .claude/hooks/brainstorm-read.sh at 6:45pm Eastern

## What is happening (checked against the live cards, 4 players, 10-card decks)
- Ghost (grey, round II): "Worth 0 members. Whenever this member joins your cult, −5 followers." Costs 5 any. Cory: "ghost should stay 5 cost" (Nov 6) and "ghost and loyalists should always be same price" (Nov 12).
- Every other member that joins in round II or III costs 6 or more (Protesters 6, Mutineers 6, then 7 to 11). Nobody recruits Ghost, so once it is face up in round II or III it sits in the row as the cheapest for the rest of that round; only a leftover round I member costing 4 or 5 beats or ties it.
- Two live cards take "the cheapest" with no choice: Borrow (Red round I space, open all game) "Recruit the cheapest shop member, for free. It returns to the shop at round end, before scoring. +1 red." and Mentor (Red member, 5 red + 3 any) "On Recruit: recruit the cheapest member in the shop." With Ghost face up, Borrow in round II/III reads "+1 red, −5 followers", and recruiting Mentor costs you 5 followers.

## Idea: Two Cheapest

1. Printed text: Borrow "Recruit one of the 2 cheapest shop members, for free. It returns to the shop at round end, before scoring. +1 red." Mentor "On Recruit: recruit one of the 2 cheapest shop members." (Claude's wording; ties at the second price are all choices). Cards it touches: Ghost "Worth 0 members. Whenever this member joins your cult, −5 followers."; Promote "+3 red. Swap any member for one costing up to 3 more, from another cult or the shop."; Demote "+3 red. Swap any member for one costing up to 3 less, from another cult or the shop."
2. Played turn: 4 players, round II, row of 7: Ghost 5, Banker 7, Brethren 7, Clan 8, Recruiter 8, Tycoon 9, Con Men 9. Today Borrow gives Ghost: +1 red and −5 followers, so nobody takes it except for the red. With this text you take Banker ("On Recruit: +3 gold. Round Start: +3 gold.") on loan: +3 gold, +1 red, and a 7-cost member to Promote into a 10-cost one. Best other round I red space, Gather "+3 red. If you recruit a member this turn of a color you already have: +4 followers. +1 red.": about 4 red. Gain: from −5 followers to about +4 resources, level with Gather.
3. Both sides: the taker picks between two members; Ghost is never forced, and when the cheapest is a real member both picks are live (On Recruit now vs a pricier body to Promote). No rival chooses.
4. Who feels it: the rival who wanted the second-cheapest member loses it for the round (a reason to recruit it first); Red players see a real pick instead of Ghost.
5. Not refused: Cory picked Borrow's "cheapest" Nov 10 and Open Door's growing-cap versions were declined Oct 27; this keeps cheapest and only widens it to two. No ledger line on Ghost or Borrow choices.
6. Judged: one plain verb on the shop row, a felt choice, same strength band as round I Gather in round I (where Ghost isn't in the deck it changes little: a choice between the two cheapest round I members, costs 4 to 6).
7. Public and certain: reads the face-up row only.
8. Color interactions: none new; Red's existing Borrow-then-Promote line keeps working.
9. Biggest case: round III, row of 8, the two cheapest are rarely above 7 (costs run 4 to 11, only 3 members cost 11). Worst real case: Borrow a 9-cost Con Men ("On Recruit: steal 5 resources") = 5 resources, or Borrow then Promote into a 10-cost member kept for good; Green copying Borrow takes the other of the two. About 5 resources or one member a turn, far under the 10-follower line and a contest's 4/6/10.
Verdict: send

## Idea: Cheapest Colored

1. Printed text: Borrow "Recruit the cheapest colored shop member, for free. It returns to the shop at round end, before scoring. +1 red." Mentor "On Recruit: recruit the cheapest colored member in the shop." (grey members, like Ghost, are never taken). Cards it touches: Ghost (above); grey guests such as Line Cutters "You go first every round." (4).
2. Played turn: 4 players, round II, same row (Ghost 5, Banker 7, Brethren 7…): you take Banker or Brethren (tie, your pick) instead of Ghost: +3 gold or a red member, +1 red, against Gather's about 4 red. Gain: from −5 followers to about even with Gather.
3. Both sides: no choice except ties, as today.
4. Who feels it: as today, the player who wanted that colored member; grey members are never borrowed.
5. Not refused: no ledger line on colored-only picks; keeps Cory's one-target Borrow.
6. Judged: smallest change, still one target, reads at a glance (grey cards are visibly grey). Loses Borrowing the cheap grey rule-benders (Line Cutters, Dabblers) in round I.
7. Public and certain: reads only the face-up row, nothing hidden or random.
8. Color interactions: none; grey counts as a color for Red's contest, so "colored" needs the cards' grey look to be obvious.
9. Biggest case: round III, cheapest colored member of 8 is 4 to 7 (Blue/Green Recruit 4 up to Elders 7): one member or an On Recruit like Banker's +3 gold; Green copying takes the next one. Under 5 resources a turn.
Verdict: send

## Idea: Ghost only stings from a rival

1. Printed text: Ghost "Worth 0 members. Whenever this member joins your cult from another cult, −5 followers."
2. Played turn: round II Borrow still gives Ghost (+1 red, nothing else); the row is just as samey.
3. Both sides: no choice.
4. Who feels it: Borrow Ghost, then Promote it onto a rival: they lose 5 followers and you take their member, for no cost to you. Too strong a sting.
5. Not refused: new, but doesn't answer "often the cheapest".
6. Judged: fixes the loss, not the sameness.
7. Public and certain: yes.
8. Color interactions: Red swaps.
9. Biggest case: round III, Borrow + Promote twice a round: −10 followers to rivals for free.
Verdict: cut (Borrow still always lands on Ghost; turns Borrow + Promote into a free −5 on a rival)
