Patterns across the table

Cult shape. Four contests are the shape of your cult: Blue more, White fewer, Red deeper (one color), Yellow wider (many colors). Blue × White and Red × Yellow are the two head-on fights.
What you hold. Magenta wants the biggest pile, Silver the least silver. Every Silver gift fattens a rival for Magenta; every Magenta steal of silver helps Silver.
The board. Purple wins by placing the round's last minion, Green by having minions on Green spaces. Every color that changes standings in one move (Red swaps, Magenta steals, Silver gifts, White cast-outs) wants the last turn, so Purple touches all of them.
Taking can be giving. Magenta taking a member helps White's Fewest members. Magenta taking silver helps Silver's Fewest silver.
Gaps. Red × Silver, Blue × Silver and Yellow × Purple barely touch. The discard pile is filled by Blue (from the shop) and White (from cults), and nothing ever takes from it.
