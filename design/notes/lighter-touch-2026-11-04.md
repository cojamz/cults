# Member changes: what to keep, what to pull back

"Taken" means how often a player owned the card in games where it showed up. "Owners won" is how often that player won; 25% is an even share at 4 players. Each line is 120 Hard bot games on the 10-card deck, with bots that can see what the new cards do.

## Live now: keep (they tested fair)
- **Spoilsport** taken 64%, owners won 35% (the strongest of these, worth watching)
- **Hostesses** 75%, 33%
- **Body Snatchers** 48%, 33%
- **Misers** 53%, 23%
- **Kidnapper** 93%, 33%
- **Impersonator** 89%, 32%
- **Godparent** 70%, 25%
- **Creditors** 49%, 24%: fair in bot games, but only because bots hoard silver. Cory said no, so it gets replaced.

## Pause
- The next 4 (Night Watch, Second-Story Man, Hawker, Sore Loser) wait until Cory has played the ones above.

## Pull the rule reworks back
- Of the 8 reworks under "no card depends on a rival holding more", only **High Society** ("Game End: +2 followers per rival with more silver than you.") checks at game end, when nobody holds anything. The other 7 check during play, when rivals do hold resources, and they work as printed. So: rework High Society, and leave Underdog, Lottery Winner, Hostesses, Upstart, Cutpurse, Extortionist and Safecracker alone.

## Smaller versions of the open picks
- **Fan Club**: rename it **Onlookers** and keep the text "On Recruit: +1 green per Green space with anyone's minion."
- **Melting Pot**: change the words only, same effect (option A in the earlier file).

## Full text of every card named above
- **Spoilsport** (guest): "Round End: choose a contest you don't lead; nobody wins it this round."
- **Hostesses** (Silver): "On Recruit and Round Start: give each rival 1 silver. +1 follower per rival who then holds more silver than you."
- **Body Snatchers** (Red): "Round Start: swap one of your members with a rival's member of the same cost."
- **Misers** (White): "Game End: +1 follower per 2 white you hold."
- **Kidnapper** (Magenta): "On Recruit and Round Start: steal the cheapest member of the rival with the most members."
- **Impersonator** (Green): "On Recruit and Round Start: choose a rival's member. Until you choose again, Impersonator has that member's ability."
- **Godparent** (Silver): "On Use, once per game: give one of your members to a rival. Gain followers equal to its cost."
- **Underdog** (Gold): "While a rival has more gold than you, double your gold gains."
- **Lottery Winner** (Gold): "On Use, once per game: gain gold equal to the most gold a rival holds."
- **Upstart** (Silver): "On Recruit: gain half the silver of the rival with the most, rounded up."
- **Cutpurse** (Magenta): "On Recruit: steal 1 resource from the rival with the most resources. Round Start: steal 1 resource from the rival with the most resources."
- **Extortionist** (Magenta): "On Recruit: steal 2 resources from the rival with the most resources. Round Start: steal 2 resources from the rival with the most resources."
- **Safecracker** (Magenta): "On Use, once per game: steal half the magenta of the rival with the most, rounded down."
- **Night Watch** (Purple): "Whenever a rival takes the Goes Last token, +1 follower per minion on it."
- **Second-Story Man** (Magenta): "Round End: before contests are scored, steal 2 resources from one rival."
- **Hawker** (Blue): "On Use, once per round: choose a shop member. Starting on your left, each rival may recruit it, paying its cost plus 2 to you. If none does, you recruit it for free."
- **Sore Loser** (guest): "Round End: if you win no contest, steal 3 followers from the rival who wins the most."
