# Silver members: three open slots (Nov 5)

Cory, in the playtest-notes thread: "we may need more brainstorming here. dedicated thread." Silver lost Charity Ball, Creditor and Hostesses. Ideas only; the deck changes belong to the playtest-notes thread.

## Gate 2: what silver is for
Rulebook: "Silver gives rivals gifts that pay you back." Anchor: **Largesse** ("Give rivals up to 10 silver from the bank, split as you choose. +1 follower per silver you give.").
His words that steer this list:
- Nov 4: "there should be more stuff where you get things per, like, you know, with people having silver. Silver minions should count for, like, how many silver minions there are."
- Nov 3: "nobody would ever have extra silver laying around. you just buy followers." So a card that needs rivals to sit on silver is weak.
- Nov 4: no member that pops a choice when its owner takes an ordinary action. Nothing pays by how much more rivals hold.
- Nov 5 notes: Socialites "more interesting than High Society because others can play around it"; wants "a bit more 'when rivals do something' in members"; Charity Ball, Hostesses, Creditor out. All three were automatic silver gifts with a fixed follower payout. Nobody at the table decides anything, so new ideas avoid that shape.

Silver's deck after the cuts (7): Godparent and Guest of Honor (give members away), Toastmaster and High Society (Silver spaces taken), Debutantes (double your silver), Smooth Talkers (silver pays any color), Debt Collectors (take silver). Missing: receiving gifts, Silver's own minions counted, giving a minion, the silver contest.

## Gate 3: seeds (about 20), cut on paper
Cut: Round End dump of any silver (that's Patronesses, "round end way too strong"); rival chooses silver or give you followers (Creditor, and dominated); rival may trade 1 resource for 3 silver (Potluck shape, disliked); gift to the trailer each Round Start (Hostesses shape); give a shop member (Grand Gift / Gift Shop, "haven't hit the right silver theme"); per-member-given game end (memory); Heirloom, a member that pays when it changes cults (same piece as Well-Wishers, Godparent and Guest of Honor; silver has two already); Round End +1 per Silver minion (High Society again); hold no silver for +3 (nobody feels it); a rival who wins the silver contest pays you (fires nearly every round, no choice).

## What survived (prices are Claude's fill-ins)

### 1. Freeloaders [3 silver + 2 any, worth 1]
"Whenever you're gifted silver, +1 follower per silver."
- Played, round III, 4 players. Ann has Freeloaders. Ben takes **Toast** ("+9 silver in round III. Each rival +3 silver.") and Ann gets +3 silver and +3 followers. **High Society** ("Whenever a rival takes a Silver space, +1 follower.") would pay her +1 there. Cara takes **Largesse** and splits her 10 between Ben and Dee instead of giving Ann any.
- Choices: whoever takes Toast now feeds Ann +3 followers, so they weigh skipping it. Largesse and **Party Gifts** ("Give rivals up to 3 silver from the bank, split as you choose. +2 followers per silver you give.") givers steer around Ann. **Debutantes** ("Each rival +2 silver") pays her +2.
- Who feels it: every silver giver. A reason to skip Toast.
- Intuitive: one sentence, "gifted" is already on Caterer. Fun: the giver has to route around you. Interacts: with all four of silver's giving spaces, Debutantes and Usher. Powerful: up to 3 followers per Toast, more if a giver can't avoid you. Engine or counter: a counter to silver's gifts. Its color: the first card for receiving gifts.
- Closest past card: Hangers-On ("Whenever a rival gives you something, +1 more of it."), retired after a bot sweep, not by Cory's no.

### 2. Ballroom [4 silver + 3 any, worth 2] (from Cory's Nov 4 idea)
"On Use, once per game: +2 followers per minion on Silver spaces."
- Played, round III, 4 players: 6 Silver spaces are open. Late in the round 4 are taken (2 yours, 2 rivals'), and you use it: +8 followers. In round I, with 3 spaces, the most is +6.
- Choices: you pick the moment. Wait for more minions, but a rival may stay off Silver spaces to keep it small.
- Who feels it: rivals who would take Toast late now pay you +2 for it. A reason to block.
- vs High Society: that one pays +1 for each rival placement over the game. Ballroom counts yours too and pays once, in a burst.
- Intuitive: count the minions on one sphere. Fun: the table sees the burst coming. Interacts: rivals' placements. Powerful: about +8 to +12. Engine or counter: rewards filling your own sphere, with Toastmaster. Its color: his "silver minions count" idea.

### 3. Plus-One [4 silver + 2 any, worth 1]
"On Use, once per game: give a rival one of your minions for the rest of the round. +6 followers."
- Played, round III, 4 players: you have 5 minions. At the start of the round you hand one to Dee, the player furthest behind. Dee places 6 minions this round and you place 4. You get +6 followers now. On the table, the minion goes back to its owner when minions come home.
- Choices: which rival gets an extra space and recruit. The leader never gets it, so it's a real gift aimed at someone. You trade a turn of your own for 6 followers.
- Who feels it: the rival who gets it, and whoever they then compete with for spaces.
- Risk: Purple's +1 minion is close; the rival gets about a turn's worth (one space and one recruit). "Hands rivals as much as you get" flopped before, so it's +6, not +4.
- Intuitive: hand over a minion. Fun: a big gift the table sees. Interacts: with spaces and turn order. Powerful: 6 followers and a whole extra turn for a rival. Engine or counter: neither, a spike. Its color: the first card that gives a minion.

### 4. Kingmaker [3 silver + 2 any, worth 1]
"Round End: give a rival 3 silver from the bank. +3 followers."
- Played, round II, 4 players: the silver contest (most silver, +6) is Ben 5, Cara 4. At round end you give Cara 3: she wins with 7 and Ben gets nothing. Or you give Dee 3 to lift a trailing cult. You get +3 followers either way.
- Choices: every round you pick who wins or loses the silver contest.
- Who feels it: everyone counting their silver for that contest. Ben's lead isn't safe.
- vs Usher ("On Recruit: give a rival 2 silver from the bank. +3 followers."): Usher gives once; Kingmaker gives at the contest every round, so the gift lands where it decides something.
- Intuitive: one sentence. Fun: a swing at scoring. Interacts: with the silver contest, Largesse and Toast. Powerful: picks a +4/+6/+10 winner. Engine or counter: counters the silver hoarder. Its color: the first silver card that touches the silver contest.
- Kingmaking is the risk; flag it.

## Round 2 (Cory: "we need an idea that's more about communal silver"; "these should be MEMBERS not buildings or things")
Ballroom is renamed to people: **Ballroom Dancers** (Claude's fill-in; plural, so counts as 2, as priced). Kingmakers already people.
Cut on paper: Chaperone ("Round Start: +1 follower per rival holding silver", in his archive pile), Silver Tongues (his no), a pot on the card that collects spent silver (nobody loses anything, so nobody feels it), give-and-take-back silver (nets zero).

### A. Bartenders [4 silver + 2 any, worth 2]
"Rivals may pay with your silver as if it were theirs. Whenever they do, +1 follower per silver."
- Played, round II, 4 players: you hold 6 silver. Dee, behind, buys 5 followers with 3 of yours and 2 of hers: you +3 followers, she saved 3. Ben, leading, pays his own way so you get nothing from him.
- Both sides (rival): free resource for them vs +1 follower for you, one for one. Behind players take it, leaders don't. A real choice. Yours: hold silver for the rivals to drink from or spend it.
- Who feels it: every rival on every recruit. Your silver pile is the table's.
- Intuitive: one sentence. Fun: a rival decides whether to help themselves and you. Interacts: everyone's recruits; Toast fills your pile. Powerful: a few followers a round with no minion. Engine or counter: engine with Toast, Debutantes. Its color: silver literally shared.
- Risk: you'll rarely win the silver contest; fine for a giver.

### B. Commune Elders [5 silver + 3 any, worth 2]
"On Use, once per game: every player puts their silver together, then it's split evenly; you keep what's left over. +1 follower per player."
- Played, round III, before round end, 4 players: Ben 8 (about to win the +10), Cara 2, Dee 0, you 2. Pool 12, 3 each. Ben drops to 3; all four tie, each gets +5 from the silver contest. You +4 followers, and the +5 instead of 0.
- Choice: when. Hoarders feel it; empty cults are glad.
- Who feels it: the silver leader (sting), the broke (gift).
- Intuitive: pool and split. Fun: the whole table's silver moves. Interacts: the silver contest, Toast, Largesse. Powerful: can swing a +10. Engine or counter: counter to hoarding. Its color: sharing out of everyone's pocket.

### C. Revelers [3 silver + 2 any, worth 2]
"Round End: if every player holds silver, each player +2 followers, and you +2 more."
- Played, round II, 4 players: everyone holds some silver: you +4, each rival +2. Ben, leading, can spend his last silver to stop it, giving up his own +2 to deny you 4.
- Both sides (rival): keep 1 silver (+2 each, and the silver contest needs at least 1 to score) or spend down to deny you. Usually keep; the leader may spoil.
- Who feels it: everyone; a shared toast with one possible spoiler.
- Intuitive: one count at round end. Fun: a table goal with a spoiler. Interacts: everyone's silver; Toast makes sure all have some. Powerful: +4 a round for you. Engine or counter: engine with Toast. Its color: the whole table celebrating together.
- Risk: it hands the table followers (shapes that give rivals as much flopped); you get double.

## Round 3 (Cory: "something that scales with number of silver members players have or resources total. not just yours but the whole board")
### A. Patron Saints [4 silver + 3 any, worth 2]
"Round End: +1 follower per silver member, anyone's."
- Played, round III, 4 players: you have 3 silver members, Ben 1, Cara 1: +5. About 2 + 4 + 5 = 11 a game.
- Rivals' choice: a silver card in the shop now also feeds you; they weigh buying it. Who feels it: anyone recruiting silver.
- Intuitive: one count. Fun: every silver recruit at the table pays you. Interacts: all of silver's members, anyone's. Powerful: about 11. Engine: silver members. Its color: the table's silver.
- Close to the old Patron Saint ("Game End: +1 follower per 2 silver members, anyone's.", never pitched to him as a round-end card).
### B. Silver Barons [4 silver + 2 any, worth 2]
"Round End: +1 follower per 3 silver all players hold together."
- Played, round II, 4 players: you 4, Ben 5 (going for the silver contest), Cara 2, Dee 1 = 12: +4. Toast and Largesse raise it.
- Rivals' choice: hold silver for the contest (feeds you) or spend it (denies you). Real both ways.
- Intuitive: count the table's silver. Fun: the contest race pays you. Interacts: every silver holder, Toast, Largesse. Powerful: 3 to 6 a round. Engine: silver's gifts. Its color: the table's silver.
### C. Auctioneer [5 silver + 3 any, worth 2]
"On Use, once per game: +1 follower per 2 silver all players hold together."
- Played, round III: right after a Largesse hands out 10, the table holds 20: +10. Earlier, about +5.
- Choice: when. Rivals who see it coming spend down.
- Intuitive: one count, once. Fun: a burst the table can see coming. Interacts: Largesse, Toast, the silver contest. Powerful: up to about 10. Engine: Largesse. Its color: the table's silver.
