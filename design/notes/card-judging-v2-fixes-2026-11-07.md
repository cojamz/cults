# Card judging, version 2: fixes after the held-out test (Nov 7, 10:05pm Eastern)

Applies on top of design/notes/card-judging-v2-2026-11-07.md (not edited; Cory's page not edited). Why: run 3 (design/eval/card-judging/run3-result.md) matched Cory's pick on 8 of 17 unseen calls; readers agreed with each other on 10 of 17, so the misses were systematic. Each fix below answers one kind of miss. Nothing here quotes a held-out call, so the next test stays blind.

## Fix 1. The current card is the default answer
Step 4 already says the current card stays unless a replacement is clearly better. Readers still swapped three times. So, for any slot that has a current card:
- Start from "keep it". To replace it, write the current card's best turn and the new card's best turn, both with numbers, in the same round. The new card must make a bigger or more distinct moment, named in one sentence.
- "Different" is not "better". A new card that adds a second step, a bonus, or a gift to a rival is not better because it does more.
- If you can't name what players would miss about the current card, say so; if you can, that's a reason to keep it.

## Fix 2. Play the top case with numbers
The moment question asks for the top case. Readers described it instead of playing it. So:
- Write the top case as a turn: round III, 4 players, the owner built around this card (e.g. 4 or 5 of the members or resources it counts), used every chance. Give the number it pays.
- Compare that number with the best round III spaces and with the follower cards a player could buy instead. If it is more than about twice the best alternative, the card is too strong and is weak until fixed, however good its usual turn is.
- Any card that pays "per" something (per member, per color, per resource) always gets this played out.

## Fix 3. Who chooses, and when
The hard rule is about another player having to decide something during your turn, or you during theirs. It is not about a card that affects rivals.
- Breaks it: "Each rival chooses: give you 1 resource or you steal 2" (each rival decides on your turn). "Whenever a rival takes two turns in a row, you may skip the second" (you decide on their turn).
- Fine: "Give rivals up to 3 of your silver, split as you choose" (you decide, on your turn). "Each rival +1 silver" (nobody decides).
- Before ruling a card out on this rule, name the player who decides and whose turn it is.

## Fix 4. "None of these" is always an answer
For any list, "none of these" is a real option. Pick it when every option is weak or muddy, or when the best one doesn't beat the current card. A list of weak options gets "none", not the least weak.

## Readers
Readers are told not to use rules that aren't in the method, the rulebook or the call itself (in run 3, one reader applied a naming rule from outside).
