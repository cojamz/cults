# How the 8 colors touch each other (Nov 7)

For Cory's ask (Nov 7, 8:23pm Eastern): "Start making an interaction map. Like, which colors interact with which other colors in which ways… a general relational mapping between the colors. And we need to figure out where there are gaps and where we could maybe stitch things together a little closer."
Read from the live cards (live-board.mjs --members, By color contests) on Nov 7. Picture: the "Cults color map" artifact.

## Each color, toward everyone
- Red: swaps members with any cult or the shop.
- Magenta: steals from anyone.
- Silver: gives to every rival.
- Yellow: wants every color; changes rivals' piles.
- Blue: reshapes the shop everyone buys from.
- Green: copies anyone's spaces and members.
- Purple: skips or jumps anyone's turn.
- White: keeps to itself (casts out its own members, turns its resources into followers).

## Ties between two colors (strength 1 to 3)
- Red ⇄ Magenta, 3, clash: both pull members out of rivals' cults (Promote, Demote, Body Snatchers / Poach, Shake Down, Kidnapper). Foster Parents and Deprogrammers fire on Magenta's takes; Loyalists stops Red's swaps.
- Silver → Red, 2, feeds: Guest of Honor and Godparent hand members to rivals; Foster Parents +2 each time one lands.
- Silver → Magenta, 2, feeds: gifts fill rivals' piles, which Magenta steals from and Magenta's contest (most resources) counts.
- Silver ⇄ Yellow, 2, feeds and overlap: silver is one more color for Yellow's full sets; Counterfeiters and Leveler can wipe a Silver player's silver lead; Polish, Sweet Talk and Smooth Talkers do Yellow's job (turning resources into other colors).
- Blue → Red, 2, feeds or stings: Peek stacks the pile Walk-In draws from; Blue's discards reshape the shop that Promote, Demote, Initiation and Tryout pick from.
- Blue → Yellow, 1, stings: Out of Style empties one color from the shop, the new color Housewarming and New Blood want.
- Red → White, 2, feeds: Recluse is paid by wide cults; Martyrs pays White when a swap takes it.
- Magenta ⇄ White, 2, clash: Martyrs pays when stolen; Heist's follower steal helps the victim toward White's contest (fewest followers).
- Blue → White, 1, feeds: Recluse is paid by Blue's wide cults (Blue's contest is most members); both send members to the discard pile.
- Green ⇄ Red, 2, copies: Tryout is a copy (a Green verb on a Red space); Sample, Impersonator, Impostors and Greatest Hits copy On Recruits, which Red's recruit-heavy members are full of.
- Green → Silver, 1, copies: copying a Silver gift space gifts the rivals too (Nov 6 call).
- Green ⇄ Purple, 1, copies: Echo and Understudy copy the previous action; Purple's spaces decide whose action that is.
- Purple ⇄ White, 1, same goal: Late Risers and White's contest both want the fewest followers (Magenta's Desperado too).
- Yellow ⇄ Magenta, 1, clash: a Magenta steal picks colors and can break a full set; both contests reward big piles.
- Yellow ⇄ Red, 1: Duty Collector pays when rivals recruit colors you hold, and Red recruits most.

## Gaps (no card ties them)
Purple with Red, Magenta, Blue, Silver, Yellow. Blue with Silver, Magenta, Green. White with Yellow, Green, Silver. Green with Magenta, Yellow.
Biggest: Purple touches everyone's turn order, but no other color's card cares about turns, and no Purple card cares about another color. Second: Green copies everyone, but only Green's own cards (Agents, Bait) care about being copied.

## Proposals (pitching.md checks, written; numbers are Claude's fill-ins)

### 1. Flatterers (new Silver member), Green ⇄ Silver
Prints: [3 silver + 3 any, worth 1] "Whenever a rival copies one of your actions, you and they each +2 followers."
Touches: Echo ("Copy the previous action. +1 green."), Reprise ("Copy an action with your minion. +1 green."), Cover ("Copy an action with no minion on it. +1 green."), Song Request ("On Use, once per round: copy an action a rival has a minion on."), Bait ("Whenever anyone copies one of your actions, they −1 follower and you +1 follower.").
Played: round II, 4 players. Ana (Silver, Flatterers) takes Sweet Talk ("+3 silver. Then turn as many of your silver as you want into other colors."). Ben (Green) is next: Echo copies Sweet Talk (+3 silver, convert, +1 green) and both get +2 followers. Ben's other choice, Build Up (+4 green), gives no followers; Echo here is +2 followers better. Ana gets +2 for a turn she already took.
Choices: none.
Who feels it: Green now has a reason to copy Silver's spaces first; Cal and Dee watch two rivals feed each other and want to take Sweet Talk's neighbour space first. A gift, Silver's theme.
Ledger: not refused. Mirrors Bait (Green's own counter) with Silver's generosity.
Principles: intuitive (one sentence, "copies one of your actions" is already Bait's words); fun (a gift both ways); interacts (Green, and every copy card); feels powerful (moderate); engine with Toastmaster and High Society; new way into Silver (being copied as a gift).

### 2. Pickpockets (new Magenta member), Magenta ⇄ Purple
Prints: [3 magenta + 3 any, worth 1] "Whenever a rival's turn is skipped, steal 1 follower from them."
Touches: Hold Up ("+2 purple in round I, +3 in round II, +4 in round III. Whoever's turn is next skips it, even yours."), Bouncers ("On Recruit: the next two rivals' turns are skipped."), Gossip ("Whenever a player's turn is skipped, +1 follower.").
Played: round I, 4 players. Ana (Purple) takes Hold Up; Ben's next turn is skipped. Cal (Magenta, Pickpockets) steals 1 follower from Ben. Bouncers later skips two rivals: Cal steals 2. Over a game with 3 to 4 skips, about 3 to 4 followers taken.
Choices: none (a follower, so no colors to pick on another player's turn).
Who feels it: the skipped player twice; Ana now weighs who sits next before she takes Hold Up. Purple's skips become a Magenta player's income.
Ledger: Hall Porters ("Whenever a rival's turn is skipped, steal 2 purple from them.") was a paper idea in Purple (Nov 4), never pitched to Cory. Not refused.
Principles: intuitive; fun (a sting stacked on a sting); interacts (Purple's skips); powerful (modest, fill-in 1); counters nothing, builds with Robin Hood; new way into Magenta (stealing from someone already down).

### 3. Last-Minute Shopper (Blue, small change), Blue ⇄ Purple
Live: [3 blue + 2 any, worth 1] "Round End: +3 blue, then you may recruit."
Proposed: "Round End: +3 blue, then you may recruit. If you placed the round's last minion, recruit twice."
Touches: Sleep In ("+4 purple in round I, +5 in round II, +6 in round III. Take the Goes Last token: your next minion is placed after everyone else's."), Last Call, Overtime, Night Managers ("Round End: if you placed the round's last minion, +1 follower per purple member you have."), Purple's contest (whoever placed the last minion).
Played: round II, 4 players. Dee (Blue, Last-Minute Shopper) takes Sleep In over Recruit Twice (+3 blue, one extra recruit now). She places the round's last minion: round end, +3 blue and two recruits. Ana (Purple) loses Purple's contest to her.
Choices: none.
Who feels it: Purple has a rival for going last; Blue players take Purple spaces.
Ledger: Cory set this card Nov 5 ("Last Minute Shopper should just be recruiting"): risk, he simplified it on purpose.
Principles: intuitive (the name already says last minute); fun; interacts (Purple); powerful (an extra recruit); engine with Power Shoppers ("Whenever you recruit your second member in a turn, +4 followers."); new way into Blue (timing your shopping).

### 4. Patroness (Blue, one word), Blue ⇄ White
Live: [6 blue + 4 any, worth 1] "Whenever shop members go to the discard pile, +1 follower."
Proposed: "Whenever members go to the discard pile, +1 follower." (drop "shop")
Touches: Farewell ("Cast out one of your members to the discard pile, gain followers equal to its cost. +1 white."), Estate Sale ("Cast out one of your members to the discard pile, gain white equal to its cost. +1 white."), Downsizers ("On Use, once per round: cast out one of your members to the discard pile. +4 white.").
Played: round II, 4 players. Eve (White) takes Farewell and casts out a 6-cost member: +6 followers. Dee's Patroness: +1 follower.
Choices: none.
Who feels it: a small gift from White to Blue. The weakest of the four; it only joins two colors that already share the discard pile.
Ledger: not refused.
Principles: intuitive (shorter text); fun (small); interacts (White); powerful (no); new way (no). Sent as the smallest stitch.

## Cut before sending
- Purple member that skips a rival who recruits twice (stings Blue): adds an out-of-turn skip state that's hard to track; Bouncers already skips.
- Estate Sale paying the cast-out member's own color: changes White's fuel, not a tie.
- Blue ⇄ Magenta ideas: every shape steals colors on another player's turn (needs a choice then).
