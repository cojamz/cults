# Color review: Purple, second pass (Nov 7)

Cory, 9:14pm: Right Away and Closer yes ("Closer… really strong… should be quite expensive"); "purple should basically be like battling for a token. You kind of want the last turn token"; "we just need more ways to swap them around… every color has a way to move them around"; "you're letting a lot of actions and members lie that could like be redone".
Purple, rethought as a tug of war over the Goes Last token. Today three spaces take it (Sleep In, Last Call, Overtime) and two cards pay for holding it (Sleepyheads, Night Managers; Pocket Watch is the cloak). Nothing takes it back between those spaces, nothing hands it on, and nothing pays the player who loses it. Numbers are Claude's fill-ins.

## Closer, priced up
"When you place the round's last minion, do that space's action again." Fill-in 6 purple + 5 any as a player sees it (Extra Shift's band), from 3 + 4.

## Go-Getter → take the token
Live: [5 purple + 4 any] "On Use, once per game: +1 minion, placed right after this turn." (third "+1 minion" card beside Extra Shift and Overtime)
Proposed: "On Use, once per round: take the Goes Last token."
Played: round II, 4 players. Ana took Last Call ("+1 purple. +1 follower and +1 purple per minion you have left. Take the Goes Last token: your other minions are placed after everyone else's.") and was paid for 3 minions left. On his next turn Ben uses Go-Getter: the token is his, Ana's minions go back to normal turns, and Ben's last minion is the round's last (with Closer, it does its space twice; Night Managers pays).
Choices: when to use it. Early, a rival may take it back with Sleep In or Overtime; late, the good spaces are gone.
Who feels it: whoever holds the token; the reason to race for it.
Intuitive: one sentence, the token is on the table. Fun: a snatch everyone sees. Interacts: the token fight, every color's last space. Powerful: the last minion each round. Engine or counter: counter to Closer and Last Call holders; engine with Sleepyheads, Night Managers. Its color: taking the token, new.
Price: up (fill-in 6 purple + 4 any).

## Courier → hand the token on
Live: [4 purple + 4 any] "Whenever you take a consecutive turn, +1 of each other color." (the every-color gain is Yellow's job)
Proposed: "Whenever you take a consecutive turn, give the Goes Last token to any player."
Played: round II. Cal takes Crunch Time ("+2 purple. Your next two turns come right after this one."). Courier fires: Dee is about to take the last good Toast; Cal gives her the token, so her next minion waits until everyone else is done and the space is gone. Another round he keeps it for himself for Closer.
Choices: yourself (go last: Closer, Night Managers, Purple's contest) or a rival (delay them past the space they need). Both win in different spots.
Who feels it: the rival handed the token; the holder losing it.
Intuitive: one sentence. Fun: a sting or a grab. Interacts: whatever space the rival needed. Powerful: yes, often. Engine or counter: engine with Right Away, Crunch Time, Lookouts. Its color: moving the token, new.

## Late Risers → catch up with the token
Live: [5 purple + 4 any, worth 2, counts as 2] "Round Start: if you have the fewest followers (ties count), +1 minion, placed before anyone else."
Proposed: "Round Start: if you have the fewest followers (ties count), take the Goes Last token: your last minion is placed after everyone else's."
Played: round III start, Eve trails. She takes the token: her fifth minion goes after everyone's, wins Purple's contest (whoever placed the last minion) unless someone takes it from her.
Choices: none. Who feels it: the leaders, who have to spend a turn on Sleep In or Overtime to take it back.
Intuitive. Fun: the trailing player starts the round holding the prize. Interacts: the token fight. Powerful: moderate. Engine or counter: catch-up. Its color: the token as catch-up, new.

## Keep, with reasons
Spaces: Sleep In, Last Call, Overtime (the three ways to take the token); Crunch Time (Cory: keep as is); Hold Up (the skip every player feels; feeds Gossip and Bouncers). Right Away changed (yes).
Members: Extra Shift (now Purple's only "+1 minion"), Bouncers, Gossip (skips), Lookouts, Hot Streak (turns in a row), Sleepyheads, Night Managers (paid for the token), Timekeeper (go-deep, Cory's ask Oct 24), Picket Line (new Nov 6).
