# Yellow's rearranging spaces: why they feel useless, and fixes (Nov 2)

Cory, Nov 2: "the yellow rearrange resources feels pretty bad" / "more that i never really wanted to take them. never felt useful."

## Data (balance-1.0 merged runs, 4 players, tables with Yellow: Normal 377, Hard 203, Very Hard 188 games)
Share of the rounds a space is open that someone took it, rank within its ring:
- Trade Seeds 61% (23rd of 24 round I spaces; VH 59%, 23rd). Dilute 79% (17th). Harvest 95% (10th).
- Big Dilute 100% (4th of 16 round II). Melting Pot 98%.
- Makeover 55% (6th of 8 round III; VH 78%, 7th).
- Takers' win rate: 25-29% everywhere (seat base 25%). No yellow space moves winning.
The "strong" memory: AI game g10, Party Crasher joined Makeover on the last placement of round III and won three contests (+30).

## Why
Follower cards take any colors. Color matters only for a member's colored part and for round-end contests. Harvest already gives +3/+4/+5 in colors you choose, so Trade Seeds' swaps (and the +2 instead of +3) buy nothing Harvest doesn't. Dilute leaves rivals' totals untouched, so it only stings a contest lead or an exact colored part, which nobody sees.

## Seeds played (4 players)
1. Makeover: "Rearrange your resources into any mix of colors. +2 followers per contest you lead after." Round III, you hold 15 (6Y 4R 3G 2P); rivals' bests R5 G6 P4 Y3. Rearrange to R6 P5 Y4: lead 3 -> +6 now, and +30 at round end if nobody answers. Best other round III space ~10 (Largesse). Rivals feel it: three contest leads to answer, a reason to take it first. Choice: when to take it (late = fewer answers, risk it's gone), which contests to aim at. Greatest Hits copy: rearrange again, followers again, capped by contests (8 at most). Survives. Not refused (Win Now was "gain the prize of a contest you lead now", parked as too strong in round III; this pays 2, not 10).
2. Dilute: "+2 yellow, then take up to 3 of rivals' resources, giving back 1 of yours of another color for each." Round I: you need 2 gold for a member's colored part, rivals hold gold; you take 2 gold and hand back 2 yellow. Rival keeps their count but loses the color they were saving. vs Harvest +3 any: Harvest makes colors from nothing, this takes the specific color a rival holds and hurts their contest or member plan. Both sides: you choose what to give (your least useful), they get it. Survives as Trade Seeds-for-rivals (Cory's Oct 30 origin). Not a steal (counts unchanged).
3. Trade Seeds: cut and replace with a space that doesn't rearrange (redundant with Harvest). Pitch 4-5 if picked.
Cut: "+1 per swap" (Plant shape, refused Oct 30 small ball); "biggest color becomes yellow" (no choice; Leveler's text); "yellow pays any colored part this turn" (cut Nov 1, no rival feels it).

## Trade Seeds replacements (Cory, 7:56pm: "let's see ideas for 3")
Slot: Yellow round I beside Harvest (+3/+4/+5 in colors you choose) and Dilute. Yellow: "rewards going broad… translate currencies"; "Yellow could potentially touch rivals". Round III is now Makeover, paid per contest led, so round I can feed contests. Bar: Harvest +3 in round I.
Seeds considered and cut: Rainmaker-lite "raise your fewest color to your yellow, at most 2/3/4" (nobody at the table feels it); "+1 of each color you hold" (Caravan's text, and Square Up as a member); "+1 of a color per color you hold none of" (Mixed Bag, refused Oct 30); "+1 follower per color you hold 3+ of" (Spice Route's text, weak); recruit paying colored parts with any colors (Haggle, refused); reading the shop (Window Shopping, refused); "each rival may swap with you" (Bazaar, refused); any rearrange (his ask).
1. Leaders' Tax: "+1 yellow. Choose a color: each rival with more of it than you gives you 1 of it." Round I, 4 players: you hold 0 gold, two rivals hold 2+ gold: pick gold, +2 gold +1 yellow = 3, the gold you need for a member's colored part, vs Harvest 3 in any colors. Round III: pick the contest you trail (prize 10): each leader loses 1, you gain up to 3, and the lead can flip, vs Harvest 5. Rivals feel it: leaders pay; the choice of color is real (the color you need vs the contest you can flip). Doesn't grow by round in count, it grows by what contests are worth. Greatest Hits copy: a second tax, at most 3 more.
2. Hard Bargain: "Give a rival 2 of one of your colors, then take up to 3 of another color from them (4 in round II, 5 in round III)." Round I: give Bo 2 yellow, take 3 red: net +1 resource, but the red you need and Bo's red lead down 3. Round III: give 2, take 5 of their contest color: net +3 and a 7-swing in one contest (prize 10). Rival feels a sting and a small gift. Your choice: which rival, which two colors. Not Magenta's steal: you always pay first.
