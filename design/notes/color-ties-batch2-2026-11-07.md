# Color ties, batch 2 (Nov 7)

Built on batch 1's takeaways (verdicts 27-32): each card makes its own moment happen, pays in things other colors want where it can, serves its own color's contest, reads no contest leads, no Green On Recruit copying. Judged against the bar, not as replacements. Names and numbers are Claude's fill-ins. Live By color contests as in batch 1.

## 1. Protection Racket (Magenta, round II space)
"Choose a rival. Steal 1 resource per member they have." (+1 magenta inherent)
- Ties: the member contests. A Blue cult chasing most members, or a Red cult stacking one color, pays the most; a White cult with few members is nearly safe. Magenta's own contest (most resources) grows.
- Own cause: steals by itself; works in any game.
- Round II, 4 players: the biggest cult holds 5 members: steal 5. Best other: Help Yourself "Steal 3 resources in round I, 4 in round II, 5 in round III." gives 4. A round III version would be huge, so round II.
- Choice: whom to hit; the biggest cult is the usual pick, but a rival about to recruit is also a target.
- Who feels: every big cult (a cost to growing); White and small cults get a quiet gift.
- Intuitive: one count on the table. Fun: a sting aimed by cult size. Interacts: Blue, Red, White contests. Powerful: scales with the target. Engine or counter: counters member engines. Its color: Magenta steals; a new target rule.

## 2. Ushers (Purple member, 3 purple + 4 any)
"On Use, once per round: give the Goes Last token to a rival, from whoever has it. After that rival places their next minion, you take it."
- Cory on batch 1's Ushers: "kind of a counter to purple, or maybe it lets you take it back? on the right track". This one gives it, then takes it back.
- What happens: the rival's next minion waits until everyone else's (sting); then the token comes to you, so your next minion goes last: the round's last placement, Purple's contest.
- Own cause: the token is Purple's; works in any game with Purple.
- Round II, 4 players: a rival took Sleep In "+4 purple in round I, +5 in round II, +6 in round III. Take the Goes Last token: your next minion is placed after everyone else's." Use Ushers: move it to the player about to take the space you want; after their delayed minion, you hold it and place last.
- Choice: whom to delay; the receiver feels it, and the Sleep In holder loses the token.
- Intuitive: the token moves twice, both visible. Fun: steal the last move by lending it. Interacts: every color that wants the last move. Powerful: tempo plus Purple's contest. Engine or counter: counters a token holder. Its color: the token fight.

## 3. Last Rites (White member, 3 white + 4 any)
"Whenever you discard one of your members, first use its On Use, even if it's used up."
- Cory, Nov 7: White is "you use stuff and then you get rid of it… you could do that with cards from… other colors". This pays off any color's On Use member one last time as it leaves.
- Own cause: White's discard spaces and members (Farewell, Estate Sale, Downsizers).
- Round II, 4 players: you used Heist Crew "On Use, once per game: steal half of a rival's biggest pile, rounded down." in round I. Take Farewell "Cast out one of your members to the discard pile, gain followers equal to its cost. +1 white.": steal half a pile again, then +9 followers.
- Who feels: the rival hit by the last use; rivals learn that White's spent members still bite.
- Intuitive: yes. Fun: a last hurrah. Interacts: every color's On Use members. Powerful: a second once-per-game use. Engine or counter: engine with White's discards. Its color: use then discard.

## 4. Movers (Red, round II space)
"Move a member from one rival's cult to another rival's. +3 of any colors." (+1 red inherent)
- Cory: Red is "organizing members… moving them around". This moves members between two other cults.
- Ties: every member contest at once. Take from the most-members cult and give to the fewest-members cult (two stings), break a rival's one-color stack, or hand a rival a new color.
- Own cause: works in any game.
- Round II, 4 players: Blue rival has 5 members, White rival has 1. Move Blue's to White: Blue loses one, White gains one; you +3 of any colors. Best other: Promote "Swap any member with one in another cult or the shop costing up to 3 more. +3 red." upgrades your own cult instead.
- Choice: you pick both cults and the member. Both rivals feel it.
- Intuitive: one move. Fun: organizing the table. Interacts: Blue, White, Red and Yellow contests. Powerful: swings two rivals. Engine or counter: counter. Its color: Red moves members; a new direction (between rivals).

## Cut on the way
- Purple "take the Goes Last token; +2 of any colors per minion its last holder has left": undoes Last Call's skips, a rules tangle.
- Green "copy a rival's action; they gain 2 of any colors": hands rivals value for little.
- Yellow "discard a member of a color you have two of, +3 of any colors": the same shape as Temps.
- Yellow "a member of a new color joins your cult, +3 of any colors": a tiny change to Guest Book.
- Silver "rivals can only steal your silver": needs another color to steal.
- Silver "give rivals up to 6 of your silver, +1 of any colors each": the same shape as Sweet Nothings.

## 5. Socialites (Silver member, 4 silver + 4 any), added after Cory: "i said batches are 5"
"On Use, once per round: trade up to 3 of your silver for the same number of one color a rival holds."
- Ties: Silver sheds silver toward its fewest-silver contest; the rival is left holding silver instead of the color they were saving, which hurts any plan that needs that color, and the trade happens on the Silver player's turn only.
- Own cause: works in any game with Silver.
- Round II, 4 players: you hold 3 silver; a rival saves 5 purple for a purple member. Trade: you 0 silver (leading fewest silver) and 3 purple; they 2 purple and 3 silver, which still pays the "any" part of a cost, not the purple part.
- Who feels: the rival (their colors turn into silver).
- Intuitive: one trade. Fun: a gift that's a sting. Interacts: whatever color the rival collects. Powerful: wins its contest and takes a color. Its color: Silver gives.
