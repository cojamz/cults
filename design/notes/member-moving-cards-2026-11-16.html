Cards that move members

"you want to buy members and build an engine, but the game is fun when you can move members around. so it sometimes feels not worth it to go for the things you want." (Nov 12)

18 cards in today's game take, swap, give or discard another player's member: 6 spaces and 12 members. Purple and Green have none. Mark each keep, weaken or cut by number.

Red

1Promote (round II space): +3 red. Swap any member for one costing up to 3 more, from another cult or the shop.
2Demote (round II space): +3 red. Swap any member for one costing up to 3 less, from another cult or the shop.
3Body Snatchers: Round Start: swap one of your members with any rival's member. every round
4Panel: On Use, once per round: promote or demote another of your members. every round

Magenta

5Swindle (round II space): Steal a rival's member costing up to 7; give them a shop member of your choice in its place. +1 magenta.
6Poach (round III space): Recruit a rival's member you choose, for free. +1 magenta.
7Double Agent: On Recruit: swap this member for a rival's member costing up to 8. once
8Kidnappers: Round End: a rival you choose gives you one of their members. every round

White

9Write-Off (round II space): Give a member to a rival for its cost in white. +1 white.
10Guest of Honor: On Use, once per round: give a rival one of your members. +4 followers. every round
11Patrons: On Use, once per game: give one of your members to a rival. Gain its cost in followers. once

Silver

12Buyout (round II space): Recruit a rival's member, paying them its cost in silver. +1 silver.

Yellow

13Exchange Students: On Recruit: swap one of your members for a rival's member that costs the same. once

Blue

14Repossessors: On Use, once per game: discard a rival's member costing up to 8. once

Grey

15Anarchist: On Use, once per round: swap any 2 members that cost the same, in any cult or the shop. every round
16Mutineers: On Use, once per game: take a member from a rival with more members than you. once
17Gift Swappers: On Use, once per game: every player, you too, gives one member they choose to the player on their left. once
18Purifiers: On Use, once per game: discard one of your members. Each rival with more members than you discards one too. once

For reference, cards that pay for or block it: Foster Parents (Red): Whenever a member joins your cult from a rival's, +2 followers. · Leavetakers (White): Whenever one of your members leaves your cult, +2 white. · Loyalists (Red): Your other members can't be swapped.

Reply with numbers, e.g. "weaken 3, cut 8". Unmarked ones stay.
