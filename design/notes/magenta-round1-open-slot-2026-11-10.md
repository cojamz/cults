## Frame
- Contest: Most resources, every color together, counted at round end.
- The set as he left it: I **Help Yourself** "Steal 1 resource per minion you've placed this round, this one included. +1 magenta."; I **Catch Up** "+2 magenta per rival with more resources than you. +1 magenta."; I (open); II **Heist** "Steal 4 followers and 4 resources. +1 magenta."; II **Swindle** "Steal a rival's member costing up to 7; give them a shop member of your choice in its place. +1 magenta."; III **Poach** "Recruit a rival's member you choose, for free. +1 magenta."
- What the two round I spaces already read: Help Yourself reads the clock (better late in the round), Catch Up reads being behind in resources. Nothing yet pays for being ahead in resources at a moment other than round end, and nothing pays early in the round.
- Start of round I: everyone holds 1 resource (the cloak's), 1 follower, no members.
- Band: round I about 2 to 4; his follower spaces "around +4 to +5".

9. "Choose a rival. You each gain 3 magenta": Silver's verb (giving). Cut.

11. "Sell up to 3 followers you bought or won for 2 resources each": dead in round I (nobody has bought followers). Cut.

## Survivor 1 (pick): Show Off
Card: "+2 followers per rival with fewer resources than you. +1 magenta." (name and the 2 are Claude's fill-ins; the 2 mirrors Catch Up)
Round I turn, 4 players: turn 8, you hold 6 (took a +4 space, then stole 1), rivals hold 5, 4 and 3 after their own first two turns. Here: +6 followers and 1 magenta. Best other space, Catch Up "+2 magenta per rival with more resources than you. +1 magenta.", gives you 1 magenta (nobody is richer). A plain fuel space gives about 4. With rivals at 7, 4 and 3 you get +4 followers; tied with everyone on turn 1 you get just 1 magenta.
Round III turn: you hold 14, rivals 16, 12 and 9: +4 followers, 1 magenta, no recruit used. A Green copy gives another +4. Ceiling 6 at 4 players, so no round III monster.
Choices: none handed to a rival. Yours is timing: take it while you're rich, before you recruit that turn; or spend first and take Catch Up instead.
Who feels it: the rich player becomes the target before their turn (Help Yourself aimed at them drops their count); rivals block it when someone is clearly richest; a poor player ignores it and takes Catch Up. Worst case for a rival: nothing is taken from them.
How often: every round has a richest player; pays 2 to 6 most times it's taken, 0 only on all ties (turn 1 of round I).
Steps: 1. Count each rival's resources against yours. 2. +2 followers per rival with fewer. 3. +1 magenta.
Ties to other colors: resources of every color count, so every color's fuel feeds it; Blue and Red players who spend down make everyone else's Show Off bigger.
Intuitive: the same count as Catch Up, turned around.
Fun: the table watches who is richest every turn, not only at round end.
Interacts: with Help Yourself and Heist (steal first, then show off; or rob the rich player before they get here), Catch Up (the pair splits the table into rich and poor), Bodyguards "Rivals can't steal from you."
Powerful: up to 6 followers for one minion in round I.
Engine or counter: pays the hoard a Magenta cult builds by stealing; countered by any steal.
Its color: Magenta's contest is Most resources; this is the same race paid in followers mid-round.
Meets the bar: strong moment (the richest player cashing in while the table groans), clean (one count, one payoff), ties through resources. Risks: it pays the player already winning Most resources, and it helps a player who only hoards and buys followers. Hard rules: public, done when taken, no members, no memory, no new token. Not the same as a refused shape: "+1 magenta per color you lead" was Claude's own cut; "steal from each richer rival" and Keep Up were about being behind.

## Survivor 2: Head Start
Card: "+1 magenta per minion you have left. +1 magenta." (name Claude's fill-in)
Round I turn, 4 players: your first turn, 2 minions left: 3 magenta. Best other space: a fuel space at about 4, or Catch Up at 1 to 7. On your last turn it gives 1 magenta.
Round III turn: first turn 5 magenta, last turn 1. A Green copy late gives little.
Choices: yours only: open with it for the most, or save the turn for a contested space.
Who feels it: a race for the first turn of each round; whoever goes second gets the same amount, so the race is mild. Mostly nobody. Weaker than Show Off on this line.
How often: every round; 3, 4, 5 at its best by round, which is the old "+2/+3/+4" grown from the table instead of printed.
Steps: count your unplaced minions, gain that much magenta, +1.
Ties to other colors: members that add a minion make it bigger.
Intuitive: yes, one count in your own column.
Fun: mild; it shapes a Magenta round (open here, close on Help Yourself).
Interacts: the opposite clock to Help Yourself "Steal 1 resource per minion you've placed this round, this one included. +1 magenta."
Powerful: no; it's the plain fuel space.
Engine or counter: fuel for the contest and for magenta members.
Its color: only through giving magenta toward Most resources. Thin tie.
Meets the bar: fine, not a star. It's the "something that just gives magenta" he floated at 3:25pm, scaled by the table. Sent as the weaker option and said so.
