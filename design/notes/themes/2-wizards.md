# Dueling wizards, the whole picture (Cory, Nov 9: "Dueling wizards is likely to be a good fit")

**Premise:** the old Archmage is gone and the tower stands empty. Rival wizards have three moons to prove themselves. Whoever gathers the most disciples takes the tower.

| Element | Is | Why it holds |
|---|---|---|
| Leader | A wizard | Rivals share one realm and want one tower. |
| Followers | **Disciples**: students who come to learn from you | It's score that means something. A great wizard draws students. You can win them over with a show of power (buy followers) or lure them away from a rival (steal followers). |
| Members | **Notable disciples and allies** with gifts of their own: the Frost Sisters, a hedge witch | They're the same kind of thing as followers, just named, so each counts 1 like today. A frost witch only joins a wizard deep in frost magic (her cost is partly that color). |
| Minions | **Apprentices** | An apprentice stands at a place of power and channels it; while they're there, nobody else can use it. You take on more each moon (3/4/5). |
| Colors | **Schools of magic** | Each school has its own adepts (members), its own essence (resource), its own trial (contest) and its own style (what the color already does). |
| Resources | **Essence**: a crystal of each school's magic | It's a thing you hold, so it can be stolen, given, hoarded and spent. |
| Actions | **Spells**, cast by the apprentice at that place of power | Each school's spells work the way that magic would. |
| Wheel | The great magic circle | Rings: the outer circle in moon I, the inner circle in moon II, and the heart in moon III, when the circle is fully awake. |
| Rounds | Three moons | Power grows each moon, so prizes grow (+4/+6/+10). |
| Contests | Each school's trial: its masters honor whoever is strongest in it | |
| Cloak | Your **robe**, the school you trained in | A boon all game plus 1 essence. |
| Winning | Archmage | |

## Schools, by what each color already does
- **Purple → Time magic.** Hold Up ("+2 purple in round I, +3 in round II, +4 in round III. The next turn is skipped, even if it's yours.") = freezing the next wizard in time.
- **Magenta → Shadow magic.** Help Yourself ("Steal 3 resources in round I, 4 in round II, 5 in round III.") = drawing a rival's essence through the shadows.
- **Green → Mirror magic.** It copies a space: you echo another wizard's spell.
- **Gold → Alchemy.** Golden Years ("+2 gold in round I, +4 in round II, +6 in round III."): your gold grows. The "Most gold" contest is the alchemists' trial: the richest wins.
- **Red → Enchantment.** It recruits and swaps members: you charm people into joining you.
- **White → Light magic.** It turns what you have into followers, the way a radiant wizard draws students.
- **Yellow → Elemental magic.** It goes broad and picks its colors: Harvest ("+3 in round I, +4 in round II, +5 in round III, in colors you choose.").
- **Silver → Blessing magic.** Gifts that come back to you: you bless rivals, and the blessing returns.

Weakest spot: Silver. Blessing a rival is generous rather than dueling.
