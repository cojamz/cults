# Purple's members for 1.0 (pitch, Nov 1, nothing built until Cory says yes)

Cory, Oct 31 night, on Purple's members: "needs rework". The deck is the 10-card one (balance on 10); the 15-card deck stays a setup option and gets the same cards.
Names and prices are Claude's fill-ins.

## What Purple is for, and what a player at a Purple table wants
Purple bends turns: in a row (Right Away, Crunch Time), skips (Sleep In, Hold Up, Last Call), +1 minion (Crowd In). Contest: most purple held.
- Today's spaces, live: **Sleep In** "+4 purple (+5 in round II, +6 in round III). Skip your next turn." · **Hold Up** "+2 purple (+3 in round II, +4 in round III). The next turn is skipped." · **Right Away** "+1 purple (+2 in round II, +3 in round III). Your next turn comes right after this one." · **Last Call** "+1 purple. +2 followers per minion you have left. Skip your turns until everyone else is out of minions." · **Crunch Time** "+2 purple. Your next two turns come right after this one." · **Crowd In** "+3 purple. +1 minion this round."
- Three plans the spaces already hold: **go now** (in a row: grab two spaces before anyone answers), **go late** (skip yourself for purple, Last Call for followers), **more minions** (Crowd In). Plus a sting: skip a rival.
- What the members gave each plan before this pass: go now had Clockmaker and Courier (good) and Hot Streak (counts past turns, which Cory ruled out Oct 31). More minions had Night Owl and Extra Shift, and Extra Shift is a worse Night Owl at a higher price (one +1 minion, once). Go late had only Closer. Skipping yourself paid nothing but purple, so Sleep In and Last Call were a fixed trade.

## The 10 (8 kept as live, 2 new)
Round I (4): **Early Bird** "Your turns can't be skipped." · **Bouncer** "On Recruit: the next two rivals' turns are skipped." · **Timekeepers** "Game End: +1 follower per purple member you have." · **Shift Crew** "Worth 2 followers." (counts as 2)
Round II on (6): **Clockmaker** "Round Start: take your first two turns in a row." · **Courier** "Whenever you take two turns in a row, +1 of each other color." · **Closer** "When you place the last minion of the round: +4 purple." · **Night Owl** "Once per round: after you place your last minion, +1 minion, placed right away." · **Insomniac** (new, top card) · **Holdouts** (new)

Out: **Extra Shift** "On Recruit: +1 minion this round." and **Hot Streak** "On Use, once per game: +3 followers per turn in a row you've taken just before this one." In the 15-card deck the two new cards take the same two places.

## New 1: Insomniac, "When your turn is skipped, you still recruit." (fill-in price 9, worth 2)
- Round III, 4 players, you hold 16 resources. You take **Last Call** with 4 minions left: +8 followers. Each of your next 4 turns is skipped as play reaches it, and you recruit at each (10 followers, then 5, then whatever you can afford). Then your 4 minions place last, each with its own recruit. The best other center pick, **Crowd In**, is 3 purple plus one more minion (one more action and recruit). Insomniac turns Purple's skips into the scarce thing of round III, the recruit.
- Round II: **Sleep In** becomes "+5 purple and a recruit now", the minion still places later.
- Who feels it: anyone holding **Hold Up** or **Bouncer** now thinks twice before skipping you, since it hands you a recruit. Early Bird's owner can't use it (their turns are never skipped).
- Why only Purple: skips are Purple's verb. No memory: it fires the moment the table sees the turn passed over.
- Risk (flag to Cory): Last Call in round III gives up to 4 extra recruits, so it's priced as the top card. Resources stay the limit.

## New 2: Holdouts, "Whenever you place a minion once everyone else is out of minions, +2 followers." (fill-in price 5, worth 1)
- Round III, 4 players: **Last Call** with 4 left pays +8 now, and Holdouts pays +8 more as those 4 land after everyone. **Crowd In**'s extra minion goes after everyone else: +2. Night Owl's minion after your last placement, if you're last: +2.
- Without a plan it pays the natural last seat about +2 a round (the snake's end), so it's never dead.
- Who feels it: rivals race to deny **Last Call**, and a skipped rival (Bouncer, Hold Up) lands late but not after everyone, so no gift.
- Pairs with Closer ("When you place the last minion of the round: +4 purple") without doing its job: Closer is the purple contest at the wire, Holdouts is followers.

## Seeds played and cut (gate 3)
- "On Use, once per round: your next turn comes right after this one": twin of Clockmaker (two in a row once a round).
- "On Use, once per round: the next turn is skipped": twin of Bouncer (skips a rival), and every round adds the skip load Cory flagged for new tables.
- "Round Start: each rival's first turn is skipped" (pitched Oct 29 as Curfew): stronger Front-runner, and heavy for Classic.
- "+2 followers per rival with minions left when you place your last": the snake's end seats get it free, and from round II the leader sits there. Win-more.
- "Whenever your turn is skipped, +2 purple": small ball, and the same trigger as Insomniac.
- "Whenever you take two turns in a row, +2 followers": Courier's trigger again.
- "Your purple counts twice for the Purple contest": not Purple's verb (turns), and contest cards wait for a contests color.
- "On Recruit: your next turn comes right after this one": a cheap tempo card worth less than Shift Crew's two bodies; kept in pocket.
- Rework Hot Streak to drop the count: every version still counts past turns or becomes a Crunch Time on a card.
