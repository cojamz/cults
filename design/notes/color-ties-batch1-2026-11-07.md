# Color ties, batch 1 (Nov 7)

Cory: batches of 5, a variety of colors, concise with brief reasoning; 4 of 5 liked starts testing. Built with the rewritten brainstorm skill (references/color-interactions.md). Live contests: Red most of one color (grey counts), Blue most members, White fewest members, Silver fewest silver, Yellow most member colors, Magenta most resources, Purple placed the last minion, Green most minions on Green spaces. Numbers and names are Claude's fill-ins. Judged against the bar, not as replacements (Cory, Nov 7).

## 1. Temps (Blue member, 4 blue + 4 any, worth 1)
"Whenever one of your members leaves your cult on your turn, recruit an extra time this turn."
- Moment: a member leaves your cult. Caused by White (Farewell, Estate Sale, Downsizers discard), Silver (Godparent, Guest of Honor give), Red (Promote, Demote, Panel swap your member to the shop or a rival). Today rewarded only for the owner of the leaving card (Martyrs) and own discards (Undertaker); nothing refills.
- Round II, 4 players: you hold Temps and a spent Accomplice (5). Estate Sale: +5 white +1, then an extra recruit. Best other: Recruit Twice "+3 blue, recruit an extra time". You get the same extra recruit plus 6 white. Round III with Power Shoppers: the second member in a turn is +4 followers.
- Choices: none for rivals. "On your turn" keeps every decision on your own turn.
- Who feels: White and Silver players see a Blue rival turn their cast-off moves into recruits; a Red player swapping with your member on their turn gives nothing (on your turn only).
- Absent checks: no White → Silver gifts, Red swaps; no Silver → White, Red; no Red → White, Silver. With none of the three, quiet.
- Intuitive: one sentence, game words. Fun: turns losing a member into a burst. Interacts: Blue's more-members race fed by the colors that shrink cults. Powerful: an extra recruit, the late-game bottleneck. Engine or counter: engine with any discard or give. Its color: Blue recruits more.

## 2. Hand-Me-Down (Silver round II space)
"Give a rival a shop member costing up to 5, for free. Gain followers equal to its cost." (+1 silver inherent)
- Moment: a member joins a rival's cult. It moves three contests at once: most members (gift), fewest members (sting), most of one color and most member colors (gift or nothing, by color).
- Round II, 4 players: shop holds a 5-cost. Give it: +5 followers. Best other: Sweet Nothings "+3 silver. Give rivals up to 3 of your silver, split as you choose: +2 of any colors each." about 3 to 6 resources and a gift. Here 5 followers and a targeted push.
- Choices: you pick the rival and the card. Give the trailing Blue player a body, or push the White leader off fewest members; giving a Red player a card of their deep color is a gift you'd avoid.
- Who feels: the White leader (sting), whoever gets it (gift), everyone who wanted that shop card (denied).
- Absent checks: reads contests and the shop only; works in any game.
- Intuitive: yes. Fun: a gift that is a sting by who gets it. Interacts: every member-count contest. Powerful: followers equal to cost. Engine or counter: counters White's fewest members. Its color: Silver gives; new way: gives members from the shop, not silver. Sibling: Godparent gives your own member once per game.

## 3. Ushers (Purple member, 3 purple + 4 any, worth 2, counts as 2)
"On Use, once per round: give the Goes Last token to a rival. +2 followers."
- Cory, Nov 8: "there should definitely be a purple that gives the go last token. maybe a couple members." Goes Last stays Purple's (decisions, 9:51pm): only Purple moves it.
- Moment: the round's last move. Every color that swings standings in one move (Red swap, Magenta steal, Silver gift, White discard) wants it; holding it also wins Purple's contest.
- Round II, 4 players: the Magenta player sits second in a contest you lead; you hand the token to the Yellow player instead. They wait for their next minion (sting: the space they wanted goes), but place last and take Purple's contest (gift). +2 followers for you.
- Choices: yours only, on your turn: who gets delayed and who gets the last move. Both sides matter, so it's a real pick.
- Who feels: the receiver both ways; the rival who wanted the last move.
- Absent checks: the token is Purple's own; it reads whatever the receiver does last. Works in any game with Purple.
- Intuitive: yes. Fun: kingmaking the last move. Interacts: every swing color. Powerful: tempo plus followers. Engine or counter: counters a Last Call or Sleep In holder. Its color: the fight over the token.

## 4. Grave Robbers (Green member, 4 green + 4 any, worth 1)
"On Use, once per round: use the On Recruit of a member in the discard pile."
- Moment: a member goes to the discard pile. Blue clears the shop into it (Savings, Out of Style, Bury, Window Shopper, Restockers); White discards spent members into it (Farewell, Estate Sale, Downsizers). Nothing reads it today (crossings page gap).
- Round II, 4 players: Blue cleared Accomplice "On Recruit: steal 3 resources." last turn. Use it: steal 3. Best other use of the turn is unchanged; this is free once a round.
- Choices: none for rivals; Blue and White now think before dumping a strong On Recruit.
- Absent checks: no Blue → White fills it; no White → Blue fills it; with neither, little enters it.
- Intuitive: the discard pile is face up. Fun: a sting on careless dumps. Interacts: Blue and White feed it. Powerful: a strong On Recruit every round. Engine or counter: engine. Its color: Green copies; a new place to copy from.

## 5. Tall Poppies (Magenta round II space)
"Steal 2 resources from each rival who leads a contest." (+1 magenta inherent)
- Moment: a contest lead, shown on every rim. Ties Magenta to every color's race without naming one.
- Round II, 4 players: two rivals lead contests (one most members, one fewest silver): steal 4. Best other: Help Yourself "Steal 3 resources in round I, 4 in round II, 5 in round III." Same 4, but aimed at leaders, and stealing a Silver leader's silver helps them.
- Choices: none hidden; leaders can be read off the rims.
- Who feels: every leader; a reason to stay second until late.
- Absent checks: reads contests only.
- Intuitive: yes. Fun: leaders flinch. Interacts: every contest. Powerful: grows with how many lead. Engine or counter: counters a runaway leader. Its color: Magenta steals; new target.
