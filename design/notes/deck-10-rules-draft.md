## The rules
1. **Round split: 4 round I, 6 later** (5 round I is fine too; 3 is too few at 4 players). Grey the same.
2. **Price spread**: every color keeps at least 2 cheap members (2 or 3) and 2 big ones (8 or more), your Oct 28 rule for the 2.0 colors. The rest fill the middle, no more than 3 at one price.
3. **Ways to win**: every color keeps at least one card that pays per member of its own color (so collecting a color stays a plan) and at least one game-end scorer.
4. **A mix of when it pays**: on arrival, on your turn, at round start, at game end, or always. No more than 3 of one kind, so a hand doesn't all fire at once or all wait.
5. **One sting**: every color keeps at least one card a rival feels (a counter, a steal or a block), so there's a reason to grab a card before someone else does.
6. **No twins**: two cards doing the same job, one goes (the cheaper or simpler survives).
7. **Your praise wins ties**: a card you've called out as liked stays unless it breaks a rule above. Bot buy and win rates only break ties; they're never the reason to cut.
8. **Grey is rule-benders only**: each grey member bends one base rule (turn order, minions, buying, blocking). No plain followers-for-resources cards.
