# Color ties, batch 3 (Nov 7)

Built on verdicts 27-38: own cause, a cause that happens often, at most two steps, stings with a ceiling, no slots he's happy with (Red round II), no contest-leader reads, no Green On Recruit copying. Five Claude would defend. Names and numbers are fill-ins.

## 1. Cory's seed, Purple space (round II): "Give the Goes Last token to a rival: all their other minions are placed after everyone else's." (+1 purple inherent)
- Cory, 1:32pm: "another interesting purple idea though of making all a players members go last". Last Call's effect, put on a rival.
- Steps: the rival takes the token; every other player places until out; then the rival places all their remaining minions in a row.
- Victim at worst: round II with 3 minions left, the spaces they wanted go first; but they get the round's last placements (Purple's contest) and turns in a row.
- Choice: whom to push back: the player about to take your space, or not the one who'd love the last swing. Real both ways.
- How often: every round someone can take it. Own cause: yes.
- Intuitive: Last Call's words, aimed at a rival. Fun: a sting with a silver lining. Interacts: every color that wants the last move. Powerful: tempo swing. Engine or counter: counter. Its color: the token fight.

## 2. Silver space (round II): "Give a rival one of your minions for this round. +5 followers." (+1 silver inherent)
- Silver gives a new thing: a placement. Trades a placement for followers (Last Call's kind of trade).
- Steps: hand over a minion; they place it on one of their turns.
- Choice: whom to help: the player furthest behind, or the one whose extra placement hurts your rivals most. The receiver can't refuse a gift, and it is always good for them.
- Who feels: the receiver (gift); everyone else loses a contested space to the extra minion; more minions change who places last.
- Round II: placing elsewhere is worth about 4; here 5 followers plus a gift you aim.
- Intuitive: yes. Fun: kingmaking. Interacts: Purple's last minion, Green's minions on Green spaces. Powerful: followers now. Its color: gives.

## 3. White space (round II): "Give up to 3 of your followers to rivals, split as you choose. +3 white per follower given." (+1 white inherent)
- Cory's White: "all about like fewer followers… you use stuff and then you get rid of it". Fewer followers means going earlier next round (fewest followers goes first), and the white feeds Cash In "Turn all your white into followers. +1 white." in round III.
- Steps: give followers, take white.
- Round II: give 3, take 9 white; round III Cash In turns 9 white into 9 followers without a recruit. Net +6 followers over two rounds, and an earlier seat next round.
- Choice: whom to give to: the rival you want behind you in turn order, never the one closest to winning.
- Who feels: receivers get followers and a later seat.
- Intuitive: yes. Fun: a gift with a catch. Interacts: turn order, every color's race. Powerful: big white for the cash-in. Its color: fewer followers.

## 4. Yellow member: "Round End: +1 follower per color among your members that no rival has a member of." (4 yellow + 4 any)
- Yellow's contest is most member colors; this rewards owning a color nobody else has, so colors become something to claim and to block.
- How often: every round end, 0 to 3 for a broad cult.
- Steps: count, for each of your member colors, whether any rival has one.
- Who feels: rivals who buy a member of your lone color to switch it off; a reason to buy a member you don't need.
- Intuitive: one count on public cards. Fun: claiming colors. Interacts: every color's members. Engine: with breadth. Its color: going broad.

## 5. Magenta member: "Whenever a rival recruits a member costing 7 or more, steal 2 resources from them." (4 magenta + 4 any)
- A tax on big buys. Capped at 2, on a moment that happens a few times a game from round II, no decisions on their turn.
- Who feels: big buyers (every cult in round III), a reason to buy cheaper or spend down first; Magenta's pile grows (its contest).
- How often: about 3 to 6 times a game at 4 players.
- Intuitive: yes. Fun: rivals flinch at the shop. Interacts: every color's recruits. Powerful: steady steals. Engine or counter: counter to big buys. Its color: steals.
