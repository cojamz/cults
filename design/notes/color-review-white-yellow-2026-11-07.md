# Color review: White and Yellow (Nov 7)

Same lens as design/notes/color-review-purple-2026-11-07.md. Live cards Nov 7; numbers are Claude's fill-ins.

## White
"I bank white and cash it in past the limit." Anchor: Cash In ("Turn all your white into followers. +1 white."). Contest: fewest followers.
White keeps to itself: its members leave for the discard pile, where nobody can use them. The one shared piece it already touches is the shop's follower cards.

### Estate Sale (tweak)
Live: "Cast out one of your members to the discard pile, gain white equal to its cost. +1 white." (may, Nov 4)
Proposed: "You may put one of your members in the shop. Gain white equal to its cost. +1 white."
Played: round II, 4 players. Eve (White) holds Panel, cost 9, its once-per-round use spent. Estate Sale: Panel goes into the shop row, Eve +10 white toward Cash In. Dee is next and recruits Panel at its cost. Live, Panel goes to the discard pile and nobody gets it.
Choices: which member to sell. A pricier one is more white, but a rival may buy it next turn; neither side always wins.
Who feels it: every rival sees a used member up for sale; whoever buys it, and the player it would have helped.
Ledger: the live shape is Cory's yes ("3 is good improves on mine", Oct 22); only where the member goes changes. Red's Seat Filler ("Round Start: you may give this to the shop and recruit a shop member costing up to 3 more, for free.") also puts a member in the shop, but for a swap, not for white.
Intuitive: the name already says it; the card sits in the shop like any other.
Fun: a choice with the table watching.
Interacts: the shop, in every game; any recruiting color gets a shot at it.
Powerful: the same white as live.
Engine or counter: engine with Cash In, Undertaker? No: Undertaker and Martyrs say "cast out" and "leaves your cult"; Martyrs still fires, Undertaker would need "Whenever you cast out or sell one of your members".
Its color: selling, a new way into White's cash.
Rule needed: the shop row gets one more card until it's bought; at round end it goes with the row.

### Keep
Spaces: Allowance, Splurge, Exchange, Farewell, Cash In. Members: Recluse, Downsizers, Barker, Heiresses, Brokers, Misers, Cashier, Undertaker, Money Changers, Martyrs, Old Money, Magpie. Exchange already takes any color, and the fewest-followers contest already reads every player.

## Yellow
"Rewards going broad… translate currencies" (Cory, Oct 30). Anchor: Makeover ("+4 yellow, then rearrange your resources into any mix of colors."). Contest: most full sets.
Yellow already touches every color through resources and members' colors, without naming any: Harvest, Survey ("+1 yellow per minion on the busiest color's spaces"), New Blood, Housewarming, Duty Collector. Nothing to stitch.
What is off is overlap, not distance: Silver's Polish, Sweet Talk and Smooth Talkers do Yellow's job of turning resources into other colors (for Silver's page), and Yellow has four cards that swap your own resources (Tinker, Night Traders, Book Cookers, Makeover). No new card pitched: the fix is trimming, Cory's call on which.
