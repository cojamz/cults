# Color review: Purple (Nov 7)

Cory, Nov 7: review every action and member "through this lens": colors stay distinct, synergize without overlapping, and touch only through what every game has (resources, members, the shop, the pile, turn order, followers). No card may lean on another color being in the game. "tweaks can be redesigns."
Live cards from live-board.mjs, Nov 7. Numbers are Claude's fill-ins.

## Purple in one line
"I act when it matters most": Purple bends when turns happen (Crunch Time, Right Away, Hold Up, going last). Anchor: Overtime ("+4 purple. +1 minion this round. Take the Goes Last token: that minion is placed after everyone else's.").

## What Purple already touches in every game
Turn order is in every game, so Purple already reaches every color: a skip or two turns in a row decides who gets the contested space of whatever colors are out, and going last gives the last word before contests are scored. What it lacks: its own gains are all purple or followers, so a Purple turn never feeds the other colors you're playing, and its last-minion cards (Closer, Night Managers) say the same thing twice.

## Spaces
- Sleep In, Hold Up, Last Call, Crunch Time, Overtime: keep. Each already bends the order everyone plays in; Cory has called Hold Up, Last Call and Crunch Time good.
- **Right Away**, tweak (below).

### Right Away
Live: "+2 purple in round I, +3 in round II, +4 in round III. Your next turn comes right after this one."
Proposed: "+1 purple. Your next turn comes right after this one, and that space gives +2 more of its color in round I, +3 in round II, +4 in round III."
Played: round II, 4 players. Ana takes Right Away (+1 purple), then right away takes Toast ("+3 silver in round I, +6 in round II, +9 in round III. Each rival +1 silver in round I, +2 in round II, +3 in round III."): 6 + 3 = 9 silver, a silver contest lead nobody can answer before her next turn. Live, the same two turns give 3 purple and 6 silver. Same total, but in the color she is fighting for instead of purple that only pays purple members.
Choices: which space to take next; the bonus follows it.
Who feels it: whoever led that color's contest, and whoever wanted that space next.
Ledger: not refused; "one turn after this one" alone was refused as net zero (Oct 22), this keeps the live turn plus a gain.
Intuitive: one sentence, done the moment the next space is taken (it is your very next turn, nothing to remember).
Fun: a two-turn combo the table watches land.
Interacts: with every color's spaces, through the space, never by name.
Powerful: same total as live, aimed where it counts.
Engine or counter: engine with Courier and Lookouts (consecutive turns) and any contest you are chasing.
Its color: timing makes the next action bigger, new for Purple.
Risk: copying it (Echo after Right Away) gives a bonus to the copier's next space too; fine, it is still one space.

## Members
Keep: Go-Getter, Late Risers, Courier, Bouncers, Gossip, Extra Shift, Timekeeper, Lookouts, Sleepyheads, Night Managers, Hot Streak, Picket Line, Pocket Watch (cloak). Each is a clean purple trigger (skips, turns in a row, the Goes Last token, last minion).
Redesign: **Closer** (its trigger repeats Night Managers').

### Closer
Live: [2 purple + 2 any printed] "When you place the last minion of the round: +4 purple."
Proposed: "When you place the round's last minion, do that space's action again."
Played: round II, 4 players, 5 spare spaces before the last placements. Dee holds Closer and took Sleep In ("Take the Goes Last token: your next minion is placed after everyone else's."). Everyone else places; two spaces are left: Help Yourself ("Steal 3 resources in round I, 4 in round II, 5 in round III from one rival.") and Build Up ("+3 green in round I, +4 in round II, +5 in round III."). Dee takes Help Yourself twice: steal 8. Live Closer: +4 purple.
Choices: the other players choose what to leave open, knowing Dee doubles it: they take the best spaces themselves or leave her scraps. Neither side always wins: taking a space only to deny her costs them their own pick.
Who feels it: every player with a late minion; a reason to block.
Ledger: not refused. "Double" language: Cory wants precise words (Oct 22, Crunch Time); "do that space's action again" is the printed effect, step by step.
Intuitive: one sentence; the last minion is visible on the board.
Fun: a reason to race for last and a reason to block.
Interacts: with whatever space is left, any color, never by name.
Powerful: a second space's worth on one minion, in rounds II and III.
Engine or counter: engine with Sleep In, Last Call, Overtime, Night Managers, Sleepyheads.
Its color: going last pays off in the space itself, new for Purple (now only purple or followers).
Price: up a step from 2+2 printed (fill-in 4 purple + 3 any).

## Hooks other colors could carry (for their own pages)
Turn order is in every game (the snake gives the end seats two turns in a row each round), so another color's member may care about it without naming Purple. Purple makes it happen more often. Two to weigh on those colors' pages, not pitched yet:
- A Magenta member: "Whenever you take two turns in a row, steal 1 resource."
- A Blue member: "Whenever you take two turns in a row, the second turn's recruit may be a member from the pile top."
