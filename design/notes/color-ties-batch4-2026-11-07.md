# Color ties, batch 4 (Nov 7)

Built on verdicts 27-43 and the liked shape (a capped tax on a common moment: Magenta "Whenever a rival recruits a member costing 7 or more, steal 2 resources from them."; Temps: self-fueled, big payoff). Five Claude would defend. Names and numbers are fill-ins.

## 1. Silver member: "Whenever a rival buys followers, give them 1 silver from the bank. +1 follower."
- Every player plays the fewest-silver contest; each gift pushes a rival up in silver, so the gift is a sting. The silver still pays the "any" part of a cost.
- How often: follower buys happen about 8 to 15 times a game at 4 players, more in round III (count holds up as the game fills).
- No decisions on their turn. Victim at worst: a few silver they can spend.
- Interacts: every player's follower buys. Its color: gives; its contest.

## 2. Purple member: "Whenever a rival recruits two or more members in one turn, they skip their next turn."
- Moment: a double recruit. Caused by Blue (Recruit Twice, Spree, Plus-Ones), Red (Initiation's free member plus the normal recruit), Yellow (Housewarming's free member plus the normal recruit), Temps. Common without any one of them.
- Capped: one skip per such turn. Victim at worst: a round III Spree turn costs one placement later.
- Interacts: every color's burst recruits. Its color: skips.

## 3. Yellow member: "Once per round, each rival may trade you up to 3 of their resources for the same number of yours, colors their choice. +1 follower per resource traded."
- Yellow translates currencies for the whole table (Cory: "translate currencies (kind of like launderer)"). Shape that landed before: a gift that pays you per rival it helps.
- Steps: on their own turn a rival swaps; you score. No decision for you on their turn.
- Both sides: a rival short one color for a member uses it and pays you followers; one ahead of you may refuse.
- How often: up to 3 rivals a round. Interacts: every color's colored costs.

## 4. Red member: "Whenever you swap one of your members, +2 of any colors."
- Red moves members (Promote, Demote, Body Snatchers, Panel, Headhunter, Seat Filler); a quiet engine paying in any colors, which feeds whatever the other colors cost. Not every card needs a sting (taste guide, pattern 6).
- How often: 3 to 6 swaps a game in a Red cult.

## 5. Magenta member: "Whenever a rival buys 5 or more followers at once, steal 1 follower from them."
- The liked tax shape on a different common moment (the 5 and 10 follower cards). Capped at 1. Only recruited followers can be stolen (rulebook).
- How often: several times a game from round II.

## Cut on the way
- Silver "give a rival 3 silver, recruit an extra time": Blue's verb.
- White "+1 white whenever any member leaves a cult": rare without Red, Magenta or Temps.
- White "+1 white whenever a rival recruits": fires 15 times a game.
- Yellow "+1 of a color when a rival recruits a color you lack": collapses as you broaden.
- Red "move a member between rivals" as a member: same idea as Movers.
- Green: nothing survived; copy targets are covered (Echo, Reprise, Song Request, Cover) and Green On Recruit copying is out.
