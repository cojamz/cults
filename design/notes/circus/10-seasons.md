# Pass 10: the time scale is the leak

Cory: "a new strongman is joining you that night because of a strongman act?" Right: within a single night, hiring makes no sense. Every recruit, steal and award needs time to happen in.

**Fix: a round is a whole season on the road, not a night.** The game is three seasons of building your circus.
- **Taking a space** = booking a run of shows in that discipline this season. Strongman numbers all season make your name in strength (gold).
- **Recruiting** = word gets around; a famous strongman comes asking to join a show known for strength (his cost is partly gold). Hiring between gigs is how circuses really grew.
- **Buying followers** = spending your name on advertising to fill seats.
- **Season's end (round end)** = the trade papers name the best act in each discipline (contests). Performers' end-of-season tricks happen. The shop row turns over as performers' contracts end and new ones come free.
- **Rings opening** = venue class, not a map: canvas tents in season 1, city arenas in season 2, the Garden (center) in season 3. Bigger venues book bigger numbers.
- **Minions 3/4/5** = your show runs more units each season.
- **Winning** = after three seasons, the biggest crowd: you're "the Greatest Show on Earth".
- **The board** is the circus world's season of bookings, a wheel of disciplines. It isn't a map, so there's nothing to travel across.

Kept from pass 8/9: colors = disciplines, members = performers (grey = crew), resources = your name in a discipline.
