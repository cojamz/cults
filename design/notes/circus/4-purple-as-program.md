# Pass 4: the board is the night's program. First example: Purple

Each space is a number in tonight's show. Only one troupe can run it. Purple's job is bending the turn order, which in a show is **the running order**: who goes on when. So Purple's numbers are the ones that move the show around. The card text is unchanged; only the name and the picture change.

| Today | Circus number | Why it fits | Card text (live) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sleep In | **Star Turn** | The star always closes the show, and the star gets paid most. | +4 purple in round I, +5 in round II, +6 in round III. Take the Goes Last token: your next minion goes after everyone else's. |
| Hold Up | **Clown Stall** | Clowns run on and stall while the next act isn't ready, so that act is bumped. | +2/+3/+4 purple. The next turn is skipped, even if it's yours. |
| Right Away | **Quick Change** | You're off and straight back on in a new costume. | +2/+3/+4 purple. Your next turn comes right after this one. |
| Crunch Time | **Back-to-Back** | Two numbers in a row with no break. | +2 purple. Your next two turns come right after this one. |
| Last Call | **Grand Finale** | Everyone you haven't used yet marches on at the end, and the crowd cheers each one. | +1 purple. +1 follower and +1 purple per minion you have left. Take the Goes Last token: your other minions go after everyone else's. |
| Overtime | **Extra Number** | One more number than planned, at the very end. | +4 purple. +1 minion this round. Take the Goes Last token: that minion goes after everyone else's. |

- **Purple's contest, "Went last"** (whoever placed the round's last minion) = **Closed the Show**. The crowd remembers the last act.
- **Goes Last token** = **the Closing Spot**.
- Purple acts are **showmen of timing**: headliners, quick-change artists, stalling clowns.
