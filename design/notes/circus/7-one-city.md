# Pass 7: one city, not three towns

Cory: parts of town make no sense if you go to different towns.

Fix: **all the rival shows are in one big city for the season, at the same time.** This really happened: Barnum and Forepaugh both played New York in 1887 and fought over the same crowd.
- **Rounds = three weeks of the season.** Each week ends with the weekend show (round end): your Troupe performs, and each part of the city picks its favorite show (contests +4/+6/+10, bigger each weekend as the season builds).
- **The wheel = the city map**, and it stays the same all game. Colors are parts of the city (Rail Depot, City Hall, the Boarding House, the Back Alleys, the Newspaper…).
- **Rings opening:** in week 1 only the edge of the city knows you, so you work the outskirts. As word spreads you get into main street (week 2) and finally downtown (week 3). Your advance men reach further in each week, and you hire more of them (3/4/5).
- **Winning:** the show with the biggest crowd at season's end owns the city; the rest pack up and leave town.

Alternative kept as backup: the shows tour, and colors are **kinds of business every show runs in every town** (the rails, the press, the law, the talent). The wheel would then be the show's business, not a map. That's less of a picture.
