# Pass 12: resources, minions and actions as physical things

Rule from pass 11: a resource is a thing you can carry off, give away and pile up. A minion is a person standing in a spot nobody else can then use. An action is what that person physically does there.

## The shared place: winter quarters
Circuses really wintered side by side in the same towns (Baraboo, Wisconsin had Ringling and Gollmar Bros; Bridgeport had Barnum). The rival shows share one winter quarters lot, with its iron yard, rigging loft and clown alley, and get ready for the season there.

- **Rounds:** three months of winter (December, January, February). Each month ends with a preview show: the press names the best act in each discipline (contests), and performers' contracts come free (new shop row). More of the lot opens each month as the thaw comes and the bigger barns can be used (rings).
- **Minions = roustabouts**, the circus's laborers. One stands at a spot and works it, so nobody else can use it that month. You hire more each month (3/4/5).
- **Resources = gear**, one kind per discipline: **iron** for strongmen, **rigging** for aerialists, **greasepaint** for clowns, **props** for magicians, **clubs** for jugglers. It can be stolen, handed over, piled up, and counted.
- **Members = performers** who sign on for the season. A strongman joins a show that has the iron he needs (his cost is partly iron). Grey members are crew.
- **Followers = advance ticket sales.** Gear can be put on show in the preview to sell tickets (buying followers), and every star sells some tickets just by being on the poster (each member counts 1).
- **Leader:** the owner of one of the shows. The most tickets sold by spring wins: your show opens the season at the Garden.
- **Actions = the job your roustabout does at that spot**, and it's physical:
  - Gold **Golden Years** ("+2 gold in round I, +4 in round II, +6 in round III.") = hauling iron from the yard; more is delivered each month. The "Most gold" contest = the press's strongman pick goes to the show with the most iron.
  - Magenta **Help Yourself** ("Steal 3 resources in round I, 4 in round II, 5 in round III.") = sneaking into a rival's wagon and carrying off their gear.
  - Silver **Toast** ("+3 silver in round I, +6 in round II, +9 in round III. Each rival +1 silver in round I, +2 in round II, +3 in round III.") = a rigging delivery you share around the lot.

Still to fit: the colors whose actions bend turns (Purple) or copy (Green). Those are the hardest to make physical.
