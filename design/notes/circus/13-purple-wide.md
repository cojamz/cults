# Purple, wider (Cory, Nov 9: "you're too myopic, go wider")
Purple = tempo: going twice, skipping others, going last, an extra minion. It doesn't have to be an act type. Analogs from across the whole circus world:
1. The band: the bandleader cues every act and sets the tempo. A real circus band plays "Stars and Stripes Forever" to signal trouble.
2. The circus train: being first into town, or delaying a rival's train. The 1880s circus wars really did this.
3. The Ringmaster's whistle: the running order of the show, who goes on when.
4. The drumroll: suspense, making everyone wait before the big moment.
5. The tent crew: the show can't start until the tent is up, and they decide when it comes down.
6. The fortune teller: knowing what comes next and acting before it.
