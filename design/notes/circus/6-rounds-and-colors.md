# Pass 6: rounds and colors (members = your Troupe, Cory yes)

## Rounds = three towns on the route
Each round is one town:
1. **Advance week:** your advance men (minions) hit town ahead of the show and grab what they can. One man per spot: first come, first served.
2. **Show night (round end):** your Troupe performs (round-end abilities happen), and the town decides whose show it loved most in each part of town (contests: +4/+6/+10).
3. The tents come down, and everyone moves on to the next, bigger town (minions come home, the shop deals fresh).

Why more spaces open each round: town I is a farm town, town II is a city, town III is the big city. Bigger towns have more to grab. You also hire more advance men as the show grows (3/4/5).

## The wheel = the town map
Each color is **a part of town** your advance men work. A color's spaces are the spots there. The rim's contest is "who that part of town loves most." The rings are the town growing: outskirts in a farm town, then main street, then the downtown center only a big city has.

## Colors = parts of town (by what the color already does)
- **Purple → the Rail Depot.** Purple bends turn order, and the depot is where you beat a rival into town or hold them up. Hold Up ("+2/+3/+4 purple. The next turn is skipped, even if it's yours.") = literally holding up their train. Right Away ("Your next turn comes right after this one.") = an express. Overtime ("+4 purple. +1 minion this round. Take the Goes Last token: that minion goes after everyone else's.") = a late extra car with one more man on it.
- **Red → the Boarding House**, where out-of-work performers live. Red recruits and swaps members: Walk-In ("Recruit the top member of the pile. Each rival +1 red. +1 red.") = whoever knocks on the door. Tryout ("+2 red. Use a shop member's On Recruit. It stays in the shop.") = an audition in the parlor.
- **Magenta → the Back Alleys**, where billposters work at night. Help Yourself ("Steal 3 resources in round I, 4 in round II, 5 in round III.") = raiding a rival's supply wagon. Poach ("Recruit a rival's member you choose, for free. +1 magenta.") = luring their star out the back door.
- **Silver → City Hall.** Silver gives gifts that pay back: Toast ("+3/+6/+9 silver. Each rival +1/+2/+3 silver.") = a banquet for the mayor where everyone's invited but you host. The Fewest silver contest = the cleanest reputation in town.
- **White → the Newspaper.** It turns what you have into crowd, the way a good write-up does.
- Gold → the Bank, Yellow → the General Store, Echo (copy) → the Barbershop, where you hear what rivals did and do it too.

## Resources (next, not settled)
What each part of town hands you: rail passes from the depot, favors from City Hall, loot from the alleys. You spend them to hire Troupe members and to fill seats (buying followers).
