# Pass 9: how the festival fits together, told as play

## Members
**Members are performers who join your troupe.** Every member is a specialist in one discipline: a red member is an acrobat, a gold member is a strongman. Grey members are crew (the band, the riggers, the costume lady), and they'll work for any troupe. A member's ability is their trick.

## One night, as a player lives it
1. **You take the ring.** You put a token on a gold space: your troupe does a strongman number tonight. Nobody else can do that number tonight. It earns you **strongman reputation** (gold), and maybe a twist, depending on the space.
2. **You sign a performer.** A famous strongman costs 4, part of it in gold: he only joins a troupe that's already known for strength. Or you skip signing and **spend reputation to win fans** (buy followers): every point of reputation in anything can become a fan.
3. **Turns go around** until every troupe has used all its times in the ring tonight.
4. **The awards.** At the end of the night each discipline gives its award. The strongman award goes to whoever holds the most strongman reputation: +4 fans tonight, +6 at the semifinal, +10 at the gala. Your performers' end-of-night tricks happen first.
5. **Next night** the festival opens bigger numbers (the middle ring), you get one more time in the ring, and a fresh crop of performers shows up looking for a troupe (the new shop row).

**After the gala**, the troupe with the most fans wins the Grand Prix. A tie goes to the bigger troupe.

## Where it still strains (honest)
- **Reputation as money.** "Pay 4 strongman rep" means "you're famous enough in strength that he joins." Spending it means it's gone, which is odd for fame. A token you could call **"Buzz"** or **"Billing"** might read better.
- **Doing a number in a discipline you have no members in.** Read it as your troupe trying the routine and earning its name in it. It's fine, but not vivid.
- **Pickpockets stealing rivals' reputation** is a stretch ("stealing their thunder"). Stealing members (Poach: "Recruit a rival's member you choose, for free. +1 magenta.") reads fine: luring a performer away.
- **White** still has no discipline.
