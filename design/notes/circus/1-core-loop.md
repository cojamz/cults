# Cults as a Circus — pass 1: the core loop

Rule for this pass: every piece is chosen because the circus version *does the same thing*, not because the word sounds circus-y.

## The board
- **The wheel → the Big Top.** It already has three rings that open one per round, center last. A circus has three rings and the center ring is the main event. Round III is literally the center ring.
- **Rounds → three nights in one town** (Opening, Second, Closing night). The contest prize grows +4/+6/+10 because closing night draws the biggest house.

## Your stuff
- **You → a rival circus owner** pitching your tent in the same town.
- **Minions → roustabouts.** The crew you send out each night to work a ring. 3/4/5 because you hire more hands as the run goes on.
- **Followers → the Crowd** (score). Members count as 1 Crowd each: a star act brings fans of its own. Stealing Crowd = luring people out of a rival's seats.
- **Members → Acts** you sign from the booking board (the shop). Lion tamer, sword swallower, the Flying Ortegas.
- **Recruiting followers (1/5/10 resources) → selling tickets.**
- **Cloak → your Billing**: the line on your poster ("The Clown Capital of the World"): a boon all game.
- **Contests → Top Billing** in a color: most clown acts headlines the clown ring that night.

## The shakiest piece: what resources are
Each color pays for its own acts, so it needs its own thing:
- A: **Gear per act**: 🔴 meat (beasts), 🟢 greasepaint (clowns), 🟣 rigging (aerial)… concrete, tabletop-friendly, but "pay 2 greasepaint for a crowd" is odd.
- B: **Buzz per act**: talk of the town about your clowns, your beasts. Spending buzz to sign acts and sell tickets both make sense; less tactile.

## Colors, by what they do (next pass goes one at a time)
- 🟢 Green copies spaces → **Clowns** (they parody other acts).
- 🟪 Magenta steals → **Pickpockets / grifters** of the midway.
- 🟣 Purple bends turns → **Magicians** (misdirection, now-you-see-it).
- 🔴 Red takes rivals' members → **Talent scouts** poaching acts.
- 🔵 Blue bends the shop → **Advance men** who book and bury acts.
- ⚪ White turns things into followers → **Ticket wagon**.
- 🟡 Yellow changes rivals' resources → **Fortune teller**.
- Silver gifts that pay back → **Free passes** (comps that bring a crowd).
