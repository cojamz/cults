# Pass 8: the whole picture at once

Cory: "you're not keeping the whole picture in mind, you're just plugging leaks." So this pass starts from every piece the theme must explain, and picks one premise that answers all of them together. Every row was checked against every other row.

## What the theme must explain
Rival players sharing one board · tokens placed one per space · a wheel of colors, 3 rings opening one per round · 3 rounds with bigger prizes · each color has its own resource, its own contest, its own members and its own behavior · a game uses only some colors · members recruited from a shop · costs partly in a member's own color · followers as score, also bought with any resources · cloaks · the winner.

## Why every earlier pass broke
Colors were places or businesses. But a color has its own **people** (members), its own **currency**, its own **contest** and its own **way of playing**, and a game picks only some of them. The only circus thing with all of that is the **discipline**: clowns, acrobats, strongmen, jugglers…

## The premise: a circus festival
Real circus festivals exist (Monte Carlo has run one since 1974; its prizes are Golden Clowns). Troupes from everywhere meet in one big top for three nights, and the festival gives an award in each discipline every night.

| Piece | Circus | Why it holds |
|---|---|---|
| You | A troupe leader at the festival | Rivals share one big top, so one board makes sense. |
| Winning | The festival's Grand Prix: the troupe the crowd loved most | Most followers wins. |
| Rounds | Three nights: opening, semifinal, gala final | One venue, so no travel. Prizes grow each night. |
| The wheel | The festival ring from above, cut into a slice per discipline | One board all game. |
| Rings | Bigger acts are allowed as the festival goes on; the center ring opens only for the gala final | Rings open outside-in. |
| Minions | Your troupe's **times in the ring**. Each token is your troupe taking the ring in that discipline tonight. | One per space: only one troupe does each number per night. 3/4/5 because later nights run longer shows. |
| Members | Your troupe: the performers who join you | They're who performs. Their abilities are their tricks. |
| Colors | Disciplines | Each has its own people, its own award and its own style, and not every festival features every discipline. |
| Resources | **Reputation in a discipline** (acrobat rep, clown rep…) | A star acrobat only joins a troupe known for acrobatics, so a member's cost is partly in its own color. |
| Followers | Fans | Rep can be turned into fans (buying followers); a star brings fans of their own (a member counts 1). |
| Contests | That discipline's award each night (+4/+6/+10) | Every festival gives a Golden ___ per discipline. |
| Cloak | Your troupe's specialty | A boon all game plus 1 rep in it. |

## Colors as disciplines (each color's behavior is the discipline's style)
- **Red → Acrobats.** Contest "biggest group of members of one color" = **the tallest human pyramid**. Promote ("+3 red. Swap any member for one costing up to 3 more, from another cult or the shop.") and Demote = moving people up or down the pyramid. Acrobat families recruiting is how real troupes grew.
- **Gold → Strongmen.** Contest "Most gold held" = **who lifts the most**. Golden Years ("+2 gold in round I, +4 in round II, +6 in round III.") = the weights get heavier each night.
- **Silver → Aerialists.** Contest "Fewest silver held" = **the lightest on the wire**. Silver gives to rivals (Toast: "+3/+6/+9 silver. Each rival +1/+2/+3 silver.") because an aerialist sheds weight onto everyone else.
- **Yellow → Jugglers.** Yellow goes broad and picks its colors: Harvest ("+3 in round I, +4 in round II, +5 in round III, in colors you choose."). Contest "Most member colors" = **the most balls in the air**.
- **Green → Clowns.** Green copies spaces, and clowns parody every other act. Contest "most minions on green" = **who holds the ring longest**.
- **Purple → Magicians.** Misdirection bends the order: Hold Up ("+2/+3/+4 purple. The next turn is skipped, even if it's yours.") = the next act vanishes.
- **Magenta → Pickpockets.** Stage pickpocketing is a real act. Help Yourself ("Steal 3 resources in round I, 4 in round II, 5 in round III."). Contest "most resources held" = **the fattest haul**.
- **White** (turns what you have into followers) → Ringmasters' announcers? Weakest fit; open.

This premise isn't settled yet. The remaining open spot is White.
