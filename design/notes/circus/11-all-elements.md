# Pass 11: every element, one picture (Cory's list)

- **Leader:** a ringmaster who owns a travelling circus. The goal is the biggest crowd after three seasons: "the Greatest Show on Earth."
- **Colors:** the circus disciplines (strongmen, aerialists, acrobats, jugglers, clowns, magicians, pickpockets). Each is a tight circle of performers with its own craft and its own way of doing things.
- **Minions:** your talent agents. You send one into a discipline's circle to make a deal. One agent per deal. You hire more agents as the show grows (3/4/5).
- **Actions:** the deal your agent makes, and it works the way that craft works. Magicians' misdirection shuffles who goes next. Pickpockets lift from rivals. Clowns copy someone else's act. Jugglers handle any colors.
- **Rounds:** three seasons. More (and bigger) deals open each season as your name grows (the rings). At season's end each circle names its favorite show (contests), and contracts turn over (new shop row).
- **Resources:** favors a circle owes you (strongman favors, clown favors). They're spent because favors get called in.
- **Members:** performers who join your circus. A strongman signs only if the strongmen vouch for you (his cost is partly strongman favors). Grey members are crew (band, riggers) who work for anyone.
- **Followers:** your crowd. A star brings their own fans (each member counts 1). You can call in any favors to fill seats (buying followers).

Example: your agent works the strongmen (a gold space) and they owe you 3 favors. You call them in and a famous strongman joins your show.
