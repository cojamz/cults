# Pass 2: who are you, what are you doing, what did you win?

The test for any theme (Cory, Nov 9): a player should know at a glance *who they are*, *what they're doing on a turn*, and *what winning makes them*. The wheel must look like the place where that happens, not an abstract dial.

## Circus: "The Succession"
- **Who you are:** a troupe leader inside one great circus. The old Ringmaster is retiring after this town. Whoever wins the crowd over the last three nights takes the top hat.
- **What winning makes you:** the new Ringmaster. One clear goal.
- **Why players share one board:** it's one tent. You aren't rival circuses (why would those share rings?); you're rivals inside the same show, all fighting for the same crowd's love.
- **The wheel, seen from above, is the tent:**
  - Outer ring (night I) = **the midway**: barkers, sideshows, drumming up a crowd outside.
  - Middle ring (night II) = **the hippodrome track**: the parade, horses, the procession.
  - Center (night III) = **the center ring**: the spotlight, the big acts.
  - So the game walks you from outside the tent into the spotlight. Rings opening outside-in already does this.
  - Each color's slice is that act's corner of the tent: the clowns' alley, the cat cages, the rigging.
- **A turn:** send a hand to work a spot in the tent (minion), then sign an act or win over some of the crowd (recruit).
- **Followers = the Crowd's favor:** people chanting your name. Followers you steal = fans who switch to cheering for you.
- **Contests = headlining:** whoever has the most clown acts closes the night in the clown spot and the crowd roars for them (+4/+6/+10, louder each night).
- **Tie at game end goes to more members:** the leader with the bigger troupe.

Weak spots to solve next: what resources are (gear vs buzz), and why the old Ringmaster would let you steal from each other (answer: he's watching who's ruthless enough).

## Two others, run through the same test
- **Cults (today):** who you are and what you win are clear (prophet, biggest flock). The wheel is the weak part. It could become a ritual circle, but it stays abstract.
- **City machine politics:** you're a ward boss, the wheel is the city map (suburbs in round I, downtown in round II, City Hall in the center in round III), and you win by becoming Mayor. Very cohesive, but less charming than the circus.
