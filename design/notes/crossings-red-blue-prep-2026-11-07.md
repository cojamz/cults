# Red × Blue, prep by the color-interactions method (Claude, Nov 7, not pitched yet)

1. Contests and verbs. Red: most members of one color (grey counts); swaps members between cults and the shop, recruits by space without the recruit, borrows an On Recruit. Blue: most members; sends shop members to the discard pile and refills, extra recruits.
2. Moments. Red: a member leaves a cult; a member joins a cult outside the recruit; a member goes from a cult into the shop. Blue: shop member to the discard pile; the shop changes mid-round; several members join one cult in a turn. Shared: a member joins a cult; the shop changes mid-round (Red's shop swap leaves a member in the shop; Blue's clears refill it).
3. Already rewarded: recruiting (Proselytizer shape), shop member leaving (Patroness shape), member leaving a cult (Martyrs). Unrewarded: a member arriving in the shop from a cult (only Red today); a cult growing by two or more in one turn (Blue's extra recruits; Red's free recruits plus the recruit).
4. Open: cards on those two moments, then absence checks both ways. Waiting on Cory's yes to the reading of the exercise.
