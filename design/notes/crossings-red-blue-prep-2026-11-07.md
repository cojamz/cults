# Red × Blue, prep by the color-interactions method (Claude, Nov 7, not pitched yet)

1. Contests and verbs. Red: most members of one color (grey counts); swaps members between cults and the shop, recruits by space without the recruit, borrows an On Recruit. Blue: most members; sends shop members to the discard pile and refills, extra recruits.
2. Moments. Red: a member leaves a cult; a member joins a cult outside the recruit; a member goes from a cult into the shop. Blue: shop member to the discard pile; the shop changes mid-round; several members join one cult in a turn. Shared: a member joins a cult; the shop changes mid-round (Red's shop swap leaves a member in the shop; Blue's clears refill it).
3. Already rewarded: recruiting (Proselytizer shape), shop member leaving (Patroness shape), member leaving a cult (Martyrs). Unrewarded: a member arriving in the shop from a cult (only Red today); a cult growing by two or more in one turn (Blue's extra recruits; Red's free recruits plus the recruit).
4. Open: cards on those two moments, then absence checks both ways. Waiting on Cory's yes to the reading of the exercise.

## Paper test (Nov 7, after the skill rewrite)
Shared moment both cause: members join your cult beyond your one recruit (Red: a space recruits for you or swaps one in; Blue: extra recruits). Also caused by Magenta (Poach) and Yellow (Housewarming), so no color is needed.

A. Member: "Whenever two or more members join your cult in one turn, +2 followers." (2 is a fill-in)
- Round II, 4 players: you hold it and 3 members. Recruit Twice ("Recruit an extra time this turn. +3 blue.") with 8 resources: two members, +2 followers on top. Best other round II space, Promote ("Swap any member with one in another cult or the shop costing up to 3 more. +3 red."), joins one and loses one: nothing. Gain +2 and a reason to fight for multi-recruit spaces.
- Round III: Spree (3 joins) +2; Initiation plus your recruit, +2. Green copying Spree triggers it too. Fires about 2 to 4 times a game: 4 to 8 followers.
- Without Blue: Red's space recruits plus your recruit trigger it. Without Red: Blue's extra recruits. With neither: Poach or Housewarming plus your recruit. Never dead.
- Who feels it: rivals block Recruit Twice, Spree and Initiation from its owner. No choice inside the card.
- Intuitive: one count you see (members that came in this turn). Fun: turns big turns into bigger ones. Interacts: Red, Blue, Magenta, Yellow all trigger it. Powerful: moderate. Engine: rewards stacking a space recruit with your own recruit. Its color: Blue (recruit more).
- Judged: survives, but it rewards what already happens; closer to restating than new. Send with that said.

B. Member: "Whenever a member joins your cult and you already have one of its color, +1 follower."
- Round I: 0 or 1 members, rarely fires. Round II/III: fires on most joins for a deep cult. A Red player's swaps into their color and a Blue player's recruits both pay.
- Who feels it: nobody but the owner. Small "+1 when" trigger (weak per the skill). Cut.

C. Swap two-for-one: already in his pocket, not resent.

Result: only A survives, and it's thin. Red × Blue's real tie is the two-for-one he kept; the rest of their meeting already happens.
