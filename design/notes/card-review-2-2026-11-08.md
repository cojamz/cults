# Card review 2: every color, through your notes and the new contests (Nov 8)

This page goes color by color: the cards that most need work, what could replace each one, small fixes, and at most one question per color.
It's a new page. Card review 1 is unchanged.
It proposes 30 card changes: 19 new or reworked cards, 11 wording or number fixes.

Labels: **[default]** is the one I'd pick. **(Claude)** marks my fill-ins (prices, numbers). Quotes are your words. Prices are what a player pays.
Every idea was played out at a 4-player table and checked against your past yeses and nos. 18 of 37 ideas were cut on that pass (list at the end).

## Red
Contest: most members of one color (grey counts as a color).

**Picked**
- **Walk-In** (round I) "Recruit the top member of the pile. Each rival +1 red. +1 red.": already cut ("I don't want randomness involved"). Nothing passed for its place. See the question.
- **Mob** "Round End: +2 followers per rival with fewer red members than you.": already cut ("don't like this"). Nothing passed for its place.
- **Mentor** "On Recruit: recruit the cheapest member in the shop.": "too hard to build around".
- **Deprogrammers** "Whenever you take a rival's member out of their cult, they lose 1 follower.": "I don't like it".

**Mentor's place**
- **Long-Lost Cousins** [default], price 13 (Claude): "On Recruit: recruit a shop member of the color you have the most of, for free." (Claude dropped "or the discard pile" from the first draft: taking from the discard pile is Blue's.)
  - At the table: round III, you hold 3 yellow and a rival holds 4 green. You buy this and take the 10-cost yellow free. Now you're tied at 4 and split the +10.
  - Risk: no price cap. At its best a 13-cost card brings in a 14-cost card free.

**Deprogrammers' place**
- **Concerned Parents** [default], price 5 (Claude): "Whenever a rival takes one of your members, +3 followers."
  - At the table: a rival's **Body Snatchers** ("Round Start: swap one of your members with any rival's member.") takes your 10-cost. You get +3. Next time, **Promote** ("Swap any member with one in another cult or the shop costing up to 3 more. +3 red.") looks at someone else's cult instead.
  - Risk: rivals just take from someone else, so it may fire only once or twice a game. That's also the point: it keeps them off you.

**Fixes**
- **Seat Filler** "Round Start: you may give this to the shop and recruit a shop member costing up to 3 more, for free." becomes "Round Start: you may discard this and recruit a shop member costing up to 3 more, for free." (your "needs to say discard"). The next player can no longer pick it up from the shop.

**Question**
1. Walk-In's round I place: should it still give a free member, or do something else? Every free-member version read as a smaller **Initiation** ("Recruit a member in the shop costing 10 or less, for free. +1 red."). [something else]

## Blue
Contest: most members.

**Picked**
- **Window Shopper** "On Recruit and at Round Start: send 2 shop members to the discard pile. +1 of each one's color.": pays resources (you called "+1 resource" boring), and it's the third Blue card that clears the shop on your own timing.
- **Regular** "Whenever you buy followers, +1 blue.": a "+1 resource" trigger. Nothing beat it; it stays.
- **Critic** "Whenever a rival recruits a blue member, +1 follower.": small, and it waits on rivals. Nothing beat it; it stays.

**Window Shopper's place**
- **Thrift Shoppers** [default], price 7 (Claude): "On Use, once per round: recruit a member from the discard pile, paying its cost."

**Question**
1. When you called the grey **Big Spender** ("On Use, once per game: recruit twice this turn.") "too blatantly blue", did you mean it belongs in Blue? If yes, a Blue version could take Critic's place. [no, Critic stays]

## White
Contest: fewest members.

**Picked**
- **Recluse** "Round End: +2 followers per rival with more members than you.": "too overlapping with the competition now".
- **Heiresses** "On Use, once per game: +1 white per member you have.": pays for holding members, against White's contest. "supposed to get changed".
- **Old Money** "Game End: +1 follower per white member you have.": "should stay and be reworked to be a little better".
- **Allowance** (round I) "+3 white in round I, +4 in round II, +5 in round III. This turn, if you recruit a member, +1 follower in round I, +2 in round II, +3 in round III.": its bonus pays you to recruit, which loses you White's contest.

**Recluse's and Heiresses' places** (your move: "should be white")
- **Guest of Honor**, price 5: "On Use, once per round: give a rival one of your members. +4 followers."
- **Godparent**, price 7: "On Use, once per game: give one of your members to a rival. Gain followers equal to its cost."
  - Risk: in White, Guest of Honor's gift also helps you win the contest every round. It may need to cost more.

**Old Money, reworked**
- **Old Money** [default], price 8: "Game End: +2 followers per white member your rivals have."
  - At the table: you give two members away with Guest of Honor, and rivals buy two white members themselves. At game end, +8. The current card would give you +2 and cost you the contest.
  - Risk: close in shape to Silver's **Patron Saints** ("Game End: +1 follower per silver member, anyone's.").

**Fixes**
- **Allowance**: the bonus pays on buying followers instead: "+3 white in round I, +4 in round II, +5 in round III. This turn, if you buy followers, +1 follower in round I, +2 in round II, +3 in round III."
- **Farewell** "Cast out one of your members to the discard pile, gain followers equal to its cost. +1 white." becomes "Discard one of your members. Gain followers equal to its cost. +1 white." (your "just say discard").
- **Estate Sale** "Cast out one of your members to the discard pile, gain white equal to its cost. +1 white." becomes "Discard one of your members. Gain white equal to its cost. +1 white."
- **Downsizers** "On Use, once per round: cast out one of your members to the discard pile. +4 white." becomes "On Use, once per round: discard one of your members. +4 white." (or see the question).
- **Magpie** "On Use, once per game: turn all your resources into white.": a magpie is a bird. Rename it **Hoarders** (Claude).
- Flag, no change: **Brokers** "Whenever a White action gives you followers, +1 follower." counts as 3 members, so it costs White 2 extra in its own contest every round.

**Question**
1. Downsizers: pay "+2 followers" instead of "+4 white"? [yes, +2 followers]

## Silver
Contest: fewest silver.

**Picked**
- **Silver Cloak** "Whenever you gift silver, +2 silver.": every time it fires, its holder loses ground in the contest. You said no to followers on it: "a little different from silver somehow".
- **Sweet Nothings** (round II) "+3 silver. Give rivals up to 3 of your silver, split as you choose: +2 of any colors each.": "weird". Giving all 3 is now always best, so there's no choice.
- **Sweet Talk** (round II) "+3 silver. Then turn as many of your silver as you want into other colors.": "too close to yellow". Nothing beat it. See the question.
- Open member places after the cuts (**Caterer**, **Debutantes**, **Debt Collectors**) and the moves to White: five.

**Silver Cloak**
- [default] "Whenever you gift silver, +2 of any colors."
  - At the table: round I, **Party Gifts** ("Give rivals up to 3 silver from the bank, split as you choose. +2 followers per silver you give.") gives each rival 1. You get +6 followers and 2 blue toward a member, and still hold 0 silver for the contest.
  - Risk: if "different from silver" means a different trigger too, take the next one.
- "Whenever any player gifts silver, +1 of any color."
  - At the table: a Red player holds it. Each time the Silver player gifts, the Red player gets 1 of any color. The cloak goes to whoever isn't playing Silver.
  - Risk: quiet.

**Sweet Nothings' place**
- **High Tide** [default], back in round II: "Each rival +1 silver. Then gain 1 follower per silver the rival with the most silver holds."
  - At the table: round II, you're at 0 and the richest rival holds 4. After High Tide they're at 5: +5 followers for you. Every rival now holds silver, so you also win the round's +6.
  - Risk: you once called it "way too strong", then kept it. Its numbers are smaller in round II.

**An open member place**
- **Silver Barons** [default], price 8 (Claude): "Round End: +1 follower per 3 silver all players hold together." (your "i also like barons")
  - At the table: end of round II, rivals hold 4, 3 and 2 silver: +3. If you hold silver too, Barons pays more, but you lose the contest.
  - Risk: four member places are still open after it.

**Fixes**
- **Kingmakers** "Round End: give a rival 3 silver from the bank. +3 followers." becomes "+2 followers" (your "maybe just a little too many followers"; the 2 is Claude's).

**Question**
1. Sweet Talk's place: keep it as Silver's way to get rid of your own silver, or make it a second card that pays you for silver rivals hold? [keep Sweet Talk]

## Purple
Contest: whoever placed the round's last minion.

**Picked**
- **Bouncers** "On Recruit: the next two rivals' turns are skipped.": a skipped turn moves to the end of the round, so it can hand a rival the last minion and the contest. It never touches the Goes Last token.
- **Picket Line** "Whenever a rival takes a Purple space, +1 purple.": a "+1 resource" trigger. Paying +1 follower instead would copy Silver's **High Society** ("Whenever a rival takes a Silver space, +1 follower."), so it stays as printed. See the question.
- **Pocket Watch** (the cloak) "Whenever you take the Goes Last token, +3 purple.": your note, "going last is secretly kind of good", and it has the same trigger as **Sleepyheads** ("Whenever you take the Goes Last token, +3 followers.").

**Bouncers' place** (your "a purple that gives the go last token")
- **Latecomers** [default], price 7 (Claude): "On Recruit: take the Goes Last token: your next minion is placed after everyone else's."
  - At the table: round II, a rival took **Last Call** ("+1 purple. +1 follower and +1 purple per minion you have left. Take the Goes Last token: your other minions are placed after everyone else's."). You recruit Latecomers on your third turn and take the token. Your fourth minion goes last: +6.
  - Risk: a rival with a minion left can take it back with **Sleep In** ("+4 purple in round I, +5 in round II, +6 in round III. Take the Goes Last token: your next minion is placed after everyone else's.") or **Overtime** ("+4 purple. +1 minion this round. Take the Goes Last token: that minion is placed after everyone else's."), if one is open.

**Pocket Watch**
- [default] "Whenever any player takes the Goes Last token, +2 purple."
  - At the table: round II, one rival takes Sleep In and another takes Last Call: +4 purple for you without going last yourself.
  - Risk: still quiet. That's by design for a cloak.

**Fixes**
- **Gossip** "Whenever a player's turn is skipped, +1 follower.": the game already counts taking the Goes Last token as a skipped turn, but no card says so. Add to Gossip: "(Taking the Goes Last token counts.)" (Claude's words).
- Rules page: a skipped turn goes "to the end of the round", not "later in the same round", so a table plays **Hold Up** ("+2 purple in round I, +3 in round II, +4 in round III. Whoever's turn is next skips it, even yours.") right.

**Question**
1. Picket Line: keep it, or put a second member that gives the Goes Last token in its place (your "maybe a couple members")? [keep it for now]
