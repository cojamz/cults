# Fan Club: making the name and the card agree

Live: **Fan Club** (Green, 2 green + 3 any, counts as 3 members): "On Recruit: +1 green per Green space with anyone's minion."
Cory, Nov 4: "Fan club really makes no sense." Read as: nothing about fans in what it does. It counts as 3 members, so a plural name stays.

## 1. Fan Club [pick]: new text, the name stays
"On Recruit: copy an action the player with the most followers has a minion on."
- Fans copy their idol, and copying is Green's verb.
- One played turn, round II, 4 players: Ana leads on followers and has a minion on **Toast** ("+3 silver in round I, +6 in round II, +9 in round III. Each rival +1 silver in round I, +2 in round II, +3 in round III."). You recruit Fan Club and copy Toast, so you get +6 silver and each rival gets +2. Today's Fan Club in the same spot, with 2 or 3 Green spaces taken, gives +2 or +3 green.
- The choice: you pick which of the leader's actions to copy, and the best one for you is never automatic. A leader with no minion on the board yet gives nothing, so you buy it once they have placed.
- Who feels it: the leader is being copied. Cards that answer copying come into play. **Agents** ("Rivals can't copy your actions.") blocks it. **Bait** ("Whenever anyone copies one of your actions, they −1 follower and you +1 follower.") punishes it. **Choir** ("Whenever you copy a space, +1 green."), **Wallflower** ("Whenever you copy an action, double the resources it gives you.") and **Impressionist** ("Whenever any player copies an action, +1 of its color.") feed off it.
- Compared with **Tribute Band** ("On Use, once per game: choose a rival. Do every action they have a minion on this round.", costs 14): Fan Club copies one action, once, from one fixed rival (the leader).
- Principles: intuitive (fans copy the leader); fun (you read the leader's board); interacts (the leader, Agents, Bait, Choir, Wallflower); feels powerful (in round III it copies the leader's best space); engine (with Wallflower and Choir); new way into Green (copying chosen by who is winning).

## 2. Onlookers: new name, the text stays
"On Recruit: +1 green per Green space with anyone's minion."
- People watching the Green spaces fill up: the name says what the card counts.
- Played: round II, 3 of the 5 Green spaces are taken, so you get +3 green. Nothing changes at the table except the name.
- Who feels it: no one. That is why it is not the pick, since the card stays as quiet as it was.

Cut on paper: "Whenever the follower leader takes a space, +1 green" (up to 5 green a round from one member is too much for its cost, and it lasts all game). "Round Start: copy a space the leader used last round" (the board is cleared by then, so a table can't see which spaces those were).
