# Yellow redesign, spaces first (Nov 7)

Brief: design/notes/redesigns/BRIEF.md. Cory, Nov 7 9:22pm: "Not all of the yellow spaces feel like winners."
Live cards from `live-board.mjs yellow --members`. Contest at the default table (By color): **most full sets** (one of every color in play), set by Cory Nov 3. The live-board script prints the "As printed" contest (most yellow), which is not what a By-color table plays.
Numbers are Claude's fill-ins. Bands for comparison: round I spaces pay about 2 to 4, round II 4 to 6 or a member changing hands, round III a burst (Initiation is a free member costing 10 or less; Largesse is +10 followers).

## The color in one line, and what is wrong with the spaces

"Yellow rewards going broad, i.e. having a combination of colors and being flexible. it also allows you to translate currencies" (Cory, Oct 30). Anchor: **Makeover** "+4 yellow, then rearrange your resources into any mix of colors." (Cory: "i like makeover as is").

Four of the six spaces only hand you resources: **Harvest** "+3 in round I, +4 in round II, +5 in round III, in colors you choose.", **Survey** "+1 yellow per minion on the busiest color's spaces. +1 yellow.", **New Blood** "+2 yellow. This turn, if you recruit a member of a color none of your members have, +2 yellow in round I, +3 in round II, +4 in round III." and **Melting Pot** "+2 yellow, and +1 of each color among your members." Red, Green, Magenta and Blue each have one such space. Yellow's own goal (full sets) is paid by no space at all: Makeover can arrange sets, nothing on the wheel rewards holding them.

Where Cory's own hand is on each space (the taste guide's first rule: he keeps a current card unless he has said he dislikes it):
- Harvest: his numbers, Oct 30. Keep, not a slot.
- Survey: his rule, Nov 5 ("+1 yellow per minion on the most occupied color... that should be the functionality"), his name.
- New Blood: his change, Nov 4 ("should give additional yellow rather than followers").
- Housewarming: Claude's, Nov 4; Cory only moved its cost cap. Yellow's biggest turn. Not a slot (borrowed free recruit, but nothing found beats its moment; see the end).
- Melting Pot: his rule, Nov 5 ("should give 1 color for each different member color").
- Makeover: his. Keep.

Slots: Melting Pot, New Blood, Survey (the three weak spaces), and Seamstress (the one member whose top case breaks the game, and it breaks through Makeover, a space).

Seeds generated for the space slots (about 30, each a verb on a piece) and why each died, so they are not pitched again:
- Window Shopping "gain a shop member's colored part" (refused Oct 30, "isnt really aligned with yello"). Haggle / pay a colored part in any colors / pay it with yellow this turn (refused Oct 30, "too focused on member costs"; Harvest already lets you pick the color you need). Mixed Bag "+1 per color you hold none of" (refused Oct 30) and Odd Jobs "+1 of each color you have no member of" (same shape, and it pays less the broader you go). Keep Up / Good Neighbors / Sister Cities "+1 of each color a rival holds or has a member of" (refused Oct 30 as small ball). Wander / Tag Along / Crossroads "+1 of each color with minions on it" (Wander refused, "max +2 round 1"; Crossroads pays 5 flat from round II, a Harvest twin).
- Any space that changes rivals' resources: Dilute, Big Dilute, Bleach, Fade, "every player's color X becomes Y" (Cory twice soured on spaces that do this: "I don't like our Round II actions", "I still don't like Dilute"; Counterfeiters already does it, his own aggressive member). Swapping piles or colors with a rival (Hard Bargain was a trap in bot games; Barterers flopped; a whole-pile swap is a Magenta steal).
- Rearranging your own pile on another space (four cards already do it; Trade Seeds "never felt useful"). Rainmaker or Fill Out on the wheel (Makeover then Rainmaker made 80; Fill Out left with "I don't like our Round II actions"). Square Up (round II "seems confusing"; uncapped it pays 16 in round II).
- Melting Pot at +2 per color (Cory cut it to +1 himself: "melting pot is too strong"). Mixed Company "+2 followers per color among your members" (scoring on member variety is ruled out).
- Turning full sets into followers (Exhibition, Quilter on the wheel): White's job (Exchange, Cash In). Swapping a member for a new color (Red's Promote). An extra recruit of a new color (Blue's job). Recruit from the discard pile (Blue's and the backlog's Dumpster Dive). Doing another color's space (Green's job; Rainbow was "so similar to green").
- Survey paying the busiest color, or in colors you choose: a tiny change, or Harvest that waits.

One new space survived: **Full House**.

## Slot 1. Melting Pot (round II)

Current: **Melting Pot** "+2 yellow, and +1 of each color among your members."
Its best turn: round II, members in all 4 colors (or Dabblers): +2 yellow and one of each, 6 resources, one full set. Round III the same 6. Why weak: the smallest round II payout beside Housewarming's free member, it hands you a full set as Caravan does every round start, and nobody at the table notices it. What a player would miss: a full set for a broad cult, and Dabblers giving one of everything (Cory's Nov 5 words).

### Option A. Full House
Printed: **Full House** (round II) "+2 followers per full set you hold. +2 yellow."

- Played turn, round II, 4 players (Red, Blue, White, Yellow in play), my 6th placement. I hold 3 red, 2 blue, 2 white, 4 yellow: 2 full sets. Full House: +4 followers, +2 yellow, and I still hold both sets for the round's full-sets contest (+6). Best other space for me: Promote "Swap any member with one in another cult or the shop costing up to 3 more. +3 red.": my 5-cost member becomes an 8-cost one (about +1 or +2 worth and a better ability) and +3 red. Melting Pot in its place would have paid 6 resources. The gain over Melting Pot: 4 followers banked now and the sets kept, against 4 extra resources.
- Round III, the same space: I take Makeover first (24 resources become 6 full sets), then Full House: +12 followers, +2 yellow, and the contest's +10 at round end. Initiation is a free member costing 10 or less; Largesse is +10 followers. Without Makeover, 4 sets: +8 followers.
- Top case: built around it (Caravan, Jack-of-All-Trades "Your yellow counts as any color in your full sets.", Makeover the turn before): 6 sets, +12 followers and +2 yellow, about 1.4 times Largesse. With today's Seamstress (doubles to 12 sets) it pays 24: that is Seamstress's break, fixed in slot 4. With the capped Seamstress, 9 sets, 18 followers, a three-card round III (two spaces and a once-per-game use).
- Choices: none for anyone else. The taker's choice is when: hold the sets and take it late (and risk a rival taking the space first), or spend the resources on a member now.
- Who feels it: the rivals in the full-sets race (you hold your sets rather than spend them, so you stay in the contest); a Counterfeiters "On Use, once per round: turn all of a rival's resources into one color you choose." owner now has a clear target; a rival in a full-sets race has a reason to take the space first (round II has only 2 Yellow spaces).
- Ties through: full sets, the full-sets contest, followers. Works in any game with Yellow in it; needs no other color.
- Ledger: "followers per full set" has been pitched three times without a yes or a no: Full Baskets "+4 followers per full set" (Oct 30, round III; he took the simpler rearrange instead), Tailors "Round Start: +1 follower per full set" (built Nov 4, not picked), Harmonist at game end (retired for Seamstress). Never as a round II space. Flag this to Cory.
- Six principles. Intuitive: one sentence, "full set" is already the contest's word. Fun: the hold-or-spend turn and a target for rivals. Interacts: with the full-sets race and Counterfeiters, through the sets. Powerful: 8 to 12 followers in round III without spending a resource. Engine: pays off Caravan, Jack-of-All-Trades, Makeover, Night Traders. Its color: the first space that pays Yellow's own goal.
- Against the current card (Fix 1): round II, Melting Pot pays 6 resources; Full House pays 4 followers and keeps 2 sets in hand. Round III, 6 resources against 8 to 12 followers. A bigger and different moment.

### Option B. Keep Melting Pot
Kept if Cory wants Yellow's round II to stay about members of many colors (his Oct 30 note: "more currency manipulation than it is treating different members").

**Pick: Full House.** Risk to say: Cory set Melting Pot's rule himself on Nov 5.

## Slot 2. New Blood (round I)

Current: **New Blood** "+2 yellow. This turn, if you recruit a member of a color none of your members have, +2 yellow in round I, +3 in round II, +4 in round III."
Best turn: round I, my first member: +2, recruit a red member, +2 more (+2 again with the Guest Book cloak "Whenever you recruit a member of a color none of your members have, +2 yellow."): 4 to 6 yellow. Round III: usually +2 (most cults hold every color by then). Why weak: the same "gain, plus more if you recruit" shape as Gather, Stash and Allowance, and the same trigger as Guest Book and Housewarming.

Seeds tried for this slot: every survivor-shaped idea above died. Full House fits here too (in round I it pays +2 yellow early and grows on its own as sets build), but early in round I nobody holds a set, so it would be a second "take it late" space beside Survey, the very feeling Cory is complaining about. It belongs in round II.

What players would miss: New Blood pays well on the first turns of the game, when resources are scarcest, and it is the only round I Yellow space that pays early except Harvest.

**Pick: keep.** No candidate beats its round I moment.

## Slot 3. Survey (round I)

Current: **Survey** "+1 yellow per minion on the busiest color's spaces. +1 yellow."
Best turn: last turn of round III, Red's 6 spaces full: 7 yellow. Early round I: 2. Why weak: you wait and take yellow; nobody changes a move because of it.

Every candidate for this slot was either refused before (Wander, Leaders' Tax in its two forms, which Cory replaced with this card), a tiny change (pay in the busiest color), or Harvest that waits (pay in colors you choose).

What players would miss: the late-round pick that reads the board, and it is two days old, in Cory's own words.

**Pick: keep.**

## Slot 4. Seamstress (member)

Current: **Seamstress** [4 yellow + 4 any as a player sees it, worth 1] "On Use, once per game: double your full sets."
Usual turn: round III, 3 sets: +3 of each color, now 6 sets, take the +10 contest. Top case: Makeover first (+4 yellow, 24 resources rearranged into 6 sets), then Seamstress: +24 resources, 12 sets, the +10 contest. About 30 followers' worth in one round III turn against Initiation's free member costing 10 or less: three times the best round III space. Breaks, through a space.

### Option A. Seamstress, capped
Printed: **Seamstress** "On Use, once per game: double your full sets, at most 3 more."

- Played turn, round III, 4 players. I hold 3 sets (12 of 4 colors) and a rival holds 3: use it, +3 of each, 6 sets, I take the +10 alone. Same as today's usual turn.
- Top case: after Makeover, 6 sets become 9: +12 resources (12 followers' worth at most) and the contest. About Initiation's size. No break. With Full House after it: 9 sets, 18 followers, three cards in one round.
- Choices: only the owner's, when to use it.
- Who feels it: the rival tied in the full-sets race; a Counterfeiters hit before the use leaves nothing to double.
- Ties through: full sets, the contest.
- Ledger: no verdict on Seamstress yet. The cap is a number, the kind of fix Cory makes himself.
- Six principles. Intuitive: one sentence and a number. Fun: a contest swing at the count. Interacts: through the full-sets race. Powerful: +3 sets in one go. Engine: pays off Caravan, Makeover, Jack-of-All-Trades. Its color: doubling what you collected.

### Option B. Keep Seamstress
Only if Makeover and Seamstress together are seen as a fair round III combo. It is three times the best round III space.

**Pick: Option A, capped at 3 more.**

Cut for this slot: Tailors "Round Start: +1 follower per full set you hold." (about 4 followers a game for 8, too small for a member; built and tested Nov 4, not picked).

## Not a slot: Housewarming
**Housewarming** "+2 yellow. Recruit a shop member of a color none of your members have, costing up to 7, for free." It is a free recruit, Red's job (Initiation "Recruit a member in the shop costing 10 or less, for free. +1 red."), but it is Yellow's biggest turn and nothing tried gave Yellow a round II moment of its own that beats it. Full House beside it gives round II one turn that is only Yellow's.

Every round I idea Yellow could print has been refused or is a twin: the color's verb (any color can become any color) is already spent on Harvest and Makeover. One sharp question would unlock round I: may a Yellow space change rivals' resources again (as Counterfeiters does), or should round I stay about your own pile?
