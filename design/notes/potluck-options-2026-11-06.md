## 1. Sweet Nothings [pick]
"+3 silver. Give rivals up to 3 of your silver, split as you choose: +2 of any colors each."
- Built already (REPLACE11=sweetnothings, unit tests in replace11.test.js); first pitched for Sweet Talk's slot, Cory Nov 4: "Why even get the plus three silver? ... Oh, I guess you don't have to, right? Yeah. This is okay." Sweet Talk then stayed ("I like sweet talk"), so this slot is free for it.
- Turn, round II, 4 players: you hold 3 silver. Take it: 6 silver, give 3 (2 to the rival last in followers, 1 to another), +6 of any colors, keep 3. Potluck today: +2 silver and 3 resources rivals pick (their least useful). Sweet Talk: 6 silver turned into 6 colors, 0 silver left.
- Choices: how many to give (more colors vs keeping silver for the most-silver contest and not feeding rivals) and to whom (the trailer, or the rival who can't use silver). Neither side always wins.
- Feels it: receivers get a gift; the rival racing you for most silver sees others catch up.
- Rule: every choice is the taker's, on their turn.
- Principles: intuitive (give silver, 2 colors each); fun (the table sees who you feed); interacts (silver contest, Socialites, Freeloaders); powerful (6 colors); engine (feeds Freeloaders/Socialites owners); its color's theme (giving, paid for giving).

## 2. Potluck, you pick (rework, Claude's words)
"+2 silver. Take 1 resource you choose from each rival; each then gains 2 silver."
- Turn, round II, 4 players: +2 silver, take the blue the leader needs, the red another needs, the purple a third needs; each rival nets +1 (2 silver for 1). You +5.
- Choices: which color from whom is yours; the rivals choose nothing. A real sting (you take what they were saving) with a payment.
- Rule: fixed. Risk: Cory dislikes Potluck itself, not only the rule; this keeps its shape.
- Principles: intuitive; interacts with every rival; theme (paid in silver for what you take). Not powerful beyond Potluck today.
