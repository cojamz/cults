# Color review: Green (Nov 7)

Same lens as design/notes/color-review-purple-2026-11-07.md. Live cards Nov 7; numbers are Claude's fill-ins.

## Green in one line
"Whatever the best play was, I get it too." Anchor: Greatest Hits ("Copy the On Recruit of each of your members, in any order. +1 green.").
Copying is already the cleanest cross-color tie in the game: it touches whatever spaces and members are in this game and never names a color. What's off: five members copy nearly the same thing (Understudy and Stunt Double; Impersonator, Impostors and Doppelganger), and nothing copies the two shared pieces every color fights over at round end, the contests, or the shop row. Red's Tryout ("Use the On Recruit effect of a shop member; it stays in the shop.") is a copy on a Red space: overlap, for Red's page.

## Spaces: keep all six
Build Up, Echo, Reprise, Sample, Cover, Greatest Hits. Each copies whatever the table has; Build Up is the plain gain.

## Stunt Double → Copycats (redesign)
Live: [3 green + 4 any] "On Recruit: copy the action you just took." (repeats Understudy's "copy the action taken the previous turn")
Proposed: **Copycats** "Round End: choose a contest you don't lead; you tie its leader."
Played: round II, 4 players. Ana leads Most silver with 9; Ben holds Copycats, has 2 silver. Round end: Ben picks Most silver and ties Ana: ties split, half of 6 rounded up, so 3 each. Ana gets 3 instead of 6. Round III: 5 each instead of Ana's 10.
Choices: which contest. Every contest pays the same that round, so Ben picks the leader he most wants to pull down; no dominated option.
Who feels it: the leader of the chosen contest, every round; a reason not to run away with one contest.
Ledger: "gain the prize of a contest you lead now" was liked but too strong in round III and held for a future contests color (verdicts 14); this one only shares a prize Green didn't earn. Risk: may step on that future color.
Intuitive: one sentence; contests are visible at round end.
Fun: a sting at round end that the leader sees coming.
Interacts: every color's contest, never by name.
Powerful: +3 / +3 / +5 a game, plus what it takes from the leader.
Engine or counter: counters a runaway contest leader; with Tiebreaker ("You win every contest tie you're in.") it takes the whole prize.
Its color: copying a lead, new for Green.

## Impostors (tweak)
Live: [3 green + 5 any, worth 2, counts as 2] "On Recruit: this member becomes a copy of a rival's member."
Proposed: "On Recruit: this member becomes a copy of a shop member."
Played: round III, 4 players. The shop shows Proselytizers ("Whenever you recruit, +1 follower.") at 4 red + 6 any. Ben recruits Impostors and it becomes Proselytizers for 3 green + 5 any; the real Proselytizers stays in the shop for anyone.
Choices: none beyond the pick.
Who feels it: whoever wanted that shop card's effect; Blue's and Red's shop moves now shape what Green can become, without naming them.
Ledger: not refused. Why: it now differs from Impersonator (a rival's member) and Doppelganger (your own member): three copiers, three different places.
Intuitive / Fun / Interacts: one sentence; the shop is in every game.
Powerful: any ability in the row. Engine or counter: engine. Its color: copying the shop, new for Green.

## Members: keep
Song Request, Impersonator, Understudy, Ensemble, Impressionist, Wallflower, Fan Club, Copyist, Tribute Band, Doppelganger, Agents, Bait. Encore stays on Cory's own rework list.
