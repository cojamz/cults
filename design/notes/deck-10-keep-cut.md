# Member decks at 10: keep and cut (Claude's recommendations)

Nothing here is built. These follow the draft deck rules (decks/deck-rules-draft.md), which still need your yes: 4 round I and 6 later, 2 cheap (3 or less) and 2 big (8 or more) per color, a card that pays per member of its own color, a game end scorer, one sting, no twins. Each color's artifact sits outside the 10. Silver and Gold are left out while they're redesigned.

The number after each name is its printed price. A price change only moves a card between round I and round II; what it does stays the same.

**Bots on this exact list** (Normal bots, 100 four-player games, 50 members): the shop never sat short in any round. Round I had about 21 members in its pile and its lowest point averaged 9.

Bot numbers only broke ties. Every cut below has a reason a table would feel.

## Red: cut Headhunter and Host. Exactly 4 round I members already.

Keep, round I (4):
- **Triplets** (3, worth 3, counts as 3 members): "Worth 3 followers."
- **Volunteer** (1, worth 1): "Worth 1 follower." The cheapest card in the game.
- **Recruit Bonus** (3, worth 2): "Your On Recruits also give +1 of that member’s color." Your own design.
- **Deprogrammers** (4, worth 2, counts as 2 members): "Whenever you take a rival’s member out of their cult, they lose 1 follower." Red's sting.

Keep, round II on (6):
- **Mentor & Protégés** (9, worth 3, counts as 3 members): "On Recruit: recruit the cheapest member in the shop." Big buy.
- **Proselytizer** (7, worth 2): "Whenever you recruit, +1 follower."
- **Mob** (12, worth 5, counts as 4 members): "Game End: +1 follower per red member you have." Big buy, pays per red member.
- **Patriarch** (6, worth 1): "Game End: +1 follower per 2 members you have."
- **Panel** (7, worth 3, counts as 3 members): "On Recruit: promote or demote another of your members." Your own design.
- **Loyalists** (6, worth 2, counts as 2 members): "Your other members can’t be swapped." The counter to Red's own swaps.

Cut (2):
- **Host** (5, worth 1): "On Recruit: +1 red per 2 members you have. Round Start: +1 red per 2 members you have." A round start trickle; you said those feel weak. Patriarch already pays per 2 members.
- **Headhunter** (6, worth 1): "On Use, once per game: swap a rival’s member with a shop member that costs as much or more." My fill-in. It does what Promote and Demote already do (swap with a rival's member), once.
