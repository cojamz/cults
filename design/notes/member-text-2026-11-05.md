# Shorter member text (Nov 5)

What I counted: the 110 members dealt in today's game (10-card decks, grey members, cloaks). Their text averages 12 words, so single cards are not long. The load comes from three things:
- 35 members say "Whenever", and 17 of those pay when a rival does something, so you watch the whole table, not just your own turn.
- 5 members print the same effect twice ("On Recruit: X. Round Start: X.").
- Long conditions bolted onto simple effects ("costing 4 or less", "the rival with the most members", "before contests are scored").

Gold is parked and left out. Cloaks are left out (the cloak thread is reworking them to pay on your own actions). Night Watch is already retired.

## A. Same rules, fewer words (no change to how anything plays)

1. **Tourist** "On Recruit: +1 follower per color you hold. Round Start: +1 follower per color you hold." becomes "On Recruit and Round Start: +1 follower per color you hold." (16 to 10 words)
2. **Dyer** "On Recruit: +1 yellow per color you hold. Round Start: +1 yellow per color you hold." becomes "On Recruit and Round Start: +1 yellow per color you hold."
3. **Caravan** "On Recruit: +1 of each color. Round Start: +1 of each color." becomes "On Recruit and Round Start: +1 of each color."
4. **Seat Filler** "Round Start: you may give this to the shop and recruit a shop member costing up to 3 more, for free." becomes "Round Start: you may swap this for a shop member costing up to 3 more." (21 to 14)
5. **Jack-of-All-Trades** "When counting your full sets (1 of each color), your yellow can stand in for any missing color." becomes "Your yellow counts as any color in full sets." (18 to 9)
6. **Night Traders** "Round End: before contests are scored, swap up to 3 of your resources for other colors, one for one." becomes "Round End: change up to 3 of your resources to other colors." (19 to 12). Round End cards always act before contests now, so the "before contests" part says nothing.
7. **Second-Story Man** "Round End: before contests are scored, steal 2 resources from one rival." becomes "Round End: steal 2 resources from a rival." Same reason.
8. **Matchmaker** "Whenever you recruit a member of a color none of your members have, +2 wild, +1 follower." becomes "Whenever you recruit your first member of a color, +2 of any colors, +1 follower." ("wild" is printed nowhere else.)
9. **Duty Collector** "Whenever a rival recruits a member of a color you have a member of, +1 of that color." becomes "Whenever a rival recruits a member of one of your colors, +1 of that color."

## B. One word for "send to the bottom of the pile"

Seven Blue and White members spell out "send ... to the bottom of the pile, then refill". The pitch: print it as **bury** ("Bury: put it at the bottom of the pile; the shop refills.") on the rules page and use the one word on the cards. Examples:
- **Window Shopper** "On Recruit and Round Start: send 2 shop members to the bottom of the pile. +1 of each one's color." becomes "On Recruit and Round Start: bury 2 shop members. +1 of each one's color."
- **Window Dressers** "On Recruit: send 3 shop members to the bottom of the pile, then refill." becomes "On Recruit: bury 3 shop members."
- **Trendsetters** "Whenever you recruit a member, send 1 shop member to the bottom of the pile, then refill." becomes "Whenever you recruit a member, bury a shop member."
- **Patroness** "Whenever shop members go to the bottom of the pile, +1 follower." becomes "Whenever shop members are buried, +1 follower."
- **Downsizers** "On Use, once per round: cast out one of your members costing 4 or less to the bottom of the pile. +4 white." becomes "On Use, once per round: bury one of your members costing 4 or less. +4 white."
Blue's spaces (Bury, Out of Style, Savings) would use it too.

## C. Small rule trims (each cuts a condition the table has to check)

Each played at 4 players. Numbers are today's.

**C1. Guest of Honor** (Silver) "On Use, once per round: give a rival one of your members costing 4 or less for +4 followers." becomes "On Use, once per round: give a rival one of your members. +4 followers."
- Played: round II, you hold a 3-cost and a 7-cost member. Today you can give only the 3-cost one. After: you could also give the 7-cost one, but that loses more than +4 (it is worth followers and is a better gift to the rival), so you still give the cheap one. The cap almost never decides anything; it is just a number to check.
- Rival feels it: a gift every round, as now. Intuitive: one fewer number. Fun, interacts, powerful, engine, its color: unchanged (Silver gives).

**C2. Downsizers** (White) drop "costing 4 or less" (with or without "bury").
- Played: round III, you hold a 7-cost member whose On Use is spent. Today you can't cash it in; after, you send it down for +4 white, then White's round III space **Cash In** ("Turn all your white into followers. +1 white.") turns it into followers. It stays a trade: you lose the member's followers and member count.
- Interacts with **Cash In** and **Estate Sale** ("Cast out one of your members to the bottom of the pile, gain white equal to its cost. +1 white."), which has no cost cap. Powerful: a little more, since late you can dump anything.

**C3. Kidnapper** (Magenta) "On Recruit and Round Start: steal the cheapest member of the rival with the most members." becomes "On Recruit and Round Start: steal a rival's cheapest member."
- Played: round II start. Today you must count every cult to find who has most members (ties need a rule). After: you pick the rival; most players still pick the biggest cult, but nobody has to count.
- Rival feels it: you lose a member, same sting. Its color: Magenta steals.

**C4. Extortionist** (Magenta) "On Recruit: steal 2 resources from the rival with the most resources. Round Start: steal 2 resources from the rival with the most resources." becomes "On Recruit and Round Start: steal 2 resources." (24 to 9 words)
- Played: round III start. Today you count every rival's pile to find the target. After: you pick whom to rob, like every other steal card.
- This also follows your Nov 3 rule ("anything that relies on a rival having more of resources should be reworked").

**C5. Anarchist** (grey) "On Use, once per round: swap any 2 members, in any cult or the shop, that cost within 2 of each other." becomes "On Use, once per round: swap any 2 members that cost the same, in any cult or the shop."
- Played: round II. "Within 2" means checking two prices and doing math; "the same" is one look. It is weaker (fewer pairs), so it may need a lower price.
