## 2. Gloaters: "Whenever you win a contest, steal 1 follower from each rival." 8 any, worth 1
- Played turn, round II, 4 players: owner leads Most red: +6, then takes 1 loose follower from each of 3 rivals (+3, each rival -1). About 3 wins a game: +9, every rival -3.
- Choices: none. A rival with no loose followers gives nothing; Bodyguards blocks it.
- Who feels it: everyone, every contest the owner wins. A reason to block: contest them on the color they lead.
- Intuitive: one sentence. Fun: a sting the table sees at round end. Interacts: every contest, every rival. Powerful: a 3-player swing per win. Engine or counter: rewards holding; rivals counter by contesting. Its color: grey's thief of contests, the mirror of Sore Loser.
- Risk: it rewards the leader (win-more); priced high for that.
