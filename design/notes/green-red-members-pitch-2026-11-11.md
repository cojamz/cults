# New Green and Red members, batch 1 (Nov 11)

Gaps from the member ranking (Cory, Nov 10 10:03pm): no Green member pays for minions on Green spaces; no Red member pays for collecting members of one color (Mob counts red only).
Contests: Green "Most minions on green" (your minions on Green spaces at round end). Red "Most members of one color" (biggest group, grey counts, each card 1).
Live Green spaces: Build Up "+1 green per minion on Green spaces, this one included. +1 green." · Echo "Copy the previous action. +1 green." · Reprise "Copy an action with your minion. +1 green." · Crowd "+1 follower per minion on Green spaces. +3 green." · Cover "Copy an action with a rival's minion on it. +1 green." · Greatest Hits "Choose a color other than green. Copy every action with a minion on its spaces. +1 green."
Live Red spaces: Borrow, Trending, Gather "+3 red. If you recruit a member this turn of a color you already have: +4 followers. +1 red.", Promote, Demote, Initiation. Mob "Round End: +2 followers per rival with fewer red members than you."
Prices are left out of the pitch; Claude's numbers are fill-ins.

## Green

### G1 Gatecrashers (pick): "Once per round, you may place on a Green space a rival has taken."
- Round II, 4 players: a rival took Crowd first turn (+1 follower per minion on Green, +3 green). Without this you take Build Up for about 4 green. With it you also take Crowd: about +3 followers and +3 green, and your minion still counts for the Green contest. Gain: one blocked Green space opened, about 3 to 6 more than the next best space.
- Round III: Greatest Hits taken by a rival; you take it too and copy every action of a color. Top case big but once a round, at a cost of 7 to 8.
- Choices: none. Who feels it: the rival who thought taking the space blocked you; Green contest rivals lose a sure count. Reason to block: none works, which is the sting.
- How often: once a round, rounds II and III; round I only if bought then. Steps: place your minion on a taken Green space, do it. Table: two minions on one space, like the old Crowd In.
- Note: Squatter (now Party Crasher, "On Use, once per game: your next minion can be placed on a space a rival already took.", grey, cut Nov 7) was his "really great"; this is a Green-only, once-a-round take.
- Intuitive: bends the rule everyone feels (taken spaces) for one color. Fun: a rival's block fails. Interacts: reads rivals' placements. Powerful: yes, rule-bender. Engine: feeds Build Up and Crowd counts, the Green contest. Its color: Green lives on others' spaces; now it can share them. Meets the bar: one clean rule-bend, no memory beyond the card's once-a-round.

### G2 Regulars: "Round End: +1 follower per minion you have on Green spaces."
- Round II: 3 of your 4 minions on Green: +3 followers. Round III: 4 of 5: +4. A Green-focused game: about 8 to 10 over rounds II and III.
- Choices: none. Who feels it: quiet income (taste-guide pattern 6), and a reason for rivals to take Green spaces first to hold your count down.
- Note: Crowd pays the same count once as a space; this pays it every round end and asks you to stay on Green all round.
- Intuitive: one visible count. Fun: watching your Green minions add up; rivals block. Interacts: Green spaces are shared, rivals can take them. Powerful: steady scorer, his "scoring or passive income" yes. Engine: pays for Echo/Reprise/Cover chains staying on Green. Its color: the contest's own count, paid in followers.

### G3 Wanderers: "On Use, once per round: move one of your minions from another color's space to an open Green space."
- Round II: you placed on Heist earlier (steal 4 followers and 4 resources); later you move that minion to the open Green space: +1 Green contest count, and Heist's space opens for a rival's later minion. Gain: +1 toward +6 green contest, minus a gift of a space.
- Choices: you give a space back to the table; real trade. Who feels it: the Green contest rival, and whoever gets the freed space.
- Steps: lift your minion, put it on an open Green space (its action isn't taken). Risk: "open Green space" is often one spare in rounds II and III.
- Intuitive: mostly; moving a placed minion is new. Fun: late swing in the Green contest. Interacts: frees a space for rivals. Powerful: medium. Engine: Build Up and Crowd counts. Its color: chaos, moving pieces around. Weaker of the three.

## Red

### R1 Clan (pick): "Round End: +1 follower per member in your biggest group of one color (grey counts)."
- Round II: you hold 3 blue of 6 members: +3. Round III: 5 blue: +5. Over a game about 8 to 12.
- Choices: none. Who feels it: rivals see the visible count; Red's swaps (Promote, Demote, Body Snatchers, Poach) can take a member out of your group.
- Note: Mob pays per rival behind you in red members only; this pays your own group of any color, the Red contest's count.
- Intuitive: the Red contest's own count, paid each round end. Fun: building one big color. Interacts: rivals can steal or swap from your group. Powerful: steady scorer. Engine: Gather ("+4 followers if you recruit a color you already have"), Trending, Promote. Its color: collecting members of one color, new for Red members.

### R2 Matchmakers: "On Use, once per round: swap one of your members for a shop member of your biggest color costing as much or less."
- Round II: you hold 3 purple and a 6-cost blue member whose ability you've used. Swap the blue for a 5-cost purple from the shop: your group goes to 4 (Red contest lead), the blue sits in the shop for anyone.
- Choices: which member to give up; giving a strong one to the shop helps a rival. Who feels it: the rival who wanted that shop member, the Red contest rivals.
- Steps: put your member in the shop, take the shop member. One step.
- Intuitive: swap down into your color, like Demote aimed at your group. Fun: a visible jump in your group. Interacts: shop row changes for everyone. Powerful: medium, a free swap every round. Engine: Mob, R1, the Red contest. Its color: Red moves members; this one shapes your cult toward one color.

## Cut
- "Whenever you recruit a member of a color you already have, +2 followers": same condition as Gather; near twin in the same color.
- "Round End: +2 followers per rival with fewer members of your biggest color": a Mob with a changed count; a tweak of a live card.
- "Round Start: recruit the cheapest shop member of your biggest color, free": Cory dislikes "recruit cheapest" (Open Door), and a free member every round is too big.
- Green "Your minions on Green spaces count as 2 for the Green contest": a count bonus, the shape he refused for focus (Nov 2 "members count as 2" is out).
- Green "Round Start: place a minion on a Green space": out-of-turn placement, ruled out.

# Batch 2: Green, grown from Regulars (Nov 11, after Cory: "Regulars is a good seed of an idea")

Real table state (20 Hard-bot games, 4 players, Red/Green/Magenta/Purple, live v0.99.712 spaces, measured at each round end): minions on Green spaces per player average 0.75 / 1.24 / 1.27 in rounds I / II / III; the player with the most averages 1.5 / 2.2 / 2.4, highest seen 2 / 3 / 4. So Regulars as printed pays a Green-focused player about 6 over a game: too small. (Same games: biggest one-color group averages 1.0 / 1.6 / 2.0, highest 4, so Clan pays about 1 to 4 a round.)

### B1 Old-Timers (pick): "Round End: +1 follower per minion you have on Green spaces, times the round."
- Focused player at the measured counts: 1.5 + 4.4 + 7.2, about 13 over a game; top case (2/3/4): 2 + 6 + 12 = 20.
- Round III turn: you hold 3 minions on Green; placing your 4th on the last open Green space (Build Up "+1 green per minion on Green spaces, this one included. +1 green.", about 5 green) instead of Heist ("Steal 4 followers and 4 resources. +1 magenta.") is worth +3 followers at round end plus the space's 5 green, against Heist's 8. Close: a real choice.
- Who feels it: rivals take Green spaces to hold the count down (a reason to block). Choices: none.
- Intuitive: one visible count times the round. Fun: round III Green spaces get fought over. Interacts: shared Green spaces. Powerful: grows into the round III burst. Engine or counter: rides Echo, Reprise, Cover, Build Up, Crowd. Its color: pays the Green contest's own count.

### B2 Regulars at +2: "Round End: +2 followers per minion you have on Green spaces."
- Measured: 3 + 4.4 + 4.8, about 12; top case 4 + 6 + 8 = 18. Pays from round I, flat.
- Same feel as B1, earlier and flatter. Intuitive / Fun / Interacts / Powerful / Engine or counter / Its color: as B1.

### B3 Holdouts: "Game End: +3 followers per minion you have on Green spaces."
- One moment: round III, measured 2.4 for the most → about 7, top case 12. Makes the last round's Green spaces the fight.
- Intuitive: yes. Fun: a last-round count everyone watches. Interacts: blocking Green in round III. Powerful: medium. Engine or counter: as B1. Its color: the contest count.

### B4 Gawkers: "Round End: +1 follower per minion on Green spaces, anyone's."
- Table totals: Green spaces fill most rounds, 3 / up to 5 / up to 6, about 12 over a game whatever the owner does.
- Who feels it: everyone who takes a Green space feeds the owner (his pattern 3: payoffs on anyone's move). Weakness: pays without the owner playing Green.
- Intuitive: yes. Fun: watching the table crowd Green. Interacts: every player's placement. Powerful: steady. Engine or counter: fits Build Up and Crowd drawing rivals in. Its color: Green lives on others' play.

### Cut
- "Round End: if all your minions this round are on Green spaces, +3 followers per minion": the measured highest is 4 of 5 in round III and 2 of 3 in round I, so it almost never pays.
- "Round End: +2 green per minion on Green spaces": small resource gain.
- "Whenever you place a minion on a Green space, +1 follower": the same count as Regulars, paid sooner; about 6 a game.
