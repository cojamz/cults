# Card review 1: from your marks and the new contests (Nov 7, new page)

The new contests:
- **Red** Most members of one color, grey counts as a color
- **Blue** Most members
- **White** Fewest members
- **Silver** Fewest silver
- **Magenta** Most resources
- **Yellow** Most member colors
- **Green** Most minions on green
- **Purple** Went last

Every contest pays its leader +4 / +6 / +10 followers in rounds I / II / III.

Easiest first.
- Part 1: cuts and moves taken straight from your notes.

Labels: **(your note)** means it comes from your marks; **(Claude)** means it's my call or idea.

## 3. Cards the new contests rescued (worth a second look)

- **Second-Story Man** (Magenta, you marked weak, "contests don't really rely on resources") "Round End: before contests are scored, steal 2 resources from one rival." Magenta's contest now counts resources. So this swings that contest by 4 right before scoring.
- **Polish** (Silver round I, you marked weak, "feels off") "Turn 2 of your silver into 4 of any colors." This is now the cleanest way to get rid of silver.
- **Smooth Talkers** (Silver, star) "Your silver pays for any color." Same reason: it lets you get rid of silver.
- **Kingmakers** (Silver, star) "Round End: give a rival 3 silver from the bank. +3 followers." Round End comes before contests. So it lands 3 silver on a rival right before Fewest silver is scored. It's a gift and a sting at once.
- These three give rivals silver. Each is now a sting as well as a gift:
  - **Toast** "+3 silver in round I, +6 in round II, +9 in round III. Each rival +1 silver in round I, +2 in round II, +3 in round III."
  - **Party Gifts** "Give rivals up to 3 silver from the bank, split as you choose. +2 followers per silver you give."
  - **Largesse** "Give rivals 12 silver from the bank, split as you choose. +10 followers." It can bury one rival in the silver contest.
- These five now also help you win Fewest members:
  - **Downsizers** "On Use, once per round: cast out one of your members to the discard pile. +4 white."
  - **Undertaker** "Whenever you cast out one of your members, +2 followers."
  - **Martyrs** "Whenever this member leaves your cult, gain followers equal to its cost."
  - **Farewell** "Cast out one of your members to the discard pile, gain followers equal to its cost. +1 white."
  - **Estate Sale** "Cast out one of your members to the discard pile, gain white equal to its cost. +1 white."
- **Recluse** (White) "Round End: +2 followers per rival with more members than you." It now points the same way as White's contest.
- These four now feed Most member colors:
  - **New Blood** "+2 yellow. This turn, if you recruit a member of a color none of your members have, +2 yellow in round I, +3 in round II, +4 in round III."
  - **Housewarming** "+2 yellow. Recruit a shop member of a color none of your members have, costing up to 7, for free."
  - **Melting Pot** "+2 yellow, and +1 of each color among your members."
  - **Yellow Cloak (Guest Book)** "Whenever you recruit a member of a color none of your members have, +2 yellow."
- **Out of Style** (Blue) "Send every shop member of one color to the discard pile, then refill. +1 blue per member sent. +1 blue." It can now starve a Red player's color or a Yellow player's missing color.
- **Tiebreaker** (grey) "You win every contest tie you're in." Fewest members and Fewest silver tie a lot, often at 0. So this is much stronger now (Claude). Watch it.

## 4. Suggestions (Claude), each played out

**A. Walk-In's place: Swap** (Red round I) "Swap one of your members with a rival's or a shop member that costs the same. +2 red."
- **Played, round I, 4 players.** Ana holds a 4-cost blue member and builds red. Ben holds a 4-cost red member. Ana swaps hers for Ben's. Now she has a red member and he has her blue one.
  The best other round I space is **Gather** "+2 red in round I, +3 in round II, +4 in round III. This turn, if you recruit a member, +1 follower in round I, +2 in round II, +3 in round III." Compared with Gather, Swap gains Ana a red member and costs Ben his.
- **Who feels it:** Ben, and anyone whose one-color group just lost a card. Red is the color that moves members, as you put it.
- **Not refused:** it's a round I version of these, and you starred Anarchist:
  - **Promote** "Swap any member with one in another cult or the shop costing up to 3 more. +3 red."
  - **Demote** "Swap any member with one in another cult or the shop costing up to 3 less. +3 red."
  - **Anarchist** "On Use, once per round: swap any 2 members that cost the same, in any cult or the shop."
- **Risk:** in round I few players hold members. So some tables will take it just for the red.

**B. Silver Cloak (Caterer)** "Whenever you gift silver, +1 follower."
- **Played, round II.** Cara takes **Party Gifts** (text in part 3) and gives 3 silver from the bank. She gets +6 followers, plus +1 from the cloak. Rivals hold more silver now, which helps her contest.
- **Why:** it keeps your starred idea (gifting pays) without handing silver back. It's also a weaker cloak, and you said you like those.
- **Clash:** it has the same text as **Gift Wrapper** "Whenever you give rivals silver, +1 follower." Gift Wrapper is a member that isn't in the 10-card deck.

**C. Debutantes' place: Debutantes** (Silver member) "Round End: give each rival 1 of your silver. +1 follower each."
- **Played, end of round II, 4 players.** Cara holds 3 silver and the others hold 2. She gives 1 to each, so she holds 0 and they hold 3 each. She wins Fewest silver (+6) and gains +3 followers.
- **Who feels it:** every rival, right before scoring. And it's silver moving between players, which you asked for with **Patron Saints** "Game End: +1 follower per silver member, anyone’s."
- **Risk:** it's close to **Hostesses** "On Recruit and Round Start: give each rival 1 silver. +1 follower for each." (not in the 10-card deck). Hostesses gives silver from the bank, not your own.

**D. Magenta's weak round I space, Stash** ("+2 magenta in round I, +3 in round II, +4 in round III. This turn, if you recruit a member, +1 follower in round I, +2 in round II, +3 in round III."; your note: "not on identity at ALL"). Nothing I tried survived.
- Stealing 1 from every rival repeats **Help Yourself** once Help Yourself splits among rivals.
- You called the earlier "steal from each rival with more X" ideas not thoughtful.

**One question:** should Magenta's round I have a space that protects your pile, since **Lie Low** "Steal 1 resource. Rivals can’t steal from you this round. +1 magenta." is weak? Or a second steal?
