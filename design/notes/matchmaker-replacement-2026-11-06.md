# Matchmaker replacement (Yellow, 10-card deck), Nov 6

Cory, Nov 6 11pm: "matchmaker: yeah this felt bad because you usally have enough. might need to replace."
Live: **Matchmaker** [5 yellow + 4 any, worth 1] "Whenever you recruit a member of a color none of your members have, +2 wild, +1 follower."
Why it felt bad: 4 colors on a 4-player table plus grey. Most cults hold 3 or 4 of those 5 by mid round II, so it fires 1 or 2 times a game, and Guest Book (artifact) and New Blood (space) already pay on the same trigger.
Yellow, in Cory's words (Oct 30): "rewards going broad, i.e. having a combination of colors and being flexible. it also allows you to translate currencies (kind of like launderer)."
Yellow's 10 today: Duty Collector, Tourist, Seamstress, Matchmaker, Dyer, Night Traders, Counterfeiters, Big Tent, Caravan, Jack-of-All-Trades ("When counting your full sets (1 of each color), your yellow can stand in for any missing color.").
Prices are Claude's fill-ins at the slot's price.

## 1. Interpreters [pick] (5 yellow + 4 any, worth 1)
Prints: "Your yellow can pay any member's colored part."
Played: round III, 4 players. Mob [9 red + 6 any, worth 4] "Round End: +2 followers per rival with fewer red members than you." is in the shop. You hold 3 red, 11 yellow, 4 blue. Without Interpreters you can't buy it (3 of 9 red). With it you pay 3 red + 6 yellow for the red part and 4 blue + 2 yellow for the rest. The best other choice that turn is buying 10 followers for 10 resources; Mob is worth 4 plus up to +6 at round end plus the red contest count.
Choices: none for rivals; the owner chooses which member to aim at. Rivals feel it as a shop race: the red player saving for Mob can't count on you being short of red.
Intuitive: one sentence, the same "stand in" idea as Jack-of-All-Trades.
Fun: the table sees you buy a member no one thought you could afford.
Interacts: with every color's members (any shop member is in reach), with Yellow's contest (most yellow: hoarding yellow both scores and pays), and Counterfeiters aimed at you (turn your pile into a color other than yellow).
Powerful: bends the one cost rule everyone feels (colored part must be that color).
Engine or counter: engine with Harvest, Survey, Dyer, Caravan; yellow becomes your money.
Its color: translating currencies, Cory's own words; no yellow card lets you pay a cost in another color today.
Risk: strong; may need 6 yellow. Earlier cut by Claude (Nov 1) as "no rival feels it"; never put to Cory.

## 2. Matchmaker, new trigger (5 yellow + 4 any, worth 1)
Prints: "Whenever you recruit a member of a color you have the fewest members of, +2 wild, +1 follower." (ties count)
Played: round III, 4 players (red, blue, yellow, purple + grey). You have yellow 2, red 1, blue 1, purple 1, grey 1. Fewest is 1, so recruiting a red, blue, purple or grey member fires: +2 any colors, +1 follower. Only another yellow doesn't. In round I and early round II, with some color at 0, it reads as today's card.
Choices: the owner picks recruits to keep colors even; no rival choice.
Intuitive: one sentence, but counting "fewest" across 5 colors is a little more to track than today.
Fun: small; a bonus on most recruits once you're broad.
Interacts: weakly; a rival taking one of your members (Red, Magenta) can change which color is fewest.
Powerful: fires 4 or 5 times a game instead of 1 or 2: about 8 to 10 resources and 4 or 5 followers.
Engine or counter: engine for a broad cult; pairs with Big Tent, Tourist.
Its color: going broad, the same way in as today; a fix rather than a new card.

## Cut
- "Whenever a rival recruits a member of a color you have none of, +2 wild": fires on rivals, but going broad switches it off, against Yellow's own goal.
- "+1 follower per color among your members it doesn't match": scoring on variety, a refused shape; Big Tent and Tourist already count colors.
- Trade one-for-two with a rival: same shape as Barterers (flopped) and Hard Bargain.
- Swap a member for a rival's of a new color: Red's verb.
