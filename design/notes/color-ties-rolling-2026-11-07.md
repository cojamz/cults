## Sent as seeds (each one I'd defend)

### R1. Blue member: "Round End: recruit the cheapest member in the discard pile, for free."
- Round II, 4 players: Blue's Bury/Out of Style and White's Farewell put 3-6 members in the pile by round II. You get a free 3-cost member each round end: +1 to Blue's most-members count, no recruit spent. Best comparable: Last-Minute Shopper "Round End: +3 blue, then you may recruit." (you still pay).
- Round III: pile is widest; the cheapest is still cheap, so it stays a steady body, not a bomb.
- Who feels it: whoever cleared or cast out a cheap member sees it land in your cult; a White rival casting out a 2-cost member feeds you.
- How often: every round end, once the pile has a card; Blue's own shop clears are the own cause (and Blue spaces are in any game this member is in).
- Ties: moment "a member goes to the discard pile" (Blue, White, Red's Seat Filler); nothing takes from the pile today. With none of those, the pile still fills from Blue's own spaces.
- Intuitive yes · Fun: watch the pile · Interacts yes · Powerful: steady free member · Engine: Blue count · Its color: recruiting from a new place. Public and certain: discard pile face up, "cheapest" ties broken by owner's choice.

### R2. Yellow space, round II: "+2 yellow. Swap up to 3 of your resources with a rival's, one for one, colors your choice. They +1 follower per resource swapped."
- Round II: you hold 4 yellow you can't use; a rival holds the 3 blue you need. Swap 3: you get exactly the colors for the 7-cost member; they get 3 followers. Best other: Melting Pot "+2 yellow, and +1 of each color among your members." gives ~4 resources but not the colors you choose.
- Choices: you pick the rival and colors; the rival has no choice. Their side: they lose colors they planned on but gain 3 followers; it stings the rival saving one color (Gold's most gold, a recruit plan) and is a gift otherwise.
- Ties: moment "a resource changes hands"; Magenta's most-resources count is unchanged for the victim (one for one), Gold's most-gold count can swing.
- Intuitive yes · Fun: a real trade with a price · Interacts: any color's resources · Powerful: picks the exact colors · Its color: Yellow translates currencies (his sentence).

### R3. White space, round II: "Give one of your members to a rival. Gain white equal to twice its cost."
- Round II: give a spent 6-cost member: 12 white, and round III's Cash In "Turn all your white into followers." makes it 12 followers. Estate Sale "Cast out one of your members to the discard pile, gain white equal to its cost." gives 6. The price of the extra 6: a rival gets a member (worth followers, an ability, +1 to their member count).
- Choices: which rival. Give it to the White rival (hurts their fewest-members count) or to the player whose ability it helps least.
- Who feels it: the receiver, gift or sting (a White player hates getting one); Martyrs "Whenever this member leaves your cult, gain followers equal to its cost." fires on it.
- Ties: moments "a member leaves your cult" and "joins a cult from a rival's" (Foster Parents pays the receiver). Gives a member, never a minion (verdict 39 as narrowed).
- Intuitive yes · Fun: who gets the gift · Interacts: White × Blue × Red × Silver · Powerful: doubles · Engine: feeds Cash In · Its color: get rid of what you used, now at someone else's table.

### R4. Purple member: "Whenever you take the Goes Last token, steal 1 resource from each rival."
- Round II: Last Call "+1 purple. +1 follower and +1 purple per minion you have left. Take the Goes Last token…" now also takes 3 resources from the table. Sleep In and Overtime fire it too, so 2-3 times a game.
- Who feels it: every rival, a capped sting of 1 each time; a reason to take Purple's token spaces first.
- Ties: Purple's own token is the cause (always in a Purple game); the payoff feeds any color and Magenta's most-resources contest. Compare Pocket Watch "Whenever you take the Goes Last token, +3 purple." (bank, no sting) and Sleepyheads "+3 followers".
- Intuitive yes · Fun: the token becomes a raid · Interacts: any colors · Powerful: 3 per trigger · Its color: going last pays.

### R5. Magenta space, round II (variation on Cory's liked Protection Racket kernel): "Choose a rival. Steal 1 resource per member they have, up to 5."
- Round II: a Blue player with 5 members loses 5; Help Yourself gives 4 from anyone. The cap keeps the sting from going to 8-9 in round III (verdict 33).
- Who feels it: big cults; White's fewest-members player is safe. Ties Magenta × Blue × Red through member counts, read off the chosen rival, not a leader.

## Round 2 (after Cory's "Ideas, no batches… They're good enough", 1:56pm)

### R6. Green member: "Whenever anyone copies an action, +1 of any color."
- Round II, 4 players: Echo "Copy the previous action.", Reprise, Cover and Sample get taken 3-5 times a round across the table; you collect 3-5 resources of your choice a round. Impressionist "Whenever you copy an action or a member, +2 of its color" pays only your own copies.
- Who feels it: rivals copying feed you; a small reason to avoid Green spaces. Own cause: Green spaces are in every game this member is in. Payoff any colors (verdict 28).
- Intuitive yes · Fun: every copy pings you · Powerful: steady · Engine yes · Its color: Green rewarded for the table's copying.

### R7. Gold space, round II: "Each rival gives you 1 gold or 2 of any other colors, their choice."
- Round II: you get 3-6 resources; Collect Debts "Steal up to 3 gold from rivals, split as you choose." gives 3 gold. Victim chooses (Shake Down's shape): a rival in the most-gold race gives 2 others, a rival with spare gold gives 1 gold. Rivals with nothing give nothing.
- Both sides: giving gold feeds your gold contest; giving 2 others costs them more resources. Neither side always wins.
- Ties: resources change hands; Magenta's resource count and Gold's count both move.

### R8. Silver member: "Whenever a rival gives you silver, you may turn it into any colors."
- Toast "Each rival +1/2/3 silver", Party Gifts, Largesse "Give rivals 12 silver…", Kingmakers: rivals dump silver on you to hurt your fewest-silver count. This member turns the dump into fuel: Largesse handing you 6 becomes 6 of any colors.
- Who feels it: the giver, whose sting becomes a gift; Silver's own counter (member-process: a set has a few of its own counters). Own cause: Silver spaces are in every game this member is in.
- Intuitive yes · Fun: flips a sting · Interacts: any colors · Counter: yes · Its color: Silver's gifts, from the receiving side.

Testing thread cut R6-R8 (small, chooser's choice always one way, rare trigger; Gold not in the live set) and R5 (twin of Protection Money). Lesson: bigger and simpler.

## Round 3 (bigger, simpler)

### R9. Purple member: "Whenever you take the Goes Last token, recruit."
- Sleep In, Last Call, Overtime, Latecomers give the token 2-3 times a game: 2-3 extra recruits. Pocket Watch "+3 purple" and Sleepyheads "+3 followers" pay on the same moment far smaller. Own cause: Purple's own spaces. Feeds Blue's most-members race from Purple.

### R10. Red member: "Round Start: you may swap one of your members with one in the discard pile."
- Red's swap verb pointed at the pile nothing takes from. Round II: trade your spent 3-cost member for the 7-cost member a rival cast out or Blue cleared. Who feels it: whoever dumped a good card. Own cause: Red's own Promote/Demote put members in the shop, Blue/White fill the pile.

### R11. White member: "Whenever this member leaves your cult, recruit a member costing up to its cost, for free."

Farewell "Cast out one of your members… gain followers equal to its cost." on it: followers and a free replacement. A rival taking it (Poach, Body Snatchers, Shake Down) refills you at once. Own cause: White's own cast-out spaces.

Round II with 3 spent members: +9 and a small cult for White's contest; Martyrs and Undertaker fire on each.
