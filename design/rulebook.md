# Cults — How to play

Lead a cult for three rounds. Send minions to the wheel for resources, recruit members, and win contests. Most **followers** after round III wins. 2 to 6 players. Point at anything in the game for its full rule.

![The table: seats on the left, the wheel in the middle, the shop on the right. Here each player is picking a cloak.](rules-img/table.jpg)

## What you have
- **Followers** are your score. You start with 1. Each member is worth 1 follower, or the number beside its member mark (Ghost: 0). It also counts as that many members, wherever members are counted, unless a rule says each card counts 1.
- **Minions** are your workers: 3 in round I, 4 in round II, 5 in round III. The spare minions are the open spaces left over once every minion still to come has one. A **+1 minion** gained during a round takes a spare; with none left it gives nothing.
- **Resources** come in the colors in play, one resource per color. **The bank** is the supply nobody holds. A dot split into the table's colors means any resources, colors your choice.
- **Members** are cards you recruit from the shop. Most have an ability.

## Setup
Turn order is drawn. Then, from last player to first, each picks a **cloak**: a boon all game plus 1 resource of its color. The first player gets the one left.

## The wheel
Each color in play has six spaces on the wheel. The outer ring opens in round I, the middle in round II, the center in round III. A space holds one minion per round. The rim shows that color's contest and who leads it.

A space that ends "+1 of its color" pays that +1 only to the first player ever to take it; a dot under the space shows its +1 is still there. A copy never gets it. (A setup setting, **Space +1**, can give it to every taker instead.)

![A color's spaces: open spaces are pale with gold edges, taken ones dim, dark ones open later](rules-img/wheel.jpg)

## Your turn
1. **Place a minion** on an open space and take what it gives.
2. **Recruit** one thing, or pass: a **member** from the shop, paying its cost, or **followers**, paying 1, 5 or 10 resources of any colors for that many.

Use your members' abilities any time on your turn. "Once per round" (badge 1× R) comes back each round; "once per game" (badge 1×) is used up, but the member still counts.

A member's opening word says when it acts: **On Use**, when you use it on your turn; **On Recruit**, when it joins you by your own doing; **Round Start**, before anyone places; **Round End**, after the last minion, before contests; **Game End**, after round III. With no word it is always on. **II+** means it works from round II on.

![Your turn: place, then recruit](rules-img/turn.jpg)

## Turn order
Turns snake: 1-2-3-4, 4-3-2-1, 1-2-3-4, one minion each, until all are placed. From round II, fewest followers goes first (a tie: whoever was later last round), unless a member says it goes first.

The **Goes Last token** comes from Purple spaces. Its holder's other minions wait until every other hand is empty, then are placed. If another player takes it, the old holder's waiting minions go back to their turns. Purple's contest, **Went last**, goes to whoever placed the round's last minion, token or not.

## The shop
The deck has 10 members per color plus 15 grey. 6 show in round I, 7 in round II, 8 in round III; gaps refill from the pile. Pricier members join the pile in round II. Members that leave play (the old row between rounds, members discarded or sent away) go face up on the discard pile beside the deck, so everyone can see what's out. When the pile runs out, shuffle the discard pile into a new pile. A cost's colored part must be that color; the rest is any color. Grey members cost any color. Where a card says "a color none of your members have", grey counts as a color.

![The shop: total price in the dark circle, its colors along the foot](rules-img/shop.jpg)

## Round end
1. Round-end abilities happen.
2. Each contest is scored in turn. The leader gets **+4 followers** in round I, **+6** in round II, **+10** in round III. Tied leaders each get half, rounded up. In a "most" contest you need at least 1 of what's counted; in a "fewest" contest, 0 counts.
3. Minions come home and the shop deals a fresh row.

## Game end
After round III, add game-end bonuses. Most followers wins; a tie goes to more members.

![Game end: final followers for each cult](rules-img/game-end.jpg)

## The colors
- **Red** recruits: free members, swaps with shop and rival members.
- **Blue** bends the shop: extra recruits, burying members, a look at the pile.
- **Green** copies spaces: a rival's, or your own again.
- **Purple** bends turns: go twice in a row, skip turns, +1 minion.
- **Magenta** steals resources and members.
- **White** turns what you have into followers.
- **Yellow** goes broad: picks its colors, changes rivals' resources.
- **Silver** gives rivals gifts that pay you back.

## Fine print
- To **recruit** is to bring in a member or followers by your own doing. Each follower purchase is one recruit. A member a rival moves to you isn't your recruit.
- To **copy** an action is to get its own printed gain, the amount on its chip, except the first taker's +1 of its color (with **Space +1** set to every taker, a copy gets that too). The taker's member bonuses are not copied. A copied steal, swap or gift is done again. "The previous action" is the last minion placed. Copying a copy lets you choose again, but never an action already in that chain.
- To **steal** is to take from a rival. The followers a rival's members are worth can't be stolen; their other followers can. Nothing drops below 0.
- A **skipped** turn is placed later in the same round; nobody loses a minion. **+1 minion** never happens in round I.
- Swapping members with a rival is a trade, not a steal. **Promote** and **Demote** swap any cult's member, yours or a rival's, with one in the shop or another cult.
- The **pile** is the face-down deck. A **pile** of resources is all you hold of one color. A **full set** is one resource of every color in play.
- The player on your **left** is the next seat from setup: P1's left is P2, and the last player's left is P1. The seats on screen are listed in turn order instead.
- Round down unless a card says otherwise.
