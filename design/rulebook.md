# Cults — How to play

Lead a cult for three rounds. Send minions to the wheel for resources, recruit members, and win contests. Most **followers** after round III wins. 2 to 6 players. Point at anything in the game for its full rule.

![The table: seats on the left, the wheel in the middle, the shop on the right. Here each player is picking a cloak.](rules-img/table.jpg)

## What you have
- **Followers** are your score. You start with 1. Each member is worth 1 follower, or the number beside its member mark.
- **Minions** are your workers: 3 in round I, 4 in round II, 5 in round III.
- **Resources** come in one color per sphere. A dot split into the table's colors means any resources, colors your choice.
- **Members** are cards you recruit from the shop. Most have an ability.

## Setup
Turn order is drawn. Then, from last player to first, each picks a **cloak**: a boon all game plus 1 resource of its color. The first player gets the one left.

## The wheel
Each player's color is a sphere with six spaces. The outer ring opens in round I, the middle in round II, the center in round III. A space holds one minion per round. The rim shows that color's contest and who leads it.

![A sphere: open spaces are pale with gold edges, taken ones dim, dark ones open later](rules-img/wheel.jpg)

## Your turn
1. **Place a minion** on an open space and take what it gives.
2. **Recruit** one thing, or pass: a **member** from the shop, paying its cost, or **followers**, paying 1, 5 or 10 resources of any colors for that many.

Use your members' abilities any time on your turn. "Once per round" comes back each round; "once per game" is used up, but the member still counts.

![Your turn: place, then recruit](rules-img/turn.jpg)

## Turn order
Turns snake: 1-2-3-4, 4-3-2-1, 1-2-3-4, one minion each, until all are placed. From round II, fewest followers goes first.

## The shop
6 members show in round I, 7 in round II, 8 in round III; gaps refill from the pile. Pricier members join the pile in round II. A cost's colored part must be that color; the rest is any color. Grey members cost any color.

![The shop: total price in the dark circle, its colors along the foot](rules-img/shop.jpg)

## Round end
1. Round-end abilities happen.
2. Each contest is scored in turn. The leader gets **+4 followers** in round I, **+6** in round II, **+10** in round III. Tied leaders each get half, rounded up. You need at least 1 of what's counted.
3. Minions come home and the shop deals a fresh row.

## Game end
After round III, add game-end bonuses. Most followers wins; a tie goes to more members.

![Game end: final followers for each cult](rules-img/game-end.jpg)

## The colors
- **Red** recruits: free members, swaps with shop and rival members.
- **Blue** bends the shop: extra recruits, burying members, a look at the pile.
- **Green** copies spaces: a rival's, or your own again.
- **Purple** bends turns: go twice in a row, skip turns, +1 minion.
- **Magenta** steals resources and members.
- **White** turns what you have into followers.
- **Yellow** goes broad: picks its colors, changes rivals' resources.
- **Silver** gives rivals gifts that pay you back.

## Fine print
- To **recruit** is to bring in a member or followers by your own doing. Each follower purchase is one recruit. A member a rival moves to you isn't your recruit.
- To **copy** an action is to get its own printed gain, the amount on its chip. The taker's member bonuses and gifts to others are not copied. A copied steal or swap is done again. Copying a copy lets you choose again, but never an action already in that chain.
- To **steal** is to take from a rival. Only followers a rival recruited or won can be stolen. Nothing drops below 0.
- A **skipped** turn is placed later in the same round; nobody loses a minion. **+1 minion** never happens in round I.
- Swapping members with a rival is a trade, not a steal.
- Round down unless a card says otherwise.
