# Cults — How to play

For 2 to 6 players. You lead a cult for three rounds. Send minions to the wheel, gather resources, recruit members and win contests. Whoever has the most **followers** after round III wins.

## Pieces
- **Followers** are your score, shown by the follower mark. You start with 1 (yourself). Each member you hold adds the followers it is worth. Followers you recruit or win are yours too, and only those can be stolen.
- **Minions** are your workers, small copies of your emoji: 3 in round I, 4 in round II, 5 in round III.
- **Resources** come in one color per sphere on the table. A resource is a plain dot in its color. A dot split into the table's colors means "any resources", your choice of colors.
- **Members** are cards you recruit from the shop. Most have an ability. Each member counts as 1 member, or the number its card shows beside the member mark, and is worth that many followers: member count = follower count. Followers from abilities never add members.

## The colors
There is one color per player on the table, picked on the setup page (Classic, Random or your own). Classic is Blue, Red, Yellow and Purple; five players add Green and six add Magenta, three drop Purple, and two drop Yellow too. Each color is a sphere on the wheel with its own spaces, members, cloak and contest (most of that color held at round end).
- **Red** recruits: free members, and swaps with shop and rival members.
- **Blue** bends the shop: extra recruits, burying shop members, a look at the pile.
- **Green** copies spaces: a rival's, or your own again.
- **Purple** bends turns: go twice in a row, skip turns, +1 minion.
- **Magenta** steals resources and members from rivals.
- **White** turns what you have into followers.
- **Yellow** goes broad: picks its colors, and changes rivals' resources.
- **Silver** gives gifts to rivals that pay you back.

## The wheel
- The wheel has one sphere per player, each in its own color, with six spaces: three on the outer ring, two on the middle ring and one in the center. Each sphere's contest is printed on its rim.
- The outer ring opens in round I, the middle ring in round II and the center in round III. Open rings stay open.
- Pale spaces with gold edges are open now. Taken spaces dim. Dark spaces open in a later round.
- A space holds one minion per round, unless a card lets you join a taken space (Party Crasher). Every minion comes home at round end.

## Setup
Round I's turn order is drawn at random. Then, starting with the player who goes last, each player picks a **cloak** from the list on the wheel: a boon for your cult all game, which comes with 1 resource of its color. Your leader wears it and your player card is outlined in its color, so everyone sees who that color's moves pay. Each sphere has one cloak (the Red Cloak, the Blue Cloak…), and a taken one leaves the list. The first player gets the one left over. This happens only before round I.

## Turn order
- Turns snake: down the order and back up, one minion per turn (1-2-3-4, 4-3-2-1, 1-2-3-4…) until everyone has placed every minion. The players at each end take two turns in a row.
- Round I's order is the random draw. From round II, fewest followers goes first; ties go to whoever went later last round.
- **+1 minion** (Overtime, Extra Shift, Go-Getter, Night Owls) gives you one more minion this round, to place and recruit with. The card says when you place it; otherwise it goes after everyone else. It never happens in round I, and later only while a space would still be free after every minion still to come has landed.
- **In a row** (Right Away, Crunch Time, Clockmakers) moves your own next turn up, so you place twice in a row. No new minion: you place the same minions, just sooner.
- What you just did happens now, even if a skip is already waiting on you: a copied Right Away after your Last Call still places right away, then the skip goes on.
- A **skipped** turn is passed over: play goes on to the next turn, and you place that minion later in the same round. Nobody loses a turn or a minion. A skip only reaches a turn in the same round: if that player has no turn left, nothing is skipped, and a skip never carries into the next round. Cards that pay "whenever a turn is skipped" pay when play reaches the skipped turn.
- The **Goes Last token** comes with Purple. Last Call and Sleep In take it and put minions on it. Its holder places the minions on it once every hand is empty. If someone takes it, the minions on it go back to their owner's hand. Turns keep going round until every hand is empty; a player whose hand is empty is passed, and that pass is a skipped turn.

## Your turn
1. **Place a minion** on an open space and do what it says. If no space is open, that minion sits out.
2. **Recruit** one thing, or pass:
   - **A member** from the shop, paying its cost.
   - Or **followers** with any resources: 1 resource for 1 follower, 5 for 5, or 10 for 10. That purchase is your recruit for the turn. The supply never runs out.

You can use a member's ability any time during your own turn, as often as its card allows. **On Use, once per round:** one time each round; it comes back when the next round starts. **On Use, once per game:** one time in the whole game; afterward the member stays in your cult and still counts for its followers. **Passive:** always on while you hold it. **Game End:** scores after round III. A space that recruits a member for you (Walk-In, Initiation, Poach) doesn't use up your recruit. Recruit Twice, Spree and Shopaholics let you recruit more than once in a turn.

## The shop
- 6 members are face up in round I, 7 in round II and 8 in round III. When one leaves, the top of the face-down pile refills the row. The pile shows how many are left.
- The setup page picks the member deck: about 15 members per color, or the trimmed 10 per color (with 10 grey).
- Round I deals only the cheaper members. The pricier round II and round III members are shuffled into the pile when round II starts (or, if the setup page says so, round III members wait for round III).
- Between rounds the whole row goes to the bottom of the pile and a fresh row is dealt.
- A cost has a colored part, paid only in that member's color, and an "any" part, paid in any resources. Grey members have no color and cost only "any".
- The pay sheet suggests a payment; tap one of your resources to swap it in.
- A discount ("your next member costs 4 less") waits until your next member that costs something, comes off the colored part first, and is used up by that member. While you hold one, each card shows what you'd pay, with its list price struck above it, or "free".
- Point at a card to see what you're short of. The "affordable" switch fades the members you can't pay for.

## Recruiting
To **recruit** is to bring a member or followers into your cult by your own doing: recruit from the shop (paid or free), take a member from a rival, swap one in with your own Red action (Promote, Demote, Panel), or recruit followers (each purchase is one recruit). A member a rival moves into your cult is not your recruit.
- "Whenever you recruit" happens on each of these.
- A member's "On Recruit:" happens when it joins your cult by your own doing, not when a rival moves it to you. "Round Start:" happens each time a round begins, before anyone places. A card can have both.

## Copying and stealing
- To **copy** an action is to get its own printed gain, the amount on its chip. The taker's member bonuses and gifts to others are not copied, and the taker keeps what they got. An action that took or traded something (a steal, a swap, a paid recruit) is done again by the copier. Copying a copy lets you choose again; never choose an action already in that chain.
- Green's copies: Echo copies the previous action, including your own on a snake double turn. Cover copies an action with no minion on it, and Reprise one with your own minion. Greatest Hits copies the On Recruit of each of your members, one at a time in the order you pick. Every Green action gives +1 green, copied or not. Sample isn't a copy of an action: it does the On Recruit of a rival's member for you, and the rival keeps the member. Agents stops rivals from copying its owner's actions.
- To **steal** is to take from a rival. Stolen resources keep their colors. Unless the card names one rival, a steal can be split among rivals, and a bonus that doubles or adds to a steal applies once to the total. Heist takes 3 of a rival's followers, and only followers they recruited or won can be stolen. Nothing drops below 0. Giving a rival one of your members and recruiting one of theirs (Promote, Demote, Panel, Double Agent, Anarchist, Headhunter) is a trade, not a steal: Bodyguards and Lie Low don't stop it.
- Rounding is always down unless a card says otherwise.

## Round end
1. Members with round-end abilities act.
2. Each sphere's contest is scored on what players hold right now. Its rim says what it counts, usually the most of its color held.
3. The leader gains +4 followers in round I, +6 in round II and +10 in round III. Tied leaders each gain half, rounded up: +2, +3 or +5, however many tie. You need at least 1 of what's counted to score.

Then minions come home, the shop deals a fresh row and the next round's order is set.

## Game end
After round III is scored, game-end member bonuses are added. Most followers wins; a tie goes to the player with more members.

- Something looked wrong? Open the menu, tap **Copy game record**, and send it to Cory.

## Card rulings
- Impostors and Doppelganger become copies when recruited. Doppelganger also does the copied member's "On Recruit" right then; Impostors doesn't. Their copied abilities and later round-start effects work normally.
- Panel's "On Recruit" promotes or demotes another member; Panel cannot move itself.
- Loyalists protects its owner's other members from every swap, its owner's own swaps included, but not itself, and it does not block theft. Two Loyalists (a Doppelganger copy) don't protect each other.
