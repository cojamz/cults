# Cory's verdicts on names, with why

Read when: step 2 of the naming skill, every time. Add every new yes or no the turn he gives it, with his words. A no also goes into "His nos" in .claude/rules/member-names.md (name-check.mjs refuses those names forever).

## His principles, in his words
- "Goal is more very accurate name for the ability/member rather than thematic link." (Nov 24, 4:17pm)
- "the idea is youve recruited this person into your cult." (Nov 24) "Promoting somebody doesnt change them into a different occupation / type of person." "The names help give the cult a funny interesting flavor."
- "they should be playful but not too specific." (Nov 24, on Missionaries) "Match it more to the playful but 5-10% dark target."
- "you're focusing too much on the concept and not what the card actually does." Clique "allows you to benefit from members of the same color.... how does it make sense as a Clique? ... the card is a Clique enabler/benefitter, not a Clique." Influencers "aren't influencers, in fact they are INFLUENCED". "They're better as professions or archetypes of a concept."
- "You're running a cult. You get a 'Landlord' in it. The Landlord.... charges every other member 1 red? somehow? Idk. it doesn't follow." (the role must naturally do the card)
- "I like old village. It doesn't have to be modern." (Nov 24, 1:00pm) "more old timey" (on Wingman, Temp).
- "there have got to be better terms here, you are thinking too simply." (on Town Crier, Apprentice, Horse Trader, Recruiting Sergeant)
- "Some dipping into fairytale/folklore stuff is fine. Just doesnt have to be literally everything." "Ok now this is TOO fairy tale/nursery rhyme maybe" (Hansel, Mother Goose). Bridge Troll, Rumpelstiltskin: "youve moved too far from general old village times."
- "doesnt make sense that the frog prince would still exist and could do the same the next round" (the name must survive the effect firing again).
- "members shouldn't have the same name lol" (Nov 24, 11:16pm, Pilgrim beside Pilgrims).
- A member's name is a person or a group of people, never a place, building or thing (Nov 5, on Ballroom: "these should be MEMBERS not buildings or things").
- On Doorbusters, Nov 25: "thats not on theme", "how did Doorbusters slip through the cracks?"
- On Restockers, Regular, Last Callers, Book Cookers, Nov 25: "yes. but rename them WELL."
- On Forestallers (the judges' 8-of-10 pick), Nov 25: "Forestallers is also bad." Lesson: judges' taste is not the bar; plain words a casual player knows.
- Nov 25: "you are really bad at naming ... i dont want to pick. i want you to improve."

## Yes (and why it works)
- **Tavern Keeper** (+1 red per 2 members): a fuller house takes in more.
- **Guild Master** (+1 follower for recruiting a color you have): more of the same trade. "ok"
- **Ferryman** (+1 red per recruit): a coin for every soul he brings across. "eh but ok"
- **Fairy Godmother** (promote or demote one of yours): magic really does change who they are.
- **Pied Piper** (send a rival's member to the shop for one costing the same or more): leads one of theirs away.
- **Warden** (your members can't be swapped): keeps everyone locked in.
- **Governess** (recruit the cheapest shop member with her): a child comes with her.
- **Journeyman** (trade itself for a member costing up to 3 more): only stays a while, moves on. "is ok"
- **Swindler** (swap yours for a rival's that costs more): always gets the better end.
- **Abbess** (+2 when a rival's member joins you): takes in anyone a rival lets go.
- **Dyer** (grey members count as your biggest color): colors the plain ones.
- **Innkeeper**, **Wet Nurse**, **Chapman** ("I like Chapman"), **Beggar**, **Night Porter**, **Spice Merchant**: plain village trades, accepted with their cards (Nov 24-25).
- **Drifters** ("Drifters is good", Oct 31).

## No (and which failure it was)
- Modern: Doorbusters, Wingman, Temp ("wingman and temp are bad"), Consultant, Brand Manager, Orientation Leader, Bean Counter, Recruiter, Headhunter, Middle Manager ("the idea is youve recruited this person into your cult"), Pyramid Schemer, Bouncer, Regular, Restockers, Last Callers, Book Cookers (modern idioms).
- Obscure: Forestallers.
- Concept, not a person you recruit: Clique, Influencers, Picky Eaters, Social Butterfly, Missionaries, Elders, Den Mother, Ride-or-Dies.
- Mechanic word for word, or "thinking too simply": Recruiting Sergeant ("feels off"), Town Crier, Apprentice, Horse Trader.
- Role doesn't do the card: Landlord, Mother-in-Law ("makes no sense"), Brainwasher ("closer but still off"), Frog Prince.
- Storybook: Hansel, Mother Goose, Rumpelstiltskin, Bridge Troll.
- Weak tie: Gatekeeper, Tailor ("eh"), Puritan, Dowager ("need better options"), Matriarch, Shepherd ("both bad"), Interior Decorator, Kinsman ("I don't like Kinsman as much").
- Not a member at all: Ballroom, and any place, building or thing.
